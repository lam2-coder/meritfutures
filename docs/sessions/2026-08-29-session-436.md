### 2. The answer, in one paragraph

**The card.** `useCertificateBackend` owes an `image_url` that addresses it; `useCertificateImageSource`
owes its bytes; and `databaseAccountReads.readCertificate`, which is already installed, refuses on
the same field. **A renderer plus somewhere for its output to live closes all three, and nothing
smaller closes any of them.** Where it belongs is named rather than left open: the renderer with the
issuer, which [M11](../plans/M11-certificates-social-proof.md) section 3.2 puts in the **worker**;
the bytes in an object store; the address and its signature with whatever serves those bytes, which
is [ADR-240](../decisions/ADR-240.md) clause 8 unchanged. **`apps/api` renders nothing and signs
nothing. It addresses.**

### 5. Where this session is most likely wrong

**THE CLAIM THAT THE TWO PORTS ARE ONE DELIVERABLE RESTS ON THE CARD BEING ONE ARTEFACT**, and
nothing here proves that the bytes `useCertificateImageSource` serves and the object `image_url`
addresses are the same object. A live re-rendering endpoint and a stored card in an object store are
**both** consistent with every approved sentence read here, and `INV-M11-08` arguably prefers the
first. If they are two objects, section 2 clause 2 of the entry is the sentence that falls.

**AND THE STATE-DEPENDENCE CASE IS A FENCE NOBODY ASKED FOR.** No approved document says a port must
refuse uniformly across callers. `usePayoutBackend`'s one-live-arm rule is the nearest thing and it
is a rule about ARMS rather than about callers.
