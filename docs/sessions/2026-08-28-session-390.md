### 1. `B4`, and why the predicate is the whole of it

The field is the `started_at` of the newest **completed** run. Both halves are transcribed, and the
predicate is the load-bearing one: `reconciliation_runs_completed_is_whole` exists because
`OVERVIEW` section 5.2 makes a sweep resumable at the account boundary, so a clock over every run
dates a crash. Live, the seeded estate holds a `failed` run at 05:12 and a `running` one at 05:40,
both later than the newest completed run at 05:04, and the reader answers 05:04.

An estate with no completed run is a **refusal** and not a blank. `last_run_at` is a required `string`
in all three declarations, `ADR-203` puts an absence at a nullable FIGURE, and `ADR-202` ruling 3
refuses the half-null object that would be the alternative.

### 2. `B3` and `B2`, and the difference between sayable and said
