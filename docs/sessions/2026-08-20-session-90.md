### The five things the file argues rather than asserts

1. **The closed vocabulary is what makes this not a report builder.** `report_schedules.digest` is a `CHECK` over exactly the four [ADR-066](../decisions/ADR-066.md) section 3 sizes. Without it, "the named set" is a sentence in a plan and the module section 8's fourth alternative rejected arrives one custom report at a time.
2. **The delivery log is the load-bearing half and the alarm reads it.** [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) `INV-M5-18`'s construction on a second sweep, and `due_at` is what makes absence askable: without a stored window, "nothing arrived" and "not due yet" are the same empty result set.
3. **There is no `skipped` outcome.** Two values. A skip that can be *recorded* as an outcome reads as normal in a list of outcomes, and the acceptance is that a failed delivery alarms and never silently skips.
4. **`GS-290` cannot degrade to zero.** An omission with no reason is unwritable, and a delivery that reached nobody cannot be recorded as `delivered`. Full degradation is a failure that has learned to look like a success.
5. **No artifact is stored, only its SHA-256.** A table of rendered digest bodies would **be** the bulk identity export `INV-M6-10` forbids, sitting behind an admin route, created by the feature admitted on the promise it was not one.

### What execution found that reading would not have

**`0040` was applied and its constraints were run: 24 assertions, 24 / 24, four successes first** on [DELTA_MANIFEST](../../packages/db/DELTA_MANIFEST.md) section 13's lesson that a probe which only attempts forbidden things passes against a guard that rejects everything. **Two of the twenty-four were refused by a constraint other than the one they were aimed at, and both are recorded in section 4b rather than tidied away.**

| Aimed at | What actually refused it |
|---|---|
| A fifth `digest` value | **The NOT NULL on the generated `cadence` column**, because a generated column is computed **before** CHECK constraints. The error names `cadence` for a defect in `digest`. Dropping the NOT NULL and re-inserting shows `report_schedules_digest_check` firing, so both are live and **each refuses a widening of the other**: admitting a fifth digest is a two-place edit and a half-admitted one cannot exist |
| The outcome `skipped` | **`report_deliveries_failure_states_its_reason`**, because the probe row carried a `failure_reason`. Re-run clean, `report_deliveries_outcome_check` fires |

**A refusal counted against the wrong constraint is a constraint nobody has actually watched work.** Without the isolated re-run this session would have claimed the two-value outcome vocabulary was exercised when what was exercised was a constraint about failure reasons.

**And the test's own tripwire fired on a denial.** The first version of the no-secrets assertion matched `/credential/i` over the whole DDL and failed on `COMMENT ON COLUMN report_schedules.recipients IS 'Never a credential'`. It now reads **column names**, which is what the assertion always meant: a tripwire that fires on the sentence promising the opposite, and would keep firing however that sentence were reworded, is a tripwire somebody switches off.
