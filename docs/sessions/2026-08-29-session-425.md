**Objective.** Measure the real entropy of a certificate code and make the
control the corpus already spends a rate limit on enforced by something.

**Outcome.** **Measured, and the measurement is that the generator did not
exist.** A mint now does, at 130 bits, and `RI-22` runs it on every `CI-01`
pass. The rate limit is ruled separately and the row's own citation for it does
not survive the line.

generator was absent rather than weak, so the deliverable is a mint and not a

### 2. What was built

`packages/db/src/certificate-code.ts`, exported from `packages/db/src/index.ts`,
with its own suite. Thirty-two distinct symbols over twenty-six positions is
**130** bits, and the figure is computed from the alphabet rather than typed:
`128` appears in no constant, here or in the check.

`RI-22` in `repo-invariants.mjs`, three legs. Leg 1 reads the threshold out of
the five corpus sites and requires them to agree. Leg 2 executes the mint and
measures two thousand draws. Leg 3 requires every writer of a `certificates` row
to name `mintCertificateCode`, which has no site in the shipped tree and is
exercised in both directions by the suite.

### 3. What was ruled

**The mint lands ahead of its issuer**, which is `ADR-231` section 6's own named
safer order applied to the half that is not a migration.

**It lives in `packages/db`** because that is the only package both deployables
that need it already declare.

**The catalog's rate budget stays declined and a rate limit is owed anyway**,

second one in its own words.

### 5. Landmines

- **AN ABSENT GENERATOR READS AS A SATISFIED SPECIFICATION.** A short code is
  visible in a diff. A missing one has never been contradicted, so five
  documents asserting it were read for weeks as descriptions of a deployment.
- **A DISPATCH'S CITATION IS NOT THE LINE.** Row `235` quoted
  `API_CONTRACT:1473` accurately and read it backwards, and the sentence it
  quoted is the last clause of a row whose first clause says the opposite.
- **ONE CHECK'S FIXTURE CAN DELETE ANOTHER'S.** `write` overwrites, and the
  first attempt at teaching the fixture about `RI-22` removed the `ts` fence
  `RI-18` reads. It appends now.
- **A WRONG LENGTH HIDES A WRONG SHAPE.** Seeding a prefix without shortening
  the loop makes `RI-22` report the length instead, which is correct precedence
  and would have looked like a miss.
- **THE MARGIN OVER `INV-M11-05` IS TWO BITS.** One position fewer misses it,
  and the suite asserts exactly that so a later trim goes red.
- **`DISPATCH_PROTOCOL` section 4's invariant row still reads 18** against a
  runner reporting **21**. Outside every fence, reported again.
