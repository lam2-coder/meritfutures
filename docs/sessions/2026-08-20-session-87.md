### The finding: the largest admitted item is a repair, not an addition

**[M07:470](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md) `DEP-M7-06` has declared "a maintained Tier-1 economic calendar, as data" since M07 was written**, assigned it to M6 admin plus seed, and [M07:267](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md) `FM-M7-08` has required its staleness alarm. **A grep over `docs/`, `packages/db/migrations/` and `packages/` finds no table satisfying either.** So `D-04` news-window clustering **has been unimplementable for the whole life of the corpus**, and no gate could see it: **a declared dependency with no satisfying object is invisible to every check this repository runs.**

**The parity check found a hole in our own corpus, not a hole against a competitor.** That is the entry's content and it is also the answer to its own strongest objection: the two MUSTs would be MUST with no vendor in the picture.

A fold that transcribed the referral would have put "four classes" into M16 itself. **The others: no reporting layer or saved views exist anywhere; and an adjustment is not on [ADR-010](../decisions/ADR-010.md)'s closed list of five ([M06:159](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md)), so dual control over it is an amendment and therefore `ADR-067`'s.**

- **A GREP REPORTED AS "NOTHING" RETURNED ONE LINE, AND THE LINE ARGUES THE OTHER WAY.** [FOLD-03](../plans/FOLD-03-vendor-parity-gap-fill.md) section 3.2 says a grep for `saved view`, `reporting layer` and `scheduled report` "returns **nothing**". `scheduled report` returns **[M06:377](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md)**, the weekly suppression digest, which M06 calls **"the single most useful recurring artifact this module produces"** and for which **no delivery mechanism is specified**. The conclusion survives (it is not a report builder) and **the hit is evidence FOR the digest item rather than against it**, so dropping it would have discarded the fold's own best supporting citation. Reported in the ADR's section 0.

- **A QUOTE DRIFTED ONE DOCUMENT AND WAS CAUGHT BY READING THE LINE.** FOLD-03 section 3.4 renders [M07:84](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md)'s approved signal model as ending *"settlement-rail destination"*. **The primary source reads "settlement-rail identity."** Nothing turns on it; it is recorded because the ADR quotes that line and a fold transcribing the fold plan would have carried the drift into a frozen document. **This is the fourth time in this corpus that re-reading a source has corrected a claim that no gate could check.**

- **"FOUR OF THE SIX ARE SHOULD" DOES NOT SURVIVE A COMPONENT-LEVEL COUNT, AND IT IS THE SENTENCE A ROADMAP READER QUOTES.** [FOLD-03](../plans/FOLD-03-vendor-parity-gap-fill.md) section 4 summarises the sizing that way. **Three of its six sizing rows are wholly SHOULD; three of the six referred items are.** The ADR therefore states **six MUST components and six SHOULD components** by name, because the split binds the [DELIVERY_PLAN](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md) rows `F5` writes and a rounded summary is not a specification.
