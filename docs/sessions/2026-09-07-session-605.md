### 1. The objective

Take the relocation [ADR-408](../decisions/ADR-408.md) section 6 priced and did not take: re-derive which `packages/*` addresses both deployables already reach, choose the home for the event producer on the merits, move it there and point `apps/api/src/events.ts` at it, so that the deployable holding the `SystemTx` can reach the sink. One objective, non-money.

### 2. Done

- **The container measured and reported.** No repository on arrival, eleventh row running by the dispatch's own count. `/home/user` held `.` and `..`, and a `find` for any `.git` under it returned nothing. This row cloned the tree itself: **17.824s real**, **NOT shallow** (`.git/shallow` absent), **4,348** commits, **185M** of `.git`.
- **The base reproduced at `08b9acf2`**, the head of `claude/wave-407s`, on an empty `git status --porcelain`: gates **33 of 33**, invariants **35 of 35**, suite **337 / 8,141 / 10 / 0**, typecheck, lint and `format:check` clean. `generate` reported *every span already matches its query* and left the tree unchanged.
- **Section 6's table re-derived from both manifests** rather than inherited, and it reproduces: the `@merit/*` intersection of `apps/api` and `apps/worker` is exactly `@merit/db`, `@merit/ledger` and `@merit/rules-engine`.
- **The home ruled `packages/ledger`, with both losers refused at a source rather than on taste.** `@merit/db`'s two refusals were read at `apps/api/test/db.test.ts` and `apps/worker/test/digests.test.ts`; `@merit/rules-engine`'s lint clearance was measured by copying the producer into that package and running `eslint` over it, and the probe was proved live before its silence was believed.

- **The probe tree reverted**, `apps/api/src/events.ts` confirmed byte-identical by `sha256sum` against its state before the first edit.

### 3. Refused

- **THE MODULE WAS NOT MOVED.** The move turns three suites red in `apps/worker/test`, three pointers red under `RI-15` and one register red under `RI-35`, and every one of those files is outside every fence this wave hands out. Shipping red is not a delivery and repairing them is not this row's to do.
- **THE ONE SHAPE THAT WOULD HAVE PASSED THE TWO CITATION INVARIANTS WAS REFUSED ON PURPOSE.** A module at the old path, long enough for the range checks and declaring `buildEvent` at the cited coordinate, turns `RI-15` and `RI-16` green while the pointer it satisfies names a declaration that has moved. It is a duplicate wearing an anchor's clothes and it is the exact defect `RI-15` exists to catch.
- **NO `VG-12` ADMISSION WAS ASKED FOR AND NO NEW PACKAGE PROPOSED.** The ruled home needs neither.
- **`apps/worker` WAS NOT WIRED**, which the row forbade and which is a separate slice regardless.
- **SECTION 4's LEG WAS NOT UPDATED.** The dispatch asked for it on the expectation that a `packages/*` module would hold a producer by the end of this row. None does, so the assertion is still true, and updating it to describe a change that was not made is worse than deleting it.
