### Landmines

1. **`wallet_balances_cents` IS READ IN TWO PLACES ON PURPOSE AND THE CHEAP REPAIR BREAKS BOTH.** [`liability.ts:534`](../../apps/admin/src/liability.ts) states the shared read as the design. Anyone who reaches for the no-migration fix by widening what that column means overstates `P-M6-07`'s float by the whole in-flight balance, and the two figures stop being one quantity.
2. **THE TERM IS A MAGNITUDE AND THE LEDGER NET IS ITS NEGATION.** [`posting.ts:235-236`](../../packages/ledger/src/posting.ts) makes a credit `-amountCents`, so a standing obligation of 25,000c is a ledger net of `-25000`. **A term written as the ledger net subtracts the obligation from the panel instead of adding it**, which is a sign error inside the one figure whose whole purpose is that it not fall wrongly. `wallet_balances_cents` is the precedent: a positive column for a liability whose net is negative.
3. **THE `NOT NULL` WINDOW CLOSES WHEN THE PRODUCER IS WRITTEN, NOT WHEN THE PANEL IS BUILT.** [`0049:97-98`](../../packages/db/migrations/0049_reserve_coverage_snapshots.sql) argues `ADD COLUMN NOT NULL` with no default from the table having no rows. The day a batch writes `liability_snapshots`, this column can only arrive with a default, and a defaulted zero reports an in-flight obligation as none.
4. **A HEADING IS A DEFINITION SITE.** `CI-06/identifier-series` counts `#### INV-M6-10 ...` exactly as it counts a table row. Any session that writes an `#### <ID> ...` heading in a corpus document has minted a definition site for that identifier, and in a series whose pending entry is waiting on coverage the effect is invisible until somebody predicts the opposite and measures it.
5. **WRITING AN `SD-nn` TOKEN ANYWHERE UNDER `docs/` IS A CITATION.** [`M06:80`](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) says so about its own unclaimed number, and it is why this entry names that delta by position. A session that spells the number out has silently taken on a `DELTA_MANIFEST` row.

### 1. The ruling, and the two grounds the dispatch did not have

**`P-M6-01` gains a third component rather than a neighbour.** The obligation is summed into Open Liability, shown separately like the other two, and `INV-M6-15` is the conservation rule that follows.

**THE GROUND THAT DECIDES IT IS BEHAVIOURAL AND NOT DEFINITIONAL**, which matters because the definitional argument genuinely favours a figure beside: [`M06:92`](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) calls `P-M6-01` *"what traders could claim if every gate vanished"*, and a trader whose withdrawal is approved can no longer claim that money.

| Posting | withdrawable | wallet | obligation | `P-M6-01` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `LT-06`, approval | unchanged | falls by `amount_cents` | rises by `amount_cents` | **UNCHANGED** |
| `LT-07`, settlement | unchanged | unchanged | falls by `amount_cents` | **FALLS by `amount_cents`** |
| `LT-09`, rail exhausted | unchanged | rises by `amount_cents` | falls by `amount_cents` | **UNCHANGED** |

**Without the third term, the panel falls at the approval, does not move at the settlement, and RISES on a rail failure.** The first is what [ADR-181](../decisions/ADR-181.md) section 7 recorded. **The second and third are not in that entry or in the dispatch, and they are the ones that close the question**: a number placed beside a total cannot fix a total that moves on the wrong events, and money that returns to Open Liability without a new obligation being created never left it.

### 3. Why the prediction was wrong, which is a finding about the register rather than about the gate

[`gates.mjs`](../../scripts/corpus/gates.mjs) holds `INV-M6` in `PENDING_SERIES` with the reason *"a table in M06 covering every member; some have no row"*, and the pending check fails a series once **every** member has exactly one definition site. `INV-M6-15` was the only member with no row, so filling it should have emptied that set and demanded a promotion into `DECLARED_SERIES`.

**It did not, because the definition-site rule counts a HEADING as well as a table row.** [`M06:218`](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md)'s `#### INV-M6-10 is not weakened, and the schema is where that is enforced` is a **second** definition site for `INV-M6-10`, which the census reads at two while every other member reads at one.

**So the register's stated reason and the live defect are two different defects**, and the one actually holding `INV-M6` pending is a duplicate rather than a gap. **Reported and not repaired.** `gates.mjs` is not this fence, and rewording an approved document's heading so that a census stops counting it is a change that should be ruled rather than slipped into a liability entry.
