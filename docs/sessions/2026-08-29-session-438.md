**THE READINGS DIFFER BY EVERY IDENTITY HOLDING MORE THAN ONE ACCOUNT**, and `AS-09` is ruled at the
gate at `M01:969` as "visibility, not a rule", so the identity reading would refuse nine of a copy
trader's ten accounts under a ceiling the corpus declined to impose. **The account reading answers
"no payout in flight" for an identity that has one.** One is wrong in the refusing direction and one
in the paying direction.

**AND NEITHER SENTENCE IS INTERNALLY SATISFIABLE AS WRITTEN.** `M01:207`'s status set is
`approved | transferring | frozen`; `ADR-028` retired `transferring` from `payout_requests` and
`ADR-040` added `held_pending_review`, so the live predicate is `approved, frozen,
held_pending_review`. `M01:531` names `transferring`, which `wallet_withdrawals` has, and `frozen`,
which it does not: `wallet_withdrawal_status` at
[`0001_extensions_and_enums.sql:95`](../../packages/db/migrations/0001_extensions_and_enums.sql)
carries no `frozen`.

**RULED UNRESOLVED RATHER THAN RESOLVED.** Four sources to two is an argument, not a ruling. `M01`
is frozen, both sentences are in it, and moving one is an amendment.
