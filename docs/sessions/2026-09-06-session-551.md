### 1. The objective

Row `356`: serve what can be served of `AdminReadSource`'s seven reads, refuse what cannot with the blocker measured at `file:line`, and rule on whether `start.ts` should install the port.

**One objective. It resolved into three rulings, one refutation of the dispatch's own premise, one finding nobody went looking for, and one control.**

---

### 2. What was ruled

**RULING ONE: ZERO servable today, FIVE on door day.** The question has two honest answers and collapsing them is the defect. Today: not one of the seven, because `AdminSourceBackend.operator` has no supplier and `ApiDb` declares five doors with no `operator(fn)` among them. Door day: `listEvents`, `listFlags`, `readAccount`, `readIdentityGraph`, `searchAccounts`. **The two that refuse anyway do so on blockers the door would not discharge**, and each is cited at a line in ADR-356 section 2.3.

**RULING TWO: `start.ts` does not install the port, and it CANNOT.** Not a preference. And the dispatch's harder question was answered rather than dodged: a partial install is a worse failure mode, measured through the real router.

**RULING THREE: `composeImplementedAdminReads` is not dead and is exactly what door day calls.** It is the compile-time half of a defence the run-time half does not subsume, and the header argues that in terms.

---

### 3. The finding nobody asked for

**FIVE IS NOT SIX, AND TWO PASSING CONTROLS BOTH IMPLY SIX.** `admin-read-constructibility.test.ts` counts producers; `admin-source-evidence.test.ts` counts the store. Both are right. **The number a deployment actually receives is the product of the two and lived in no case anywhere.** It is now `apps/api/test/admin-read-door-day.test.ts`, six cases, and its store and writer checks **redden on GOOD NEWS**: the day either lands, the case fails and names the file that supplies it.
