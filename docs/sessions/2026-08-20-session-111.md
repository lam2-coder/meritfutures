  - **`GS-059` FINDS `EC-049`'s DOMINATION CLAIM FAILING BY EXACTLY ONE TRADING
    DAY, AND THE TWO DOCUMENTS THAT DISAGREE ARE BOTH APPROVED.** `EC-049` says
    that on any plan where `cadence_gap_trading_days <= required_win_days` the
    cadence gate is dominated by `R-34` and never binds; Core EOD carries 5 and
    5. The argument holds only while `payoutAnchorDay` and `cadenceAnchorDay` are
    the same day, which `R-37`'s row asserts they are "because the internal leg
    is instant". **Every settlement in the fixture directory puts the effective
    day one trading day after the basis day**, which is what `R-10` describes
    ("the withdrawal lands at the open of `effectiveTradingDay`"), and `GS-068`
    already stands on a case where the two differ by two calendar days. When they
    differ, `R-34`'s window is one session wider than `R-37`'s and the cadence
    gate binds alone. `GS-059`'s end state is that day: **five gates pass, the
    cadence gate fails, and `engine_eligible` is false with one nameable cause.**
    Reported, not resolved. Moving either sentence is an ADR.

  - **A GRIND IS A TRAJECTORY AND A GOLDEN SIBLING PINS ONE END STATE, WHICH IS A
    BETTER REASON THAN THE ONE ON THE RECORD.** `GS-049`'s recorded blocker is
    that its "100-day flat grinds" probe needs 100 sessions against a calendar
    holding 83. That is true and it is the shallower half: **the end state after
    twelve flat days and after one hundred is the same state, field for field**,
    because a flat day moves no counter, no balance and no floor. A longer grind
    buys a golden file only a longer `events` list. So a longer calendar would
    not have unblocked the probe, and the vehicle the row itself names for it,
    "runs as a property suite as well as a fixture", is the one that can carry a
    trajectory. **This file therefore carries all three of the row's SHAPES and
    pins the two the format can pin, and its sibling records that as a reading
    that may be rejected** rather than as a fact. The precedent for recording a
    reading in the sibling is `GS-032`'s.

  - **WRITING THE HALF OF A ROW THE FORMAT CAN CARRY IS REFUSED HERE, AND THE
    OBJECTION IS NAMED AFTER `GS-049`.** `GS-079` could have been written on its
    `R-21` half alone, since the floor comparator is expressible; that file would
    be `GS-013` and `GS-014` again under a third id, and it would assert nothing
    about the operator agreement the row exists for. `GS-047` has no half at all.
    Neither was written. **Three of four beats four thin ones was the brief's
    instruction and two of four is what the tree allowed.**

  - **THE CHRISTMAS FIGURE IN THE REGISTRY ROW IS NOT REALIZED AT ANY ANCHOR IN
    THE COMMITTED CALENDAR.** `GS-059`'s row says "five trading days spanning the
    Christmas cluster is 9 calendar days". Against
    [`calendars/cme-2026.json`](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/calendars/cme-2026.json)

    above it**, and it is reachable, at an anchor of 2026-12-24 whose fifth
    counted session is 2027-01-04 across both transcribed holidays. The registry

  - **THE JUNE HALF OF `GS-059`'s COMPARISON IS OUTSIDE COVERAGE AND THE CONTRAST
