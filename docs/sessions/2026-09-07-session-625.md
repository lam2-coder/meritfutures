### 1. The objective

[ADR-426](../decisions/ADR-426.md) section 12 item 1: the other twenty-four port families are still rows and `recon` is the cheapest next one by its section 3 table. **Re-derive that claim rather than trust it**, perform the blast radius rather than predict it, and spend [ADR-303](../decisions/ADR-303.md) limit 4 on the family by showing **one hand-written mapping actually deleted**. Keep every runtime check. Price the trade ADR-426 section 9 names if this row makes it too. A refusal with the measurement would have been a full delivery.

### 2. Done

- **The container measured and reported.** No repository on arrival. `gh` absent. `git push` refused `403` by the proxy on the first attempt and succeeded after `add_repo` with `access: "push"`; the repository was **not** pre-checked, because the dispatch records that `git ls-remote` returns `404` for a private repository and would have misled.
- **The base reproduced at `d915dc14`** on a clean tree before the first edit, and **it is not green**: gates **33 of 33**, invariants **35 of 35**, suite **2 failed / 339 passed (341)**, both failures `RI-35` timeouts rather than assertions, proved by re-running those two files at `--testTimeout=60000` for **355 passed (355)**.
- **The cheapest-next claim re-derived at source.** Every `ports.ts` under `apps/worker/src/` read for limit 4's verbs; read tables counted against `schema.ts` by walking `pgTable` bodies; the walker hand-checked against two tables before any number was used. ADR-430 section 3 is the table and section 3.1 is what the sibling got wrong.
- **The blast radius PERFORMED**, the port moved alone before the mapping was touched, and the zero in the only consumer recorded as the argument for the deletion.
- **`ReconRow` and `asRow()` deleted**, the three readers made generic over the row so `tsc` checks every column NAME, the adapter's three casts priced, and the barrel's export removed with the leg count derived.
- **Three cases added as section 8 of `recon-sweep.test.ts`, each watched falsified**, two RED on the base tree and the third RED under a deliberate neutering of `requireCents`.
- **Two self-citations in `sweep.ts` repointed** after `RI-15` caught them.

### 3. Not done, and why

- **`packages/db/**` is untouched although it was granted.** It was not needed, and ADR-430 section 8 is why that matters for every remaining family.
- **`apps/api/**`, `packages/ledger/**` and `packages/tooling/**` are untouched.** Forbidden to this fence; concurrent rows hold two of the named files.
- **`packages/db/migrations/` is untouched and no migration number is reserved or taken.** Nothing here is DDL.
- **`insert` and `updateAt` were not narrowed.** A write returns what the database made of what was sent, which is a different claim from a read, and case 8.1 now asserts they are still `unknown` so the next row is told it is answering a different question.
- **The two RED cases on the base were not repaired**, and no timeout was raised to make them pass.
- **`digests.test.ts`'s low column count was not corrected.** It is a comment in a file this fence does not hold.
- **`lockScope` was not touched.** ADR-426 section 10's derivation stands and nothing here changes it.

### 4. What the next row should know

**`breaker` is next and costs more than this one did**: five tables, 95 columns, eleven of them `*_cents`, one consumer, no control freezing its port's source. Expect five adapter casts rather than three and a fixture bill larger than this row's 41 columns, because `purchases` alone carries 30. **Its fence does not need `packages/db/**`.** `sweeps` and `withdrawals` still need a ruling before either can move, and `sweeps` additionally wants the founder's read [ADR-421](../decisions/ADR-421.md) section 13 item 2 reserves for the six `FOR UPDATE` sites.
