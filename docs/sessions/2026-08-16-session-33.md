### The one thing the fold's own brief could not settle, and why it was raised rather than inherited

FOLD-01 section 6.1 gives M10 "a sixth integration, the SMS sender" in six words, and the six words are correct. **What they do not carry is that this integration is unlike the five it joins**, and the difference is not a matter of degree.

Every existing integration is post-commit and asynchronous, and `INV-M10-01` is the invariant that makes that true rather than customary. The SMS sender is synchronous, it runs before anything has committed, and phone verification is mandatory, so **its outage is a closed front door rather than a delayed message**. `AS-M10-06` is a whole adversarial scenario about a vendor becoming load bearing by drift over six months with no moment anybody could have said no; here the moment is available, it is now, and the answer is that the vendor is load bearing by design.

**The temptation was to settle it by inheritance**, because [ADR-039](../decisions/ADR-039.md)'s degradation ruling fits the shape so exactly. Registration is where nothing has moved and nothing is owed; a refusal protects only a bill or an uptime figure; the mechanism to reuse is built, alarmed and countable. Applying it would have taken one sentence.

> **It was not applied, and the reason is the asymmetry the ruling itself is built on.** Degrading on a **cost** trip is a decision about Merit's own SMS bill, which Merit controls. Degrading on a **vendor** outage hands the same lever to anyone who can take a third-party SMS provider offline, and an SMS provider is a substantially easier target than Merit. **The founder ruled on the first bargain and has not been shown the second**, and "the same reasoning obviously applies" is precisely the move that produced this corpus's three worst recorded errors.

Both answers are testable and the fifth chaos flow is written to whichever lands. **What is not testable is the absence of an answer**, which is the only reason it is a blocker-shaped item in a session with no blockers.
