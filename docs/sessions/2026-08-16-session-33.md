### The founder's restraint in `0032` is what made this session cheap

`0032` could have added this trigger. It found the gap, wrote the finding into its own header **and** into `OI-06` **and** into the design record, and stopped, because [ADR-042](../decisions/ADR-042.md) was silent and a money-path trigger is not a thing a session grants itself. **The cost of that restraint was one open item for one day.** The cost of the alternative would have been a guard the founder had never ruled on, sitting inside a file that is sacred once merged, discovered at the E2 read with nothing to compare it against.

**And the gap it left was bigger than the row it wrote.** `OI-06` named the `UPDATE` case and called the `DELETE` question "the same question"; it is not quite the same, it is the bypass, and `TRUNCATE` past a row trigger is a third door neither had named. A session that had added the trigger quietly would very likely have added only the first of the three.

Its `covers` line is honest and says "is linked from"; the title overstates it. So a merged ADR fell out of the registry it belongs to and twelve gates stayed green.
