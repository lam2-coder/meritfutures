### Three findings in STATE_MACHINES that the fold did not go looking for

**All three are in the document the constitution makes authoritative for lifecycles, and all three were found by reading it end to end rather than by a gate.**

**1. Section 3 draws `payout_transfers`, not `wallet_withdrawals`.** Its five states are exactly `payout_transfers.status`'s CHECK in [`0010`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql). `wallet_withdrawal_status` in [`0001`](../../packages/db/migrations/0001_extensions_and_enums.sql) carries **seven** values and **has no drawing anywhere in the document**. The ADR-040 correction sent readers to section 3 "where `wallet_withdrawals` owns them", which was wrong in the same commit that fixed a different wrong statement about the same leg. Corrected in place. **The missing machine is recorded rather than drawn**: a seven-state machine is a fold of its own and is not this one's.

**2. That same correction named two guards that do not exist.** `G-SETTLEMENT-CONFIRMED` and `G-TRANSFER-EXHAUSTED` appear nowhere in section 10; the guards that exist are `G-WEBHOOK-SETTLED` and `G-RETRY-BUDGET-EXHAUSTED`. Corrected. **Section 9's own opening line says guards are defined once in section 10 so the same condition is never written twice**, and a prose paragraph naming guards that were never defined is that rule failing quietly.

**3. `G-ENFORCEMENT-CLOSE` is named by the identity machine and defined nowhere, and this fold did not define it.** Section 10 has never carried a row for it. The corpus does not agree on what it closes: [ADR-041](../decisions/ADR-041.md) says closure for cause is "terminal and **per account**", while the drawing routes an **identity** to `closed`. Those are two different acts on two different objects.

> **Settling it by writing a definition here would be one ruling's words used as evidence about another act**, which is exactly the defect [M07](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md) `OQ-M7-05` exists to refuse, in the same document, raised eight days earlier, about the phrase "hard link". **Recorded as a gap for an ADR rather than an edit.**

Two guards **were** defined, and they are this fold's: `G-ENFORCEMENT-RESTRICT` and `G-RESTRICTION-LIFTED` had the same defect and [ADR-041](../decisions/ADR-041.md) states their content in full, so writing them is transcription rather than invention. The discriminator between the two decisions is whether a primary source says what the guard means.

### The two places a control was cited as binding and bound nothing

**This is the fold's actual content, and both were found by checking a citation rather than by trusting it.**

**`G-ELIGIBLE` named `payouts_frozen` on the account and on the identity and never named `identities.status`.** So the one state in the system that halts a human across every account they hold **did not reach the payout gate at all**. [ADR-041](../decisions/ADR-041.md) states this as the whole finding, and it is why Ruling B reads as asking for a new state when the state had existed since `0001`: the existing one did almost nothing.

**`INV-M20-06` named `payouts_frozen` alone.** Meanwhile [M20](../plans/M20-wallet.md) AS-M20-02's counter-argument, the paragraph explaining why laundering a frozen balance through the product door fails, cites "expired KYC, `recon_blocked`, and an active restriction all block spend as well as withdrawal" as though something enforced it. **Nothing did.**

> **A control that exists in the paragraph explaining why the attack fails, and nowhere that binds, is the attack succeeding on schedule.**

Both are now enumerated in the invariant itself and asserted in the machine, and the same shape has a third instance already recorded in this fold: `wallet_withdrawals` carried a freeze clock, a freeze flag and a freeze-expiry index from `0011` and **nothing refused settlement on any of them**. Three instances of one defect in one fold is a pattern rather than a coincidence: **columns and prose that record a control are not the control**, and the interval between writing them and enforcing them is invisible from both ends.
