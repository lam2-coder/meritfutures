### 4. Three claims inside M10, and one inside INFRA, that the tree refutes

**These are findings for a session that holds those files, not edits made here.** M10 is `approved` and changing it takes an ADR.

**a. "The nine `EVENTS` section 11 triggers", twice, and there are twelve.**
[M10](../plans/M10-integrations.md)`:31` (`IN-M10-03`) and `:165` (section 5) both say nine. [EVENTS](../architecture/EVENTS.md)`:306` is `## 11. Event-driven lifecycle messaging (M10 triggers)` and

```
awk '/^## 11/,/^## 12/' docs/architecture/EVENTS.md | grep -cE '^\| `'
```

returns **12**. The three M10 does not count are `payout.held`, `payout.hold_released` and `identity.restriction_lifted`, and **all three are marked "always send"** in that table, one of them with the note that *"a restore nobody was told about is, from the trader's side, still a restriction"*. This is not a cosmetic count. `M10-C-nn` is nine negative allowlist tests wide if the plan's number is read, and section 8.3 says the tests are generated from the contract rows, so **the generated set would be correct and the plan's prose would be the only thing wrong** until somebody compared them. It is the hand-maintained-count defect [ADR-034](../decisions/ADR-034.md) rules against, sitting in a plan that itself replaced a hand-maintained "4" with a rule in section 8.1.

**b. `OQ-M10-06` declines an `OI-nn` for a reason that is no longer true.**
[M10](../plans/M10-integrations.md)`:346` reads *"the `OI` series is the one registry with no allocation table, which is the [ADR-034](../decisions/ADR-034.md) race the three allocation tables exist to end"*. [ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md)`:203` onward is that table, moved there by **session 141** under [ADR-074](../decisions/ADR-074.md) section 7, and it runs to `OI-30`. The stated blocker is discharged; the two `OI-06` rows are still two, and the ruling that they are cited with their subject attached rather than renumbered is still the ruling, so **nothing about the collision prevents `OI-31` being taken**. `OQ-M10-06` is `CLOSED` by [ADR-046](../decisions/ADR-046.md), so what is owed is a manifest row for a closed item rather than a number for an open one, which is cheaper than the plan's paragraph implies.

**c. "Twenty-nine migrations", and there are forty-six.**
[M10](../plans/M10-integrations.md)`:312` says *"there is no plaintext telephone number in any of the twenty-nine migrations"*. `ls packages/db/migrations/*.sql | wc -l` is **46**. **The claim's SUBSTANCE survives the wider count and that was checked rather than assumed**: re-measured across all 46,

finds `phone_preview text` ([`0029`](../../packages/db/migrations/0029_phone_identity_and_auth.sql)`:111`, which is *"enough to recognise, not enough to reconstruct"*), two `*_key_id text` columns from `0034`, and prose. **No plaintext number in any of the forty-six.** The number in the sentence is stale; the sentence is true.

**d. [INFRA](../architecture/INFRA.md)`:121` gives Metabase a role no migration creates.**

So `INV-M10-06`'s enforcement, *"a replica role with `SELECT` only"*, names a role that does not exist, and the role that does exist is in the primary with an enumerated nine-table grant that was never reconciled against section 3.3's exclusion list. **This is `OI-06`'s shape a third time: a control citing an input that does not exist.**
