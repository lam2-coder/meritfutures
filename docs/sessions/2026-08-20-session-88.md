### The finding, which is blunter than the fold expected

**The admin surface contains zero endpoints that perform a trader action on a trader's behalf.** Every admin mutation in the corpus is enforcement, correction, configuration, conferral or annotation. The two rows counted as having an equivalent, **account closure** ([API_CONTRACT:560](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md)) and **plan config** ([API_CONTRACT:602](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md)), are actions that **were never trader routes**. So the parity gap is not a set of holes in a surface that mostly has parity. **There is no parity anywhere, and closure is the single existence proof that the admin-attributed pattern works.**

### Three things the enumeration surfaced that the fold plan did not have

| | |
|---|---|
| **Contract signature is not a route** | There is no signature endpoint. Acceptance is `accept_tos_version_ids`, a **required field** on checkout ([API_CONTRACT:274](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md)) and reset ([:293](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md)), recorded to append-only `tos_acceptances`. **So `OQ-F4-01` is not one row, it gates the entire commerce group**: purchase, wallet spend, reset and offer redemption cannot be closed admin-side while it is open |
| **Closing a C-27 gap substitutes the factor rather than replicating it** | Nine trader routes in the matrix are C-27 sensitive and require `passkey or dual_channel` ([SECURITY:45](../architecture/SECURITY.md)). The admin surface authenticates under `admin_sso` ([API_CONTRACT:671](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md)). The trader route asserts *the human proved possession on a second channel*; an admin route asserts *an operator with a hardware key says the human asked*. **Different claims, and the second is the one an evidence pack carries into a dispute.** Better than the impersonated version, where the recorded claim is simply false, and `ADR-069` should still say which claim each action makes |
| **A fourth class was needed, and it is what keeps the audit honest** | `an owner admin can perform any trader action` stated without exception would authorize an admin to **register a passkey on a trader's identity**, **mark a trader's notification read** (which manufactures proof-of-notice evidence, AS-M16-05), and **write in a trader's journal**. Ten rows are classed **NOT-PARITY**: an admin equivalent would be a different and worse act, usually credential forgery or manufactured evidence. Recorded with reasons and **put to the founder rather than ruled here** |

### Two gaps outside the fold's expectations that a support organisation will hit first

**Account recovery has no admin path at all.** No route revokes a compromised trader's sessions ([API_CONTRACT:215](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) is trader-only), opens or cancels the phone-change ceremony ([:196](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md)), or touches a contact channel ([M16:196](../plans/M16-notification-center.md)). **A trader who cannot log in cannot be helped back in**, and the phone-change ceremony requires an elevated session, which is precisely what someone who lost their number does not have.

**Payout origination is missing while resolution exists.** `POST /admin/payouts/:id/release` and `/enforce` ([M05:306](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)) resolve a request the trader already made; `release` *"posts the stored decision unchanged and re-evaluates nothing"*. Nothing creates one. **A locked-out trader with an eligible account cannot be paid, and the cadence clock does not pause for support latency.** External withdrawal ([M05:307](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)) is the same and worse, and it is the gap most likely to generate pressure to widen impersonation.

### Two findings that are gaps in the corpus rather than in the parity matrix

1. **`POST /offers/redeem` is cited by its consumer and absent from its owner.** [M14:217](../plans/M14-loyalty-retention.md) names [M17](../plans/M17-offers-engine.md) as owning redemption; M17's surface table, lines 146 to 151, does not carry the route.
2. **Affiliate creative approval has no route on either surface.** [M08:160](../plans/M08-affiliate-system.md) says creatives are submitted *"for approval"*, and nothing approves them.

### The correction carried, and it is now recorded in two places

**`D5` is the matrix, not a test identifier.** [API_CONTRACT:658](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) heads section 12 *"Negative-authz test matrix (D5, required in CI)"*, and [M06:329](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) reads *"RBAC and negative authz across all three roles | `M6-N-nn` | one per mutating route per role, enumerated from the router"*. Negative-authz tests are **`M6-N-nn` entries in the D5 matrix**. Any document saying *"a D5 test"* is wrong.

**A cost attached to it, for `ADR-069`.** M06:329 requires *one per mutating route per role*. Closing 18 gaps therefore adds **18 routes across three roles**, and each needs placing on the `ops` boundary that [API_CONTRACT:516](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) draws at *"read plus account actions, no config or role changes"*. Most of the account-recovery group reads as an account action; most of the money-out group does not. The audit does not propose where the line falls, and it records that the placement decision has a test count attached to it.

### One landmine

**The proposed exception in `OQ-P-01` writes to an append-only table.** `tos_acceptances` has `UPDATE` and `DELETE` revoked from the application role and from `PUBLIC` ([DATA_MODEL:51](../architecture/data-model/README.md)), and INV-M3-09 calls it *"the first artifact any enforcement dispute asks for"* ([M03:59](../plans/M03-billing-checkout.md)). **An admin-recorded acceptance is permanent and unmarkable after the fact.** If `ADR-069` permits it, the marker distinguishing an admin-recorded acceptance from a trader-recorded one has to land in the **same** migration that permits it, or the table starts making a claim it cannot support and no later commit can repair the rows already written.
