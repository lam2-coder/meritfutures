**Objective.** Rule what a citation proves, strengthen `RI-15` to match, and repair
what the strengthened check finds.

**Outcome.** Ruled, strengthened, eight repaired and four reported. **The most
valuable measurement is a negative one**: the window `RI-15` was built and tuned
around moves no site at any setting and was not what let any of the surviving
five through.

### Findings

1. **THE WINDOW WAS NEVER THE DEFECT, AND NARROWING IT MOVES NOTHING.** Over the
   225 resolving citations on `origin/main` at `37d70b3`, the distance
   histogram for the 52 that bind a name is **44 at zero, then nothing until
   three**. `CITATION_WINDOW` at 2 and at 0 are the same rule on this corpus.
   The parameter the check's own header argues at length about is inert, and
   every future session tempted to fix a citation defect by moving it now has
   the measurement saying it will not help.
2. **FOUR OF THE FIVE SURVIVORS WERE GREEN BECAUSE THE READER NEVER ASKED.** Two
   comma lists were never parsed -- **including their first numbers, which
   would have resolved on their own** -- and two bind no name, one because the
   token in front carries spaces and one because the glue is the word *and*.
   The reservation's stated mechanism (a common token satisfying the window) is
   real, is true of `scope.ts`, and was the mechanism for the citation session
   399 had **already repaired**.
3. **THIS SESSION BROKE A CITATION WHILE REPAIRING CITATIONS.** Repointing
   `payouts.ts:67` added one line, which moved `PayoutSubject` from
   `routes/payouts.ts:328` to `:329`, which falsified a pointer in
   `wiring.test.ts` written before the edit. The check written an hour earlier
   caught it. The derive-citations-last rule is not advice.
4. **A REGISTER ENTRY THAT RAISES NOTHING IS A FINDING, AND ONE WAS WITHDRAWN
   FOR IT.** `provisioning/payload.ts:217` cites `ports.ts:98`, blank in the
   `ports.ts` the sentence means; it was registered, the register's shrinks-only
   guard fired on the next run, and the entry was removed rather than the rule
   widened. The vacancy rule fires only when EVERY file a suffix path could name
   is vacant there, and this tree holds eleven `ports.ts`.

### Refused

- **The window was not narrowed**, because it moves nothing and tightening it
  would have read as a fix.
- **Requiring the cited line to DECLARE the name**, at 26 of 52 red with 18
  honest. A citation of `class: 'firm'` proves a class and declares nothing.
- **Requiring the anchor of every citation**, at 116 of 225 red.
- **Refusing a citation into a comment**, at 57 of 225 red. This corpus argues
  by quoting a header, and that is a citation into something; a citation into
  `},` is a citation into nothing.
- **`RI-20` was derived as the next free invariant number and returned unspent.**
- **`apps/worker/**` was not touched**, so three measured-wrong citations there
  are reported with their true lines rather than repaired.
- **`ALLOCATION` rows 164 and 168 were not repaired**, on the precedent
  `RI-16`'s register already sets three times for that file.

### Landmines

- **A CITATION CAN LAND ON `},` AND READ AS VERIFIED.** Three of the five did.
  It is what a pointer becomes when the declaration it named moved down, and
  nothing about it looks wrong to a reader who does not open the file.
- **A COMMA LIST WAS INVISIBLE, INCLUDING ITS FIRST NUMBER.** The tail was
  anchored at the end of the token, so one comma turned a whole citation into
  prose. There are 51 more of these under `docs/`.
- **THE NEAREST BACKTICKED TOKEN IS THE PREDICATE AND THE SUBJECT IS ONE FURTHER
  AWAY.** In a registry file that makes the check's proxy nearly free, and the
  one token a runner could have checked is the one it drops.
- **RE-TOKENIZING A SPAN PAIRS BACKTICKS FROM WHEREVER THE SPAN STARTS.** The
  anchor's first draft did that and lost subjects a sentence plainly writes,
  which is `citedIdentifier`'s own recorded defect arriving at the other end of
  the same reader.
