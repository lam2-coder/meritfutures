### 3. Item 1, and the part that was not the sweep

**The glob.** `format` and `format:check` read `"*.{js,mjs,ts,json}"`, `".prettierrc"` and `"{apps,packages,e2e}/**/*.{ts,tsx,mts,js,mjs,json}"`. The directory term becomes `{apps,packages,e2e,scripts}`.

**What it newly covers, derived on the widening commit: 29 file(s) under `scripts/` match the extension list and 10 were unformatted.** The sweep is **360 insertion(s) and 179 deletion(s)**, all of it quote style and line wrapping, and `gates.mjs` carries 242 of the changed lines. `.sql` and `.sh` stay out because prettier does not parse them and `.md` because no term of this pair has ever carried it.

**THE WHOLE SWEEP WAS TAKEN IN THE WIDENING COMMIT**, which is session 346's trade for `.tsx` taken again on its own facts: a widened gate that lands red is a gate nobody can use, and a widened gate landing green over a tree nobody swept was never widened. Ten files is not a large sweep and none of it is behaviour.

**AND THEN THE INTERESTING PART.** 33 of 33 gates and 16 of 16 invariants passed across the sweep, RI-15 and RI-16 included, which is what was checked and it was not enough.

| Table in `gates.mjs` | Rows `falsify.mjs` could read, before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `DECLARED_SERIES` | 117 | 117 |
| `PENDING_SERIES` | **36** | **13** |
| `WITHHELD_MEMBERS` | **1** | **0** |

`seriesTable` matched `^\s*\['([A-Za-z0-9-]+)',\s*(?:'|")` and then sliced to the end of that line to take the detail, so a row prettier had split into a bare `[`, the name, and the string on three lines was invisible to it. **`node scripts/corpus/falsify.mjs` went from clean to 3 problems**: two `SEED IS STALE` on the two withheld cases, and one `CONTROL DID NOT FIRE` on `withheld-is-excluded`, whose control removes the `WITHHELD_MEMBERS` entry through a second anchor of the same shape.

**THE GATE'S OWN READER NEVER STOPPED WORKING, AND THAT IS WHY NOTHING WENT RED.** `falsify.mjs` says one comment above the break why its reader is deliberately looser: *"A harness that imported the gate's own parser would agree with it by construction and could never catch it reading the wrong thing."* **So the repair is to this reader and not to the coupling.** Both anchors become `\[\s*'NAME'`, which spans the wrap and matches the unwrapped shape unchanged, and the counts come back 117, 36 and 1.

### 4. Item 3, executed rather than inherited, and why it is reported

[`0009:103-104`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql) reads *"A reversal may not reverse itself, and may not chain onto another reversal: a reversal of a reversal is an adjustment and should be posted as one."* Beneath it, `ledger_transactions_no_self_reversal` is `CHECK (reversal_of IS NULL OR reversal_of <> id)`.

**ALL 57 MIGRATIONS WERE APPLIED IN ORDER TO A REAL PostgreSQL 16 AND THE CHAIN WAS WRITTEN.** Three `ledger_transactions` rows, B naming A and C naming B, every constraint and trigger in the set installed: **all three INSERTs land.**

**THE GROUND IS STRUCTURAL AND NOT AN ACCIDENT OF THIS DDL.** A row-level `CHECK` cannot query another row, so `reversal_of <> id` is the whole of what that constraint can express. Closing the comment's second clause needs a trigger or a foreign-key shape, which needs a migration number.

**`account_adjustments` DOES NOT CLOSE IT.** `assert_adjustment_reversal_is_sound` check 1 at [`0038:323`](../../packages/db/migrations/0038_account_adjustments.sql) refuses a chain and cites `0009` as its authority, but it is a trigger on `account_adjustments` and the three rows above were written straight into `ledger_transactions` with no adjustment row anywhere. [`0057:156-157`](../../packages/db/migrations/0057_terminal_withdrawal_obligation.sql) says it *"already enforces it where reversals are actually written today"*, and **the word doing the work is "today"**: `reversalPosting` in [`reversal.ts`](../../packages/ledger/src/reversal.ts) writes `reversal_of` outside `account_adjustments`, it refuses only key-equality, and it has no caller outside `packages/ledger` yet. The slice that posts `LT-09` is the slice that ends that sentence.

**`0057`'s UNIQUE index does not reach it either.** It refuses a second reversal of ONE transaction; a chain gives each row a different `reversal_of`.

**So this is reported and left, and no number is taken for it.** The comment is right and the constraint is narrower than the comment, in a merged migration that is never edited.
