### 1. The objective

Take [ADR-410](../decisions/ADR-410.md) section 9's three assignments to `packages/tooling`, derived at source rather than inherited from the dispatch: `RI-35`'s register `site` for `event-sink-caller`, which is still the pre-move path; the `sweptBy` prose on that same entry, which counts the lines the two exported names reach; and the registration ADR-410 section 7 leaves owed in the same file. And decide, rather than assume, whether section 7 is right to call its seventh binding a finding about the check rather than about the move.

### 2. Done

- **The container measured and reported.** No repository on arrival, twelfth consecutive row. `/home/user` held `.` and `..` and nothing else. Cloned by this row: **15.942s real**, **NOT shallow**, `.git` **188M** and the working tree **59M**. `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile` took **11.782s real** and left `git status --porcelain` empty.
- **The base reproduced at `0f89c6ac`** on a clean tree: `generate` reported *every span already matches its query*, gates **33 of 33**, invariants **35 of 35**, suite **337 / 8,152 / 10 / 0**, typecheck 0, lint 0, `format:check` clean.
- **`RI-35`'s first leg read at source before anything moved.** It counts the lines of the registered `site` containing the `claim` string and demands exactly one; it stores no coordinate. Both halves of what re-anchoring costs follow from that, and only the first is machine-checkable.
- **The `sweptBy` figure re-derived with a named instrument and anchored to a commit**, and the old figure re-derived at the one commit that wrote it, where it does not reproduce under any reading.
- **ADR-410 section 7's registration landed in `sweptBy`**, with the false positive fired, its counterfactual held open beside it, and the probe's power over a real install asserted beside both, so the registration cannot be read as a case for widening.
- **Eight cases added to `packages/tooling/test/absence-claims.test.ts`.** Two of them were watched RED under seeds and restored.
- **The `names` field's stale address repaired in place, beside its correction**, under `RI-14`'s habit rather than by deletion.

### 3. Refused

- **THE `site` MOVE.** It is honest, it is prepared, it was measured green at `RI-35`, and shipping it means shipping a red suite in `apps/api/test/event-placement.test.ts`, which this row may not write and no row this wave holds. [ADR-389](../decisions/ADR-389.md) ruled this trade one wave over and reverted rather than take it. The refusal is recorded in the register itself as well as in the ADR, because a constraint stated only in a document is a constraint the next reader of the code will not find.
- **EVERY REPAIR OF THE PROBE.** Excluding the barrel is a second exclusion; reading the specifier list is a parser. Both make a check easier to satisfy, and this wave's rule about that is sharper than usual because the row is working on the checking machinery.
- **`worker-queue-door-caller`'s STALE FIGURE**, which is in this row's fence and is still not this row's. It is a different artifact under a different ADR, its figure depends on files row 414 is moving this wave, and a derived figure deserves the seed and the anchor this row gave the other one rather than a second undispatched integer in the same diff.
- **ASSERTING THE INTEGER IN A TEST**, with the reason written where the cases are.
