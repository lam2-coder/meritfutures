### 2. What was actually broken

`unavailable`'s doc sentence has always read *"nothing is in flight, nothing is
absent from a populated response, and nothing failed"*. **The third clause was
false the moment the segment could fetch**, and the arm it describes renders a
list of API paths under a sentence blaming the server. That is right for a
route this deployment does not serve and wrong for every other failure, and the
worst of them is a 401: **not a problem on our side, and the trader can fix it
by signing in.**
