#### 1. The premise, RE-DERIVED AT SOURCE rather than taken from the dispatch

of it is reproduced from the primary source before anything is ruled, and then again

#### 2. The experiment, because watching the database refuse it is worth more than reading

`initdb` as `postgres`, `0001` through `0051` forward-only under `ON_ERROR_STOP`, then a
fixture and five writes: the correction exactly as the contract describes it; the same
correction through `0038`'s door; the correcting DEBIT the contract admits; the
`admin_actions` row `admin-wallet.ts` already writes; and a `goodwill` credit, which is
the case that decides whether `corrects_entry_id` can be REQUIRED.

**If the database does NOT refuse the contract's instruction, that is the finding and the
dispatch's premise is wrong.**

#### 3. The question ADR-158 did not ask

Is the correction's provenance recorded ANYWHERE ELSE: `admin_actions.before`,
`evidence_refs`, the adjustment's own row? Session 288 established that on a payout hold
`admin_actions.before` is the entire durable record once a CHECK blanks the columns, and
[ADR-158](../decisions/ADR-158.md) clause 7 is that ruling. **If the same instrument
answers here, the answer is "the record exists and is not where the contract says",
which is a third and better outcome than either direction the dispatch named.**
