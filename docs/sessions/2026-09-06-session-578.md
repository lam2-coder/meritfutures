### 4. Blockers and landmines

- **A remedy's price is what it turns QUIET as well as what it turns RED.** [ADR-380](../decisions/ADR-380.md) priced the four-line repair by its one red and read its one pass as free, and the pass is the guard being disarmed. **When a repair newly binds N citations, open the ones that PASS as carefully as the one that reddens.**
- **`sameName` admits containment from four characters up.** `flag_type` reaches `D01_FLAG_TYPE`; `PayoutTx.subject` reaches `PayoutTx`. That is fine in a conjunct and is not fine in a disjunct, and it is the whole of why section 3 refuses.
- **`flattenReasons`'s joiner is shared.** The reason a citation can bind across a wrapped line is the reason a negation can. Any repair phrased in LINES will break one of the two.
- **A `covers` cardinal corrected by hand drifts within one row.** Three consecutive rows have now spent part of themselves on these numbers.
- **A pointer into a docblock is invisible at two lines and red at three.** The window is a tuning constant standing where a reading belongs, and section 6 of the ADR has the pair that shows it.
