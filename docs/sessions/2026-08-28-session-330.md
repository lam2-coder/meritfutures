### The founder question, in one paragraph

**The class and the scope are derived; the HOME is judged.** That the in-flight obligation is a
firm-scoped `liability` follows from two slots [ADR-180](../decisions/ADR-180.md) already ruled plus
one sign convention. **What does not follow is that it deserves a NEW class.** The coherent
alternative is that `psp_clearing` means *funds held at a payment provider for onward payment*
rather than *a receivable from a processor*, in which case shape (iii) is right, costs no migration,
and the eighth code would be a class Merit carries forever for a role one of the seven already had.
This entry reads [`checkout.ts:1682`](../../apps/api/src/routes/checkout.ts) as the only statement of
that code's nature in the tree and refuses to overrule it from the posting that needs an answer.
**The second question is whether the fence should have been widened**: taking `0054` means amending
GLOSSARY and five other files outside this session's ownership in the same diff, and a founder who
holds that is the right call is holding a defensible position. The cost of over-caution is one more
session doing a transcription this one has already measured and made mechanical; the cost of the
other error is a merged migration widening a vocabulary while four documents still say it is closed.
