## 2026-08-17 - Session 49: golden fixtures batch 5, thirty of 284, and four held-back reasons checked against their sources (non-money)

- **Done:** **One fixture, [GS-064](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/GS-064-breach-and-payout-eligibility-on-the-same-day.yaml), and four corrections to the held-back table.** [Session 48](2026-08-17-session-48.md) established mechanically that every M1-owned scenario was either a fixture or a named held-back row, and it was right, so this batch had nothing left to transcribe. It **checked the reasons instead**: one was wrong and three were incomplete. GS-064's row said "not inside five sessions", which is arithmetic on `win_days.required_count = 5`; that is [Appendix A.1](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)'s figure and [Appendix A.2](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) carries **3**, so the fixture fits in four sessions on [`MERIT-RAPID-50K.json`](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/plans/MERIT-RAPID-50K.json), which has been in `fixtures/plans` since batch 2. `pnpm run verify` green: **15 of 15 gates**, 30 fixtures loading with 0 load failures. **`packages/rules-engine/src/` and `test/` are untouched**, per the session fence.
- **Next:** **The fixture-wiring session is unchanged as the bottleneck and this batch adds one item to its list.** It owns the `bigint` conversion in `diffEndState`, the `L-13` citation rule, then [ADR-048](../decisions/ADR-048.md)'s polarity derivation. The new item is **`sessions[].kind`**, which is carried and ignored with no `L-nn` rule refusing it. **Nothing else in the M1 partition is writable**: 30 written, 43 held back, and every one of the 43 now names a blocker that was re-derived this session rather than inherited.
- **Blockers:** **`INV-07` versus the funded reset is a founder ruling and it is unchanged**, the successor to the one ADR-050 closed. The **floor-lock jump disagreement** (M01 section 3.4 against 3.6) is unchanged. **Neither holds a field on GS-064**, which is the first fixture since GS-055 that can say that: it never locks and never passes an eval.
- **Landmines:**

  - **THE WRONG HELD-BACK REASON IS THIS CORPUS'S OWN RECURRING SHAPE ARRIVING A FOURTH TIME.** "Eligibility is six trading days after the win-day gate is satisfied" is a **correct observation about Core EOD** carried one unchecked step to the directory. [Session 47](2026-08-16-session-47.md) named the pattern, session 48's engine log found it in the DO ordering table (`R-02`, `R-06` and `R-10` filed as blocked on a calendar transcription that three of them never needed), session 47's fixture log found it in the R-30 denominator row, and this is the fourth. **The plan record that dissolved it was written by the session that recorded the reason's neighbours**: batch 2 transcribed Merit Rapid precisely because Core EOD could not express the eval-consistency block, and nobody re-read the rest of the table against the record it had just added. **A held-back row records a reason, and a reason nobody re-derives becomes a fact** is the lesson batch 3 already wrote into this repository. It is cheaper to re-derive the table than to trust it.

  - **THE CALENDAR ROW'S REASON WAS TRUE AND WAS NOT THE BLOCKER, WHICH IS WORSE THAN BEING WRONG.** GS-001, GS-003, GS-004 and GS-030 to GS-032 were filed under "`cme-2026.json` declares five full sessions and no fill stream, and inventing either would be transcription from recollection". Every clause of that is true. **`EngineInput` is `{ planConfigVersion, accountState, dayMarks }` and carries no calendar at all**, so `is_half_day` and `halted` reach nothing whatever the calendar declares, and `DailyMark` carries `fillCount` and no instant. **A founder who transcribed the CME publication to unblock those six would have unblocked none of them.** A true reason standing in front of a binding one is a reason that will be acted on and will not help, and it survives review precisely because it is true.

  - **`sessions[].kind` IS THE ONE PLACE THIS FORMAT CAN SILENTLY DROP A STATED CONDITION.** `loadCalendar` reads `sessions[].trading_day`, builds a `Set` of strings for `L-08`, and discards the rest; every session in `cme-2026.json` states `"kind": "full"` and nothing reads it. The equivalent on a `days:` row is `L-10`'s refusal and on an `account:` block is `L-09`'s, and both exist because "the fixture states a condition, the engine never sees it, and the scenario passes while pinning something else" is the worst outcome available on a money path. **It is not on `AWAITING_M01_INPUT` either**, because that list is about fixture fields, so `L-14` cannot notice it rotting. Recorded rather than fixed: every `L-nn` rule is the fixture-wiring session's.

  - **A LONGER SYNTHETIC CALENDAR WAS WORKED OUT IN FULL AND THEN NOT ADDED, AND THE ORDER MATTERS.** It would have been legitimate on [`calendar.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/calendar.ts)'s own `GAPPED_SLICE` precedent, where session 47 ruled that what TR-01 forbids is writing down which days the exchange trades and that a slice supplied to make two answers disagree is not that. It was dropped **because it unblocks nothing**: GS-064 fits in four sessions once the plan is read correctly, GS-070's blocker is not the calendar, and every other row is blocked on a settlement, a clamp, a nested gate, a second account, or an input `EngineInput` does not have. **The failure mode avoided is a convention introduced for a fixture that did not need it**, which the next session inherits and cannot audit. Finding the cheaper repair is what made the expensive one unnecessary, and it was found by reading the plan record rather than by reasoning about the calendar.

  - **`engine_eligible` IS PINNED FOR THE FIRST TIME AND ONLY IN THE DIRECTION M01 STATES WITHOUT AMBIGUITY.** GS-055's sibling declined to pin `true` because M01 lists `R-38` as a funded gate in group F while declaring its input on `ExternalGates`, and `R-40`'s context list does not name it, so **the conjunction's membership is unruled**. That does not reach `false`: `R-25`'s row says "no eligibility" in its own words and section 3.6's breach block writes `engineEligible: false` outright. **A contested conjunction still has one uncontested value.** The same asymmetry is why GS-080's second half ("asserts that `engineEligible` is unaffected") has a **second** blocker beyond the nested-object one, which its row now records.

  - **`engine_eligible: false` ALONE WOULD HAVE BEEN WORTH NOTHING, AND THE SIX CARRIED FIELDS ARE THE FIXTURE.** An engine that never computed eligibility at all also reports `false`. What separates them is that `win_days_count`, `traded_days_count`, `withdrawable_cents`, `high_water_balance_cents` and both consistency accumulators are pinned at **day three's** values while `balance_cents` is pinned at **day four's**, because the breach block writes the trading day, the phase, the breach fields, the floor at open, the closing balance and `engineEligible`, and nothing else. Day four is deliberately a **traded win day closing at a new high**, so an engine that ran DO-6 reports 4 and 4, one that ran DO-9 reports a withdrawable of 40,000, and one that ran DO-7 reports a high-water balance of 5,140,000 and a floor of 4,890,000. **Four eligible-looking wrong engines, each failing on a different field.**

  - **GS-070's REAL BLOCKER IS THAT ITS ASSERTION CANNOT BE ISOLATED, NOT THAT THE FORMAT LACKS A SHAPE.** The row said the sibling has no shape for `assertions`, which is true and is downstream of the harder fact: **`funded_start_not_size` cannot be reached independently of `opening_mismatch`.** DO-3 tests `opening == prior.balance + adjustment` first, `L-11` fixes `adjustment_cents` at 0, and the prior balance after `R-31`'s reset **is** `size_cents`, so any opening that trips AS-14's check trips `INV-18` in the same pass. A fixture could state that the day was refused; it could not state which of the two refused it, and AS-14 is a claim about the second one.

  - **284 IS THE WRONG DENOMINATOR FOR THE QUESTION A READER OF THAT DIRECTORY IS ASKING, AND IT WAS THE ONLY ONE THERE.** The registry defines 284 and this directory can only ever hold **M1's partition of it, which is 73**; the other 211 are other modules' fixtures in other suites, and writing one here would double-count against that module's coverage. "29 of 284" reads as 255 fixtures still owed here. **Both counts are now carried**, because dropping the registry-wide one would understate how far the suite as a whole has to go, and P2's done-condition already quotes the owned set of 73 rather than the registry total.
