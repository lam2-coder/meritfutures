### 1. The premise that did not survive

The dispatch says `B5`'s condition is *"stated TWICE, in two places, with TWO OF ITS
THREE TERMS EACH"* and instructs that a red `RI-19` against `B5` is correct and is the
point. It also says to verify both quotations at `file:line` before building on them.
**Doing so refutes them.** Both are session 392's PRE-repair text, and that session's
own `Done` list says it wrote the condition *"once with all three terms"*.

| Source | What it says today |
|---|---|
| `liability.ts:238` | `**CLEARING CONDITION, ALL THREE TERMS, AND THE GROUP NEEDS EVERY ONE:**` over a numbered `1.` `2.` `3.` |
| `admin-source-liability.test.ts:697` | `CLEARING CONDITION, ALL THREE TERMS:` over all three in one sentence |

**So `RI-19` is green on `B5` and the green was not forced.** The repair was already
standing; what it lacked was a control. Nothing in `apps/api` was opened, which is what
the dispatch asked for in the case where the terms belong to sessions 394 and 395.

### 2. What it derives, and why it holds no list

The check stores no condition, term, blocker, file or count. It finds a statement by
its own declared term count, takes the module's numbered enumeration AS the term set,
and derives each term's identifiers from its backtick spans, split on everything an
identifier cannot hold, so `` `eligible_next_7d: EligibleNext7d | null` `` is three of
them. **Distinctiveness is derived from the enumeration rather than declared:**

```
B5, as RI-19 reads it out of liability.ts:238
  term 1  writeRuleState nightly scripts demo world rule_states apps worker packages
  term 2  engine_gates EngineGateResults bigint adr
  term 3  eligible_next_7d EligibleNext7d null adr api_contract routes admin reads apps src api types
  shared  apps (1,3)  adr (2,3)      <- bind nothing
  case    writeRuleState engine_gates eligible_next_7d null
```

`apps` sits in two terms and buys nothing. `engine_gates` sits in one and is the whole
of what term 2 is about. A fourth term makes this check about four terms with nobody
editing it.

### 4. What it does not catch, asserted rather than claimed

Two blindnesses are green `expect` calls in the suite and not only prose in `covers`.

1. **It reads identifiers and not prose.** *"gains its `| null`"* and *"LOSES its
   `| null`"* are the same term to it.
2. **A restatement that does not declare its own term count is invisible.** Derived at
   reporting time: **27 sites outside `packages/tooling` mention a clearing condition
   and exactly 2 declare their term count**, so `RI-19` sees one pair and 25 sites are
   outside it. It binds the form session 392 established and makes that form
   load-bearing. **This is the larger hole and it is written down as one.**

Also outside it: a condition stated on one side of a pair only, a third statement in a
file that is neither the module nor its name-derived test, and a marker wrapped across
two comment lines. A term with no distinctive identifier, a module that enumerates
nothing, and a count word it cannot read are **reported rather than passed**.
