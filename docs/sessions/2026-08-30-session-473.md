### 3. The ruling, in one line each

1. A generated SPAN may appear many times in one file. A LINE carrying one may not appear twice.
2. On this corpus no such line is legitimately repeated, and the measurement is stronger than the argument: zero identical gen-carrying lines exist in two different files, anywhere.
3. All fifteen live instances are defects, ruled per site rather than per class.
4. The discriminator is the span, the scope is one file, the ceiling is one, and there is no length floor.
5. `RI-12` stays exactly as it is, and its number is correct rather than loose.
