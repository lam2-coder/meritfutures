### 4. The measurement, which is the whole row

`pgBossQueue` was constructed over a `pg` client running `SET ROLE merit_app` against a freshly applied `0001`..`0082` on **PostgreSQL 16.13**, and driven through `start()`, `declareQueue`, an `enqueue` inside a caller's open transaction, `consume`, a wait long enough for the flow poller, the cron worker, the cron monitor, the queue cache and one full supervisor pass, and `stop()`. The executor logged every statement and every error, so a failure a poller swallows into an `error` event is still counted.

| Grants in force | Result |
|---|---|
| none, which is `0001`..`0081` | **every method throws** at `permission denied for schema pgboss`, at `start()`'s first statement |
| `USAGE` alone | `start()` fails at `permission denied for table version`, `declareQueue` and `enqueue` at `permission denied for table queue` |
| `0082` | **132 statements, zero permission errors, zero poller errors** |
| each of the sixteen terms revoked in turn | **all sixteen produce `permission denied`** |

**THE REACHABLE STATEMENT SET IS BOUNDED BY THE INTERFACE AND THAT IS WHY THIS IS A MEASUREMENT RATHER THAN A SAMPLE.** `pgBossQueue` fixes `migrate: false` and `useListenNotify: false` and passes no other option, so `supervise` and `schedule` are ON and `persistQueueStats` and `persistWarnings` are OFF and **cannot be turned on through `PgBossQueueOptions`**. That is why `bam`, `warning`, `queue_stats` and `subscription` get nothing.
