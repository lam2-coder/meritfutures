### 2. The dispatch asked to be checked, and one of its two premises did not survive

The dispatch's instruction was explicit: *"A claim of mine you cannot reproduce is the finding, not a premise."* It also pointed at the exact door: *"If `IdempotencyStore.complete` needs an addressed update, `updateAt` may already be exactly that -- check before concluding it is impossible."*

**It was, and the check is what refuted the premise.** The store is not merely constructible; it is CONSTRUCTED, at [`idempotency-store.ts:144`](../../apps/api/src/idempotency-store.ts), and has been since [ADR-112](../decisions/ADR-112.md).

The sharpest evidence is that **the tree already knew and disagreed with itself**:

| `wiring.test.ts` | Says |
|---|---|
| `:150` | *"`databaseIdempotencyStore` already exists and satisfies the `idempotency` member alone."* |
| `:228` | *"an `IdempotencyStore` implementation, which no file in this tree provides."* |

**Both cannot be true. The first is.**

### 3. Why the cash door stays at 503 anyway, which is the finding worth more than the number

The store was never the only thing between `POST /wallet/withdrawals` and a wiring. **The route posts nothing** -- its own header says so at `:10-60` -- and its `WithdrawalTx` declares no ledger handle, so it was never waiting on a construction at all.

What it waits on is a **driver**, and the consequence of wiring without one is arithmetic:

1. The route writes a row at `requested` or `cooling` (`:1338`).
2. **Nothing in this tree performs `requested --> approved` or `cooling --> approved`** (`:57-60`).
3. Both statuses are in `OPEN_WITHDRAWAL_STATUSES` (`:283-288`).
4. `gateNoInFlight` (`:1233`) refuses on any open row.

**So the first withdrawal a trader requests is the last one they can ever request.** The dispatch's constraint was that *"the failure direction must be refusal rather than duplication"*, and both options refuse -- **but a 503 is a reversible refusal and a lockout is not.** That is the whole of clause 5.

### 4. The guard was watched failing, because a guard that has not been is not a control

The false premise was a SENTENCE, and no test, typecheck or gate could see it. So the new suite asserts on the reason string itself, and the assertion was seeded against:

`git diff --stat` on the mutated file reported **empty** after the restore, so the tree is byte for byte what it was.

**It reads the REASON STRING and not the file**, and that boundary is deliberate: the correcting comment quotes the claim it retires, and a blunter check would have forbidden the record from explaining itself.

### 5. What was refused, and the two things reported rather than reached for

**`packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts` was not touched**, no `SystemReason` or `SqlExecutorReason` member was added, no `pg` was imported, and nothing was cast past a key type. **No migration number was claimed**, and section 5 of the entry shows why none is needed: `0010` already carries every column the ruling's driver reads.

**Two things are reported and left**, both outside the fence:

* **`databaseWithdrawalBackend`'s doc comment still carries the refuted claim.** [`routes/wallet-withdrawals.ts`](../../apps/api/src/routes/wallet-withdrawals.ts) was not in this session's fence and is not edited. One edit for whoever holds it next.
* **`routes/payouts.ts` is where clause 2 is applied and it was not in the fence either.** Deleting `PayoutTx.ledger` is a money-path slice with its own `E2` read, and doing it here would have been the reach the dispatch warned against.
