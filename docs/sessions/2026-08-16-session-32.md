## 2026-08-16 - Session 32: the AI and LLM policy (ADR-044), written by the review desk

- **Done:** [ADR-044](../decisions/ADR-044.md), Merit's first ruling on whether it may use a language model in its own product. Four permitted classes, three prohibitions, an amendment to constitution [E3](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md), and `CI-06o` claimed. **`044` and the gate letter `o` were claimed in [ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md) before the ADR was written**, per [ADR-034](../decisions/ADR-034.md). No migration, no schema, no engine behavior and no running gate changed. All 12 gates pass clean and fail dirty.
- **Next:** The **M10 and M15 threat-model pass** is the only named precondition ADR-044 creates, and it blocks nothing until someone proposes the support assistant. **`CI-06o` is unwritten**, and until it runs, prohibition 1 is prose. Everything else in the queue is unchanged: FOLD-01 s4, then FOLD-02 s4, then `OI-06`.
- **Blockers:** None.
- **Landmines:**
  - **The brief cited E3 as authority for something E3 does not permit, and it read as a citation.** E3 scopes the support bot to **published docs** *until a threat-model pass says otherwise*; the brief added the trader's live gate state, which is internal per-identity data. That is the "otherwise", and E3 had already priced it. **A citation that widens its source is the hardest defect class to see, because the source is right there and it looks checked.** The ADR amends E3 explicitly rather than borrowing its authority.
  - **"Labeled advisory" does not answer [M13](../plans/M13-trader-analytics-journal.md), and a reader will think it does.** M13's governing sentence is that a second computation of anything the engine computes means two rulebooks, and its failure mode is **a trader who believes something different from what the engine will enforce**. A label addresses a misreading. M13's failure is a misbelief. The **narration boundary** (a model may narrate engine-computed numbers, never derive one) is the answer, and its value is that it is **checkable** where a label is not.
  - **The brief prohibited its own permission.** "No unreviewed AI-generated user-facing copy" and "per-trader runtime insights" cannot both hold, because runtime narration can never be pre-reviewed. Scoping the prohibition to **published, static** surfaces is what makes the pair consistent, and the ADR records that it was a contradiction rather than smoothing it.
  - **ADR-020's pattern does not transfer whole and the difference is the fallback.** [ADR-020](../decisions/ADR-020.md)'s indicative tier is nondeterministic in *freshness* only and always degrades to a correct stale value. A model layer has **no stale-but-correct fallback**, so "graceful degradation" here can only mean the deterministic surface is complete with the model layer **deleted**. Stated in those words because the phrase otherwise gets built as a spinner.
  - **Permitting is not specifying, and under FREEZE the difference is four uncosted features.** `grep` for `llm`, `genai`, `machine learning`, `generative` and `ml` across `docs/` returns **zero hits**, so every one of these behaviors is currently out of scope by omission. An ADR that listed four permitted features without saying it adds no scope would have committed five frozen module plans to build them.
  - **`OI-09`: `CI-06n` matches any markdown link, not a table row.** Its `run()` accepts a **prose mention** as a registry row. Its title says "has a README row" and its `covers` line honestly says "is linked from"; the check matches the weaker one. **Found because `ADR-043` had no row in [decisions/README](../decisions/README.md) and the gate was green**: one ADR of 43 missing from the registry index, and it was the ADR that wrote `CI-06n` to pay for its own `CI-06c` exemption. The row is added; **the gate is not fixed**, because tightening a parser without a `falsify.mjs` seed watched failing is how the 109 phantom anchors happened. **Third instance of the loose-parser class**, after `CI-06g`'s `[a-z_]+`.
  - **`OI-10`: keep-both merges duplicated four passages in STATE and no gate could see it.** The schema-delta paragraph twice identically, the `CI-06, corpus integrity` row three times, action item 3 three times with three different tails (`0032` next, `0032` done, `0032` not started), and a **stale** P1 row asserting `0029` to `0031` were "reserved and unwritten" after all three merged. **`CI-06g` cannot catch this**: duplicated spans carrying the *same* value match their query, so the gate passes over a document holding a false sentence. The dedup step in the merge recipe is a human obligation with nothing behind it. **The `Eleven checks` count was also wrong against the twelve `gates.mjs` reports**, corrected in the same pass.
  - **The gate that would catch `OI-10` was deliberately NOT claimed.** "No restated span-carrying sentence appears twice in one document" needs a letter, and claiming one for a gate nobody has scoped precisely is the prose-reserves-a-number failure [ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md) already records against itself. It stays a recommendation until someone scopes it.
  - **The ADR State column was stale in five rows at once**, `039` to `043`, after PR #17 and PR #18 merged them. **Fifth through ninth instance.** [ADR-036](../decisions/ADR-036.md) already records that the column is prose no gate can check, so this needs no ruling and will recur.
