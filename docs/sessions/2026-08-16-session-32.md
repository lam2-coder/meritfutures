### The one place the ruling and the plan disagreed, and the ruling won

[FOLD-01](../plans/FOLD-01-phone-identity.md) predates the founder's degradation ruling, which is recorded in [ADR-039](../decisions/ADR-039.md) and carried into this session's brief: **the cost breaker degrades, it does not stop.** The plan's section 6.1 row for SECURITY says only "C-28 pre-identity OTP velocity and the cost breaker", which a reader arriving cold would implement fail-closed, because every other breaker in this estate is fail-closed and `plan_breaker_state` has a `paused` state to copy. **Fail-closed protects money on provisioning and destroys it on registration**: phone verification is mandatory, so a stopped breaker means no new customers, tripped at the price of the traffic that trips it, which is the attacker's business model in amendment 2 anyway.

`0029` already encodes it (`otp_send_budget.state` has no stopping value), and this session put the reason in three places a reader arrives from: C-28's row, `INV-M16-12`, and `AS-M16-07` counter 4. **The second-order attack is priced beside it rather than left for whoever notices**: an attacker who trips the breaker on purpose buys registrations and not funded accounts, because `pre_funded` fires before Merit's capital is at risk, the window is alarmed while it runs, and `deferred_registrations` makes the cohort countable rather than anonymous.
