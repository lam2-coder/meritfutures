### 1. The objective

[ADR-413](../decisions/ADR-413.md) item 4, restated unchanged by [ADR-416](../decisions/ADR-416.md) section 12 item 3, [ADR-420](../decisions/ADR-420.md) item 5 and [ADR-421](../decisions/ADR-421.md) item 3: *"`SystemTx` satisfying `AdminSourceTx` is held by prose in nine docblocks and by no control."* Re-derive the count rather than inherit it, establish what a control would have to assert and where it could live, re-test the obstruction the four entries deferred on, and either write the control inside the fence with a watched RED or state precisely why it cannot be written and what would unblock it.

### 4. One thing this row got wrong and fixed in the open

**The first measurement of "which handles were already controlled" was wrong and reported all ten as controlled.** It appended a method to each interface and counted any `tsc` error, which counts every hand-written fake in the suites that implements the interface, and those exist for handles nothing binds to `SystemTx`. `LiabilityTx` came back as controlled by `admin-source-liability-book.test.ts`, which is a fixture object and not a witness.

**The second pass counts only errors whose text names `SystemTx`**, which is the property actually in question, and it gives 6 and 4. The wrong figure was never written into the entry, but it was believed for one step, and the direction of the error is the one worth recording: **a coarse probe reports a property as held when what it is really detecting is that somebody wrote a stub.**
