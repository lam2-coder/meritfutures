- **Next:** `FOLD-05` **P5** (fee-back and ladder, `0044`, **money path**), **P6** (display-name decoupling), **P7** (the harness). `OQ-M21-02` needs an ADR to correct two approved documents. `OQ-M21-07` is a one-line move in the ownership index.
- **Blockers:** M21 is `draft` and [ADR-071](../decisions/ADR-071.md) is still `proposed` with its approval line unsigned. **The plan is written on an unsigned ruling**, which is what P4 was scheduled to do, and neither is a session's to sign.
- **Landmines:**

  - **THE HARNESS IS BETTER SPECIFIED THAN "IT DOES NOT EXIST", AND READING THE PRIMARY SOURCE IS WHAT FOUND IT.** [SIMULATION_HARNESS](../testing/SIMULATION_HARNESS.md) is an **approved 282-line port spec** with ten population parameters, eleven calibration bands, an eight-output contract and a CI wiring, and its **section 9 records that `research/calibration/mc_lifecycle.py` landed at the FREEZE gate and was run**. `git log` confirms the file is committed. **What does not exist is the TypeScript port**, which is section 8.3 step 8 of a checklist that has already had steps 1 to 7 executed. `DEP-M21-01` says that instead of inheriting "a harness from nothing".

  - **FIVE OF REQUIREMENT (b)'s EIGHT OUTPUTS ALREADY HAVE IDENTIFIERS AND THE PLAN'S JOB WAS TO NOTICE.** Projected eval pass rate is `RE-S-01`, funded-to-payout is `RE-S-02`, payouts per payer is `RE-S-03`, the per-day extraction ceiling is `RE-S-05` and the lifetime bound is `RE-S-06`. **The simulation preview is the calibration bands evaluated against a candidate configuration rather than the live one**, which is a far smaller thing than a new metric set. The three with no identifier are named as **owed `HO-nn` additions** and are deliberately not numbered here, on the discipline [M06](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) applied to the missing restore event: claiming in a registry this session does not own is the failure, not the omission.

  - **"A PARAMETER FORM OVER EVERY `plan_version` FIELD" IS 43 FIELDS, NOT 47.** The [plan-config audit](../reviews/2026-08-20-plan-config-audit.md) enumerated four states, and **11 of the 43 are MATERIALIZED**: a ratio in `rules` projected onto a constrained cents column at publish. **The form edits the ratio and displays the cents value, never the reverse**, or a founder can make `buffer_bp` and `buffer_cents` disagree and find out at publish. The 4 ABSENT parameters have no field at all until `0044` exists, which is `DEP-M21-06`.

  - **A PREFILLED FORM WOULD PUT A PLAN PARAMETER IN APPLICATION CODE, WHICH THE SCHEMA'S OWN COMMENT FORBIDS.** [`0004_catalog.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0004_catalog.sql): *"There is no plan parameter anywhere in application code."* [`simulator/population.ts`](../../packages/rithmic/src/simulator/population.ts) already made this call for itself, in its own words: *"a simulator with an account size in it has quietly made one a constant in the one place nobody looks for plan configuration."* `INV-M21-10` says an empty draft is empty, and it is the invariant most likely to be traded away for a nicer first-run experience.

  - **`AS-M21-01` IS THE SHIPPING CONDITION, NOT A SCENARIO.** [SIMULATION_HARNESS section 9.4](../testing/SIMULATION_HARNESS.md) records the committed calibration engine as **stale in four places**, and [STATE](../STATE.md)'s surviving item 5 is the re-run, owner "claude, build phase". **A console built without the provenance binding would ship straight into the condition the scenario describes**, so `0045`'s argument is not hypothetical. `DEP-M21-05` names it.

  - **THE CONTROL IS THE RECORD, NOT THE REFUSAL, AND THE ALERT TABLE HAS TO AGREE.** A freshness check that blocks a publish is a check that gets waived under pressure, and the waiver is where the record disappears. So publishing without a simulation is **permitted and recorded**: `SD-M21-02`'s `CHECK` requires exactly one of a run reference or a written waiver reason, and the waiver **digests rather than pages**. Paging on the honest path teaches people to avoid it, which is the failure the module was admitted to end.

  - **DUAL CONTROL IS WIDER HERE THAN [ADR-010](../decisions/ADR-010.md)'s SENSITIVE SET, DELIBERATELY.** ADR-010 names cap, split and cadence gap. **In a console whose purpose is to make plan changes easy, the difference between a sensitive change and an ordinary one is one keystroke**, and asking the payload to classify itself makes the control optional. `INV-M21-06` requires the second approval on every publish and the cost is one approval on a copy-only change.

  - **`in_review` AND `pending_approval` ARE NOT PLAN-VERSION STATUSES AND THE PLAN REFUSES TO MAKE THEM ONE.** `plan_version_status` is `draft`, `published`, `retired`. The workflow states live in [M06](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md)'s `dual_control_approvals` row, because widening the rule contract's own state machine to hold a console's workflow is an argument that belongs in [STATE_MACHINES](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) rather than in a module plan.

  - **THE MANIFEST ROWS WERE NOT OPTIONAL AND THE FENCE EXCEPTION IS GATE-FORCED.** `ADR-026`'s completeness gate matches `(?:SD|U)-(?:\d{2}|M\d{1,2}-\d{2})` anywhere in `docs/`, so `SD-M21-01` trips it the moment it is written. [DELTA_MANIFEST](../../packages/db/DELTA_MANIFEST.md) section 4b states the rule directly: *"a delta identifier is claimed by its manifest row"*. Adding the three rows then moved the `manifest_changes` span from 107 to 110 in three more files. **Four files beyond the fence, all of them mechanical, all of them recorded in the pull request** rather than discovered in a diff.
