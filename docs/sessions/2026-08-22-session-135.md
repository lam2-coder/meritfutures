## 2026-08-22 - Session 135: the consistency-period bound was pointed at the wrong column, and the row it exists for is exempt from its replacement (money path)

- **Done:** **[ADR-079](../decisions/ADR-079.md) (`proposed`, unsigned) and [`0046`](../../packages/db/migrations/0046_supersede_rule_states_consistency_period_started.sql).** `0015`'s `rule_states_consistency_period_started` required `consistency_period_start_day <= trading_day`; `R-47` defines the period start against the **anchor** and sets it to the next trading day **after** it, so every eval-pass row was refused by PostgreSQL. **Superseded by `rule_states_consistency_period_after_anchor`**, which asserts `R-47`'s own strictness against the day `R-47` names. **No engine change and no fixture change**; `packages/rules-engine` is untouched. Both database states **executed rather than predicted** on PostgreSQL 16.13: `0001` to `0045` refuses the row by name, all 46 apply forward-only and it inserts. `node scripts/corpus/gates.mjs check`: **28 of 28**. `node scripts/corpus/falsify.mjs`: **28 gates pass clean and fail dirty, 52 scope cases, 10 loader cases**. `pnpm vitest run`: **1410 pass, 45 skipped, 101 files**.
- **Next:** the founder's **E2 line-by-line read**, which this branch waits on. `0047` stays free and no `CI-06` letter is claimed.
- **Blockers:** none. The entry is complete and unsigned by design.
- **Landmines:**

  - **THE ROW THIS ENTRY EXISTS FOR IS EXEMPT FROM THE CONSTRAINT IT INSTALLS, AND THE FIRST DRAFT OF THE APPROVAL CLAUSE DID NOT SEE IT.** On the eval-pass row `payout_anchor_day IS NULL`, which is exactly `payouts_settled_count = 0` by `rule_states_settlements_imply_anchors` ([`0015:188`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql)). So the row inserts clean at `0046` **because the constraint is silent, not because it permits**, and the clause *"inserts clean at 0046 and is refused at 0045"* is satisfied by a constraint that refuses nothing, ever. **A `CHECK` only ever watched not-applying is a `CHECK` nobody has tested**, and it would have shipped that way. The review desk caught it before implementation and the probe now asserts in **three** directions.

  - **`check_violation` ALONE WOULD HAVE PROVED NOTHING.** `rule_states` carries **twelve** CHECK constraints and a probe catching the bare condition reports green on a row refused by any of them. `REJECTION 1` and `REJECTION 2` assert the constraint NAME via `GET STACKED DIAGNOSTICS`, and the name was confirmed by hand before the handler was written. This is the near-miss [`0040`](../../packages/db/DELTA_MANIFEST.md) already recorded, where two of twenty-four assertions were refused by a constraint other than the one they were aimed at.

  - **THE DEFECT IS A REFERENCE POINT, NOT A DIRECTION, AND THAT IS WHY THE REPAIR IS NOT A QUALIFIER.** [ADR-053](../decisions/ADR-053.md) scoped a predicate by adding `floor_locked OR`. This one could not be repaired that way: the **other operand** was wrong. `0015` compared against `trading_day`; `R-47` names the anchor. That is also why the retired name had to go rather than be reused, and the reason is stronger than the precedent's: a reader grepping `rule_states_consistency_period_started` must not find a constraint comparing a different pair of columns.

  - **SCOPING TO `phase = 'eval'` WOULD HAVE BEEN VACUOUS, AND IT IS THE ALTERNATIVE MOST LIKELY TO BE PROPOSED NEXT.** [`advance.ts:120`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/advance.ts) sets `consistencyPeriodStartDay: null` for the whole eval phase, so the `IS NULL` disjunct already passes every eval row. **The constraint would have covered nothing while looking like a control**, which is worse than none because the next reader counts it as coverage. Recorded in ADR-079 section 3(c) rather than left as a road not taken.

  - **THE BRIEF SAID SETTLEMENT ROWS "GET EQUALITY". THEY SATISFY BY `<=`.** The basis day is *"The LAST CLOSED DAY the decision used"* ([`0010:63`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql)) and the row is written for the effective day ([`0010:97`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql)), so equality holds only when the effective day is the very next trading day. **`GS-068` is the non-equality case**, with Thanksgiving between `2026-11-25` and `2026-11-27`. The conclusion is unchanged and the mechanism is not, and **a mechanism stated loosely in a brief is how a later session cites it as fact** — which is why the founder asked for it in the entry.

  - **THE MANDATED GREP WAS RUN BEFORE ANY `0015` CONSTRAINT WAS CITED, AND SESSION 129's SECOND FINDING IS NOT REPRODUCED.** `rule_states_consistency_period_started` is defined once, at `0015:193`, and nothing supersedes it. The `0015:208` high-water bound was repaired by [`0037`](../../packages/db/migrations/0037_supersede_rule_states_high_water_bounds_balance.sql) under ADR-053 on 2026-08-17, which [session 132](2026-08-22-session-132.md) established.

  - **THE CONSTRAINT TOTAL DOES NOT MOVE, AND THAT IS THE CHEAPEST AVAILABLE CHECK THAT NOTHING ELSE DID.** 879 constraints and 12 `rule_states` CHECKs at both `0045` and `0046`. A supersession whose count moved would mean something was added or dropped alongside it.
