## 2026-08-17 - Session 52: the GS-024 floor divergence, ruled against the engine on a derivation nobody had checked (money path)

- **Done:** **[ADR-052](../decisions/ADR-052.md) is written and `proposed`**, ruling `R-15`'s lock effect for section 3.6's **assignment** against section 3.4's **`max`**, which is the reading [`floor.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/floor.ts) ships. The divergence day was computed by hand under both readings and is **2026-11-03**, `GS-024`'s day two. **Three findings the corpus did not carry**: `CV-11`'s derivation of `INV-21` fails under the `max` and is the argument that decides the ruling; **`GS-024` is the only fixture whose pinned floor differs between the readings** and the existing catalogue of affected files omits it; and **[`0015`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql)'s `high_water_balance_cents >= balance_cents` check refuses to store any locked account that makes a new closing high**, which is every successful funded account. **No fixture and no engine file was touched**, per the brief. 16 of 16 corpus gates pass.
- **Next:** **The founder's signature on [ADR-052](../decisions/ADR-052.md), then two sessions in this order.** First `floor.ts`, where the `max` becomes a branch, the sub-step `neverRetreats('R-15', ...)` call is deleted, `CI-02/engine-R-15`'s mutant inverts, and **`GS-024` goes green so CI-03's six failures become five**. Then the `0015` constraint: its own ADR, its own superseding migration, its own money-path session. **`GS-065` is the natural third**, and section 2 of the ADR is already its stream.

- **Landmines:**

  - **THE ARITHMETIC IN ADR-052 SECTION 2 IS `GS-065`'S CONTENT, AND IT SHOULD BE TRANSCRIBED RATHER THAN RE-DERIVED.** `MERIT-RAPID-50K`, funded, lock-day close `5,350,001`, floor `5,100,001`, three payouts of `100,000 + 100,000 + 50,001`, balance `5,100,000`, **one cent below the account's own floor with no losing day in the stream**. `GS-065`, `GS-081` and `GS-083` are `INV-21`'s three fixtures and **all three are unwritten**, which is why nothing in the tree would have caught the `max`. A session that re-derives this may derive it differently; `TR-01` says transcribe from the document, and the document now exists.

  - **THE ENGINE IS WRONG BECAUSE A TEST WAS MADE STRICTER THAN THE RULE IT WAS TESTING, AND THIS FILE PRAISED THE TIGHTENING.** [Session 45](2026-08-16-session-45.md) changed `R-14`'s tripwire to fire after each sub-step and STATE called that "the more useful half of the finding". **`INV-06` is a property of the stored day-over-day floor series** and section 3.6's own tripwire compares against the **prior day's** floor; under the assignment `GS-024`'s series is `4,900,001 -> 5,010,000 -> ...`, monotone non-decreasing. The sub-step check asserts a property no document states, the assignment `R-15` specifies violates it, and **the engine resolved the contradiction by changing the rule to satisfy the test.** The generalisable form: a check tightened beyond its invariant does not find defects, it manufactures them, and the rule is what loses.

  - **"NEITHER IS OBVIOUSLY WRONG" WAS FALSE, AND THE THING THAT DECIDED IT WAS A DERIVATION RATHER THAN A RULE.** Both readings satisfy `R-14`, `R-15`'s trigger, `INV-06` at day granularity and `INV-07`. **The discriminator is `CV-11`, which derives `INV-21` from "post-lock it equals it"** — a clause about the floor's value, stated inside a config-validation row, that nothing was checking. **No rule table, no state diagram and no invariant row distinguishes the readings.** Reading only the sources the brief named would have produced a tie.

  - **A PUBLISH-TIME CONTROL CANNOT BOUND A RUNTIME QUANTITY, AND THAT IS WHY THE `max` IS NOT REPAIRABLE.** [ADR-014](../decisions/ADR-014.md) deliberately moved `INV-21` from a runtime recompute to `CV-11` and `CV-17`. Under the `max` the post-lock floor depends on the lock-day closing balance. **A new validation cannot be written**, because the quantity is the trader's to choose after the plan is published. This is the shape to look for elsewhere: an invariant whose enforcement layer no longer contains its inputs.

  - **THE 40,001 WAS NOT A ROUNDING DECISION, AND THE BRIEF'S HYPOTHESIS THAT IT WAS SENT THE INVESTIGATION SOMEWHERE PRODUCTIVE ANYWAY.** It is exactly the overshoot above the trigger, `5,300,001 - (5,000,000 + 260,000)`, and the odd cent is **`GS-023`'s moved cent**, which `GS-024`'s own header states: day one carries `150,001` where `GS-023` carries `150,000`, and it is still in the balance on day two. **No division exists anywhere in the floor path**, so no remainder could arise there. Chasing the cent is what located the boundary pair and proved the divergence is the overshoot rather than the scenario.

  - **TWO READINGS WOULD HAVE MADE EVERY SENTENCE IN THE CORPUS TRUE AT ONCE, AND A MERGED MIGRATION REFUTES BOTH.** Freezing `hwb` at the trigger (`5,260,000`) or before the lock day's update (`5,150,001`) makes the `max` resolve to `5,010,000` and `CV-12`'s redundancy claim literally correct. **Both store a `hwb` below that day's `balance_cents` and `0015`'s check rejects the row.** The reconciliation that saves the most text is not available, and finding that out took reading a merged migration rather than re-reading M01.

  - **`INV-06` OCCUPIES TWO ROWS OF M01 SECTION 1.5**, different wording, both citing [ADR-050](../decisions/ADR-050.md). Same shape as the `ADR-046` duplicate-heading defect, and **no gate reads that table for duplicates** — the same absence that let `ADR-046` pass fifteen gates. Recorded, not fixed.

  - **A STATED GATE COUNT WAS CHECKED AND IS 16, FOR A REASON THAT WAS NOT THE ONE OFFERED.** The count is 16 because **`ADR-026`'s manifest gate runs alongside the fifteen `CI-06` letters**, not because `CI-06p` pushed the letters to 16: `CI-06p` is one of the fifteen (`a` to `n` plus `p`, with `o` reserved and unwritten). The number is right and the derivation was not, which is `ADR-034`'s subject from the other direction.
