### 1. The objective

Row `361`: rule whether money can leave Merit through either of the trader's two exits, and if not, name exactly what stops each.

**One objective. It resolved into a ruling, twenty-four executed cases, one finding about a fail-closed default, one stale blocker reason reported as owed, and one hole in a live invariant isolated by a controlled pair.**

---

### 2. What was re-derived before the first edit

**The baseline was measured on `aa7ab356` before anything was touched: 326 files / 7,921 passed / 10 skipped / 0 failed.** It was measured a second time at reporting time by removing this branch's changes, and both readings agree.

Both of the dispatch's declaration claims reproduce exactly: `usePayoutBackend` at `payouts.ts:690` over `UNWIRED_PAYOUT_BACKEND`, `useWithdrawalBackend` at `wallet-withdrawals.ts:1134` over `UNWIRED_WITHDRAWAL_BACKEND`, and neither setter is among the eleven `start.ts` calls.

**The finding is not in either of them.** It is in what the dispatch assumed underneath: that somewhere on these ports is a member `ApiDb` cannot supply.

---

### 3. The ruling

[ADR-361](../decisions/ADR-361.md) sections 2 to 6 hold it.

**NEITHER PORT IS SHORT OF A DOOR.** Six of the payout pair's eight members are built; the two that are not wait on a clause vocabulary counsel owes. All fourteen of the withdrawal pair's members are built. Every read and write both ports need is `owned` or `derived` on the caller's identity and reachable through `ApiDb.scoped`.

**MONEY CANNOT LEAVE, AND THE TWO DOORS SHUT AT DIFFERENT LAYERS.** The payout door is reachable and answers 503 from the port. The withdrawal door is refused by `C-27` before the port is consulted at all, so the 503 it would produce is unreachable in any deployment.

**THE REFUSAL CANNOT QUOTE A FIGURE.** The whole of what the two fail-closed defaults produce is one `Date`.

### 5. The `RI-14` repairs this row owed, wrote, and could not keep

Three prose facts were measured stale in files this row owns: `payout-backend.ts`'s header disagreeing with itself across one screen about whether five or six members answer, the same header's `TEN OF TWENTY-FOUR` triple that [ADR-357](../decisions/ADR-357.md) already superseded, and `UNWIRED_WITHDRAWAL_BACKEND`'s *"refuses every call"*.

**All three corrections were written and all three were reverted.** Writing them moved `payout-backend.ts` by twenty-two lines and `wallet-withdrawals.ts` by twenty-eight, and `RI-15` and `RI-16` fell to 33 of 35 on four citations: `planLeg` from `wiring.test.ts`, `APPROVAL_HOLDS` and `decideApproval` from `apps/worker`, and `lockScope` from a row of `ALLOCATION.md` this session does not own.

**So an `RI-14` correction to a comment in a heavily cited money-path file is a line-number change, and `RI-15` and `RI-16` bind line numbers.** A row whose fence does not reach its citers cannot make one. The alternative, compressing correct prose to hold the line count, destroys content other rows cite in order to make room for a correction. All three facts are recorded as owed in ADR-361 sections 7 and 9.

### 7. Blockers and landmines

- **The `E2` read of [ADR-361](../decisions/ADR-361.md) is owed.** Money path directly: these are the two doors cash leaves through.
- **OWED, and it is the one a session would act on: `usePayoutBackend`'s `BLOCKED` reason says FOUR MEMBERS OF EIGHT and names `subject` and `listPayouts` as rejecting.** Both answer. `wiring.test.ts` belongs to a sibling this wave and was not touched.
- **OWED: `payout-backend.ts`'s header contradicts itself about five versus six across twelve lines, and carries ADR-357's already-superseded triple.** Section 5 is why this row could not repair it.
- **OWED: `UNWIRED_WITHDRAWAL_BACKEND`'s docblock claims a whole refusal it does not perform.**
- **LANDMINE: the payout 503 does not come from the member you would predict.** `idempotency.find` refuses first and `transact` is never reached, so a reader debugging from `UNWIRED_PAYOUT_BACKEND.transact` predicts the wrong member.
- **LANDMINE: the withdrawal 503 is unreachable and an install would be observable to nobody**, until an unrelated row lands a WebAuthn ceremony or an SMS sender, at which point it arms itself with no signal.
- **LANDMINE: making `UNWIRED_WITHDRAWAL_BACKEND.now` refuse, on its own, turns the money-out refusal into a 500.** The clock read must move inside the handler's `try` first. It is executed in `money-out-refusal.test.ts` in both directions.
- **`CI-06f` reports `ADR-360` as a hole.** It is a concurrent sibling's and this row may not invent its subject, which is `ALLOCATION.md`'s own recorded precedent from the `053`/`054`/`055` collision.
