#### Open item 2: `GS-052`'s internal half. **The atomicity claim is unasserted, unassertable in the fixture format, and NOT STRUCTURALLY GUARANTEED EITHER.**

**The row.** [Section 39:117](../testing/golden-scenarios/39-fixture-status-and-blockers.md) is `GS-052 | blocked | format-cannot-express`, reason *"neither half is one account's day fold."* `GS-053`, its sibling, is `written` with a yaml and an expected sibling on disk. **The registry row** ([section 5](../testing/golden-scenarios/05-gs-052-to-gs-070-adversarial-scenarios-m1-section-7.md):5) states the internal half as *"three requests fired in the same transaction window produce exactly one wallet credit, one ordinal, and one win-day reset, because the internal leg completes atomically and leaves no window to stack inside."*

**What would assert it, in order of what the tree can actually run.**

1. **The `psql` probe is the only executable surface, and this is checkable rather than inferred.** `CI-04`'s `integration` job has **no `services:` block** ([`ci.yml`:475-506](../../.github/workflows/ci.yml)), and [`corpus.yml`](../../.github/workflows/corpus.yml) runs `postgres:16` with **12** `probe_*.sql` steps, **none touching a wallet table**. So the assertion's home is a thirteenth probe, which is **M20-b**, and not a vitest file.
2. **A probe can assert the REPRESENTABILITY half and not the SCHEDULING half, and the honest version says so.** A single `psql -f` script drives one session; it can watch a constraint refuse a second row and it cannot schedule two concurrent transactions. That is enough for *"one ordinal"* and *"one wallet credit"* stated as constraints, and it is not enough for *"leaves no window to stack inside"* stated as a race.
3. **Two of the three expectations already have a structural refusal and the third does not.**
   - **One ordinal:** `payout_requests_account_ordinal_uq ON payout_requests (account_id, payout_ordinal) WHERE status <> 'failed'` ([`0010:187-188`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql)). Three requests claiming ordinal `N` collide. **This is assertable today.**
   - **One in flight:** `payout_requests_no_in_flight_uq ON payout_requests (account_id) WHERE status IN ('approved','frozen')` ([`0010:200-201`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql)), whose own comment names `GS-052` and `EC-040` and says *"the engine refuses it at R-38 and this index refuses it again, because the engine is not the only writer."* **This is assertable today.**
   - **One wallet credit: NOTHING REFUSES IT.** `wallet_entries` has no unique index at all. Three `wallet_entries` rows with `provenance = 'payout'` and the same `reference_id` insert cleanly, and so do two rows citing one `ledger_transaction_id`. **The only backstop anywhere is `ledger_transactions.idempotency_key text NOT NULL UNIQUE`** ([`0009:88`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql)), which stops a second **ledger transaction** and says nothing about a second **wallet entry** against the first one. `0038`'s `ADJ-C3` proves the shape is writable, because it asserts exactly-one for **adjustments** and for nothing else.

**So the answer to *"what would assert that"* is that the probe should be written and the constraint it would watch does not exist yet.** That is **M20-c**, it is a ruling before it is a migration, and *"a request is not a mark"* is the right sentence with a second half this session can now name: **a request is not a mark, and neither is a credit, until something refuses the second one.**
