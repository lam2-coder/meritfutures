### 1. The objective, and the answer

**Row `272` asked one question before the rename: whether [ADR-146](../decisions/ADR-146.md) clause 2's suffix convention is a RULE the schema must satisfy or a CONVENTION the schema mostly follows, on the ground that only one of those makes seven columns a defect.**

The ruling is [ADR-272](../decisions/ADR-272.md), nine clauses, `proposed` and UNSIGNED.

**The answer is neither, and the reason is in clause 2's own words.** It governs *"a RESPONSE FIELD named `*_at` ... AT THE POINT OF PROJECTION"*. Nothing in it reaches a `CREATE TABLE`. What makes the schema-level property a rule anyway is not the convention: it is that [ADR-271](../decisions/ADR-271.md) section 3 shipped a global `setTypeParser(1082, ...)` and bounded its blast radius by arguing *"an instant is spelled `*_at` and a day is spelled `*_day` or `*_on`, so the two vocabularies do not overlap"*. **A property a landed repair has been argued from is a rule.** Until this session nothing checked it.

---

### 2. The rule is narrower than the convention, and that is the whole entry

**A column name may be SILENT about its temporal type and may not be FALSE about it.**

The tidy rule, every suffix matching its type, would have turned five columns red instead of two. It is wrong, and the test is detectability:

| | `daily_marks.win_day boolean`, trusted | `calibration_observed_at date`, trusted |
|---|---|---|
| The reader gets | `true` | `'2026-08-14'` |
| What refuses it | `tsc`, the driver, any comparison against `trading_day` | **nothing.** Both are `string` in drizzle, both order lexicographically, and a day is a legal prefix of an instant |
| Noticed | first run | possibly never |

So the rule earns its keep exactly where the type system is blind, and it is blind between a day and an instant **because Merit stores both as text**, which is [ADR-271](../decisions/ADR-271.md) section 3 fact 1 in its own words: *"every one of them is a `PgDateString`, whose `mapFromDriverValue` returns a `string` on both branches"*.

### 3. The check found the deciding fact before the entry knew to look for it

`RI-26` was written with **empty** exception sets and run before anything was ruled. It reported two findings. The first was the dispatch's. **The second was not in the dispatch, not in [ADR-271](../decisions/ADR-271.md) section 9, and not in row `272`:**

`effective_from` is `date NOT NULL` in `geo_restrictions`, `contract_specs`, `detector_definitions`, `statistic_definitions`, `loyalty_criteria` and `identity_signal_weights`, and `timestamptz NOT NULL` in `wallet_spend_limits`, `price_floors` and `firm_parameters`. **Nine tables, one word, two temporal types.**

**The row's second trap said the burden is on the rule to justify churning `effective_from`, not on the name to justify existing. That is right, and a measurement discharges it where a convention argument would not.** `period_start` is a `date` in one table: read the name, learn nothing, open one file, know. `effective_from`: read the name, learn nothing, open one file, and now know something false about the other eight. [ADR-271](../decisions/ADR-271.md) section 3 already had to check this by hand and recorded the check in its own door table, *"That column is `timestamp with time zone`, not `date`"*. **A reader had to look, and the name did not help.**

**The other six are not churned.** Each denotes exactly one type everywhere it appears. The trap holds for them and the entry says so.

### 5. `RI-26`, and the four seeds

**Two legs, each in both directions.** Leg 1: no `*_at` column is declared with a non-instant type. Leg 2: no column name is declared with a day type in one table and an instant type in another, which is why leg 1 alone was the wrong check.

**Each ruled exception carries its GROUND and its WITNESS migrations.** An exception is asserted only where the migrations it names are present, so the synthetic fixture is out of its subject rather than exempt; and on a tree that carries them, **the stale-entry half is also the sentinel.** If the reader stops matching, the exceptions stop being observed and both legs report. A broken reader turns the check RED, which is the failure mode `RI-24` and `RI-25` each needed a separate sentinel to reach.

| Seed, on the real tree | Result |
|---|---|
| A new `_at` column declared `date` | **RED**, leg 1 forward, exit 1 |
| The ruled `_at` exception repaired | **RED**, leg 1 stale entry, exit 1 |
| A new day/instant collision | **RED**, exit 1 |
| The ruled collision repaired, all three instant sides | **RED**, leg 2 stale entry, exit 1 |

Every restore confirmed byte-identical by `sha256sum -c` over all 62 migrations, green again after each. **Twelve permanent cases** in [`repo-invariants.test.ts`](../../packages/tooling/test/repo-invariants.test.ts), including the three that would let it pass for the wrong reason: a comment describing a bad column does not declare one (`RI-25`'s seed, one file type over, and `0045`'s header discusses its own defect by name), a plpgsql `DECLARE` block is not a table, and **witness migrations present with nothing parsed THROWS** rather than passing.

**A fence judgement is stated rather than assumed**, on [ADR-271](../decisions/ADR-271.md) section 8's precedent: the row fences `packages/tooling/checks/**` and not `packages/tooling/test/**`, and shipping the gate without the seeded red-watch its 24 siblings carry would read the *never weaken a gate* limit backwards.

### 6. Two register defects, and the second was found by adding rows beside it

**`RI-25` had no row.** [ADR-271](../decisions/ADR-271.md) section 8 states the check was *"minted here with its row in the same commit"*. The `RI-nn` table carried no such row and still read *"`RI-25` IS THE NEXT FREE NUMBER"* while `CHECKS` had carried `ri25` since that merge. **This is `RI-14`'s defect for the third time**, after `RI-19` and `RI-20`, and the table's own note already records it happening twice. Written here, late, from the live check rather than from the entry describing it, and said to be late.

**`RI-23`'s row was short a cell.** It carried its claim and its outcome in ONE cell against a delimiter declaring three, and a blank line had absorbed it past the end of the table, so it rendered as prose and no table gate read it. `CI-06/table-row-width` was registering it as one of 17 ragged rows *"each one a repair this gate is waiting for"* and `CI-06v` was counting it as a one-line orphan, under its minimum orphan length of two. **Adding two rows beside it turned that silent report into a failure**, which is two gates finding it together rather than either finding it alone. Split into the cells its delimiter declares, reordered into the table, **no wording dropped and no count invented**: the third cell states the title read live from the check and declines to claim what `check:invariants` reported at its landing, because that figure is not derivable from the tree today.
