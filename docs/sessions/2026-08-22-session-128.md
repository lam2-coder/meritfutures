### `X3`: `GS-141`'s severity half, one `expect` and three clauses

**[ADR-076](../decisions/ADR-076.md) section 2 rules `GS-141` `covered-elsewhere` and records the residual in the same breath.** The exact-set block at the head of `Appendix A.4  the approved lineup publishes` compares **ids** through `diffIds`, so the row's first half is asserted and its last sentence, *"Asserts the two are never rendered identically"*, is exactly what an id comparison cannot see.

**One new case, `PW-02a and PW-02b are never rendered identically, which is GS-141 last sentence`**, in that same describe. One `expect`, over an object carrying three clauses at once:

| Clause | Value |
|---|---|
| `coBindingSeverity` | `PW-02a` off Core EOD 50K is `info` |
| `dominatedSeverity` | `PW-02b` off Merit Rapid 50K is `warning` |
| `textsDiffer` | their `message` strings are not equal |

**Three clauses and not two, because no two of them are the row's claim.** Two findings carrying the same text under different severities are rendered identically to a reader who reads the text; one tone over two texts collapses co-binding into dominated at a glance. The row asks that both differ at once, so both are in one `expect` and a partial failure prints which half moved.

**The two cannot be read off one plan.** [`validate.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/plan/validate.ts) builds `PW-02a` and `PW-02b` in the two arms of a single `if` on `lag + gap` against `required_count`, so no plan emits both. Which plan carries which is Appendix A.4's own row, and the exact-set block immediately above has just pinned it, which is why this case may read them off two plans without asserting that mapping a second time.

**The `X1` comment above the exact-set block was updated and not replaced.** It said the severity and the text *"are unasserted today"* and named `X3` as the missing `expect`. That sentence is false the moment this case lands, so it now points at the case rather than at the gap. No assertion in it moved.
