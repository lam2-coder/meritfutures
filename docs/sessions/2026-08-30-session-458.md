### 1. The objective, and the answer

**Row `267` asked one question and it was genuinely open: does [ADR-176](../decisions/ADR-176.md)'s remedy transfer to `LT-06`?**

**IT DOES NOT.** The full ruling is [ADR-267](../decisions/ADR-267.md), six clauses, `proposed` and UNSIGNED.

The row was explicit that *"it worked for `LT-01`" is not an argument*, and that the precedent for a non-transfer is in the corpus already. Both halves were read. **The `LT-08` precedent turned out not to be the one that decides this**, because `LT-06` has no `DEP-M20-02` and does not need one: the pins that bind it are different sentences in the same two documents, and they are stronger rather than weaker.

---

### 2. What pins `LT-06`, at source

| Pin | Where | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| **The direction of the leg** | [M05 section 2.1](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) | `LT-01` is `` credit **`trader_wallet`** (identity) `trader_cents` ``. `LT-06` is `` debit `trader_wallet` (identity) `amount_cents` ``. **A deferred credit understates a position; a deferred debit overstates one**, and every gate that lets money out reads the position |
| **`INV-M20-01`** | [M20 section 1.4](../plans/M20-wallet.md) | *"A wallet balance is **never negative**, and every debit is checked against the live position inside the same transaction"*. `LT-06` is a wallet debit on one of the position's two drawn exits (section 3.1). **The consequence sits in the same sentence as the rule**, exactly where `DEP-M20-02` puts `LT-08`'s |
| **M20 section 3.3a** | [M20](../plans/M20-wallet.md) | `` \| Ledger at entry \| **nothing posted** \| LT-06 already posted \| ``, and *"**it is the whole of the difference between the two legs**: the internal one holds a decision that has posted nothing, and this one holds a payment whose ledger entry already exists"* |
| **The one blessing of a deferred posting, and where it is** | [M05 section 2](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) | The payout hold: *"**Only the ledger posting is deferred.**"* **`grep` finds that sentence in M05 and nowhere else.** M20 has the opposite one |

**THE FAILURE IS CONCRETE RATHER THAN NOTIONAL, AND THE LOCK IS WHY.** `decideWithdrawal` opens with `await tx.lockScope()` and `lockScope` is a locking select on the identity row ([`scoped-db.ts:3311-3320`](../../packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts)), released at `COMMIT`. A trader with 20,000 cents requests 20,000 cents, approved and unposted; a wallet-funded purchase takes the same lock, reads a position that still holds the cents and debits them under `INV-M3-13`; the deferred door then finds nothing to debit. **Either the wallet goes negative or the approval is unpostable forever**, and the second is the trap the row named.

**AND THE CONTROL CARRYING THE SAFETY TODAY REACHES ONE DOOR OF TWO.** [ADR-232](../decisions/ADR-232.md) section 6 said *"nothing double-spends it only because `gateNoInFlight` refuses the second withdrawal anyway"*. Measured: `grep -rn gateNoInFlight apps/api/src` returns **4 lines in one file**, and `routes/checkout.ts` does not contain the string. `LT-08` debits the same position from that file.
