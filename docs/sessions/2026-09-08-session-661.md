### 7. Landmines

- **The budget is a product of four factors and the largest of them is BORROWED.** The x2.0 machine factor comes from `ADR-465`'s 6183ms, which this row could not reproduce. **Drop it and the budget should be 15000 rather than 30000**, and the entry says so in its own approval block rather than burying it.
- **The scaling measurement is contaminated in a known direction.** The clones are identical copies, so the page cache is warm and the finding count rises from 0 to 112. Both make the large sizes cheaper than a real tree of that size, so **3.2 is a floor, not a ceiling**.
- **A per-case budget covers the cases it is written on and nothing else.** Seven sites, and the eighth will be written without one.
- **`repo-invariants.test.ts`'s `RI-35` case passed at 3761ms in the post-edit loaded run and that is luck.** It aborted at 5402ms in the pre-edit one and nothing about it changed.
- **`docs/` is not in the sweep and every intuition that says "the corpus grows so the scan slows" is wrong about the mechanism.** 105 ADR files bought this scan under 52ms.
- **A BRANCH THAT ADDS A PRICING ENTRY CANNOT LAND `price-register.mjs` GREEN, AND THAT IS THE CHECK WORKING RATHER THAN A PROBLEM.** Row `467` stayed green only because it deliberately priced nothing. **An integrator seeing this red should register the anchors, not investigate the checker.**
- **THIS ROW'S FIRST READING OF THAT RED WAS WRONG AND IS CORRECTED IN THE ENTRY RATHER THAN LEFT STANDING.** It called a settled, routine step an open design question, which is this wave's named error class arriving inside the entry that names it: a claim reasoned about rather than looked at. [`ADR-464.md:5`](../decisions/ADR-464.md) had already written down why the red arrives.
- **The checker's own findings are the anchor authority and typed line numbers are not.** The seven anchors moved once inside this session, from `:264`-`:274` to `:266`-`:278`, purely because a paragraph was added above them. That is why the paste-ready rows carry `PPP` rather than a number.
