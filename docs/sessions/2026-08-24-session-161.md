### 6. What I could not determine, and what the canary leg actually needs

**`CI-09`'s detector-canary leg cannot reopen itself, and that is the finding.** [`gates.mjs`](../../scripts/corpus/gates.mjs):6545 registers *"M07's detector code"* in `UNPROBEABLE_ARTIFACTS`, reasoning that the condition *"names a module plan and not a path or a manifest key"*. `CI-07`'s build script and `CI-08`'s Playwright are **probed** and the gate fails on good news the day they land. **This one is not.** So every detector in M07 could land and `CI-06/gate-inventory` would stay green at 29 of 29 with the leg still reading *"waiting, 2026-08-20"*. The only mechanism that catches it fires in the other direction, when [STRATEGY](../testing/STRATEGY.md) is edited first and the register entry becomes a finding for naming no live condition.

**So the smallest activation is six things, and two of them are not code:**

1. **One detector, pure compute, no database: `D-01`.** It is the only candidate whose inputs exist. `fillsReportTable` already emits account, symbol, side and `fill_time`; the window is 2 seconds so it needs no history; and `AS-M7-01`'s counter 1 classifies it as a pattern detector rather than a statistical one, with counter 2 saying same-second clustering is computable the moment fills land. **Every other detector needs something absent**: `D-02`, `D-03`, `D-06`, `D-11`, `D-13` and `D-14` need `daily_marks` and an estimator that does not exist; `D-07`, `D-08` and `D-12` need `identity_signals` rows; `D-04` needs `0039` loaded; `D-09` needs `payout_transfers`; `D-15` and `D-18` need the vendor.

3. **The near-miss.** Section 8 makes it a rule: a detector tested only against a case that should fire proves nothing about its threshold. For `D-01` the near-miss is a same-identity pair, which section 3.4 filters **at the detector**.
4. **A runner and a job in `nightly.yml`**, because `ADR-073` disposition (a) requires a workflow file **and** a top-level job key, and `gates.mjs` resolves both.
