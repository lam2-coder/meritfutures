### 1. The objective

Take [ADR-448](../decisions/ADR-448.md) section 8 item 5. That item says `updateStatementOn` has three guards, that no check asserts they are the whole set, and that the price is *"an assertion over the builder's body, which is a shape this tree does not have anywhere yet."* **The row was open between building it and refusing it on [ADR-450](../decisions/ADR-450.md)'s precedent**, and the binding condition was falsifiability: a control nobody has watched fire is a control nobody should trust, and a green check that cannot fail retires the question falsely.

### 2. What the container arrived with

**NO REPOSITORY.** No checkout, no working tree, no `origin`, no `node_modules`. This is the fifth consecutive row to find it, after [ADR-449](../decisions/ADR-449.md), [ADR-456](../decisions/ADR-456.md) and [ADR-457](../decisions/ADR-457.md). **The dispatch anticipated a shallow-clone refspec problem and that is not the fault that occurred**, which is the same correction row 457 made. The repository was located through the session's own repository listing, attached for push, cloned, and `74381018` resolved in it; `git fetch --depth=200 origin claude/wave-458s` then succeeded on the first attempt.

### 3. What was derived

The guard list, re-derived rather than inherited, and it agrees with the dispatch: **three** on `updateStatementOn`, at the two cited lines the dispatch names and at `:4685`. Beyond it: **nine** `refuse*` declarations file-wide of which only **three** guard a write, and **six** functions that construct an INSERT, UPDATE or DELETE, carrying **five different guard sets between them**. The matrix is already non-uniform, and most of the non-uniformity is deliberate and argued at source.

### 4. The verdict, which is BUILD and why the obvious version was refused

Pinning the three names would have gone red when the update builder changed and **stayed green for the failure item 5 actually names**, because a fourth rule added to a sibling builder leaves that list untouched. The property is the matrix, not the list, and the fourth leg requires every empty cell in it to carry a written reason. **The objection that would have justified a refusal, that such a check breaks on every refactor, was tested rather than assumed**: keyed on names instead of positions, it survives reordering, reformatting and movement within a body.
