### 1. The objective

Make `RI-35` insensitive to formatting without making it insensitive to meaning. Re-derive the premise at source rather than trusting the dispatch or a sibling ADR, **demonstrate the defect firing before repairing it**, price each candidate repair rather than assuming one, say explicitly what the repair no longer catches, and land a case that fails on the old behaviour beside a case that proves the check still fires on a genuine change. **Repair a check; do not weaken a gate to pass it.**

### 2. Done

- **The container measured and reported.** No repository on arrival, seventeenth consecutive row; `/home/user` held `.` and `..`. Cloned with no depth flag, `.git` **202M**, `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile` **11.9s**, exit 0. **`git push` refused `403` by the proxy** until `add_repo` was called with `access: "push"`.
- **The base reproduced at `d915dc14`** on a clean tree before the first edit: `generate` reported *every span already matches its query* and left the tree clean, gates **33 of 33**, invariants **35 of 35**, suite **341 files / 8,264 passed / 10 skipped / 0 failed**.
- **The premise re-derived at source and corrected in two places.** [ADR-415](../decisions/ADR-415.md) section 6 is about the PROBE and not leg 1, and the dispatch's suspicion of leg 1 is refuted by the file's own docblock on the `claim` field.
- **The defect demonstrated firing, twice, by two routes**, in a throwaway `git worktree` and never in the working tree, with the verbatim red recorded and the worktree clean between every seed.
- **The account two files carry found half wrong and measured.** A four-row table of spellings shows the separator comma is the trigger and the line break is not, including a one-line form that fires and a wrapped form that does not.
- **Three candidate repairs priced, two refused with numbers**, and the taken one validated at 1,393 spans before it was trusted.
- **Both punctuation-proxy probes repaired with one shared helper**, and all four caller probes measured identical on the real tree before and after.
- **Seven cases landed**: three that fail on the old behaviour and pass on the new, and four that prove the probe still fires on a genuine install, **all four watched RED under two deliberate over-repairs of the fix itself**.
- **Leg 1's own sensitivity found, demonstrated and refused with its measurement.** **ADR-431 written**, `proposed`, UNSIGNED.

### 3. Not done, and why

- **Leg 1 is not repaired.** ADR-431 section 8. `prettier --write` over the whole format glob changes zero files, whitespace normalisation repairs 0 of 33 anchors, and the only repair with teeth costs the one-line contract legs 2 and 3 stand on. The exposure is named with its numbers so a later row can disagree.
- **`replay-audit-src-caller`'s `reexport` heuristic is not unified with the shared helper**, although the file now carries two mechanisms for one problem. That probe is not broken today and rewriting it is a behaviour change to a third probe nobody asked for. Named as a finding.
- **The value-position proxy is not widened**, which is the honest close of the accident the repair removes. It changes what the probe ASSERTS rather than how it reads formatting, and it was forbidden.
- **[`packages/ledger/src/index.ts`](../../packages/ledger/src/index.ts) lines 148 to 174 are now stale and are not repaired.** Outside this fence and outside every fence the dispatch named; the register's `sweptBy` carries the correction, which is the furthest this row can reach. **No gate reports it**, because leg 1 binds the old path and this artifact registers no needle.
- **`CI-06f` is red at ADR-429 and ADR-430 and is reported rather than repaired**, on [ADR-428](../decisions/ADR-428.md)'s precedent. No gate weakened, silenced or narrowed.
- **`pnpm run verify` and `scripts/corpus/falsify.mjs` were NOT run**, both forbidden by the row.
