### 1. The objective, and the answer

**Row `273` asked whether a door that consults no coverage is right only if every caller remembers, and whether anything enforces it.** It asked rather than asserted, because [ADR-268](../decisions/ADR-268.md) built the split deliberately, and it said in terms that a ruling that the current design is correct and needs only a check would be a complete outcome.

**It is that outcome.** The ruling is [ADR-273](../decisions/ADR-273.md), seven clauses, `proposed` and UNSIGNED.

**The answer is sharper than the question.** The split is safe, and what holds it is a TYPE rather than a memory: `anchorCalendar` hands back a discriminated union from which the day is unreachable without narrowing past an `uncovered` arm. **A consumer that forgets does not compile.**

---

### 2. The row's premise, and the one line that did not hold

The row read the door's own comment as saying that `readLastClosedTradingDay` consults no coverage and that its caller `anchorCalendar` then checks `trading_calendar_loads`.

**The comment says those of two different functions**, at [`scoped-db.ts:3046-3050`](../../packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts):

| Function | Where | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `readLastClosedTradingDay` | `apps/worker/src/batch/adapter.ts` | **none** |
| `lastClosedDay` | `apps/api/src/admin-source/liability.ts` | **its caller `anchorCalendar` reads it** |

`anchorCalendar` has never called the worker's fold. **The splice matters because the two halves answer differently**: one has a compile-time fence and the other has nothing at all, and reading them as one function makes the reassuring half stand for both.

### 4. What is measured, what is derived, what is judged

**MEASURED**, by running and by counting: the seven-file census; `lastClosedDay` unexported with exactly one call site inside `anchorCalendar`; three `anchorCalendar` callers and three `uncovered` guards; the union carrying `anchorDay` on one arm; the refusal returning `anchor_day: '2026-08-28'` on a June-covered estate; the worker adapter and its caller naming no coverage table.

**DERIVED**: that the row's premise is a splice; that the four folds are four statements of one rule.

**JUDGED**: that coverage stays out of `lastClosedDay` (one read shared across three walks beats one read per walk); that a total refusal costs nothing today and that this is a measurement rather than a licence; that the census deserves an `RI-nn`.
