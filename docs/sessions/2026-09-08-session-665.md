### 1. The objective

Take [ADR-468](../decisions/ADR-468.md) section 10 item 1, the thing that entry made worse rather than better, and **build the check**: assert that a case in `packages/tooling/test/` whose input is this repository carries `CORPUS_SCAN_MS`, with a predicate derived from INPUTS rather than from the identifier `REPO_ROOT`, which three of the eight walking files never name.

### 2. Done

- **Measured the base before the first edit, and measured it DIRECTLY.** The container arrived with no `node_modules`. This row edits no existing file, so the base was recovered exactly by moving both new files outside the repository: gates **33 of 33**, invariants **35 of 35**, `write-guard-set.mjs` and `price-register.mjs` **PASS**, suite **347 files, 8713 PASSED, 11 skipped, 8724 TOTAL**. Those suite figures are identical to `ADR-468`'s at its own base, which is evidence rather than a coincidence: everything between the two bases is documents.
- **Re-derived every figure the dispatch handed me and none came out different.** 33 sites, 11 files, 8 of 11 importing, 67 cases (32 plus a `CHECKS` roster read at run time as **35**), and three files that walk while naming `REPO_ROOT` nowhere. `ADR-468` corrected `ADR-466` on three figures; this row corrects `ADR-468` on none, and what it adds is a second derivation sharing no step with the first.
- **Built [`tree-input-budget.mjs`](../../packages/tooling/checks/tree-input-budget.mjs)**, three legs, keyed on a VALUE and never on a name. Leg A throws; leg B is the subject; leg C is the reverse direction, without which leg B is satisfied by pasting the constant onto a fixture case.
- **Watched it fire on a seeded thirty-fourth case and watched it NOT fire on a named read under the tree root**, both located with `git diff -U6`, both confirmed applied before anything was concluded, both restored byte-identical with `git status --porcelain` empty.
- **Wrote 32 cases** that watch every leg fail, every form separately, and each of the six false-positive classes stay out. **Not one fixture in the suite spells `REPO_ROOT`.**
- **Found four parsing defects in my own fold, all of them silent**, and held each open with a case. Two of them were found only because the check was pointed at its own new suite.
- **Found that the grep count of `CORPUS_SCAN_MS` call sites is wrong by four on this branch**, and priced replacing the published derivation command.
- **Did not touch `PRICE_REGISTER.md`**, so `price-register.mjs` is red on this branch with four findings, all this entry's own anchors.

### 5. Landmines

- **A COUNT DERIVED BY SEARCHING TEXT COUNTS TEXT, AND THIS BRANCH IS THE FIRST ON WHICH THAT BITES FOR `CORPUS_SCAN_MS`.** `grep -c` over the fence reports 42 sites where there are 38. Cite `node packages/tooling/checks/tree-input-budget.mjs` instead.
- **THE UNKNOWN DEFAULT IS INCLUSION.** A helper that hands a repository directory to something this fold cannot follow puts its case in the population. That is deliberate, it is what keeps five real cases in, and it will occasionally ask for a budget nothing needs. Leg C is where that shows up.
- **A SIGNATURE DEFAULT IS NOT A USE.** Only helper bodies are read. A helper written like `transcript(checks, root = REPO_ROOT)` that ACTUALLY walked would be missed, and no such helper exists today.
- **`ENUMERATORS` IS A WRITTEN REGISTER OF FIVE PRIMITIVES.** A walk done by `execFileSync('git', ['ls-files'])` alone is invisible; `dependants.mjs` is caught only through its `readdirSync` fallback.
- **THE CHECK READS `packages/tooling/test/` AND NOTHING ELSE.** Every walking suite elsewhere in the workspace is out of scope, including `ADR-466` section 7's eight.
