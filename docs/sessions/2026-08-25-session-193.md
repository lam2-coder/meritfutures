### What was transcribed that nothing checks, stated so it is a record rather than a gap

**Three things below are in [`schema.ts`](../../packages/db/src/schema.ts) because omitting them would be a false statement, and all three are invisible to every assertion in the suite.**

1. **`notification_kinds.mutable` and `rate_limit_exempt` are `GENERATED ALWAYS ... STORED`.** `mutable` is `class IN ('account_state','marketing')` (`SD-M16-01`) and `rate_limit_exempt` is `class IN ('security','money')` (`SD-M16-07`). Both exist as generated columns precisely so a careless write cannot make a money notification silenceable or mark the registration-OTP kind rate-limit exempt, which `INV-M16-11` and `INV-M16-12` are about. **As plain booleans they would compile and satisfy the column-name comparison.**
2. **Two composite primary keys**, `notification_preferences (identity_id, kind, channel)` and `otp_send_budget (scope_kind, scope_key, evaluated_on)`. Both are table-level clauses that `ddlColumns` filters out by design, so a declaration without them would say these tables have no key and nothing would object.
3. **`contact_channels.value_hash` and `value_ciphertext` are `bytea`**, declared through the custom type this file already carries rather than approximated as `text`.

**Column TYPE and NULLABILITY are transcribed and asserted nowhere, on all twenty-six registered tables.** [ADR-094](../decisions/ADR-094.md) section 3 names that gap, states why closing it is its own session, and leaves the `ALTER COLUMN` refusal standing in its place. **This session added five tables to the set that gap covers and closed no part of it.**

### `contact_channels` holds an address reversibly, and that is a property of the table rather than of the accessor

**[ADR-046](../decisions/ADR-046.md) is worth reading before writing a `why` for anything holding a contact address, and it changes what registering this table means.** `value_hash` is a one-way digest; `value_ciphertext` is envelope-encrypted under a key named by `value_key_id` **and not present in this database**; `merit_app` holds table-level `SELECT` and can read the ciphertext and cannot decrypt one. **So a scoped read of `contact_channels` returns the rows and not the addresses**, and the `why` says so rather than leaving a later reader to infer that scoping the table hands out contact details.

The three sealed columns are all NULLABLE and the null is load bearing in two directions: a row written before [`0034`](../../packages/db/migrations/0034_reversible_contact_addresses.sql) has a hash and no ciphertext, and **erasure is a NULL** that leaves the hash, the lineage and the account-takeover evidence standing.
