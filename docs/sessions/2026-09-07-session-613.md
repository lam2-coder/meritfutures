### 1. The objective

Retire the compatibility module [ADR-410](../decisions/ADR-410.md) left at `apps/api/src/events.ts`, if it can be retired honestly, by moving its only consumer onto the producer's home and deleting the shim; and resolve the contradiction [ADR-415](../decisions/ADR-415.md) section 8 recorded, where `apps/api/src/events.ts` and `packages/ledger/src/events.ts` both claim to carry the sentence `RI-35` anchors its `event-sink-caller` claim to. **Both claims were to be derived at source and neither inherited, including from the dispatch.**

### 2. Done

- **The container measured and reported.** No repository on arrival, thirteenth consecutive row. `/home/user` held `.` and `..` and `git rev-parse` answered `fatal: not a git repository`. Cloned by this row: **14.312s real**, **NOT shallow**, `.git` **191M**, working tree excluding `.git` and `node_modules` **59M**. `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile` **8.531s real**, tree clean after it.
- **The base reproduced at `de5f90b5`** on a clean tree, before the first edit: `generate` reported *every span already matches its query* and left the tree clean, gates **33 of 33**, invariants **35 of 35**, suite **337 / 8,163 / 10 / 0**, typecheck exit 0, lint exit 0, `format:check` clean.
- **The consumer set derived by RESOLUTION rather than by grep.** Every relative `import`, `export`, `require` and dynamic `import` specifier in the **771** tracked source files was resolved against the importing file's directory and compared to the target, trying the bare path and the `.ts`, `.tsx`, `.mts`, `.js` and `/index.ts` extensions the resolver would. **One consumer**, and the package-name spelling checked separately and absent.
- **The deletion performed, measured, and restored.** Five populations recorded in ADR-418 section 4, two of them in a package this row may not write.
- **The anchored sentence settled from the register.** `RI-35` leg 1 read at source: it opens the file the `site` names, counts the lines containing the `claim` string, demands exactly one and stores no coordinate. The `site` is `apps/api/src/events.ts`, so that file's claim is true and the producer's is false.
- **The false clause repaired in place at `packages/ledger/src/events.ts`, quoted beside its correction** under `RI-14`'s habit rather than deleted, and the true sentence kept.
- **The marker `RI-35 BINDS THIS COPY` written at the bound copy**, with the reason and the measurement beside it.
- **One case added to `apps/api/test/event-placement.test.ts` and watched RED first**, deriving the bound path out of the register itself.
- **One repair nobody asked for**, the barrel's one-wave-stale sentence about an owed registration ADR-415 has since landed.

### 3. Refused

- **THE RETIREMENT.** `RI-35`'s register names this path as its `site` and leg 1 opens it. The register is `packages/tooling`'s, which this row was forbidden. The refusal is measured in ADR-418 section 4 and the remaining slice is priced in its section 8.
- **MOVING THE CONSUMER ALONE.** It resolves, it was seeded, and it fires the tripwire built to notice it. It was refused because it buys nothing this wave and costs the one assertion that proves the module load-bearing. ADR-418 section 7.
- **EVERY EDIT UNDER `packages/tooling/**`.** The register, its suite and `RI-35` itself were read, seeded against and never written.
- **REPAIRING ANY DATED POINTER** into the old producer path. [ADR-386](../decisions/ADR-386.md) rules those NAMED and never repaired.

### 5. What row 417 needs from this row, in plain terms

**NOTHING IN THE REGISTER CHANGED AND NOTHING IN IT NEEDS TO.** What changed is the prose either side of it:

- `packages/ledger/src/events.ts` **stopped claiming that `RI-35` watches its copy of the sentence**. It keeps the sentence, which is true, and the two false clauses are quoted above their correction rather than deleted.
