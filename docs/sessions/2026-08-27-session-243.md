### 1. The count, recomputed rather than taken from the dispatch

The dispatch said one free letter and said not to trust the figure. Recomputed from [`gates.mjs`](../../scripts/corpus/gates.mjs) and [ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md):

| Reading | Measured |
|---|---|
| Letters the runner implements | `a` through `w` |
| Letters the ALLOCATION table claims | `a` through `w` |
| Implemented and unclaimed, claimed and unimplemented | none in either direction |
| Free in `a` to `z` | `x`, `y`, `z` |
| Free inside `nextFreeLetter()`'s scan | `x`, and it is one |

**The figure is right and its UNIT is what the ruling turns on.** [`falsify.mjs`](../../scripts/corpus/falsify.mjs)'s `nextFreeLetter()` scans `a` to `x` and throws when all are claimed, so the free letter is one letter **inside the harness's scan**, and `y` and `z` sit outside it by construction.

### 2. The letter is not spendable, and that was WATCHED rather than argued

The `CI-06p` seed claims **two past** what `nextFreeLetter()` returns, so one seed consumes `x`, `y` and `z`. A row claiming `x` therefore does not take a name, it takes the falsifier down. Seeded by hand and run:

```
SEED IS STALE       CI-06p  <- seeded: a CI-06 letter claimed two past the last one
      seed anchor exhausted: the "## CI gate identifier allocation" table claims a through x
```

**So answer 1 of the three, spend the letter and retire the series after, was never available.** [ADR-065](../decisions/ADR-065.md) section 5 had already said so; nothing had enforced it.

**And the closure is what makes the harness permanent.** Closed at `w`, `nextFreeLetter()` returns `x` on every future tree and the seed writes `z` on every future tree. That case can no longer go stale.

### 3. The rename was costed and refused on the record rather than on the bill

`grep -roE "CI-06[a-w]\b"` over `.md`, `.mjs`, `.ts`, `.yml` and `.json`: **2,563 citation sites across 283 files, 145 of them session logs and 60 under `docs/decisions/`.**

**The bill alone would not have settled it.** What settles it is that 145 of the 283 are RECORDS. [ADR-118](../decisions/ADR-118.md) rules that a section headed by a date or a session number is a record of a measurement made that day, and `CI-06/derivable-counts` implements it as its third exclusion: rewriting one **makes it say something it did not say**. A log reading *"`CI-06n` passed on this tree"* is a statement about a gate that was called `CI-06n` that day.

**Both endings of a rename are worse than the letters standing.** Sweep the records and the corpus loses the property it exists to have; skip them and it decodes two namespaces forever with the letter table still needed for the old one, which is the whole cost bought and none of the benefit.

**`CI-06n` was not touched**, which is what the fence required. Session 244 owns its body; this session owned the names and changed none of them.

### 4. The mechanism, because a convention nobody enforces is prose

`CI-06/closed-letter-series`, a slug, because the ruling it enforces is why slugs exist.

| # | Assertion | Fires on |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | no `CI-06<letter>` gate id outside the closed set | the artifact |
| **2** | no ALLOCATION letter-table row outside the closed set | **the earliest**: [ADR-034](../decisions/ADR-034.md) rules the claim precedes the artifact |
| **3** | no STRATEGY section 4.4 row outside the closed set | the document a reader consults first |
| **4** | every closed member still claimed by a row | the other direction, which is what keeps the written constant load bearing |

**The closed set is a written string and never computed**, per [ADR-074](../decisions/ADR-074.md) section 2. A set derived from the letters the tree holds admits every new letter on the commit that adds it.

**Assertion 4 is not tidiness.** Three subset assertions over sets that only shrink are all satisfied by a tree that has lost its inputs.

**It is not `CI-06p`.** That gate asks whether the three registries AGREE, and agreement cannot express a closure: a letter claimed in all three places agrees with itself perfectly.

**A gate could see both**, and naming the shape is cheaper than the sweep: a paragraph appearing twice verbatim in one file is `CI-06u`'s question asked of prose rather than of table rows. Not written here; `scripts/corpus/` is fenced to the closure gate this session shipped.
