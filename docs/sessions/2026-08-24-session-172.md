**The consequence for phasing is the part the brief actually asked for, and it is one no module plan can state.** **Ten of the fourteen proposed the same first slice**: their own tables into `schema.ts` and `scope.ts`. `SCOPE_RULES` is total over `TableKey` by a `satisfies` clause, so **that is one piece of work proposed ten times, not ten pieces**, and it is [P3](../plans/P3-ledger-billing-identity.md)'s serial-on-`pnpm-lock.yaml` lesson on a file nobody thinks of as shared. **Whether the transcription is done once for the estate, once per phase, or once per module with a queue is a ruling nobody has taken**, and it is the single decision that most changes how many remaining phases can run concurrently.

**And the second-order consequence, which is why P4 rather than another phase goes next.** The accessor is **read-only**, and **P4 is the only phase whose contents are mostly reads**. Every other phase needs a write: M03 posts, M05 pays, M19 writes a verification row, M06 writes `admin_actions`. P4 renders. So P4 is buildable against the accessor exactly as `ADR-084` left it and the others are not, which is the opposite of what a reader of the aggregate would conclude.
