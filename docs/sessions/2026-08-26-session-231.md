**The precedent for the move is four days old and was found rather than assumed.** [ADR-095](../decisions/ADR-095.md) section 6 re-ruled `CI-07`'s condition into a path the day its `build`-script artifact arrived, and `gates.mjs` records the reasoning inline. ### Landmines

- **`runNightlyBatch` CANNOT DRIVE A WORLD MORE THAN ONE DAY DEEP, AND THIS IS NOT ONLY A DEMO PROBLEM.** `NightlyBatchReport`'s written outcomes carry `row` and `events` and **not `state`**. Day two needs a `RuleState` prior; the report drops it, and `RuleStateRow` cannot rebuild one because of the three missing columns. So the seed calls `foldAccountDay`, whose own comment is the sentence this file is the caller for: *"THE STATE IS RETURNED SO A REPLAY CAN CHAIN ITS OWN PRIOR, and without it INV-04 is not expressible."* **The production consequence is that `loadAccountDay` has no source for `AccountDay.prior` either**, so the adapter `apps/worker`'s header has been waiting for cannot be written against this schema as it stands. Handed on, not repaired: `0048` was held free and the ruling is `packages/db`'s.
- **`falsify.mjs` REPORTS THREE PROBLEMS AND ALL THREE ARE ONE ROOT CAUSE, AND THE TWO EXTRA ONES ARE A PROPERTY OF THE HARNESS NOBODY HAS WRITTEN DOWN.** Measured both ways on this tree: with the `CI-09` row reverted, `falsify` reports **all 30 gates clean and dirty, 70 scope cases, 10 loader cases, zero problems**; with it, **three problems**. One is the clean-phase red. **The other two are `CI-06/gate-inventory`'s own out-of-scope cases** (`playwright-near-miss` and `playwright-peer-declaration-is-not-an-install`), which assert the gate reports **nothing** on a tree that does not violate them and **cannot distinguish "no finding of this kind" from "no finding at all"**. So the day a fail-on-good-news condition fires, the harness also reports that gate's scope evidence broken. **ADR-073's design has a second cost and this is the first run that could see it.**
- **A HASH LEFT UNRECOMPUTED MAKES A FALSIFICATION PROVE THE OPPOSITE OF WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE.** `perturbDemoWorld` moves one cent **and recomputes `state_hash` over the wrong value**. Leaving the original hash would leave the stored bytes AGREEING with the replay, `diffStoredAgainstRecomputed` would return no divergence, and the case would have proved that the audit ignores a field it never reads. Checked by running it: with the hash left alone, **four of the sixteen cases go red**, which is why the suite is worth having.
- **`scripts/demo` HAD ALREADY COMPUTED THIS WORLD EVERY DAY AND THROWN IT AWAY.** [`main.ts`](../../scripts/demo/main.ts) folds three cohorts over 25 weekday sessions through the real engine and [`render.ts`](../../scripts/demo/render.ts) prints a table. Nothing had ever kept what the fold produced. **The seed reuses that population, that seed and that window deliberately**: a second demo population whose divergence from the first nobody would notice is how a fixture stops describing anything.
- **THE R-31 RESTART IS THE NON-OBVIOUS THING A FRESH SEED WOULD GET WRONG.** R-31 resets a funded account's balance to `size_cents` on the day its evaluation passes, the simulator has no notion of a phase, and `INV-18` refuses the next day for every account that ever passed. Without `fold.ts`'s segment restart this world is a world of **evaluations** and the funded half of the engine is never stored, let alone replayed. The restart draws no different trade, because every key in the simulator's rng is `(seed, accountRef, tradingDay, ...)` and none of them is a session index.
- **`scripts/demo/run.mjs` AND `scripts/ci/nightly-harness.mjs` HAVE NO `INDEX.md` ROW**, while `scripts/corpus` and `scripts/db` each have one per file. [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md)'s own convention is *"if a thing is not in INDEX.md, it does not exist"*. The fence held **"your row"**, so one row was added for `world.ts` and the two absences are reported rather than filled.
- **`gates.mjs`'s `covers` PROSE AND ITS PRINTED NOTE BOTH STILL SAY "Four of the six conditions name neither a path nor a manifest key", AND AFTER THIS ENTRY IT IS THREE.** The runner's own computed count line already prints `3 registered as unprobeable` correctly, so **the tree reports two different numbers for one fact in one run**. It is prose rather than an assertion, so no gate reads it; it is the exact hand-maintained count [ADR-034](../decisions/ADR-034.md) exists about, and it goes with the owed probe.
- **`pnpm run verify` DOES NOT EXIT 0 ON THIS BRANCH AND CANNOT**, because it chains `gates` and `gates` is red by design. Every other stage is green on its own and each was run separately: `check:invariants` 9 of 9, `typecheck` clean across all packages plus `scripts/demo`, `lint` clean, `format:check` clean, `vitest run` 135 files and 2,260 passing. **The stop condition asked for both "a RED row you predicted and justified" and "`verify` exit 0", and the two cannot both hold**; this is reported rather than resolved, because the only ways to green the gate are to edit another session's file, to leave the row wording at a string whose register reason had become false, or not to land the world.
