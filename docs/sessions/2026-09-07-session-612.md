### 1. The objective

Take [ADR-415](../decisions/ADR-415.md) section 8's second finding, which is that `RI-35`'s register carries derived figures nobody derives and nothing checks. **The objective is the control and not the two repairs**, though the repairs come with it. Enumerate the population rather than inheriting the two figures ADR-415 measured; re-derive `worker-queue-door-caller`'s figure and `event-sink-caller`'s on this row's own base; and make the arithmetic checkable, watching it fail on a seeded wrong figure before making it pass. The constraint attached to all of it: `RI-35`'s first leg already reddens on a prettier reflow, and a control over derived figures that reproduced that defect would be shipping the thing the row was sent to fix.

### 2. Done

- **The container measured and reported.** No repository on arrival, thirteenth consecutive row. `/home/user` held `.` and `..` and nothing else. Cloned by this row: **18.413s real**, **NOT shallow**, `.git` **191M** and the working tree **59M**. `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile` took **14.132s real** and left `git status --porcelain` empty.
- **The base measured on `de5f90b5`** on a clean tree: `generate` reported *every span already matches its query* and changed nothing, gates **33 of 33**, invariants **35 of 35**, suite **337 / 8,163 / 10 / 0**, typecheck 0, lint 0, `format:check` clean.
- **The population enumerated and the criterion written down.** 20 artifact entries and 33 claims, read off the register rather than counted in the file. **Eleven entries carry a tree-derived figure**, fifteen figures between them, and **five entries carry one that is false**.
- **`worker-queue-door-caller` re-derived on this row's own base**, where ADR-415's finding reproduces: **12 lines over 3 files**, 3 in the declaring module, 8 in the adapter that hands the door to a factory, 1 in the deployable saying no adapter over it has a caller.
- **`event-sink-caller` checked before anything was repaired**, as the row required. This base does **not** hold the *"exactly seven lines"* sentence ADR-414 reports: ADR-415's repair had already landed, so what the entry carried was **"23 line(s) over 7 file(s)" on `0f89c6ac`**, and section 4 is what happened when that was tested.
- **`digest-transport` found wrong, which nobody had named.** Three lines under the instrument its own words state, against a written four.
- **LEG 7 built, and watched RED on a seeded wrong figure before it was made green.**
- **Three entries given a `census` and their integers deleted.** `worker-queue-door-caller`, `event-sink-caller` and `digest-transport`, the last of which is also what proves the census carries an INSTRUMENT and not only a scope, because it reads code where the other two read raw text.
- **Twelve cases added to `packages/tooling/test/absence-claims.test.ts`**, one of which binds the reflow invariance over a fixture where the reflow really does move the derived total.

### 3. Refused

- **A REPLACEMENT INTEGER, IN ALL THREE REPAIRS.** Section 4 is the reason and it is not a preference: the corpus's standing remedy for a stale count was measured failing inside one wave.
- **REPAIRING THE OTHER EIGHT FIGURES.** Four of them are false and all eight are named in [ADR-417](../decisions/ADR-417.md) section 8. **Seven of the eleven name no instrument**, so repairing one means first deciding what reader its sentence meant, and a reader chosen to make a number come out right is the worst way to choose one. ADR-415 refused a second undispatched integer in one diff for the same reason.
- **MAKING LEG 7 MANDATORY.** It would need a check that parses prose to decide which sentences state a figure, which is the defect one layer down and is refused by this file's own header.
- **MAKING AN EMPTY ACCOUNTED PLACE A FINDING.** It would hand row 418 a failure for doing the work it was sent to do.
- **EVERY WIDENING.** No probe changed, no exclusion added, `stripComments` untouched, no needle registered, no marker added to the absence vocabulary.
