- **Landmines:**
  - **ADR-019 is a return to basis-day anchoring, and ADR-013 rejected exactly that on a 40 percent liability argument.** This is not a contradiction and it is very easy to read as one. ADR-013's own calibration note records that the founder's simulation was **basis anchored all along**, so settlement anchoring made realized liability *at most* the modeled figure. The wallet **spends that margin rather than exceeding the model.** Anyone quoting a reserve number from here on is quoting a central estimate with no cushion, and that sentence needs to survive into every reserve conversation.
  - **Two numbers in the corpus disagree by 25 percent and both are on the record.** ADR-018's recalibrated per-day ceiling is about **$240**; Merit Rapid's published parameters imply **30,000c ($300)** on a 3 trading day cycle. I did not pick one. The gap runs in the direction where the model understates extraction, which is the dangerous direction, and it is flagged in ADR-018, M01 AS-03, STATE, and SIMULATION_HARNESS so it cannot be resolved by whoever reads only one of them.
  - **The wallet moved Core EOD and Direct too, which may not have been the intent.** Their cycles compress from 7 to 8 trading days to 5 and their per-day extraction rises from 16,875 to 19,286c up to 27,000c. The ruling was written about Merit Rapid's calibration; the anchor is global. Raised in STATE for confirmation rather than assumed either way.
  - **The publish-time cadence warning now fires on all three plans and means two different things.** With `min_settlement_lag_trading_days` driven to 0, the comparison catches Core EOD and Direct as **co-binding** (gap and win days tie at 5, neither redundant) and Merit Rapid as genuinely **dominated** (1 against 3). The warning text must distinguish them or it reads as three identical false positives and gets dismissed, which is AS-M6-02's failure applied to a publish gate.
  - **The copy-trading ruling removed noise at the query, not in review, and that was the point.** Same-identity clustering is filtered inside D-01 rather than dismissed by an operator. A trader with five accounts on one strategy previously generated ten technically-correct flags a day forever, which is AS-M7-03's attention attack arriving with no attacker. This is safe to do at the query only because the clause makes it a rule rather than a heuristic.
  - **Wallet refunds must never reach a card.** A refund path that returned wallet-funded purchases to an external destination would be a withdrawal route bypassing KYC, name matching, and destination cooling, which is a laundering primitive wearing a refund's paperwork. Written into M03 section 3.4 as a structural absence (no PSP adapter call on that path) rather than a policy.
  - **MID health must be computed against card volume, not total volume.** Wallet purchases carry no chargeback risk, so growing wallet adoption shrinks the denominator while the numerator holds, and a healthy shift would read as deteriorating chargeback ratio and trip failover for no reason.
  - **`V-M2-15` changed the vendor call from a fact-finding exercise into a negotiation.** Fail-closed provisioning means Merit cannot bring accounts online without an acknowledgement artifact or a readable risk setting. That is a commercial precondition, and the call's agenda should be reordered so it is raised first rather than as item fifteen.
