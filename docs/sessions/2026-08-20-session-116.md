### The finding: `L-11` was lifted, and it was never the binding clause on any of these six

WAVE-04 section 2.2 justifies the allocation in one sentence: *"`R-46` to `R-50` are declared and live in `payout/settle.ts`. `settlements` reaches an engine input, which `fixtures/README.md` records as the field whose emptying `L-11` was written for."* **Both halves are true and the conclusion does not follow.** `fixtures/README.md` records, in the same batch that lifted `L-11`, that **`L-11` was the whole blocker on exactly two rows** — `GS-067` and `GS-068`, both written — and was never the binding one on the rest.

**This is the corpus's own recurring shape, one level up.** A correct observation about one clause, carried one unchecked step to a set of rows. [Session 47](2026-08-16-session-47.md) named the pattern, `fixtures/README.md` counts four prior instances of it, and WAVE-04's own section 1 is six claims in a brief that the tree refuted. This is the seventh.

### `GS-241`'s four figures are correct, and its reachable half is already on disk

The objective said the two exact figures are the point of the row and that a disagreement would be the finding. **There is no disagreement.** Computed by hand from `R-44`'s ceiling, `R-50`'s accumulation and `INV-17`'s bound against the two plan records:

| | `payout_cap_cents` | ladder | gross bound | `R-44` trader leg per rung, at `split_bp` 9000 | trader total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Core EOD 50K | 150,000c | 5 | **750,000c** | `(150000 * 9000 + 9999) / 10000 = 135,000c` | **675,000c** |
| Merit Rapid 50K | 100,000c | 5 | **500,000c** | `(100000 * 9000 + 9999) / 10000 = 90,000c` | **450,000c** |

All four match the registry row, and both trader legs divide exactly, so `R-44`'s ceiling adds nothing at the cap and the firm leg is the clean 10%.

**And the one half a fixture could reach is pinned already.** [`GS-067`](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/GS-067-graduation-fires-exactly-on-the-ladder-count.yaml) runs on `MERIT-RAPID-50K`, settles five facts at 100,000c each — exactly `payout_cap_cents` — and its sibling pins `lifetime_settled_cents: 500000`. **That is `GS-241`'s Merit Rapid gross figure, on disk today.** A `GS-241` file would restate it, or restate it at the other size, and in neither case could it state the 675,000c and 450,000c the row spends half its numbers on.
