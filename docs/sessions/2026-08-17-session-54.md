## 2026-08-17 - Session 54: ADR-055, the open end of a trade date's session, and a finding that was narrower than it was recorded (money path)

- **Done:** **[ADR-055](../decisions/ADR-055.md), status `proposed`.** `session_rule` computes a trade date's open as 17:00 CT on the prior calendar day, and it is **wrong by twenty-four hours after a mid-week holiday**: CME opens trade date `2026-09-08` on Sunday `09-06`. Merit's computed session is a strict subset of the real one, so a fill on the Sunday evening of Labor Day weekend lands inside **no Merit session at all**, which is the condition `R-01` exists to detect. The ruling adopts the review desk's recommendation, **narrows the finding it is ruling on**, and amends [ADR-042](../decisions/ADR-042.md)'s `OQ-SE-02` on its mechanism rather than its number. **17 of 17 gates pass.** **No transcription, no `generate.mjs` change, no shape edit to the source file.**
- **Next:** **The shape and its six named rejections land together**, in the session that writes both `cme-2026-2028.source.json`'s widened schema and the `generate.mjs` checks for it. **The holiday list is still the bottleneck** and is unchanged by this session: it is a different page of the same site, it has still not been retrieved as an artifact, and `coverage`, `holidays` and `early_closes` all stay `null` until it is.
- **Blockers:** None taken. `OQ-SE-02`'s factual premise (how far CME publishes) is **deliberately still open** and section 5 says why.
- **Landmines:**

  - **THE FINDING WAS NARROWER THAN IT WAS RECORDED, AND THE TWO ARTIFACTS THAT SHOW IT ARE THE TWO NOBODY RE-READ.** `FINDING 3` generalised from Labor Day and Thanksgiving to a rule about holidays. Parsing the day-before column across **every asset class in all four holiday exports** shows the other two behaving oppositely: Christmas `12-24` carries `12:15 (CLOSED)` and **nothing after it**, and New Year `12-31` carries `16:00 (CLOSED)` and nothing after it, in all nine futures classes. **A mid-week holiday is absorbed; a Friday holiday is not.** The finding was written from the two artifacts that had landed first, and the two that landed later were committed in the same session that wrote it without being read against it. **That is this corpus's own recurring shape for the fifth time**, and this instance is the cheap one: it was caught before a schema was designed around it rather than after.

  - **THE EMPTY EVENING IS EVIDENCE AND IT WOULD HAVE BEEN EASY TO FILE AS MISSING COVERAGE.** A three-day export whose last two columns are blank looks exactly like a window that stopped short. It is not: the format lists evening opens **in the prior calendar day's column**, and it does so for Labor Day and Thanksgiving in files of the identical format. **The absence is the exchange saying the market does not reopen that evening.** Filing it as a coverage limit instead would have left the Friday case unruled and a derived open looking safe, which is the opposite conclusion from the same bytes.

  - **THAT NARROWING IS WHAT KILLS THE DERIVED OPEN, AND WITHOUT IT THE ARGUMENT WAS ONLY A PREFERENCE.** The recommendation "transcribe the bounds rather than derive them" was stated as a judgement about which is easier to get wrong. **The Christmas artifact turns it into a finding**: the obvious derivation, walk back through the run of non-trading days, computes a Thursday `12-24 17:00` open for trade date `12-28`, and the artifact **positively contradicts** it. A correct derivation needs at least three interacting cases. **An argument from taste and an argument from a falsifying artifact read the same in a diff and are not the same thing.**

  - **THE DERIVATION IS KEPT, DEMOTED FROM SOURCE TO CROSS-CHECK, AND THAT IS THE ADDITION THE ARTIFACTS FORCED.** The ruling carries the absorbed session's **close** as well as its open, although the close is independently derivable from `session_rule` and `early_closes`. That is deliberate and it is this file's existing `declared` pattern, whose own note reads "two independent statements of one number is the point of stating it twice". The transcriber states, the generator derives, and **disagreement is a named rejection**. Discarding the derivation entirely would have thrown away a free check on the transcription that motivated the whole ruling.

  - **THE INTRADAY PAUSE IS NOT RECORDED AND THE REASON IS A LANDMINE THIS CORPUS ALREADY OWNS.** Trade date `2026-09-08` halts Monday 12:00 to 17:00. Recording it was tempting and is refused on three grounds: no fill can occur in a `PREOPEN` so the outer span is a **safe superset** for `R-01` (which is `F-3`'s own reasoning for taking the latest close), ordinary days already carry unrecorded pauses (`Grains` shows `07:45 (PAUSED)`), and **a field nothing reads is [session 49](2026-08-17-session-49.md)'s `sessions[].kind` landmine**, recorded there as "the worst outcome available on a money path".

  - **THE COVERAGE QUESTION WAS RESOLVED BY REFUSING TO ANSWER IT.** `OQ-SE-02` rests on a claim about the publisher, and the only evidence for or against it is a **paste**. The calendars README already names ruling from a rendering as the mistake this desk made once this week, so ruling it would have repeated that in the document warning against it. **The mechanism moved instead**: `coverage.to` may not exceed the last date whose exceptions are transcribed from a committed artifact. That is true whatever CME publishes, it makes the premise moot, and it is what `F-4` already implies. **The horizon alarm gains its purpose from it**: at six months against an evidence bound it fires when a retrieval is actually due, where against a declared-but-empty two-year tail it could never fire at all.

  - **THREE ADR NUMBERS WERE ASSIGNED WITHIN AN HOUR AND NONE WAS RESERVED, SO THE FIRST SESSION TO COMMIT PAID FOR ALL THREE.** Claiming `055` alone made `CI-06f` report `053` and `054` as holes. **The gate was right**: it refused a sequence nobody could account for, which is what [ADR-034](../decisions/ADR-034.md) built it to do. The session could not invent the two subjects and stopped to ask. The remedy now in [ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md) is to reserve a number **when it is handed out**, not when the receiving session first commits.

  - **A SIBLING SOLVED THE SAME PROBLEM BETTER WHILE THIS BRANCH WAS SOLVING IT, AND THE MERGE TOOK THEIR ANSWER.** PR #78 reserved `054` and `055` on `main` concurrently. Their `055` row records "reserved, unwritten" and **deliberately does not link `ADR-055.md`**, naming the exact reason this branch had already hit the hard way: `CI-06a` fails on a link to an absent document. **This branch linked it and left `CI-06a` red for one commit; theirs never did.** The conflict was resolved to their three rows on reasons rather than recency, and the process note survived the automerge **twice, byte-identical**, because both sessions were told to record the same thing and wrote the same words.
