**`apps/api/src/surface.ts` has already settled auth's deployment topology, and only its phase was open.** [ADR-083](../decisions/ADR-083.md)'s split chooses a route set at startup over path prefixes, `OPERATOR_PREFIXES` is `['/admin', '/internal']`, and `/auth/*` is neither, so every auth endpoint is on the **public** deployment. What was missing is a phase, which is exactly what the ruling supplies and nothing more.**

**`packages/queue` exists and it is not on auth's path.** Three files, `index.ts`, `job-queue.ts`, `pg-boss-queue.ts`, landed by session 147 under [ADR-086](../decisions/ADR-086.md), with `pg-boss` in the catalog. **Auth needs none of it**: the OTP send is [ADR-039](../decisions/ADR-039.md)'s degrading breaker rather than a queued job, and the seven endpoints are request-scoped. **What auth does need and does not have is the route registry**, and that is the blocker section 8 item 4 of the entry names.

**P3 IS NOT CLOSED, so the cost the brief hypothesized for reading 3 does not exist.** [P3's build plan](../plans/P3-ledger-billing-identity.md) section 6 wave 3 reads *"the six modules, and this plan deliberately does not dispatch it"*, with **no session numbers claimed** and every fence refused on the ground that it would be a path inside a deployable [ADR-083](../decisions/ADR-083.md) had not yet named. Its own title is *"none of P3's six stated contents exists"*. **So reading 3 does not reopen a finished phase. It adds a row to an ordering table nobody has spent yet**, which is the cheapest place in the corpus to put new money-path work.
