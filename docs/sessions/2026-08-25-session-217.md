- **Landmines:**
  - **THE CARD LEG IS NOT `amount_paid_cents` AND THE MISTAKE IS INVISIBLE TO EVERY CONSTRAINT IN THE SCHEMA.** `purchases_wallet_leg_matches_method` guards what goes IN; charging the card the full `amount_paid_cents` on a `mixed` row is a mistake on the way OUT, and ADR-019 says that row is the common case rather than an edge one. **`cardLegOf` is the only producer of a `CardAmountCents` and that is the whole control.** A second producer added later removes it, and the removal will look like a convenience.
  - **`timingSafeEqual` THROWS ON A LENGTH MISMATCH RATHER THAN RETURNING FALSE.** The length guard in front of it is not defensive tidying: without it a well-formed MAC of the wrong size raises a bare `TypeError`, the route's catch block sees something that is not a `WebhookVerificationError`, and **401 becomes 500**. This was found by mutation and had no test until one was written for it.
  - **A NON-FATAL UTF-8 DECODER HIDES BEHIND `JSON.parse`.** The obvious invalid-UTF-8 body fails the parse anyway, so the mutation survived until a body was written that is invalid UTF-8 **and still parses once U+FFFD is substituted**. That event verifies under a tolerant decoder and `payload` then holds a replacement character where `raw` holds `0xFF`. **Two representations of one signed event disagreeing is the shape a dispute is argued from.**
  - **THE NONCE IS NOT A HEADER AND API_CONTRACT SECTION 10 READS AS THOUGH IT IS.** Section 10 lists it beside the timestamp. One of the two real webhook shapes has no nonce channel at all, so the requirement is that the nonce be COVERED BY THE SIGNATURE, satisfied by a header for one shape and by the signed body for the other. **A later reader who takes section 10 literally will add a nonce header requirement and break the compound-header provider.**
  - **`packages/psp` IS MONEY PATH BY SUBJECT AND `CI-06o` CANNOT SEE IT.** `MONEY_PATH_WORDS` is `['rules-engine', 'payout', 'ledger', 'auth']` and the gate recognises a money path by DIRECTORY NAME. **A green `gates` run says nothing about this package**, and the failure mode is silence rather than a red gate. `packages/ledger` will trip the same gate correctly because its word IS in the list, which is precisely why this absence is easy to miss.
  - **THE FRESHNESS WINDOW IS CHECKED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS AND THAT IS A TRADE.** A provider whose clock runs ahead of Merit's by more than five minutes will be refused rather than degrade quietly. The alternative leaves a future-stamped capture valid forever.
  - **`verifyWebhook` REJECTS RATHER THAN THROWING SYNCHRONOUSLY, AND BOTH FAKES CONVERT DELIBERATELY.** `verifyHmacWebhook` throws; the port promises a `Promise`. A future adapter that forgets the `try` around it throws synchronously out of an `async`-looking call, and a caller's `.catch` will not see it. **Nothing in this package enforces the conversion**, and it is the one place a third adapter can get the contract wrong without failing a test.
