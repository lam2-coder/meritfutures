### 6. Is an offer plan configuration under ADR-070?

**Measured first, read second.** [ADR-070](../decisions/ADR-070.md) contains **0** occurrences of `offer` and **0** of `M17`. So do [ADR-071](../decisions/ADR-071.md) and [FOLD-05](../plans/FOLD-05-plan-config-and-designer.md). **ADR-070 does not answer the question, and this session does not answer it either.**

**What the corpus does say, and where.** The line is drawn twice and neither time by ADR-070:

- [M21:46](../plans/M21-plan-designer.md) rows *"Pricing an offer, or discounting a plan"* to **M17**, on the reason that *"an offer prices a plan version and never edits one. M21 is the other side of that line and owns the version."*
- `INV-M17-01` names the three objects an offer may not write: `plan_versions`, `plan_version_sizes`, `accounts.plan_version_id`.

Read together, an offer is **outside** ADR-070: ADR-070's subject is the plan version and its publication, and an offer may not touch either. **But that reading is assembled from a module plan and an invariant, not taken from the ruling**, and section 3 finding 7 shows the invariant has no enforcement in the tree.

**And the converse is already live and unruled, which is the sharp edge.** ADR-070 section 2 rules fee-back credit *"a configuration and a trigger"*, three fields on the plan version. Its implementation, [`0044`](../../packages/db/migrations/0044_fee_back_and_ladder_unlock.sql), **ALTERs `promotional_credit_grants`, which is `SD-M17-03`'s table**, and adds `plan_versions.fee_back_repeats`. `0044:84-85` states the problem in its own words: *"A FEE-BACK CREDIT IS ISSUED BY A PAYOUT, BY THE PLAN VERSION, NOT THROUGH M17'S ISSUANCE PATH. So it is outside the only control that bounds every other credit source."* **The corpus has already met the case where plan configuration issues an M17 instrument, and it resolved it by locking the flag rather than by ruling the boundary.**

**Three further facts the founder should have in front of them.**

1. **`price_floors` is the harder half and nobody has classified it.** The [admin-parity audit:36](../reviews/2026-08-20-admin-parity-audit.md) counts `price-floors` as **configuration**, in the same list as `plans/:planId/versions` and `plans/versions/:versionId/publish`. But ADR-070 section 1's ruling attaches to a **status transition** on `plan_versions`, and `price_floors` **has no status**: it is effective-dated, nothing supersedes it, nothing revokes it, and there is no publication boundary for a validation receipt to attach to. If a floor is plan configuration, ADR-070's mechanism does not reach it. If it is not, nothing in the corpus says what constrains it.
2. **ADR-070 section 2's own configuration is unvalidated.** `grep -rn 'fee_back' packages/ --include=*.ts` returns **nothing**. [`validate.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/plan/validate.ts) checks `phase_funded`'s drawdown, daily loss limit, win days, consistency, cadence gap and split at `:159-210` and never `fee_back`. Of ADR-070 section 2's three fields, only `repeats` was materialized, and it was materialized in order to be **locked off**. **The trigger ordinal and the amount are blob keys with no validator**, which is the exact hole ADR-070 section 1 says it is closing.
3. **The delivery order puts the credit before the engine that governs it.** [DELIVERY_PLAN:50](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md) scopes M17 to *"minimal"*, `SHOULD`, and `:147` puts *"M17's experiments, bundles, and promotional credit"* **post-launch**, with reset pricing in P4. Fee-back credit is not post-launch: it is a plan-version feature, and `fee_back_repeats` is locked while `enabled` is not. So a plan can be configured to issue promotional credit **before M17 exists**, with no issuance cap (finding 12), no floor path, and nothing that consumes it at checkout.
