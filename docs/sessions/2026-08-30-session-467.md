### 1. The objective, and the answer

**Row `276` asked whether `effective_from` being a `date` in six tables and a `timestamptz` in three is a defect or a correct distinction spelled the same way, and forbade a migration, a reserved number and an `RI-nn` in advance.**

The ruling is [ADR-276](../decisions/ADR-276.md), ten sections, `proposed` and UNSIGNED.

**It is a correct distinction and the nine are right as they stand.** The row offered that as a complete outcome and it is the one nine sites support. What the row did not anticipate is that the estate already discloses the difference in six of the nine places, and that it does so because a gate compels it rather than because anybody chose to.

### 3. The rule that was chosen, and the three that were discarded

| Candidate | Fits | Where it breaks |
|---|---|---|
| config is a `date`, an operator's act is a `timestamptz` | 8 of 9 | `price_floors` is configuration by content and an act by grain |
| a table with a `version` column dates in days | 7 of 9 | `contract_specs` and `geo_restrictions` have none and are both `date` |
| `effective_from` in the primary key means `timestamptz` | 8 of 9 | `contract_specs` puts a `date` in its key |
| **two supersessions inside one day must be distinguishable** | **9 of 9** | -- |

**A rule that fits eight of nine is a rule with an unexplained exception**, and an unexplained exception is where the next session goes wrong. The winning rule explains all three near-misses rather than surviving them.

### 4. What was written, and the one thing deliberately not written

[data-model/README](../architecture/data-model/README.md) section 1 gains a `Temporal columns` block: the three-token vocabulary with its definitions, the rule that a `timestamptz` row declares no unit, [ADR-272](../decisions/ADR-272.md) clause 3's silent-may-false-may-not rule, and [ADR-276](../decisions/ADR-276.md) clause 4's narrowing. Its Types bullet now points there instead of leaving `date` looking like one thing.

All nine design records carry the disposition in the `effective_from` row's own `Why` cell. Two of the three `timestamptz` rows had an **empty** cell before this.

**No `**Unit:**` marker was written on any `timestamptz` row**, and that is the decision most worth attacking. `CI-06m` reads that marker on `date` rows only, so one written elsewhere is checked by nothing and is indistinguishable from six that are. A declaration nothing reads is prose wearing a control's clothes.
