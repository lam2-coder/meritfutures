### 1. The objective

[ADR-430](../decisions/ADR-430.md) section 12 item 1: `breaker` is the cheapest next family and costs more than `recon` did. **Re-derive that claim rather than trust it**, perform the blast radius rather than predict it, and spend [ADR-303](../decisions/ADR-303.md) limit 4 on the family by showing **one hand-written mapping actually deleted**. Keep every runtime check. Price what the narrowing makes harder. Confirm the narrower fence that entry's section 8 argued for, and stop and report rather than widen if it turns out `packages/db/**` is needed. A refusal with the measurement would have been a full delivery.

### 2. Done

- **The container measured and reported.** No repository on arrival, eighteenth consecutive row. `git push` refused `403` by the proxy twice and succeeded after `add_repo` with `access: "push"`; the repository was **not** pre-checked, because the dispatch records that `git ls-remote` returns `404` for a private repository and would have misled.
- **The base reproduced at `9eb52656`** on a clean tree before the first edit, and **it is green**: gates **33 of 33**, invariants **35 of 35**, suite **341 files / 8,272 passed / 11 skipped / 0 failed / (8,283)**, typecheck, lint and `format:check` clean. The two `RI-35` timeouts [ADR-430](../decisions/ADR-430.md) section 2 recorded did NOT reproduce, on a 124-second run, which confirms its load-dependent-flake diagnosis rather than contradicting it.
- **The cheapest-next claim re-derived at source**, every `ports.ts` under `apps/worker/src/` read for limit 4's verbs and every read table counted against `schema.ts` by a brace-matching walker calibrated against two of ADR-430's own hand-checks and one of this row's. ADR-432 section 3 is the table and section 3.1 is what the sibling got right and wrong.
- **The blast radius PERFORMED**, the port moved alone before the mapping was touched, and the zero in the only consumer recorded as the argument for the deletion.
- **`BreakerRow` and the cast deleted**, the four readers made generic over the row so `tsc` checks every column NAME, `foldWindow` and `latestPrevious` typed to the rows their keys declare, the adapter's single cast priced, and the barrel's export removed with the leg count derived rather than typed.
- **The row-shape refusal KEPT**, as `requireRow`, against both precedents, with the argument written beside it and a case watching it.
- **Three cases added as section 9 of `breaker.test.ts`, each watched failing**, two RED on the base tree and the third RED under two separate deliberate neuterings.
- **The suite's fixture store made to hold `DeclaredRow`**, at a cost of 71 columns no case reads, priced in ADR-432 section 9.

- **The two wrong `detectors` read-table counts in ADR-426 and ADR-430 were not edited.** Both are dated entries and neither is this row's.

### 4. Blockers

- **None for this row.** The `E2` read is OWED and is not a blocker on the work; the entry ships `proposed` and UNSIGNED and nothing here was signed.
- **`detectors` is the only unblocked family left** and is roughly 2.4 times this row's size with three consumers rather than one. Whether one row should take it at all is a dispatch question.

### 5. Landmines

- **`requireRow` LOOKS LIKE DEAD CODE AND IS NOT.** Under the narrowed port its argument is already typed as a row, so a reviewer may read `typeof value !== 'object'` as unreachable and delete it. **The suite will be green on every case but one.** ADR-432 section 5 is the argument and it rests on `ADR-112` foreclosure 4 rather than on taste.
- **A SUBSTRING IS NOT AN ASSERTION ABOUT A GUARD.** `toContain("typeof value === 'bigint'")` passes on a guard WIDENED to admit `number`. Any case in this corpus asserting a refusal by a fragment of its condition has the same defect.
- **`breaker/ports.ts` and `breaker/evaluate.ts` are cited BY LINE from seven documents**, none of them in this fence. The citations survive only because `RI-16` excludes dated headings. A row that edits these files and a live document in the same wave should check the shape of the citing set, not its size.
- **`breaker-adapter.test.ts:556` reads `ports.ts` as text** and pins `insert`'s signature verbatim. A row narrowing the write path must decide about that case first.
