### 1. The objective, and what the re-derivation changed

**Row `275` asked for the leg that reads the live `CHECKS` array against the `RI-nn` register in [ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md), in both directions, plus a title-drift leg.** It handed over facts already verified and required them re-derived anyway.

They held. **One got sharper.** The row said nothing reads the register. The sharper statement is that the SAME DOCUMENT holds four allocation tables and `gates.mjs` gates three of them:

| Table | What reads it |
|---|---|
| ADR numbers | `gates.mjs`, through `ADR_ALLOCATION` |
| Migration numbers | `gates.mjs`, through `MIGRATION_ALLOCATION` |
| `CI-06` letters | `gates.mjs` `CI-06p`, through `LETTER_ALLOCATION` |
| **The `RI-nn` invariant register** | **nothing** |

**The letter table is an exact precedent** and its own comment in the runner says so: *"for a week nothing read it"*, and *"the table that was written to end three plan documents hand-maintaining one sequence was itself the one registry with no gate over it, and it said so in its own words."* The invariant register said so in its own words too, at [ALLOCATION:523](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md): *"THE ROWS ARE READ OUT OF `repo-invariants.mjs`'s `CHECKS` ARRAY, NEVER TYPED."*

### 2. What it found on its first run

| Finding | Why nothing else could see it |
|---|---|
| **`RI-11` has no register row, and never had one** | It is a member of `CHECKS`, imported from `ui-server-endpoints.mjs` on [ADR-138](../decisions/ADR-138.md) ruling 3. A prose paragraph below the table describes it, and prose beside a list is not a derived span |
| **`RI-08`'s title had drifted** | The cell carried the EMPTY-`DB_ADMITTED` branch of a COMPUTED title. It stopped being true at [ADR-120](../decisions/ADR-120.md) |
| **`RI-18` carried no title** | Its cell states the subject in the check's own vocabulary. That title is computed from `SUBJECTS` |
| **`RI-27` carried no title** | The cell says the title was copied out of the live array. No title appears in the row |
| **`RI-28` carried no title** | Same sentence, same absence, written one wave ago |

**The two kinds fail differently and that is the finding worth carrying forward.** A missing row is a gap somebody could notice by counting. **A row that asserts its title was read out of the array while carrying none reads as compliant**, and no amount of careful reading finds it.

### 4. What was refused

**A dispatch control.** Nothing in this repository can read a prompt handed to a session, the three sessions of wave BA each read a note that was true at the moment they read it, and a check that fired afterwards would report a renumber the rows already record better. [ADR-275](../decisions/ADR-275.md) section 6 rules it rather than inventing a control nobody runs, on [ADR-274](../decisions/ADR-274.md)'s precedent.

**An exemption list inside the check.** `RI-17` would have been the one entry in it. That list is the second copy this register exists to end.

**Equality on the title leg.** It would rewrite twelve rows in a frozen document to make one check simpler. Containment was taken and section 8 of the entry states what it admits.

### 5. Landmines

1. **`RI-08`'s and `RI-18`'s titles are COMPUTED**, so a growth in `DB_ADMITTED` or `SUBJECTS` moves the register. That is `RI-29` working, and the fix is to copy the new title out of the array in the same commit as the list change.
2. **The home-link admission is a text read.** A row could link any source file that mentions the id. Stated in the entry's section 8 rather than implied.
3. **[STATE](../STATE.md) carried its `The gate that closed` sentence twice**, landed by session 465's merge and repaired here. It is `RI-12`'s exact subject at a count of 2 against a threshold of 8, so no gate could see it. A reader who wants that threshold lowered should say so in a row.
