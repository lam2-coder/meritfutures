## 2026-08-20 - Session 99: ADR-070, plan configuration and the four gaps (non-money, and it rules a money-path document)

- **Done:** **[ADR-070](../decisions/ADR-070.md) is written, `status: proposed`, approval line unsigned.** [FOLD-05](../plans/FOLD-05-plan-config-and-designer.md) section 4's four gaps are ruled, and so are the two open questions the gap list does not contain: `OQ-F5-01` on constraining `plan_versions.rules`, and `OQ-F5-04` on whether the marketed label is versioned. **Contract limits are picked rather than deferred.** 22 of 22 gates pass. **Nothing was folded**, per the brief.
- **Next:** **The publication constraint in section 1 is owed a migration and has none.** It is a money-path schema change under [ADR-003](../decisions/ADR-003.md) and belongs to its own session. `ADR-071` and `M21` are the sibling session's and are not decided here.
- **Blockers:** ADR-070 is unsigned. It rules the shape of the document every plan is sold under and assigns Merit an ownership it cannot yet transport, so it is E2's.
- **Landmines:**

  - **"COLUMNS OR BLOB" IS A FALSE PAIR AND TAKING IT AT FACE VALUE COSTS THE THING BEING BUILT.** `OQ-F5-01` reads as a schema-tidiness question and is not one. A `jsonb` rule set is what lets a plan shape change without a migration, and `M21` exists to make plan design an in-product experiment; **an experiment that needs a migration per shape is not one.** The defect is real all the same: a malformed blob written by anything other than [`validate.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/plan/validate.ts) is accepted by the database. **The ruling puts the constraint on the STATUS TRANSITION rather than on the shape** -- no row reaches `published` without a validation receipt -- which is the seam [ADR-014](../decisions/ADR-014.md) already chose one layer down, in its own words: fail at publish time instead of at settlement time.

  - **THE CONTRACT-LIMIT DECISION TURNS ON WHOSE LIABILITY IT BOUNDS, AND THAT IS NOT WHERE THE QUESTION LOOKS.** It reads as a vendor question, and half of it is. But the auto-liquidation setpoint caps the **trader's** loss at the floor and does **not** cap the **firm's** below it: a position gapping or slipping through closes beneath the setpoint. **The corpus already contemplates exactly that** -- `GS-087` is "day low below the floor with no liquidation record" -- and [M02:326](../plans/M02-rithmic-bridge.md) states the exposure in one sentence: "Merit's entire intraday risk posture is one number pushed to the vendor." **A parameter whose risk lands on Merit but whose value the platform sets is a control Merit cannot price.**

  - **OWNERSHIP IS RULABLE NOW AND TRANSPORT IS NOT, AND CONFLATING THEM IS HOW THIS PARAMETER REACHED A FREEZE WITH NO HOME.** Whether `set_risk` accepts a contract limit is a **fact about the vendor** that no ruling can establish, and [M02](../plans/M02-rithmic-bridge.md) is `review` under [ADR-005](../decisions/ADR-005.md) pending that call. So the ADR rules ownership, states plainly that transport is not ruled, and leaves `GS-311` to pin whichever answer the call produces. **Deferring the whole question to the call is what "out of scope" would have meant, and for a prop firm's position limit that is an unpriced liability rather than a scope decision.**

  - **THE CHEAPEST GAP IS CHEAP FOR A STRUCTURAL REASON AND THE AUDIT SAYS SO RATHER THAN PROPOSING A RULE.** `promotional_credit` is an **existing** ledger class, outside the withdrawable set, posted by `LT-08` only. So the referral's "withdrawable until earned" constraint **cannot be violated by construction**: the credit posts into a class the withdrawable calculation does not read. **A constraint that holds by construction is worth more than the same constraint holding by discipline**, and no invariant or gate has to be written for it.

  - **FOUR OF THE SEVEN GOLDEN SCENARIOS ARE OFF CASES, DELIBERATELY.** `GS-306` is the one worth naming: a fee-back rule that fires at `N` on a plan configured to zero and posts a zero-value ledger row is a real defect in a double-entry system -- a journal entry asserting that nothing happened -- and **the off case is the one nobody writes**. All seven were checked against the registry before being cited, because `CI-06d` resolves every `GS-nnn` in the corpus and a cited scenario that does not exist fails the gate rather than the reader.

  - **THE RESERVATION ROW COULD NOT BE AMENDED IN PLACE, WHICH IS [ADR-065](../decisions/ADR-065.md) T3 UNMET FOR A FENCE REASON.** `ALLOCATION`'s `070` row still reads "Reserved, unwritten" and unlinked, because `docs/decisions/ALLOCATION.md` is not in this session's fence. T3 says a landed reservation is amended in place rather than joined by a second row; **the amendment is owed and this log is where it is recorded**, so the next session that holds that fence does not add a second row instead.

### What was ruled, and what was deliberately not

**Ruled:** the blob stays and publication is constrained; fee-back is a config and a trigger over an existing class; a ladder unlock reads the **hard-merged** identity; the marketed size label is **versioned with the plan version**; contract limits are **Merit-owned**.

**Not ruled, and each says why in the ADR rather than going quiet:** the mechanism of the publication receipt (a schema question, ADR-003), the vendor transport for a contract limit (a fact about Rithmic), and `M21`'s admission (ADR-071's, a separate approval).
