### 6. What could not be determined

- **Who WRITES `balance_reflection_status = 'observed'`.** The columns are on `payout_requests`, which is M05's table; `DEP-M5-02` puts the observation on M2. The corpus names the observer and not the writer, and that decides which slice owns `AS-M5-01`'s counter.
- **Where `M5-4`'s ledger posting lives.** A new `packages/ledger`, a directory inside `packages/db`, or a module inside `apps/api` are all consistent with what is written. `RI-04` forbids an app depending on an app and says nothing about this. It is an `ADR-083`-shaped ruling that nobody has been asked for.
- **Whether `POST /admin/accounts/:accountId/freeze` needs a contract field for the 48 hour clock.** `FreezeRequest` has none and the prose names no clock; a server-side default is a defensible reading of `SD-M5-01` and I found no ruling either way.
- **Whether a probe is money path.** `M5-3` is marked YES on the reasoning that its subject is the payout table's enforcement, and [session 135](README.md)'s precedent folded a probe into its migration's own label. **A probe with no migration beside it has no precedent in this corpus** and the marking above is a judgment, stated as one.

### Landmines

- **THE SCHEMA BEING FINISHED IS THE DANGEROUS PART, NOT THE REASSURING PART.** Nine deltas, eleven CHECK constraints, seven indexes, two widened predicates and a probe that watches five refusals fire. **Every one of those reads as a control and not one of them has ever refused a row written by Merit**, because no code writes to these tables.
