### 1. The objective

Convert *"these never run"* into *"these never run, and here is exactly what happens when they do."* Derive the population of cases that CI skips honestly rather than inheriting the dispatch's figure, stand PostgreSQL up locally on the repository's own precedent, apply the migrations forward-only under `ON_ERROR_STOP`, run the never-run cases against a real backend, and record whatever comes back. **Fix nothing, skip nothing, wire no CI.**

### 2. Done

- **The container measured and reported.** No repository on arrival; `/home/user` held `.` and `..`. Cloned by this row at `--depth 1`, `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile` **14s**, exit 0.
- **The base reproduced at `bfd6c400`** on a clean tree before the first edit: `generate` reported *every span already matches its query* and left the tree clean, gates **33 of 33**, invariants **35 of 35**, suite **341 files / 8,246 passed / 10 skipped / 0 failed**. **The dispatch's baseline triple reproduces exactly.**
- **The population derived by running the suite** with `--reporter=verbose` and reading the ten `↓` lines, rather than by grepping the tree. **9 database-conditioned, 1 not.** ADR-428 section 3 is the table.
- **Seven conditional run/skip sites found where the dispatch named four**, and all three it missed evaluate to RUN, so the site count is corrected and the skip set is not.
- **PostgreSQL 16.13 stood up and 75 of 75 migrations applied forward-only from empty**, on `CI-06h`'s own loop verbatim, in **4.18s**, zero errors. `CI-06h`'s object-count assertions re-run against it and agreeing at **118 tables / 32 triggers**, both directions.
- **The never-run cases run.** `pool-executor.test.ts` **4 of 4 green**, reproducing [ADR-332](../decisions/ADR-332.md)'s hand-run exactly. `admin-writes.test.ts` **3 of 5 green, 2 RED**. Verbatim transcript in ADR-428 section 6.
- **The two REDs diagnosed to one cause at `file:line`**, a `23503` on `admin_actions_actor_is_an_operator`, and the constraint-ordering that lets the three refusal cases still pass **measured through `psql` rather than reasoned about**.
- **[ADR-237](../decisions/ADR-237.md) read at source and found to have met this exact failure on the day it landed**, repairing the two `.sql` callers `CI-06h` reaches and missing the one `.ts` caller nothing runs. ADR-428 section 8.
- **The repair sized on a separate database**, one `operators` row per actor, **32 of 32 green**, so the transcript of the RED stands reproducible.
- **The cost of wiring CI derived by running the whole suite with `DATABASE_URL` set**: `8,246 / 10 / 0` becomes `8,252 / 2 / 2` over an unchanged 8,256 cases. **ADR-428 written**, `proposed`, UNSIGNED.

- **A hand-run is a measurement with no expiry date on it.** Both blocks carry a record of having been run; one of those records is 28 migrations stale and nothing went red when it aged.
- **Setting `DATABASE_URL` in CI-02 would switch a passing case OFF**, silently, because a skip reports as a skip and the stage stays green.
- **Three of the five admin-writes cases pass one layer shallower than the assertion they carry.** `NOT NULL` and `CHECK` fire before a referential trigger, so a refusal case can be green against a database that refuses everything.
