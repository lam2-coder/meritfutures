### 1. The starting measurement, which is the same finding twice running

**56 of 56 green before a line changed.** Session 377 section 6 inverted both of B1's
clearing-condition assertions in the diff that lifted the blocker, exactly as session 374's

So this is the second consecutive dispatch written to read a red that the lifting session

### 2. The horizon, and why "next 7 days" is not one question

`eligible_next_7d` is two folds and only one of them was ever blocked by B1.

| Fold | State |
|---|---|
| The next seven TRADING days | **BUILT.** `readTradingHorizon`, executed live |
| Which accounts clear their gates on each of them, and for how much | **B5.** No source |

The first is worth having on its own and the difference it makes is measurable rather than
rhetorical. Anchored on `2026-11-24`:

- seven **calendar** days ends `2026-12-01`
- seven **trading** days ends `2026-12-04`

Three days apart on one ordinary week, because `2026-11-26` is Thanksgiving and two weekends
fall inside the run. `AS-06` is the rule that makes the difference matter: any counter
published in trading days must be rendered as a date "or the firm has published a rule its
own traders cannot evaluate".

**No day is computed anywhere.** Every returned day is a `trading_calendar` row, filtered to
`is_holiday = false` and taken in ascending order. `is_half_day` and `halted` are carried and
neither removes a day, on `0004`'s own B4 #2 and #3.

raises**."

### 4. B5, at its primary sources, and it is not one leg

**LEG 1. Six gate groups decide eligibility and exactly one publishes a forward-looking
date.** `cadenceGap.nextEligibleTradingDay` is `AS-06`'s resolved date. `tradedDays`,
`winDays`, `buffer`, `consistency` and `minimumAmount` clear only when the trader TRADES, so
nothing stored says when.

That one date lives in `rule_states.engine_gates`, and **nothing in this tree writes it**.
`writeRuleState` is a port; its only implementations are a test double and
`scripts/demo/world.ts`, which refuses in terms. Its stored shape is declared by no primary
source: `EngineGateResults` types every cents member `bigint`, which JSON cannot carry;
API_CONTRACT carries no `engine_gates` shape at all; and `0015`'s own column comment names
eight gates where the engine produces six.

**The contrast with `integrations.batch` is what makes this precise.** ADR-199 clause 4 could
rule the batch's two figures readable off an event nothing has emitted, because EVENTS
section 5 **declares** that body in the approved catalogue. Nothing declares this bag.

**LEG 2. `R-37` counts by `sequence` subtraction and no such column exists.** ADR-199 section
6's other route is to recompute the gate from `plan_versions.rules`, `accounts` and
`rule_states`, all of which are real columns on registered tables. It ends at
`CalendarDay.sequence`: the engine takes its slice from a port the caller supplies, `0004`'s
CREATE body declares no such column, no `ALTER TABLE trading_calendar ADD COLUMN` exists in
any of the 59 migrations, and the seed assigns none. The only substitute is the date
arithmetic `R-02` forbids.

**The group goes whole or not at all**, on B2's stated reason. Producing only the accounts
eligible today would understate `total_cents`, and that is the figure the payout wallet is
funded against.

### 5. The assertion that was wrong, and the census that could not carry it

The first draft of leg 2 asserted `migrationColumnNames()` does not contain `sequence`. **It
went red.** `0050` declares `live_account_state.sequence`, a live tick ordinal that is
"1-based PER ACCOUNT PER DAY", which is a different figure from a dense index into the
exchange calendar.

**A census over 735 column names cannot make a claim about one of 114 tables.** The
assertion is table-scoped now, over `0004`'s CREATE body folded across every
`ALTER TABLE trading_calendar` in the tree, and the near-miss is pinned beside it so the
estate-wide reading cannot come back.

### Landmines

- **LIFTING A BLOCKER IS NOT THE SAME AS UNBLOCKING A FIELD.** B1 and B5 sat on the same five
  leaves and only B1 had ever been looked for. A blocker list is a list of what somebody
  checked, and the count it holds is a floor.
- **A CLEARING CONDITION IS SPENT BY THE SESSION THAT LIFTS THE BLOCKER**, for the third time
  running. Sessions 372, 377 and this one; sessions 374 and 380 were each dispatched to read
  a red that no longer existed.
- **`AdminReadError` ALONE CANNOT TELL TWO CONTROLS APART.** A `rejects.toThrow(Class)` case
  passes against any refusal on the path, including one several lines further down. Where two
  controls can refuse the same row, pin the message.
- **A CENSUS OVER THE ESTATE CANNOT MAKE A CLAIM ABOUT ONE TABLE.** `sequence` exists and is
  not the `sequence` this blocker is about.
- **THE CALENDAR IS BOUNDED AND `trading_calendar` IS NOT PRUNED.** `OQ-SE-02` puts coverage
  at the current year plus two, so the horizon's whole-table read is bounded in what it can
  ANSWER for and not in what it SCANS. `TradingHorizonCost` is what makes that visible.
- **`liability_snapshots` HAS NO `trading_day` COLUMN.** Any future reader of this response
  that needs a day has to resolve the instant through the calendar, and taking the instant's
  UTC date is the error `day()` refuses one value at a time.
