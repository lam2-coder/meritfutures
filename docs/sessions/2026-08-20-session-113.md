  - **THE TEN UNREGISTERED `OI` NUMBERS ARE THE STATED SCOPE WORKING AS STATED, NOT A LAPSE.** `OI-16` onward were opened by sessions about engine purity, gate coverage, lint scope and the fixture inventory. **None of that is a schema-delta manifest's namespace**, so the argument that put the table in `packages/db/` is the same argument that keeps those ten out of it. **A register whose declared subject excludes 40 percent of its series is not that series' register.**

  - **"ROUGHLY FORTY SERIES" DOES NOT REPRODUCE AT ANY GROUPING THE COMMAND PERFORMS.** [WAVE-04 section 1](../plans/WAVE-04-fixture-backlog-and-gate-inventory.md)'s own command returns **215** rows; folding the module scope in gives **67**; discarding the 14 module-first prefixes and the 3 non-identifiers gives **50**. **Nothing in the wave depends on which is used**, and it is corrected so the next session types a measured number. `OI`'s 220 became 271 for a reason worth keeping: **27 of the new occurrences are inside WAVE-04 itself.** An occurrence count is a fact about a moment, which is why no assertion in the gate may rest on one.

### What was ruled, and what was deliberately not

**Ruled:** a definition site is a **table row or a heading that LEADS with the identifier, inside a declared register**, one per member, everything else a citation; the register is a **file or a file and one `##` section**, must be a corpus document or a package README, and may sit anywhere in the repository rather than under `docs/`; the scope is **written, never computed**; **118 series and 1,086 members** are in scope on arrival and the gate **passes**; four exemption classes carry an argument each; **44 pending series are named rather than exempted**, in a register that fails when an entry stops naming a defect; and **`OI`'s allocation moves to `ALLOCATION.md`**, superseding `DELTA_MANIFEST` section 16.

**Not ruled, and each says why in the ADR rather than going quiet:** `OI-06`'s double allocation (a disposition question about two live findings, belonging to the session that moves the table), the `OQ-M*` convention (a question about how rulings are recorded, 20 series), and the repair of any of the 44 pending series (this ADR counts them; it does not fix them).
