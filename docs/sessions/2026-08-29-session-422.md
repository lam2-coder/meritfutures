**Objective.** Build the withdrawal approval transition that does not exist and
put the dual-control threshold on it, because those are the same edge.

**Outcome.** **Built, guarded, bounded, and the edge turned out not to be the
one the row was worried about.** The approval transition exists and is dual
controlled above `500000` integer cents on an operator's hand alone. It does
not end a lockout, it cannot, and the reason is one line of the enum.

### 2. What was ruled

**The trader arm takes no dual control at any amount**, on three grounds, of
which the first is decisive: there is no first hand for a second hand to check.

**An operator's hand takes it at or above `500000`**, and `approved_by` is the
column that tells the hands apart, so the control is written over what a
database row actually carries rather than over a role or a session kind.

**The `cooling` arm carries every term of `G-WITHDRAWAL-CLEARED` and the clock
as well.** The guard table gives it only the clock, which read literally lets a
withdrawal leave `cooling` with KYC expired and the identity restricted. **A
guard that gets weaker the longer you wait is a queue an attacker joins.**

**A halted withdrawal is not approved**, fail closed, on the ground that
approval is where `LT-06` moves the balance into the firm's obligation. **No
approved document rules it** and it is one branch of one function to overturn.
