### 1. What the slice turned out to be, which is not what the reservation says

**The word "catalogue" names two different objects in this corpus and the reservation picked the wrong one.**

| | [ADR-141](../decisions/ADR-141.md)'s catalogue | `P5-d`'s catalogue |
|---|---|---|
| **The object** | `plans`, `plan_versions`, `plan_version_sizes`: the thing a trader buys from | [EVENTS](../architecture/EVENTS.md): the registry of event names |
| **Where it lives** | three tables, registered `firm` at [`scope.ts:828`](../../packages/db/src/scope.ts) | one `approved` markdown document |
| **What the ruling was about** | a trader-scoped response naming a catalogue row, and which side of the join may hold a filter | on what authority a name becomes a row with a payload |
| **Code in the fence** | [`catalog.ts`](../../apps/api/src/routes/catalog.ts), its test, one wiring line | **none** |

**So three of the dispatch's instructions are unreachable rather than satisfied.** The BOLA pairing has no identity-scoped read to run against, the scope-class warning has no read to inspect, and the *"start the process and exercise anything you added"* step has nothing to exercise: the deliverable is prose. The process was started anyway, once, for the one claim it could settle, and section 4 is what it said.

**The number is spent regardless**, because [P5 section 8](../plans/P5-payouts-and-wallet.md)'s `P5-d` row marks `docs/decisions/ADR-1NN.md` **REQUIRED** in the plan's own words while [ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md)'s row marks it CONDITIONAL. Two registries disagreed and the plan is the one that describes the work.

### 2. The ruling the fold could not avoid making

[EVENTS](../architecture/EVENTS.md) section 6 refused to invent three event shapes and said why: *"inventing three event shapes for a machine this fold did not draw is how a catalogue acquires rows nobody ruled on."* **That refusal was right about the danger and it deferred the question rather than answering it.** [P5 section 5.2](../plans/P5-payouts-and-wallet.md) makes this slice the one that closes the gap, so the question arrives whether or not a number is available for it.

**RULED: a name becomes a row only where every field is a column a migration declares or a field of a mirror a plan names.** Two authorities, no third. `wallet.withdrawal_cooling`, `.approved` and `.sent` had a name and no shape anywhere in the corpus; their payloads are derived field by field from [`wallet_withdrawals`](../../packages/db/migrations/0011_wallet.sql) and from the `payout.transfer_*` mirror [M05 section 5](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) names in a sentence, and [ADR-159](../decisions/ADR-159.md) section 4 writes the derivation down rather than asserting it.

**And a field that resolves to neither is a refusal.** That is why `wallet.withdrawal_cancelled` is reported and not written: the missing artifact is a transition row in a table this fence holds one row of, and a name invented here would leave the drawing exactly as unmet while adding a row to the registry with no producer.

### 3. Five amendments, and why a fold that could only transcribe would have installed a defect

The permission and its limit are the same sentence: **the registry may amend a payload at the fold and may not amend one silently.** [EVENTS](../architecture/EVENTS.md)' own precedent is the M1 gate, where `payout.win_days_reset` gained `anchor_trading_day` because *"reset to zero" without the anchor does not explain the next cycle*.

The load-bearing one is `wallet.credited`. [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) writes its payload unconditionally and [`0011`](../../packages/db/migrations/0011_wallet.sql) declares `wallet_entries.provenance` `NOT NULL CHECK (provenance IN ('payout', 'refund_wallet_funded', 'correction'))`. **A refund credit has no payout request and no basis trading day; a correction has neither either; and the table carries no `account_id` column at all.** Transcribing would have installed a registry row that two of three storable credits cannot produce. The row gains the discriminator and makes three fields conditional on it, which is section 1's *"a payload may add optional fields at the same version"* used for the purpose it exists for.

The other four are smaller and each has a rule behind it: `expected` and `actual` become `_cents` and `window` becomes `window_days` under the catalogue's own money and unit conventions, which `cusum.alarm` and `affiliate.chargeback_rate_updated` already follow; `expected_release` gains its `_at`; and `payout.freeze_expiring` gains `lead_hours`.

| Surface | Registered |

Nothing in this slice depends on the figure; it is checked because the dispatch asked for it to be, and because a claim that is wrong in a harmless place is the same claim in a harmful one.
