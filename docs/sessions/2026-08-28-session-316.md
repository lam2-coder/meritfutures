### 4. What the verification changed about the dispatch

**The dispatch and [ADR-172](../decisions/ADR-172.md) both said `INV-M5-06` survives because all three doors build the key from the request's STORED key. That is true of the two doors that release a hold and it was NOT true of the request path**, which built the string from the header variable and never wrote the column. So the property the ruling relied on was, at the moment of the ruling, held by two of the three doors and by the handler's memory. **Removing the posting removes the memory. The column is what has to carry it, and nothing wrote the column.** That is the whole of [ADR-176](../decisions/ADR-176.md) clause 2, and it was found by reading `PayoutRequestInsert` member by member against `0010_payouts.sql` rather than by trusting the sentence.
