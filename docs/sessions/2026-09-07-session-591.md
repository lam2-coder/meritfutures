### 4. Landmines

- **`git blame -w` is the wrong instrument for `G2` and every row since [ADR-388](../decisions/ADR-388.md) has used it.** Nothing has re-run that ruling's corpus-wide census of 289 named pointers under a corrected instrument, and its 76-against-42 split is what the whole ruling rests on.
- **The dispatcher's environment sentence has now been false in two consecutive waves in the same way.** It reports a property of a clone it did not check.
- **A "pointer repair" inside a generated span is not a thing that can be done**, and a row told to do one will either fail `CI-06g` or have its edit silently overwritten.
- **`git log -S` over a coordinate has a known hole**: where the same coordinate appears twice in one file the first introduction is attributed to both. Neither case is present in this fence, checked; neither is ruled out corpus-wide.
- **The two coherence arguments in [ADR-396](../decisions/ADR-396.md) sections 6 and 10 point in opposite directions from the same premise.** One compels a two-sided repair and the other forbids a one-sided one. A founder who thinks that premise is being stretched should read section 10 first, because that is the one that left a wrong pointer standing.
