## 2026-08-17 - Session 50: the fixture wiring, and the premise ADR-048 rests on (non-money)

- **Done:** **All three items, in the stated order, each watched failing on its own seeded violation.** `L-13` makes `source:` a resolvable citation ([ADR-048](../decisions/ADR-048.md)'s stated prerequisite); `diffEndState` compares `bigint` cents against JSON numbers; ADR-048's polarity derivation is per fixture, reported per rule group. `pnpm run verify` green: **670 tests, 15 of 15 gates clean and dirty, 16 scope cases, 10 new loader cases.** **`packages/rules-engine/src/` is untouched**, per the session fence, and no fixture was authored or regenerated.
- **Next:** **The fold, and it is now the only thing between this stage and its own assertion.** `runFixture` calls `evaluate`, which is not one of [M01 section 1.3](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)'s six functions. Rewiring it to `resolvePlan` -> `initialState` -> `advanceDay` makes `declaration.holds` true, at which point 30 per-fixture assertions switch on with nothing edited. It needs `PLAN_CONFIG_SOURCE` widened past `plan_version_id` and `EngineInput` retired from the loader, and **it wants a plan-mode session** because it changes how every fixture is loaded. Two founder rulings still hold fields on some fixtures and would surface the moment it lands: **`INV-07` versus the funded reset**, and the **floor-lock jump disagreement**.
- **Blockers:** **Nothing blocked this session.** The one decision above the code was taken by the founder mid-session and is recorded below.
- **Landmines:**

  - **`IMPLEMENTED_RULES` IS TRUE OF THE PACKAGE AND FALSE OF THE FOLD, AND THAT IS A HOLE ADR-048 DOES NOT NAME.** The ADR enumerates four ways the derivation can be wrong and closes each, three of them "before any fixture is consulted". **Its failure mode 3 is "a rule id is added to the declared set without the rule being implemented", answered by cross-checking against the passing `RE-U-nn` set.** That cross-check is satisfied here: all 45 declared rules are implemented and every one has a passing unit test. **They are implemented in `advanceDay`, `applySettlement` and `evaluatePayout`, and CI-03 folds `evaluate`**, the scaffold placeholder that returns its input by reference and emits nothing. So a rule can be implemented, unit-tested, declared, cross-checked, and **unreachable from the stage that derives polarity from the declaration**. ADR-048 speaks of "the engine" as one thing and M01 section 1.3 exports six functions; the loader calls none of them. `checkDeclarationAgainstFold` is the check that names it, and it is the retired probe's own warning promoted from a comment, **because the case that header named stopped being hypothetical**.

  - **ALL 30 FIXTURES FLIP, AND THE FLIP IS NOT THE FINDING.** Every fixture on the tree cites only rules the engine declares, so all 30 derive `direct` and none derives `inverted`. That is not a fixture problem and none was edited: the batches were written to cite resolvable rules and [session 48](2026-08-17-session-48.md) said in its own words that "all four derive to `direct`". **What the derivation surfaced is the premise underneath it**, and the honest count is that 30 of 30 would fail today for a reason that has nothing to do with any of their subjects.

  - **THE VACUITY CLASS SURVIVES `L-13` BY ONE PREFIX, AND THE ADR'S OWN PREREQUISITE DOES NOT CLOSE IT.** ADR-048 case 4 is a fixture citing nothing, closed "by a prerequisite rather than by care": [P2 section 2](../plans/P2-rules-engine.md)'s resolvable citation. **But that prerequisite accepts "at least one `R-nn`, `CV-nn` or `INV-nn`", three prefixes, and polarity is derived from rules.** A fixture citing only `INV-06` satisfies `L-13`, cites no rule, and "every rule this fixture cites is implemented" is vacuously true of it exactly as before. Closed structurally in `derivePolarity`: an empty cited-rule set derives `inverted`, never `direct`. **This is the sixth costume of the same defect** the ADR already lists in five, and it arrived inside the repair for the fifth.

  - **THE `bigint` DEFECT WAS INVISIBLE AND WOULD HAVE STAYED INVISIBLE UNTIL THE MOMENT IT MATTERED.** `INV-02` makes every money field the engine returns a `bigint`, JSON has no literal for one, and `Object.is(4770000n, 4770000)` is false. Under the inverted polarity every fixture must FAIL and every fixture did, **so a comparison that could never agree with anything looked exactly like one that worked**. It would have surfaced as thirty unexplained red fixtures on the day the first group flipped. **No seeded fixture can catch it**, which is why its three seeds mutate source rather than data: that is the discriminating experiment, and a data seed would have been decoration.

  - **THE REPORT PRINTED TWO IDENTICAL NUMBERS AND CALLED THEM A DIFF.** `String(4770000n)` and `String(4770000)` are the same characters, so the finding read "expected 4770000, engine produced 4770000". **A reader seeing that goes looking for a bug in the differ**, which is the most expensive possible way to spend the first hour of an investigation. `show` now renders a `bigint` with its `n`. Whatever a comparison decides about two types, it may not describe them in a way a reader cannot tell apart.

  - **A COERCION FIX IS ONE CHARACTER FROM BEING WORSE THAN THE DEFECT, SO BOTH DIRECTIONS ARE SEEDED.** The old comparison could never assert a cent. A comparison that agrees with everything asserts **every** cent is right, and money moves in the second direction. `compare/over-agreement` mutates `bigintAgrees` to `return true` and watches the one-cent case go quiet. The third seed removes `Number.isSafeInteger`, at which point a fractional expectation reaches `BigInt()` and **throws**: an exception is not a diff, it names no field, and CI-03 would report a crash where a fixture defect belongs.

  - **THE RULE GROUPS ARE READ FROM M01 BECAUSE THE ENGINE STATES THEM IN COMMENTS.** ADR-048 requires coverage to report polarity per rule group and rejects deriving polarity from the group, "because the group is not written on the fixture and would become a second hand-maintained mapping, which is ADR-034's class". **That argument applies to the report too.** `RuleId` in `rules.ts` carries the eight boundaries as comments, which no machine reads; restating the ranges in the loader would have been the second expression. M01 states them as headings above the rule tables, so the document that defines a rule also says which group it is in. **Empty groups are printed**: G and H have no fixture citing them, and a table that listed only the groups with work done would shrink to the work done.

  - **`falsify.mjs` COULD NOT RUN A TYPESCRIPT LOADER AND NOW CAN, AND THE FIX WAS IN THE CHECKER RATHER THAN THE HARNESS.** `copyTree` omits `node_modules`, so nothing needing vitest or a workspace resolution runs in a tree copy. `check.mjs` therefore imports `./src/loader.ts`, `./src/compare.ts` and `./src/polarity.ts` **directly and never the barrel**, which re-exports the two modules that import the engine as a value. **That is what lets a case seed the RULE and not only the DATA**, which is the whole reason the `bigint` and polarity seeds exist at all. `polarity.ts` takes the declared rule set as a parameter for the same reason, which is the argument `compare.ts` already makes for itself.

  - **THE DEFERRED ASSERTION IS ONE COMPUTED CONDITION AND NOT A `pending` FLAG, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS WHERE IT LIVES.** TR-03 forbids the per-fixture escape hatch this package names in its own source. What landed instead: **one condition, derived, covering the whole directory, printed in bold in the stage's own output on every run, and unreachable from any fixture.** The two blocks are mutually exclusive and nothing is edited to move between them, so the derived assertion starts and the standing one stops on the day the fold changes. **A test asserts the block still says it**, because a finding that exists only in a variable is the quiet direction.

  - **THE CORRUPTION PROBE HAD TO BE RE-AIMED AT THE ASSERTION THAT RUNS.** It measures whether a corrupted expectation still passes, and it had one global polarity to measure against. With the derived direction reported rather than enforced, measuring against the DERIVED direction would have reported 0 blind fixtures while all 30 were blind, **describing a stage other than the one that just ran**, which is the exact defect `coverage.ts` exists to prevent.

| Option | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Enforce now | CI-03 red on 31 assertions. [STRATEGY section 1](../testing/STRATEGY.md): a permanently red required stage is worse than a smaller one nobody clicks through |
| **Report, do not enforce** | **RULED.** The stage asserts what it can honour and says in bold what it is not asserting and why |
