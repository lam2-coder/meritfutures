## 2026-08-21 - Session 124: WAVE-05 `X6`, `GS-030`'s surviving half is a count and not a duration

- **Objective:** assert [WAVE-05](../plans/WAVE-05-tier2-fixture-shapes.md) `X6` in [`trading-calendar-generator.test.ts`](../../packages/db/test/trading-calendar-generator.test.ts): across each DST transition, one trading day and one calendar row, whatever the clock did.
- **Done:** **one test**, in the existing `the DST transitions` describe block, over **all six transitions** in the source file's coverage. Five assertions, each watched failing on a perturbed expectation, and `FM-14`'s two shapes (a duplicated boundary day, a missing boundary day) each seeded into the subject and watched rejecting. **1404 -> 1405 passing.** No fixture written, no loader touched, nothing in `docs/decisions/` touched.
- **Next:** `X7`, the ADR repairing `GS-030`'s and [EC-012](../edge-cases/EC-012-to-033-appendix-b4-battery.md)'s falsified clause. The evidence it depends on is section **"Evidence for `X7`"** below and is **wider than [ADR-076](../decisions/ADR-076.md)'s**: six transitions and 60 sessions rather than two and sixteen.
- **Blockers:** none.

### What landed

**One case, `produces one trading day and one row across each transition, whatever the clock did`.** For each of the six transitions [`findDstTransitions`](../../packages/db/src/seed/calendars/generate.mjs) discovers over 2026 to 2028, it generates a Sunday-to-Sunday window around the transition Sunday (two whole trading weeks, ten weekdays) and asserts:

| | Assertion | Why it is there |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The generated `trading_day` list equals the weekdays in the window | **One trading day and one row.** The expected list is derived from the weekday INSIDE the test, so the two statements are independent |
| 2 | `new Set(days).size` equals `days.length` | A duplicate and a reordering are different defects and `toEqual` alone reports both as "the arrays differ" |
| 3 | The transition day carries no row | Names WHY the count is unchanged: it is a Sunday, in the gap between Friday's 16:00 close and Sunday's 17:00 open |
| 4 | Both sessions either side close at `T16:00:00` CT | The wall time did not move |
| 5 | Their UTC instants are an hour apart, in the direction `kind` names | **Whatever the clock did.** Without this the count passes on any ordinary fortnight |

**`FM-14` ([M01:839](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)) is the failure this detects**: *"DST transition handled by arithmetic instead of data -> a duplicated or missing trading day at the boundary"*. Assertion 1 is written against an independently derived expectation for exactly that reason; a case that compared the generator's output to the generator's own count could not fail.

**Assertion 5 is the half a reader should check first.** It is what makes the other four about DST rather than about weekdays. `2026-03-06` closes `22:00:00Z` and `2026-03-09` closes `21:00:00Z`; the CT wall time is `16:00` on both. The offset moved by an hour, and the row count did not.

### Watched failing, six times, and one perturbation was a no-op that taught something

Each assertion was perturbed, the rejection read, and the file restored from a saved copy.

| Perturbation | Rejected with |
|---|---|
| Window start shifted **one** day | **PASSED.** `from` is a Sunday; shifting to Monday leaves the weekday list identical. **The perturbation was the no-op, not the assertion** |
| Window start shifted **two** days | `expected [ '2026-03-02', … ] to deeply equal [ '2026-03-03', … ]` |
| `toBe(days.length)` -> `days.length - 1` | `expected 10 to be 9` |
| `.not.toContain` -> `.toContain` | `expected [ '2026-03-02', … ] to include '2026-03-08'` |
| The spring-forward arm swapped with the fall-back arm | `expected [ '22:00:00Z', '21:00:00Z' ] to deeply equal [ '21:00:00Z', '22:00:00Z' ]` |
| The second `T16:00:00` -> `T15:00:00` | `expected [ 'T16:00:00', 'T16:00:00' ] to deeply equal [ 'T16:00:00', 'T15:00:00' ]` |

**Then `FM-14` itself, seeded into the subject rather than into the expectation**, because a perturbed expectation proves an assertion is live and does not prove it catches the defect it was written for:

- **a duplicated boundary day** (`days.splice(1, 0, days[0])`) -> `expected [ '2026-03-02', '2026-03-02', … ] to deeply equal [ '2026-03-02', '2026-03-03', … ]`
- **a missing boundary day** (the first session after spring forward filtered out) -> `expected […(7 more)] to deeply equal […(8 more)]`

**The first perturbation is recorded because it passed.** A one-day shift off a Sunday changes nothing a weekday filter can see, and had it been the only perturbation run, this case would have been committed as "watched failing" on a run that never failed.

### Evidence for `X7`, which this session does not write

**[ADR-076](../decisions/ADR-076.md) section 5 measured the two 2026 transitions and sixteen sessions. The same measurement over all six transitions in coverage is 60 sessions and the answer does not change.** Run through the committed [`generate.mjs`](../../packages/db/src/seed/calendars/generate.mjs), Sunday-to-Sunday window around each transition, no holidays and no early closes:

| Transition | Kind | Rows | Distinct trading days | Session lengths |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `2026-03-08` | spring forward | 10 | 10 | **23 hours, all ten** |
| `2026-11-01` | fall back | 10 | 10 | **23 hours, all ten** |
| `2027-03-14` | spring forward | 10 | 10 | **23 hours, all ten** |
| `2027-11-07` | fall back | 10 | 10 | **23 hours, all ten** |
| `2028-03-12` | spring forward | 10 | 10 | **23 hours, all ten** |
| `2028-11-05` | fall back | 10 | 10 | **23 hours, all ten** |

| `2027-03-14` spring forward | `2027-03-12` `2027-03-11T23:00Z` -> `2027-03-12T22:00Z` | `2027-03-15` `2027-03-14T22:00Z` -> `2027-03-15T21:00Z` |

| `2028-03-12` spring forward | `2028-03-10` `2028-03-09T23:00Z` -> `2028-03-10T22:00Z` | `2028-03-13` `2028-03-12T22:00Z` -> `2028-03-13T21:00Z` |
| `2028-11-05` fall back | `2028-11-03` `2028-11-02T22:00Z` -> `2028-11-03T21:00Z` | `2028-11-06` `2028-11-05T23:00Z` -> `2028-11-06T22:00Z` |

**The clause to repair, stated precisely for `X7`.** `GS-030`'s registry row ([`04-gs-030-to-gs-051`](../testing/golden-scenarios/04-gs-030-to-gs-051-the-appendix-b4-battery.md)) reads *"The 23 hour and 25 hour sessions each produce exactly one trading day and one mark"*, and [EC-012](../edge-cases/EC-012-to-033-appendix-b4-battery.md) reads *"The 23 hour and 25 hour sessions each produce exactly one trading day"*. **Three separable claims sit in that sentence and they do not have the same truth value:**

| Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **A DST transition produces a 23-hour session** | **Vacuously true and misleading.** Every CME weekday session is 23 hours, transition week or not. The number is right for a reason that has nothing to do with DST |
| **A DST transition produces a 25-hour session** | **FALSE.** No session in coverage is 25 hours, or 24, or any length but 23. Both transitions fall at 02:00 CT on a Sunday, inside the gap between Friday's 16:00 close and Sunday's 17:00 open, so no session contains a transition and no session's length changes |
| **Each produces exactly one trading day and one mark** | **TRUE for the trading day, and it is now asserted.** The **mark** half is not, and is not this row's to assert: `DailyMark` carries no instant ([session 59](2026-08-18-session-59.md)) and `EngineInput` carries no calendar |

**So the repair `X7` owes is narrower than "fix the arithmetic".** The sentence is not off by a number; it names a DST artifact that does not exist. Recommended replacement wording, offered as input and not as a ruling: **"the sessions either side of a DST transition each produce exactly one trading day and one mark, and their identical CT close resolves to UTC instants an hour apart"**. That is what assertion 5 pins and it is the property `R-05` actually buys.

**A second thing `X7` should carry.** [Section 39](../testing/golden-scenarios/39-fixture-status-and-blockers.md)'s blocker cell for `GS-030` cites `cme-2026.json`'s `what_this_format_cannot_carry` and says *"the 23 hour and 25 hour halves are not statable"*. **After this commit that cell is understated in one direction and overstated in the other**: the count half IS stated, in `packages/db/test`, and the duration half is not merely unstatable but false. [ADR-076](../decisions/ADR-076.md) section 5 already re-words the citation; the row still says the old thing.

### Landmines

- **`build()` runs `checkDstTransitions`, which requires exactly two transitions per calendar year FULLY inside coverage.** A fifteen-day window clips both years at its ends, so both are skipped and the check is a no-op here. **A future case that widens this window to a whole year inherits that requirement**, and it will fire the day tzdata moves.
- **The windows in this case carry `holidays: []` and `early_closes: []` and are SYNTHETIC**, per the file's own header. They are not a claim about what CME published in March 2027. The transition DAYS are real, from IANA; the surrounding calendar is not.
- **`weekdaysBetween` and `shiftDay` do their arithmetic in UTC**, where a day is always 86,400,000 ms. That is correct here and would be wrong the moment either is asked about a CT wall time. Neither is exported.
