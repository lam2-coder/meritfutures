### Done

**[ADR-197](../decisions/ADR-197.md) landed**, `status: proposed`, UNSIGNED, with a `What a founder read adds` block. Three rulings.

1. **The key is a deployment secret.** `MERIT_OTP_MAC_KEY`, per [INFRA section 7](../architecture/INFRA.md), with `MERIT_OTP_MAC_KEY_RETIRING` admitted for verifying during one TTL. Refused on a merged `GRANT` rather than on preference, and the slow-KDF alternative refused on 82 cores.
2. **The digest binds the address AS TYPED**, case-folded ASCII-only, with the channel and length prefixes. It is what separates two people `email_normalized` deliberately merges.
3. **The door is one verb.** `establishmentDb()` writes `identities` and its first `users` row in one unit of work, with the unique violation allowed to raise.

**Three stale refusals repaired in `auth-backend.ts`**, two of which had been false since [ADR-126](../decisions/ADR-126.md) and one of which no entry had reported. **The suite that pinned them is repaired to assert the property rather than the sentence**: a refusal may not cite a construction that exists.

**`resolutionDb`, `establishmentDb` and `normalizedEmail` are exported from `packages/db`.** The first had never been, which is the whole of why it had no caller.

**`0061` returned to the pool unspent.** The A/B was reproduced, not inherited: 15 of 15 probes pass.

### Landmines

1. **THE CONSUMPTION IS UNRULED AND IT IS THE NEXT RACE.** `otp_challenges` carries `consumed_at`, `attempts BETWEEN 0 AND 5` and `otp_challenges_unconsumed_uq`, a partial unique index on `(id) WHERE consumed_at IS NULL`. **Nothing says which row a verification selects, how `attempts` is incremented on a wrong code, or that consumption and minting are one unit of work.** A handler that reads, matches, then consumes in a second statement lets one code be spent twice under concurrency, **and that race has no arbiter as convenient as `users_email_key`.**
2. **THE CROSS-PERSON SEPARATION IS ENFORCED IN `apps/api` AND NOWHERE ELSE.** A later handler that digests `email_normalized` instead of the address as typed re-opens it silently, and no constraint, probe or invariant would object. The suite case is the only guard.
3. **THE DOOR'S ONE-VERB SHAPE IS HELD BY A TEST THAT READS SOURCE**, not by a `CHECK`. Widening `EstablishmentTx` is a diff no probe and no constraint objects to, and clause 2's enforcement lives entirely in that shape.
4. **`RI-15` AND `RI-16` DO NOT READ THE BARE `` `:NNN` `` FORM**, which is the form this corpus uses for a second citation into the same file. One such citation was 69 lines adrift for an unknown period with every gate green. **Registered, not repaired: the invariant is `packages/tooling`'s.**
5. **`databaseAuthBackend` holds no key and that is deliberate.** Whoever wires it must not admit the key before the handler, and must read `resolveOtpMacKeys`'s rotation contract rather than reaching for a single key.
