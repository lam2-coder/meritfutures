### 2. The list envelope, and why this session took it

Session 285 refused to wire `GET /accounts/:accountId/marks` for two reasons and this session met both on `/timeline`:

- **The portal declared no envelope.** `grep -n 'next_cursor' apps/portal/src/api/types.ts` returned nothing; the API's own `TimelinePage` and `MarksPage` are `{ data, next_cursor }`, which is API_CONTRACT section 1.
- **Nobody had ruled what the portal does with a cursor.**

The dispatch permitted this fence to hold `api/types.ts` for the envelope, on the ground that the second segment waiting is worse than the first choosing. It is transcribed once, generically, because section 1 is a CONVENTION rather than an endpoint: the contract writes the pair out longhand per response (`WalletEntriesResponse`, `CertificateListResponse`) and those are instances of one rule. `GET /plans`'s cursor-less `PlansResponse` is a different type rather than this one with an optional field, because an optional cursor makes *"there may be more"* and *"the endpoint does not page"* the same value.

### 3. The paging decision, in three parts

1. **ONE PAGE, NOT ALL OF THEM.** Following the cursor to exhaustion is unbounded round trips inside one server render, every one of them uncached under ADR-162 clause 4, on a list that grows for the life of an account. There is no bound the corpus supplies and inventing one is this same decision taken again one loop in.
2. **THE `limit` IS SENT AND IT IS THE CONTRACT'S MAXIMUM.** `apps/api` defaults an absent `limit` to 25; relying on that would make how much history the screen shows a property of a constant in another deployable, changeable with no diff here, on a screen that has to state what it read.
3. **THE SCREEN SAYS WHICH IT GOT AND CANNOT COMPILE WITHOUT SAYING.** `TimelinePaging` is a required prop with no default, which is `AsOfFreshness`'s mechanism beside it. The answer comes from `next_cursor` and not from a row count against the limit: an exactly-full final page is `complete`, and inferring truncation from the count would report a whole timeline as partial forever.

**Neither arm names an end of the list**, because API_CONTRACT gives this endpoint the word *"Chronological"* and no direction, and the backend that would settle it by observation is unwired. **There is no control to load the rest**, because a next page needs a navigation carrying a cursor and this segment's URLs are already provisional; the truncation is stated and the control is reported rather than invented.
