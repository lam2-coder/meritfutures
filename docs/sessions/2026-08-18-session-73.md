## 2026-08-18 - Session 73: `ADR-060`, what `engine_eligible` contains and why it was unrulable (planning, money path)

Fence: [`docs/decisions/ADR-060.md`](../decisions/ADR-060.md) (new), one row each in
[ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md) and [decisions/README](../decisions/README.md), the
`adr_count` spans, an appended STATE section, and this file. **No code, no fixture, no M01 edit.**

- **Objective:** frame, as a proposed ADR, the M01 contradiction that makes `engine_eligible`
  unpinnable, so that a fixture may eventually assert it without ratifying a ruling nobody made.

- **Done:** [ADR-060](../decisions/ADR-060.md) exists, `status: proposed`, founder-approval line
  **unsigned**. **18 of 18 gates. 60 test files, 908 passed, 40 skipped.** Every factual claim in
  the ADR carries a `file:line` opened in this session.

  **THE RECORDED REASON DID NOT SURVIVE RE-DERIVATION, AND THAT IS THE SESSION'S RESULT.** The
  [fixtures README:228](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/README.md) states the blocker as "the
  engine has CHOSEN a reading; M01 has NOT", and gives as its fourth premise that `R-41` "says
  NOTHING about which gates are in the left operand". **`R-41`'s ROW says nothing. The sentence
  three lines above it, under the same heading, says it outright**: "`engineEligible` is the
  conjunction of the engine gates; the context gates are combined at read time (INV-23)"
  ([M01:478](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)). M01 states the exclusion **five times** — `INV-23` at
  M01:134, the `ExternalGates` sketch at M01:175 with `hasPayoutInFlight` inside it at M01:178, the
  `RuleState` sketch at M01:214, `SD-06` at M01:253, and M01:478.

  **WHAT M01 HAS NEVER DONE IS ENUMERATE THE ENGINE GATES.** `INV-15` reads `engine_eligible ==
  AND(every engine gate)` ([M01:126](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)) and no document says which those
  are. The only closed list in the repository is
  [`types.ts:954`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts). **That missing enumeration is what
  blocked the fixture; `R-38` is one instance of it**, and the two are unblocked by different edits.
  The fold list has **nine sites across six documents plus one code comment** for that reason. Had
  this been ruled as "a contradiction about R-38", `INV-15` would still have no referent and
  `GS-080` would still be unwritable the day after signature.

  **READING B IS DEFEATED BY ITS OWN TWO CITATIONS, ARITHMETICALLY.** The pro-inclusion evidence is
  R-38's placement in `#### Group F: funded gates` (M01:476 to :491) and `DO-9`'s rule range
  "R-33 to R-41" (M01:341). **`R-40` is in that group and in that range, and `R-40` IS the
  context-gate rule; `R-41` is the conjunction itself.** A group containing the context-gate rule is
  not the engine-gate list, and a range containing `R-41` is not its enumeration.

  **The recommendation is derived from M01 and not read off the engine, and that is its whole
  safety.** Group F minus `R-38` (input on `ExternalGates`), minus `R-40` (definitionally context),
  minus `R-41` (the conjunction, not a term) leaves **six**: `R-33`, `R-34`, `R-35`, `R-36`, `R-37`,
  `R-39`. They are the six in `types.ts:954`. **Agreement reached independently is what makes a
  fixture safe; agreement reached by transcription is what `TR-01` forbids**, and copying the list
  out of `types.ts` into M01 would ratify the engine one level up and harder to see. That is stated
  in the ADR as the third argument against its own recommendation.

- **Next:** the ADR is **unsigned and nothing may be folded until it is**. On signature, item 1 of
  the fold list (`INV-15` gains the closed list) is the load-bearing edit and the only one that
  changes what a fixture may derive; items 3 to 6 are coherence. `GS-055` becomes pinnable
  immediately; `GS-080` and `GS-060` gain one of their two blockers back and still need
