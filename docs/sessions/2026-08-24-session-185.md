### 1. What the ruling turns on, in one table

**P4 asks which of three readings `P4-e` takes. The tree answers a narrower question first: which of them can reach the data at all.**

| Reading | What it does | What the tree says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. a third `SystemReason` member | widens a closed vocabulary | **NO-OP.** `rows()` is generic over `TableKey` and none of the site's eight tables is one |
| 2. a scoped accessor with no identity | drops the identity argument | **NOT EXPRESSIBLE.** `TS2554` before a brand is consulted, and `ScopedTableKey` after the cast |
| 3. something that does not exist yet | **RULED** | and what does not exist is **not in `packages/db`**: it is three endpoints, a fourth nobody has specified, and an HTTP adapter |

**The entry's whole method is that an argument about a security boundary should be settled by running the compiler at it rather than by reasoning about the design**, and both refusals were watched failing to compile rather than predicted.

### 2. The one thing this session found that neither P4 nor `ADR-084` could have

`ADR-084` closed the vocabulary at two and argued that *"the legitimate unscoped readers are a list somebody has to join"*. P4 read that vocabulary and asked whether a marketing page joins it. **Both questions are about the reason, and the reason is the least of it.**

The refusal that actually holds is at [`scoped-db.ts:155`](../../packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts), one line below the vocabulary, and it is the same refusal `ADR-084` section 3 built for the `firm` class: **a table with no rule in `SCOPE_RULES` is unreachable through either accessor.** `ADR-084` reported that as *"seven of 111, reported as seven of 111"* and called the resulting diff on `scope.ts` *"the cost and the point"*. **The site is what happens when the cost lands on a consumer that was never going to be an application**: it needs eight of the 104, all eight would be `firm`, and the class the scoped accessor **refuses** would become the class the public website reads through.

**That is the sentence the ruling comes out of, and neither prior document was in a position to write it.** `ADR-084` was looking at the seven; P4 was looking at the vocabulary; the answer is in the gap between them.
