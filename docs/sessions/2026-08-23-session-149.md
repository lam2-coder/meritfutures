### Every one of the seven was ALREADY MERGED, which is the defect being closed

**Live code on `main` was enforcing seven unsigned rulings.** That is the shape [ADR-073](../decisions/ADR-073.md) was signed to end after a gate had been enforcing it unsigned for a week, and it had recurred sevenfold. `0046` is a merged money-path migration under `ADR-079`; `hash.ts` is a hand-rolled cryptographic primitive in the engine under `ADR-081`; `rail clock` is a live token in `UNIT_TOKENS` under `ADR-082`. **A ruling nobody signed and a gate nobody enforces are the same defect one direction over**, and this session had the second kind in quantity.

### Two entries could NOT be signed as written, and the checks are what found it

**[ADR-078](../decisions/ADR-078.md)'s approval clause was FALSE against the tree.** It reads *"`Object.keys(engine).sort()` is exactly the fourteen names pinned in [`evaluate.golden.test.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/test/evaluate.golden.test.ts)"*, and the pinned list is **twenty**, because [ADR-081](../decisions/ADR-081.md) added six one entry later. **Signing it unchanged would have contradicted the standard [session 126](2026-08-21-session-126.md) set when it WITHHELD [ADR-076](../decisions/ADR-076.md) on exactly this ground.** The clause is time-anchored instead, and the signature is given against it as anchored. Subtracting ADR-081's six from the twenty leaves exactly the fourteen ADR-078 named, `evaluate` gone and nothing else removed, which is what makes the anchoring a repair and not a dodge.

> **The entry had already warned about this in its own words and aimed the warning one level too low.** It says *"a clause that names the list cannot drift the way a count can."* True of the list's CONTENTS, false of its LENGTH. What drifted was the word **exactly**, which reads as a standing property of the engine and was only ever a measurement of a moment. That is [ADR-074](../decisions/ADR-074.md) section 3's rule arriving in a place nobody had applied it.

**[ADR-077](../decisions/ADR-077.md)'s clause SPLIT: the ruling held and the evidence for it did not.** The clause asks for a bounded grep and says *"a sixth site, or a `-` between the two endpoints at any of the five, refutes the ruling."* Run over the line's own four needles and its own four globs outside `node_modules`, that grep returns **sixteen files. Section 5.3's table names eight.**

**All eight missing sites were read, and none of them is a subtraction.** Two are computations: [`trading-calendar-generator.test.ts:216`](../../packages/db/test/trading-calendar-generator.test.ts) compares a Friday open against a Wednesday close, which is an ordering across the WEEKEND GAP and therefore this entry's own subject rather than a session length, and `:388` compares one endpoint's wall-clock face before and after a transition. Two are probes that write the columns and read one back as text. Four are prose and fixture literals. **Section 5.3 is amended in place with the eight, and the signature says so.**

> **A sixth FILE exists. A sixth COMPUTATION OF THE KIND THE CLAUSE NAMES does not.** The clause states its own subject one sentence above the literal sentence: *"read each for a subtraction or for a comparison of the interval against a constant."* The signature is given against the property, the inventory is corrected in the same pass, and the difference between *"the list was short"* and *"the ruling was wrong"* is recorded rather than collapsed.

### The hand-rolled SHA-256 was re-tested for the signature, and the count in the entry is the count that ran

**[ADR-081](../decisions/ADR-081.md) says of itself THIS ONE ASKS FOR THE REAL READ.** It is money path, it is a hand-rolled cryptographic primitive, and the founder's line-by-line read has not happened. **The signature records that as an open debt and not as a formality discharged.**

What was done instead: a fresh differential oracle sharing no code with [`hash.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/hash.ts), run against `node:crypto` over **281 inputs** covering every length from 0 to 200 bytes, the block and padding boundaries 55, 56, 63, 64, 119, 120, 127 and 128 in one-byte and two-byte encodings, the full single-code-unit range, lone high and lone low surrogates alone and embedded, astral pairs to `U+10FFFF`, and the entry's length-prefixed framing at every field count 1 to 19 and value widths 0 to 1024. **281 of 281 matched, zero mismatches.** `RI-07` reports PASS, and the digest `6f640ab7...12f60d60` is pinned in two files rather than one.

> **THE FIRST DRAFT OF THAT PARAGRAPH SAID 322 AND THE RUN SAID 254.** 322 was the figure from an earlier corpus, carried forward from memory into a sentence about to be signed. The corpus was then widened deliberately and re-run, and **281 is what the second run printed.** The paragraph now states the number that executed. This is the [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) caution in miniature, and the cost of getting it wrong here would have been a false figure inside the one document whose job is to be checkable.

### A registry column had been wrong for twenty rows and no gate could see it

**`CI-06r` compares an ADR heading with that same ADR's body and states its own boundary in its `covers` line: *"IT COMPARES A FILE WITH ITSELF AND CAN CHECK NEITHER HALF AGAINST THE GATE RECORD."*** Nothing compares the heading against the **row in [`decisions/README.md`](../decisions/README.md)** that transcribes it. So the registry's status column records the status **on the day the row was written** and never moves again.

**Twenty rows were already stale on arrival**, including [ADR-071](../decisions/ADR-071.md) through [ADR-074](../decisions/ADR-074.md), signed by [session 126](2026-08-21-session-126.md), whose rows still read `proposed` a day later. Adding seven signatures without touching it would have made twenty-seven. **All twenty-seven are repaired**, derived from each ADR's own heading rather than typed.

**Four session claim rows had the identical shape.** `135`, `136`, `137` and `139` were WRITTEN, their entries were in the list below, and their claim rows still read **CLAIMED**. Struck through under [ADR-065](../decisions/ADR-065.md) T3 rather than deleted.
