### 2. The finding, re-derived, and the one thing re-deriving changed

Every sentence of the dispatch was checked against its own primary source. [ADR-189](../decisions/ADR-189.md) section 1 is the table. **All of them hold.** `wallet_withdrawal_status` reads back from `pg_enum` as the seven values `0001:95-97` declares; [STATE_MACHINES:203](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) draws `transferring --> failed: G-TRANSFER-EXHAUSTED` word for word and [`:224`](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) rows it with `wallet.withdrawal_failed`; `M05` section 2.1's rows lead `LT-01` through `LT-08` and none is the external leg's failure.

**WHAT CHANGED IS THE SHAPE OF THE ARGUMENT AND NOT THE ANSWER.** The finding reads as though `failed` matters because it is where the rail gives up. It does not. **`failed` is the only terminal state reachable from a state at or past `approved` that does not itself discharge the obligation**, which is a property of the drawing rather than of the rail, and it is what a later session needs in order to know whether a NEW terminal state would need `LT-09` too.

### 4. Why `0057` was spent, which is the session's judgement

The dispatch put it as a binary. **The answer is that the two halves prove different things.**

| Property | What can prove it | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The reversal is EXACT | **TypeScript, structurally.** `reversalPosting` swaps each transfer's two sides and negates nothing. A partial reversal is not refused, it is unavailable: there is no parameter for an amount | `packages/ledger` |
| Posted AT MOST once | **A UNIQUE index, and nothing else in this database** | `0057` |
| Posted AT LEAST once | **A deferred constraint trigger, and nothing else in this estate** | `0057` |

**THE SECOND WAS MEASURED BEFORE A LINE OF THE MIGRATION WAS WRITTEN.** `ledger_transactions_reversal_of_idx` is created NOT UNIQUE at `0009:98-99`, read from `pg_indexes`. A second transaction naming the same `reversal_of` under a different `idempotency_key` **LANDS**: the obligation goes to **+25,000** and `trader_wallet` to **-25,000**, and the global sum stays **0**, so the zero-sum trigger passes, `LEDGER-C1` passes, and the unique key does not see it because the second posting has a different key.

**THE THIRD IS WHAT DECIDES THE NUMBER.** The defect being closed is *a state transition happened and no posting followed*. Closing that with a builder reproduces it: a builder proves what a caller who called it built and says nothing about a caller who did not. [STATE_MACHINES:213](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) records that failure mode against **this exact table** -- the halt that was *"representable and entirely unenforced for four migrations"*, which *"arrived by writing half a mechanism and reading the other half as done"*.

### 7. Findings, with their owners

**1. [`0009:103-104`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql)'s comment claims two rules and the constraint beneath it enforces one.** *"A reversal may not reverse itself, and may not chain onto another reversal"*, against `CHECK (reversal_of IS NULL OR reversal_of <> id)`. EXECUTED: a chain LANDS. The rule is enforced one table over by [`0038:323`](../../packages/db/migrations/0038_account_adjustments.sql), which cites this comment as its authority. **Reported and not closed**: it is a general ledger rule and closing it from a withdrawal's fence would be this session ruling the ledger on the way past. Owner: whoever holds `packages/db`'s ledger next.

**2. [ADR-187](../decisions/ADR-187.md) section 9's nominated discriminator is not one.** That entry names the failed-withdrawal case as the cheapest place to see whether `LT-06`'s and `LT-07`'s slots are the same account. **`LT-09` touches `LT-06`'s slot and no other**, so under a two-account reading the obligation still returns to zero and the account `LT-07` would have debited was never touched. The settlement path is no better: `ADR-187` section 6 row 4's zero was CONSTRUCTED on the single-account reading. **The question stays open and this ruling does not depend on it.** Owner: the entry that reopens `LT-02`.

**3. [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) section 2.1's lead-in says *"one of these five shapes"*** above a table that held eight rows before this session and nine after. Already false, and made more so. **Not corrected**, because the fence over that section is additive and correcting prose in an approved plan on the way past is how a fence becomes advisory. Owner: whoever holds `M05` next.

**4. `wallet_withdrawals` has no transition enforcement at all.** Zero non-internal triggers, seven CHECKs, none reading a transition. `WD-C1` narrows it without closing it, exactly as `0055` narrowed `0054`'s named hole.

**5. `wallet_withdrawals_approved_has_provenance` exempts `failed`**, which is the one terminal state where the summary is evidence of something. It is what makes `WD-C1`'s second assertion a proxy rather than a fact, along with [session 303](2026-08-27-session-303.md)'s finding that `'[]'::jsonb <> '{}'::jsonb`. **Not repaired**: narrowing it supersedes a constraint from `0011` and `0031` and would additionally refuse an undrawn `requested --> failed`.
