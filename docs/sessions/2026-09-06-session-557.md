### 3. THE RULING

[ADR-362](../decisions/ADR-362.md) sections 2 to 5 hold it.

**RULED: `PRODUCTION_CHECKOUT_ADAPTERS` DOES NOT REFUSE. IT FAILS ON ONE ARM AND SERVES ON THE OTHER.**

**RULED: `UNWIRED_CHECKOUT_BACKEND` DOES REFUSE**, completely, in the ordinary way, and cannot quote a figure under any configuration.

**RULED: THE SAFETY OF THE MONEY-IN PATH TODAY IS THE BACKEND PORT'S AND NOT THE ADAPTER PORT'S**, and nothing in the tree fails if a wiring slice installs the first without the second.

 "payment_method":"wallet","wallet_debit_cents":34900}

**A PAYMENT IS TAKEN, A PURCHASE IS RECORDED AND A FIGURE IS QUOTED**, against the value named `PRODUCTION_CHECKOUT_ADAPTERS`.

**The condition is named wherever the finding appears.** It requires the checkout backend to be installed, and `start.ts` installs neither port, so **no request this deployment serves today reaches it.** A reader who takes this as *"checkout is taking payments right now"* has read it wrong.
