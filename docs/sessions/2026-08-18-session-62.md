  - **`PT-07` FOUND THE GUARD ORDER THE SAME WAY.** It asserted the second application refuses with `not_forward` and got `expected ['account_closed'] to deeply equal ['not_forward']`. `advance.ts` line 188's terminal guard sits **ahead** of the same-day guard at line 198, so a day that closed the account is refused for being **terminal** before it is ever refused for being a **repeat**. Both refuse and both carry `prior` back unchanged, **which is exactly why the no-op half cannot tell them apart** and why the second half has to.

  - **BOTH EXCEPTIONS ARE PINNED RATHER THAN SKIPPED, ON `PT-01`'s RULE.** A test that skipped breach rows would accept **any** withdrawable there, including the negative one `PT-04` exists to forbid; a test that accepted "either of two kinds" would stop distinguishing a terminal account from a repeat. So the breach row must equal the prior day's value **exactly**, and the refusal kind is selected by the state rather than loosened.

  - **THE VACUITY RISK FOR `PT-04` WAS THE PHASE AND IT DECIDED THE WHOLE DESIGN.** `withdrawableCents` opens `if (state.phase !== 'funded') return 0n` ([`gates.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/payout/gates.ts) line 79). A fold that never reaches funded asserts `0 >= 0` forever and passes against an engine with **no floor at all**. Crossing the eval pass does not rescue it: a generated sequence reaches funded **at most once**, because `R-31` resets the balance and `DO-3`'s `INV-18` check refuses the next mark. Hence `fundedPrior`, and hence the three reachability counts.

  - **`>= 0` ALONE IS SATISFIED FOREVER BY A VALUE STUCK AT `0n`**, which is why the sign is asserted **with** the formula rather than instead of it, and why the reachability block insists the balance goes **above** `size + buffer` as well as below it. Without the upper case the equality could not tell `R-35` from a constant.

  - **A REACHABILITY ASSERTION WAS WRONG IN ITS FIRST FORM AND THE FIX IS NOT A LOOSENING.** It read "every folded step is funded" and failed, because a funded account that breaches closes and `DO-5` writes `phase: 'closed'` on that row. It now asserts funded days exist and **dominate**, which is the property that actually matters: those are the only rows on which the phase guard lets `R-35` run.

  - **THE PRIOR IS DATED `2026-01-01` AND THE DATE IS LOAD BEARING.** `fundedPrior` dates its own prior `2026-11-02` while `daySequenceArbitrary` draws its first session from `addCalendarDays('2026-01-02', n)` over `[0, 1400]`, so most sequences start **before** it and `DO-1` would refuse their first mark. One day before the generator's earliest possible session is strictly less than every mark it can draw. That day is compared at `advance.ts` line 198 and **never resolved against the calendar**, because every field `fundedPrior` inherits from `initialState` that could reach it is `null`.

  - **THE `PT-07` MUTANT IS CLEAN IN A WAY WORTH NAMING.** Narrowing `<=` to `<` leaves the **first** application's behaviour identical, since a real next-day mark is strictly after the prior either way. So the property fails on exactly the case it is about and on nothing else, which is what separates a mutant that proves a property bites from one that proves the suite notices damage.
