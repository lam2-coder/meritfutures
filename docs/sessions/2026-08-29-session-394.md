### Landmines

- **A COLUMN THAT CONSTRAINS NOTHING TELLS YOU NOTHING, AND AN EMPTY TABLE IS NOT A
  BLANK SLATE.** Three shapes already existed and each was defensible from where it sat.
- **THE WIRE SHAPE IS NOT THE STORE'S.** An allowlist may be lossy because a contract is
  an allowlist. The audit's left-hand side may not.
- **A PRECISION ARGUMENT CAN BE RIGHT FOR THE WRONG REASON.** `jsonb` holds a large
  integer exactly; `JSON.parse` does not. Naming the wrong leg would have made the ruling
  look overcautious to the next reader.
- **A BARE `toContain` ON A SYMBOL IS SATISFIED BY EVERY SUPERSTRING OF IT.** Twice now,
  on two different sessions, on two different names.
- **A RULING THAT LEAVES ITS OWN INPUT UNDECLARED MOVES NOTHING.** `ADR-204` ruled a
  projection over one leaf of a bag it never described, and three sessions recorded the
  result.
