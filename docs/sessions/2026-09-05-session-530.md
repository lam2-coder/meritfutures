Row `349`: re-derive the claim that `apps/worker/src/detectors/runner.ts` is blocked only on an adapter, write the adapter, and take three rulings on the record: what a detection writes and whether anything reads it, whether to schedule the job, and where a false positive could enter.

**One objective.** The adapter is a small file. The work was the re-derivation and the three rulings.

### 3. The finding: three blockers, and one of them is an adapter

**BLOCKER 1, THE EVENT SINK, IS THREE REFUSALS.** No sink is reachable from `apps/worker` at all (`RI-04`, `node-linker=isolated`, and `test/event-sink.test.ts` section 3). `detector.run_degraded` is a row in no version of `EVENTS.md`; [M07](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md) section 5 carries it marked NEW, so [ADR-159](../decisions/ADR-159.md) clause 1 would refuse the NAME. `detector.run_completed` is accepted by name since [ADR-205](../decisions/ADR-205.md) and would be refused at the SUBJECT, because the payload does not carry `detector_run_id`; `test/event-sink.test.ts` section 4b is the live pin on that and this row does not clear it.

**AND THE CONSEQUENCE IS THE PART NOBODY HAD WRITTEN DOWN.** `runOne` inserts the run row, the flags and the groups and THEN calls `emitRunEvents`, all inside one `io.transact`, and it calls it on every run rather than only on a degraded one. A rejecting `emit` rolls the whole thing back, `runOne` catches it, and the outcome is `recorded: false`. **A deployment holding the composed value writes zero `detector_runs` rows**, which the new suite asserts directly.

**BLOCKER 2: `detector_definitions` HAS NO PRODUCER.** `packages/db/src/seed/detectors/` holds a README, a generator, a source JSON and a rows JSON, and **no non-test file under any `src/` reads any of them** (derived by walking `apps/` and `packages/` with comments stripped: **zero**). A wired runner therefore raises `DetectorUnregistered` eighteen times.

**BLOCKER 3: 11 OF THE 18 SEEDED ROWS STATE NO NUMBER.** Derived with the generator's own `hasAStatedNumber` predicate transcribed and cross-checked against the file's declared counts: **18 rows, 7 with at least one stated number, 11 with none, 18 sensitive.** A detector reading an `unstated` parameter raises `DetectorDeclined`. **THE FIRST DERIVATION OF THIS WAS WRONG AND THE SUITE CAUGHT IT**: counting rows where no parameter has `state: 'stated'` gives **zero**, because every row states something that is not a number, and a case built on it would have passed over an assertion about nothing.

### 4. The three rulings

**RULING 1: A RAISED FLAG HAS NO READER, AND THIS ROW DOES NOT CHANGE THAT.** `apps/api/src/admin-source/flags.ts` implements `listFlags` in full, `AS-M7-03` clause 3's corroboration ordering included, and **`composeImplementedAdminReads` is called by nothing under any `src/`**: one occurrence, which is its own declaration. So `GET /admin/flags` serves nothing. **This row changes what could be WRITTEN and not what could be SERVED**, and the two absences are independent: wiring either alone leaves the pair useless.

**RULING 2: THE ADAPTER IS WRITTEN AND THE JOB IS NOT SCHEDULED.** First because it would do nothing: every run is `unrecorded`, and `CRON_INVENTORY`'s switch for this row alarms on a run's ABSENCE, so the estate would be paged nightly by a job that was running, which is worse than the job not existing. Second because that reason would lift and this one would not: `M07` section 3.3 makes severity a money decision every time it is written, and 4 and 5 is the band `G-HOLD-REQUIRED` reads to hold a payout for 48 hours under [ADR-040](../decisions/ADR-040.md). **A first run against the real estate is a decision with a date on it, not a consequence of an adapter landing.**

**RULING 3: NOTHING IN THIS DIFF IS A THRESHOLD.** The adapter reads no registry row on its own account, holds no constant and has no argument a threshold could enter through. `OQ-M7-02` and `OQ-M7-03` are the founder's, unanswered, and named as owed rather than guessed. **AND THE ESTATE IS PROTECTED FROM THE `sla_hours` GAP TODAY BY THREE INDEPENDENT MECHANISMS**, measured rather than assumed: `fills.ts` caps a wanted 4 or 5 to 3 and puts the reason in the evidence, `identity.ts` declines, `graph.ts` refuses. **Three files agreeing is not one decision**, and the day `sla_hours` is seeded all three unlock at once with no single place that says so; that is registered in the ADR's approval block rather than repaired.

### 5. The falsification, both directions

The real `D-01` through the real adapter over a recording door, same registry row, same code, differing only in the seeded rows.

| Case | Seeded | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Fires** | two accounts, one symbol, one side, four fills each at the same instants | `ok`, recorded, **two** `copy_cluster` flags at `open`/`internal`, each joined to the run id, each below severity 4 with no `sla_due_at` |
| **Silent** | the same accounts, every fill an hour or two from the other's | `ok`, recorded, `rows_scanned` non-zero, **zero** real flags, canary found, `degraded` empty |

**TWO FLAGS AND NOT ONE.** A copy cluster is an accusation about a relationship and the runner has no evidence which side led; asserting one would have been asserting that a detector picks a culprit.

**THE SILENT CASE WAS WATCHED FAILING.** `window_seconds` widened from 2 to 7200 in the registry row, nothing else changed, and the hours-apart fills become a cluster: `expect(quiet.realFlags).toEqual([])` goes red. **That is the whole argument for the case existing.** A firing case alone passes over a detector that fires on everything.

**AND THE COMPILE-TIME HALF OF THE BOUNDARY CAST WAS WATCHED FAILING TOO**: adding a bogus member to the detector read-table union makes `tsc` report `TS2322: Type 'true' is not assignable to type 'never'` at the assertion line.

### 6. Two neighbouring assertions went red, and one of them is a finding

**`breaker.test.ts:990` HELD A HAND-WRITTEN `29`** over barrel legs with a directory segment. The leg made it 30; the number and its history line are updated and the property that a leg DISAPPEARING fails is preserved.

**`expiry.test.ts` BROKE ON FORMATTING.** That case proves the two sweep files do not import the accessor by first proving `src/db.ts` does, and its positive control matches `from '@merit/db'` on the SAME LINE as the `import`. Adding three names pushed the statement past `printWidth`, prettier wrapped it, and **the control silently stopped finding the one file it is calibrated on.** Repaired on this row's side by importing the terms on a line of their own with the reason written above them. **A control a formatter can switch off is a finding**, it is not specific to that file, and auditing the class was not attempted.

### 8. Blockers and landmines

- **THE ADAPTER RECORDS NOTHING UNTIL THE EVENT SINK QUESTION IS SETTLED**, and the settling is not an adapter: it is either a reachable producer or a ruling on whether `emitRunEvents` should be unconditional. The ADR puts the second as an open question against `runner.ts`.
- **THE SECOND PARAMETER OF `postgresDetectorRunnerIo` IS A HOLE A NO-OP SINK FITS**, guarded by a text scan over `src/` and by a paragraph. A no-op there would commit every run and drop every `detector.run_degraded` page, which is `AS-M7-05` produced deliberately.
- **THE TEN RESERVATION ROWS ARE NOT WATCHED BY A GATE.** `CI-06f`'s [ADR-065](../decisions/ADR-065.md) T3 leg matches the literal `**Reserved, unwritten` and none of the ten carries it, on row `337`'s precedent. What catches a stale one is `CI-06w`'s one-row-per-key and the merge conflict itself. The dispatcher resolves them by hand.
- **`git diff --stat origin/main -- packages/db/migrations scripts/db` is EMPTY.**
