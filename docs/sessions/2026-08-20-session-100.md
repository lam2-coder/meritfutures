## 2026-08-20 - Session 100: ADR-071, `M21` admitted as the first new module after FREEZE (non-money)

- **Done:** **[ADR-071](../decisions/ADR-071.md) is written, `status: proposed`, approval line unsigned.** It admits `M21`, the Plan Designer and Simulation Console, subject to six requirements and nothing wider; states the Monte Carlo harness as **`DEP-M21-01`, a dependency that does not exist**, in `DEP-M7-nn`'s shape and with a degradation column; and rules `AS-M21-01` structurally, which is the argument for the contingent `0045`. **22 of 22 gates pass. The `M21` plan document is NOT written**, per the brief.
- **Next:** `FOLD-05` **P4** may write the `M21` plan on this ruling. **P7** is the harness and is not scheduled by this ADR. `0045` stays contingent.
- **Blockers:** ADR-071 is unsigned. It admits the first module after FREEZE and **sets the standard by which the next one will be argued**, so it is E2's rather than a session's.
- **Landmines:**

  - **THE REFERRAL'S "PORTED MONTE CARLO HARNESS" NAMES SOMETHING THAT IS NOT IN THIS REPOSITORY, AND BOTH QUOTES WERE CHECKED AGAINST THE SOURCE RATHER THAN THE BRIEF.** [`session.ts`](../../packages/rithmic/src/simulator/session.ts) opens `THE DAY MODEL. One account, one session, in integer cents and integer ticks.` and exists because *"the low is THE BREACH COMPARISON INPUT"*. **It produces equity paths. It produces no pass rate, no liability and no margin.** Inheriting the word "ported" would have put requirements (b) and (d) into a module plan with no delivery behind them.

  - **AND THE CORRECTION CUTS THE OTHER WAY TOO, WHICH IS THE HALF WORTH KEEPING.** A day model that is **breach-comparison-correct** is exactly the inner loop a Monte Carlo harness needs, and [`rng.ts`](../../packages/rithmic/src/simulator/rng.ts) says *"DETERMINISM IS THE WHOLE VALUE OF THIS PACKAGE, so the randomness is keyed"*. **Keyed determinism is what a reproducible trial loop requires** -- without it `AS-M21-01`'s remedy is not implementable at all. So the harness is a trial loop and an aggregator over something that already exists, which is a far smaller thing than a harness from nothing.

  - **`AS-M21-01` IS THE ONLY ONE OF THE FOUR WITH A STRUCTURAL ANSWER, AND THAT IS WHY IT DECIDES A TABLE.** `AS-M21-02` and `AS-M21-03` are answered by showing the sample size and showing the diff, and **both remain defeatable by a reader who does not look**. Stale calibration is not defeatable by attention: the numbers are as plausible as fresh ones at the moment of the decision. Binding the calibration identity and sample size onto the **publish record** rather than only onto the run is what makes a live plan traceable to what it was decided against.

  - **A CONSOLE THAT STORES NOTHING WOULD REPRODUCE THE SPREADSHEET'S AMNESIA IN A NICER INTERFACE**, and that is the sentence the whole admission turns on. The intent is not that a form beats a migration; it is that the parameters pricing the product are currently chosen in a document **nothing versions, nothing validates and no gate can read**. `M21` is admitted for moving that decision inside the system, so a designer with no record would be admitting a module for no gain.

  - **`CI-06a` REFUSED THE LINK TO `ADR-070` AND THAT IS THE SAME CONSTRAINT BOTH ADRs ARE ABOUT.** ADR-070 lands on the sibling P2 branch, so a link to it from this ref fails the link gate. Cited by bare name with the reason stated in the text, which is the convention `ALLOCATION`'s reservation rows already use -- **and it is exactly the mechanism [ADR-065](../decisions/ADR-065.md) T3 identified as the cause of that file's duplicate rows**: a reference cannot be a link until the thing it names is on the ref reading it, so the author who lands the file later must edit rather than append.

  - **THE SIX REQUIREMENTS ARE A CEILING RATHER THAN A LIST**, because "a plan designer" is the kind of name that grows a module by implication after the ruling that admitted it. Requirement (a) says the publish-time severity model is **displayed as it will fire, never re-implemented**: a console carrying its own copy of `PW-01` to `PW-04` is a second copy of a rule set, which is the drift [ADR-034](../decisions/ADR-034.md) exists to end.

- **Files touched:** [ADR-071](../decisions/ADR-071.md), [decisions/README](../decisions/README.md), [sessions/README](README.md), this file, plus the `adr_count` span in [INDEX](../INDEX.md) and [STATE](../STATE.md), which is the fence exception recorded in the pull request.

### What was admitted, and what was deliberately withheld

**Admitted:** the module, six requirements, `DEP-M21-01` with its degradation column, and `AS-M21-01`'s structural remedy.

**Withheld, each with its reason in the ADR rather than by omission:** the `M21` plan document (P4's), the harness (P7's, and named as a dependency rather than assumed), `0045`'s schema (contingent, money-adjacent, ADR-003), and the console's surface and authorization (the plan's). **No second module is admitted by implication** -- the harness is a dependency delivered in `packages/`, not a module.
