#### 1.3 THE FINDING, AND IT IS THE ONE THAT DECIDES WHAT THIS SLICE SHIPS

**Two approved documents disagree about whether `POST /wallet/withdrawals` reaches `approved` and posts
`LT-06` in the request, and the disagreement is on the money path.**

| Source | What it says |
|---|---|
| [M05 section 1.1](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) | ``POST /wallet/withdrawals -> ... -> post LT-06, debiting the wallet position -> status: approved -> enqueue transfer`` and *"There is no approval step in either list because there is no approver"* |
| [P5 section 8](../plans/P5-payouts-and-wallet.md)'s `P5-h` row | *"... the 10,000c minimum, one in flight, FIFO composition into `source_provenance_summary`, then `LT-06`"* |
| [API_CONTRACT:685](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) | `status: "requested" \| "cooling"`, annotated ***"The creation's reachable states, per STATE_MACHINES section 3.2"*** |
| [M05 section 2.1](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) | `LT-06` is kind **`wallet_withdrawal_approval`**, *"The external leg's **approval**"* |
| [STATE_MACHINES section 3.2](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) | `requested --> approved: G-WITHDRAWAL-CLEARED` is an edge **out of** `requested`, not into it |

**This slice takes the contract and does NOT post `LT-06`, and the argument is asymmetry of harm rather
than seniority.** A withdrawal created at `requested` or `cooling` with nothing posted is a row a later
ruling can advance; a `LT-06` posted at creation is an irrevocable entry against `trader_wallet` under a
`text NOT NULL UNIQUE` idempotency key, and the response would carry a `status` value the contract's own
union cannot express, on the one screen that tells a trader their money is leaving. **The contract is
also the document that governs the wire**, it was written FROM the DDL by [ADR-158](../decisions/ADR-158.md)
after seven contract-schema disagreements were found, and its clause 5 and finding 6 show its author
reading this exact seam. **And M05 section 1.1's external-leg listing is the same listing the corpus has
already caught twice carrying `settled_to_wallet`, a status value [ADR-028](../decisions/ADR-028.md)
explicitly rejected**, which makes it the weaker source for a status word specifically.

**IT IS REPORTED AS NEEDING A RULING AND THE NUMBER IS NOT TAKEN.** The dispatch allocates no ADR.

**The endpoint declares `required: 'passkey or dual_channel'` and `c27: 'external withdrawal'` and adds

neither `elevated_at` nor `elevated_by_factor`), and *"the reading a later engineer takes from two
controls covering one route is that one of them is redundant"*.

#### 1.5 The ordered behavior, every step cited

Validation at the wire, then ONE transaction:

1. **`Idempotency-Key` required** -> `validation_failed`. Section 1, and `wallet_withdrawals.idempotency_key` is `NOT NULL` under `wallet_withdrawals_identity_idempotency_uq`.
2. **Body**: `amount_cents` an integer > 0 and **>= 10,000c**, `destination_ref` a non-empty string -> `validation_failed`. Integer cents, `bigint`, no float anywhere.
3. **`beginIdempotent`** on the shared [`idempotency.ts`](../../apps/api/src/idempotency.ts) layer, outside the transaction, exactly as `payouts.ts` does: replay returns the original response verbatim, a different body is `idempotency_key_reuse` 409.
4. **`tx.lockScope()`** FIRST inside the transaction. [ADR-157](../decisions/ADR-157.md) clause 4: it is `INV-M20-01`'s per-identity lock, it takes no argument, and **it is the lock "one in flight" needs**, because the row a second request would create does not exist yet and cannot be `lockAt`ed. **An advisory lock through `sqlExecutor` is refused by name.**
5. **`identities.status === 'active'`** -> `identity_restricted` 422 ([ADR-075](../decisions/ADR-075.md)).
6. **`identities.payouts_frozen`** -> `payouts_frozen` 422.
7. **KYC `verified`** -> `kyc_required` 422, read with `accounts.ts`'s supersession rule.
8. **`G-NO-IN-FLIGHT`** over `requested, cooling, approved, transferring` -> `conflict` 409. **The application check is the whole control on this leg**: `wallet_withdrawals_open_idx` is NOT unique ([ADR-158](../decisions/ADR-158.md) finding 8).
9. **Balance** from `wallet_entries`, cross-checked against the stored running balance; `amount > balance` -> `insufficient_funds` 422.
10. **`G-DESTINATION-COOLING`** against `payout_destinations` at `(identity pinned, destination_ref)`. **An ABSENT row is "register it and cool it" and never "not cooling"**, which is [ADR-169](../decisions/ADR-169.md)'s stated obligation on this slice by name.
11. **FIFO composition** over `wallet_entries` oldest first, debits consuming the oldest credits, then `amount_cents` taken from the front -> `source_provenance_summary` and `earliest_credit_at`.
12. **INSERT** at `cooling` or `requested`.

#### 1.6 What cannot be computed, named rather than invented

- **The name-match score.** `kyc_verifications` carries **no name column** and no table anywhere holds a destination holder's name; the request body is `{amount_cents, destination_ref}`. `destination_name_match`, `name_match_score` and `name_match_method` stay NULL, which `wallet_withdrawals_score_has_method` permits.
- **`provenance_review` (P-1).** It needs *"payout credits from accounts purchased with promotional credit"* and **nothing records that**: `purchases.payment_method` closes at `psp | wallet | mixed`, and `promotional_credit_grants.funding_purchase_id` is the purchase that FUNDED a grant. It renders `false` for the same reason [`wallet.ts`](../../apps/api/src/routes/wallet.ts) renders `held_cents: 0`.
- **`insufficient_funds` is named by the endpoint row and is ABSENT from section 2's closed code table.** Reported; 422 is taken as the band every other business refusal there sits in.
- **The events.** `wallet.withdrawal_requested` and `wallet.withdrawal_cooling` are `P5-n`'s producer and no route module in this tree emits anything yet.
