### 1. What was measured, and the two commands that replaced a grep

**A dispatch prompt is not a source, and the brief that commissioned this session says so about six of its own claims.** Section 1 of [the plan](../plans/P6-live-tier.md) carries the full table; these are the two that changed a conclusion.

| Claim | Method | Result |
|---|---|---|
| *"The API serves 54 registered routes across a `public` and an `operator` surface"* | `discoverRouteModules()` then `buildServer({ surface, modules })`, reading `CompositionReport` for each surface | **36 registered / 18 withheld on `public`; 19 registered / 35 withheld on `operator`.** 54 is the UNION, and `GET /health` is the one path both register |
| *"The portal renders 12 routes and `next build` exits 0"* | `pnpm --filter @merit/portal build` | **Exactly right.** 12 rows, one of which is Next's own `/_not-found` |

**The route correction is load bearing for P6 rather than cosmetic.** The trader's socket is served by the deployment that registers 36 and the founder's by the one that registers 19, and the `withheld` list being non-empty **is** [ADR-083](../decisions/ADR-083.md)'s operator 404. A plan that reasoned about one surface of 54 would have put the admin live figure on the public origin without noticing.

### 2. The finding, and why it is a KIND split rather than a content split

`grep -rli websocket docs/architecture/` returns **nothing**. The live cache is named in six documents and always as the object of a grant, and in none of them with a medium. Against that, three pieces of tier 2 are already built and tested: the admin's live Open Liability arithmetic with its suppression and its three separately tiered terms, the simulator's streaming mode with a tick type carrying `indicative: true` as a required literal, and the portal's `Tier` and `Tiered`.

**So the phase is not "mostly absent" and not "mostly built". It is built wherever the work was a computation and absent wherever the work was a decision about a container**, and that is why wave 1 is four sessions none of which writes code.

### 3. The contradiction, and the sentence ADR-020 links that its own tier 2 breaks

[ADR-154](../decisions/ADR-154.md) carries the twelve findings. The shape is that `INV-M2-11`'s enforcement is an ARTIFACT (*"the simulator emits files, not objects"*), that [`packages/rithmic/src/index.ts`](../../packages/rithmic/src/index.ts) refuses the simulator satisfying `PlatformAdapter` on that artifact's account, and that **[M02](../plans/M02-rithmic-bridge.md) section 3.5 rule 4 nonetheless claims the discipline extends to a tier with no artifact.**

**The detail worth keeping is that [ADR-020](../decisions/ADR-020.md)'s own definitional link is one of the two clauses the ruling edits.** Its tier 2 clause links *"platform adapter"* to [GLOSSARY:298](../GLOSSARY.md), which enumerates five operations and ends *"v1 ships one implementation (Rithmic)"*. Both clauses are true today and both are false the moment tier 2 ships.

### 4. `GS-132` is weaker than it reads, and it was caught before the control was written

Its registry text poisons the cache and asserts the engine byte-identical. **`RI-01` and `RI-07` are green on every run and together mean the engine cannot read a cache of any kind**, so the assertion holds under any cache state including one that does not exist.

**The reachable failure is one layer out**, in the batch assembling the engine's inputs from a tick, and `P6-k`'s row says so and requires the test body to say so too. This is the same class as [session 261](2026-08-27-session-261.md)'s allowlist finding and [M06](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) `M6-N-04`'s tripwire: a green test that exercises nothing reads as coverage.
