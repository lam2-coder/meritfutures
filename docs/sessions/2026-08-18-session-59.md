### The refuting fact, verified twice and independently of #90

**Read first.** [`floor.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/floor.ts) line 116 guards the `R-15`
block with `!floorLocked`, and line 99 opens `advanceFloor` with `floorCents = input.priorFloorCents`.
On an already-locked day neither `R-13` nor `R-15` is entered and the prior floor is returned untouched.

**Then executed**, because reading a guard is how the original claim was made. `advanceFloor` was
called once with `priorFloorLocked: true`, `priorFloorCents: 4,750,000c` and a CORE-50K funded
drawdown, on a green day closing 5,100,000c:

| Returned | Value | What it refutes or confirms |
|---|---|---|
| `floorCents` | **4,750,000c** | **ADR-056 predicted 5,010,000c.** The locked floor is not re-assigned |
| `highWaterBalanceCents` | **5,000,000c**, against a close of 5,100,000c | The `hwb` freeze, and the row `0037` exempts |
| `lockEngagedAtProfitCents` | **null** | No re-lock event on a locked day, which is the 232-to-zero measurement in one call |

The script lived in the session scratchpad and not in the repository, so the tree carries no new file
from it. The two committed test files from #90 were also re-run and are green, 16 of 16.

- **Landmines:**

  - **THE REFUTED SENTENCE IS STILL IN THE ADR, ON PURPOSE, AND A LATER READER WILL BE TEMPTED TO
    TIDY IT AWAY.** It is quoted as a blockquote where its section used to be, under a heading that
    says it is false. **A ruling that quietly drops a wrong argument teaches its next reader nothing**,
    and this particular wrong argument is instructive twice over: it was a **correct reading of
    `floor.ts`'s header and a wrong reading of its code**, and the header is still there saying
    `floor(d) = floorLocked ? floor_lock_floor_at_cents : max(...)`. Anyone who re-derives the
    counterfactual from the comment will get ADR-056's original answer again. The quote is what stops
    that being a rediscovery.

  - **THE LEAD PARAGRAPH CARRIED THE REFUTED CLAIM AND SITS ABOVE THE SECTION THAT WAS DELETED, WHICH
    IS WHERE THIS REWRITE WAS MOST LIKELY TO MISS IT.** Line 5 read "the correction would breach every
    funded account". Deleting the named section and its table would have left that sentence standing
    as the ADR's **first** statement of the defect, in bold, three lines from the title. **The
    instruction named the section and the table; it did not name this sentence**, and a rewrite scoped
    literally to what was named would have shipped the false claim in the most-read position in the
    file.

  - **[STATE](../STATE.md) RESTATED THE REFUTED BREACH AS FACT AND NOTHING POINTED AT IT.** The
    founder-item row said "so the account breaches on its second funded day" with no hedge.

    right about the thing it was reasoning about: **the founder ITEM closes on the signature, not on a
    run.** The side effect was a false sentence living in an `approved` document for a day. **The two
    are different acts**: closing the item is the founder's, repairing a restatement the run falsified
    is not a ruling and needs no signature. The row now records the refutation and says in its own
    words that it is **still open**.

  - **[ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md)'s `056` row ALSO INVOKES ADR-052 AND IS DELIBERATELY
    LEFT ALONE.** It reads that ADR-052 "sharpened it: the lock is now an assignment rather than a
    term, so what happens to it at the reset is a harder question than it was", which is **not false**
    and asserts no breach. Editing a registry three branches are appending to, for no change in truth
    value, is a merge conflict for nothing, which is [session 58](2026-08-18-session-58.md)'s own
    reasoning applied to the same file. **Named here so it is fixable by whoever is next in it.**

  - **THE COUNTS IN THE ADR ARE ONE RUN'S AND ARE NOT REPRODUCIBLE TO THE DIGIT.** 3,442 folded funded
    days, 1,790 shipped breaches, 1,485 carried, 232 re-locks, 2,000 streams. The sweep asserts each
    `> 0` rather than pinning it, deliberately, so a re-run yields different numbers and **that is not
    drift**. The ADR says so in the sentence that carries them. What is structural, and what the
    argument actually rests on, is the ordering: both accounts leave `R-31` with the same floor, the
    carried one is frozen by two `!floorLocked` guards, the shipped one is non-decreasing, so the
    carried floor is at or below the shipped floor on every day.

  - **DIRECT-50K's NARROWING IS AN ABSENCE AND READS LIKE A REPRIEVE.** "The defect does not reach
    Direct" is true and is the wrong sentence. `phase_eval.enabled` is `false` with a null
    `profit_target_cents` (Appendix A.3), so Direct **opens funded and `R-31` never runs on it**: the
    transition `INV-07` is being amended against does not exist there. A reader who files Direct under
    "unaffected plans" will look for the same exemption on a future plan that does have an eval phase
    and not find it. **Merit Rapid was checked rather than assumed**, in Appendix A.2 and not only in
    the test transcription: eval profit target 300,000c and floor lock at profit 260,000c at 50K, both
    identical to Core, so its every eval pass is a lock day too.

  - **`0037` IS NOT WRONG AND THE ADR HAD TO SAY SO WHILE USING IT AS THE EVIDENCE.** Its
    `floor_locked OR high_water_balance_cents >= balance_cents` **correctly** stops refusing the rows a
    legitimately locked account produces, which is `GS-016`'s expected end state and the whole reason
    `0037` exists. Its side effect is that a carried-lock row is **indistinguishable from a legitimate
    one**. The finding is not "a constraint is too weak", it is **a right constraint hiding a wrong
    state**, and writing it the other way would send the next session to strengthen a migration that
    should not move.

  - **THE SILENT-FAILURE NARROWING IS THE ONE THAT CHANGES THE RISK AND IT ARRIVED THIRD IN A LIST OF
    THREE.** #90 recorded it alongside two narrowings that shrink claims. This one does not shrink
    anything: it says that under the literal reading there is **no breach, no assertion, no refusal and
    no rejected row**, so the state would be stored, published and settled against for the life of every
    funded account, in the trader's favour, with the first evidence a payout nobody could account for.
    **A defect that trips a constraint the day it ships is bounded by its own noise. This one has
    none**, and that is why it got a consequence row of its own rather than a clause in the table.

  - **THE ADR'S TITLE, RULING, CONSEQUENCES AND FIXTURE SECTION SURVIVED THE REWRITE UNCHANGED, AND
    THAT IS THE EVIDENCE FOR NOT WITHDRAWING IT.** A ruling whose conclusion needs a new wording after
