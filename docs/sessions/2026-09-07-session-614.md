### 1. The objective

Two, and they were dispatched together. **One:** take the retirement of the compatibility module at `apps/api/src/events.ts` that [ADR-418](../decisions/ADR-418.md) section 8 priced at three files, in one commit, re-deriving the three-file list rather than inheriting it. **Two:** re-derive the eight figures [ADR-417](../decisions/ADR-417.md) section 8 left carrying an unbound integer, report which are false on THIS base rather than on ADR-417's, and bind or delete them on ADR-417's own precedent.

### 6. Landmines

- **A PRICE HANDED ON IN PROSE IS A FIGURE NOBODY DERIVES.** ADR-418's price was short by one file and one case, and the row that quoted it truncated the sentence at the clause that made it look complete. **Re-derive a handed-on price before acting on it**, which is what this row was told to do and what found both errors.
- **DO NOT RE-DERIVE A REGISTER'S FIGURE WITH YOUR OWN WALK.** `scriptSources` filters out any file under a directory named `test` and `walk` honours `SKIP_DIRS`. A reimplementation that misses either is wider than the register and every figure it produces is about the reimplementation. **Use leg 7 with an empty `places` list**, which reports the total and the partition through the register's own code.
- **LEG 7 CANNOT BIND A ZERO REACH**, and `every census reaches lines on this repository` refuses the vacuous census correctly. `pgboss-job-store-migration` is the entry this hits.
- **LEG 7 ACCOUNTS FOR REPO-ABSOLUTE PATHS**, so censusing an artifact whose cases build synthetic trees taxes every future fixture with a finding it is not about. `replay-audit-src-caller` is that artifact.
- **THE ONE BINDING ON THE ANCHORED SENTENCE NOW SITS IN A FENCE NOBODY HOLDS.** ADR-418 put the marker `RI-35 BINDS THIS COPY` at the compatibility module and bound it with a case in `apps/api/test`. No row this wave may write that file.
