- **Landmines:**

  - **THE RESERVATION'S LITERAL WORDING WOULD HAVE RELEASED `0043`, AND RELEASING IT WOULD HAVE BEEN WRONG.** [ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md) made `0043` contingent on whether `admin_actions` "may already carry the shape" and said to release it "if the **attribution** is expressible without one". **Attribution is expressible without one and was already carried in full**: [`0017`](../../packages/db/migrations/0017_events_and_audit.sql) has `actor`, `action`, `subject_kind`, `subject_id`, `reason NOT NULL`, `before`, `after`, `evidence_refs` and `ip`, and `events.actor_kind IN ('system','trader','admin','vendor')` carries the trader half of the dual timeline through `GET /accounts/:accountId/timeline`. **What is carried nowhere is ON WHOSE INITIATIVE, which is a different question the reservation never separated from attribution.** Nothing in the schema distinguishes an admin acting **on** a trader from an admin acting **for** one, and in a dispute or a chargeback representment those are two different defences. **A contingency answered against the words rather than against the requirement is a contingency answered wrong**, and the requirement is `ADR-069`'s own rationale: an admin-attributed action preserves provenance only if the row says which claim it is making.

  - **`CloseRequest.kind` HAS NO COLUMN ANYWHERE, AND IT IS THE ROW THE WHOLE AUDIT RESTS ON.** The audit calls row 33 (`POST /admin/accounts/:accountId/close` with `kind: "trader_request"`) *"the shape the other 18 gaps would take if they were closed"* and *"the existence proof that the pattern works"*. **The existence proof cannot be queried.** `kind` is a field on a request type; [`0007`](../../packages/db/migrations/0007_accounts.sql)'s `account_status_history` carries `from_status`, `to_status`, `from_phase`, `to_phase` and a **nullable** `reason` and no `kind`, so the discriminator lands in `admin_actions.after` as jsonb or nowhere. `0043` takes that vocabulary unchanged rather than inventing a second one, and after it the existence proof is a column.

  - **THE COLUMN HAD TO LAND NOW OR IT COULD NEVER BE UNAMBIGUOUS.** `admin_actions` is append-only ([`0026`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql) revokes `UPDATE` and `DELETE` from `merit_app` and from `PUBLIC`) and its retention is forever. **A discriminator added after rows exist leaves every historical row `NULL`, and `NULL` is then ambiguous between "Merit's own act" and "written before the column existed".** So `NOT NULL` is only reachable while the table is empty, and E2 makes a merged migration unfixable rather than expensive. **The tempting alternative was "add it with the first parity route", and it is the one that quietly loses the property.**

  - **THREE OF THE EIGHTEEN ROUTES LOOK LIKE THE AFFORDANCE `AS-M6-06` SAYS MUST NOT EXIST, AND THE DISTINCTION IS OPEN VERSUS EDIT.** [M06](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) `AS-M6-06` reads *"Identity changes have no admin path at all. There is no screen that edits a verified identity"*, and `DEP-M6-06` says the same from M19's end. **Rows 9, 10 and 11 open a ceremony rather than editing a value.** Row 9's admin route **cannot complete** the phone change, because completion is `POST /phone/verify`, which is row 8 and is permanently excluded, so possession still has to be proved by whoever holds the handset; row 11 adds an **unverified** channel and [EC-115](../edge-cases/EC-115.md)'s ceremony holds; `withdrawal_hold_until` is not waivable by an operator. **An operator who runs all three has started a clock and proved nothing.** This was checked against `AS-M6-06`'s own text rather than assumed compatible, and the answer would have been different for a route that set the new number directly.

  - **A COVERAGE TEST WITH NO EXCLUSION LIST FAILS ON TEN ROWS ON ARRIVAL.** `GS-304` is registered as *"a coverage test rather than a behaviour test"* that fails when a trader route arrives with no admin equivalent. **Ten rows will never have one and four of them are credential forgery.** Without a declared exclusion set the gate is red the day it is written, and [STATE](../STATE.md)'s own sentence is that a gate which fails on arrival is a gate somebody switches off. **So ruling the NOT-PARITY rows was not tidying: it is the precondition for `GS-304` being writable at all**, and the audit deliberately left them unruled because a review verdict does not rule.

  - **DUAL CONTROL ON ROW 17 NEEDED NO AMENDMENT TO [ADR-010](../decisions/ADR-010.md), AND REACHING FOR ONE WOULD HAVE COLLIDED WITH `ADR-067`.** An admin-originated external withdrawal is money leaving the firm on an operator's assertion that the trader asked, so it is dual controlled. The obvious route to that is amending ADR-010's sensitive set, which today is cap, split, gap, treasury and rail credentials. **`ADR-067` is amending that same set concurrently, for adjustments.** The precedent that avoids the collision is one route over and already in the corpus: [M20](../plans/M20-wallet.md)'s `POST /admin/wallet/:identityId/correct` is already *"compensating entries only, **dual controlled**, reason required"*, declared in its own plan. **A control declared on a route is not an amendment to the ruling that lists sensitive config.**

  - **`M06:329` IS CITED FOUR TIMES FOR A ROW THAT SITS AT `M06:330`.** [FOLD-04:39](../plans/FOLD-04-impersonation-and-admin-parity.md), FOLD-04:55, the audit at :81 and at :340. **Line 329 reads "Breaker and CUSUM behavior including small samples"** and the `M6-N-nn` row is the next one. **The quoted text is right and the number is off by one**, so nothing downstream is wrong and no repair is proposed. It is recorded because it is the fourth propagation of a citation nobody re-ran, and because `M06` is a file four sessions were appending to simultaneously: **this session cites `M06` by section and identifier rather than by line**, which is the only stable citation into a document under concurrent append.

  - **THE AUDIT'S ARITHMETIC WAS RECOUNTED AND IT HOLDS, WHICH IS WORTH THE FIVE MINUTES IT COST.** `34 = 2 + 18 + 4 + 10` reproduces row by row off its section 4 tables, and the section 5 grouping (4 + 2 + 4 + 5 + 1 + 2) reproduces to eighteen. **A ruling that takes its input's counts on trust has an unchecked claim in its foundation**, and the reconciliation session's three worst errors were all failures to check a claim against its primary source.

  - **`0043` SHIPS WITHOUT A PROBE AND THAT IS A CHOICE.** The biconditional is a `CHECK`, so the workflow's forward-only apply proves it **installs**; a probe would prove it **refuses**. Wiring one needs a step in `corpus.yml` and a pin in `gates.mjs` under `CI-06s`, **neither in this session's fence**, and an unpinned probe is `OI-07` for the fifth time. `0041` shipped without one for the same reason.

### What was ruled, and what was deliberately not

**Ruled:** eighteen gaps close at `owner` with the initiative recorded; the C-27 factor substitution is accepted and `initiative = 'trader_request'` is the record of which claim was made; the phone-change hold is not waivable; the reset-velocity signal and the preference-change detector both read `actor_kind`; admin-originated external withdrawal is dual controlled and payout origination is not; the ten NOT-PARITY rows are permanent declared exclusions; `0043` is spent.

**Not ruled, and each says why in the ADR rather than going quiet:** whether an `owner` admin may record a ToS acceptance (`OQ-F4-01`, legal, and it blocks the whole commerce group), whether a provider will honour an operator-initiated KYC session (`OQ-F4-02`, a vendor answer in writing), where the `ops` boundary falls for the account-recovery group (**no `OQ-nn` identifier claimed**, because [FOLD-04 section 8](../plans/FOLD-04-impersonation-and-admin-parity.md) owns that namespace and was not in this fence), and how a requester is verified (a support-process vocabulary nobody has agreed, and a `CHECK` over invented values reads as a control and behaves as a coin flip).

**Three findings carried out and deliberately not repaired**, each owed to a plan this session does not hold: `POST /offers/redeem` is cited by [M14](../plans/M14-loyalty-retention.md) and absent from [M17](../plans/M17-offers-engine.md)'s surface table; affiliate creative approval has no route on either surface ([M08](../plans/M08-affiliate-system.md)); and the eighteen routes are owed to API_CONTRACT.
