#### 2. The shape the two holes force, and it is not a preference

The receiver's storage divides cleanly and the division is what decides this slice.

**What IS reachable today: the `psp_webhook_events` INSERT.** The table is `firm`, so `transaction(firmDb(), tx => tx.insert('pspWebhookEvents', ...))` runs `unscopedInsertStatement`, which carries no `WHERE` and needs none. **This is why the receiver never updates a row**: the business result is decided before the insert and written IN it, and the duplicate is the unique index refusing the second insert. That is the dispatch's own instruction ("your dedupe RIDES ON IT rather than re-checking it in application code") arriving as the reason the defect in item 4 cannot bite this path.

**What is NOT reachable today: naming one row.** The idempotency layer's whole job is to find ONE row by its key and stamp THAT ROW with a response. No accessor in [`scoped-db.ts`](../../packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts) takes a predicate. `scopedTx.update('idempotencyKeys', values)` would stamp **every idempotency record this identity owns** with one response body, and the next replay of any key on any endpoint would serve it. `systemTx.update` would do it to every identity at once.

**So this session writes no `packages/db` implementation of the layer, and says so rather than routing around it.** Both halves are declared as PORTS, the protocol over them is pure and fully asserted, and the ruling that unblocks them is [ADR-109](../decisions/ADR-109.md).
