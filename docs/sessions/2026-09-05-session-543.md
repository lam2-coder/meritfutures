**LANDMINES.**

- **A SEEDED DIVERGENCE THAT DOES NOT MOVE THE DIGEST IS NOT A DIVERGENCE, AND THE SUITE WILL AGREE WITH YOU.** `B.2` compares `state_hash` FIRST and diffs columns only on mismatch, so a stored row whose balance was edited and whose hash was not is a row the audit correctly ignores. The first draft of case 3.2 seeded exactly that and passed while claiming to detect a divergence. **A real divergence is a row storage BELIEVES that the replay does not reproduce**, so the seed has to re-hash the moved state. `stateHash` is imported by the suite and by no line of `adapter.ts`, and case 1.3 asserts that on the imports.
- **`external` AND `prior` ARE FILLED BY `accountDaysFrom` AND READ BY NOTHING, AND ONE OF THEM CAN STOP THE WHOLE AUDIT.** `resolveExternalGates` refuses a leg it cannot derive, `runReplayAudit` catches nothing, and its account loop is sequential, so one account with an unreadable KYC chain stops the audit for the entire book over a field `INV-23` excludes from the comparison. That is `OQ-346-02` and the narrower port is the repair.
- **THE AUDIT IS A SECOND READER OF THE SAME TABLES AND THERE IS STILL NO ACCOUNT LOCK.** `FM-10`'s lock is on the ACCOUNT and `SystemTx.lockAt` publishes one over a ROW. Both of the audit's reads are on ONE transaction so they cannot disagree with each other; what they can disagree with is the world after the audit started. A settlement webhook committing mid-run does not corrupt a report, it dates it.
- **A GREEN REPLAY REPORT IS A DETERMINISM CLAIM AND NEVER A RULE-COVERAGE CLAIM, AND NOTHING ON THE REPORT SAYS SO.** `ReplayAuditReport` counts accounts, rows, in-scope, matched and diverged, and carries no rule figure. Four rule ids are outside the fold by design and a fifth reading of the same trap is available every time somebody sees `diverged: 0`.
- **`repo-invariants.mjs` ON A TREE WITH NO `node_modules` IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF THE TREE, AND ITS OUTPUT LOOKS EXACTLY LIKE A FINDING.** This row's first baseline reported `ERROR RI-18 ...
