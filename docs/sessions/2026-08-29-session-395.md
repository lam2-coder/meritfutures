Session 392 recorded the dependency one way: "a landed writer clears term 1 and leaves

`rule_states` has two `jsonb` columns and they reached a declared shape by two routes.

| Column | How it is declared |
|---|---|

**THE FIRST ANSWER WAS AN INJECTED PORT WITH A REFUSING DEFAULT AND NO RULING TAKEN**,

RULING IMPLEMENTED**, because `ADR-206` landed and declared it. Both are in the file: the

**`encodeEngineGates` IS RULINGS 1 TO 5 AS CODE**: the engine's own value, six groups and

  strong enough that the guard behind it became unreachable from every case, and the suite
  stayed green with the call deleted. **A guard is watched at its CALL SITE or it is not
  watched.** Section 8.
- **A SYNTAX `tsc` ACCEPTS CAN BE A SYNTAX THE DEPLOYABLE CANNOT LOAD.** Four controls
  green, `node --experimental-strip-types` refusing at load. Parameter properties, `enum`
  and `namespace` are the three measured members.
- **A DRIVER ERROR ARRIVES WRAPPED.** A guard reading `error.code` on a Drizzle failure
  reads `undefined` forever and never fires. Walk `cause`, and bound the walk.
- **AN ORM WILL TAKE A `number` FOR A `bigint` COLUMN AND SAY NOTHING.** The row is
  written, the value reads back as a `bigint`, and the only thing lost is the precision
  nobody looked for.
- **`jsonb` RE-ORDERS KEYS BY LENGTH THEN BYTEWISE.** Any comparison of a stored bag with
  a written one must be order-insensitive, and any hash over a round trip is a different
  serializer.
- **A WRONG COUNT INSIDE A PERSUASIVE PARAGRAPH ROTS QUIETLY.** `hash.ts` said four leaves
  were `Cents` and seven are; the number understated its own argument, so nothing read it
  again for months and this session inherited it into two more files.
- **A CLEARING CONDITION'S TERMS CAN BE MUTUALLY DEPENDENT WITHOUT SAYING SO**, and two of
  them can land the same night from two fences. Term 1 read as a code slice and term 2 as
  a document, so the pair read as parallel. It was not.
