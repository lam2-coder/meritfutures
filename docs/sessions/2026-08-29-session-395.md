Session 392 recorded the dependency one way: "a landed writer clears term 1 and leaves

finding: a writer cannot fill `engine_gates` either.

`rule_states` has two `jsonb` columns and they are in different positions.

| Column | Position |
|---|---|
| `context_gates` | **DECLARED IN CODE.** `batch/ports.ts`'s `StoredContextGates` is the stored shape, its own comment rules the engine's five fields over `0015`'s four, and every leaf is a boolean, a string or `null`. It goes to the column as it stands |

   witnesses it is not contrived: sixteen of the twenty-five leaves pass through an encoder
