### 8. Landmines

**THE FENCE WAS CROSSED IN [`apps/worker/src/index.ts`](../../apps/worker/src/index.ts) BY TWO LINES AND IT WAS FORCED.** `digests.test.ts` case `9.3` fails any module under `src/` that is in none of the barrel's three lists, and those lists live in that file. The alternative, `WORKER_MODULES_NOT_RE_EXPORTED`, stays inside the fence by shipping a barrel that does not carry the slice. **A row that adds a module to `apps/worker/src` should name `index.ts` in its fence from the start.**

**SEVEN CITATIONS OF `ledger-posting-authority.test.ts:175` ARE INVALIDATED AND NONE IS REPAIRED.** [ADR-315](../decisions/ADR-315.md), [ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md) row `315`, [STATE](../STATE.md) twice, [this README](README.md) and [session 506](2026-09-04-session-506.md) twice all cite it for *"this deployable declares no `@merit/ledger`"*. **The assertion no longer exists at any line**, so the repair is a rewrite and not a number, and repointing it would aim it at a line that now says the opposite. Five of the seven are outside this fence. [ADR-212](../decisions/ADR-212.md) already rules the remedy: a pointer quoted as HISTORY is written out of citation grammar.

**`wiring.test.ts`'s `useWithdrawalBackend` REASON IS NOW TWO THIRDS FALSE.** It says *"the worker declares no `@merit/ledger`, `ExpiryLedgerPort.postLt01` has no adapter, and there is no job store"*. Only the third clause survives, and `0079` may yet move that one. It is a `BLOCKED` reason checked for length only, so nothing is red. **Outside the fence, not repaired.**

**A PRE-EXISTING STALE CITATION, NOT MINE AND NOT REPAIRED.** [ADR-239](../decisions/ADR-239.md) cites `apps/worker/src/index.ts:1268` for `export function main(): void {`. [ADR-241](../decisions/ADR-241.md) moved `main` to [`job.ts`](../../apps/worker/src/job.ts); the line held something else before this row and holds something else after it. **The repair is a path and not a number.**

**THE ADAPTER'S REFUSAL IS A RUN-TIME ONE AND THE SWEEP RUNS UNATTENDED.** A wiring that opens a transaction and forgets to record it does not fail a build; it fails on a clock as a rolled-back release, a payout that stays held and a nightly assertion. `recordExpiryTransaction` returning its argument is the narrowing that makes the wrong arrangement hard to write. It is not a compile error and cannot be made one without reversing [ADR-315](../decisions/ADR-315.md).
