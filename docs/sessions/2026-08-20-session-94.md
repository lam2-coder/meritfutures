- **Landmines:**

  - **THE ELIGIBILITY QUESTION IS A FALSE PAIR AND TAKING THE TRADE AT FACE VALUE COSTS THE RULING.** FOLD-03 section 6 offers "excluded and therefore unwithdrawable" against "admitted and therefore an obligation under zero denial", and **both horns assume one balance**. [M20:41](../plans/M20-wallet.md) says in terms that the wallet is "the only balance in the system with two exits". **Worse, the second horn describes a door that does not exist**: [`gates.ts:78`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/payout/gates.ts) computes `withdrawable = max(0, balance - size - buffer)` from the **trading** balance, so no ledger posting is an input to it and "admit into eligibility" is not reachable from any table. **The ruling is a third path and the trade is stated rather than skipped**: the credit lands in the wallet, is excluded from the payout computation by construction, and is withdrawable on the wallet rail. **The residue is real and is written down**: the wallet rail's own gates apply, including [M20:137](../plans/M20-wallet.md)'s **10,000 cent minimum**, so a goodwill credit smaller than that cannot be withdrawn on its own.

  - **THE DEBIT IS THE DANGEROUS DIRECTION AND THE ANSWER IS A ROW THE DATABASE REFUSES, NOT A RULE AN OPERATOR FOLLOWS.** A debit must name a credit **this table itself posted** and match it in identity, destination and cents, with at most one per credit. **So a debit can only remove money Merit itself put there by adjustment and can never reach a cent the trader earned**, which is what keeps it clear of `INV-M6-03`. Forbidding the debit entirely was close and is recorded as the rejected alternative it was: it would have left a mistaken credit uncorrectable in the direction it was made, and the remedy would then be an ad hoc ledger posting **outside** this table, which is the unaudited path the whole entry exists to close.

  - **THE THREE ERRORS [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) NAMES WERE ALL LIVE IN THIS EXACT FILE, AND ONE OF THEM ALMOST LANDED.** A ledger class that does not exist: the chart of accounts has **no expense class**, so goodwill has no natural home and an eighth class was the obvious reach. A debit account that breaks ruled recognition timing: `firm_treasury` is the available mistake and books a cash movement when no cash moved, which [M05:140](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) already refused once on `LT-01`. A sign written backwards: `ledger_entries` is signed with **positive as debit** and `account_adjustments` follows `wallet_entries`' magnitude-plus-direction convention instead, so the file holds **two conventions at once**. Each is answered in the migration header against a primary source, and the sign mapping is written out as a table in both the header and [ADR-067](../decisions/ADR-067.md) section 2.4.

  - **THE ASSERTION A READER WOULD HAVE COUNTED AS A CONTROL COULD NOT FAIL, AND ONLY RUNNING IT SAID SO.** `ADJ-C1` checked "the row you are reversing must be a credit" before "it must not itself be a reversal". Both catch a chained reversal, so the second never ran and **its message is the one that names [`0009:104`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql)'s rule in the corpus's own words**. Swapping them exposed the deeper half: `account_adjustments_debit_is_a_reversal` makes every debit a reversal, so **the direction branch is now unreachable for any input**. It is kept as `LEDGER-C2`'s kind of second line and **the comment says in terms that it is not the control**. Nothing about it looked wrong on the page; it took 18 counterfactuals against a real database to find that one of six branches was decoration.

  - **TWO OF THE TESTS WERE WRONG IN THE SAME SHAPE, WHICH IS THE TRANSFERABLE PART.** `trader_withdrawable` and `firm_treasury` both appear in `0038` **in prose explaining why they are not used**, inside a `COMMENT ON` payload and a `RAISE` message. A substring search could not tell the documentation of the rule from the rule being broken, and both assertions failed on a correct migration. `ddl()` now strips `COMMENT ON` statements as well as `--` lines, and the `fees_revenue` assertion matches **the comparison** rather than the word. **[`economic-calendar.test.ts`](../../packages/db/test/economic-calendar.test.ts) already carried the `--` half of this lesson and not the `COMMENT ON` half.**

  - **`0038` WIDENS NO CLOSED LIST, AND THAT IS EVIDENCE RATHER THAN TIDINESS.** No new `wallet_entries.provenance` value: [`0011:74`](../../packages/db/migrations/0011_wallet.sql) has carried `correction` since the wallet was written, described there as "a compensating entry, never an update". No new `ledger_accounts.code`, no new `identity_status`, no new payout status. **The wallet was built to hold this and nobody had built the door.** Three properties therefore hold by construction with no rule written for any of them: an adjustment cannot manufacture payout eligibility, promotional credit cannot become wallet balance, and a debit cannot overdraw a wallet. **The third is enforced by a `CHECK` four migrations old** and the honest way to record it is as inherited rather than established here.

  - **GS-296 AND GS-298 DESCRIBE OUTCOMES THIS RULING DOES NOT PRODUCE, AND THE REGISTRY IS OUTSIDE THE FENCE.** `GS-296` expects "excluded **and** unwithdrawable, or admitted and flagged", and the ruling is **excluded and withdrawable**. `GS-298` expects "refused or held" against a `restricted` identity, and the ruling is **permitted**, because [M20:62](../plans/M20-wallet.md) `INV-M20-06` already blocks spend and withdrawal and a second gate here would be a second expression of one that exists. **Both are owed an amendment in place** ([ADR-065](../decisions/ADR-065.md) T3), numbers unchanged, on session 89's precedent. `GS-297` and `GS-299` stand as written. **The migration's tests assert the ruling and not the stale rows**, so they are what fails if the amendment lands the other way.

  - **FOUR SIBLINGS ARE WRITING [M06](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) AND THREE THINGS WERE DELIBERATELY NOT WRITTEN THERE.** No `INV-M6-15` was claimed, though the identity-status display requirement and the live-figure term both read like invariants: **an invariant number is an allocation**, and claiming one against three concurrent holders is how two `INV-M6-15`s land in one merge. Both ride on the `SD-M6-09` row instead. Section 3.5's prose is owed the **third term** the ruling gives the live Open Liability figure, same reason. And **[M06:64](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) still reads "Six deltas"**: it was already wrong before this session and will be wrong by a different amount after every sibling lands, and **a four-way merge conflict on one numeral is worse than a number the next session fixes once.**

  - **FOUR [ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md) ROWS NOW SAY SOMETHING FALSE.** `ADR-054`, `ADR-056`, `ADR-058` and `ADR-059` each end with the words "`0038` stays free". **They do not any more.** `docs/decisions/ALLOCATION.md` is not in this fence, so the amendment is owed to whichever session next holds it, in place rather than as a second row. **This is the same situation [session 99](2026-08-20-session-99.md) recorded one registry over**, and recording it here is what stops the next holder adding rows instead of amending them.

  - **THE PER-ROW DUAL-CONTROL `CHECK` IS NOT THE WHOLE CONTROL AND THE FILE SAYS SO.** Repeated sub-threshold credits by one actor are unconstrained. `OQ-F6-02` names it, the shape of the answer exists at `INV-M20-07`, and its home is velocity policy and [CRON_INVENTORY](../ops/runbooks/CRON_INVENTORY.md), both outside this fence. **`account_adjustments_actor_idx` was created so that whoever rules it already has the query.** The reason this matters more here than for the other five members of ADR-010's set: for a cap or a split, a compromised session must wait for traders to act before money moves; **for an adjustment it credits a wallet it controls and withdraws.**
