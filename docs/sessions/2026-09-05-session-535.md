### 4. What was decided

**WHAT A PUBLISHED STATISTIC COMMITS MERIT TO.** The checkability is the product, so the failure that matters is not a wrong number but **a number that is right about a set nobody described**, which a reader cannot detect. Three ways this run could produce one are refused: a denominator short of its definition, a suppression whose stated reason is false, and a set selected by wiring rather than by a person. None of that licenses withholding an unflattering figure; `INV-M12-08` has no approval step and the only path to a suppression is the sample floor fixed before the data existed.

**THE TWO REFUSALS.** `ST-02` and `ST-06`, section 4 of the ADR, each with the artifact that discharges it named: a per-plan config row for the first, a stored `settled_trading_day` for the second.

**SCHEDULING.** Adapter yes, schedule no, and the adapter is not among the four reasons why.

### 5. Findings

1. **`SettledPayoutFact.terminalSettlement` was documented as a null test.** The column is a foreign key naming which payout closed the account, `ST-04` excludes terminal settlements, and the null test therefore removes every payout a graduated trader ever received from the published mean and median. Amended in place under `RI-14`, correction beside original; the migrations are untouched.
2. **The supersession pointer was read backwards** in the first `effectiveDefinitions`, retiring the successor and keeping the predecessor. Watched red by the case written before the predicate.
3. **A run that publishes nothing reports `published`.** [ADR-119](../decisions/ADR-119.md)'s defect at a second site. Pinned by a case, reported, not repaired: the remedy is a halt-reason member and that is a ruling.
4. **The transparency surface is 404 rather than stale.** Section 3.
5. **A second hand-maintained barrel-leg total exists** in `test/breaker.test.ts`, independent of `WORKER_BARREL_LEGS`, and it moves 29 to 30 here in the idiom its five previous increments use.
