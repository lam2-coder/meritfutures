### The two decisions the brief asked for, and the first one came back a different shape

**Decision one was "how many of the six can run concurrently, and on what axis do they collide", with [ADR-092](../decisions/ADR-092.md) offered as the precedent.** The precedent is exactly right and the subject is not routes.

| The question | The answer, measured |
|---|---|
| What do the six actually collide on? | **`packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts`.** Every P3 content is a WRITE and both accessors carry `rows()` and nothing else |
| Who says so? | **[ADR-092](../decisions/ADR-092.md) section 9, about itself**: *"This entry rules NOTHING about the write accessor"*, and [ADR-094](../decisions/ADR-094.md) section 5 repeats it |
| Had anyone else found it? | **Two module measurements, independently.** [Session 157](2026-08-24-session-157.md)'s `M3-a` and [session 168](2026-08-24-session-168.md)'s `M19-0` |
| Is the route registry still an axis? | **Yes, second**, and the transferable part of ADR-092 is the third clause: the queue is the TYPE CHECKER. It transfers **only if** `P4-d` declares the registry total over a closed endpoint union with a per-endpoint disposition and a count assertion |
| So how many run concurrently? | **`P3-f` alone first**, then **two library slices always concurrent**, then **four route slices that are four branches under a total registry and one queue without it**. Both branches are dispatched |

**Decision two was where auth goes.** Third. **Not last**, because `POST /checkout` at [API_CONTRACT:285](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) reads `Auth: session`, `POST /kyc/session` at `:488` reads the same, and section 12's first sentence is *"Every row is a named test that must exist **before the endpoint ships**"*. **Not first**, because its front door writes a table the registry cannot hold. It does not. So **nothing in this workspace can enqueue a job**, and [session 147](2026-08-24-session-147.md) recorded exactly that and could not fix it from inside its fence.

  - **`SystemReason` IS CLOSED AT TWO AND A REQUEST HANDLER IS NEITHER, WHICH TAKES OUT THREE CONTENTS AT ONCE.** `psp_webhook_events`, `coupons` and `integration_contracts` are all **`firm`** in [`scope.ts`](../../packages/db/src/scope.ts), therefore excluded from `ScopedTableKey` by construction, therefore readable only through `systemDb('nightly-batch' | 'operator-console')`. **The webhook receiver does not know whose purchase it is until after it has persisted the event**; checkout recomputes the coupon server side; the enrichment adapter reads its contract row. **[ADR-096](../decisions/ADR-096.md) is the precedent and it went the OTHER way**, refusing a third member for `apps/site` and ruling the site *"is not a reader of this database at all"*. **That remedy is unavailable here, because under ADR-096 `apps/api` is the process the site reads THROUGH.**
