## 2026-08-18 - Session 56: `INV-21`'s two missing fixtures, `GS-081` and `GS-083` (non-money)

**Written as a stub first so the session was on the record before it had a result, and completed in
place.** The fence is [`packages/rules-engine/fixtures/`](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/README.md)
and this file.

- **Objective:** `INV-21` is "a settled payout can never breach the account that earned it", derived
  from `CV-11` (lock enabled) and `CV-17` (lock disabled), and the objective as received names **three** fixtures
  for it. **`INV-21`'s own row names two**, which is the first landmine below. `GS-065` landed in session 55. **`GS-081` and `GS-083` do not exist.** Write them from
  their registry rows and M01.
- **Why now:** [ADR-052](../decisions/ADR-052.md) was accepted **because** the `max` reading made
  `INV-21` unenforceable, and the counterexample that decided it was **computed by hand in an ADR
  rather than pinned by a fixture**. `INV-21` is now safe and nothing executable says so.
- **Done:** **`GS-081` is written and green; `GS-083` is not writable in this format and the reason was
  re-derived rather than inherited.** Files:
  [`GS-081-...untouched.yaml`](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/GS-081-settlement-leaves-the-floor-the-high-water-balance-and-the-lock-untouched.yaml)
  and its `.expected.json`, plus the held-back table in
  [the fixture README](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/README.md). The golden stage loads **34**
  fixtures with **one** red, `GS-064`'s `withdrawable_cents`, which is [ADR-054](../decisions/ADR-054.md)'s
  and not this batch's. Group H goes from **0** fixtures to **1**.

  **`GS-081` PINS THE LOCKED HALF OF ITS ROW AND SAYS SO IN THE FILE.** The row asks for a capped payout
  settling "on a locked account and on an unlocked one", and two facts split it. The format pins **one**
  `end_state`, so a fold carrying both settlements could only state the second. And `cme-2026`'s **seven**
  sessions cannot carry two gate-legal payouts on any v1 plan: `R-47` restarts the win-day count at the
  basis day, so a second `CORE-50K` payout needs five more win days, eleven sessions in all; on
  `MERIT-RAPID-50K`, whose cadence gap is 1 rather than 5, it is nine. `GS-065` is the unlocked half and
  is already in the directory, so the row is covered by two files rather than half-covered by one.

  **THE LOCK DAY CLOSES AT EXACTLY THE TRIGGER, AND THAT IS THE ONE DESIGN DECISION IN THE STREAM.** Five
  days of exactly 52,000c reach 5,260,000c, where `closing - size` is 260,000c and
  `floor_lock_at_profit_cents` is 260,000c, so `R-15`'s `>=` fires on equality. There the assigned floor
  (5,000,000 + 10,000) and the trailing floor (5,260,000 - 250,000) are **both 5,010,000c**. ADR-052 is
  accepted but the fixture is silent on it: a lock day where the two readings disagree would make this
  file a second measurement of that question instead of a measurement of `R-19`.

  **THE SETTLEMENT DAY CARRIES NO FILLS AND NO REALIZED P&L**, which is what makes "balance falls by
  exactly `approved_cents`" a reading rather than an inference. 5,260,000 - 5,110,000 = 150,000c with no
  second term in the subtraction.

- **Next:** **`GS-083` needs a home outside this directory and the row now names it.** A publish
  validation returns a `ValidationResult` against `(rules, sizes[])`; `EXPECTATION_KEYS` is
  `{end_state, events, pins, note}` and the loader folds a day stream and diffs a `RuleState`, so no
  fixture shape can state "publishing fails with `CV-17` named". `validatePlan` is never called anywhere
  in the fold. `RE-C-nn` owns it, alongside `GS-076` to `GS-078`, and writing it is one session in
  `packages/rules-engine/test`. **After that, `L-11` is still the biggest single blocker**: six rows
  (`GS-052`, `GS-053`, `GS-066`, `GS-067`, `GS-068`, `GS-082`) are behind a `settlements:` block, down
  from eight.

- **Blockers:** none for `GS-081`. `GS-083` is blocked structurally and permanently **in this format**,
  which is a routing decision rather than a blocker.

- **Landmines:**

  **`INV-21`'S ROW NAMES TWO FIXTURES, NOT THREE.** [M01](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) line 132 reads
  "Derived from CV-11 (lock enabled) and CV-17 (lock disabled), GS-065, GS-083". `GS-081` is named by
  `R-19`, `R-48` and `INV-06`, not by `INV-21`. [ADR-052](../decisions/ADR-052.md) line 52 and
  [session 52](2026-08-17-session-52.md) both say "`INV-21`'s three fixtures", and
  [STATE](../STATE.md) line 589 says the same. **The three-fixture count is a restatement that drifted
  from the row it restates**, which is the class `ADR-037` extended `CI-06g` to cover. Nothing here is
  wrong about the coverage; the count is wrong about where it comes from.

  **ADR-052 SECTION 2'S COUNTEREXAMPLE IS `CV-11`'S, AND THE STANDING INSTRUCTION TO TRANSCRIBE IT INTO
  AN `INV-21` FIXTURE WOULD PUT IT IN THE WRONG FILE.** [Session 52](2026-08-17-session-52.md) says the
  arithmetic "is `GS-065`'s content, and it should be transcribed rather than re-derived". It is
  `MERIT-RAPID-50K` with the lock **enabled**, landing one cent under a **locked** floor. `GS-083` is
  `CV-17`: lock **disabled**, `cap >= drawdown`, landing under a **trailing** floor. `CV-17`'s own row
  in M01 already carries that arithmetic. The two are `INV-21`'s two halves and transcribing either into
  the other's fixture is exactly the drift `TR-01` and `FM-16` exist to stop.

  **`R-15`'s ROW STILL CARRIES THE CLAIM `ADR-052` REFUTED.** [M01](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) line
  445 reads "CV-12 forces the trigger to sit exactly where the trailing floor already is, so **the floor
  never jumps**". `ADR-052` established that `CV-12` does no such thing in general: the post-lock
  relationship depends on the lock-day closing balance, which is runtime data. `CV-12`'s own row was
  corrected and `R-15`'s was not. **`GS-081` is deliberately built on the case where the claim happens to
  be true** (the lock day closes at exactly the trigger), so this fixture does **not** catch it and no
  fixture in the directory does. It is an M01 edit and wants an ADR, not a commit.

  **THE HELD-BACK TABLE'S REASONS ARE NOT SELF-CHECKING AND THIS IS THE SECOND BATCH TO FIND ONE WRONG.**
  `GS-081`'s recorded reason was "the settlement itself", which reads as though `applySettlement` computed
  something the row pins. It computes none of the row's five claims: the balance falls at `DO-3` through
  `R-10`'s adjustment, which is the route `GS-065` established a session earlier and which nobody carried
  back to the neighbouring row. **A reason nobody re-derives becomes a fact**, and the only defence found
  so far is re-deriving each one at the point of use.
