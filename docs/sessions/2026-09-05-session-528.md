### 3. What the table actually claims, which is the part the dispatch asked for first

**A ROW COUNT IS NOT A CLAIM COUNT.** The first row keys the range `1 to 13`, so **28 rows claim 39 numbers**.

**A LANDING-RECORD COUNT IS NOT ONE EITHER, IN EITHER DIRECTION.** Sections `1` to `13`, `15`, `16` and `22` take numbers and record no landing; `## 14.` heads **three** sections for three landings on **one** claimed number, which the `14` row records as *"CLAIMED THREE TIMES"*. A reader demanding one row per landing heading is wrong both ways at once.

**A LETTERED HEADING TAKES NO NUMBER**, which is the table's own written rule and not the check's taste.

**SO THE COMPARISON IS TWO SETS.** Measured on `f93a0cdd`: **56 `## ` headings, 50 numeric over 47 distinct numbers, 6 lettered; 28 rows claiming 39 numbers; 8 heading numbers unclaimed and 0 claims untaken.**

**`27` IS WHY THE ANSWER IS EIGHT AND NOT NINE.** It heads no section at all. The `31` row said that precisely and the `45` and `48` rows compressed it to *"26 to 30"*.
