### 7. Blockers and landmines

- **OWED AND IMPOSSIBLE FROM INSIDE THIS FENCE: the `catalog.ts` docblock cannot be annotated.** `RI-15` binds a citation to a line within two lines, and `apps/api/test/wiring.test.ts:958` cites `catalog.ts:1211` for `readSize`. A one-line insertion lands that pointer on a blank line and a two-line one on a bare closing brace, and `RI-15` refuses a pointer landing on nothing by a rule the window does not soften. **`wiring.test.ts` is owned by nobody this wave and this row was told not to open it.** Whoever repairs the handler under `OQ-373-01` repoints that citation in the same commit.
- **THE FOUR REPAIRS THAT LANDED WERE SIZED BY THE SAME MECHANISM.** A full-length correction in `admin-payouts.ts` breaks three citations in `ALLOCATION.md` row `365`, another row's cell, so all four are three lines and the argument lives in the ADR. `admin-certificates.ts`, `admin-wallet.ts` and `admin-writes.ts` each tolerate fourteen added lines with no damage at all, measured; they were held to three so that one defect found four times reads as one repair.
- **LANDMINE: nothing in this tree goes red on a PARTIAL install**, which is the only state in which the admin four's outer leg is reachable. ADR-365 section 10 recorded it and this row reproduces it rather than closing it.
- **LANDMINE: REACHABLE is measured with an empty body.** A status a module reaches only behind a well-formed request is invisible, and `checkout.ts` is the one place that bites today. A second such module would present as a false finding and needs another named explanation rather than a widened rule.
- **THE ADVERTISED PARSER READS ENGLISH.** Two narrowings are defended by seven sentences from this tree, and a docblock in a shape none of the seven anticipates would be read wrong. `OQ-373-02` records the alternative.
- **Concurrency: sibling rows are running now** and this row reserved two `ALLOCATION` numbers for them. A registry conflict at merge is expected and is the designed workflow.
