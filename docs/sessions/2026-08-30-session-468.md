### 1. The objective, and the answer

**Row `277` asked whether `calendarCarriesDay`'s row-existence test and `trading_calendar_loads` coverage coincide by construction**, and said in terms that ruling it a documentation defect would be a complete outcome.

**It is not that outcome.** The ruling is [ADR-277](../decisions/ADR-277.md), seven clauses, `proposed` and UNSIGNED.

**They do not coincide, the wrong-answer path is real, and the row had the two branches the wrong way round.** The branch it called sharper is the milder one; the branch it treated as merely coverage-blind is the one that folds a two-month-old day and stamps `rule_states` with it while exiting 0.

### 2. What the premise rests on, read at four sources

| Asked | Read at |
| --- | --- |
| A foreign key either way between the two tables | **NONE.** `0032` creates both; the only FK in the family is `trading_calendar_revisions.trading_day` |
| A trigger tying a calendar INSERT to a load | **NO, AND ON PURPOSE.** `0048` header item 7 names the coverage-window test and refuses it: loading a year writes one load row and then inserts roughly 250 days inside it |
| What direction the DDL commits to | **ONE ONLY.** *"A day inside these bounds with no trading_calendar row is a bug in the load. A day OUTSIDE them is unknown"* ([`0032:313`](../../packages/db/migrations/0032_trading_calendar_holidays_coverage_revisions.sql)). Silent on the direction that folds |
| Whether a loader makes them coincide in practice | **THERE IS NO LOADER.** It is `S-E4` and unwritten |
