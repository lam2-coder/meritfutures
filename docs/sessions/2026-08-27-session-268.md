#### 0.2 What this slice REFUSES, and the aggregate is the one that was asked for

**`P7` section 10 item 1 asks this entry for an AGGREGATE and says every `P7` wave-2 slice is blocked
without it. The answer is NO, and the reason is not scope.** `P7` section 3.1 states what its detectors
need in its own words: `D-01` is a **self-join** on `fills` inside a two second window, `D-02` a rolling
correlation over twenty trading days, `D-03` a variance of sums against a sum of variances. **Not one of
those is served by a scalar aggregate over one table.** An aggregate admitted here would spend the
widening, leave `P7` blocked on the JOIN it is actually blocked on, and hand the next reader a foreclosure
that reads as answered. Section 5 of the entry is the full argument, including the money half: a `SUM`
over `bigint` cents returns `numeric`, which `pg` hands back as a string, so a money aggregate is a
ruling about numeric transport and not about a predicate.

**Also refused, each named in the entry as one line with an argument when a caller has one:** strict
inequality, `OR`, `IN`, `IS NOT NULL`, `LIKE`, a term anywhere on the WRITE path, a multi-row lock,
`NOWAIT`, `SKIP LOCKED`, and every lock strength that is not `FOR UPDATE`.

**`SqlExecutorReason` stays at one member and `SystemReason` at two.** Both verified at the file rather
than from the dispatch's line numbers.
