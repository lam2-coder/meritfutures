### 4. The finding that matters most

**ADR-227 SIZED ITS RESIDUE BY ASKING WHETHER AN ENTRY'S `What a founder read adds` BLOCK DECLARES
AN OUTSTANDING JUDGEMENT. ITS SECTION 3 CLASSIFIES A DIFF BY THE SHAPE OF ITS CENTRAL CLAIM.** Those
are different questions and they come apart immediately, because `RI-13` REQUIRES every unsigned
entry to write that block and a good entry names residual choices even when its central claim is a
property.

Re-run over today's 52 money-path entries the classifier scores **46** as residue, **and it scores
all six entries read in full this session**, four of which now carry an assertion watched failing.
[ADR-176](../decisions/ADR-176.md) is the clean case: a property asserted at four sites, and a
founder block naming three genuine choices none of which is that claim.

**THE CORRECTION MAKES THE QUEUE SMALLER, SO IT IS OFFERED AS FOUR DEMONSTRATIONS AND NOT AS AN
ESTIMATE.** ADR-227 corrected its own classifier upward and wrote that a measurement corrected only
where the correction flatters the measurer is not a measurement. **This one runs the other way**, so
no replacement number is proposed. Four of fifty-two is not a sample.

### 5. The non-finding, stated rather than dressed up

### 6. Where this session is most likely wrong

**EVERY CLASS ASSIGNMENT RESTS ON READING ONE ENTRY'S HEADING AS ITS CENTRAL CLAIM**, and a heading
is a summary somebody wrote. **[ADR-192](../decisions/ADR-192.md) is the fragile one**: its heading
claims an ordering property and its own bullets say what is JUDGED is clause 2's trade. A reader who
calls that trade the point of the diff makes ADR-192 class C and one of the four falls.

**AND THREE OF THE FOUR ASSERTIONS ARE STRUCTURAL**, reading source text with a regular expression.
The behavioural leg of the new file and ADR-192's surface sweep are the two that execute something;
ADR-191's read rendered SQL with no database behind it. **A regime that counts a structural
assertion as an earned approval is buying less than it looks like, and nothing here measures how
much less.**
