### 1. The objective

Take `B5` term 1: export `planLeg` from [`payout-backend.ts`](../../apps/api/src/payout-backend.ts) or lift it beside the fold, and supply `EligibleFoldIo.resolvePinnedPlan` from it. [ADR-411](../decisions/ADR-411.md) section 12 item 2 and its approval note price the term as ONE EXPORT AND ONE CALL, and say the only reason that row declined it is that the cheap version lives one file outside its fence, *"a dispatch shape rather than a cost"*. **THIS DISPATCH WAS THAT WAVE.** It also required the claim to be re-derived before it was acted on, the losing option named, and the second `PlanVersionSizeRow` mapping refused.

### 2. Done

**THE CLAIM IS REFUTED AND THE TERM IS NOT TAKEN.** [ADR-413](../decisions/ADR-413.md) records it.

- **`catalogRowAt` IS DECLARED ON `ScopedTx` AND ON NO OTHER HANDLE IN `packages/db`**, derived over the interface declarations rather than read off one of them.
- **AND AN ADMIN READ HOLDS `SystemTx`**, through `AdminSourceBackend.operator`, whose authority its own docblock names as `systemDb('operator-console')`. So the export would compile and no caller on this path could produce its first argument.
- **THE ROUTE THAT REMAINS IS `SystemTx.rowAt` RETURNING `unknown`**, because `planVersions` and `planVersionSizes` are `FirmTableKey`s inside `TableKey`. That is the second `PlanVersionSizeRow` mapping in this deployable, which [ADR-411](../decisions/ADR-411.md) section 10 refused and this dispatch forbids independently.
- **SO THE HOME IS A `packages/db` DOOR**, the half [ADR-303](../decisions/ADR-303.md) did not take of the remedy [ADR-286](../decisions/ADR-286.md) section 5 named. `packages/db` is outside this fence.

The refusal is held by a case rather than by a sentence.
- **THE SECOND `PlanVersionSizeRow` MAPPING**, on [ADR-303](../decisions/ADR-303.md) limit 2 and on the dispatch. The census that holds it is derived and was watched red.
