### 2. The build, and why the page renders what it renders

`app/layout.tsx` calls `toShellView` and renders its two chrome fields around `{children}`. **That is the mechanism, not decoration**: the App Router renders this file around every page in this app, so `ADR-068` requirement 4's impersonation band and `INV-M4-09`'s footer have nowhere to be dropped. `content` is `ready` at that level because a layout renders when it has children to wrap, and the per-screen state belongs to the screen.

`app/page.tsx` renders the one state that is true today. **There is no API client in this app and no `/api/v1` to call**, so the content state is `toPortalErrorKind(401)` computed rather than typed, and the error vocabulary is rendered beside it as five live calls over the statuses [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) names, with `403` landing on `unexpected` because `INV-M4-07` keeps the word "forbidden" off the screen.

**Nothing on the page is invented trader data**, and that is the design constraint rather than a shortcut. [`M04:25`](../plans/M04-trader-portal.md) makes this app one *"consuming `/api/v1` and nothing else"* and the module's governing sentence is to render exactly what the engine computed. A first page showing a balance would have been a number this app did not receive.

Rendered, verified in `.next/server/app/index.html`: the disclosure sentence in the `<footer>`, `data-state="unauthenticated"` on the content section, and the five vocabulary rows.
