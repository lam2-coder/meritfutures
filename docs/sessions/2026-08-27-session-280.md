### 1. Why the parameter is required rather than optional

**The schema had already made the decision and the endpoint could not express it.** [`0008_risk.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0008_risk.sql) declares `audience`, `redaction_profile` and `includes_detector_detail` all `NOT NULL` on `evidence_packs`, and constrains exactly one combination out of the four audiences: a `trader` pack may never carry detector detail. [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) required only `?reason=`, and [`admin-reads.ts`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-reads.ts)' `EvidenceExportRequest` was `{ accountId, reason, actor }`.

**Three `NOT NULL` columns with no input means the generator invents one**, and [M06 section 4](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) already forbids exactly that: the profile *"follows from the audience and is recorded on the pack, not chosen per export"*.

**The argument for a default was taken seriously and it loses on the schema's own terms.** `trader` is the narrowest profile and looks like the safe fallback. But the merged DDL names it as the single combination worth making unrepresentable rather than as a floor, and the column's own header says the audience must be *"a DECLARED, AUDITED PROPERTY of the export rather than a judgment made in the moment by whoever is answering the ticket at the time"*. **A default is that judgment made by a process instead of by a person**, which is what `AS-M6-01` is about. The compatibility a default would buy is with nobody: the port is wired by nothing and the generator is `P7-j`, unwritten.

### 2. The measurement, and why a grep would have got it wrong in a new way

**Built rather than grepped**, `discoverRouteModules()` then `buildServer({ surface, modules })` on each surface:

```
public   registered 36  withheld 18
operator registered 19  withheld 35
```

`GET /admin/eligible-forecast` is registered on `operator` and withheld from `public`. **`GET /admin/loss-ratios` and `GET /admin/cusum` match ZERO lines across both reports**, in `registered` and in `withheld` alike.

**That distinction is the whole finding and a grep over route files cannot see it.** [`registry.ts`](../../apps/api/src/registry.ts) says of `withheld`: *"THE PUBLIC DEPLOYMENT'S 404 IS THIS LIST BEING NON-EMPTY AND NOTHING ELSE."* A withheld path 404s on the public origin **and answers on the operator origin**. These two are declared by no module at all, so they 404 on both. **A contract row that looks served, a path withheld by design, and a path nobody built produce three different behaviours and one appearance.**

### 3. Why these two rows are NOT deleted, where [ADR-153](../decisions/ADR-153.md)'s were

[ADR-153](../decisions/ADR-153.md) repaired a section 12 row naming `GET /auth/otp` and its clause is *"the contract names no endpoint it does not define"*. Its section 4 says running that comparison is how the next one is found, and this session ran it. **It found something of a different kind, and treating it as the same kind would have deleted a specification.**

| | `GET /auth/otp` | `/admin/loss-ratios`, `/admin/cusum` |
|---|---|---|
| Defined by a heading | **no** | **yes**, a shared one |
| Rate-limited or otherwise specified | no | prose: cursor-free, cached for 60 seconds |
| Allocated to a slice | no | **yes**, `P7-k`'s fence names both by name |
| Registered | no | no |
| Correct repair | **delete the row** | **give each its own row** |

**The registered column is the only one they share, and it is the one a check reports on.** That is why the repair is three headings rather than a deletion, and why the entry states the difference rather than assuming the precedent reaches.

### 4. What was deliberately not decided

**The two unbuilt bodies.** `P7-k` writes `sample_size` beside `min_sample` and an `insufficient_data` state, and no field on `LiabilityResponse.per_plan` carries either. The CUSUM additionally has no storage: [P7 section 5.3](../plans/P7-risk-and-abuse.md) found `0049`'s `per_plan` disposition checked four of five fields, leaving the running statistic no column it fits, and where it lives is `ADR-167`'s. **A projection cannot be specified over an evaluator that does not exist or a table nobody has chosen.**

**`redaction_profile`.** No `CHECK` in the DDL, so a profile name is free text and two exports can spell one profile differently. `INV-M7-10` makes the strip list computed from `detector_definitions.is_sensitive`, and [P7 section 8](../plans/P7-risk-and-abuse.md) gives `P7-j` an entry *"only if `redaction_profile`'s vocabulary needs a ruling"*. **Publishing it on the wire here would fix a vocabulary that is deliberately open**, so the response carries the audience and stops.

**`OQ-F4-03`, and the reason it is stated in both halves rather than only the open one.** Whether a regulator pack carries **detector internals** is closed: [M06 section 7](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md), the [wave 3 batch 1 gate closure](../decisions/gates/wave-3-batch-1-gate-closure-2026-08-14.md) and [EC-071](../edge-cases/EC-071.md) each give all four audiences a profile and collapse `internal`, `counsel` and `regulator` into one. Whether an **impersonation record** appears in a regulator pack is open and is counsel's, in [ADR-068](../decisions/ADR-068.md) section 3's own words: *"A pack that omits impersonation for a trader audience and a pack that omits it for a regulator are different decisions, and this entry rules only the first. The second is a legal question and belongs with the counsel sitting."*

**The temptation is real precisely because the closed half is nearby**, and a slice arriving at `SD-M6-04` and finding a required `audience` parameter could read the open half as settled by its presence. Clause 5 exists to be quoted at that reading.
