## 2026-08-22 - Session 136: what closes a `VG` row, and two of the twelve are unwired for no reason at all

- **Objective:** propose `ADR-080`, extending [ADR-073](../decisions/ADR-073.md)'s disposition (b) to [STRATEGY](../testing/STRATEGY.md) section 4.2's twelve `VG` rows, and amend section 4.2 to carry the dispositions. One ADR. No gate, exactly as ADR-073 wrote none.
- **Done:** **[ADR-080](../decisions/ADR-080.md)**, `status: proposed`, unsigned by design. **A chained condition names its NEXT LINK and never its whole chain**, and it is available only while the row it names is **not** implemented. Four dispositions: wired, waiting, discharged, chained. [STRATEGY](../testing/STRATEGY.md) section 4.2 gains a `Disposition` column over all twelve rows plus the three paragraphs that state what section 4.1's own closure rule does not imply. **Three wired, four chained, one waiting, three discharged, two findings, and three rows carry two legs. 28 of 28 gates; 101 files / 1,410 passed / 45 skipped, unmoved, because this session writes no code.**
- **Next:** the gate is a later session's and [ADR-080](../decisions/ADR-080.md) section 8 states its six assertions rather than leaving the parse to be inferred. **It cannot be turned on until `VG-5` and `VG-8`'s hook are wired or re-ruled**, and that is stated in the entry rather than discovered by its implementer. Two founder decisions are handed over and neither is taken here: which reading of P1's definition of done binds, and whether `VG-7`'s register is good enough.
- **Blockers:** none for this session. The signature is the founder's.
- **Landmines:**

  - **TWO OF THE NINE "ASSIGNED TO STAGES THAT DO NOT EXIST" ARE NOTHING OF THE KIND, AND THIS IS THE SESSION'S MOST USEFUL OUTPUT.** [STATE](../STATE.md) has recorded since P1 that *"the other nine are not late, they are assigned to stages that do not exist"*. **`VG-5`'s stage is `CI-06`, which runs on every push**, and its subject is [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) section 12's matrix, present and headed *"required in CI"*. Nothing is waiting on anything: it is simply not written. **`VG-8`'s `PreToolUse` half is named as its implementation in three documents** ([STRATEGY](../testing/STRATEGY.md) section 4.2, [INFRA](../architecture/INFRA.md) twice) **and `.claude/settings.json` carries `SessionStart` and `Stop` and nothing else.** There is no hooks directory. Both are findings and neither had been counted as one.

  - **THE CHAIN RULE IS WHAT EXPOSED `VG-5`, AND THE OBVIOUS FORM OF IT WOULD HAVE BURIED IT FOREVER.** A chained leg that named its stage regardless of that stage's state would render `VG-5` as *"waiting on `CI-06`"*, closed, against a stage that has run since P1. That is [ADR-073](../decisions/ADR-073.md)'s own rejected *"marker no gate reads"* reintroduced one table lower. **The clause that prevents it is four words long**: the named row must not be implemented.

  - **SECTION 4.2'S LEGS ARE CONJUNCTIVE AND SECTION 4.1'S ARE NOT, AND THE GATE WILL INHERIT THIS WRONGLY.** `CI-06/gate-inventory` states, correctly for its own table, that a row *"is OPEN for each activation condition it carries"*. `CI-09`'s four legs are four independent deliverables and one shipped alone. **`VG-10`'s two legs are prerequisites for a single test**: it is chained on `CI-07` and waiting on a bucket, and it reopens only when both hold. A gate that reused 4.1's semantics would report `VG-10` open the day a `build` script landed, with no bucket to test against. **Exactly one row exercises this today**, which is why it is stated rather than left to be inferred from an example.

  - **A `VG` ROW'S (a) HAD TO BE STRENGTHENED OR `VG-5` WOULD READ AS WIRED.** Section 4.1's `Implemented` asserts that a job exists and [ADR-073](../decisions/ADR-073.md) is explicit that it *"asserts that a job exists, never that its contents cell is true"*. Inherited unchanged, `VG-5` satisfies it: its stage is named and `integrity` resolves. **A `VG` row's wired leg names the step or the rule id**, which resolves for `VG-1`, `VG-4` and `VG-12` and resolves to nothing for `VG-5`.

  - **THE REFERRAL'S APPROVAL CLAUSE DOES NOT SURVIVE THE MEASUREMENT AND IS CORRECTED IN THE ENTRY.** It offered *"every one of the twelve `VG` rows resolves to exactly one disposition"*. **Three rows carry two legs**: `VG-8` is discharged and a finding, `VG-10` is chained and waiting, `VG-12` is wired and discharged. A gate written to "exactly one disposition" fails on arrival against three rows, which is the failure [ADR-073](../decisions/ADR-073.md) section 1 widened its own scope to avoid. Its second half is the ruling and is kept.

  - **`VG-8`'S STRONG HALF IS REAL IN THE DATABASE AND ASSERTED BY NOTHING IN CI.** Four migrations revoke `UPDATE` and `DELETE` on the exact append-only set, from the application role and from `PUBLIC`. **Eleven `scripts/db/probe_*.sql` files exist and not one reads `relacl` or `has_table_privilege`**, so a migration that quietly restored `UPDATE` would be caught by no gate. A `probe_append_only_grants.sql` would be pinned automatically by `CI-06s` and `CI-06h`. It is named in the entry and **deliberately not written**: this session writes no gate.

  - **P1'S DEFINITION OF DONE CONTAINS A FEATURE MERIT HAS RULED OUT.** [DELIVERY_PLAN](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md) section 4 asks for *"every VG gate wired"*. Twelve wired rows transitively requires a bundler nobody has chosen, a provisioned Neon branch, an object store, and **a file-upload path**, while [VIBE_FAILURE_POSTMORTEMS](../../research/VIBE_FAILURE_POSTMORTEMS.md)'s own definition of `VG-11` says *"(v1 has minimal uploads; the rule stands so it is never improvised.)"* and [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) rules that *"Merit never proxies documents"*. **The two readings and their distances are tabled in the entry; which one binds is not a session's to decide.**

  - **[STATE](../STATE.md)'S `VG-11` ASSIGNMENT IS STILL STALE, NOW NAMED BY TWO ENTRIES.** The `VG-1 to VG-12` row reads *"VG-2, VG-10 and VG-11 to CI-07"*. [ADR-073](../decisions/ADR-073.md) moved `VG-11` to `CI-02`, predicted this row would carry the losing assignment, and recorded it unrepaired. **This session's fence is append-only on that file**, so it is named again rather than fixed. The count of three is unaffected either way; it is a one-line repair for whoever next holds that fence.

  - **`VG-7` IS THE WEAKEST DISPOSITION IN THE TABLE AND IS ADMITTED AS SUCH.** [ADR-073](../decisions/ADR-073.md) requires that a discharged row's register *"carry the work"*, and `CRON_INVENTORY` sets the bar by rowing `VG-9`'s drill with what it records. [INFRA](../architecture/INFRA.md)'s `VG-7` line **states the control** and rows no recurring check. It is admitted as discharged with the repair named, rather than failed on a standard no other row in this table is held to today.
