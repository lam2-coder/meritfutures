### 2. What makes `status: "transcribed"` mean something, and it is not the word

[`calendars/`](../../packages/db/src/seed/calendars) next door carries the same word and names the danger for its own authority: **a holiday written from recollection** looks identical to a correct transcription, passes every structural check, and is falsified retrospectively, after trading.

**The analogue here is a threshold written from judgment.** A row reading `correlation_floor_bp: -8000` looks exactly like a row reading `-7500`, on a page of eighteen of them, and the first thing that could falsify the wrong one is a ring that was not flagged. Judgment and transcription are indistinguishable once they are both JSON.

**The difference, and it is the whole reason this slice can do better than the calendars one: the authority is a file in this repository.** So the check is mechanical rather than procedural. Every value carries a `cite` of the form `<path>:<line>` and a `quote`, and `generate.mjs` **opens that file at that line and requires the quote to occur in it verbatim**.

| Failure | What happens |
|---|---|
| a paraphrase | `quote-not-at-the-cite` |
| a line number that has drifted, quote verbatim | `quote-not-at-the-cite` |
| a value somebody supplied | there is no line to point it at |
| a cite to a file the seed could have written itself | `cite-outside-the-authority` |
| the authority has moved under every cite | `authority-digest-disagrees` |

Each of the five is seeded and watched failing on its own finding. **197 citations resolve on the committed transcription**, and the test runs the resolution over the **committed rows file** rather than over a fresh build, so a hand-edited artifact is caught here and not only by the regeneration check.

**This is the assertion [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) asks for in place of a bigger model**, against the error class its own caution names. The reconciliation session's three worst errors were each a claim never checked against its primary source, and escalating a model does not fix that class.

### 3. Three states, and why the citation travels into the row

| State | What it says | `value` |
|---|---|---|
| `stated` | `M07` gives this value, at the cite, in the quote | the value |
| `unstated` | `M07` **names this knob and gives it no number** | `null` |
| `not_applicable` | the knob **cannot exist** here, and this is why | `null`, plus a `reason` |
| `contextual` | `M07` states it per **condition** rather than per detector | `null`, plus `cases` |

**`unstated` and `not_applicable` are not interchangeable and conflating them is the defect the vocabulary exists to prevent.** `unstated` sends a later session to find the number `M07` owes; `not_applicable` tells them there was never one to find. Two rows turn on it:

- **`D-10`** has **no numeric threshold at all**, because its statistic is a condition: an edge exists between the affiliate identity and the buyer, or it does not.
- **`D-12`** writes **no `risk_flags` row at all**, because *"Output is a watched-cluster set, not a flag"*. `flag_status: "open"` there would be **false** rather than merely cautious, and a runner reading `open` off every row would look for a flag `D-12` never raises and report it missing.

**And the citation travels into the row rather than staying in the source file.** [`0008_risk.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0008_risk.sql)'s own header says a threshold tuned by deploy is one whose *"why did this not fire in March"* answer is an archaeology exercise. **A threshold whose provenance lives in a JSON file in git and whose value lives in the database is the same archaeology one layer in.** So `parameters.<name>` is `{state, value, unit, cite, quote}` and `INV-M7-04` is answerable from the row alone. The wrapper also makes the three-valued case unmissable: `null` under a bare number reads as zero to a careless consumer, and `{state: "unstated", value: null}` cannot.

### 4. Eleven of eighteen carry no number, and the posture is recorded unexercised

`M07` section 10 says it in its own words: *"Every threshold in section 3.2 is currently a number from the dossier or from judgment."* Most of them are written down nowhere.

| Rows with a stated number | The number `M07` gives |
|---|---|
| `D-01` | a **2 second** window |
| `D-02` | **20** trading days, floor **-8000 bp** |
| `D-09` | **more than one** unrelated identity per destination |
| `D-16` | **10000 bp** exclusive maximum on an inferred edge |
| `D-12` | **zero** trading data, at funding |
| `D-13` | **5** trading days, floor **-9500 bp** |
| `D-18` | **four** legs, all four required |

The other eleven are `unstated` throughout, each carrying the `M07` phrase that names the knob and no value.

**The posture is recorded and it is UNEXERCISED, which is the honest thing to say about it.** `OQ-M7-02` recommends tuning for recall over precision during beta. That is advice about **which way to be wrong when choosing a number**, and this seed chooses no number: every threshold is either quoted from `M07` or left null with the phrase that names it. So the posture governs nothing here and will govern the first session that fills a null in. **Writing a number and citing the posture for it would be exactly the move *"a number you cannot cite is the finding"* forecloses.**

**Money is integer cents and no float appears anywhere.** The generator walks every number in the generated artifact and rejects a non-integer on `float-in-seed`. `M07` writes two correlations as decimals; both are carried as basis points, and a Pearson correlation is a pure ratio in `[-1, 1]`, so basis points express it **exactly**, with no rounding at all.

### 5. `is_sensitive` is uniformly true, and the consequence belongs to `P7-j`

`INV-M7-10` is unqualified: *"Detector parameters never appear in a trader-audience evidence pack."* **Nothing in `M07` designates any detector parameter as trader-visible**, so every row is sensitive and no row is a counter-example. Each states its own reason, in the row rather than in a README, and no two reasons are the same, because a reason copied from a neighbour is a value that was not decided.

**Two rows are where the argument is closest, and both were argued rather than defaulted.**

- **`D-07` is where marking sensitive OVER-STRIPS.** Its bound is the **plan maximum**, which a trader can read off the plan catalogue, so stripping it removes a number the trader already has. It stays `true`: the invariant is unqualified, the over-strip costs a trader nothing, and an exception carved here to save one lookup is the precedent for carving one where it costs something.
- **`D-02` is where the number is ALREADY LEAKED.** `AS-M7-02` calls `-0.8` industry folklore and assumes the ring knows it. It stays `true` because **folklore is not disclosure**: confirming the number turns a guess into a specification, and the whole defence of the detector is that the ring must guess.

**THE FINDING, AND IT IS [`P7-j`](../plans/P7-risk-and-abuse.md)'s TO ACT ON.** While this column is uniform, a `trader` pack that **computes** its strip list from `is_sensitive` and one that strips every detector **unconditionally** produce **byte-identical output**. So `GS-112` passes either way and `INV-M7-10`'s mechanism is untested: the assertion cannot distinguish the control from its absence, which is the shape `falsify.mjs` exists to refuse.

`P7-j` needs a fixture row with `is_sensitive: false` to tell the two apart, and **it cannot come from this seed**, because there is no `M07` line to cite for one. The test carries an assertion that goes **red** on the day the column acquires its first `false`, so whoever writes it must revisit `P7-j`'s test in the same change rather than discovering the gap later.

### 6. Two sentences read, named, and deliberately not encoded

**`P7`'s own rule is that this is the phase whose temptation is a Behavior column in an accepted entry**, and `M07` carries that column twice.

| Where | The sentence | Why it is not a row |
|---|---|---|
| `D-16`'s statistic cell | *"Hard links auto-enforce"* | **`OQ-M7-05` is open.** Section 3.1's third tier says *"no state changes automatically"*, [M19](../plans/M19-kyc-identity.md) `INV-M19-04` agrees, and `M07` section 10 says in terms that this plan does not extend one ruling's reach by assumption |
| section 7.9's link-class table | *"Auto-enforce."*, on the Hard row naming `D-08` and `D-09` | The **class** is a fact about the strength of the signal and is recorded. The **behaviour** is the half [ADR-155](../decisions/ADR-155.md) and `INV-M7-02` foreclose |

**A seed that merely omitted them would be indistinguishable from a seed whose author never read them, and the next session would encode them.** So the source carries a `refusals` block naming both with the authority for each refusal beside it, and the generator **requires the block to exist**.

The refusal is mechanical rather than trusted to a comment: any parameter whose **value** spells an enforcement outcome is rejected on `parameter-implies-enforcement`, every row's `flag_status` is `open` or `not_applicable` and nothing else, and `D-16`'s `auto_enforce` is `not_applicable` with its reason. **Writing either value there would settle an open question by being written**, which is [ADR-042](../decisions/ADR-042.md)'s fixture-is-a-control reading one artifact over. `ADR-155` forecloses the `true` half regardless; `false` is the reading the corpus is likelier to adopt, and it is still not this seed's to adopt.

**`D-18`'s test is seeded as DATA for the same reason.** `footprint_present` is three-valued because the lookup fails open: `null` is *"we did not find out"* and `false` is *"the vendor looked and there is none"*. A detector written against `IS NOT TRUE` scores every vendor timeout as a fleet member, which converts a supplier outage into a flood of flags against real customers. `footprint_present_test: "IS FALSE"` is in the registry so [`P7-h`](../plans/P7-risk-and-abuse.md) has something to be **checked against** rather than a comment to be trusted, and `IS NOT TRUE` is refused there on its own finding.

### 7. The test, and the three seeds that were wrong

**A seed test that asserts the seed loads proves almost nothing.** What this one asserts is that the seed **is** the registry.

**The expected set is PARSED out of `M07` rather than written.** A hand-kept list of the detectors `M07` names is one more hand-maintained count, and it goes stale on the day section 3.2 gains a row: **silently, and in the direction that matters**, because the stale list would agree with the stale seed. So section 3.2 is parsed and the two failures are separate findings, both seeded: `detector-named-by-m07-and-not-seeded` and `detector-seeded-and-not-named-by-m07`.

**The row ORDER is checked too, and not merely the set.** `M07`'s table runs `D-01` to `D-10`, then `D-15` to `D-17`, then `D-11` to `D-14` and `D-18`. That is a reading order a set comparison would silently let a seed reshuffle, and the reshuffle is seeded.

**The generator can produce 62 distinct rejections and 59 of them are watched failing on their own finding**: 46 table-driven seeds, 10 written out on their own, and 3 reached through an exported function because no source file can get there. A test asserts that **every `reject()` in the generator** has a seed, a direct reach, or a stated proof of unreachability. The remaining three are **backstops with their proof stated**: the generator builds rows itself from `ROW_COLUMNS` and `describe()`, so no source file can make a row miss a column, carry a column `detector_definitions` does not declare, or hold an empty description. That is [`trading-calendar-generator.test.ts`](../../packages/db/test/trading-calendar-generator.test.ts)'s `absorbed-session-not-claimed` treatment, and if any becomes reachable it owes a seed of its own.

**THREE SEEDED VIOLATIONS FAILED ON A MORE SPECIFIC FINDING THAN THE ONE THEY NAMED, AND THE SEEDS WERE WRONG RATHER THAN THE GENERATOR.** Deleting only a `cite` and leaving its `quote` is caught earlier by `quote-without-cite`, which is the right answer to a **different** question: a quote with nothing to resolve it against. `detector-field-uncited` and `contextual-case-uncited` are about a field that carries **no citation at all**, so both seeds now remove the pair. The third was `row-defaults-not-transcribed`, which had **no seed at all** and was found by the exhaustiveness test rather than by a reader. That is the suite doing its stated job on its first run.

### 8. Three things reported and not decided

1. **`M07` gives no version vocabulary for `detector_definitions.version`.** `v1` is taken from section 7.9's shipping-tier table, which is the only tier vocabulary `M07` owns, and it is a **naming choice** rather than a transcribed value. The column is in the primary key so it cannot be null and the choice cannot be deferred; what it can be is **labelled**, and the generator refuses a `chosen` value that carries a `cite` at all.
2. **`M07` gives no effective date and no launch date.** `2026-08-27` is the date the rows were transcribed, which is the only date this session can state as a fact. It is **not** a claim that they were loaded on it: a later load carries a retroactive effective date, and the honest repair is a **superseding row** rather than an edit, because a table whose effective dates are edited in place cannot answer the question it exists for.
3. **`OQ-M7-02`'s routing half is contested inside `M07` and neither reading is written.** Read literally, *"everything above severity 3 going to the digest"* sends severity **4 and 5** to the digest. That is the band `G-HOLD-REQUIRED` reads to **hold a payout**, the band `SD-M7-02` gives `sla_due_at` to, and the band whose time-to-first-touch is a tracked metric; and it is **severity 1** that `M07` twice sends to the digest. A flag that holds a trader's money for 48 hours while sitting in a digest nobody works is `FM-M7-04`'s **documented negligence at the top of the scale**. Routing is not a column of `detector_definitions` and section 3.2 gives no routing value, so declining to write it costs nothing and is what the fence requires. **It is the founder's, and it bears on money**: under [ADR-040](../decisions/ADR-040.md) the reading decides whether a high-severity flag reaches a human before the payout auto-releases and pays.

**A fourth is procedural.** **No dispatch row exists in [sessions/README](README.md) for this session or for any of the other ten in this wave** — the table stops at 272. So [P7](../plans/P7-risk-and-abuse.md) rule 1's *"strike your row"* had no row to strike. The entry was added through the generated span and **no dispatch row was invented**, because a row written by the session it dispatches is not a dispatch.
