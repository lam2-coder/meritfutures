### 3. Refused

- **The runner was NOT wired.** Blockers (1) and (2) stand without qualification, and section 11 of the ADR states the argument this repository already carries: an install nothing can exercise is an install nothing falsifies.
- **`detector_run_id` was NOT added to the payload**, although `runner.ts` is inside this row's fence. `apps/worker/test/event-sink.test.ts` pins its absence and is outside the fence, and more importantly clearing that one leg clears nothing while no sink is reachable at all.
- **No no-op sink was passed anywhere**, which `adapter.ts` argues would be the worst available value in that file.
- **Nothing was scheduled**, and `apps/worker/src/schedule.ts`, `apps/worker/src/index.ts` and `packages/db/src/seed/**` were read, cited by name, and not written.
- **The declines were NOT partitioned past the two classes that are legible in the runner's own message.** Separating *wants a number* from *wants a severity* cleanly needs the decline messages to agree on a form they do not agree on, and a partition that over-fits their wording breaks on a rewording rather than on a change of fact.
