### Three findings, none of them fixed here, and none of them a capability failure

**1. PT-02's row names ONE exception and the engine has TWO.**
[`src/day/progression.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/progression.ts) zeroes
`winDaysCount` inside `passedState` at the R-31 funded reset. So a `phase.passed` day
decreases the counter as well, on every plan with an evaluation phase.
[STRATEGY](../testing/STRATEGY.md) section 3.1's PT-02 row says only "except at a payout
reset". **This is ADR-050's shape one property over**: RE-P-01 met exactly this on the floor,
and the ruling was to state the exception and make the assertion at that step STRICTER rather
than to skip it.

The property does three things and no more: the guard names **both** exceptions, the R-31
step is pinned to **exactly zero** in its own `describe`, and the gap is reported here rather
than patched. **A frozen document moves by ADR, never by commit**, and `docs/decisions/` was
outside this session's fence.

**Why the guard matters rather than being a footnote.** The default fold starts FUNDED, where
R-31 cannot fire, so a guard naming only the payout reset would be correct today **by accident
of the fixture**. The next session to fold from `prior: null` would get a red suite and a
counterexample describing nothing wrong, and the cheapest way out of that is to delete the
finding. The two-exception list is written into the guard for that reason.

**2. The mark's `win_day` COLUMN and R-09 are not the same predicate.**
`0014`'s `daily_marks_win_day_implies_traded` makes the stored column false on an untraded
day whatever the pnl was, and
[`day-sequence.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/test/generators/day-sequence.ts) writes it as
`tradedDay && clearsFloor`. R-09 as M01 section 3.5 states it, and as
[`counters.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/counters.ts) implements it, is
`realized_pnl_cents >= win_day_floor_cents` with R-04's halted clause and **no traded-day
clause**. So on a day with zero fills whose pnl clears the floor, the column says `false` and
the engine counts a win day.

**The engine follows M01 and is not wrong here.** What is unasserted anywhere is that the two
agree, and they do not. PT-02 derives from M01's expression, which is the primary source, and
**measures the disagreement** in its support block so the finding is a live number rather than
a sentence. Closing it is a rule question for a session holding the fence on `0014` or on
`counters.ts`.

**Found by writing the equality against the column and watching it go red on a real
sequence**, not by reasoning about it.

**3. Two ordering laws take a day out before DO-6, and a property that assumes otherwise is
wrong.** R-25 is "breach beats everything on the same day" and DO-5's row reads "nothing after
this runs"; R-49's graduation returns at DO-2 because "no trading day follows". On either day
the stored counters are the ones DO-2 left, and the day's own win day is not among them. The
first version of PT-02's day-level equality did not name them and went red on a settlement day
that also breached. **Both are now asserted at the stricter value (exactly zero) rather than
skipped**, and `reachedCounters` on the fold's `Step` is what tells them apart.

| PT-08 | line 197, `payoutsSettledCount >= plan.funded.maxPayouts` -> `>` (R-49's boundary) | **3 of 22 tests red, and every one of them on the OVER-LADDER stream.** The `ordinary` variants of the same three tests stayed green |

**The second mutant is the design argument, proved rather than asserted.**
`settlement-sequence.ts` states that while R-42 and R-49 hold, INV-17 holds arithmetically:
it is their conjunction over a life, not an independent constraint. So a generator that
respects the ladder by construction cannot test the ladder. PT-08 therefore folds a stream the
ORACLE rejects on purpose (`omit: {'INV-17/lifetime-bound'}`, `max_payouts + 1` settlements
every one at its cap) and asserts that the ENGINE's own total still stops at the bound. Under
the mutant, CORE-50K reached **900,000c against a bound of 750,000c** and MERIT-RAPID-50K
reached **600,000c against 500,000c**.

**One detail worth keeping.** Under the mutant, CORE-50K's *bound* assertion passed at 300
runs while its *ladder count* assertion failed. That is the file's own stated reason for
asserting both halves of the conjunction: a defect in one can be masked by slack in the other,
and an account that settled six times below its cap sits under the bound and has still broken
the ladder.

### Landmines

- **`materializedFrom`, `toEngineMark` and `sliceOf` now exist FOUR times on `main` after this
  wave.** The originals are private to `floor-monotonicity.property.test.ts` at lines 143, 252
  and 264; session 62's copy is `test/property-harness.ts`; session 63's is
  `test/settlement-fold.ts`. **The end state is one module imported by all three, and it is a
  follow-up session** because every one of those files was inside somebody's fence this week.
  Both new headers name all the copies so the collapse session can act without rediscovering
  them.
- **`applySettlement` has no phase guard.** Calling it directly on a `graduated` state applies
  again and pushes the lifetime past INV-17's bound. Nothing in this session found that
  reachable, and it is not: `advanceDay` returns at DO-2 the moment the phase moves, DO-2's
  own loop cannot revisit a day, and `settlement-sequence.ts` places every settlement on a
  distinct effective day. **The bound is enforced by the FOLD and not by the function**, which
  is why PT-08 asserts through `advanceDay` and not by iterating `applySettlement` alone.
- **No support counter is asserted against a band.** Every one is `> 0`. The figures a
  sampling run produces are one run of a randomized generator, and a band copied out of one is
  a hand-maintained count in files whose purpose is to end hand-maintained counts.
