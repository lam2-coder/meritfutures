### The question P4 asked has an answer P4's three readings cannot express

[P4](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md) calls this *"the single decision that most changes how many of the remaining phases can run concurrently"* and offers three readings. **Counted, they do not differ on that axis at all.**

| Reading | Concurrent transcribing sessions | Sessions to reach 111 |
|---|---|---|
| Once for the estate | **1**, then unbounded | 1, and [ADR-003](../../CLAUDE.md) and [E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) both forbid that one |
| Once per phase | **1** | one or more per phase, and 91 tables belong to phases with no plan |
| Once per module, taken literally | **1** | 22, at depth one |
| **As ruled** | **N, bounded by disjointness and not by the file** | 22, concurrent wherever the sets are disjoint |

**All three treat the pair as a resource to be held, so all three serialise.** The ruling is reading 3 plus the two definitions P4 left open, and both are load bearing: a per-module owner with a real queue is one session at a time, and a compiler-checked queue with per-module ownership deadlocks on the 54 shared tables.

### The number no measurement session could compute, and it refutes the owner half

Fourteen sessions measured fourteen modules in fourteen containers, each blind to the others. **The question "does my module's table set overlap anyone else's" is the one question none of them could answer**, and it is the one the ownership ruling turns on. Run here:

| Tables named by | Count |
|---|---|
| **more than one module plan** | **54 of 111** |
| exactly one | 53 |
| **no module plan at all** | **4** (`payment_disputes`, `affiliate_statements`, `ledger_halts`, `identity_signal_weights`) |

**`accounts` is named by 21 of the 22 plan documents.** A rule that assigns a table to "its module" has no answer for it, and no answer for the four that belong to nobody.

**AND THE FAILURE IS NOT HYPOTHETICAL.** [Session 159](2026-08-24-session-159.md) claims `wallet_withdrawals`, `wallet_entries`, `wallet_spend_limits` and `wallet_dormancy` under `M5-1`. [Session 169](2026-08-24-session-169.md) claims **exactly those four** under `M20-a` and writes that `schema.ts` and `scope.ts` are *"M20-a's alone"*. Both are right about their own plan, which is why per-module ownership cannot decide it and per-table registration can.
