### 2. The source that decides it, which nobody had quoted

`ADR-248` reached AS-01's **residual** and stopped. The residual is an inference: *"None at account
level. At identity level, ten accounts can each hold one in-flight payout"* is only true if the rule
is per account.

**`AS-09` does not infer. It states the grain as a premise of its own attack**, at
[`M01:965`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md):

> "Each payout is individually correct and individually capped at 150,000c. The aggregate is
> 1,500,000c in one day from one human, and **AS-01's in-flight rule does not help because each
> account has its own.**"

**And the corpus at large had already ruled it.** [`STATE_MACHINES:405`](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md),
the authoritative guard drawing, **corrected under [ADR-040](../decisions/ADR-040.md) on 2026-08-15**,
reads *"No `payout_requests` row for this account in status `approved`, `frozen`, or
`held_pending_review`"* and adds *"It is written here exactly as `payout_requests_no_in_flight_uq`'s
predicate, because the database enforces it too and a guard that disagrees with its own index is a
gate that holds on Tuesdays."*

**So the question was open only inside `M01`.** Nine sources take the account and two take the
identity, one of the two transcribing the other.

### 3. Why the identity reading lost, with its sources quoted

Full quotations are in [ADR-254](../decisions/ADR-254.md) section 3. The five grounds:

| # | Ground |
|---|---|
| 1 | **[ADR-019](../decisions/ADR-019.md)'s premise was removed by [ADR-040](../decisions/ADR-040.md).** ADR-019 migrated the guard *"to the external leg only"* because *"there is no window in which an internal payout is in flight"*. ADR-040 put `held_pending_review` BEFORE approval, ruled **"A held request is outstanding"**, and **re-created** the account-grained unique index |
| 2 | **ADR-040 named M01's `SD-09` and its `hasPayoutInFlight` comment as its own unswept sites**, treating the account-grained pair as the live one needing a vocabulary repair |
| 3 | **The losing sentence is satisfiable on NEITHER table.** `payout_status` has no `transferring`; `wallet_withdrawal_status` has no `frozen`. Its status set is `payout_requests`' pre-ADR-028 vocabulary, so only its grain word came from ADR-019 |
| 4 | **The trap, inverted.** `OQ-7` rules *"no identity-level extraction ceiling in v1"*. An identity-grained boolean **is** that ceiling, of one, and it would refuse nine of a copy trader's ten accounts. **Conservative here is a denial nobody authorized** |
| 5 | **AS-09: *"The engine stays per-account, because cross-account state inside a pure fold would destroy replay."*** An identity gate is not merely wrong about the population; it is unavailable to the evaluator |

**AND ADR-019 IS NOT OVERTURNED.** Its per-identity rule is the **external leg's**, its object is
`wallet_withdrawals`, and it is **already built**: `gateNoInFlight` refuses with *"A withdrawal is
already open for this identity. One withdrawal is in flight at a time."* [ADR-158](../decisions/ADR-158.md)
finding 8 already described `G-NO-IN-FLIGHT` as two enforcements on two legs, and the handler that
serves the identity one never mentions `ExternalGates`.

### 8. Where this session is most likely wrong

**THE JUDGEMENT IS THAT ADR-019's RULE AND `hasPayoutInFlight` ARE TWO RULES RATHER THAN ONE RULE AT A
CONTESTED GRAIN.** Everything else is derived and executed. A reader who takes ADR-019's
*"`G-NO-IN-FLIGHT` migrates to the external leg only"* as a wholesale move of the RULE rather than of
one of its two enforcements would rule the other way, and would then owe an answer to AS-09, to
`STATE_MACHINES:405`, and to why ADR-040 re-created an account-grained unique index for a gate that
had left.

**AND THE STATUS-VOCABULARY FOLD IS A SCOPE CALL AND NOT A RULING.** `M01:207` and `M01:283` are
ADR-040's remainder. A founder who wants this entry to move only what it rules should strike those
two rows and accept that M01 then states two status sets for one predicate.
