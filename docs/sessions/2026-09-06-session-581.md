### Landmines

- **LANDMINE: nine sessions have justified line-count neutrality by keeping `RI-15` and `RI-16` green, and those checks catch 10.2 percent of what one inserted line moves.** The real reason is the one [session 176](2026-08-24-session-176.md) gave first and nobody repeated: a SIGNED or dated record cites the line, and repairing that pointer is forbidden rather than merely expensive.
- **LANDMINE: [session 485](2026-08-30-session-485.md)'s prediction is falsified by this row's own diff, on the very file it was made about.** It said inserting a paragraph into [`repo-invariants.mjs`](../../packages/tooling/checks/repo-invariants.mjs)'s header shifts every pointer as high as `:7060` and *"`RI-15` and `RI-16` will find it."* This diff inserts thirteen lines above `:4301` and **both checks are green**, because every document citing that file is a dated record. Its other half, *"Edit at the foot"*, is the correct advice and the corpus kept the wrong one.
- **LANDMINE: whole-file `git diff --numstat` neutrality is not the property anybody wants.** A diff that adds three lines high and deletes three low reports equal insertions and deletions and re-points every pointer between them. No check sees it and no entry before this one has named it.
- **LANDMINE: a `covers` cardinal that counts a walk over this tree cannot be maintained by hand.** Fourteen were derived and fourteen were stale, and one of them had been hand-corrected by [ADR-380](../decisions/ADR-380.md) one row before [ADR-383](../decisions/ADR-383.md) found it stale again.
- **LANDMINE: this row broke 24 dated-record pointers to publish a ruling about broken pointers**, and the free alternative was available. ADR section 13 says it is the one decision a founder could reverse by reading section 10 alone.
- **LANDMINE: [ADR-370](../decisions/ADR-370.md) is in no fence anybody has dispatched, confirmed a SIXTH consecutive time.** Line 84 and its section 3.1 cells are still uncorrected.
