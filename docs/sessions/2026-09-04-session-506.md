### 1. The objective

Row `315` dispatched [ADR-305](../decisions/ADR-305.md) section 7 slice 3, its `F3`: **a ruling first and then a small build**, on the disagreement between `sweeps/ports.ts`'s adapter sentence and the port declared immediately below it.

The stop condition was **one of two things, in writing**. Either `ExpiryTx` gains a `ledger: LedgerTx` member on the same transaction, with the `EXPIRY_TABLES` exclusion restated and shown to still hold, or the docblock is corrected to the shape the wiring will actually take, with the reason the first shape is unreachable. **A third outcome, in which the file is left saying two things, was named as the failure the slice exists against.**

---

### 3. The ruling, in one paragraph

**The sentence is wrong and the port is right.** `ExpiryTx` keeps three members, `EXPIRY_TABLES` is unchanged, and the `LedgerTx` stays in the wiring, recovered by the identity of the `ExpiryTx` that `postLt01` is handed. The member was not refused on taste: this file cannot name `LedgerTx` at all, a restatement puts both excluded ledger keys back inside the port, and the member would end the header's `SystemTx` assignability. [ADR-315](../decisions/ADR-315.md) section 3 states all three with the line each rests on, and section 6 puts the member's case as the alternative rather than as a strawman.
