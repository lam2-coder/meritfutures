### 6. Landmines

- **`firm` IS STILL AVAILABLE ON THIS TABLE AND STILL PASSES `tsc`.** ADR-253's control refuses it, and that control checks a NECESSARY condition rather than the truth of a firm claim. Nothing in this entry narrows it further.

A rule, a door or a query reaching for the wrong one is correct today and wrong after the first reassignment or hard merge.
- **`GET /affiliate/stats` WILL STILL REFUSE AFTER `0078` AND AFTER THE REGISTRATION.** Three of nine fields are unblocked by them and `conversions_30d` is not. A session that lands the migration and expects the endpoint to be an adapter away will find one field short.

A `derived` registration makes `tx.rows('affiliateCommissions')` available to anybody holding a scoped handle, forever, and every row it returns carries `attribution_id`. ADR-253 section 6's condition is what bounds that and it is a condition about what OTHER doors do.
