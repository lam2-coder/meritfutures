### 4. The residue, answered in two halves

**FIRST: NO MECHANICAL CLASSIFIER CAN ASK THE RULE'S QUESTION, AND THE REASON IS ASYMMETRY.** Class
B is DEMONSTRABLE -- [ADR-227](../decisions/ADR-227.md) section 6 says what a witness is and a
session exhibits one. **Class C is its COMPLEMENT** and its claim quantifies over every assertion
anybody could write, which no search settles and no pattern approximates. **So the residue is
bounded above and never measured, and the cheapest procedure that bounds it is the class B procedure
itself.**

**SECOND, AND THIS IS WHAT [ADR-243](../decisions/ADR-243.md) DID NOT REACH: THE CLASSIFIER IS NOT
WRONG.** [ADR-227](../decisions/ADR-227.md) section 8 says so about its own number in capitals:
*"THIS IS THIRTY DECISIONS AND NOT THIRTY DIFFS."*

| | Counts | Blocks | Tier 1 today |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Section 3's rule | **diffs** whose central claim is a choice | a MERGE | **1** |
| Section 8's classifier | **entries** naming a choice a founder owns | nothing | **21** |

**A GOOD ENTRY NAMES A RESIDUAL CHOICE EVEN WHEN ITS CENTRAL CLAIM IS A PROPERTY**, because `RI-13`
requires the block. **The defect was asking one number to do two jobs.** Class B empties the merge
queue and does not touch the decision queue, and **a session reporting the first without the second
would have converted a real list of founder questions into a claim that they were answered.**

**AND WHAT A HUMAN MUST READ IS MEASURED**: 23 of the 53 money-path entries and **9 of the 21 in
tier 1** carry an explicit `WHAT IS MEASURED, WHAT IS DERIVED, AND WHAT IS JUDGED` bullet, which
answers the rule's question in a paragraph the entry already wrote. **For the other 30 the entry has
to be read.**

### 5. The finding, and it is about this session's own instrument

**NO ASSERTION WENT RED ON UNMODIFIED SHIPPED SOURCE.** What happened instead is worse for the
regime: **[ADR-230](../decisions/ADR-230.md) section 3.4 records two seeds turning one case red, and
one of them is GREEN on the tree today.**

**The tree is not defective and that entry's ruling stands.** `pairInsertStatement` throws on the
writer column in both spellings before the values object is spread, so the ORDER of the spread is
unreachable while the refusal is live. **What is narrower is that leg 1 of that suite's own three,
the stamp itself, is not independently held**: relax the refusal and nothing reports the ordering
gone. The suite has not drifted -- `git log --first-parent` returns one commit, ADR-230's own merge.

**A TRANSCRIPT THAT NO LONGER REPRODUCES IS INVISIBLE TO EVERY CHECK IN THIS REPOSITORY, `RI-23`
INCLUDED**, because catching it means running the seed. **The only instrument that catches it is a
later session re-running it**, which is how this was found.

### 7. Where this session is most likely wrong

**TWO OF THE ELEVEN TURN ON READING A CONJUNCTION'S PROPERTY HALF AS ITS POINT.**
[ADR-228](../decisions/ADR-228.md), where the value `500000` could be called the point rather than
the ceiling, and [ADR-233](../decisions/ADR-233.md), where the membership of the five-key list could
be called the point rather than the closed-list mechanism. **If a reader takes the other reading on
both, tier 1's residue is three rather than one**, and that direction is upward, which is the
direction this session had no incentive to find.

**AND SEVERAL OF THE FIFTEEN REST ON STRUCTURAL ASSERTIONS.** The seeds prove the assertion is LIVE
and not that it is DEEP. The sharp illustration is in this session's own table:
[ADR-234](../decisions/ADR-234.md)'s deleted enum member reddened five behavioural cases and
[ADR-230](../decisions/ADR-230.md)'s reordered spread reddened none.
