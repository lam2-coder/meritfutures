purchase blocks from what we simply have not built.

**Outcome.** **The separation is the deliverable and it moved the line.** Six of
the seven shapes were already built. The seventh is not behind the purchase and
the port is, which is the opposite of what the row, the port and two entries
above it all said.

### 3. What was ruled

**`setAdminReadSource` is behind the SSO purchase**, at one remove. An
`AdminSourceBackend` takes a `SystemTx`, `systemDb` is the only constructor that
yields one, `db.ts` declares no `operator(fn)`, `db.test.ts` pins that file's
import list and that nothing else may take a handle, and [ADR-171](../decisions/ADR-171.md)
section 9 makes the door takeable only by the slice that lands an installable
`AdminSessionSource`. **Three mechanical controls, not a convention.**

**The purchase blocks six ports and not four.** [ADR-171](../decisions/ADR-171.md)
counted four; `useCertificateRevokeBackend` landed after it, and this entry adds
the read source.

**[ADR-171](../decisions/ADR-171.md) is NOT reopened.** Its condition is unmet,
the door stays shut, and its section 5 argument survives intact. **Only the
reason for one row moves**, and with it which side of the purchase line that row
sits on.

**`readLiability` is unbuilt rather than blocked**, on one term: a
`writeRuleState` implementation. `rule_states` holds zero rows, five of the six
eligibility gate groups clear only when a trader trades, and EC-074 makes
`eligible_next_7d` whole or nothing.

- **A COMMENT CANNOT FAIL, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE DEFECT HERE.** A true sentence
  went false one producer at a time. Each landing session **reported** the
  narrowing rather than editing the claim, correctly, because the claim was in
  another fence. **Nothing compared them.** The wrong reason then propagated four
  levels: port header, `wiring.test.ts`, [ADR-171](../decisions/ADR-171.md)
  section 4, ALLOCATION row.
- **`admin-reads.ts` MAY NOT CONTAIN THE SUBSTRING `admin-source`**, asserted by
  the composition's own suite. The first draft of the header repair cited a test
  by path and tripped it.
- **`db.ts` NAMES `systemDb` IN PROSE**, in the paragraph explaining why it does
  not import it. **A substring check reds on the explanation.** The new control
  got this wrong on its first run and asserts the import instead. The same trap
  is documented one file over, which is evidence it is the default mistake.
- **A LAZY MATCH WITH `$` UNDER THE `m` FLAG ENDS AT THE FIRST NEWLINE.** The
  BLOCKED-list parser silently returned one-line reasons and matched nothing.
- **`admin-feed.ts:18` CITES `admin-reads.ts:1361` FOR A SENTENCE AT `:2004`**,
  already wrong by 643 lines before this branch. Outside this fence, reported.
  This branch moves the target further and says so.
- **The composition header states the triple as `{ 23, 6, 17 }`** against a
  runner reporting `{ 24, 7, 17 }`. Outside every fence, reported.
