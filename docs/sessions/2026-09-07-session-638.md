### 5. Landmines

- **A GREEN COMPARISON OF TWO TEXTS IS NOT A CLAIM ABOUT TWO VALUES.** `a - b` and `-(b - a)` compute the same number and this check calls them a disagreement; nothing here evaluates an expression against a database. It is stricter than semantics in one direction and no stronger in the other.
- **A SECOND GENERATED MONEY COLUMN EXISTS AND FOUR COMPARISONS CANNOT REACH IT.** `live_account_state` carries three `*_cents` columns plus a generated `*_cents` column and is transcribed nowhere. **Third row to name this table.**
- **drizzle-orm PUTS AN IDENTITY IN `generatedIdentity` AND AN EXPRESSION IN `generated`.** That is why this comparison needs no exemption for the twenty identity columns where the `DEFAULT` comparison needed a whole branch.
- **`bigint` AND `bigint({ mode: 'number' })` TRANSCRIBE THE SAME DDL WORD.** One of them hands JavaScript a float64 holding cents. `dataType` is the only assertion in 8,560 that sees it.
- **A LAZY REGEX WOULD HAVE READ `rcr_bp` WRONG.** `(reserve_cents * 10000) / NULLIF(cvar99_cents, 0)` defeats `\(([^)]*)\)` and a greedy one swallows `STORED`. The reader is a paren walk and cases 6 and 7 are both directions of it watched failing.
- **THIS DISCHARGES NONE OF [ADR-437](../decisions/ADR-437.md) SECTION 7's REMAINING THREE REASONS.** The transcription is now compared on every fact it carries and the write path is still refused, on rulings nobody has made.
