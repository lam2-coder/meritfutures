- **Landmines:**

  - **THE FINDING MADE THE RULING SMALLER, AND THAT IS THE PART A LATER READER WILL UNDO.** [SECURITY:45](../architecture/SECURITY.md) `C-27` already refuses a sensitive action from a session that cannot elevate, and requirement 2 says an impersonation session never inherits or intercepts the trader's OTP or passkey. **So it can never elevate, and external withdrawal, payout-destination change and contact change were already closed to it.** Four explicit refusals, three inherited. **The danger is the opposite of the usual one**: a later engineer finding seven refusals in impersonation middleware and `C-27` implemented separately sees a redundancy and deletes one. `C-27` protects a SIM-swapped **trader** session and has nothing to do with impersonation. **Two controls covering one route are not one control written twice**, and `ADR-068` section 1 says so in those words because the alternative reading deletes a control that is load bearing somewhere else entirely.

  - **THE COROLLARY RUNS THE OTHER WAY AND IS WHY THREE TESTS EXIST THAT LOOK REDUNDANT.** If impersonation ever gains a path to elevate, **three refusals disappear silently** and nothing would fail, because the inherited three were never asserted against impersonation-specific code. `M6-N-03`, `M6-N-04` and `M6-N-05` assert the **outcome** rather than the mechanism, so the outcome is pinned whichever control produces it.

  - **A SINGLE-SIDED BOUNDARY PASSES AN INVENTORY OF REFUSALS, AND THIS WAS DEMONSTRATED RATHER THAN ARGUED.** With the mirror trigger dropped, **`REJECTION 1` still passed** and only `REJECTION 2` failed. A guard only on `impersonation_sessions` is satisfied by writing the `sessions` row **second**: at the moment the impersonation row was checked, the trader row did not exist. **The hole is an impersonation token resolving on the trader auth path**, which `GS-303` calls the failure that makes every other control on that table decorative. Two triggers is not redundancy, it is the difference between a guard and half of one.

  - **`IMPERSONATION-C2`'s BOUND IS `LEAST(expires_at, COALESCE(ended_at, expires_at))` AND THE NAIVE FORM PASSES FOUR OF THE FIVE TESTS.** Rewritten as plain `expires_at`, **`REJECTION 4` still passed** and `REJECTION 5` failed. The naive bound admits a page view recorded after an **explicit exit** but before the original expiry: a view of a trader's account after the session ended, recorded as though it were live. **Only the case that exits early and then views can see the difference**, and it is the case nobody writes.

  - **A `CHECK` CANNOT REFUSE A REQUEST, AND THAT TURNED OUT TO BE THE STRONGER POSITION.** `GS-301` says an expired session's next request is *"refused, not silently served"*, which is an application property. What the database can do is refuse the **row**, which makes a request served past the box **unauditable**: the system fails loudly at the moment it tries to record what it did rather than quietly succeeding. **A control that converts a silent failure into a noisy one is doing the job an invariant can do from where it sits**, and it is worth more than the prose it implements.

  - **TWO OF EIGHT CATALOGUE FIGURES WERE WRITTEN FROM ARITHMETIC AND BOTH WERE WRONG IN THE SAME DIRECTION.** The `0001` to `0041` index and check counts were assumed three below the new totals, because `0042` declares three named indexes and three named table constraints. **The real deltas are seven and seven.** The counts include two primary keys and a unique index, which nobody writes the word `INDEX` for, and three column-level `CHECK`s, which nobody writes the word `CONSTRAINT` for. **Corrected by querying the counterfactual database, which is the only method that could have caught it.** The paragraph recording the error is kept in [DELTA_MANIFEST section 21](../../packages/db/DELTA_MANIFEST.md) because the arithmetic was persuasive.
