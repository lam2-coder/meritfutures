### 3. Landmines

- **A correct measurement whose stated scope does not survive being cited is a failure mode no gate catches.** [ADR-392](../decisions/ADR-392.md) declared plainly that it was not applying the class test. Three later entries cited its table and carried the conclusion without the caveat, and the caveat sits four paragraphs above the table. **Four waves of records said "not repairable" about eight pointers that were repairable the whole time.**
- **Every citation in every dated record in this corpus is unchecked, in both directions, at any magnitude of error.** Section 7 of the ADR measured it in three document classes. `RECORD_HEADING` masks a file from `RI-16` if its first heading carries a date or a session number, which is true of every session log, every ADR whose title carries its date, and every dated review. **The only control on that surface is a row being given the fence.**
- **`G1` passes on a token that cannot be resolved.** A house phrase repeated on five rows of the cited file satisfied *"a token a later reader can search for"* and let a born-wrong pointer look exact for a year. **The eight repaired here were all checked against uniqueness and all pass at exactly 1 occurrence**, but that was derived after the repairs were chosen, not before, and a row repairing a non-unique subject would have had no warning.
- **These eight are repaired to COORDINATES and the corpus's recent practice has been to repair to NAMES.** The argument is that the restructure is finished and a number that will hold is more useful to a reader. **If the runbook moves again, this row re-armed eight pointers with a fresher date, which reads as more trustworthy than the 2026-08-24 originals.** One insertion above line 141 does it.
- **The whole entry rests on `git log -S` being the right instrument and that ruling is six days old.** [ADR-396](../decisions/ADR-396.md) named its own open cost: [ADR-388](../decisions/ADR-388.md)'s corpus-wide census of 289 has never been re-run under it, and its 76 against 42 is what the repairability ruling stands on.
- **The repository was not named in the dispatch and no source was attached to the session.** It was recovered from the account's repository list and cross-checked against the parent session's own `sources` record before anything was cloned. **The container also arrived with no checkout at all, for the third consecutive wave, and the clone this row made is FULL rather than shallow**, so the dispatch was wrong in both directions across two waves.
