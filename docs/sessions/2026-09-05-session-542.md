### 5. Blockers and landmines

- **FOUR MERGED MIGRATIONS ARE WATCHED BY NOTHING AND TWO ARE MONEY PATH.** `0044`'s fee-back idempotency index and `0074`'s closed vocabulary are the ones that matter; `0063`'s OTP write-once trigger is the third and `0041` the fourth. The only exercise any of them has ever had against a running database is the seven statements in [ADR-351](../decisions/ADR-351.md) section 3, taken by hand and gone the moment that section is read rather than run.
- **`0044`'s CONTROL IS JOINTLY HELD BY THE SCHEMA AND ONE WRITER.** A settlement retry writing a fee-back grant with `source_payout_request_id` left `NULL` falls outside the partial index entirely and doubles the credit with no refusal. The DDL cannot make that column mandatory. **This is the single most valuable probe the session found and did not write.**
- **`RETIRED_REGISTER` HAS NOW ADMITTED A RAISE.** The rule allowing it was written in the same commit that used it. A register that admits a raise whenever the raiser argues well has a different property from the one it advertises, and the founder should decide whether that is the right property.
- **`data-model-columns.mjs` HOLDS FIVE POINTERS INTO `gates.mjs`'s LINE NUMBERS.** Any edit to a comment nine thousand lines above them breaks all five. `RI-15` catches it every time, which is what makes the cost affordable rather than an argument that the pointers are safe.
- **`RI-37`'s SUITE NOW WRITES INTO THE CHECK'S OWN REGISTER.** `withRegisterEntry` seeds and restores in a `finally`, and the restore is what keeps a failing expectation from leaving the register dirty for the next case in the file. It is the only writer, and a second one would make the export a hazard rather than a seam.
- **TWELVE RESERVATION ROWS AND ELEVEN NAME NO BRANCH.** They collide in [ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md) at merge by construction, and the dispatcher resolves them by hand.
- **THE SESSION NUMBER `542` IS DERIVED AND ITS ASSUMPTION IS UNMEASURABLE.** `530` was not free. `542` assumes rows `340` to `350` take one session each, and none of those eleven is on any ref.
