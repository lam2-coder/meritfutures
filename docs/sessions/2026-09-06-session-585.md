### 3. The ruling, and what it costs

**A document whose own title dates it is a record end to end.** A document whose title does not is unchanged, which is what keeps [`STATE.md`](../STATE.md) in scope: its first heading is `# STATE` and no recogniser matches it.

**The population, derived on `3b664fcf` through the checkers' own reader:** 1,342 documents under `docs/`, 1,101 title-dated, **25** where a later level 1 or 2 heading cancels the date, **16** of those carrying citations, **286** citations inside them. **ADR-388 reported 15 and 248.** Its ten reviews and 182 reproduce at the character; its five ADRs and 66 are right as far as they go and miss [`unsigned-adr-audit-2026-08-18.md`](../decisions/gates/unsigned-adr-audit-2026-08-18.md), which carries 38 more.

**The cost is zero where it matters and the check was opened rather than estimated.** Of the 286: 0 name a missing path, 0 point past a file's end, **0 carry a name**, and 0 would raise a finding. Corpus-wide the name-bound population is **73 before and 73 after**, against 8,732 citations the reader sees on the base and 8,733 on this head.

### 4. The leg, and its seeds

**Assertion 3 on `CI-06/table-row-width`: a body row of a claimed table must close with an unescaped pipe.** Measured population, derived at reporting time: **0 of 24,590 body rows over 3,857 tables**, and 0 header rows and 0 delimiter rows too, which is why the leg is body-scoped. **No register entry of any kind.**

Four seeds, every one restored byte-identical and verified with `sha256sum -c`. **A**, the exact defect [ADR-384](../decisions/ADR-384.md) found at [`README.md`](README.md) line 141: **RED at 32 of 33**. **B**, the same on a body row of [`ALLOCATION.md`](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md): **RED**. **C**, the header row of that table: **GREEN at 33 of 33**, which is the control for the body scope. **D**, the closing pipe replaced with an escaped `\|`: **RED**.
