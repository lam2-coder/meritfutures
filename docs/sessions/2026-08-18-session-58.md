  - **THE BRIEF'S CONCLUSION WAS RIGHT AND ITS PREMISE MERGED TWO DIFFERENT HORIZONS, AND THE MERGER
    WOULD HAVE SET A NUMBER WRONG BY AN ORDER OF MAGNITUDE.** The brief describes the source as
    publishing "~2 weeks ahead". **The artifacts say 138 days.** The two-week figure is real but it
    belongs to a different fact in a different document: the [work-list paste](../../packages/db/src/seed/calendars/reference/cme-holiday-page-2026-globex-schedule.pasted-2026-08-17.md)

    137 days and the **hours** are final at 14. **A horizon recalibrated to fourteen days would produce
    an alarm that almost never fires**, which is `FM-17`'s twin, and it is what this session would have
    shipped if the instruction to confirm from the artifacts had been taken as a formality.

  - **AND THE MERGED PREMISE HID THE BIGGER FINDING UNDERNEATH IT.** Separating the two horizons is what
    surfaced the money-path half, which the objective did not name and which is worth more than the
    coverage ruling: **the Thanksgiving export was taken 100 days out and carries exact times**

    falsifiable by a file in its own directory.** What the artifacts support is narrower and still

    rather than rounding it off.

- **Landmines:**

  - **THE OBJECTIVE SAID GROUPS A, F AND H WERE "BLOCKED ON THOSE SHAPES", AND F AND H ARE NOT.** Every
    group H row in the held-back table is behind `L-11`'s settlement block, and the table says so in

    find that out anyway**, which is the cost of a premise nobody re-derived before acting on it. The

  - **"ABSORBED" MEANS TWO UNRELATED THINGS IN THIS CORPUS AND THE TWO SIT NINE ROWS APART IN ONE
    TABLE.** [ADR-055](../decisions/ADR-055.md)'s `absorbs_into` is a **holiday's session handed to

  - **THE ABSORPTION IS THE ONE FACT THE OBJECTIVE ASKED FOR THAT THIS FORMAT CANNOT CARRY, AND THE

    discharged at ingest. **What a holiday DOES give the fold is the gap**: `2026-11-25` and `2026-11-27`
