in place.** The fence is `docs/` and this file.

- **Landmines:**

  - **THE BRIEF'S CONCLUSION WAS RIGHT AND ITS PREMISE MERGED TWO DIFFERENT HORIZONS, AND THE MERGER
    WOULD HAVE SET A NUMBER WRONG BY AN ORDER OF MAGNITUDE.** The brief describes the source as
    publishing "~2 weeks ahead". **The artifacts say 138 days.** The two-week figure is real but it
    belongs to a different fact in a different document: the [work-list paste](../../packages/db/src/seed/calendars/reference/cme-holiday-page-2026-globex-schedule.pasted-2026-08-17.md)
    records that CME finalises **hours** roughly two weeks before each holiday. So the **dates** reach
    137 days and the **hours** are final at 14. **A horizon recalibrated to fourteen days would produce
    an alarm that almost never fires**, which is `FM-17`'s twin, and it is what this session would have
    shipped if the instruction to confirm from the artifacts had been taken as a formality.

  - **AND THE MERGED PREMISE HID THE BIGGER FINDING UNDERNEATH IT.** Separating the two horizons is what
    surfaced the money-path half, which the objective did not name and which is worth more than the
    coverage ruling: **the Thanksgiving export was taken 100 days out and carries exact times**
    (`12:15` Equities, `13:45` Energy) for a schedule the publisher says is not final for another
    eighty-six days. **Merit transcribes hours, not dates.** The exposure is one-directional and is
    `F-3`'s own asymmetry: a close moving **later**, a holiday **removed**, or an `absorbs_into` that
    should have been an object, each leaves the recorded session a **strict subset** and a fill in the
    uncovered part lands inside no Merit session at all. That is `R-01`'s condition, and it is the
    third door into the room [ADR-055](../decisions/ADR-055.md) closed on the open end and `F-3` on the
    close end.

  - **THE ARTIFACTS ARE NOT LITERALLY FORWARD-ONLY AND THE QUALIFICATION SURVIVED INTO THE RULING.**
    The all-products export reaches back one day, to `2026-08-16`, inside a three-day window containing
    the retrieval date. **A ruling that had asserted "the tool never looks back" would have been
    falsifiable by a file in its own directory.** What the artifacts support is narrower and still
    decisive: no artifact reaches a holiday that had already passed. The ruling states the qualification
    rather than rounding it off.

  - **THE STRONGEST EVIDENCE WAS INSIDE THE `.xlsx` MEMBERS AND IT DISAGREES WITH ITSELF ON ONE FILE.**
    `docProps/core.xml` gives `dcterms:modified` for the four holiday exports as `2026-08-17T03:29:56Z`,
    `04:43:22Z`, `04:43:33Z` and `04:44:34Z`. **The all-products file's is `2021-10-13T13:16:40Z`**, so
    that export path does not restamp and its retrieval date rests on the filename alone. All five share
    a `dcterms:created` of `2020-05-18T15:33:05Z`, which is the template's and is not a retrieval fact.
    **Reading `created` as the retrieval date would have dated every artifact to 2020**, and reading the
    filenames alone would have missed that four of them carry independent corroboration.

  - **TWO CLAIMS COULD NOT BE SOURCED TO AN ARTIFACT AND BOTH ARE LOAD-BEARING SOMEWHERE ELSE.** The
    eleven-row `2026 CME Globex Trading Schedule` (four ahead, seven passed, arithmetic closing at
    eleven) and the two-week finalisation note both come from a file whose **first line reads "NOT AN
    ARTIFACT"**. The counts agree across the brief, the calendars README and `ADR-055`'s `FINDING 2`,
    which is agreement among restatements of one paste rather than corroboration. **Each is marked where
    it is used and the ruling is written so that no part of it fails if either is wrong.** That is
    `ADR-055`'s own discipline, which declined to rule from the same paste, applied to a session that
    had to cite it anyway.

  - **THE ALARM DEFECT IS NOT REPAIRED BY A SMALLER NUMBER, AND THAT WAS THE FIRST DRAFT.** Thirty days
    is unsatisfiable at the year-end trough for the same reason six months is unsatisfiable in August:
    with the schedule ending `2027-01-01`, a reading on `2026-12-20` has twelve days of coverage and **no
    threshold above twelve clears**. **Merit does not control the ceiling**, so no constant duration
    works. The defect is that one alarm answers two questions and reports them identically, and a
    suppression applies to the key, so muting the chronically noisy question mutes `F-4`'s.

  - **`ADR-055` IS NOT WRONG AND ITS ONE SENTENCE NEEDED A QUALIFICATION.** It wrote that the alarm
    "gains its purpose" from the evidence bound, "at six months against an evidence-bounded horizon it
    fires when the transcribed calendar actually runs short". **True of the direction and false of the
    number.** Against a declared-but-empty two-year tail the alarm could never fire; against the evidence
    bound it can never stop. **The bound moved it from one unreachable end of its range to the other and
    the six was carried across unexamined**, which is the drift `CI-06g` exists for in generated spans and
    which no gate covers in prose.

  - **`OQ-SE-02` CARRIES A THIRD STALE CLAIM THAT NOBODY WAS LOOKING FOR.** It says
    [CRON_INVENTORY](../ops/runbooks/CRON_INVENTORY.md) "today carries 90-day and quarterly rows and no
    annual one". **It carries two annual rows**, admin domain renewal and breach-notification contacts.
    It was true when written and the document moved under it. Checked line by line rather than by grep,
    which is also how the absence of any calendar row was established: **the word "calendar" appears in
    that document only as the name of a section.**

  - **`057` WAS RECORDED IN [ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md) BY A BRANCH THAT DOES NOT HOLD IT,
    AND SKIPPING THAT STEP WOULD HAVE STOPPED THIS SESSION THE WAY IT STOPPED THE `055` ONE.**
    Gaplessness is asserted over allocated **plus reserved**, so claiming `058` with no `057` row leaves
    a hole this branch cannot account for. `claude/test3-3p7a5b` carries `ADR-057.md`, and the subject in
    the new row was read off **that ref's own claim row** rather than inferred. **Neither number is
    linked**, because `CI-06a` fails on a link to a document this ref does not carry. This is the `044`
    row's precedent and the remedy the paragraph under that table already prescribes.

  - **`ADR-056`'s README ROW IS IN THE WRONG TABLE AND IS DELIBERATELY LEFT THERE.** It sits inside the
    **Gate closures** table, whose columns are `Closure | Rulings | ADRs`, carrying two cells instead of
    three. `CI-06n` passes because the row resolves; nothing checks which table a row is in.
    **`ADR-058`'s row was placed correctly in the ADR listing** and `ADR-056`'s was not moved: a
    one-line move in a registry three branches are appending to is a merge conflict for no change in
    behaviour, which is [session 56](2026-08-18-session-56.md)'s own reasoning for leaving a `generate.mjs`
    em-dash alone. **Naming it here is what makes it fixable by whoever is next in the file.**

  - **[ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md) CARRIES TWO PRE-EXISTING APPENDIX F EM-DASHES**, at lines
