### The one thing this session had to decide rather than transcribe

**[ADR-041](../decisions/ADR-041.md) states the ruling completely: partial, +3 to 5 days, P8, on `INV-M15-06`. What it does not state is what "partial" leaves standing on launch day**, and that is not a gap in the ADR: it is the question that only appears once somebody writes the scope into a phase with a definition of done attached.

The tempting fold was to transcribe the ADR's reason into both documents and stop. **That would have produced a P8 whose stated justification is an invariant nothing in P8 can violate**, and a reader arriving at the build with "we pulled M15 forward because role removal must not publish an enforcement" would reasonably look for the batching discipline in the launch scope and not find it, because it is not there and should not be.

> **The honest form is that the ruling bought an ordering, not an enforcement.** Restriction exists first; role sync arrives into a corpus that already has it. The invariant becomes load bearing the day role sync ships and not before, and `M15-B-nn` plus `DEP-M15-04` are its gate at that moment rather than at launch.

**Nothing in the ruling moved.** Scope, phase and cost are folded exactly as ruled, and the observation is recorded beside them rather than used to reopen them. This corpus's worst recorded errors came from reasoning about what a ruling implied instead of reading what it said; the counterpart discipline is to say plainly which half of a module a stated reason reaches.
