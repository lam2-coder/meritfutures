### 1. The objective

[ADR-421](../decisions/ADR-421.md) section 13 item 1: limit 4 is still a row and it is now priced. Hold `packages/db/src/**` and one port family under `apps/worker/src/**` together, choose the best first family by deriving rather than inheriting, retype the accessor and the port in the same commit, and **show one hand-written mapping DELETED**. Watch a RED before making it pass. Report anything that reddens outside the fence rather than fixing it.

### 2. Done

- **The container measured and reported.** No repository on arrival: `/home/user` held `.` and `..`, `git rev-parse` returned `fatal: not a git repository`, no `gh` binary. Cloned by this row, NOT shallow, **14.908s real**, `.git` **196M**. `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile` **8.9s**, exit 0.
- **The base reproduced at `bfd6c400`** on a clean tree before the first edit: `generate` reported *every span already matches its query* and left the tree clean, gates **33 of 33**, invariants **35 of 35**, suite **341 files / 8,246 passed / 10 skipped**, typecheck exit 0, lint exit 0, `format:check` clean.
- **The candidate families derived at source.** Nine `ports.ts` modules read for declarations of `rows`, `rowsWhere`, `rowAt` and `lockAt`; six declare at least one and three declare none. Read tables counted against `schema.ts` by walking `pgTable` bodies rather than by grepping. ADR-426 section 3 is the table and the reason `digests` is first.
- **The accessor narrowed for one verb on one handle** and the blast radius PERFORMED before any port followed: 2 errors, both ADR-421's own type case, zero in `apps/`.
- **The port, the door, the two adapters and the two consumers moved together**, and `DigestRow` and `record()` deleted.
- **Three cases written first and watched RED**, and two existing controls inverted with the argument for each written into the files.
- **`ALLOCATION.md`'s one drifted citation repointed**, `scoped-db.ts:4291` to `:4317` for `BEGIN`, because that registry is inside this fence.

### 3. Not done, and why

- **`apps/api/**` is untouched.** It is forbidden to this fence, it holds twenty of the port declarations limit 4 describes, and section 8 of the entry records the one place that mattered: four of the five citations a top-of-file edit would break live there, and this row may not repoint them. It made its own diff line-neutral instead.
- **`packages/db/migrations/` is untouched and no migration number is reserved or taken.** Nothing here is DDL.
- **The other three verbs are unspent.** `rows`, `rowAt` and `lockAt` return `unknown` at every declaration. `lockAt`'s six callers are the `FOR UPDATE` money sites [ADR-421](../decisions/ADR-421.md) section 13 item 2 names, and that row wants the founder's read before it is dispatched rather than after.
- **`insert` and `updateAt` were not narrowed.** A write returns what the database made of what was sent, which is a different claim from a read, and the suite now says so where the producer's fake appends.
- **`lockScope` was not reconciled.** ADR-426 section 10 is the derivation and the reason: the lists cannot be reconciled without [ADR-303](../decisions/ADR-303.md) itself and the type cannot be reconciled without `apps/api/**`, and neither is in this fence.
- **No pull request opened, nothing signed, no `E2` read performed or waited for.**

### 4. Two things this row got wrong and fixed in the open

**THE FIRST WAS A CONTROL, NOT THE CODE.** Case 11.3 was written to assert that the deletion took no refusal with it, by demanding `throw new DigestRowError(` in every reader's body. It went RED on `readNullableText`, which has never thrown: it returns `null` for an absent value and hands everything else to `readText`. **The case was wrong and the code was right**, and it now reads for a refusal reached directly or through a delegate. Its first draft also asserted a TOTAL count of throws, which was refused as an instrument on two grounds: it is a derivable number written down, and it passes just as happily when one reader loses its `throw` and another grows a second.

**THE SECOND WAS THE PLACEMENT OF A TYPE, AND IT COST A FULL RE-RUN TO FIND.** `DeclaredRow` was first declared beside `CatalogRow`, where it belongs. That moved every symbol below it by thirty-seven lines and `RI-15` and `RI-16` reported five findings, four of them in `apps/api/**`. **The discovery is the finding**: a fence may hold `packages/db/src/**` in full and still be unable to add a sentence to the top of its largest file, because the gate that fires is satisfiable only in a directory the same fence excludes. The diff above `export interface SystemTx` is now line-neutral, limit 4's item 4 carries its correction in the same five lines with the superseded sentence quoted whole under `RI-14`, and the type is declared at the foot of the file.
