### 4. Done

- **The census is DERIVED and PINNED BY NAME, in both directions.** A port gaining a member grows the gap; an entry gaining a bare name shrinks it. Both redden, naming the member. **A bare name is therefore not free**, which seed 2 watched.
- **The requirement that every entry account for every member is REFUSED**, and that is a ruling. It is a policy nobody has ruled and is measurably wrong for the five port-level entries: `useTurnstileVerifier` waits on nothing, and three others are blocked because calling the setter installs what is already installed. **[ADR-367](../decisions/ADR-367.md) section 5's reason applies to that half and to neither of the other two.**
- **"Accounted for" is defined as NAMED and defended.** Two stronger definitions were built and measured and both are worse: one counts five of forty-five, and one drops four true accountings. **Naming is the necessary half and the entry says so.**
- **[ADR-366](../decisions/ADR-366.md) section 10 item 1 is repaired and BOUND.** The wallet entry enumerates `operator`, `writeCorrection` and `reconcile`, the cardinal binds to that list, every listed name is asserted a real member, and `operator` is pinned by name. **The retired figure is NAMED rather than quoted** (`RI-14`).
- **Ten falsification seeds, every one restored byte-identical under `sha256sum -c`.**
