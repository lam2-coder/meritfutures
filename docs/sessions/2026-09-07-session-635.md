### 5. Landmines

- **THE WORD "STALE" IS WRONG AND THE WHOLE ESTATE USES IT.** Seven live sentences, four wordings, none ever true. **A change-triggered control catches none of them**, so anybody designing one from the word "stale" designs the wrong thing.
- **LINE-COUNT NEUTRALITY IS NOT THE RULED PROPERTY AND TWO DISPATCHES HAVE NOW COMMISSIONED IT.** [`repo-invariants.mjs:3008-3016`](../../packages/tooling/checks/repo-invariants.mjs) says it is neither necessary nor sufficient and that the property that is both is **no cited line moves**.
- **LINE-NEUTRALITY PRESERVES THE POINTER AND NOT WHAT IT LANDS ON.** Nine citations name `:3446` or `:4573`. `:4573` still holds the quoted sentence; **`:3446` now holds the middle of the correction**, so a reader following *"the stale sentence is at `:3446`"* meets the repair instead.
- **THE 429 FIGURE MEASURES THE WHOLE CITATION POPULATION AND NOT WHAT AN EDIT MOVES.** It is **447 over 155** today; the number an insertion at limit 1 would move is **134**.
- **A GREP FOR THE SENTENCE STILL RETURNS SEVENTEEN SITES AFTER THIS ROW**, two of them now corrections. That is `RI-14`'s idiom working, not a failed repair.
- **`packages/db` IS THE MONEY PATH'S OWN PACKAGE**, so a comment-only diff there is still reported as money path. Not one executable line moved and typecheck is exit 0.
