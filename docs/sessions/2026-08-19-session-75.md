**A gate landing red on 105 findings cannot land at all**, and that is a mechanical constraint rather than a preference: [`falsify.mjs`](../../scripts/corpus/falsify.mjs) runs every gate against the tree as it stands and **a gate that cannot pass there is an ERROR**. So `CI06U_REGISTER` pins the exact `(file, key)` pairs that exist on `main` today, **and it shrinks only**.
