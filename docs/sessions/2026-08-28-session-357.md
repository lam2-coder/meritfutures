### Landmines

- **`prettier --write` OVER A DOCUMENT UNDER `docs/` IS A SWEEP, NOT A FORMAT.** `format:check`'s glob is `{apps,packages,e2e,scripts}` and the corpus's markdown is deliberately outside it. Formatting `API_CONTRACT.md` by hand rewrote its frontmatter into a multi-line array and requoted every `ts` block in the file. `git checkout` and re-apply is the repair; the reflex to run prettier over everything you touched is the hazard.
- **A TERM LIST TRANSCRIBED TWICE AND BOUND BY NOTHING IS THE DEFECT THAT CREATED THIS RESERVATION**, and the same shape is live elsewhere: `admin-reads.ts` transcribes API_CONTRACT section 8 in several other places and only the roles and now the search terms are asserted against the document.
- **A COMPARISON OF TWO PARSED LISTS PASSES WHEN NEITHER COULD BE PARSED.** The binding assertion here is tied to a sentence prefix, which is exactly what a later reflow breaks silently.
