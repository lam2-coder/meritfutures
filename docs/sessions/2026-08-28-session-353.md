### 1. THE ADAPTER, AND WHY IT IS NOT HERE

**The dispatch's objective is the screen AND the adapter that serves it, and it ordered
the feasibility check first. The check says no, and the reason is one table.**

`admin-source/flags.ts` and `admin-source/graph.ts` are the shape: a module declares the
tables it reads as a closed tuple, declares a `Tx` whose `rowsWhere` and `rowAt` are keyed
by that tuple, and `SystemTx` satisfies it structurally. Two assertions in
[`admin-source-flags.test.ts`](../../apps/api/test/admin-source-flags.test.ts) hold that
shape together: every key those tuples name is in `TABLE_KEYS`, and
`const handle: AdminSourceTx = null as unknown as SystemTx` compiles.

**`AdminReadSource.readAccount` must fill eight sections and seven of them clear that
bar.** Derived at reporting time by importing the registry rather than by grepping:

| Claim | How it was produced | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `TABLE_KEYS` holds the seven tables the other sections need | imported [`packages/db/src/scope.ts`](../../packages/db/src/scope.ts) and read the array | **106 keys**, and `accounts`, `identities`, `dailyMarks`, `ruleStates`, `riskFlags`, `payoutRequests` and `adminActions` are all members |
| `TABLE_KEYS` does not hold `events` | the same import | **absent.** The only event-ish members are `pspWebhookEvents` and `kycFunnelEvents` |
| A `Tx` naming `'events'` is not satisfied by `SystemTx` | seeded an `AccountTx` under `src/admin-source/`, intersected it into `AdminSourceTx`, ran `tsc` | **`TS2322`**: *"Type `"events"` is not assignable to type `"identities" \| ... \| 91 more`"* |
| The other seven are the whole of the blocker | deleted `'events'` from the same seeded tuple and re-ran `tsc` | **exit 0** |

**`gate_results` IS NOT THE PROBLEM AND A READER WILL EXPECT IT TO BE.** `M06` section
3.2 makes it the load-bearing part of this screen, and there is no such table.
[`admin-source/evidence.ts:374`](../../apps/api/src/admin-source/evidence.ts) already
recorded why: `SD-06` split it into `rule_states.engine_gates` and
`rule_states.context_gates`, which [`schema.ts:720`](../../packages/db/src/schema.ts) and
`:721` declare as `jsonb ... notNull`. **A gate result is two columns on a rule state, and
`ruleStates` is a registered key.** So the section the corpus warns about hardest is the
one that would have worked.

**WHERE THIS BLOCKER IS HARDER THAN THE FEED'S.** `listEvents` returns a page and a
deployment without it simply has no feed. `readAccount` returns a RECORD OF EIGHT
SECTIONS and [`admin-reads.ts:1224`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-reads.ts)'s
`projectAccountDetail` refuses a response that omits one, in its own words: *"a
drill-down missing a section renders as a complete answer with a hole in it."* **So
seven of eight is a rejected response and not a smaller screen**, and there is no
partial adapter to land while the registration decision is pending.

**The three ways to land it today are each a refusal this corpus already made.**
Registering `events` is `packages/**`, is another session's fence this wave, and is an
entry rather than a commit. Reaching `sqlExecutor` needs a second `SqlExecutorReason`
member, which `ADR-157` closed. Declaring a second port is the shape `ADR-184` section 5
refused by name. **The fourth option is to report, and it is the one taken.**
