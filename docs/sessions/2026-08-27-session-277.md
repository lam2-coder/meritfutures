### 1. What the recommendation was, and why taking it silently was the one disposition the number forecloses

[ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md)'s `163` row reserved the number **REQUIRED rather than conditionally**, and it says why in its own words: *"The plan RECOMMENDS `apps/api` on both surfaces rather than a sixth deployable and permits the slice to refuse it with an argument, which is what makes the number required rather than conditional."*

**A recommendation that may be refused is a ruling whichever way it goes.** So the work was not choosing `apps/api`, which [P6 section 3.2](../plans/P6-live-tier.md) had already narrowed to it by elimination; the work was establishing what the elimination is worth, and the answer is that four of the five candidates are refused by something landed and **the fifth is refused by nothing at all.**

**The third row is the argument.** A sixth deployable serving the founder's numbers is [P6 section 10 item 2](../plans/P6-live-tier.md)'s failing direction landing in the one place nothing in this repository looks, and `RI-04`'s `covers` predicted it in writing: *"a sixth application directory added without an entry here is invisible to it, exactly as `apps/api` was."*

**It is a gap somebody left open on purpose, which changes what this entry may claim about it.** [Session 153](2026-08-24-session-153.md), the session that widened `DEPLOYABLES` from four to five, recorded the same gap and its disposition: *"deriving the list from disk was refused as a different check with a different failure mode."* So the entry's approval clause is **that different check, run once by hand against this ruling**, and not a gate this session found weak. Turning it into a standing check is minting `RI-12`, and this fence holds no file it could live in.

### 4. The diagram, and the edge that is deliberately absent

Three dotted edges: `Rithmic` to `Worker` for the indicative ticks, `API` to `Portal` and `API` to `Admin` for the frames. **The dotted edge is the diagram's only new vocabulary and it carries the whole distinction**: a solid edge is a request somebody made and an answer that is authoritative, a dotted edge is delivery nobody asked for carrying a value labelled indicative on the number itself (`INV-M4-11`).

**The leg between the ingest and the channel is not drawn.** It is a store, its medium is [P6 section 10 item 1](../plans/P6-live-tier.md)'s open ruling, and **two of the three available readings draw no node here at all**: a Postgres table sits inside `DB`, and a process-local store sits inside `API`. Only the third, a second stateful service, is a node. **A diagram that guessed would settle that ruling in mermaid**, so the omission is stated under the diagram rather than left to be noticed.

**And `P6-c`'s fence holds neither document**, so under the second and third readings the amendment this diagram will need has no owner. That is section 5 item 1 of the entry and it is the largest thing this session found that it could not fix.
