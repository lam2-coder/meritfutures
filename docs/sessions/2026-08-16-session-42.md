### `EC-157`: two arithmetics for one row, and they agree only where the column is unused

**The generator could not be written without choosing**, which is how a contradiction that had sat in a merged migration since `0014` surfaced in a non-money session.

| Artifact | The adjustment lands |
|---|---|
| `INV-18` (M01 §1.5), `R-10`, [`EC-034`](../edge-cases/EC-034.md), and `0014`'s own comment on the column | at the **open**, between sessions |
| `daily_marks_balance_arithmetic` in [`0014`](../../packages/db/migrations/0014_marks.sql), labelled `INV-18` by its own comment and by the [design record](../architecture/data-model/daily_marks.md) | **inside the day** |

Worked in integer cents on the case `SD-01` exists for: a 250,000c payout against a prior close of 5,000,000c on a day that makes 30,000c. `INV-18` gives an opening of **4,750,000**, `INV-19` gives a closing of **4,780,000**, and the `CHECK` demands **4,530,000**. **The database refuses the row the two approved identities require**, so the mark for every settled payout is unwritable as specified.

**Nothing had noticed because every mark in this repository carries a zero adjustment**, and at zero all three agree exactly. That is also why a reading catches it and an inspection does not: the defect is invisible on any single row anyone has yet written.

**It is not ruled here.** One repair supersedes a merged migration; the other reopens what `SD-01` closed and takes `INV-21` with it. Both are money path. What this session did instead is make the conflict **executable**: `checkStoredBalanceArithmetic` transcribes the `CHECK` verbatim, and a test watches every emitted mark with a non-zero adjustment satisfy `INV-18` and `INV-19` and fail the constraint. **That test fails the moment either side moves**, which is what makes it the record rather than a paragraph.

**The reading a session under pressure will re-derive is that the constraint wins because it is executable.** It is the one artifact of the five that was never read against the other four.

### What the generator does not do, named rather than implied

It emits **one account's run from its open**, so `INV-20` binds on `marks[0]` and `INV-18` on everything after. It does not model **phases**, because phase is engine state; it does not model **supersession**, because `R-11` says the engine reads only live marks; and it does not model **settlements**, because those are the third generator. What a settlement leaves on the day is `adjustment_cents`, and that is a column of the mark and is drawn.

It cannot catch an engine that reads `CalendarSlice`'s **precomputed index** wrongly, because it emits the constructor's input and ADR-046 gives the constructor to `calendar.ts`. That is deliberate: guessing the index's shape is how this generator would have been written twice, which is the cost the deferral from PR #39 was paid to avoid.
