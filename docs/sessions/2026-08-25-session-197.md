### 2. The three registrations, with the column each rule turns on

| Table | Class | Column, and the trap beside it |
|---|---|---|
| `affiliates` | **owned** | `identity_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES identities(id) ON DELETE RESTRICT` ([`0005`](../../packages/db/migrations/0005_affiliate_program.sql)). AN AFFILIATE IS AN IDENTITY is the table's own DDL comment. **The trap is `parent_id`**, which references this same table for the sub-IB trees unused in v1: a derived rule through it scopes a person's own affiliate row to their RECRUITER, and on a null parent to nobody |
| `affiliate_creatives` | **derived**, `hop` | `affiliate_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES affiliates(id)`, single-valued, so the join cannot multiply rows. **`reviewed_by` is not the tenancy and could not be**: the DDL declares it `text NULL` with no foreign key, and a creative belongs to the affiliate who submitted it rather than to the operator who read it |
| `affiliate_clicks` | **derived**, `hop` | `affiliate_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES affiliates(id)`. **A click belongs to the affiliate and not to the person who clicked**, and the DDL is what says so: no identity column, no `user_id`, no `purchase_id`, because a click happens before anybody has signed in. `ip`, `user_agent` and `click_fingerprint` are the three columns that look like a clicker and none is a tenancy |

**`balance_cents` is why `affiliates` is money path**, and it is signed: negative is owed to Merit, tied to `negative_balance_since` in both directions by `affiliates_negative_balance_has_clock`. A wrong rule on this table shows a debt to the wrong human.

### 3. `attributions` is refused, and the DDL argues the refusal better than this log can

[ADR-092](../decisions/ADR-092.md) section 9 names `identity_links` and `attributions` together as **per-table rulings** and takes neither. [Session 192](2026-08-25-session-192.md) refused the first. This refuses the second, the same way and for the same reason.

Both columns are `uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES identities(id) ON DELETE RESTRICT` at [`0012`](../../packages/db/migrations/0012_disputes_and_affiliate_settlement.sql), and the migration's own comment states the tenancy problem without naming it:

> Both identities are stored rather than joined, because the check is a statement about the two of them AT THE MOMENT OF PURCHASE

**A row that is a statement about two people has no single owner**, and `attributions_literal_self_deal_is_void` is the constraint that proves the two are normally different: the row where `buyer_identity_id = affiliate_identity_id` is refused unless it is voided. So the choice is real on every live row.

- **Naming the affiliate** returns a buyer's own purchase attribution to their referrer. That is a read of another human's purchase, it returns rows, it raises nothing, and it is the BOLA failure [ADR-008](../decisions/ADR-008.md) scoped the accessor to bound.
- **Naming the buyer** hides every referral from the affiliate who earned it, which breaks `GET /affiliate/stats` rather than leaking, and is wrong in the quieter direction.

**Neither is taken.** The table is absent from `TABLES`, from `SCOPE_RULES`, from `SQL_NAME` **and from [`schema.ts`](../../packages/db/src/schema.ts) entirely**, on session 192's stated reason: a declared-but-unregistered `pgTable` leaves a declaration no drift assertion compares, which [ADR-092](../decisions/ADR-092.md) section 4 row three watched compiling at exit 0.

### 4. `affiliate_commissions` comes out too, and the type checker is what says so

**This is not a second judgment.** The table's columns are `id`, `attribution_id`, `amount_cents`, `status`, `payable_after`, `chargeback_window_ends_on`, `clawback_of`, `paid_in_statement_id`, `created_at`, `updated_at`. Read for a path to an identity:

- `attribution_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES attributions(id)` -- the only real path, and `attributions` has no key.
- `paid_in_statement_id` reaches `affiliate_statements`, which is one of [ADR-092](../decisions/ADR-092.md) section 9's four and is not registrable here.
- `clawback_of` is a self-reference. `status`, the two dates and `amount_cents` reach nobody.

`DerivedRule.via` is typed `TableKey`, so **the rule cannot be written**. This is [ADR-092](../decisions/ADR-092.md)'s *"a session cannot know its own slice size before it runs"* in the direction [ADR-094](../decisions/ADR-094.md) section 6 did not name: there, `P4-a` reported **four of three** because totality forced `users` into the slice; here the count is **three of five** because totality forced a table out of it.

**The honest cost is stated rather than softened.** `amount_cents` is signed money in integer cents and `affiliate_commissions` is the one M8 table holding cents per sale. It is unreachable through either accessor until `attributions` gets the per-table ruling [ADR-092](../decisions/ADR-092.md) section 9 declined to take, and **that ruling is an ADR, which this session does not have and did not allocate.**

### 5. [ADR-094](../decisions/ADR-094.md)'s fold applies nothing, measured rather than assumed

`grep -rn "ALTER TABLE" packages/db/migrations/*.sql` filtered for `affiliate` or `attribution` returns **zero lines across all 47 migrations**. So for all five M8 tables the fold replays nothing, refuses nothing, and the comparison is byte-identical to a `CREATE TABLE` read. **No table here is registered by being excused from the assertion and the fold was not widened to fit one.** Neither of the tree's two `ALTER COLUMN` refusals, `otp_challenges` and `trading_calendar`, is M08's; `identity_links` and `dedupe_matches` are untouched.

### 8. Reported and deliberately not acted on

**Registering `affiliates` makes two already-declared columns eligible for an inline reference they do not carry.** [`0006`](../../packages/db/migrations/0006_commerce.sql) declares `coupons.affiliate_id` at `:44` and `purchases.affiliate_id` at `:122`, both `uuid NULL REFERENCES affiliates(id) ON DELETE RESTRICT` inline, and both are transcribed as a bare `uuid('affiliate_id')` because the target was not one of this file's tables when they were written.

**Nothing is violated.** [`schema.ts`](../../packages/db/src/schema.ts)'s header rule is a NECESSARY condition -- a column carries `.references()` *only when* the FK is inline and the target is registered -- and not a sufficient one. No assertion moves either way, because the drift assertion compares column NAMES and the fold reads `ADD COLUMN` while deliberately ignoring `ADD CONSTRAINT`. **Adding the two references is two lines inside two other sessions' table literals**, which is [ADR-092](../decisions/ADR-092.md) section 4's hunk-boundary hazard paid for no mechanical gain. Named here, left.

### 9. The gap that is still open, restated because nothing here closes it

**Column TYPE and NULLABILITY are asserted nowhere, now across fifty-six registered tables.** [ADR-094](../decisions/ADR-094.md) section 3 names it and this session does not touch it. It is why three columns in this diff are transcribed rather than approximated: `affiliates.code` is `citext` because a referral code differing only in casing is the same code to every human who types it, `affiliate_clicks.click_fingerprint` is `bytea`, and `affiliate_clicks.ip` is `inet`. **A `text()` on any of the three would compile, would satisfy the column-name comparison, and would be a wrong transcription on the one axis nothing looks at.**

- **Next:** **`attributions` needs a per-table ruling and it is an ADR, not a transcription.** Until it lands, `attributions` and `affiliate_commissions` are unreachable and `M08-4`, `M08-5`, `M08-6` and `M08-7` cannot read the commission table through either accessor. Beyond that, [session 162](2026-08-24-session-162.md)'s three founder items stand unchanged: the event-name split (`M08-8`), `OQ-M8-05`, and `OI-06`.

- **Blockers:** none for this session. Both refusals are ruled absences rather than blocked work.

- **Landmines:**

  - **A ROW OWNED BY TWO IDENTITIES HAS A CHILD, AND THE CHILD LEAVES WITH IT.** The `attributions` refusal was ruled per-table by [ADR-092](../decisions/ADR-092.md) section 9 and is cheap. What was not anticipated anywhere is that `affiliate_commissions` has **no second parent**: every other column on it reaches a date, a status, a signed amount or itself. **A per-table refusal on a hub table silently refuses its dependents**, and the count a later reader sees is three where the plan's deltas say five.

  - **`affiliate_statements` IS DISCUSSED IN M08 ON EVERY OTHER PAGE AND NAMED IN IT NOWHERE.** `INV-M8-09`, `INV-M8-12`, `FM-M8-08`, `FM-M8-09`, `FM-M8-10`, `FM-M8-11` and section 3.4 are all about affiliate statements, and `grep -c "\baffiliate_statements\b"` over the plan returns **0**. [ADR-092](../decisions/ADR-092.md) section 9's whole-word measurement is therefore correct AND its consequence is sharper than it reads: the table nobody's plan names is the table this module's most load-bearing invariant lives on.

  - **`coupons.affiliate_id` AND `purchases.affiliate_id` ARE NOW ELIGIBLE FOR A REFERENCE THEY DO NOT CARRY.** Section 8. Not a defect today, and the next session to open either table's literal for another reason is the cheap place to close it.
