### 1. Why the three were ruled apart rather than together

**The dispatch offered a difference between the two certificate endpoints as evidence and that difference does not exist.** It said `GET /certificates/:code/image.png` cites `M11` by section while `GET /certificates` is *"marked NEW by its own author with no citation at all"*. `ports.ts:50` reads:

> `M11` section 4 names `GET /certificates` NEW, "the trader's own list, including `deferred` entries with their reason", and that row is `M11`'s and is in no `API_CONTRACT` section today.

**The clause that marks it NEW is the citation, and it quotes `M11` verbatim in the same sentence.** So both certificate sites rest on the same source at the same strength, and they get the same answer for that reason rather than by being adjacent.

**The affiliate endpoint is a different shape and the difference is not that the contract defines the `POST`.** It is that `M08` section 4 **enumerates**. Its own sentence is *"M8 owns API_CONTRACT section 7's affiliate endpoints and adds one"*, over a table of four, and the one it adds is `POST /affiliate/creatives`. An enumeration that names four and adds one is a closed list, and a read of the collection is not on it. **`M11` section 4 also enumerates, and the two certificate rows ARE on its list.** That is the whole ruling: the same test, run against the same kind of table, returns yes twice and no once.

### 2. The half of the finding the dispatch did not have

**`M11` section 4 marks SIX endpoints NEW and `API_CONTRACT` defined none of them.** `GET /verify/:code`, `GET /certificates/:code/image.png`, `GET /certificates`, `POST /admin/certificates/:id/revoke`, `PATCH /me/leaderboard`, `GET /public/leaderboard`. **So the contract was not missing two certificate rows; it was missing the module.** Two land here and four are named as owed, because landing all six would have meant typing a leaderboard whose metric set `OQ-M11-03` has not chosen and an operator revocation write that belongs to the admin surface.

**The one that hurts is `GET /verify/:code`.** Section 6's singular certificate row already ships `verify_url: string; // public verification page`, so this contract has been shipping a field whose value addresses a page it does not define. **That is precisely what [ADR-153](../decisions/ADR-153.md) repaired for `GET /auth/otp`, one week and one section apart**, and it is not repaired here because the verify endpoint is `AS-M11-04`'s oracle and needs a session that can spend its whole attention on what a public lookup over Merit's own payout book may answer.
