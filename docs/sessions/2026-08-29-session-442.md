### 2. The two closures, because one of them is not a `CHECK`

`firm_parameters_vocabulary_is_closed` admits exactly one member and a second is an ADR plus a
superseding migration, on `operators.role`'s precedent. **That closes what may be NAMED and says
nothing about what may be STORED**, and the settings bag does not usually arrive as a fifth enum
member. It arrives as a `text_value` column beside the integer one, added by a session that needed
somewhere to put a string, passing every existing constraint untouched. **So the table declares ONE
value column and its type is part of the vocabulary**, and the column set is asserted by name in
both the DDL and the Drizzle transcription.
