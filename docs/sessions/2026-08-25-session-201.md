### 2. The firm-hop trap was present here, and it wears the most plausible dress it has yet worn

`plan_size_unlocks` carries **`plan_version_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES plan_versions(id) ON DELETE RESTRICT`**. That is a `NOT NULL` single-valued foreign key to a registered table, which is character for character the shape of the `hop` [`daily_marks`](../../packages/db/src/scope.ts) and `rule_states` make to `accounts`.

**It is not a hop, and the reason is a sentence about the domain rather than about the type system.** `plan_versions` is `firm`: the published product catalogue, sold identically to everybody. **A ladder's rungs are firm and a trader's position on the ladder is owned.** A row recording that THIS identity may now buy THAT size points at the rung definition because the rung is what the entitlement is denominated in, not because the rung owns the row.

**`DerivedRule.via` is typed `TableKey`, which includes every firm key**, so the wrong rule compiles everywhere and throws the first time anybody reads the table. Seeded, and watched:

```
$ npx tsc --noEmit -p packages/db/tsconfig.json ; echo exit=$?
exit=0

$ npx vitest run packages/db/test/scoped-db.test.ts
FAIL  every rule resolves against the schema >
      every derivation chain ends at an identity, so none of them ends at a firm table
AssertionError: planSizeUnlocks derives through planSizeUnlocks -> planVersions, and
  planVersions is firm, so a scoped read of it constructs no predicate and throws:
  expected 'firm' to be 'derived'
Tests  1 failed | 76 passed (77)
```

**The bounded assertion is what caught it and it was left to do that rather than worked around**, which is the dispatch's instruction taken literally. This is [session 193](2026-08-25-session-193.md)'s `notification_kinds` finding recurring on a different module, and it is worth recording that the two look nothing alike from the outside: there the firm parent was an enum-like catalogue keyed by a text `kind`, here it is a versioned product row keyed by a uuid. **What they share is that the child is a record of PROGRESS and the parent is a DEFINITION**, which is the shape M18 is made of.

### 3. `certificates`' finding, taken as it stands, in its third and fourth places

The dispatch names `certificates` as already registered `owned` on `identity_id` with a recorded finding that the row reaches an identity two ways and no constraint ties them. **Two of this slice's three tables have exactly that shape**, and the landed rule was taken rather than re-argued.

| Table | Direct column | The second path | What ties them |
|---|---|---|---|
| `graduation_benefits` | `identity_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES identities(id)` | `account_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES accounts(id)`, and `accounts` is `owned` on `identity_id` | **Nothing.** No CHECK, no trigger, no composite FK |
| `plan_size_unlocks` | `identity_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES identities(id)` | `earned_account_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES accounts(id)` | **Nothing** |

**The direct column is the rule in both**, for [`certificates`](../../packages/db/src/scope.ts)' own stated reason: a derived rule would make the table's tenancy depend on a join rather than on a column the database itself declares against `identities(id)`. **The second column answers a different question in each case** -- `account_id` says which account the benefit accrued on, `earned_account_id` says which ladder completed and is what a dispute is argued from -- and neither says who holds the row.

**This is now the finding's third and fourth instances**, after [session 193](2026-08-25-session-193.md)'s `contact_channels.superseded_by` and [session 194](2026-08-25-session-194.md)'s `phone_change_requests.old_phone_id`. **Four instances across four modules is a schema-wide shape rather than four coincidences**, and it is named here as such without being ruled, because a transcription rules nothing.

**Seeded the other way and caught.** `graduation_benefits` scoped by `account_id` instead of `identity_id` leaves `tsc` at exit 0 and fails the DDL check with the column named and its DDL quoted:

```
AssertionError: graduation_benefits.account_id must reference identities(id), and its
  DDL is: account_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES accounts(id) ON DELETE RESTRICT
```

**That assertion is narrower than the finding it happens to sit next to.** It refuses an `owned` column that is not declared against `identities(id)`; it says nothing about whether the two paths agree. **Nothing in this package compares them and nothing written here implies otherwise.**

### 5. What the transcription says and what it deliberately does not

Three CHECK constraints carry this module's actual discipline and **none of them is a column, so none is transcribed and none is compared by anything in `packages/db`**:

- `graduation_benefits_not_both_conferred_and_withheld` -- a benefit is conferred, or withheld with a reason, never both (`INV-M18-10`)
- `graduation_invitations_one_response` and `graduation_invitations_expiry_after_issue`
- `plan_size_unlocks_revocation_is_explained` -- a revocation without a reason is a disappearance

Each is named in the comment beside its table so a later reader does not mistake a faithful column transcription for an enforcement. **`accrued_cents` and `unlocked_size_cents` are `bigint` in integer cents, never `numeric` and never a float**, on the money rule. `basis text NOT NULL` beside `accrued_cents` is `INV-M18-06`'s whole mechanism -- a number with no stated derivation is read as a promise -- and it is transcribed as `NOT NULL` for that reason.

### 6. The merge, and the collision that produced two correct answers reading the same number

**Disjointness was measured before the merge and not discovered during it**, by set difference over the keys of `TABLES` on each side:

```

only on mine:  graduationBenefits  graduationInvitations  planSizeUnlocks

```

**No table collided, so first-writer-wins decided nothing, no `why` was re-argued and no session's rows came out.**

**All five files conflicted textually**, which is [ADR-092](../decisions/ADR-092.md) section 4's fourth row happening as written: [`schema.ts`](../../packages/db/src/schema.ts), [`scope.ts`](../../packages/db/src/scope.ts), [`scoped-db.test.ts`](../../packages/db/test/scoped-db.test.ts), [STATE](../STATE.md) and [sessions/README](README.md). **The three `packages/db` files were rebuilt from `main`'s copy plus whole declarations lifted from this branch** rather than resolved hunk by hunk, because git aligns the conflict on the `createdAt` / `updatedAt` / `});` tail every `pgTable` shares and a keep-both there splices one table's fields into another table's object literal and yields `TS1005`. - **Landmines:**

  - **THE FIRM-HOP TRAP IS NOW A SHAPE RATHER THAN A LIST OF THREE TABLES, AND `plan_size_unlocks` IS ITS MOST PLAUSIBLE INSTANCE YET.** `plan_version_id` is a `NOT NULL` uuid FK to a registered table and is indistinguishable from a legitimate hop in a column list; only the knowledge that `plan_versions` is the published catalogue decides it. **A session transcribing a PROGRESS table whose parent is a DEFINITION should assume it is looking at this trap.** The bounded assertion catches it, `tsc` does not, and [`scoped-db.test.ts`](../../packages/db/test/scoped-db.test.ts) is the only thing standing between a plausible rule and a throw at the first read.

  - **`certificates`' TWO-PATHS FINDING HAS FOUR INSTANCES OVER FOUR MODULES AND NO RULING.** `graduation_benefits`, `plan_size_unlocks`, `contact_channels` and `phone_change_requests` all reach an identity twice with nothing tying the two, and every one of them is registered on the direct column by precedent rather than by an entry. **The failure it stands in front of is not hypothetical**: the day a row's `identity_id` and its account's `identity_id` differ -- after a merge that repointed one and not the other, or after a write that took the account's owner from a stale read -- a scoped read returns the row to one of the two people and nothing raises. **Nothing in `packages/db` compares them and this session added two more places where that is true.**
