- **Done:** **[ADR-065](../decisions/ADR-065.md) rules the shape of [ALLOCATION](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md) and the ruling is applied.** Twenty duplicated keys merged to one row each, four blank lines removed from the letter table, the `u` double-claim ruled, and `docs/decisions/ALLOCATION.md` **removed entirely from `CI06U_REGISTER`** with its eighteen keys. **22 of 22 gates pass**, `falsify.mjs` green with **30 scope cases**, `pnpm vitest run` **939 pass, 42 skipped, 62 files**. `CI-06f`, `CI-06h` and `CI-06p` all read these tables and all three are green.
- **Next:** **The slug migration ADR-065 section 5 rules for, and it has a hard precondition rather than a priority.** There are **zero allocatable letters**, so no twenty-fourth gate can be written until it lands. `CI-06v` (no blank line in a table) and `CI-06w` (no two gates sharing a runner id) are already reserved and are the executable forms of two of this ADR's rulings.
- **Blockers:** ADR-065 is `proposed` and unsigned. It retires the naming scheme twenty-three gates use and leaves a specified gate deliberately unlettered, so it is E2's rather than a session's.
- **Landmines:**

  - **THE ALPHABET IS NOT NEARLY GONE, IT IS GONE, AND THE MARGIN IS NEGATIVE RATHER THAN ONE.** Measured rather than inherited: `a` through `w` are claimed, [`nextFreeLetter()`](../../scripts/corpus/falsify.mjs) scans `a` to `x` only, and **the `CI-06p` seed writes a row claiming two past the first free letter**, so one seed consumes `free`, `free + 1` and `free + 2`. With `x` free that is `x`, `y`, `z`. **`x` is the harness's headroom, not an allocation**, and the next gate to claim a letter breaks the falsification of the gate that guards this table. The brief said one letter remained; the seed's arithmetic says none does.

  - **A BLANK LINE INSIDE A TABLE IS INVISIBLE TO A READER AND FATAL TO EVERY GATE.** `markdownTables()` builds a table from a maximal run of consecutive pipe lines and keeps the run only if it carries a delimiter row. Four blanks made the letter table **five runs**, only the first with a delimiter, and **nine rows sat outside `CI-06f`, `CI-06h`, `CI-06p` and `CI-06u` at once** while rendering perfectly. Two duplicate claims hid in them, one of them **two different gates claiming one letter**.

  - **THE REPAIR AND THE BLANK LINES HAD TO BE ONE COMMIT AND THE BRIEF WAS RIGHT THAT THIS IS THE TRAP.** Removing the blanks merges the fragments, which makes `r` and `u` duplicate keys that `CI-06u` can suddenly see. The register **shrinks only**, so they could not be added to it. Split across two commits, this branch fails its own gate.

  - **`046` IS A SECOND REAL COLLISION AND NOTHING IN THE TREE NAMED IT AS ONE.** The register filed it with the fifteen reservation-plus-merge pairs. Its two rows carry **different subjects**: `OQ-P2-01`'s `CalendarSlice` ruling and `OQ-M10-06`'s reversible contact addresses. The first was renumbered to [ADR-049](../decisions/ADR-049.md), whose row already says "written as `046` and moved here". The brief named `0034` as the one historical collision among the eighteen and warned to read each row before sweeping it; **`046` is why that instruction was worth following.**

  - **`0034` KEEPS ITS HISTORY WITHOUT KEEPING ITS SECOND ROW, WHICH THE BRIEF FEARED WAS IMPOSSIBLE.** The concern was that deleting a row erases what the `### 0034 was claimed twice` heading exists to explain. It does not, because the history lives in **two other places that both stay**: that heading, and the `0035` row where [ADR-047](../decisions/ADR-047.md)'s subject actually landed. The surviving row names the file on disk and points at both.

  - **THE REGISTER'S STATED REASON FOR `r`'s ABSENCE WAS FALSE IN BOTH HALVES.** It read "the two rows are byte-identical but for one link, so the dedupe reached it". The rows differed by **a whole parenthetical clause**, and **no dedupe reached them**: they sat in different unparsed fragments and the gate never compared them. Corrected in place rather than deleted, because a wrong reason in a register is how the next reader concludes there is nothing to look at.

  - **THE GATE REPORTED ITS OWN REPAIR, WHICH IS THE STALE-REGISTER CHECK EARNING ITS KEEP.** After the merge and before the register entry was removed, `CI-06u` failed with eighteen findings, one per key, each saying the register "claims X is a known duplicate and it is not one on this ref". That is the ratchet refusing to hold a stale exemption, and it is how the completeness of the repair was confirmed rather than counted by hand.
