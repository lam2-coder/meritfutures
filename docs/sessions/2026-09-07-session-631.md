### 1. The objective

Confirm at source that [`digests/ports.ts`](../../apps/worker/src/digests/ports.ts) and [`recon/ports.ts`](../../apps/worker/src/recon/ports.ts) state something about themselves their own imports contradict, **re-deriving it rather than trusting [ADR-432](../decisions/ADR-432.md)'s account**; repair both headers keeping the superseded sentence beside its correction on `RI-14`'s idiom and `breaker/ports.ts`'s worked example; **derive** why no gate caught it; and **price that hole without closing it**.

The `git log -S` for each import identifies the commit and the ADR that added it.
