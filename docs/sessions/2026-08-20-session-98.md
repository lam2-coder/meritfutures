- **Next:** [`ADR-070`](../decisions/ALLOCATION.md) is the ruling this audit is the input to, and it now has **four findings to dispose of on top of the four gaps**. `0044` stays reserved and unwritten.
- **Blockers:** none. The audit is complete against its own scope.
- **Landmines:**

  - **THE REFERRAL'S THREE-STATE TAXONOMY IS MISSING A FOURTH STATE, AND THE MISSING ONE HOLDS 11 OF THE 47.** [FOLD-05:34](../plans/FOLD-05-plan-config-and-designer.md) offers first-class, versioned-unconstrained, and absent. **The tree also contains MATERIALIZED**: declared in `plan_versions.rules` as a ratio or a flag, projected at publish onto a **constrained column**, with the two asserted to agree by a `CV-nn`. [`0004_catalog.sql:183`](../../packages/db/migrations/0004_catalog.sql) states the mechanism and the reason in its own comment, that *"a CHECK constraint cannot read another table"* and a trigger *"is a weaker control: it can be disabled, and it fires per row rather than per constraint"*. **Filing these under "unconstrained" would report the corpus's strongest control as its weakest.**

  - **THE BLOB/COLUMN SPLIT IS DELIBERATE AND THE AUDIT NEARLY REPORTED IT AS A DEFECT.** [`types.ts:409`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts) quotes M01 section 2.4: *"The engine reads two things and never anything else: `plan_versions.rules` for **STRUCTURE** and `plan_version_sizes` for **EVERY CENTS VALUE**. No percentage is ever applied to a money value at runtime."* **So the 29 unconstrained leaves are types, flags, counts and ratios, which is the half of the split that is supposed to be there.** A reader who counts blob keys and calls the total a gap has counted a design and reported it as damage.

  - **`payout_cap_schedule_cents` IS A COLUMN IN THE EXEMPLARY TABLE WITH NO CONSTRAINT OF ANY KIND.** The brief said `plan_version_sizes` is *"every one a bigint with a CHECK"* and named seven columns. **The table has twelve configurable columns and ten carry a column-level `CHECK`**, derived by parsing the DDL rather than reading it. `floor_lock_enabled` is covered by the table-level [`plan_version_sizes_floor_lock_complete`](../../packages/db/migrations/0004_catalog.sql); **`payout_cap_schedule_cents` is covered by nothing**, and a grep for it across `packages/db/migrations/` returns exactly one line, its declaration. **The brief's seven are all correct. The table is not seven columns.**

  - **TWO COLUMNS EACH RECEIVE FROM TWO PHASES, AND ONE PHASE'S LOCK HAS NO COLUMN AT ALL.** [`types.ts:598`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts) says it on the column: *"CV-02, materialized. **ONE COLUMN, and `rules` declares a drawdown PER PHASE**"*, and the same for `daily_loss_limit_cents`. Running the other way, `PublishedDrawdown` gives **both** phases a `lock` and the size row carries exactly one `floor_lock_*` trio, attached to `phase_funded`. **So `phase_eval.drawdown.lock` is three leaves with no column**, and which phase's drawdown reaches `drawdown_cents` is a publish-path convention rather than a schema fact.

  - **THE CONTRACT-LIMIT GREP DOES NOT RETURN ZERO, AND THE FINDING SURVIVES ANYWAY.** The brief said the five terms return **ZERO** across `docs/` and `packages/`. They return **15**: `contract limit` 11, and the four `snake_case` terms once each. **Every hit is a description of the absence** — FOLD-05's own sentence declaring them absent, the registries and the GS section it spawned. **This is the third session in a row where a briefed "grep returns nothing" was literally false and substantively right**, and the pattern is now worth naming: a claim of absence, once written down, becomes its own grep hit. The audit records the count and the disposition rather than repeating the zero.

  - **TWO PARAMETERS HAVE NEITHER A DATABASE CONSTRAINT NOR AN APPLICATION CHECK, BY DESIGN.** `limits.max_accounts_per_entity` and `kyc.triggers` are in the stored jsonb and are deliberately **not** in the engine type: [`types.ts:555`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts) says *"M01 section 1.2 puts entitlement and KYC outside this module, and a type that carried them would invite a rule to read them. What `validatePlan` may not see, it may not validate, and neither key has a `CV-nn`."* **"Validated in application code" is true of 27 of the 29 and not of these two**, and `ADR-070` should see that stated rather than inferred.

  - **`min_settlement_lag_trading_days` EXISTS BECAUSE TWO APPROVED DOCUMENTS DISAGREE, AND THE DISAGREEMENT IS STILL OPEN.** [`types.ts:521`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts): *"DATA_MODEL SECTION 11's EXAMPLE DOES NOT CARRY THIS KEY AND M01 SECTION 2.4 REQUIRES IT TO EXIST, which is a disagreement between two approved documents rather than a shape decision this file is making."* It is not one of the four gaps and it is a live inconsistency inside the parameter set the audit enumerates.

  - **NOTHING IS HARDCODED, AND THE CLAIM WAS TESTED RATHER THAN REPEATED.** [`0004_catalog.sql:78`](../../packages/db/migrations/0004_catalog.sql) asserts *"There is no plan parameter anywhere in application code."* A grep for plan-parameter literals across `packages/*/src/**` and `apps/` returns **one hit and it is a comment** ([`validate.ts:214`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/plan/validate.ts)). **All 19 `CV-nn` and all 5 `PW-nn` are implemented**, confirmed by extracting the identifiers from the source rather than from the plan.
