### 1. What forced a ruling rather than a transcription

**[ADR-153](../decisions/ADR-153.md)'s route was already the rule**: [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) makes an edit to a document the freeze covers an entry rather than a commit, regardless of size, and that entry paid the ceremony this morning for one wrong verb. **What is different here is that the ceremony is not the reason.**

[API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) specifies requests and responses, and section 1's conventions each presume a client that asked: a base path, a content type, an `Idempotency-Key`, a cursor, and a rate limit stated per endpoint. [`registry.ts`](../../apps/api/src/registry.ts) closes the verb list at the five the contract uses and `compose` keys on `METHOD /path`. **[ADR-020](../decisions/ADR-020.md)'s tier 2 is server-initiated delivery of a value nobody requested, and a socket upgrade is none of those five verbs.**

So the live tier does not fit inside what the contract already says, and the choice was between an entry that says what it DOES fit inside and three later slices each inventing an answer where nobody can cite it.

### 3. The two things the full suite caught that reading could not

**1. The approval clause was FALSE in its first form.** It read *"every `METHOD /path` under section 8 or section 9 begins with one of `OPERATOR_PREFIXES`"*, and `GET /health` is section 9's own row, marked *"Public, returns `{ status: \"ok\" }` and nothing else"*, which [`surface.ts`](../../apps/api/src/surface.ts) registers on BOTH surfaces deliberately because a constant response *"discloses nothing the origin's own reachability does not already disclose"*. **A clause naming a prefix cannot see a deliberate exception**, and the application's own classifier already carries it as a third path class. The clause now imports the classifier and the exception rather than restating either.

**2. A draft added `### GET /admin/liability/live` and the suite refused it.** [`admin-reads.test.ts`](../../apps/api/test/admin-reads.test.ts) parses every `### GET /admin` line out of the contract and asserts the unserved set is exactly `['/admin/cusum', '/admin/loss-ratios']`. **[P6 section 9](../plans/P6-live-tier.md)'s collision table does not row that file, and neither `P6-d`'s fence nor `P6-j`'s contains it**, so the fence-respecting options were to report it red or to look again at the draft.

**Looking again is what was owed, because the draft was inconsistent with its own clause 1.** Fixing an operator PATH is the same transport ruling the entry refuses to take for the trader, and taking it for one surface while refusing it for the other was a defect the test found rather than a fence the test blocked. Clause 6 now applies clause 1 to both surfaces; clause 2 still binds the path's shape wherever `P6-j` and `P6-g` put it.
