### The one instruction I did not follow, and the ruling is why

**The brief said "four of six are SHOULD, per FOLD-03 section 4". That sentence is not carried, and [ADR-066](../decisions/ADR-066.md) is the reason.**

FOLD-03 section 4 does summarise the split that way. **The ruling that came after it records that the sentence does not survive a component-level count at either grain, and says the component table is what `F5` transcribes and the sentence is not.** So the brief was repeating a summary its own authority had already retired. The count that ships is **six MUST and six SHOULD**, and section 2.1 states in the document itself that the sentence was dropped and why, because **a sizing sentence is exactly the thing a roadmap reader quotes** and a retired one left lying in a plan gets re-quoted forever.

**The record already carries this shape twice.** [Session 76](2026-08-20-session-76.md) refuted five claims in the record against the tree, and [session 86](2026-08-20-session-86.md) is the session that found four of these six referred items mis-scoped. The habit that catches it is cheap and is the same habit each time: **read the authority before transcribing the summary of it.**

### What the check cost, which is the line worth keeping

**Four of the six referred items were mis-scoped against our own tree**, and section 6.2 carries all four with `ADR-066`'s `file:line` for each. The one that matters:

**The Tier-1 economic calendar was not a design choice, it was an outstanding commitment nobody had noticed was unsatisfied.** `DEP-M7-06` has declared it since M07 was written and `FM-M7-08` has required its staleness alarm. No table satisfies either, so `D-04`'s news-window clustering **has been unimplementable for the whole life of the corpus**. No gate saw it, because a declared dependency with no satisfying object is invisible to every check this repository runs.

**And the corpus had already called it table stakes.** PROP_TECH_LANDSCAPE section 3's Wave 1 MUST list names the news-window detector among the tier-1 detectors. **The capability was on a MUST list from the beginning and the data it reads was never sized.** That cross-check is now written into section 6.2, because the general shape is the reusable part: a MUST can sit on a list for a year while its dependency has no object.

### The landmine

**A declared dependency with no satisfying object is invisible to every gate here.** `CI-06` proves citations resolve, spans balance, numbers are allocated and table keys are unique. **Nothing proves a `DEP-` row points at something that exists.** This defect was found by a vendor demo, which is not a control. If the same class exists elsewhere in the corpus, the same accident is what will find it.
