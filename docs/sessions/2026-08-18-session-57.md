  **THE PUBLISH HALF IS NOT NEW COVERAGE AND CLAIMING IT WOULD BE THE EASY VERSION OF THIS SESSION.**

  **THE COUNTERFACTUAL'S CAUSE IS COVERED AND ITS CONSEQUENCE WAS NOT.** Reintroducing a post-payout
  floor recompute in [`applySettlement`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/payout/settle.ts) --
  `floorCents = balance - approved - drawdown`, the `reset_to_balance_minus_dd` `ADR-014` retired --
  turns `RE-U-019`, `RE-U-048` and both of `GS-083`'s counterfactual cases red. `RE-U-019` and
  `RE-U-048` assert **the floor field did not move**. `GS-083` asserts **what follows from its not
  moving**, which is that the account breaches, and `INV-21` is a statement about the consequence.
  Nothing in the tree had ever run the unsafe config; `GS-065` and `GS-081` both pin `breached: false`
  on the safe one.

  **THE MINIMAL ARITHMETIC WOULD HAVE COLLIDED WITH `ADR-052` SECTION 2, AND THE DRIFT SESSION 56
  WARNED ABOUT WAS REACHABLE WITHOUT COPYING ANYTHING.** The obvious counterexample is `cap = drawdown
  + 1` on `CORE-50K`, which needs a closing high of **5,350,001** to make one full cap withdrawable.
  That is the exact figure [ADR-052](../decisions/ADR-052.md) section 2 lands on, by a different route
  on a different plan, because both are `size + buffer + cap` and the two plans share a buffer. A
  reader comparing the two files would have read one as a transcription of the other, which is what
  [session 56](2026-08-18-session-56.md) said must not happen: ADR-052's counterexample is **`CV-11`'s
  half** (lock ENABLED, a LOCKED floor), and `GS-083` is `CV-17`'s (lock DISABLED, a TRAILING floor).
  **A cap of 300,000c was chosen instead**, 600bp against A.1's 500bp drawdown, and it is better on its
  own terms: the shortfall is then `cap - drawdown` exactly, which states `CV-17`'s inequality as
  money, and no figure in the block appears in ADR-052. **The near-collision is the finding.** The
  guard against filing one half of `INV-21` under the other is not "do not copy"; it is deriving the
  numbers from `CV-17`'s own row and then checking what they landed on.

  **`cap_bp` HAD TO MOVE WITH `cap_cents` AND NOTHING WOULD HAVE CAUGHT IT IF IT HAD NOT.**
  `MZ-cap-ordinals` compares **ordinals**, not amounts, so a rules block saying 300bp beside a size row
  saying 300,000c publishes with no finding at all. Leaving A.1's `cap_bp: 300` behind would have made
  the file claim "this config is Appendix A.1 apart from two changes" while carrying a third that its
  own `toEqual(['CV-17'])` could not see. Set to 600bp, because 600bp of 5,000,000c **is** 300,000c.
  The same applies to the lock: disabled on the jsonb and on the size row together, or `MZ-lock-flag`
  fires and the block measures a materialization finding instead of `CV-17`.

  **M01's REASON THAT NO V1 PLAN REACHES `CV-17` IS TRUE AND IS NOT THE WHOLE REASON.** The row says
  "all three enable the lock, and `CV-11` covers that case", and the precondition is
