## 2026-08-18 - Session 57: ADR-056's central claim EXECUTED, and it does not reproduce (money path, test only)

**Written as a stub before any work, so the session was on the record before it had a result, and
completed in place.** The fence was [`packages/rules-engine/test/`](../../packages/rules-engine/test/)
and this file.

- **Objective:** [ADR-056](../decisions/ADR-056.md) is `proposed` and asks not to be signed until the
  claim it turns on is **executed rather than argued**: "the second-funded-day breach in the table
  above is a DERIVATION FROM THE `DO` ORDERING, NOT AN EXECUTION ... The signature should follow a
  run, not this paragraph." Run it.

- **Done: THE BREACH DOES NOT REPRODUCE.** It reproduces on no v1 plan, on no funded day, and not for
  the reason the ADR gives. Two files, both green, both watched failing:
  [`adr-056-carried-lock.test.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/test/adr-056-carried-lock.test.ts) (6

  [`adr-056-carried-lock.property.test.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/test/adr-056-carried-lock.property.test.ts)
  (10 tests, the sweep). **783 passing from 767, no other test moved**, 17 of 17 gates, 7 of 7
  invariants, 19 scope cases. **No engine file was edited: `packages/rules-engine/src` is
  byte-identical to `origin/main` on this branch.**

### The result, in the order a reader needs it

**EVERY PREMISE ADR-056 ARGUES FROM IS TRUE, AND ITS MECHANISM IS NOT.**

| The ADR's claim | Executed |
|---|---|
| Every v1 eval pass is also a lock day, so every funded account arrives locked | **TRUE.** The pass day emits `rule.floor_locked`, `phase.passed`, `day.closed`, in that order, on both plans that have an eval phase |
| `floor_lock_floor_at_cents` is `size_cents + 10,000c` and `R-31` writes exactly `size_cents` | **TRUE** on all three v1 plans, resolved from the published rows |
| So a funded account whose floor was re-assigned to the locked value stands 10,000c under water | **TRUE**, and it is the ADR's whole point |
| **`DO-7` "assigns `floor = floor_lock_floor_at_cents` on every day the account is locked"** | **FALSE.** This is the sentence the ruling's table rests on |
| Therefore every funded account breaches on its second funded day, on all three v1 plans | **DOES NOT REPRODUCE** |

**`advanceFloor` ASSIGNS THE LOCKED FLOOR ONCE, ON THE DAY THE LOCK ENGAGES, AND CARRIES IT
AFTERWARDS.** `R-15`'s block is guarded by `!floorLocked` ([`floor.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/floor.ts)),
so on an already-locked day the assignment is not reached and `floorCents` is `input.priorFloorCents`
untouched. **The file's own header states the expression form** (`floor(d) = floorLocked ?
floor_lock_floor_at_cents : max(...)`) **and the implementation is stateful**, and the two agree on
every day of every ordinary account, because nothing between the lock day and today ever moves the
floor. **They part company at exactly one place: a state whose floor was moved by something other
than the lock while `floorLocked` stayed true.** That state exists nowhere in the corpus except the
counterfactual this session built.

**AND THE SIGN IS REVERSED, WHICH IS THE MEASUREMENT WORTH READING TWICE.** On the walked stream, with
a funded day two whose low is 4,800,000c:

| | Carried lock (the literal `INV-07`) | Shipped (what the engine does) |
|---|---|---|
| Funded day 1 floor | 4,750,000c, frozen | 4,850,000c, trailed (`R-13`) |
| Funded day 2, low 4,800,000c | **survives**, 4,800,000 >= 4,750,000 | **BREACHES**, 4,800,000 < 4,850,000, shortfall 50,000c |

**The carried lock is STRICTLY MORE PERMISSIVE than the engine it was supposed to be more dangerous
than**, because its floor is frozen at the lowest value the funded phase ever has. The property sweep
puts it beyond one stream: over **3,442 folded funded days across 2,000 generated streams**, with
**1,790 shipped breaches and 1,485 carried breaches**, the carried lock breached on **zero** days the
shipped fold survived. The reason is structural rather than statistical: both accounts leave `R-31`
with the same floor, the carried one is frozen there by the two `!floorLocked` guards, and the
shipped one is non-decreasing from there by `R-13` and `R-14`, so `R-18`'s comparator is at or below
the shipped one on every day.

**WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENS UNDER THE CARRIED LOCK IS ADR-056's OTHER ARGUMENT, AND THAT ONE REPRODUCES
EXACTLY.** The funded floor freezes at `size_cents` minus the funded drawdown and never moves again:
it never trails a cent of the profit, and the lock can never re-engage, because `R-15`'s trigger is
guarded by the same flag. **The sweep watched the shipped account re-lock 232 times and the carried
account not once.** The ADR's words for it are still the right ones: "That is not a constrained
funded floor. It is the funded floor deleted." **The ruling's conclusion survives its own table.**

### Three narrowings the run produced, each smaller than the one above it

**"ALL THREE V1 PLANS" IS TWO.** DIRECT-50K carries `phase_eval.enabled: false` (Appendix A.3) and a
null `profit_target_cents`, so `resolvePlan` gives it no eval rules, `initialState` opens it
**funded**, and **`R-31` never runs on it at all**. The consequence for Direct is not that the breach
fails to reproduce; it is that the transition `INV-07` is being amended against does not exist there.
A Direct state claiming the eval phase is refused (`eval_phase_without_eval_rules`), which is the
executable form of it and is pinned.

**EVEN GRANTING THE MECHANISM, "EVERY FUNDED ACCOUNT ON ITS SECOND FUNDED DAY" IS CONDITIONAL.**
Measured under the mutant, on CORE-50K: a funded day one that closes **flat** or **+9,999c** breaches
on day two; **+10,001c does not**, because the balance has cleared the locked floor and the day's low
can stay above it. The ADR's table assumes the balance is still `size_cents` on day two, which is
true of the account that made no money on its first funded day and of no other. **This narrowing is
moot while the mechanism is false**, and it is recorded because a re-derivation of the ADR on other
grounds would inherit it.

**NEITHER THE FOLD NOR THE SCHEMA ANNOUNCES THE CARRIED LOCK.** `R-13`'s guard freezes `hwb` at
`size_cents`, so every profitable funded day leaves the high-water balance **below the account's own
balance**, on 2,000 of the swept days. [`0037`](../../packages/db/migrations/0037_supersede_rule_states_high_water_bounds_balance.sql)
replaced `0015`'s unconditional bound with `floor_locked OR high_water_balance_cents >=
balance_cents`, so **a locked row is EXEMPT and the database stores it without complaint**. The
literal reading produces no breach, no assertion, no refusal and no rejected row. It fails silently
and permanently, in the trader's favour and against the house.

### Landmines

- **THE ADR's MECHANISM CLAIM IS A CORRECT READING OF THE FILE'S HEADER AND A WRONG READING OF ITS
  CODE, WHICH IS WHY IT SURVIVED A SESSION AND A REVIEW.** [`floor.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/floor.ts)
  opens with `floor(d) = floorLocked ? floor_lock_floor_at_cents : max(...)`, which is a **stateless
  expression over the day**, and the implementation is a **stateful carry**. Anyone deriving the
  counterfactual from the header gets ADR-056's answer; anyone running it gets this one. **The two
  forms are observationally identical on every account the corpus admits**, so no fixture, no property
  and no gate can tell them apart, and the disagreement is only visible in the one state the corpus
  has not decided is reachable. **This is the shape of defect the constitution's own caution is
  about**: not a capability failure, a failure to check a claim against the primary source, where the
  primary source here is the executable and not the comment above it.

- **THE COUNTERFACTUAL WAS CONSTRUCTED BY FLIPPING ONE FIELD ON THE FOLD's OWN OUTPUT, AND ANY OTHER
  CONSTRUCTION WOULD HAVE MEASURED A DIFFERENT ENGINE.** `R-31`'s effects list names phase, `balance`,
  `hwb`, `floor`, the counters and the consistency period start, and does not name `floorLocked`, so
  the literal reading is that the flag is carried and **every other field is exactly what the shipped
  fold computed**. `DO-7` set it true on the pass day and `DO-8` set it back to false in the same
  fold, so **the flip restores a value the engine itself produced and then overwrote**. Editing
  `progression.ts` to produce the state, which the brief forbade, would have folded a different engine
  and the result would not have been about the shipped one.

- **THE REMEDY WAS PROVED IN THE ONLY DIRECTION THAT MATTERS HERE, AND A GREEN TEST ASSERTING AN
  ABSENCE IS WORTH NOTHING UNTIL IT IS.** A test whose result is "the predicted thing did not happen"
  is indistinguishable from a test that asserts nothing, so **ADR-056's predicted mechanism was
  temporarily reintroduced into `advanceFloor`** (`if (floorLocked && lock.enabled) floorCents =
  lock.floorAtCents`) and both files were watched failing: the walk with `expected 5010000n to be
  4750000n` on funded day one, and the sweep shrinking to **the ADR's own scenario, a breach on the
  SECOND funded day at `floorOpen` 5,010,000c against a shipped fold surviving at 4,750,000c**. The
  mutant was reverted, `git diff --stat -- packages/rules-engine/src` is empty, and no commit was made
  while it was live.

- **THE SWEEP's SUPPORT IS MEASURED RATHER THAN ASSUMED, BECAUSE "BREACHES ON NO DAY THE SHIPPED FOLD
  SURVIVES" IS TRUE FOR FREE ON A SWEEP WHERE NOTHING EVER BREACHES.** Six reachability tests, each
  asserting a case is visited: funded days folded at all, the shipped account breaching, the carried
  account breaching, the two floors diverging, the shipped funded lock re-engaging, and the carried
  `hwb` sitting below its own balance. **The counts at the time of writing are 3,442 / 1,790 / 1,485 /
  1,127 / 232 / 2,000**, and each is asserted `> 0` rather than pinned, because pinning a generator's
  yield is how a property test becomes a change-detector.

- **NEITHER FILE PINS A RULING AND BOTH SAY SO IN THEIR HEADERS.** `ADR-056` is `proposed`; nothing
  asserted here says clearing the lock is correct. Every expectation is about a **counterfactual
  input** and constrains no ruling, which is what makes it a test rather than the fixture the ADR
  names. `GS-044` and `GS-242` declined to pin `floor_locked` across the pass for exactly this reason
  and that reasoning is still correct.

- **THE PROPERTY FILE CLAIMS NO `RE-P-nn` NUMBER, DELIBERATELY.** The `RE-P` series is M01 section 8's
  list of properties the engine **must have**; this is a measurement of a state the corpus has not
  decided is reachable. Giving it a number would put a counterfactual in the register of commitments.

- **A LONG CALENDAR SLICE HAD TO BE BUILT AND IT NAMES NO EXCHANGE.** `CME_WINDOW` holds five sessions,
  which leaves three funded days after a pass, and a sweep needs more. `LONG_WINDOW` follows
  `GAPPED_SLICE`'s precedent verbatim: `TR-01` forbids writing an exchange's calendar from
  recollection, so what the slice asserts is a property of the engine's arithmetic over whatever slice
  the caller supplied. **The funded fold reads only the ordering**: `R-37` needs a cadence anchor and
  there are no settlements, `R-32` needs `max_days` and no v1 plan configures one.
