### 1. The objective, and what it turned into

The row was dispatched at [ADR-407](../decisions/ADR-407.md) section 11 item 1, an "unruled" gap cause carried unchanged through four entries, with three branches: state the question, price the options, and rule it if it is a row's to rule.

**ALL THREE HAPPENED, AND THE RULING IS A NEGATIVE ONE.** There is no fourth `LiabilityGapCause` member, the question dissolves rather than clearing, and two larger questions were found behind it.

### 2. Why the inherited phrasing was the wrong question

*"What gap cause does an unwired producer get"* presupposes that an unwired producer produces a `gaps` entry. Unfolded, a partial deployment answers three questions and only the third is about a vocabulary: at what granularity the absence sits, what the method returns, and only then which cause.

**THE TREE HAS ALREADY ANSWERED THE FIRST TWO, FOUR TIMES.** The fourth instance is the one that decides it and it is inside this fence: `EligibleFoldRefusalCause` is a closed four-member refusal vocabulary in `eligible-next-7d.ts`, carrying `cause`, `awaiting` and `detail` in `LiabilityGap`'s exact idiom, and `UNWIRED_ELIGIBLE_FOLD_IO` THROWS rather than returning a fifth member of it. A file with every reason to mint that member did not.
