- **Landmines:**

  - **THE HANDOFF NAMED FOUR ROWS AND TWO ARE WRITABLE, AND THE FOURTH IS THE DELIVERABLE.** `GS-082` was listed as unblocked by the nested comparison. **It is not blocked by that at all.** Its registry row asserts the cadence gap "passes on every one of those days, **including the ones that fail**", which is a gate verdict on **three different days**. `EXPECTATION_KEYS` is `{end_state, events, pins, note}`: a sibling pins **one** `end_state`, and `events` is a list of type **strings** with no payloads, so the `engine_gates` a `day.closed` carries never reaches an expectation. A fixture can state day three's verdicts and nothing whatever about days one and two, and writing it on either end is a half-row wearing a whole row's id -- `GS-049`'s objection, and the same reason batch 10 held `GS-060` back. **The nested comparison was NECESSARY for `GS-082` and is not SUFFICIENT.** The desk's other three readings were verified against the README and stand exactly as written.

  - **THE NEWLY-REACHABLE PATH ALREADY HAD A BUG, AND IT WAS FOUND BY LOOKING RATHER THAN BY A FIXTURE TRIPPING OVER IT.** The loader's empty-expectation guard was **root-only**, so `engine_gates: {}` walks, finds no leaf, and reports agreement **against any engine at all**. The guard's own comment states the reasoning -- "an empty expectation is the one shape that passes against any engine" -- and that reasoning applies exactly one level deeper. **Nothing could reach it before**, because a nested expectation could never match by any value. **That is [`run.ts`](../../packages/golden-loader/src/run.ts)'s shape a second time**: it passed every settlement to every day and was unobservable while `L-11` forced `settlements: []`. The transferable form is worth stating on its own: **a rule that makes a path unreachable also makes its bugs unfalsifiable**, so the session that makes the path reachable owns finding them. `L-04` now refuses an empty object at any depth and names the path.

  - **THE SUCCESS CASE LED, AND THAT ORDERING IS NOT A FLOURISH.** `probe_payout_hold` records the lesson in DELTA_MANIFEST section 13: a guard that rejects everything passes every rejection test written against it. Before this change `engine_gates` could never match by any value, so a suite opening with divergence tests would have been **green against a comparator that still could not agree with a correct engine**. The first assertion written was that a legitimate nested match produces no diffs, and the loader's positive case -- a nested expectation that DOES pin a leaf still loads -- is what keeps the new refusal from being a guard that rejects everything.

  - **A DIFFER THAT REPORTS THE OBJECT IS NOT THE DELIVERABLE.** `engine_gates: expected {...}, engine produced {...}` hands a reader two twenty-five-field records and asks them to find the disagreement, which costs more than it saves. The walk reports `engine_gates.traded_days.skipped`, in the **fixture's** snake spelling rather than the engine's camel, because a reader chasing a diff is looking at their own YAML and needs to find the line by search. [`ENGINE_GATE_LEAVES`](../../apps/worker/src/batch/state-hash.ts) already carries dotted paths for this reason and the idiom is followed rather than re-invented.

  - **THE `bigint` COERCION HAD TO SURVIVE THE RECURSION AND IS ASSERTED AT DEPTH.** `compare.ts` already recorded that `Object.is(4770000n, 4770000)` is false and that the loader has two types that are never `Object.is`-equal. Losing that inside a nested object would have made every nested money assertion unmatchable, which is the exact defect this change exists to remove, one level down. Both the agreeing case and the fractional-expectation case are asserted through a nested path.

  - **BOTH FIXTURES PASSED ON THE FIRST RUN AND NEITHER WAS TRUSTED FOR IT.** A first-run pass against an engine nobody consulted is the good outcome and is **indistinguishable from a vacuous fixture** until it is falsified, which is batch 10's discipline. Seeded: `GS-060`'s `skipped` to `false` gave `engine_gates.traded_days.skipped: expected false, engine produced true`; `GS-053`'s win-day gate to passing gave `engine_gates.win_days.pass: expected true, engine produced false` beside `have: expected 5, engine produced 0`.

  - **WHAT THE TWO FIXTURES DELIBERATELY DO NOT PIN IS STATED IN THEIR SIBLINGS.** `GS-060` leaves consistency unpinned because four identical 1,000c days cannot concentrate a best day, so the farming attack never reaches that gate and pinning a passing verdict would suggest it did. `GS-053` records that **three other gates also refuse on its day** -- cadence, minimum amount, and consistency on a one-day period -- and does **not** claim the win-day gate is uniquely binding, because that claim needs several days' verdicts and is `GS-082`'s. **Neither pins `engine_eligible: true`**, which stays unpinnable while M01 lists `R-38` under Group F and declares its input on `ExternalGates`.
