- **Landmines:**
  - **The workbook's plans tab predates the M1 and batch 1 gates, and it is the most dangerous artifact now in the repository.** It still says "Rapid Daily", 5 win days, and settlement-anchored cadence. Anyone who ports a harness from it without reading the README's divergence table will model the pre-ADR-018 firm and produce a reserve number that looks authoritative and is wrong. **The corpus wins; the README table is the checklist.**
  - **The Risk Engine tab, by contrast, is current and is now load bearing.** It independently derives the conservatism ruling: mean monthly payouts are flat near $45.3K across every correlation level while CVaR99 nearly doubles from $84.8K at rho=0.05 to **$132.9K at rho=0.30**. **The tail is all correlation.** That is why M07's correlation detectors are a **reserve control**, not only an abuse control, and it is the strongest argument in the corpus for funding them.
  - **The selection math was never written down and three design decisions rest on it.** P(skilled | funded) is **6.8 percent**; roughly 93 percent of the funded book has zero or negative edge and will revert. The funded time-gates work *because* they let the 93 percent revert before cash leaves. **Pass rate is a price knob, not a quality filter**, and any copy implying otherwise is a marketing-versus-implementation gap. There is **no learning effect**, which is what makes rebuy revenue durable and is an uncomfortable fact to hold next to M17's offer design.
  - **`second_purchase_any` and `second_distinct_account_purchase` reach different populations and the difference is easy to lose.** Resets make a loyal repeat customer a "second purchaser" without ever giving them a second account. Choosing the broader trigger buys corpus coverage and simultaneously buys friction on Merit's best customers. GS-234 pins it.
  - **ADR-022's tier ordering is forced by data availability, not by ambition, and it will be tempting to skip ahead.** Weights tuned on no data are guesses wearing a number, and a behavioral-fingerprint corpus with three members is a false-positive engine. The v1 tier is deliberately only the facts.
  - **The verification vocabulary rule is testable and should be tested.** No trader-facing string may contain "fraud", "suspicious", "risk", "flagged", or "review". A lint over the string catalogue belongs in CI next to the Appendix F em-dash check, because ADR-022's soft-link queue makes the wrongly-flagged-but-legitimate population **larger**, not smaller.
  - **Merit now refuses a market norm explicitly** (payout-time fraud friction, Apex's screen-recording requirement). That refusal only holds if identity friction actually lands upstream of funding. If ADR-021's triggers get deferred under schedule pressure, the friction does not disappear; it reappears at payout, which is the one place the brand cannot afford it.
