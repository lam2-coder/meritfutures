### 2. Why `reverses_adjustment_id` is not the linkage

Three grounds, each sufficient on its own and each failing in a different direction.

| Ground | What refuses it |
|---|---|
| **The table** | It references `account_adjustments(id)`. The corrected entry is a `wallet_entries` row, `bigint` at [`0011:49`](../../packages/db/migrations/0011_wallet.sql), a decimal string on the wire by [ADR-158](../decisions/ADR-158.md) clause 3, named `kind: 'wallet_entry'` in `evidence_refs`, and resolved against **this identity's own wallet rows** at [`admin-wallet.ts:1650`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-wallet.ts) |
| **The direction** | [`0038:220-221`](../../packages/db/migrations/0038_account_adjustments.sql) is `(direction = 'debit') = (reverses_adjustment_id IS NOT NULL)`. **A credit cannot carry one**, and a credit is what repairs a trader who is short |
| **The subject** | [`0038:184`](../../packages/db/migrations/0038_account_adjustments.sql), *"THE ONLY DEBIT THAT EXISTS"*. It is Merit undoing its own adjustment. The entry being repaired may be a `payout` or a `refund_wallet_funded`, neither of which has an adjustment to reverse |

**And (a) fails on the same sources.** `ledger_entries` has `ledger_account_id` and **no identity
column** ([`0009:113-116`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql)), so *"does not belong to
this identity"* is not expressible against it. The grain was never the question.
