- **Landmines:**

  - **THE RULING IS THAT A NAMED EXCEPTION IS NOT THE THING "NO EXCEPTION" FORBIDS.** `INV-06`'s clause exists to prevent **unstated** exceptions, and a single named, cited, testable one is the opposite of what it guards against. The alternative, scoping `INV-06` per `(account, phase)`, was rejected because it **makes the invariant true by redefining its domain**: the floor still falls on the pass day, by at least the 260,000c between the locked floor and the funded one and by 400,000c on `GS-019`'s own day, and every future reader has to reconstruct why the invariant no longer speaks to it.   - **THE EXCEPTION IS PINNED, NOT EXCUSED, AND THAT IS WHAT MAKES THE ADR EXECUTABLE.** A property that merely **skipped** the pass day would accept any floor at all there, which is precisely the unstated exception the clause forbids. So on the one step the exception covers, `RE-P-01`'s assertion gets **stricter**: `>=` becomes `===`, against `size_cents - funded drawdown_cents`. `CI-02/engine-RE-P-01-exception` seeds a reset landing **one cent low** and is watched failing on it, and **no other check in the repository catches that mutant**: `DO-7`'s tripwire compares within one day's trail-then-lock and never spans the reset, deliberately.

A generator that knew where the exception landed would make the property a restatement of the generator.
