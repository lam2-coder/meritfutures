## 2026-08-20 - Session 109: WAVE-04 W2, payout arithmetic and the gate object (MONEY PATH)

- **Objective:** six golden fixtures, `GS-026`, `GS-027`, `GS-028`, `GS-029`, `GS-042` and `GS-080`, every expected end state computed by hand from M01's rule text (`TR-01`).
- **Done:** **one fixture pair of six.** [`GS-080`](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/GS-080-a-gate-configured-to-zero-renders-as-disabled-not-as-satisfied.yaml) is written, derives `direct`, and matches on the first run with no value adjusted to the engine. **The other five are not writable in this fixture format**, and the blocker is one fact re-derived from the tree rather than five separate ones. 24 of 24 gates pass; the golden project is 41 fixtures, 41 direct, 0 inverted.
- **Next:** the five need a **second fixture shape** whose expectation is a `PayoutEvaluation` rather than a `RuleState`. That is a ruling and a loader change, not a transcription, and it is the same blocker the publish-time validation group waits on.
- **Blockers:** none for this session. The five scenarios are blocked on the format, and the finding below is what a wiring session needs.

### The finding: WAVE-04 put six scenarios in tier 1 and five of them are tier 2

**[WAVE-04 section 2.2](../plans/WAVE-04-fixture-backlog-and-gate-inventory.md) lists all six as writable today, and its stated reason is that "`R-43` and `R-44` are declared and live in `payout/clamp.ts`".** That is true and it is not the question. **[ADR-048](../decisions/ADR-048.md) already establishes that an unimplemented rule is not a blocker at all**, so "the rule is declared" cannot be what makes a fixture writable; the plan's own section 2 says the backlog is bounded by `W2` and `W3`, the FORMAT and the open questions. The tier-1 justification answers `W1`, which was never the constraint on this row.

**What the format can express is decided by two lines in [`run.ts`](../../packages/golden-loader/src/run.ts).** The fold is `advanceDay`, and the diff is `diffEndState(state, expected.end_state)` where `state` is a `RuleState`. So an expectation may pin a `RuleState` field and nothing else.

**`approved_cents`, `clamp_reason`, `trader_cents` and `firm_cents` are not on `RuleState`.** They are on `PayoutEvaluation.clamp` ([`types.ts:1056`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts)), which `DayOutput` does not carry: `DayOutput` is `{ state, events, assertions }`. **And the input half is missing too.** M01 states `clampPayout(state, plan, requested)` in its own section 3.5 pseudocode, and `DayInput` has no `requested` field, so a payout request cannot be stated as a fixture input in either direction.

**The fixture directory had this right and the wave plan did not.** [`fixtures/README.md`](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/README.md) carries `GS-026 to GS-029, GS-042` on a held-back row reading "`evaluatePayout` and `clampPayout` are not the day fold, and the expectation sibling has no shape for a clamp result". **That row is correct as written and this session confirms it against the tree.**

**Writing them anyway would not have produced a thin fixture, it would have produced a permanently red suite.** `R-43` and `R-44` are both in `IMPLEMENTED_RULES`, so a fixture citing them derives **`direct`** and MUST MATCH. An expectation naming `approved_cents` gets `the engine result carries no "approvedCents"` from `compare.ts` on every run, forever. **That is a false red on a money path**, which is the one thing `ADR-048`'s polarity derivation exists to prevent, and it would be indistinguishable at a glance from the engine defect a golden failure is supposed to mean.

### Two blockers the fixture README still records that the tree has retired

Both were re-derived rather than inherited, which is why `GS-080` could be written at all.

- **`compare.ts` DIFFS NESTED EXPECTATIONS NOW.** The README holds `GS-060` and `GS-080` on "a nested expectation can never match" because the diff used `Object.is` on flat fields. `diffEndState` now walks the expectation and reports a **leaf path** in the fixture's own spelling, `engine_gates.traded_days.skipped`. It also coerces a `bigint` result against a JSON number, without which no money field could ever match.
- **POLARITY IS WIRED AND ENFORCED.** The README states that `run.ts` "still folds `evaluate`" and that "**every fixture here is inverted today**". The fold is `advanceDay`, the declaration check holds, and the stage's own re-derived report reads **41 direct, 0 inverted, enforced**. The 41 skips in the golden project are the retired `describe.runIf(!declaration.holds)` escape hatch, not the assertions.

**These are stale paragraphs in a file this session's fence forbids it to edit**, and they are recorded here rather than fixed. `fixtures/README.md` is shared with two concurrent sessions.

### `GS-080` pins `engine_eligible: true` and it is the first fixture that may

[ADR-060](../decisions/ADR-060.md) ruled `R-38` a context gate, and **its own section 3 says the ruling "DOES NOT BY ITSELF MAKE `engine_eligible: true` PINNABLE"**: the load-bearing edit was item 1 of its fold list, putting a closed enumeration into `INV-15`, because `AND(every engine gate)` named a set no document listed. **[M01 section 1.5](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) now carries it** -- the closed six `R-33`, `R-34`, `R-35`, `R-36`, `R-37`, `R-39`, derived in that document and explicitly not transcribed from `types.ts`. So the conjunction the fixture asserts comes out of a plan document, which is what `TR-01` asks and what ADR-060 warned a careless session would skip by copying the engine's own list one level up.

**The row needs all six gates to pass, and that is a design constraint rather than a flourish.** `GS-080` asserts that a DISABLED gate leaves `engineEligible` unaffected. On a stream where some other gate fails, the flag is `false` for a reason unrelated to `R-33` and the second half of the row is proved by nothing. The disabled gate has to sit inside a conjunction that is otherwise true, where a `pass: false` on `R-33` would be the single term that flipped it.

### Landmines

- **`GS-042` IS ONE THIRD REACHABLE AND WAS NOT WRITTEN, ON THIS DIRECTORY'S OWN PRECEDENT.** Its row (`EC-024`, B4 #13) makes three claims and only the `>=` boundary is a `RuleState` field; the exact tie and the one-cent request are both clamp results. [`GS-060`](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/GS-060-zero-risk-day-farming-buys-nothing-against-the-real-gates.yaml)'s header states the standard in its own words -- "**BOTH HALVES OR NEITHER**... a half-row wearing a whole row's id is worse than no row: a later reader sees `GS-060` present and concludes the disabled-gate claim is covered" -- and batch 10 declined exactly this trade. **The asymmetry worth flagging is that `GS-060`'s blocker was temporary and `GS-042`'s is structural**, so waiting costs more here, and the repair is probably to split the registry row rather than to relax the standard.
- **THE FIXTURE MATCHED ON THE FIRST RUN, WHICH IS EVIDENCE AND NOT A RELIEF.** Fifteen hand-computed values agreed with `advanceDay` with nothing edited. Under `TR-01` that is the outcome a golden file is for; it is recorded because the alternative -- a diff, on a money path -- is the outcome the brief pre-committed to reporting rather than smoothing away, and no such diff arose.
- **`best_day_share_bp` IS UNPINNED AND THE REASON GENERALIZES.** `R-29` states the verdict as an integer cross multiplication and adds "**no division exists anywhere in the engine**"; the share is a rendered ratio with no formula in M01. Pinning it would mean transcribing the implementation's arithmetic into a golden file. **[API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) also names it `max_bp` in both of its declarations against the engine's `maxDayShareBp`**, which is a live spelling divergence on a published shape and belongs to whoever owns that contract.
