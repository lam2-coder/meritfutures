### What the clause says, what the tree says, and how I know

**The evidence was [session 124](2026-08-21-session-124.md)'s and every number here was re-measured rather than inherited.** [ADR-076](../decisions/ADR-076.md) section 5 measured two transitions and sixteen sessions; session 124 widened it to six and 60; **this session ran three whole calendar years and got 782, and the answer does not move.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **The clause** | *"The 23 hour and 25 hour sessions each produce exactly one trading day"* |
| **The tree** | **782 of 782 sessions are 23 hours.** No 25. No 24. The transcribed `session_rule` is `open_ct 17:00`, `open_day_offset -1`, `close_ct 16:00`, which is 23 hours every day of the year |
| **Why no length can change** | Both transitions fall at **02:00 CT on a Sunday**, between Friday's 16:00 CT close and Sunday's 17:00 CT open. Measured for all six: the transition day carries no row at all |

### THE FINDING THIS SESSION ADDS, AND NO PRIOR ENTRY MEASURED IT

**If no session's length changes, the hour has to be somewhere.** It is the **gap between sessions**, and it is measurable:

| Gap | Count | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 hour | 625 | an ordinary weeknight, unchanged by DST |
| 49 hours | 150 | an ordinary weekend |
| **48 hours** | **3** | **the three spring-forward weekends**, and there are exactly three in coverage |
| **50 hours** | **3** | **the three fall-back weekends** |

**The registry did not say a wrong number about a session. It said a number about a session when the thing that moved was the gap between sessions.** That is why re-deriving the arithmetic would never have found it: the arithmetic was applied to the wrong subject. **The 48 and the 50 are deliberately kept OUT of both rows**, because an unasserted number in a frozen registry is the defect being corrected and swapping a wrong one for a right one leaves the mechanism intact.

### The two documents state the clause DIFFERENTLY, which is a finding and not a detail

`GS-030` obligates *"one trading day **and one mark**"*. `EC-012` obligates *"one trading day"* and adds a `new Date()` clause. **[`fixtures/README.md:290`](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/README.md) quotes `EC-012`'s wording while citing `GS-030`'s row.** So three sites carry **two obligations under one quotation**, and one ruling over "the sentence" would have moved an obligation nobody asked to move. **They are ruled separately.** `GS-030`'s mark half is kept: it is true and unasserted, and dropping it would narrow a frozen row under cover of an arithmetic repair.

### The constitution's own premise is ALSO false, and this session did not rule it

[Appendix B4 item 1](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) at line 264 reads *"DST transition day — session spans clock change; day boundary must follow exchange calendar, not wall clock."* **"23 hour" and "25 hour" appear nowhere in the constitution**; the two numbers were invented in the transcription. And *"session spans clock change"* is false by the same measurement.

> **This is why the error survived.** The **consequent** is correct and is the whole design. The **antecedent** is false. A false premise supporting a true and independently justified conclusion is invisible to every reader who checks the conclusion, and every reader checked the conclusion, because the conclusion is the interesting part.

**RECORDED AND NOT RULED.** The file is read-only and outside this fence.

### The scope question, which was the hard part

**Asked in writing before the ruling was taken, and answered against the tree.** [ADR-077](../decisions/ADR-077.md) section 5:

- **No `INV`.** No invariant in the corpus is stated over a session length. `INV-01` ([M01:112](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)) forbids the engine a clock.
- **No `R-nn`.** The three rules citing `GS-030` are `R-01` (containment, `Computed by` **Ingest**), `R-05` (provenance of the bounds, `Computed by` **Calendar data**) and `FM-14` (**a count**, and session 124 asserted it green). `R-02`'s gap counting is `calendar.sequence` subtraction, an integer index.
- **No trading-day count.** That half is asserted and unchanged.
- **`CalendarDay`** ([`types.ts:217`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts)) carries four fields and none is a bound.

> **NOTHING IN THE TREE SUBTRACTS A SESSION OPEN FROM A SESSION CLOSE.** Five reading sites, all named in section 5.3; the only expression holding both endpoints is `close > open`, an ordering predicate in two migrations and one generator check that 23, 25 and 43.25 satisfy identically.

**That absence is the ONE clause the approval line asks a reader to check**, because it is the only claim here about an absence and a wider grep is the only thing that can refute it. **Cost if wrong:** two frozen documents amended under an entry that states in writing it moved no rule input, which is the exact failure it was commissioned to avoid, and unwinding it costs a second ADR.
