The dispatch says: make the producer callable. **`ADR-204` section 1 proves it cannot be**, and
[`M01:785`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)'s own third cell already says so. Re-derived here at
the three sources rather than carried:

"Projected forward" cannot mean asking the same state about a later day, because the state IS the day |
| [`advance.ts:179`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/advance.ts) | `export function advanceDay(input: DayInput): DayOutput {` | The only producer of a `RuleState` for any day |

`evaluatePayout` is genuinely the evaluator: it is called once per horizon day with its own
unchanged signature, over a state whose `tradingDay` never moves.

### 3. The population is DERIVED, which is what keeps `FM-16` out

Ruling 2's `E` union `C` appears nowhere as a predicate. An account is in the population exactly
when the engine's own `allGatesPass` clears on some horizon day, so:

- eligible today, the gap already clear -> clears on day one, `eligible_now`
- every gate but the gap, the gap expiring inside the horizon -> clears on that day, `cadence_pending`
- one win day short -> clears on no day, `excluded`

**There is no second statement of "every engine gate except the cadence gap passes" to drift
from**, which is the whole of `FM-16` and the reason this belongs in the engine rather than in
the admin-source directory that will consume it.

### 5. Which of the three shapes this is

Three instances of one class landed in a day: `0002`'s OTP index over the primary key, which
`0063` superseded; `ADR-201`'s threshold with no window; and this producer. **They are not all
the same shape and the difference is worth naming.**

`0002`'s index was **SUBSUMED**: a wider thing already covered it, so it was redundant rather
than inert, and the estate lost nothing by its absence. **`ADR-201`'s threshold and this producer
are the MISSING-INPUT shape**: a mechanism fully specified except for the one input that would
let it run -- a window to measure over, an argument to call with. **The specification is what
makes the absence invisible.** A reader looking for the mechanism finds it, in an approved
document, and stops looking. That is why this took four sessions and a redundant index took one.

### Landmines

- **"MAKE IT CALLABLE" WAS THE WRONG SHAPE OF INSTRUCTION AND THE RIGHT THING TO DO WAS TO SAY
  SO.** A session that had tried to satisfy the words would have widened `evaluatePayout` or made
  `DayInput.mark` optional, and both are seeds here that go red.

  MATTERS.** Ordering the horizon by `sequence` is unobservable to every case (seed 4). It is
  taken because `R-02` says so, not because a control demands it.
- **`Function.length` IS A CONTROL OVER PARAMETERS AND NOT OVER CONTRACTS.** A record parameter
  can grow a field without moving it (seed 11).
- **REFERENCE IDENTITY IS THE ONLY WAY TO ASSERT A VALUE WAS CARRIED.** Deep equality passes when
  the arithmetic agrees twice, which is exactly what a recomputation would do on the day it is
  correct and not on the day it is not.
