### 5. Blockers and landmines

- **THE DRAIN IS OWED AND IT IS ORDERED.** The failure channel is `packages/queue`'s row and comes first; the process shape is [ADR-241](../decisions/ADR-241.md)'s and comes second. A session that wires a saga caller without reading [ADR-338](../decisions/ADR-338.md) section 4 will write rows into a table nothing bounds.

- **A HAND-WRITTEN COUNT IN A BARREL HEADER HAS NOW BEEN WRONG FOUR TIMES IN `apps/worker`.** The lists are swept; the numerals are not, and nothing derives them.
- **ROW `337` IS RESERVED BY THIS BRANCH ON A SIBLING'S BEHALF** and its owner must amend that cell in place when it merges. The two branches will collide in `ALLOCATION.md`, and that is a conflict to resolve by hand rather than a keep-both.
