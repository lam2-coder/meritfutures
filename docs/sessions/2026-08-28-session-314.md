### 5. The replay control is TWO controls, and telling them apart is the whole of `replay.ts`

API_CONTRACT section 10 names both in one sentence and they have different HTTP answers:

| Control | Refuses | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| **The nonce** | a CAPTURED delivery re-presented by somebody who is not the rail. The bytes verify and the window is open; the only thing that says no is that this nonce has been seen | **401**, `replay_detected` |
| **The event id** | nothing. It RECOGNISES the rail's own retry, which re-signs with a fresh nonce and a fresh timestamp | **200 and stop**, which is `S-2` |

**Fold them together on the event id and a capture inside the window reads as a retry and is applied twice.
Fold them together on the nonce and a legitimate retry is answered 401 and the rail sends forever.** Both
folded ledgers are written in the suite and each is asserted to give the wrong answer where the real one
gives the right one.
