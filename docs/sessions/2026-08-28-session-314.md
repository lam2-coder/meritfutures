#### 0.3 The route is not written, and that is a refusal rather than an omission

[API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) section 10 rows **three** inbound webhooks and
`POST /webhooks/rail` is not one of them. `POST /webhooks/rise` is row two and it is **already registered**

contract row in an `approved` document, which is an ADR this session does not hold. **P5 section 5.5 already
says so** ("what is left of `P5-m` is the rail PORT and its sandbox adapter"); the fence's file list predates
that amendment.
