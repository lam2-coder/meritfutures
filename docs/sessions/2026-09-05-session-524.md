### 4. What was measured and NOT taken

- **A door publishing all five methods.** The obvious design, and the one this row expected to ship. Refused on two measurements: `start()` in this deployable exits 1 on a vendor's stack when the pool is released, or never exits when it is not; and a supervise failure reaches no caller of `JobQueue` at all.
- **`start()` as a precondition for `enqueue`.** Read out of `manager.js` and believed, then measured false: `declareQueue` and `enqueue` both succeed with no `start()`, because the queue cache is an empty object from the constructor and falls through to a per-name read. **A reading of a control flow was wrong and one run said so.**
- **Importing `poolSqlExecutor` into the queue door.** Refused: it would make `@merit/db` a two-file capability and loosen an assertion standing since session 292.
- **Widening `JobQueue` with a sixth method or an emitter.** The only real repair for the failure channel, and it is `packages/queue`'s subject; taking it here would be taking it as a MEANS ([ADR-165](../decisions/ADR-165.md) clause 5, [ADR-331](../decisions/ADR-331.md) section 4 clause 4). Named as owed with a `RI-35` artifact binding it.
- **Passing `applicationName` to `pgBossQueue`.** Reached for, then measured INERT: pg-boss reads `application_name` only in its own pool-backed driver, and `getDb()` returns a supplied `db` verbatim. Not passed; recorded as a finding.
- **Making the door a barrel leg.** `./sweeps/ledger.ts` is one and this is not: that file exports an adapter over a port and this exports the capability, which is `./db.ts`'s class.
- **Wiring the saga, and any clock at all.** [ADR-326](../decisions/ADR-326.md) section 4 stands entire and **the withdrawal driver still gets no clock**.

### 5. Landmines

1. **NOTHING CONSUMES A JOB AND NOTHING MAINTAINS THE STORE.** The door can declare and enqueue; no module in this workspace calls `consume` or `start`, so a job enqueued through it is never fetched. **That is now the visible consequence of the failure-channel gap rather than a separate absence**, and `RI-35`'s `job-queue-failure-channel` artifact binds both sentences.
2. **THE SUPERVISOR'S FAILURES STILL REACH NOBODY, AND THE ROW THAT REPAIRS IT NOW HAS A MEASUREMENT.** [ADR-333](../decisions/ADR-333.md) section 2.4 injects one deterministically and shows it arriving as an `unhandledRejection`; section 2.3 prices `start()` in a one-shot process in both directions.
3. **THERE IS NO ADMISSION LIST FOR `@merit/queue`.** A second deployable adding it to its manifest passes every gate in this repository. `RI-08` guards the accessor and nothing guards this. Said out loud in the ADR rather than left implied.
4. **THE SAME MISTAKE WAS MADE TWICE IN ONE ROW, IN TWO FILES.** A first-draft case counted the STRING `from '@merit/db'` and reported seven accessor importers, six of them headers saying they do not import it; and a first-draft `RI-35` probe counted the name `LIVE_QUEUE` and reported the door wired, off the sentence saying it is not. **That is [ADR-165](../decisions/ADR-165.md) section 9's recorded finding walked into by the row that quotes it.** Both instruments read a statement or a call now.
5. **[`apps/worker/src/provisioning/ports.ts`](../../apps/worker/src/provisioning/ports.ts) still says the manifest declares `@merit/rules-engine` "and nothing else"**, false since [ADR-165](../decisions/ADR-165.md) and now false by three names. Read and left, as [ADR-165](../decisions/ADR-165.md) section 10 finding 3.
6. **Unchanged from the rows before this one**: [ADR-331](../decisions/ADR-331.md)'s three stale citations of `scoped-db.ts:4088`; [ADR-305](../decisions/ADR-305.md) slice 8's stale citation; `0080` and `0081` carrying no `DELTA_MANIFEST`; `identity_restriction_episodes.sla_due_at` invisible to `CI-06l`; the four `DATABASE_URL`-gated cases still skipping in CI under `CI-04`'s dated second leg.
