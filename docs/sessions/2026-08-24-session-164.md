### 6. What I could not determine, and the M12 check

**The M12 question, answered at the source: M12 and M13 define no statistic twice, and the boundary is drawn from both sides.**

M12's seven are firm-level aggregates. [M12-statistic-definitions:44-164](../plans/M12-statistic-definitions.md): `ST-01` evaluation pass rate, `ST-02` funded-to-first-payout, `ST-03` total paid, `ST-04` average and median payout, `ST-05` request-to-wallet-credit, `ST-06` request-to-settlement, `ST-07` share of eligible approved. M13's are per-account: win rate, average and largest win and loss, expectancy, equity curve, R-multiple, profit factor ([M13:27-33](../plans/M13-trader-analytics-journal.md)). **Not one name appears in both**, and a grep for `win rate|expectancy|profit factor|R-multiple|Sharpe` across the corpus outside M13 returns only M11's certificate argument, M16's notification example, `EC-090`, `EC-102` and the simulation harness. **Both plans then exclude the other's territory explicitly**: [M12-transparency:56](../plans/M12-transparency-platform.md) publishes *"Any per-trader or per-account figure"* as one of three deliberate exclusions, and `INV-M13-09` forbids any comparison to a population. The two agree, in writing, in both directions.

**One borrowing, and it is where a number could still get defined twice later.** [M13:248](../plans/M13-trader-analytics-journal.md) borrows *"`INV-M12-05`'s discipline wholesale"* for minimum samples and **states no number for any ratio**. M12 fixes hers per statistic and stores them in [`statistic_definitions.min_sample`](../../packages/db/migrations/0021_transparency.sql):54 (250, 100, 50, 50, 50). M13's only numeral in that neighbourhood is [M13:243](../plans/M13-trader-analytics-journal.md)'s *"Sharpe ratios on fewer than thirty observations"*, which describes a trap rather than declaring a floor. **Undetermined, and it is a real fork**: whether M13's definitions reuse `statistic_definitions` (M12's table, whose `stat_code`, `effective_from` and `method_body_mdx` shape is publication policy for **public** statistics) or get their own. Reusing it puts a private per-account definition in the publication registry; not reusing it is a second definitions store.

**A second M12 asymmetry, which is not a disagreement and is worth a slice's attention.** [ADR-031](../decisions/ADR-031.md) ruled M12's published value `bigint` carrying its unit beside it, *"no `numeric` on this surface"*, and [ADR-032](../decisions/ADR-032.md) made the measure set declared. **M13 states no unit discipline at all.** `INV-M13-04` requires a stated definition and says nothing about a type, and a win rate is the natural place a float enters a module the constitution's no-floats rule reaches only through its financial paths. `M13-h` is where that gets decided.
