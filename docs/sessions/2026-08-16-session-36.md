### The confirmation that was asked for, performed rather than accepted

[ADR-041](../decisions/ADR-041.md)'s surface table has seven rows and six of them say where the enforcement is written. The affiliate row says something different: **blocked**, because "[ADR-017](../decisions/ADR-017.md) put every outbound payment on one rail, and a restriction that stops one door and not the other is not a restriction", followed by **"Confirmed against M08 during the fold."**

The sentence is true and the conclusion does not follow from it, and the gap between those two facts is the whole finding.

**One rail buys three things and they are not the same thing.** It makes the **destination** shared, so `D-09`'s concentration detector sees an affiliate payment and a trader payout in one query (`INV-M8-10`). It makes the **destination-change path** shared, so an affiliate destination cools for 48 hours exactly as a trader's does (`INV-M8-11`, and [ADR-017](../decisions/ADR-017.md) added that half precisely because AS-M8-05 had missed it). What it does not buy is the **authorization to pay**, because that decision is taken before anything reaches the rail: `G-ELIGIBLE` is evaluated on `payout_requests` and an affiliate statement is not one.

So `identities.status`, which [ADR-041](../decisions/ADR-041.md) added to `G-ELIGIBLE` in the same document, reached the trader's door and did not reach this one. **The affiliate gates that existed were a `NOT NULL` ToS column, a chargeback-rate threshold, and a sentence in the consumed-events list reading "an enforced affiliate stops being paid" with no mechanism named.**

> **Three instances of one shape in one fold is not a coincidence.** `G-ELIGIBLE` named `payouts_frozen` and never `identities.status`. `INV-M20-06` did the same while AS-M20-02's counter-argument cited the restriction as though something enforced it. And here the sentence doing the work sits in an **accepted ADR**, which is worse than the other two rather than better: a module plan invites a reader to check, and an ADR is where a reader stops.

**The transferable part is what "confirmed" has to mean.** ADR-041 was right that no schema change was needed, right that the join exists, and right that the rail is one rail. It was wrong that nothing needed writing, and the only way to find that out was to open [`0005`](../../packages/db/migrations/0005_affiliate_program.sql), open [`0012`](../../packages/db/migrations/0012_disputes_and_affiliate_settlement.sql), and look for the clause that reads the status. **A confirmation that does not name the line it confirmed is a plan to be surprised later**, and the cost of doing it properly here was one invariant and twenty minutes.
