## EC-106: A free earned spin that resolves into a purchase is still a purchased loot box  (2026-08-14, module: M14, status: specced)
- Scenario: ADR-019a's two clauses can each be satisfied while their composition is violated. A trader earns a spin through activity, the spin is free, its odds are published, and it awards a **discount of randomized size on a reset they then buy**. Nothing was purchased randomly and the product is a paid random outcome, because the randomized result determines what the subsequent payment yields. The same composition appears as a "mystery bundle" of evaluation credits earned through a milestone and redeemed at a fixed price for unknown contents, and as a randomized promotional-credit grant spendable only on a purchase. A separate but related failure: promotional credit that becomes withdrawable converts a marketing instrument into cash, which is an unpriced and possibly regulated liability and hands an attacker a laundering path.
- Correct behavior: State the composition as a rule rather than relying on the two clauses: **a randomized outcome that determines what a subsequent purchase yields is a purchased loot box regardless of how the randomization was obtained.** A mechanic that needs this explained has already failed, which is ADR-019a's own test. v1 randomizes nothing at all and every benefit is deterministic against published criteria; this is a position rather than a limitation to work around. `promotional_credit` has **no path to `trader_wallet` and no path to a withdrawal**, asserted by a ledger test rather than inferred from the absence of a feature. Every discount is a stated amount on a stated product before payment.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-181.
