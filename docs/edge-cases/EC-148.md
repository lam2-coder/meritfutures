## EC-148: A composite signal is a score or it is an accusation, and the schema cannot tell which  (2026-08-16, module: M07, status: specced)
- Scenario: [ADR-039](../decisions/ADR-039.md) (a) names the **fleet signature**: a VoIP line type plus a fresh email plus a datacenter IP plus no digital footprint, recorded as a named composite. Each component is individually weak and legitimately present on real customers, and the composite is genuinely strong, which is exactly the condition under which a scored signal drifts into a rule. A named composite with a memorable name and a high hit rate is the thing an implementer under time pressure reaches for as a refusal, and the refusal is one line. **Every population that composite selects for is also a population of ordinary people**: travellers and privacy-conscious customers on VoIP numbers, anybody who made an email address for a new service, anybody behind a corporate or cloud VPN, and anybody young enough or careful enough to have no digital footprint to find. [AS-M19-05](../plans/M19-kyc-identity.md) already establishes that the fleet-killer is also a false-accusation engine, and this is that finding arriving one signal class earlier, at the door rather than at review.
- Correct behavior: **VoIP is scored, never rejected**, and the ruling says so in those words rather than leaving it to judgment. The composite is a **named input to D-16's aggregate**, scored by the detector that already weighs device, payment and footprint edges, and it is never a verdict on its own. **GS-258 asserts that the refusal does not happen**, which is an unusual shape for a fixture and is the point: the pin is on the absence of an outcome, because the failure this entry describes is a control being added rather than removed, and nothing else in the suite would notice. Two properties bound it. **A refused signal is worth less than a scored one**, since a refusal at the door teaches the detector nothing and teaches the operator to re-register differently, while a score accumulates across a graph the operator cannot see. And **the verification UX ruling binds here too**: whatever the composite scores, the trader meets a milestone rather than an accusation, because a control landing on honest customers and fleet operators identically must read as routine to the honest ones.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-258
