## EC-153: A reversible enforcement whose reversal depends on an artifact nobody has yet  (2026-08-16, module: M02, status: specced)
- Scenario: [ADR-041](../decisions/ADR-041.md) sells `restricted` on its reversibility. That is what distinguishes it from closure for cause, it is why the episode table records a restore with an actor and evidence, and it is the property that makes restriction the proportionate action an operator can take without ending someone's relationship with the firm. **The reversal is the half that is not fully in Merit's hands.** Restoration is fail-closed by design: `set_risk` at the account's current floor, **confirmed first**, then entitlement, then permissions, because re-enabling an entitlement against an unconfirmed setpoint is an unenforced funded account and `INV-M2-13` forbids it. `0007` already makes it unwritable for a `set_risk` row to reach `confirmed_inferred`, which is exactly the guarantee restoration needs and is also exactly what blocks it: **with no acknowledgement artifact and no readable current risk setting from the vendor, a restored account cannot be confirmed at all.** `V-M2-15` is the corpus's one open commercial precondition, and [M02](../plans/M02-rithmic-bridge.md) is still at `status: review` under [ADR-005](../decisions/ADR-005.md). **Suspension is always available. Restoration is contingent.** A restricted trader would be revocable and not restorable, which turns a reversible state into a terminal one without anybody deciding that.
- Correct behavior: **The asymmetry is published as an asymmetry rather than softened into a caveat**, because the honest form of a provisional dependency is a statement about which direction works. Restoration is **refused** against an unconfirmed `set_risk` rather than proceeding optimistically, and the episode **stays open** with `restored_at` null, so the failure is visible in the row that is the authority on the restriction rather than swallowed by a partial restore. `identity_restriction_restore_is_complete` makes the half-restore unwritable in the first place: `restored_at`, `restored_by` and `restore_evidence` are all-or-none, so there is no state in which the database says a restriction was reversed and cannot say by whom. **The fold moves neither M02's status nor `V-M2-15`**; it adds a row to the vendor agenda, because a plan that quietly assumed the artifact would arrive is how the whole leg would ship untested.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-282
