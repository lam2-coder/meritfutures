## EC-149: A per-message cost on the acquisition path is a unit economic, and it arrives untracked  (2026-08-16, module: M03, status: specced)
- Scenario: [ADR-039](../decisions/ADR-039.md) (e) puts **SMS delivery and lookup costs into [M03](../plans/M03-billing-checkout.md)'s Cost Stack**, and the reason it needs saying is that this is the first cost in the corpus that scales with **attempts** rather than with **customers**. Every other Cost Stack line is incurred against somebody who bought something: a payment fee, a provisioning cost, a payout rail charge. An SMS at registration is paid for every attempt, including every abandoned signup, every retry, every honest fat-fingered number and, per EC-144, every message an attacker induces. **The unit that consumes it is not the unit that pays for it.** Untracked, it appears as a single growing vendor invoice with no attribution, which is precisely the shape of cost that gets discovered during a margin review rather than during a funnel change, and by then the funnel change that caused it is several months old.
- Correct behavior: **Per-message delivery and per-lookup cost are Cost Stack lines attributable to the acquisition path**, priced per attempt rather than per customer, so the cost of a registration funnel is legible in the same place as the cost of serving one. **A degraded window's spend is attributable to the window** rather than smeared across the month, which is what makes EC-142's breaker reviewable after the fact: the trip, the degraded window and the recovery each alarm, and the spend that caused the trip is readable against them instead of being reconstructed from a vendor statement. Deliverability belongs in the same line for the same reason, since a country or carrier with a low delivery rate is paying full price for messages that do not arrive, and that is a funnel defect wearing a cost's clothing. The values themselves live in `otp_send_budget` (`spend_cents`, `budget_cents`) as data rather than in prose, on the standing rule that a plan parameter is an input to a running system and a document restating one is a copy that drifts.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-272
