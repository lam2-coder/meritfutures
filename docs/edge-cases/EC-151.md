## EC-151: A releaser that reports success is not evidence the release happened  (2026-08-16, module: M05, status: specced)
- Scenario: The hourly freeze-expiry sweep carries an **S1 dead-man switch**, and the switch watches for the **absence of the completion signal**. That is the right control for a job that stops being scheduled, and it is the wrong control for the failure that actually costs a trader money. A sweep can run on time, complete, emit its signal and **release nothing**: a predicate that no longer matches the state it was written for, a batch size that silently truncates, a transaction that rolls back after the log line, a query filtered on `status = 'frozen'` after a second held state arrived. In every one of those the switch is satisfied, the dashboard is green, and rows sit past their own expiry. **This is [M02](../plans/M02-rithmic-bridge.md) `FM-M2-11`'s idiom one module over**, where the same distinction was drawn for the provisioning leg: a vendor acknowledging a request is not the vendor having done it. The version here is worse in one respect, because the party harmed by the silence is the trader rather than Merit, and the trader cannot see the sweep at all.
- Correct behavior: **The alarm fires on the query, not on the job.** A nightly assertion that no row sits past its own expiry, **evaluated independently of whether the sweep reported success**, carried as its own row in [CRON_INVENTORY](../ops/runbooks/CRON_INVENTORY.md) with its own expected-by time rather than folded into the sweep's dead-man switch. The two controls answer different questions and neither substitutes for the other: the switch asks whether the job ran, the assertion asks whether the work happened. It covers all three clocks the sweep carries, `payout_requests.freeze_expires_at`, `payout_requests.hold_expires_at` and `wallet_withdrawals.freeze_expires_at`. **It is the fourth unsuppressible alarm** ([ADR-040](../decisions/ADR-040.md), closing [M06](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) `OQ-M6-01`): the other three mean Merit cannot safely pay anyone, and this one means Merit has stopped paying and nobody is being told. **The honest limit is recorded with it rather than after it**: `alarm_suppressions.alarm_key` has no CHECK and no reference list, so nothing in the schema refuses a row muting any of the four, and adding a fourth member costs nothing today precisely because the list binds nothing today.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-276
