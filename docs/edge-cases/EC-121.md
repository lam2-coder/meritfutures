## EC-121: A live graduation program would change the firm's regulatory character  (2026-08-14, module: M18, status: specced)
- Scenario: The module's own name presumes a live program, and the market pressure to have one is real: it is the natural ending to a ladder and excellent marketing. Building it on Merit's paper means routing a trader's orders to a venue or allocating real firm capital against their decisions, and every compliance position in the corpus rests on one sentence repeated in the footer, at checkout, in the ToS, on certificates, and in the GLOSSARY: all Merit trading including the funded phase occurs in a simulated environment, with the firm taking the other side internally rather than routing to the exchange. Routing customer orders makes Merit an intermediary in a regulated market; allocating firm capital against a trader's decisions is a managed-account or adviser relationship, and pooled it is a vehicle with its own regime; either imports custody, suitability, and disclosure obligations no corpus document addresses. And it makes a disclosure Merit repeats everywhere conditionally false, which is worse than never having made it. The version that actually happens has nobody proposing to become a broker: the marketing site says "graduate to a live funded account" because it converts, and the product is then asked to mean something, which is the reverse of the order the business can survive.
- Correct behavior: v1 ships **continuation**, honestly framed: the ladder completes, the account closes with everything paid, the trader keeps their record and certificate, and may open a new account under the published rules. **No surface promises a live program unless one exists, is contracted, and is disclosed**, including aspirational phrasing and roadmap language, enforced by copy lint. A third-party introduction is the viable middle and is a commercial and legal arrangement rather than an engineering one, requiring a named counterparty, a written agreement, disclosed compensation, and an explicit statement that Merit makes no representation about the third party's terms. A counsel item covering all three paths is filed in legal/, and **no graduation copy is written until the ruling exists**, because the copy is what commits Merit, not the code.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-205.
