## EC-142: A control that protects revenue becomes a denial-of-service on it  (2026-08-16, module: M16, status: specced)
- Scenario: Phone verification is mandatory at registration and [ADR-039](../decisions/ADR-039.md) amendment 2 puts a **global cost circuit breaker** in front of the SMS that carries it. Every other breaker in this corpus is fail-closed, and each is fail-closed for the same reason: the moment it guards is one where the wrong answer moves money or provisions an unenforced account. Applied here the identical posture inverts. **A tripped breaker means no new customers**, and the cost of tripping it is the price of the SMS traffic that trips it, which is [AS-M16-07](../plans/M16-notification-center.md)'s attacker's whole business model already. So the control written to cap an SMS bill becomes a cheap, repeatable, attacker-triggerable shutdown of Merit's acquisition funnel, and it is triggered by doing more of the thing the attacker was doing anyway.
- Correct behavior: **The breaker degrades, it does not stop** (founder ruling, [ADR-039](../decisions/ADR-039.md)). On trip, **registration continues** with phone verification **deferred to a hard gate before first funding**. That gate is not a promise: `pre_funded` is already one of [ADR-021](../decisions/ADR-021.md)'s configured composite triggers, it fires before the funded account exists and therefore before any Merit capital is at risk, and **an identity that registered under a tripped breaker cannot reach a funded account without the verification it skipped**. `otp_send_budget.state` is `armed | degraded | manually_overridden` and **has no stopping value at all**, which is the ruling expressed as a vocabulary rather than as a handler that could be changed back. **The alarm is the half that decays and is therefore not optional**: `otp_send_budget_degraded_is_alarmed` refuses to store a silent trip, the trip, the degraded window and the recovery each alarm, and `deferred_registrations` is a reported figure, because a degraded mode nobody watches becomes the normal mode and a queue nobody drains is a fail-open with extra steps. **Degrading converts a denial-of-service into a queue**, and that is the whole trade.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-271
