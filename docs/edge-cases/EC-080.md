## EC-080: An affiliate recruiting their own fleet buys evaluations at a discount no coupon rule sees  (2026-08-13, module: M8 + M7, status: specced)
- Scenario: An operator becomes an affiliate and recruits traders who are their own synthetic or family identities. Ignoring trading outcomes entirely, they recover the commission rate on every fee they pay, so a 30 percent commission lowers the effective cost of running a fleet by 30 percent, with no coupon rule limiting it and no per-identity cap seeing it. The existing self-deal check misses this completely: it tests whether the buyer is the affiliate, and here the buyers are deliberately different identities.
- Correct behavior: Not a new rule, a change in what the existing signals join on. Both the buyer identity and the affiliate identity are recorded on every attribution (SD-M8-05), making the affiliate a first-class node in the identity graph so the cluster query that already finds fleets simply includes the referrer edge. A **concentration signal** fires when an affiliate referred buyers cluster on shared devices, payment fingerprints, or ASNs far above the population rate. And the self-deal void extends from "the buyer is the affiliate" to "the buyer is linked to the affiliate above the confidence ceiling". Residual, stated honestly: an operator with genuinely clean separation is indistinguishable from a successful affiliate, and should be, because at that point they may simply be one.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-124.
