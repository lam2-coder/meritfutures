## EC-147: The first bought vendor on the critical path of anything, on day one  (2026-08-16, module: M10, status: specced)
- Scenario: Phone verification is mandatory at registration, so [ADR-039](../decisions/ADR-039.md) (a)'s carrier and line-type lookup sits on the acquisition path from launch. **`IN-M10-06` is the first purchased vendor on the critical path of anything, by design.** Every other integration in [M10](../plans/M10-integrations.md) sits behind a moment that can wait, and this one sits in front of the moment a stranger decides whether Merit works. A vendor outage, a rate-limit change, a datasheet that quietly stops carrying portability history, or a timeout under load all land on registration, and the tempting posture is the one every other breaker in the corpus uses.
- Correct behavior: **The registration call site inherits checkout's failure posture verbatim: non-blocking, fail-open on timeout, VoIP scored and never rejected.** Registration completes with the `identity_phones` row written, `lookup_provider` recorded and the carrier fields null, so the absence of a lookup is a **fact on the row** rather than an inference from missing data, and the identity is scored on what is known. It takes **its own `integration_contracts` row** under `SD-M10-01`'s field allowlist (`INV-M10-02`, `INV-M10-04`) rather than reusing checkout's, because the call site is new even though the vendor is not: registration is a new moment and the phone number is a new field crossing the boundary, since it never left Merit before. **One consequence is recorded and deliberately not decided.** `OQ-M10-05`: [SECURITY](../architecture/SECURITY.md) C-28's degradation ruling is about the **cost** breaker and says nothing about a **vendor** outage. The shape is identical and the mechanism already exists, but degrading on a vendor outage means anyone who can take an SMS provider offline can turn off Merit's phone verification, which is a different bargain from the one the cost breaker struck and is the founder's to make. Recorded here so a later session does not assume it was decided by analogy.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-259
