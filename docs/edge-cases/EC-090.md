## EC-090: A per-trade certificate is a cherry-picking machine with the firm's signature on it  (2026-08-14, module: M11, status: specced)
- Scenario: The Axcera brochure ships per-trade certificates and PROP_TECH_LANDSCAPE records them as a SHOULD. A card publishing one trade's entry, exit, size, and result has exactly one use, which is publishing the best one. A trader with a 40 percent win rate over 200 trades has roughly 80 winners to choose from and the card shows one; nothing on it is false and the impression it creates is unrelated to the account's performance. The artifact is most valuable to paid passing services and signal sellers (dossier item 3), who need credible proof of skill to sell and for whom a firm-signed winning trade is the best available, issued free and on request.
- Correct behavior: Deferred rather than shipped (CT-M11-04, OQ-M11-01). The bright line, written so it survives the next competitor brochure: **Merit signs facts about accounts and periods, never facts about selected events.** If the kind is ever enabled, the only defensible shape renders the trade **alongside the account's own aggregate for the same period** (win rate, trade count, net result), which is honest and is deliberately far less attractive to publish, which is why competitors ship the other shape. The legitimate need behind the request is served privately by M13's per-account breakdown, where a trader can learn from a trade without publishing it.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-155.
