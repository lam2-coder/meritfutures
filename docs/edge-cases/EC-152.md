## EC-152: A second enforcement becomes a route around the first one's clock  (2026-08-16, module: M05, status: specced)
- Scenario: [ADR-040](../decisions/ADR-040.md) binds Merit to 48 hours on a held payout, and the binding is the whole point: the hold either pays or produces a documented enforcement action inside the window, and there is no third outcome. [ADR-041](../decisions/ADR-041.md) then creates a **second** enforcement, reversible, at the identity level, that halts every linked account at once. **The two compose into an unbounded hold if nobody rules on the interaction**, and the composition is not exotic, it is the ordinary case: a payout is held on a severity-5 flag, the investigation widens to the human, a restriction opens, and the payout is now sitting behind a state that has no clock of its own. Nobody has to intend this. **The operator who does it is following both documents correctly**, and the trader experiences a hold that was promised to last 48 hours and has lasted a week. It is worse than a plain indefinite hold, because each half is individually defensible and the failure lives only in the join.
- Correct behavior: **The 48 hour SLA binds the restriction rather than the payout, and a restriction cannot hold a held payout past its own 48 hours.** `identity_restriction_episodes.sla_due_at` carries the clock **where a payout is pending** and is null where none is, so the restriction inherits the payout's deadline rather than the payout inheriting the restriction's open-endedness. **`hold_expires_at` is not writable from any admin route after the row exists** ([M06](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) `INV-M6-13`), and no console affordance offers to extend a hold, a freeze or a restriction, because expiry is the security control rather than the flag and an "extend" button is the one control-shaped affordance that would delete the control. A case needing longer than 48 hours **produces an enforcement inside the window or it produces a payment.** Asserted rather than intended: the golden scenario opens a restriction over a held payout and requires the payout to pay at its own expiry with the restriction still standing.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-284
