## EC-125: KYC placement silently sets the size of the biometric dedupe corpus  (2026-08-14, module: M19, status: specced)
- Scenario: Constitution section 10 prices the placement tradeoff on cost and conversion: pre-eval costs roughly $1.50 to $2 on 100 percent of buyers and suppresses conversion on an impulse purchase, while pre-funded verifies about 15 percent for an 85 percent saving with friction landing on people already invested. That analysis omits a variable the same section gestures at without following through, when it says fleets are caught by biometric dedupe before liability exists. **Dedupe is a cross-applicant face match and its power is a function of corpus coverage, and under pre-funded 85 percent of buyers never enter the corpus.** An operator running dossier item 6's 20 to 30 accounts buys 30 evaluations, has 4 or 5 pass and get verified, and **25 faces are never seen**. They have bought reconnaissance: they learn which synthetic identities survive verification at a cost of four checks, and can iterate in batches reusing the winners. Pre-eval would have matched all 30 faces against each other on day one.
- Correct behavior: Record and price the finding rather than simply switching placement, since the honest counter-argument is real: under pre-funded no liability exists until they pass. That claim is smaller than it appears, because the operator still gains tolerance intelligence and funded accounts caught only if dedupe fires across a small set, and the constitution is explicit that the business dies from liability rather than foregone fees. Launch `pre_funded` as directed and **extend the (g) telemetry to capture corpus coverage**: the share of purchasing identities ever verified, and dedupe matches per 1,000 verifications at that coverage. Recognize that under pre-funded **the device and payment graph carries the whole first line of defense** for 85 percent of buyers and resource it as a primary control rather than as corroboration for a face match that will never be taken. And pre-agree a **targeted escalation**: placement is per plan version, so verification can be raised to purchase on the plan and size combinations the beta shows fleets using, preserving conversion on the impulse entry plan while closing the corpus gap where it matters.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-212.
