## EC-133: A freeze that covers the cash door and not the product door is not a freeze  (2026-08-14, module: M20, status: specced)
- Scenario: M05's freeze path and Appendix D4's destination controls all guard the external leg, and the wallet has a second exit none of them touch. An identity whose withdrawals are frozen, whose destination is inside its 48 hour cooling window, or whose KYC has expired simply **spends** the balance at checkout. The value does not sit still: spending converts a frozen balance into accounts, accounts produce payouts, and those payouts credit the wallet again in a position that may by then be unfrozen, on an account carrying no freeze of its own. SECURITY §4.7 reasons that internal spend is contained because it never leaves Merit's books and is reversible by ledger entry, which is true of the spend and **not true of what the spend buys**: a frozen $5,000 becomes five evaluations, then funded accounts, then fresh unfrozen credits. The freeze bought a delay and a change of clothes.
- Correct behavior: **A payouts-frozen identity cannot spend either.** The freeze covers both exits, because a freeze that covers one is not a freeze, and this is the module's most important single line. The same applies to every context gate blocking the external leg: expired KYC, `recon_blocked`, and an active restriction all block spend as well as withdrawal, so the wallet's two doors share their context gates even though their mechanical gates differ. The velocity limit is a rate control and **not a freeze substitute**, since conflating them leaves a frozen identity draining slowly, which is the same attack with patience. And accounts purchased while an identity was under any hold are marked, so their eventual payouts carry that provenance into the review path and the chain does not launder the provenance along with the value.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-223.
