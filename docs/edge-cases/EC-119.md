## EC-119: Stacked instruments sell a funded account for nothing  (2026-08-14, module: M17, status: specced)
- Scenario: B4 #11 pins the coupon race and says nothing about arithmetic once several instruments coexist, and by launch there are at least five: a campaign coupon, a contextual reset discount, promotional credit from a loyalty grant, an affiliate code, and a bundle price. `stackable=false` on coupons prevents two coupons combining and says nothing about a coupon plus credit plus a bundle. On **Direct**, which funds immediately with no evaluation, a 40 percent coupon over a bundle already 25 percent below list, plus enough promotional credit to cover the remainder, approaches a zero-price purchase that issues live simulated capital with real payout liability and effectively no fee behind it: at 25K Direct with a 75,000c cap and a 6 payout ladder that is up to 405,000c of trader-side lifetime exposure created by a transaction collecting almost nothing. Nobody configured that; three people configured three reasonable things. An operator who works it out repeats it up to the entity cap.
- Correct behavior: **Price floors per product**, clamping the resolved price regardless of how many instruments applied, as a hard stop rather than a warning. The **Direct floor is a liability decision rather than a margin one**, carrying a written reason and an approver, because the number it must exceed is a function of expected payout liability. Floor edits are **dual controlled** on the same footing as cap, split, and gap, since a control anybody can lower is not a control. `offer.floor_applied` alerts, because the floor firing means a live configuration would otherwise have sold below it, which is a bug to find rather than a clamp to be grateful for. And a **stacking simulation runs the full cross-product** of every live instrument against every product nightly, because testing instruments individually is what produced the gap.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-201.
