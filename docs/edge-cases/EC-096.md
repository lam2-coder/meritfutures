## EC-096: An auto-review-request on payout settlement is review gating  (2026-08-14, module: M12, status: specced)
- Scenario: The Axcera brochure ships a Trustpilot auto-review-request on payout settlement, reaching the trader at the happiest moment of the relationship. Trustpilot's guidelines prohibit selective invitation and this is the definitional case; separately, the FTC's rule on consumer reviews and testimonials treats soliciting only customers likely to be positive as a deceptive practice. Neither is a gray area. It is worse for Merit than for the firm shipping it: a rating built by selecting whom to ask is the same sin as a pass rate built by selecting a denominator, committed on the surface where the firm is loudest about not doing it, and the story that writes itself is not "prop firm solicits reviews" but "transparency firm was gaming its reviews".
- Correct behavior: Invite across a **neutral trigger set that includes unfavorable outcomes by construction**: breach, refund, account closure, and rejected KYC alongside payout settlement, sampled at rates proportional to each outcome's share of the population, so the invited set resembles the customer base rather than its best quartile. Record `trigger_class` on every invitation so "were the people you asked representative" is a query rather than a memory, and alarm on skew as a compliance alert rather than a quality one. **No incentive of any kind**, which also keeps the surface clear of ADR-019a's bright line. **No pre-screening question**, since routing only the happy answers onward is gating with an extra step and is its most common implementation. Suppression is for genuine conflict only (open dispute, open severity 4+ flag, opt-out, recent invitation), and **"the outcome was bad" is never a suppression reason**. The accepted consequence, stated to the founder in advance: Merit's rating will be lower than a gated competitor's, and the correct response is to publish the seven statistics next to it rather than to close the gap.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-164.
