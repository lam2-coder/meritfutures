## EC-122: The payout ladder is only a ladder while the trader is winning  (2026-08-14, module: M18, status: specced)
- **CONFIRMED unchanged 2026-08-14 (founder ruling, folded with [ADR-025](../decisions/ADR-025.md)).** Two elements of the correct behavior below were raised for reconsideration and are confirmed as written: **the countdown tracker**, which counts down from the final ordinal rather than up from zero, and the **same-breath continuation disclosure**, in which the continuation clause sits inside the same sentence as the limit rather than a page away. **This entry is the ladder-finiteness edge case.** [ADR-024](../decisions/ADR-024.md) cited it as EC-137; EC-137 is a Merit Wallet entry and the citation is corrected there. Now that [ADR-025](../decisions/ADR-025.md) has replaced per-account cap growth with cross-account loyalty, the continuation clause carries more weight rather than less: opening another account is no longer only permitted, it is the path that earns something ([EC-139](EC-139.md)).
- Scenario: The constitution calls M18 "the marketing face of the payout cap", which honestly describes a slightly uncomfortable fact: the ladder bounds lifetime extraction (INV-17), and presenting it as a progression makes a limit feel like a goal. That becomes serious in one case: a trader who does not know the ladder is finite until it ends. They have taken four payouts, the system has been mechanical and correct every time, which is what built their trust, and on the fifth the account graduates and stops. **The shorter ladder ([ADR-024](../decisions/ADR-024.md)) makes this arrive sooner**, which raises the disclosure stakes rather than lowering them. From where they sit the firm stopped paying an account that was working at the moment it worked best, and every previous instance of reliability now reads as setup. ADR-018 makes the ladder one of three named defenses of Merit Rapid's headline rate, and a defense the customer does not know about is one that feels like a trap when it fires.
- Correct behavior: All disclosure, done early, rather than any mechanism. State finiteness **before purchase** on the plan's rules page, in the plan comparison, and in the account's tracker from ordinal zero, not as "up to 5 payouts" in small type but as **"each account pays up to 5 payouts, then completes. Open another anytime."**, with the lifetime figure spelled out in money. The continuation clause is load bearing. **The tracker counts down rather than up**, because "2 remaining" and "3 of 5 taken" are the same fact and only one cannot be misread. Publish the lifetime number, **$6,750 on Core EOD at 50K, $5,400 on Direct, and $4,500 on Merit Rapid** at the 9000bp split, which ADR-018 already uses internally as a defense and which publishing converts into a feature, since it is a large and honest number. And keep a public graduation page linked from every rules page so what happens at the end is readable before the beginning.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-206.
