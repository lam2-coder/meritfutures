## EC-087: A suppression guard evaluated at enqueue has nothing left to suppress  (2026-08-14, module: M10, status: specced)
- Scenario: EVENTS section 11 requires breach commiseration to be suppressed when the identity is restricted or carries an open severity 4+ flag. The ordering defeats it: the breach event fires at 00:20 in the nightly batch, the detector run flags the ring at 00:40, and a human applies the restriction at 09:15. A guard evaluated when the message was enqueued saw a clean identity at 00:21 and sent. The version with money attached is worse: the same ordering sends a discounted reset offer to an account under active investigation, so Merit offers in writing, with a coupon code, to sell more capacity to a ring it is mid-way through detecting.
- Correct behavior: **Guards evaluate at send time against live state**, never at enqueue; the dispatcher re-reads restriction status, open-flag severity, and account state immediately before the send. The two offer-bearing messages, breach commiseration with a reset offer and win-back, additionally **hold for a configured window** (proposed: until the next detector run for that identity completes, and never less than 2 hours), because the entire value of a late guard is that something arrived in between, and a commiseration email is not urgent. Suppressed sends are counted per guard and surfaced: a suppression count of zero for a week means the guards are not wired, not that the week was clean.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-151.
