## EC-095: Publishing on-chain payout proof publishes the treasury  (2026-08-14, module: M12, status: specced)
- Scenario: The crypto settlement rail Merit uses is indexed by public trackers, which is what makes on-chain proof possible and is a genuine competitive gift; TOP10_FIRMS records that this is exactly why Tradeify, MyFundedFutures, and FundedNext have public payout volumes while Apex and Topstep, settling by Wise and ACH, do not. The second edge is sharp: a published address is a permanent, irrevocable, real-time disclosure of its balance, every inflow from operations, every outflow, payout timing and size distribution, and every counterparty. A competitor computes volume and growth and sees the weekly funding rhythm as a regular inflow. **A ring watches the balance and times a correlated wave (M05 AS-M5-03) to the low point of the funding cycle**, which means Merit published the one number needed to schedule the attack. A community member watches the balance fall between funding events and reports the firm is running out of money, which may be false and is unanswerable because the observation is true. And trader destination addresses are exposed by association.
- Correct behavior: The two goals are in genuine tension and the design chooses a point rather than pretending otherwise, because a fresh address per payout hides the aggregate and destroys the proof. If an address is published it is a **dedicated settlement address that is not the reserve and not the ops account**, funded to a swept working balance sized to a short horizon so its balance discloses operational tempo rather than position, with a ceiling alarm. Reserve and ops addresses never appear in any artifact and are never linked from a published one. Trader destination exposure is disclosed to traders in plain words at the point they set a destination, because Merit cannot prevent it and they deserve to know before choosing a rail. Every proof link is an audited row with a written scope note, since publishing an address is one of the very few actions in the system that cannot be undone. The recommendation (OQ-M12-02) is to **cite third-party trackers and publish no address of Merit's own**: the proof strength lost is modest because the tracker indexes the rail regardless, and the disclosure avoided is permanent.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-163.
