## EC-078: Entity resolution merges a family and punishes them for each other  (2026-08-13, module: M7, status: specced)
- Scenario: A father funds his son's evaluation with his card. A married couple share a laptop. Two students share a dorm IP and ASN. Each produces exactly the signals the adversary dossier lists for a fleet. An over-eager resolver merges them, aggregates their caps, and eventually enforces against all of them for one member's behavior. The cost falls on people who did nothing wrong, and unlike a ring, the wronged trader is sympathetic, articulate, and telling the truth, which is precisely the review-page content that kills firms.
- Correct behavior: **Two tiers.** Only a biometric dedupe hit or an explicit admin merge with evidence performs a hard merge that aggregates caps. Shared device, IP, ASN, and payment produce **soft links** that make the cluster visible and change nothing a trader may do. `confidence_bp` is never 10000 on an inferred edge, so the schema itself refuses to express certainty it does not have. A **dispute path** (SD-M7-04) lets a trader contest a link, renders the dispute on the graph, and puts it in front of the admin **before** they act rather than after. And enforcement is always per behavior and never per edge: an edge is a reason to look, never the evidence.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-121.
