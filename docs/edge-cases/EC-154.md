## EC-154: An outbound rail gated at one door and not the other, and the citation was inside an ADR  (2026-08-16, module: M08, status: specced)
- Scenario: [ADR-041](../decisions/ADR-041.md)'s surface table has seven rows and six say where the enforcement is written. The affiliate row says something different: blocked, because **"[ADR-017](../decisions/ADR-017.md) put every outbound payment on one rail, and a restriction that stops one door and not the other is not a restriction"**, followed by "confirmed against M08 during the fold". **Performing that confirmation rather than accepting it found the half that was missing.** The join is real (`affiliates.identity_id NOT NULL REFERENCES identities`) and the rail is genuinely one rail, and **nothing gated a statement on the identity's status.** "One rail" is a claim about the **destination** and about the **detector**; the **authorization to pay** is a third control that the phrase reads as covering and does not. So a restricted human could not request a payout, could not spend the wallet, could not withdraw, and **would still have been paid their affiliate statement on the next cycle.** This is the third place in one fold where a control was cited as binding and bound nothing, after `G-ELIGIBLE` not naming `identities.status` and checkout reading no restriction at all, and **this one's citation was inside an ADR**, which is where a reader stops looking.
- Correct behavior: **Affiliate settlement is gated on the identity's status like every other outbound door**, and the statement is **held rather than voided** (`INV-M8-12`). Voiding would make a reversible enforcement permanently cost the affiliate money, which is [ADR-040](../decisions/ADR-040.md)'s "Merit's own hold cost the trader money" one door over, and a restriction is reversible by design. **The positive control is the half that matters**: a gate asserted only on the refusal passes just as well when it refuses everybody, so the scenario requires settlement **in full on the next cycle after a documented restore**, not merely a refusal while the restriction stands. And the quiet half is `FM-M8-11`: **a restore with no release leaves the money held forever and raises nothing**, because a held statement is silent by construction, so the restore path must release the hold rather than only clearing the status.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-281
