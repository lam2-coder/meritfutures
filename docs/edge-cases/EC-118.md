## EC-118: A free trial deletes the identity signal it most needs  (2026-08-14, module: M17, status: specced)
- Scenario: Free-trial accounts are a recorded brochure input and an obvious funnel improvement, and they remove Merit's strongest identity signal exactly where it matters most. D-08 watches payment velocity and the dossier names the payment fingerprint as a primary entity-resolution input, with constitution B1 making the identity graph the system's spine. A free account has **no payment fingerprint at all**, so entity resolution on a trial cohort falls back to email normalization, device fingerprint, and IP or ASN, each cheap to defeat with disposable domains, browser profiles, and residential proxies. Dossier item 6 describes one operator running 20 to 30 accounts under different names to beat per-entity caps, and free trials drive the marginal cost of an additional identity to approximately zero, which is the one condition under which the account cap stops being a real constraint.
- Correct behavior: **Do not ship at launch.** The funnel benefit is real and the timing is wrong: trials should follow a beta's worth of detector calibration and M19's placement telemetry. If they ship, **KYC precedes provisioning unconditionally**, regardless of the M19 placement config, because the placement tradeoff inverts here: the cost argument for pre-funded verification is that verification costs money on 100 percent of buyers, and a free trial has no purchase to protect, so the fraud argument wins outright. Trials are capped per identity and per resolved entity and count against `max_accounts_per_entity` rather than sitting outside it. And **a trial cannot reach a funded account without a paid step**, which preserves the property that real liability always sits behind a real payment and costs the funnel little, since a trial's job is to demonstrate the product rather than to fund anyone.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-199.
