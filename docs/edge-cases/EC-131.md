## EC-131: Data minimization creates an evidence dependency on the provider  (2026-08-14, module: M19, status: specced)
- Scenario: Appendix D2 and VG-10 are emphatic and correct that documents and biometrics never touch Merit's storage. The untraced consequence is that **the evidence for Merit's strongest fraud finding lives entirely at the provider**. Three ways that hurts, none requiring bad behavior: the provider's retention expires the applicant record while Merit's `kyc_verifications` retention is forever for AML reasons, so Merit holds a conclusion whose basis is deleted; Merit changes providers and every historical dedupe match becomes an assertion referencing an applicant id at a company Merit no longer contracts with; or a trader disputes an enforcement two years later in a forum with discovery and Merit's evidence pack contains a boolean. The batch 1 gate made evidence packs two tier with detector detail in the internal tier, and M07's posture rests on court-grade packs, so an enforcement grounded in a dedupe hit with no retrievable basis is the weakest link under the strongest-sounding claim.
- Correct behavior: Thread the minimization requirement rather than weakening it. Store an **`evidence_snapshot` of decision metadata at the time**: match score, method identifier, model or technique version, provider decision id, and timestamps, with **no images, no templates, no document data**, which is fully compatible with D2 because a score and a method are not biometric data. Rest enforcement on **corroborating conduct**, so the pack's spine is fills, marks, devices, and payments that Merit holds forever, with the dedupe hit supporting rather than load bearing. Put **retention and export commitments in the provider contract** aligned with Merit's AML retention, including export of decision metadata for historical matches on a provider change, as a procurement requirement settled before the first verification rather than after the first dispute. And run a payload scanner plus canary in CI, because the counter to this scenario adds a field and the risk of a field is that it grows.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-218.
