## EC-157: The mark identities and the constraint that checks them place the adjustment on opposite sides of the open  (2026-08-16, module: M1 + M2, status: open)
- Scenario: [M01](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) asserts two mark identities at `DO-3` and [EC-034](EC-034.md) states them in the same words: **`INV-18`, `opening == prior.balance + adjustment`**, and **`INV-19`, `closing == opening + realized_pnl`**. [`0014_marks.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0014_marks.sql) carries one `CHECK` for both, named `daily_marks_balance_arithmetic`: **`closing = opening + realized_pnl + adjustment`**. **The three have exactly one common solution and it is `adjustment_cents = 0`**, which is the one value `SD-01` was added to make impossible to assume. Worked in integer cents on the case the column exists for, a settled payout of 250,000c against a prior close of 5,000,000c on a day that makes 30,000c: `INV-18` gives an opening of **4,750,000**, `INV-19` gives a closing of **4,780,000**, and the constraint requires **4,530,000**. The database refuses the row the two approved identities require, so **the mark for every settled payout is unwritable as specified**, and the engine that received one would raise a `DO-3` assertion failure against a row the database had already accepted. The disagreement is not about the day's net movement, which is `prior.closing + adjustment + realized_pnl` under both readings; it is about **whether `opening_balance_cents` is the balance before or after the withdrawal lands**, and that is the number `GS-065` is written to pin. Nothing has noticed because **every mark in the repository carries a zero adjustment**, and at zero the three agree exactly. Four artifacts say the adjustment lands at the open and one says it lands inside the day: `INV-18`, `R-10` ("the withdrawal lands at the open of `effectiveTradingDay`, never inside a session"), `EC-034`, and `0014`'s own comment on the column ("applied at the **open** of the effective trading day ... never inside a session"), against `daily_marks_balance_arithmetic`. **Both `0014`'s comment above the constraint and the [`daily_marks` design record](../architecture/data-model/daily_marks.md) label it `INV-18`**, and it is neither identity.
- Correct behavior: **NOT RULED. This entry is the finding and the founder's ruling is the resolution**, because the two repairs cost different things and both touch the money path. **Repair A, the constraint is wrong**: a superseding migration replaces `daily_marks_balance_arithmetic` with `closing = opening + realized_pnl`, and `INV-18` becomes uncheckable in the database because it reads a row the constraint cannot see, which is `rule_states` and not `daily_marks`. That is the reading four artifacts support, and its cost is that the schema stops asserting the adjustment at all. **Repair B, the invariants are wrong**: `INV-18` becomes `opening == prior.balance` and the adjustment moves inside the day, which contradicts `R-10` and `EC-034` and reopens the question `SD-01` closed, since a withdrawal applied inside a session is a withdrawal the intraday low can be measured against and `INV-21` stops following from `CV-11` and `CV-17`. **The reading a session under pressure will re-derive is that the constraint is authoritative because it is executable**, and that is the reading to be most careful about: an executable statement of the wrong identity is still the wrong identity, and it is the only one of the five artifacts that was never read against the other four. **Migrations are superseded and never edited** (constitution E2), so neither repair is an edit to `0014`. Until it is ruled, the conflict is asserted executably in [`day-sequence.property.test.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/test/generators/day-sequence.property.test.ts): the arbitrary day-sequence generator emits marks satisfying `INV-18` and `INV-19`, and a test watches every one of them with a non-zero adjustment **fail** `daily_marks_balance_arithmetic`, transcribed verbatim in `validate-day-sequence.ts`. That test fails the moment either side moves, which is what makes it the record rather than this paragraph.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-065, and the dependency runs the other way from the usual one. **GS-065's fixture cannot be written until this is ruled**, because it asserts "the day's opening balance after a maximum capped payout still sits above the floor", and that is precisely the number the two arithmetics disagree about. GS-081 and GS-083 are unaffected: neither reads an opening balance on a settlement day.
