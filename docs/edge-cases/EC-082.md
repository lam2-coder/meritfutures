## EC-082: The promoter claim the firm has to answer for  (2026-08-13, module: M8, status: specced)
- Scenario: An affiliate publishes a guaranteed-payout claim, fabricated earnings screenshots, or omits the simulated-environment disclosure. Under NFA I-26-12 a promoter claim is the firm compliance problem, and separately it is a brand problem: a trader who arrived believing a guarantee and then meets a real rule will say Merit lied, and from their side that is true. The existing control, a `creative_approved` boolean on the affiliate, records that something was once approved but not what, not when, and not against which disclosure text, so it cannot answer the only question a compliance conversation asks.
- Correct behavior: Approval is **per asset and per disclosure version**, with a reviewer and a timestamp (SD-M8-03). A new required disclosure **automatically withdraws** every creative bound to the superseded version, so a disclosure change cannot leave a field of stale approved assets that looks healthy from the inside. Approved URLs are re-fetched periodically and a changed page reverts to `pending`, because editing an approved landing page is otherwise the easiest bypass in the system. The affiliate ToS enumerates the prohibited claim classes explicitly (guarantees, fabricated results, omitted simulated-environment disclosure, implied partnership with Merit) rather than gesturing at "misleading", which is not a standard anyone can comply with. Enforcement is graduated and published: withdrawal, suspension, then termination.
- Golden scenario ref: GS-126.
