This entry chooses.** The arithmetic and the prose are each unanimous and opposite, both halves are re-derived at their own sources here rather than inherited, and both measurements are CONFIRMED. **What separates them is not their unanimity but what each unanimity is made of**: the prose is many hands over many sessions, four of which ACTED on the belief, and the arithmetic is three cells of one table at the documented site of this corpus's documented failure mode. **So `firm_treasury` is an `asset`, `LT-02`, `LT-06` and `LT-07` have the `firm_treasury` leg on the wrong side, and [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) section 2.1 is AMENDED for all three.** Migration [`0053`](../../packages/db/migrations/0053_firm_treasury_kind.sql) is TAKEN and writes the ruling into the schema.

### 1. Both halves, re-derived at their own sources

**The convention, read and not trusted.** [`posting.ts:235-236`](../../packages/ledger/src/posting.ts) pushes `{account: t.debit, amountCents: t.amountCents}` and `{account: t.credit, amountCents: -t.amountCents}`, under a header calling itself *"THE ONE PLACE A SIGN IS APPLIED IN THIS PACKAGE"*. [`0009:118`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql) states the same from the schema side. **A DEBIT is `+amount_cents` and a CREDIT is `-amount_cents`**, so a debit increases an asset or an expense and decreases a liability, a revenue or an equity.

**THE ARITHMETIC, RE-READ AT [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) AND CONFIRMED UNANIMOUS.**

| Posting | What the row said before this entry | Signed | Under `asset` | Under `liability` |

### 2. THE RULING

> **1. `firm_treasury` IS AN `asset`.** [`0053`](../../packages/db/migrations/0053_firm_treasury_kind.sql) supersedes [`0052`](../../packages/db/migrations/0052_chart_of_accounts.sql)'s `ledger_accounts_kind_matches_code` with a fifth arm and seeds the row. **`ledger_accounts` goes from 1 row to 2.**
>
> **2. THE PROSE IS RIGHT AND THE ARITHMETIC IS WRONG, AND THE WRONG SIDE IS AMENDED.** [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) section 2.1's `LT-02`, `LT-06` and `LT-07` rows are AMENDED IN PLACE ([ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3): **only the leg naming `firm_treasury` moves in each**, and each row keeps its original text quoted inside it, which is [ADR-027](ADR-027.md)'s own precedent in this exact table. **`LT-02` and `LT-07` CREDIT it** at settlement, because cash derecognizes at settlement. **`LT-06` does not name it at all**, because no cash moves at an approval. The six prose sites stand unamended because they are right.
>
> **3. ALL THREE ROWS ARE MARKED UNPOSTABLE, WHICH IS STRONGER THAN WHAT THEY CARRIED BEFORE.** They used to read as postable and wrong. The counterparty slot in each is [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) section 3's, and section 3 below is why this entry can amend where that entry refused to.

> **4. > **5. ### 3. Why this entry may amend the rows where [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) refused to

[ADR-174](ADR-174.md) section 6 considered exactly this amendment and rejected it: *"the row cannot be half-corrected without naming what the credit account is, and a row reading `debit the in-flight obligation; credit firm_treasury` would state a design this entry has just refused to take."* **That ground was correct and it no longer holds, which is recorded rather than stepped around.**

**The half that was undecidable then is the half this entry decides.** When [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) wrote that sentence, `firm_treasury`'s kind was unknown, so neither leg of `LT-07` was determined and any rewrite would have had to invent both. With the kind ruled, **the cash leg is determined by the kind plus a control that was already ruled** (the batch 1 gate's *"cash derecognizes at settlement"*), and the only thing still open is which code holds the in-flight obligation. **So the row can now be half-corrected, and half is exactly what is corrected.**

**[ADR-174](ADR-174.md) clause 4 already RULED that the role exists**, from [STATE_MACHINES section 3.2](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md)'s three-state rail and [M20:156](../plans/M20-wallet.md). Naming the open slot as that role is citing a landed ruling, not stating a new design. **Naming a CODE for it is what this entry does not do**, and that is the whole of [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) section 3's question, left exactly where it was.

**And [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) clause 5's other ground is honoured rather than overturned**: *"rewriting half a defective row is how a reader stops being able to see that it was defective."* Each amended row quotes what it used to say, in the row.

### 6. THE DEFECT THIS SESSION FOUND, WHICH WAS NOT IN THE DISPATCH

**[`0052:115-116`](../../packages/db/migrations/0052_chart_of_accounts.sql) states this entry's conclusion backwards, in a file `E2` makes unamendable, and it drifted from the entry it transcribes.** [ADR-177](ADR-177.md) section 4 writes that the GLOSSARY's *"funds set aside"* is *"asset-shaped prose of exactly the kind that turned out to be **contradicted** for `firm_treasury` one row up"*. The migration transcribing that sentence writes *"turned out to be **wrong** for firm_treasury one row up"*.

**Contradicted and wrong are not the same claim, and the difference is the whole of what [ADR-177](ADR-177.md) refused to say.** That entry established both halves were unanimous and declined to name a loser; the migration names one, and names the one this entry now rules was right. **A merged migration is never edited, only superseded**, so the sentence stands and is corrected here instead.

**Its CONCLUSION about `reserve` survives untouched**, because that refusal rests on its other stated ground, which is that nothing posts against `reserve` and nothing reads it. **Only the reason attached to it is wrong.** Recorded because the next session to read `0052` for guidance on the two remaining codes will read this sentence as a warning against asset-shaped prose, and the warning is now backwards.

### 8. Approval

- **CLASS C, DECIDED 2026-08-29 UNDER [ADR-227](ADR-227.md) SECTION 3 AND RECORDED BY [ADR-243](ADR-243.md). THE READ IS OWED AND NO ASSERTION SUBSTITUTES FOR IT.** The central claim is *"`firm_treasury` is an `asset`"*, and this entry's own third bullet says it: **"THIS IS A JUDGEMENT AND NOT A DERIVATION."** A test can assert that [`0053`](../../packages/db/migrations/0053_firm_treasury_kind.sql) writes `asset` and that the three amended rows read as amended; **it cannot assert that `asset` is the right answer**, and one that pinned the ruling to itself would be the tautology ADR-227 section 6 condition 2 names. **This is the ruling working rather than a shortfall**, and section 8's argument against stands exactly as written.

- **Approval line: PENDING, UNSIGNED.** **MONEY PATH, `E2` READ OWED** on [`0053`](../../packages/db/migrations/0053_firm_treasury_kind.sql).

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.**

**THE QUESTION, PLAINLY. Two readings of this corpus are each internally coherent and each unanimous, and you are being asked to confirm which one is the wrong one.** Under `asset`, six documents are right and three cells of one table are backwards. Under `liability`, three postings are right and six documents are wrong, and the chart of accounts turns out to have no cash account in it, which nobody has ever written down. **This entry ruled `asset` and section 1 is the weighing in full, weakest ground last.**

**WHAT IT COSTS IF THE ANSWER IS THE OTHER WAY.** Every firm-side posting moves in the opposite direction from the one [`0053`](../../packages/db/migrations/0053_firm_treasury_kind.sql) now permits. The repair is one more migration of the same shape superseding this constraint and this row, and one more ADR amending [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)'s three rows back. **Nothing else in the tree moves, because nothing posts these three transactions yet.** That is cheap, and it is cheap TODAY: the day a receiver posts `LT-07`, reversing this ruling costs compensating entries against an append-only table instead of a migration. **The argument for answering now is that the price only rises; it is not an argument for answering this way.**

**WHERE THIS ENTRY IS MOST LIKELY WRONG, named rather than left for a reader to find.** Ground (b) is the load-bearing one and it is an argument from silence: it says that because nobody recorded a missing cash account, no cash account is missing. **A founder who remembers writing the seven codes with `firm_treasury` as a pooled firm-level payable would overturn it in a sentence**, and the corpus contains one hint in that direction: [ADR-027](ADR-027.md) records that `LT-01`'s row originally read `credit firm_treasury trader_cents` in the slot that is now the per-identity `trader_wallet` payable, which is a LIABILITY slot. **This entry reads that as the same directional error [ADR-027](ADR-027.md) corrected**, and the other reading is that it is a trace of what the account was for.

**THE SECOND JUDGEMENT IS THAT SEEDING WAS RIGHT AT ALL, and refusing was available.** [ADR-177](ADR-177.md) refused to seed on this code and was right to, because it had not chosen. The case for seeding now is that the ruling's own content is *"prose was right and arithmetic was wrong"*, and leaving that in prose only would reproduce the exact failure it diagnoses: a statement in a document that the schema does not enforce and a posting can contradict. **The case against is that `E2` makes a judgement permanent in a way it does not make a derivation permanent**, and a founder may prefer the ruling to land as prose now and as a constraint after the read. **That is one migration number and no lost work.**

**THE THIRD IS THE FENCE.** [`lt-07.test.ts`](../../packages/rail/test/lt-07.test.ts) and [`settlement.ts`](../../packages/rail/src/settlement.ts) are in `packages/rail`, which this session does not own. They were edited because their assertions were ARMED to fire on this answer and did, so the alternative to updating them was a red suite. **[ADR-177](ADR-177.md) did the same thing for the same reason**, and a founder may prefer the armed-assertion update to land in its owner's review instead.
