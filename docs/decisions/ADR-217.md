## ADR-217: the wallet gains an `error` arm, and the boundary between "we do not serve this" and "this failed" is the contract's 404 ruling  (2026-08-29, status: proposed)

**The dispatch's premise was refuted at its source and the ruling is what replaced it.** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `217` says [`apps/portal/src/app/wallet/source.ts`](../../apps/portal/src/app/wallet/source.ts) "declares `REQUIRED_ENDPOINTS` naming both, defines the parse, defines a `WalletLoad` union with `ready` and `unavailable` arms, **and contains ZERO `fetch(` calls**", and concludes that the page "does not call it". **The first half is true, the second does not follow, and the page has been calling both endpoints since `96ed60c` on 2026-08-28, sixteen hours before the row was written.**

**`fetch(` IS THE WRONG NEEDLE AND THE CORPUS IS WHY.** [ADR-162](ADR-162.md) put the one `fetch(` in this application in [`src/http/client.ts`](../../apps/portal/src/http/client.ts) and `apps/portal/test/surface.test.ts` fails, by name and line, on a second file that grows one. **So every wired segment in this application contains zero `fetch(` calls, by construction and on purpose.** A needle the corpus deliberately holds at one file cannot distinguish a wired segment from an unwired one, and row `217` read the invariant as the defect.

**WHAT WAS ACTUALLY MISSING IS SMALLER, REAL, AND WORSE.** The wallet called both endpoints and then collapsed every outcome that was not a well-formed 200 into a single `unavailable` arm whose rendered sentence is *"Your wallet cannot be shown right now. This is a problem on our side and your balance is unaffected."* **A trader whose session expired read that sentence.** It is not a problem on our side, and the screen was authoring a fault that did not happen, on the surface where a trader decides whether to call support about their money.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `217`, session **407**, dispatched as `claude/portal-wallet-calls-its-api`. **The row's wiring half returns unspent.** The row is conditional and instructs that `217` be taken "only if a ruling is missing". A ruling is missing, and section 2 is the measurement that establishes it: the eight segments that read this API **disagree with each other four ways**.

- **NOT A MONEY PATH BY FILE AND MONEY-FACING BY CONTENT.** No migration, no `SD-nn`, no ledger, no engine, no payout write, no auth surface, and no write of any kind: `apps/portal` is a read client. **The clause worth a founder's eye is clause 3**, because it changes which sentence a trader reads on the screen that states their balance. This entry ships `proposed` with an **UNSIGNED** approval line. **Merging is not signing.**

- **WHAT IS MEASURED, WHAT IS DERIVED, AND WHAT IS JUDGED.** The segment inventory in section 2, the four verbatim copies of one doc sentence, the three spellings of one helper, and every count in section 6 are **MEASURED** over the tree at the moment of writing. That `fetch(` cannot serve as the wiring needle is **DERIVED** from ADR-162 and `surface.test.ts`. What is **JUDGED** is that 404 is the boundary (clause 2) and that a failure outranks an absence (clause 4).

- **A DISPATCH PROMPT IS NOT A SOURCE.** Every claim below was read at its own file. **Three of the row's premises did not survive** and they are in section 5.

**SO THE DISPATCH'S OWN TEST IS MET.** Row `217` says to "follow whichever pattern they already establish" and that "if the five disagree with each other, THAT is the thing to report". **They disagree on whether the arm exists (four yes, three no, one thrown), on what the payload is called (four names), and on how the classification is spelled (result-returning, throwing, inline, thrown-class).** No entry rules any of it: [ADR-162](ADR-162.md) foreclosure 1 recorded the gap on the payout centre and deferred it, and the four segments that later grew an arm each did so without a ruling.

### 3. Why ADR-162 deferred it and why that reason is spent

[ADR-162:91](ADR-162.md) is exact about the blocker:

> **1. THE PAYOUT CENTRE CANNOT SAY "THIS FAILED", AND THE REPAIR IS NOT ONE FILE.** [...] An error arm needs `./sections.ts` to render it and `./page.ts` to branch on it, and this session's fence holds neither, so it is reported here rather than reached for. **The client already carries what the arm would need**: `ApiFailure` has the `PortalErrorKind` and the status.

**The blocker was a fence and not a design problem.** Row `217`'s fence is `apps/portal/src/app/wallet/**`, which holds `source.ts`, `sections.ts` and `page.ts` -- **all three files ADR-162 named, for the first time in one session's fence.** Nothing else changed, and ADR-162's own last sentence says the client already carries the payload.

### 4. The ruling

**Clause 1. The `error` arm is the answer and the wallet takes it.** `WalletLoad` gains `{ kind: 'error' } & WalletFailure`. `./sections.ts` renders it and `./page.ts` branches on it through a `switch` on the discriminant rather than a ternary, so a fourth arm would be a compile error at the page.

**Clause 2. THE BOUNDARY IS 404, AND IT IS THE CONTRACT'S RULING RATHER THAN A PREFERENCE.** [API_CONTRACT:639](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) rules that *"An identity with no `wallet_entries` row is `0` and not a `404`"*, restated as [ADR-158](ADR-158.md) clause 9. **On this screen a 404 therefore cannot be a data condition; it can only be a route this deployment does not serve**, which is what `unavailable` has always meant here. So 404 keeps the `unavailable` arm and keeps naming WHICH endpoint. Everything that reached a server which then refused or failed -- 401, 429, 5xx, a transport that never reached a status line, a 2xx whose body the guard rejects -- is `error`.

**Clause 3. `unavailable` DID NOT CHANGE MEANING AND ITS OWN WORDS ARE NOW TRUE.** The arm's doc sentence has always read *"nothing is in flight, nothing is absent from a populated response, and nothing failed"*, and the third clause was false the moment the segment could fetch. Failures no longer arrive there, so the sentence describes the arm again. **This is the half of the ruling that fixes something a trader reads**, and `wallet.test.ts` asserts at the rendered bytes that a 401 produces "You are signed out" and does **not** produce "problem on our side".

**Clause 4. A FAILURE ON EITHER READ OUTRANKS AN ABSENCE ON THE OTHER, and the balance read wins a tie.** Two reads compose this screen and only one arm can render. The rule is the one that cannot state a falsehood: a 401 on the statement beside a 404 on the balance is a trader who is signed out, and rendering "this is a problem on our side" over that is the exact sentence the arm exists to stop. `GET /wallet` wins a tie because it is what `SC-M4-10` is; the statement is what it is composed with.

**Clause 5. THE PAYLOAD IS DERIVED AND NOT DECLARED.** `WalletFailure` is `Omit<ApiFailure, 'ok'>`. It adds no fifth hand-written copy, and a field added to `ApiFailure` reaches it where a hand-written copy would not notice. **The four existing copies should become this spelling and the three spellings of the helper should become one**, and neither is taken here: `accounts`, `calendar`, `(purchases)` and `referrals` are outside this fence. **Reported rather than repaired.**

**Clause 6. NO ARM RENDERS A FIGURE IT DID NOT RECEIVE, and the new one is held to it by test rather than by resemblance.** `wallet.test.ts` walks every member of `WALLET_ERROR_COPY` and asserts no `0.00` reaches the markup. A wallet screen that rendered `0.00` because a request failed would tell a trader their money is gone.

**Clause 7. THE ERROR COPY ADDS NO MEMBER TO `PortalErrorKind` AND WORDS NO REFUSAL OF PERMISSION.** `WALLET_ERROR_COPY` is `Readonly<Record<PortalErrorKind, string>>`, which is [`app/referrals/states.ts:93`](../../apps/portal/src/app/referrals/states.ts)'s and [`app/kyc/copy.ts:191`](../../apps/portal/src/app/kyc/copy.ts)'s mechanism: a member added to the union at [`app-shell.ts:77`](../../apps/portal/src/shell/app-shell.ts) and not here is `error TS2741`. **Three of the five members share one sentence deliberately.** `unexpected` is the 403 case, which `app-shell.ts` calls "FM-M4-10 firing" and "a rendering bug until proven otherwise", and `INV-M4-07` spends its whole row keeping a "forbidden" vocabulary off this screen: giving it its own sentence would tell a trader they lack permission to see their own money, on the reading of an alertable defect. A test asserts no sentence in the record contains "permission", "forbidden" or "not allowed".

### 5. What did not survive contact with the sources

1. **"the page does not call it" is false**, and had been false for sixteen hours when the row was written. `96ed60c` and `53a24a9` landed the segment's suites and its seam on 2026-08-28; row `217` was written in `62338b1` on 2026-08-29.
2. **"five fetch and two do not" is wrong in both terms.** Six of the seven `REQUIRED_ENDPOINTS` declarers call the client, and the population that matters is eight files rather than seven.
3. **`fetch(` cannot be the wiring needle at all**, because ADR-162 and `surface.test.ts` deliberately hold it at one file. **This matters beyond this row**: row `218` uses the identical needle on `app/sign-in/availability.ts` and happens to be right by accident.   **What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.**

  **THE FIRST THING TO DECIDE IS CLAUSE 2, BECAUSE IT IS THE ONE JUDGEMENT THAT COULD BE WRONG AND STILL PASS EVERY GATE.** The boundary rests on API_CONTRACT:639 meaning what it says on both wallet paths: that a 404 can never be a trader with an empty wallet. **It is checkable in a minute** -- read that line and `ADR-158` clause 9 -- and if the API can ever 404 either path for a reason that is not "unregistered", then a real refusal renders as a build defect and the arms are the wrong way round for that case. **The asymmetry the contract itself draws with [ADR-139](ADR-139.md) clause 6, where an account with no `rule_states` row IS absent, is the thing to weigh**: two adjacent reads in this corpus answer the same question differently on purpose, and this ruling leans entirely on the wallet being the one that does not.

  **THE SECOND IS CLAUSE 5, BECAUSE THIS ENTRY DECLINES A REPAIR IT HAS THE EVIDENCE FOR.** Four hand-written copies of one type and three spellings of one helper is the two-copies defect in the open, and the correct home -- `ApiFailure`, which already exists -- is one import away for all four. **This entry rules the spelling and repairs only the file inside its fence**, which leaves the corpus with five spellings instead of four until somebody holds a fence over `src/http/client.ts` and the four segments at once. **If that is the wrong trade, the thing to say is that a consolidation of this size gets its own session with a fence drawn for it**, and the slice is one type export plus four call sites.

  **THE THIRD IS WHETHER `payouts` AND `security` SHOULD HAVE MOVED IN THIS SESSION.** They carry the identical defect, `payouts` is the screen ADR-162 foreclosure 1 was actually written about, and both are one file each outside this fence. **This entry refused them on the fence rather than on the merits**, which is the standing rule and is also how a defect stays live in two places after the session that understood it. **The count worth knowing is that the wallet was the third of three and is now the first repaired.**

  **THE ONE OBJECTION WORTH CHECKING IN A MINUTE, because it is the strongest thing on the other side.** Row `217` said in terms **do not invent a third state without ruling it**, and a reader could reasonably ask whether a third state was needed at all rather than better copy on the existing one. **The answer is that the existing arm renders a LIST OF API PATHS** -- `GET /wallet`, `GET /wallet/entries` -- which is correct for a build defect and is a stack trace on a money screen for an expired session, so no rewording of one arm serves both readers. **If the founder disagrees, the cheaper answer is one arm with two copy sets** and clause 1 is where this entry chose otherwise.

  **WHERE THIS ENTRY IS MOST LIKELY WRONG, named rather than left for a reader to find.** **`not_found` is written into `WALLET_ERROR_COPY` and cannot be reached**, because clause 2 routes every 404 to the other arm.
