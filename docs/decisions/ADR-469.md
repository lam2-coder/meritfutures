### 3. The sweep: the method, and what it is measuring against

**The population is not "a line number in prose". It is a line number NO derivation in this corpus can see.** Three derivations exist and each was read rather than assumed:

| derivation | what it enumerates | where it reads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `git grep -oP '<file>[#:]L?\K[0-9]+'` | only an **adjacent** `path:NNN` | wherever it is pointed |
| `RI-15` | `path.ext:NNN`, plus a bare `` `:NNN` `` inheriting a path cited within `CITATION_INHERIT_LINES` = **6** lines above | every `.ts .tsx .mts .mjs .js` in the walk **outside `packages/tooling/`**, which is `OWN_PACKAGE` |
| `RI-16` | the same over tracked `.md` under `docs/` | `docs/` |

So a site is INVISIBLE when it carries a line number in one of the prose forms and no derivation above can reach it. Mechanically: the word form `line NNN` is invisible **always**, because every reader here parses a colon token and `line 270` has no colon; a bare `` `:NNN` `` or `at :NNN` is invisible unless a `path.ext:NNN` sits within 6 lines at or above it; and everything under `packages/tooling/` is invisible by `RI-15`'s own-package exclusion, which that check states is deliberate because its citations *"are the grammar WORKED EXAMPLES and the suite SEEDS rather than claims about this tree"*.

The instrument requires a filename, an ALL-CAPS document name, or the words `its own` / `this file` in the three-line neighbourhood, which is the dispatch's *"names a file in the sentence"*. It is a script, not a gate, it lives in the session scratchpad, and **nothing from it is committed**, on [ADR-450](ADR-450.md)'s precedent and `ADR-467` section 10's.

### 8. Why this row prices nothing, and the measurement that decides it

**No price is written and [`PRICE_REGISTER.md`](PRICE_REGISTER.md) is not touched**, so `price-register.mjs` is **GREEN on this branch** and section 9 records it. The dispatch anticipated red and permitted it; there is nothing to register.

**The self-pointer looked like the decidable subclass, and this row's own subject falsifies it.** *"its own line NNN"* reads as a claim about the containing file, and a rule binding it there would be mechanical and cheap. **In the site this row repaired, *"its own"* meant `write-guard-set.mjs`, the file named earlier in the sentence, not `refusal-naming.js`.** So even `its own` is anaphoric and needs the same resolution everything else needs. Measured at head, live source now carries **exactly one** `its own` + number site, [`price-register.mjs:81`](../../packages/tooling/checks/price-register.mjs), and it is a MENTION quoting the form as an example; the `this file's own` form has **one** site, `adapter.ts:81`, and that one does bind to its container. **One and one is not a population to build a checker on, and the two forms do not mean the same thing.**

**What this row offers instead of a price is the list.** Section 6 is the repaired set and section 7 is the survivors with their verdicts, at `file:line`. That is the artefact a checker would have produced, produced once, by hand, honestly, for the population that exists.

### 12. Approval

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.**

**THE FIRST IS THAT THIS ROW CONTRADICTED ITS OWN DISPATCH ON THE FACT THE DISPATCH RESTS ON.** The dispatch says every number previously written into the repaired comment *"was already false in the commit that wrote it"*. Section 2 shows this one was true in the commit that wrote it, by reading the blob at that commit. **The repair was made anyway, on grounds restated for the aged case**, and a founder may reasonably want the ruling itself re-examined now that its motivating fact is narrower than the dispatch believed. This entry says the aged case argues for deletion at least as strongly; that is an argument, not a measurement.

**THE SECOND IS THAT SIXTEEN LIVE-SOURCE COMMENTS WERE EDITED ON ONE ROW'S OWN ADJUDICATION.** Every verdict in section 6 is a judgement about what a sentence claims, checked against the named file at this base, and nothing mechanical confirms any of them. **Sixteen judgements is more than a founder can spot-check cheaply, and three are close calls**: `gates.ts` `:185` for a literal on `:186` and `:178` for a sentence on `:181` are drifts of one and three, and `RI-15`'s tuned window would have tolerated the first. They were repaired because the sentences assert a literal quote at a line that does not carry it. A founder may prefer the tolerance.

**THE THIRD IS THAT THE INTERESTING NUMBER IS THE ONE NOBODY CAN ACT ON.** Live source held 98 invisible sites and 16 of the 49 readable ones were dead: a defect rate near a third, in a class no check has ever looked at. Dated records hold **4,091**. **If that rate carries across, the corpus contains on the order of a thousand dead prose pointers that no ruling currently permits anyone to touch**, and the sampling to find out has not been done. This entry does not claim the rate carries; the two populations differ in kind, because a dated record's pointer was often true at its date and is not a defect at all. **Whether that distinction holds is exactly the founder's question in `ADR-467` section 12 item 3, and it is now a question about four thousand sites rather than five.**

**THE FOURTH IS THAT NO PRICE IS PROPOSED FOR A DEFECT THIS ROW JUST MEASURED AT A THIRD.** Section 8's ground is that the binding problem is anaphoric and that this row's own subject proves *"its own"* does not bind to its container. **That is one counterexample doing a lot of load-bearing work.** A founder who wants the class policed may reasonably ask for a checker restricted to `this file's own`, which has one site today and binds correctly, and accept that it would have caught none of the sixteen.
