Requiring the orders to match would be a real additional guarantee and it is not taken, because a reordering is not a defect this page has ever suffered and a gate that fails on a correct page gets switched off.

### 11. Where this entry is most likely wrong

- **THE THIRTEEN REPAIRED ARE THE POINTERS THIS ROW COULD PROVE WERE EXACT, NOT NECESSARILY EVERY POINTER THIS DIFF MOVED.** The test applied was whether the citing sentence's subject stood at the coordinate on `ebc21238`. **Where a sentence names its subject loosely, a second reader could call a pointer exact that this row called already-wrong**, and the direction of that error is toward repairing too few. The 47 sites are enumerated in section 6's method and the judgment is reproducible from them.
- **`normJob` IS THE WHOLE BINDING AND IT IS LOSSY BY DESIGN.** It strips parentheticals, so two jobs differing only inside brackets normalise to one key and the leg would read them as a duplicate. **No such pair exists on this tree**, and the leg would report a real page as defective on the day one is added. The alternative is matching on the raw cell, which is `CI-06a`'s 109 phantom anchors one table over, and this row took the same trade `CI-06l` already took.
- **THE DUPLICATE DIRECTION IS THIS ROW'S OWN ADDITION AND IT IS THE LEAST TESTED BY USE.** It is seeded and it fires, but unlike the two directions [ADR-389](ADR-389.md) derived from an observed failure, **nothing has yet been observed to fail this way.** It is a hole closed on reasoning about how the section is edited rather than on a defect anybody suffered.
- **SECTION 8 ITEM 1 IS A JUDGMENT ABOUT FENCING AND NOT A MEASUREMENT.** The gate now checks more than its STRATEGY row specifies. This row read that as better than the reverse and left the row for its owner, **but a reader who holds that the inventory is the specification would say this leg should not have landed until the row named it.**
- **THE `covers` LINE IS LONGER AND NOBODY READS IT.** The argument that a runner's `covers` keeps a gate honest rests on somebody reading it, and the only enforced reader is `list`.
