### 4. Can a control fire only on entries newer than a stated ref: YES, and `ADR-473`'s three grounds do not carry to it

`ADR-473` section 4 refused a gate on four grounds, of which three are load-bearing. **Each was tested against THIS control rather than inherited, and none of the three reaches it.** That is the whole of the argument for building one.

**GROUND ONE WAS THAT THE GATE CANNOT SEE ITS OWN MOTIVATING CASE.** That entry measured 3,114 path-keyed sites against 1,357 bare `` `:NNN` `` tokens reachable only by inheriting a path from a nearby line, and 15 against 37 inside `ADR-469` alone. **That is fatal to ENUMERATING a published list and it is harmless to DETECTING that an entry published one.** The corpus's dominant form is a path written ONCE and then a run of bare line tokens, so the entry is visible at its first site whatever happens to the other twelve. **This control needs one site per entry and the form that defeats enumeration supplies it by construction.** Measured: `ADR-469` is in this row's publishing population, `ADR-471` is, `ADR-473` is.

**GROUND TWO WAS THAT A GATE HAS NO REF TO READ AT**, and `ADR-473` measured the cost at 11.1 percent of sites classifying differently against the worktree. **This control opens no source file and resolves no ref.** It reads `docs/decisions/` and nothing else, and its question is whether the AUTHOR said which tree they read. Ground two has nothing to bite on.

**GROUND THREE WAS SCALE: 1,678 findings inside dated records.** Keyed forward this control's finding count inside dated records is **zero by construction**, and `legGrandfatheredIsPinned` is what makes that a measured claim instead of a hope: the 239 entries below the cutoff are counted on every run and pinned, so an emptied harvest goes red on the half of the population that cannot change.

**THE FOURTH GROUND IS THE ONE THAT DOES CARRY, AND IT IS `ADR-450` SECTION 6's FIRST RATHER THAN ITS SECOND.** *"A check that passes while the property it names is false"* is the real risk here, and it arrives as vacuity rather than as approximation: a forward-keyed control whose harvest breaks reports PASS on an empty population and nobody looks. **That is answered structurally and not by care.** `legDerivationIsReal` throws rather than reports, on four separate emptinesses, and the grandfathered pin is a second, independent tripwire on the half of the population that is frozen. **The shape is taken and not invented**, from [`tree-input-budget.mjs`](../../packages/tooling/checks/tree-input-budget.mjs), which asserts the budgeted cases AND the unbudgeted ones in the same run.

**AND `ADR-450` SECTION 6's THIRD GROUND HAS BEEN OVERTAKEN BY THE TREE, WHICH IS WORTH SAYING PLAINLY BECAUSE IT IS THE REASON THIS ROW COULD BUILD AT ALL.** That entry reported that *"this fence cannot register a check"*, because a new invariant only runs if its id is in the `CHECKS` array inside a file that row was told not to edit. **Four controls have landed since without touching it**: `write-guard-set.mjs`, `price-register.mjs`, `tree-input-budget.mjs` and `repairable-sites.mjs`. ### 5. The key is the ENTRY NUMBER, and git history was refused on a measurement rather than a preference

**5.1. THE OBVIOUS KEY IS THE FILE'S ADD-COMMIT AND IT FAILS SILENTLY, WHICH IS WORSE THAN FAILING.**

[`.github/workflows/ci.yml`](../../.github/workflows/ci.yml) runs this repository's suites in `CI-02` under a bare `actions/checkout@v4`, which is a clone **one commit deep**. Exactly one job asks for more: `CI-05` sets `fetch-depth: 0`, and its own comment says why, that a shallow clone has no history to scan and *"the gate would pass by being unable"* to read it.

**IN A SHALLOW CLONE `git log --diff-filter=A` DOES NOT FAIL. IT ANSWERS WITH THE GRAFT.** Measured in this session, on a clone 567 commits deep with 21 grafted boundaries:

- `docs/decisions/ADR-001.md` reports an add-commit of `c0d15f0c`, dated **2026-09-05**.
- `docs/decisions/ADR-100.md` reports the same commit and the same date.
- `457 of 457` entries returned an answer, no command failed, and **every answer older than the graft is wrong**.

Under `CI-02`'s actual checkout it is worse than wrong, it is uniform: with one commit fetched, every entry was added in that commit, so a history-keyed control classifies all 457 as new and goes red on all 233. **That is the permanently red gate this row was sent to avoid, arriving by a different road and wearing a plausible derivation.**

**5.2. SO THE KEY IS THE NUMBER IN THE FILENAME**, at [`citation-base-ref.mjs:153`](../../packages/tooling/checks/citation-base-ref.mjs). It costs no history, it is identical on every clone, and this corpus already spends real effort keeping it monotone and unique: [ALLOCATION.md](ALLOCATION.md), [ADR-034](ADR-034.md) and `CI-06f` exist for that and for nothing else. **An entry's number IS its position in the sequence, and it is already enforced by three things that are not this check.**

**THE CUTOFF IS DERIVED AND NOT CHOSEN.** It is one above the highest-numbered entry that publishes a source `file:line` and names no base, which is `ADR-416`, publishing one site. **`legCutoffIsTightest` at [`citation-base-ref.mjs:437`](../../packages/tooling/checks/citation-base-ref.mjs) holds it there**, so the cheapest way past a future finding, raising the cutoff above the entry that failed, requires the entry immediately below the new cutoff to publish and name nothing, which no entry above `ADR-416` does or now can.

**THE SENSITIVITY IS STATED BECAUSE IT IS REAL.** `ADR-416`'s single site is one bare pointer. If a harvest missed it the cutoff would fall to 404 and the forward population would grow rather than shrink, so the ruling is robust to that sensitivity even though the exact cutoff is not. **What is not sensitive is the finding: there is no unnamed publisher anywhere above 416.**

**5.3. THE PREDICATE IS ADJACENCY AND NOT SHAPE, AND THAT IS `ADR-467` TAKEN RATHER THAN RE-ARGUED.** A hex-shaped token alone is not a claim about a tree. Over the 53 entries whose opening lines carry one, the word immediately before it is **`at` 40 times, `off` 9, and `commit` and `from` once each**. The two remaining are `ADR-217`, behind *since*, which names another commit and not its own base, and `ADR-228`, whose token is **`9223372036854775807`**, the `bigint` ceiling, behind *naming*. **That is a cardinal being read as a commit, in the corpus, today**, and it is the exact class this estate keeps finding. [ADR-467](ADR-467.md) section 9 already ruled the axis: *"The productive line is not use against mention. It is adjacent against non-adjacent, and the corpus is already on the right side of it."* `NAMING` at [`citation-base-ref.mjs:188`](../../packages/tooling/checks/citation-base-ref.mjs) is that ruling applied, and it costs a future entry one word.

**5.4. RESOLVING THE NAMED REF WAS AVAILABLE AND IS REFUSED.** One line of `git cat-file -e` would report which named bases actually exist, and two entries' would not resolve in this clone. **It is refused because the verdict must not depend on how CI cloned.** A red that appears on a shallow clone and vanishes on a deep one is a red about the runner, and `CI-05`'s `fetch-depth: 0` comment is the same observation written from the other side.
