## ADR-075: the three extraction doors read `identities.status = 'active'`, and the two commerce doors are a different question  (2026-08-21, status: accepted)

**`OQ-062-01` has been open since [ADR-062](ADR-062.md) named it, restated by [ADR-068](ADR-068.md) section 7, carried by [FOLD-04](../plans/FOLD-04-impersonation-and-admin-parity.md), and renumbered `OQ-M20-07` by [M20](../plans/M20-wallet.md) when that module finally assembled the door inventory. It has been recorded four times and owned by nobody, because the file it lives in was never in the fence of the session that asked. This entry closes the half of it that is a live hole and refuses to close the half that is a policy question wearing the same clothes.**

- **Context:** [ADR-062](ADR-062.md) section 1 rules the payout gate as `identities.status = 'active'`. Four other doors read the same column the other way. [`0001_extensions_and_enums.sql:27`](../../packages/db/migrations/0001_extensions_and_enums.sql) declares `identity_status AS ENUM ('active', 'restricted', 'closed')` and [ADR-041](ADR-041.md) refused a fourth value, so **`closed` is the witness that separates the two shapes**: it fails `= 'active'` and passes `<> 'restricted'`.

  **`grep identities.status packages/rules-engine/src` returns zero hits.** No code implements any of these gates. That is what makes this ruling cheap today and is the same argument `ADR-062` made when it was the one door being moved.

---

### 1. The door inventory, and the split that decides the ruling

**[M20](../plans/M20-wallet.md) assembled this and it is the contribution that made the question answerable.** Five doors read `identities.status`. One is written one way and four the other.

| Door | Site | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| Payout request | `G-ELIGIBLE`, [STATE_MACHINES](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) section 10 | **extraction**, already `= 'active'` |
| External withdrawal, at approval | `G-WITHDRAWAL-CLEARED`, same table | **extraction** |
| Wallet spend and withdrawal, at request | `INV-M20-06`, [M20](../plans/M20-wallet.md) | **extraction** |
| Purchase and reset | [M03](../plans/M03-billing-checkout.md) `INV-M3-15` | commerce |
| Affiliate statement settlement | [M08](../plans/M08-affiliate-system.md) `INV-M8-12` | commerce |

**M20's own sentence is the one to keep: the four doors are not one kind of door.** A `closed` identity that can still **take money out** is a hole. A `closed` identity that can still **buy** is a policy question with two defensible answers. Answering both with one predicate because they share a column is how a consistency argument produces a decision nobody made.

### 2. The ruling: extraction only

> **The three extraction doors read `identities.status = 'active'`.** `G-ELIGIBLE` already does. `G-WITHDRAWAL-CLEARED` and `INV-M20-06` are amended to match by this entry.

**This closes `OQ-062-01` exactly.** The question was *"a `closed` identity cannot request a payout and can still spend its wallet"*, and wallet spend is extraction. After this entry, **no `closed` identity can move value out through any door.**

**Two reasons, in order of weight.**

1. **`<>` fails open and `=` fails closed on every value the enum gains later.** A fourth `identity_status` value passes `<> 'restricted'` silently on the day it is added and fails `= 'active'` loudly on the same day. `ADR-041` refusing `suspended` in August is not a promise about the enum. **The form that breaks safely when a stranger widens a type is the form an outbound door takes**, and that is `ADR-062`'s argument applied to the doors it could not reach.
2. **`INV-M20-06`'s own first clause requires it.** The invariant opens *"every context gate that blocks the external leg blocks wallet spend too"*, and [M20:286](../plans/M20-wallet.md) calls the shared-gate rule *"the module's most important single line"*. **A payout gate strictly narrower than the wallet gate is that rule failing in the direction the invariant exists to prevent.** The amendment makes the invariant true of itself.

### 3. What is NOT ruled, and why refusing is the substance rather than the omission

> **The two commerce doors keep their current shape. `OQ-075-01` is open: may a `closed` identity purchase, and must an affiliate statement settle to one?**

**They are left alone because the obvious move is wrong on at least one of them.** [M20](../plans/M20-wallet.md) `INV-M20-09` says a wallet balance is *"payable on demand forever"* and that dormancy never forfeits it, and calls a forfeiture clause on money already earned **the single most brand-destroying term available to this firm**. An affiliate statement is money already earned. **Moving `INV-M8-12` to `= 'active'` would withhold it from a closed identity permanently**, which is that clause arriving through a consistency sweep rather than through a decision.

**Purchase is the opposite risk and also not obvious.** A `closed` identity that can still buy a new evaluation is a closure that undoes itself at checkout. That argues for `= 'active'`. But `closed` also covers voluntary closure, and the two readings differ on whether the trader is being protected or excluded.

**Neither is a hole today**, because neither moves value **out**. That is what makes leaving them safe, and it is the whole reason the extraction half could be ruled without them.

### 4. Why this entry exists at all rather than a fifth restatement

**Four documents have now recorded this question and none could close it**, each for the same structural reason: the session that asks does not hold [M20](../plans/M20-wallet.md). **A finding recorded four times and owned by nobody is on its way to being treated as handled**, which `ADR-068` section 7 said in those words while itself being unable to fix it. This entry is written from a session whose fence was extended to hold the file, which is the only thing that was ever missing.

---

- **Decision:** `G-WITHDRAWAL-CLEARED` and `INV-M20-06` are amended to read `identities.status = 'active'`, matching `G-ELIGIBLE`. **`OQ-062-01` and its `OQ-M20-07` restatement are CLOSED.** The two commerce doors are unchanged and carry `OQ-075-01`.

- **Alternatives considered:**

  - **Move all five doors to `= 'active'`** (rejected, section 3). It is the tidy answer and it silently withholds an affiliate settlement that was already earned. A consistency sweep is not a mandate to decide a forfeiture.
  - **Move the payout gate back to `<> 'restricted'`** (rejected). It restores one shape at the cost of the safer one, and `ADR-062` already ruled the direction on the fail-open argument. Reversing it here would be re-litigating a ruling this entry depends on.
  - **Leave `OQ-062-01` open and record it a fifth time** (rejected, section 4). That is the status quo, and the status quo is a live extraction hole whose only protection is that no code implements the gate yet.

- **Consequences:**

  - [M20](../plans/M20-wallet.md) `INV-M20-06` and [STATE_MACHINES](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) `G-WITHDRAWAL-CLEARED` are amended. Both are frozen documents, which is why this is an ADR and not a commit.
  - **No migration.** No code implements these gates, so there is nothing to supersede; the cost of this ruling is one predicate in each of two documents.
  - **`OQ-075-01` is open and it is owned by this entry**, numbered here rather than in `M03` or `M08` because taking `OQ-M3-nn` or `OQ-M8-nn` would allocate into a namespace another session may hold, which is the collision shape [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) records three times.
  - **A future fourth `identity_status` value is now refused by three outbound doors rather than admitted by two of them.**

- **Founder approval: GRANTED 2026-08-21.** Given by the founder in session 95 as an **explicit delegated approval**, not as a line-by-line read, and this entry is **money path**, so that distinction is recorded rather than glossed: [E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) reserves the line-by-line read for exactly this class and it has not happened. **What makes the grant defensible is that the ruling is strictly narrowing and reverses nothing.** `= 'active'` admits a subset of what `<> 'restricted'` admitted, so no identity is paid, and no identity spends, who would not have before. The founder should still read section 3, because the half this entry declined to rule is the half where the next mistake lives.
