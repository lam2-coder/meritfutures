### 5. What was written instead, and why no number is spent on it

#### 5.1 The remedy is [ADR-034](ADR-034.md)'s and it was named for this exact class in this exact table

#### 5.2 Why [ADR-337](ADR-337.md)'s argument for leg 4 does NOT transfer, which is the honest answer to the question the dispatch put

**AND THE `CI-06<letter>` SERIES IS CLOSED AT `w`** ([ADR-131](ADR-131.md)), enforced by `CI-06/closed-letter-series`. **No letter is proposed and none is needed**: `CI-06g` already governs every generated span and this is one more of them.

### 6. What the register cannot report, in its own words

**THREE THINGS, AND THE FIRST IS THE ONE A READER WHO WANTED A GATE WILL MIND.**
