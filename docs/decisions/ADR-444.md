### 5. THE RULING

**A CLAIM ABOUT WHAT THIS TREE'S SUITE DOES OR DOES NOT VERIFY IS WRITTEN IN EXACTLY ONE PLACE AND CITED EVERYWHERE ELSE.** This is `RI-15`'s move made for a claim instead of for a pointer, and `RI-14`'s idiom governs the transition at each site.

#### 5.1 Where the canonical statement lives

**IN LIVE SOURCE, IN THE DECLARATION WHOSE READERS THE CLAIM IS FOR. NOT IN AN ADR.** For this claim that is **limit 1 of `CatalogRow`**, [`packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts:3466`](../../packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts), whose docblock already carried the numbered limits every one of the sixteen sites was paraphrasing.

**THE ARGUMENT AGAINST AN ADR IS THE CORPUS'S OWN AND IT IS DECISIVE.** [ADR-386:169](ADR-386.md) rules that a pointer in a DATED RECORD is NAMED rather than repaired, because a dated record cannot be corrected by a commit; [ADR-432](ADR-432.md) section 10 item 1 is off by one through four entries and is deliberately left that way. **A claim whose whole defect is that it goes false when the tree moves is the worst possible tenant of a document that may never be edited.** The canonical statement has to be somewhere a commit can fix it, which means live source.

**AND THE SITE WAS NOT INVENTED.** Three of the sixteen already called it *"`CatalogRow`'s own limit 1"* or *"ADR-303's OWN LIMIT 1"*. The tree had already converged on a canonical NAME for this fact and was restating its content anyway. The ruling adopts the name the tree chose and forbids the restatement.

**WHAT WAS DISPLACED, SAID PLAINLY.** Fitting the canonical statement into limit 1 without moving a cited line (section 9) cost the sentence *"the limit this file exists to carry rather than the ADR"*. Its content is not lost; it is promoted into this ruling, which is that sentence generalised.

#### 5.2 What a citing site may say, and what it must not

**MAY:** state the RULING that justifies its own code. Every one of the sixteen sites is standing in front of a `throw` or a cast, and what keeps that code is [ADR-299](ADR-299.md) section 5.1 item 5, *"a type derived from a TRANSCRIPTION does not retire a runtime check"*. **That is a ruling, not a measurement, and it does not decay.** It stays at every site.

**MAY:** name the canonical statement's location, as a `file:line` citation with the declaration's name beside it, so `RI-15` binds it.

**MUST NOT:** restate what the suite compares or does not compare. No list of legs, no *"TYPE and NULLABILITY for every column of every registered non-view relation"*, no *"DEFAULT is compared nowhere"*. **This is the clause with teeth and it is the clause [ADR-440](ADR-440.md)'s repair fails.**

**AND WHERE A SITE PREVIOUSLY CARRIED A FALSE CLAIM**, `RI-14` governs: the struck sentence is KEPT beside its correction, and **the correction is a CITATION rather than a replacement claim.** Replacing a false measurement with a true measurement is what produced the fourth instance of this defect class; replacing it with a pointer cannot.

#### 5.3 How a reader gets from a citing site to the canonical one

One hop, and it is checked. Every citing site carries the same greppable form, **`stated ONCE at limit 1 of `CatalogRow` (`packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts:3466`)`**, so `grep -rn "stated ONCE at limit 1"` enumerates the whole citing population in one line, and `RI-15` asserts of every copy that the path resolves, that the file reaches the line, that the line is not blank or a bare closing brace, and that `CatalogRow` is declared there.

### 6. The alternative priced: a runner that checks every copy

**IT CANNOT BE GRAMMAR-KEYED, AND THAT IS RULED RATHER THAN ARGUED HERE.** `ADR-442` section 9 and the census's own header both hold that a gate over this grammar is satisfiable by REWORDING, which converts an honest sentence into a liability. Section 3.1 of this entry is the empirical form of the same point: **one missing word, `nowhere`, hides two of three sites from the instrument built for exactly this class.**

**SO THE ONLY RUNNER THAT WORKS IS A PROBE PER CLAIM, AND ITS COST SCALES WITH THE COPIES.** On [ADR-440](ADR-440.md) section 9.1's own shape, one artifact plus one register row per site: **sixteen rows on this base, not five.** `ADR-442` section 8 built it as described and measured it landing **16 findings red**, needing `apps/worker`, `apps/api` and `packages/db` in one fence to land green, which is three deployables and a package.

**AND THE DECISIVE OBJECTION IS NOT THE PRICE. IT IS THAT THE RUNNER DOES NOT REMOVE THE COPIES.** Sixteen true copies still go false together on the day the suite changes. A register tells you sixteen times, and it must be edited in the same commit by the same author who just typed the sentence, which is `ADR-442` section 16's own counter to itself. **The convention removes the multiplicity.** After it, the population a probe would have to watch is ONE, and a probe over one site is trivially affordable by whoever owns the suite.

**THE TWO ARE THEREFORE NOT ALTERNATIVES AND THIS ENTRY DOES NOT TREAT THEM AS SUCH.** The convention is what makes the control affordable later. That is the strongest form of the case and it is a synthesis rather than a choice.

### 7. The counterfactual, which is this chain's own fourth instance

**[ADR-440](ADR-440.md) REPAIRED TWO FALSE SENTENCES AND CLOSED BOTH REPAIRS WITH A NEW SENTENCE OF THE SAME CLASS, AND THAT SENTENCE IS FALSE ON THIS BASE.** Both closed *"DEFAULT is compared nowhere"* while [ADR-438](ADR-438.md) had already landed the DEFAULT comparison. `ADR-442` section 6 found it by reading.

**THE CONVENTION WOULD HAVE PREVENTED IT AND THE REGISTER WOULD NOT.** Under section 5.2 that closing clause is a RESTATEMENT and is not permitted at a citing site at all; the repair would have been a pointer, and a pointer cannot be false about a comparison it does not describe. Under a register, the clause is a new claim needing a new probe, written by the author who had just typed it. **This is the one counterfactual in this chain that discriminates between the two answers, and it discriminates in the convention's favour.**
