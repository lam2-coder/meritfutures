## ADR-033: The reviewer subagent is a citation check, not an adversarial one  (2026-08-14, status: proposed)

- **Context:** [GUIDE_BRIEFING.md:27](../GUIDE_BRIEFING.md) states the problem this ADR addresses: **"Claude Code cannot be its own reviewer."** The corpus answered it with the guide chat, a review desk whose standing instruction is to **disagree** ([GUIDE_BRIEFING.md:35](../GUIDE_BRIEFING.md)). That desk works and is unchanged. What 2026-08-14 established is that **disagreement is the wrong instrument for the errors that actually reach committed documents**, and that a second, narrower reviewer is needed beside it. Constitution [C10](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) already requires the split in principle: **"the writer/reviewer split runs review in a fresh session/subagent with its own system prompt (the agent doing the work is never the one grading it)"** ([MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md:375](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md)). It was never instantiated. This ADR instantiates it.
- **Decision, two parts, and only the first is implemented.**

  **(1) A `reviewer` subagent exists at [`.claude/agents/reviewer.md`](../../.claude/agents/reviewer.md), and its job is citation.** Its system prompt loads [GUIDE_BRIEFING.md](../GUIDE_BRIEFING.md) and this file. On any diff or plan its task is: **for every factual claim (parameter value, column name, enum member, invariant reference, ADR citation, count, cross-reference, quoted text) locate the primary source and quote `file:line`. Any claim that cannot be cited to a primary source IS the finding.** It does not assess whether the reasoning is sound. It assesses whether the facts are real. Verdicts are written to `docs/reviews/<item>-verdict.md`.

  **(2) A `Stop` hook requiring a current verdict before a money-path session may end is PROPOSED and deliberately NOT IMPLEMENTED.** See the section below. It is a separate ruling because implementing it reverses a recorded decision.

### The five errors, on one day, and what caught each

All five occurred on 2026-08-14 during the schema-delta reconciliation and are recorded on the unmerged branch `claude/schema-delta-reconciliation`, cited here by their location on that branch. **Four of the five landed on a single ledger posting, LT-01.**

It was folded into GLOSSARY, DATA_MODEL and M05, and **committed** | Collapsed two positions that move by **different magnitudes in the same transaction** (`approved_cents` against `trader_cents`) into one | **Re-reading M05's own text**, which stated the split explicitly and had been read past |

**A count note, recorded rather than smoothed, because this ADR is about citation discipline and would be a poor advertisement for it otherwise.** This session was briefed on **three** unauthorized float exemptions. The record carries **two** columns that shipped outside the authorization (`numerator`, `denominator`), plus a **third non-integer column, `published_statistics.value_numeric`, which is authorized and was left authorized** while `DELTA_MANIFEST` section 9 notes it does not appear to need the exemption. Three non-integer columns exist; two were unauthorized. The difference is immaterial to the rationale and is stated because **an uncited number in an ADR about uncited numbers is the finding**.

### Why an adversarial reviewer would have caught none of them

**Every one of the five was plausible and internally consistent.** Error 1 was a ruling the founder made on a correct-looking reading of two documents, folded correctly by the author. Error 3 books a real account for a real reason and is wrong only against a timing rule stated elsewhere. Error 4 had **the right magnitude and the right conclusion** and was written backwards. A reviewer asked whether the argument convinces would have been convinced, because the arguments were convincing.

**Each was caught by contact with a primary source**: the module plan's own sentence, the chart of accounts, the recognition ruling, the sign convention, the schema's actual column list. Two of the five were caught by **contact with the DDL**, which is the sharpest form of the same thing: **a migration applies or it does not, and it cannot be talked past.**

The constitution already says why: **"Looks confident is not a signal. AI mistakes arrive well-formatted with explanatory comments; reviewer instincts trained on messy human errors mis-fire on polished-but-wrong code. Review against the plan doc's acceptance criteria with the spec open in a parallel window: requirement fidelity, not code aesthetics"** ([MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md:396](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md), [E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md)). **The reviewer defined here is that sentence made into a subagent.** It is the parallel window, and nothing else.

**This is also why the reviewer must not be given an opinion.** A reviewer that ranks findings by severity, recommends fixes, or argues about designs will spend its attention where the argument is interesting, and the errors above were not interesting. They were boring, local, and load bearing. **A citation check is cheap enough to run on everything, and its value comes precisely from being mechanical.**

### Scope, stated as limits

| | |
|---|---|
| **Founder gates are unchanged** | Human diff-reads on `rules-engine/`, `payout/` and `ledger/` paths remain unconditional per constitution [C7](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) and [E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md). **A PASS verdict is an input to the E2 read, never a substitute for it**, and the agent definition says so in those words |
| **Strategy stays in the guide chat** | Rulings, economics, brand posture and whether a design is right belong to the founder and the guide ([GUIDE_BRIEFING.md](../GUIDE_BRIEFING.md)). The reviewer is silent on all of it, and its silence is not agreement |
| **The reviewer never edits** | It reports; the author fixes. A reviewer that fixes has authored the thing it is grading, which is the split [C10](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) exists to preserve |
| **Verdicts are artifacts** | `docs/reviews/<item>-verdict.md`, overwritten per item rather than accumulated. Four statuses only: **CITED, UNCITED, CONTRADICTED, UNRESOLVABLE**. PASS requires zero UNCITED and zero CONTRADICTED |

### PROPOSED, NOT IMPLEMENTED: a `Stop` hook requiring a current verdict on money paths

**The proposal.** A `Stop` hook that, when the session's diff touches a money path, checks for a verdict at `docs/reviews/<item>-verdict.md` that is current for the head commit, and **blocks the turn from ending** without one.

**Why it is proposed rather than written.** Constitution [C10](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) mandates exactly this shape (**"Stop -> completion gate ... blocks the turn from ending until green"**, [MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md:371](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md)). The committed hook set does the opposite **on purpose**: [CLAUDE.md:22](../../CLAUDE.md) records that both hooks **"report failure loudly and exit zero rather than blocking, so a network outage cannot wedge a session."** That is a decision, made and written down. **Implementing a blocking hook would reverse it silently, which is the failure mode this whole ADR exists to prevent**, so it is put in front of the founder instead.

**The tension to rule on, stated once:** C10 says hooks are law and a `Stop` gate must block; the committed set says a hook must never be able to wedge a session. Both are correct about different failure modes. A blocking verdict gate makes an unreviewed money-path diff impossible to end a session on, **and** makes a session unendable if the reviewer is unavailable, the item name does not match, or the container has no network. **Any implementation therefore needs a ruled answer to "what happens when the check itself cannot run", and that answer is a founder decision rather than an implementation detail.**

- **Alternatives considered:**
  - **An adversarial reviewer subagent** (the obvious shape, and rejected on the evidence above: all five errors were plausible, internally consistent, and would have survived it. It also duplicates the guide chat, which already disagrees and does it better with the founder in the loop).
  - **No subagent, rely on the guide chat and the E2 read** (rejected: the guide reads summaries rather than diffs, and four of the five errors lived in a single ledger posting inside a 7,000-line migration set. The E2 read catches them and is the founder's scarcest resource; spending it on facts a mechanical check can verify is the wrong use of it).
  - **CI assertions only** (rejected as a replacement, adopted as a complement: LEDGER-C1, LEDGER-C2 and the no-floats `DO` block are the right control for the two shapes that already occurred, and they are strictly better than review because they cannot be forgotten. They cannot generalize to "this document cites an ADR that does not say this", which is most of the surface).
  - **Implementing the blocking `Stop` hook now** (rejected for this session: it reverses [CLAUDE.md:22](../../CLAUDE.md)'s recorded decision and needs the founder's answer on the fail-open question).
- **Consequences:**
  - `.claude/agents/reviewer.md` is committed and [INDEX.md](../INDEX.md) carries it. `docs/reviews/` is created by the first verdict.
  - **The reviewer is available immediately and is not yet mandatory anywhere.** Until the hook question is ruled, invoking it is a session's judgment, and the natural first use is the sixteen money-path migration files awaiting the E2 read.
  - **The ADR numbering skips 026 to 030**, which are claimed by the unmerged `claude/schema-delta-reconciliation` branch. This entry is 031 so the two branches do not collide on a number. Both branches append to the end of this file, so a merge conflict here is expected and resolves by keeping both blocks in numerical order.
  - **A verdict is evidence and is subject to the evidence doctrine** ([MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md:374](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md)): a PASS with no quoted lines under it is not a PASS, and the verdict format requires the cited claims be listed so the enumeration can be audited rather than trusted.
