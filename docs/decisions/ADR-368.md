### 2. THE RULING

### 6. What survives as a rule, and it is two rules rather than one

**BOTH ENTRIES COLLAPSED TWO INDEPENDENT PROPERTIES INTO ONE SENTENCE, WHICH IS WHY ONE SENTENCE COULD NOT CARRY.**
