## ADR-327: the schema the application role could not see. The privilege set is measured rather than reasoned, `CREATE` is refused and nothing needed it, and the sentence saying otherwise is superseded in a file nobody may edit  (2026-09-05, status: proposed)

**[ADR-326](ADR-326.md) section 3.3 made a ruling it could not execute and named its own residue: *"What is NOT ruled is the exact privilege set ... the honest way to enumerate that is to run `start()` against the applied schema under the role and read what it refuses, which needs an estate this row does not have. The migration that writes the grant owes that measurement."*** This entry is that measurement and [`0082_pgboss_app_grants.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0082_pgboss_app_grants.sql) is that migration.

**THE SET IS SIXTEEN TERMS AND EVERY ONE OF THEM WAS WATCHED BEING NEEDED.** `pgBossQueue` was driven against `0001`..`0082` under `SET ROLE merit_app` through all five of `JobQueue`'s methods and all three background pollers: **132 statements, zero permission errors**. Then each granted term was REVOKED in turn and the run repeated: **all sixteen produce `permission denied`**. A privilege ruling justified by reasoning rather than by a transcript is what [ADR-326](ADR-326.md) refused to make, and section 3 below is the transcript.

**`CREATE` WAS NOT NEEDED, SO THE RULING STANDS UNDER MEASUREMENT RATHER THAN ON TRUST.** No statement pg-boss issues under this interface's configuration asks for DDL in the `pgboss` schema. The application role keeps the no-DDL property [`0026:64`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql) gives it on `public`, on a schema sitting inside the PITR boundary that protects the ledger.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `327`, branch `claude/a-schema-it-cannot-see`, session **518**. [ADR-305](ADR-305.md) section 7 slice 8's remainder, on [ADR-326](ADR-326.md) section 3.3 and [ADR-318](ADR-318.md).

- **MONEY BY WHAT IT GOVERNS AND NOT BY WHAT IT TOUCHES.** Not one cent moves and no ledger column changes. What lands is a widening of what the application role can REACH inside the ledger's restore boundary, which is [ADR-165](ADR-165.md)'s precedent and [`0079`](../../packages/db/migrations/0079_pgboss_job_store.sql)'s own classification of the schema. **`E2` READ OWED**, with the reads on [ADR-326](ADR-326.md), [ADR-318](ADR-318.md) and [ADR-305](ADR-305.md) still owed under it. The entry ships `proposed` with an **UNSIGNED** approval line. **Merging is not signing.**

- **NOTHING IS SCHEDULED AND NOTHING IS WIRED.** No job gained a clock, [ADR-326](ADR-326.md) section 4 stands unmoved, and the wired counts do not move: **ten wired of twenty-four declared, fourteen blocked.** The `@merit/queue` manifest line lands; **no module imports it**, and section 5 is why that half could not land with it.

### 2. The correction, and why it lives here rather than in the file that is wrong

**TWO FILES CARRY THE SAME FALSE SENTENCE.** [`0079`](../../packages/db/migrations/0079_pgboss_job_store.sql)'s header, finding 1: *"pg-boss's `create_queue` runs `CREATE TABLE pgboss.%I`, so making the queue usable by the application role means granting CREATE on a schema inside the ledger's restore boundary"*. [`probe_pgboss_job_store.sql`](../../scripts/db/probe_pgboss_job_store.sql)'s header said it again.

**THE FIRST CLAUSE IS TRUE AND THE SECOND DOES NOT FOLLOW.** At [`0079:459-463`](../../packages/db/migrations/0079_pgboss_job_store.sql):

```sql
IF queue_created_on IS NULL OR options->>'partition' IS DISTINCT FROM 'true' THEN
  RETURN;
END IF;
EXECUTE format('CREATE TABLE pgboss.%I (LIKE pgboss.job INCLUDING DEFAULTS)', tablename);
```

The `EXECUTE` is below the `RETURN`. **A queue that is not partitioned never reaches it**, and this workspace declares no partitioned queue: `declareQueue` calls `boss.createQueue(queue)` with no second argument, `manager.createQueue` defaults `options` to `{}` and sends `{policy: 'standard'}`, and `PgBossQueueOptions` has two fields, `schema` and `applicationName`, neither of which is a queue option. **So the partitioned arm is unreachable from this workspace by TYPE and not only by habit.**

**`0079` IS MERGED AND IS NOT EDITED.** Migrations are sacred: once merged, never edited, only superseded (constitution `E2`). The correction therefore lives in `0082`'s header, which names the sentence and quotes it, and here. **The probe is a script rather than a migration and IS rewritten**, in place, with the old sentence quoted inside the paragraph that retires it.

**AND THE CORRECTION IS ENFORCED RATHER THAN WRITTEN DOWN.** `REJECTION 6` of the probe runs `pgboss.create_queue('probe-partitioned-as-app', '{"partition":true}')` as `merit_app` and asserts `insufficient_privilege`, and asserts that the queue row the function inserts BEFORE the `CREATE TABLE` is gone with the subtransaction. **A partitioned queue is refused by the database even if somebody writes one**, which is a stronger statement than "no caller passes that option today".

### 3. THE MEASUREMENT

#### 3.1 How it was taken

`0001`..`0082` applied forward-only from empty under `ON_ERROR_STOP=1` against **PostgreSQL 16.13**. `pgBossQueue` was constructed over a `pg` client that had run `SET ROLE merit_app`, so every statement is checked against the role the application connects as, and driven through `start()`, `declareQueue`, an `enqueue` inside a caller's open transaction, `consume`, a wait long enough for every background poller to run, and `stop()`. The executor logged every statement and every error, so a failure a poller swallows into an `error` event is still recorded.

**THE REACHABLE STATEMENT SET IS BOUNDED BY THE INTERFACE AND THAT IS WHY THIS IS A MEASUREMENT AND NOT A SAMPLE.** [`pg-boss-queue.ts`](../../packages/queue/src/pg-boss-queue.ts) fixes `migrate: false` and `useListenNotify: false` and passes no other pg-boss option, so the library defaults it does not name are in force and cannot be changed through `PgBossQueueOptions`: `supervise` and `schedule` are **ON**, `persistQueueStats` and `persistWarnings` are **OFF**.

#### 3.2 What it found

| Grants in force | Result |
|---|---|
| none, which is `0001`..`0081` | **every method throws.** `start()` fails at its first statement, `SELECT to_regclass('pgboss.version')`, with `permission denied for schema pgboss` |
| `USAGE ON SCHEMA pgboss` alone | `start()` fails at `permission denied for table version`; `declareQueue` and `enqueue` at `permission denied for table queue` |
| `0082` as written | **132 statements attempted, ZERO permission errors, zero background-poller errors** |

**AND THE NARROWNESS WAS PROVED BY BREAKING IT SIXTEEN TIMES.** Each granted term was revoked on its own, the whole run repeated, and the term restored:

| Revoked | The run reports |
|---|---|
| `USAGE ON SCHEMA pgboss` | `permission denied for schema pgboss` |
| `SELECT` / `UPDATE` on `pgboss.version` | `permission denied for table version` |
| `SELECT` / `INSERT` / `UPDATE` on `pgboss.queue` | `permission denied for table queue` |
| `SELECT` on `pgboss.schedule` | `permission denied for table schedule` |
| `SELECT` / `INSERT` / `UPDATE` on `pgboss.job` | `permission denied for table job` |
| `SELECT` / `INSERT` / `UPDATE` / `DELETE` on `pgboss.job_common` | `permission denied for table job_common` |
| `SELECT` / `DELETE` on `pgboss.job_dependency` | `permission denied for table job_dependency` |

#### 3.3 THE TRAP IN THE LOOP, WHICH FIRED, AND WHICH IS THE FINDING WORTH CARRYING

**`DELETE ON pgboss.job_dependency` CAME BACK SPARE ON THE FIRST PASS AND IT IS NOT SPARE.** `cleanupDependencies` runs only inside the supervisor's MAINTAIN phase, and `trySetQueueDeletionTime` gates that phase on `pgboss.queue.maintain_on` being older than `maintenanceIntervalSeconds`, which is **86400** at the library default. The FIRST iteration of the loop set `maintain_on = now()`, so every later iteration skipped the maintain phase and the statement that needs that privilege never ran. With `UPDATE pgboss.queue SET maintain_on = NULL` before the run, revoking that one term fails at `permission denied for table job_dependency` like the other fifteen.

**A PRIVILEGE MEASURED AGAINST A LEASE SOMEBODY ELSE ALREADY TOOK READS AS A PRIVILEGE NOBODY NEEDS.** A loop that did not reset the lease would have produced a grant one privilege too narrow, and the failure would have arrived in a deployable, once a day, in a maintenance pass nobody watches. **The direction of the error is the point:** a too-wide grant is caught by review, and a too-narrow one is caught by an outage.

#### 3.4 The ruled set, and what each term is for

| Object | Granted | The path that needs it | Refused, and why |
|---|---|---|---|
| schema `pgboss` | `USAGE` | the first statement of `start()` | **`CREATE`.** DATA_MODEL section 14, restated on a schema inside the ledger's restore boundary |
| `pgboss.version` | `SELECT`, `UPDATE` | `Contractor.check()`; the `flow_on` and `cron_on` leases | `INSERT` makes the pin ambiguous; `DELETE` turns every later `start()` into `pg-boss is not installed` |
| `pgboss.queue` | `SELECT`, `INSERT`, `UPDATE` | the queue cache; `create_queue`; the `monitor_on` and `maintain_on` leases and the counter cache | **`DELETE`.** `pgboss.delete_queue` deletes from this table, `JobQueue` publishes no method that calls it, and a deployable that can drop a queue can drop the jobs enqueued against it |
| `pgboss.schedule` | `SELECT` | the cron monitor reads it at `start()` and every 30 seconds | `INSERT` and `UPDATE`. [ADR-241](ADR-241.md) rules the schedule EXTERNAL, so this table is empty and stays empty; a write here is a second, in-process scheduler |
| `pgboss.job` | `SELECT`, `INSERT`, `UPDATE` | the flow poller's `FOR UPDATE` and its `pending_dependencies` write; `INSERT` is the dead-letter arm of the timeout and heartbeat sweeps, **planned and therefore permission-checked on every supervisor pass even with no dead letter configured** | `DELETE`. Every delete pg-boss issues names the partition |
| `pgboss.job_common` | `SELECT`, `INSERT`, `UPDATE`, `DELETE` | the DEFAULT partition, where every job lands. `enqueue`, the fetch, the completion, the retention pass and the delete-and-reinsert of an expired job | nothing |

| `bam`, `warning`, `queue_stats`, `subscription` | **nothing** | no path reaches them | `bam` needs `migrate: true`; `warning` and `queue_stats` need `persistWarnings` and `persistQueueStats`; `subscription` needs `publish`/`subscribe`. **This interface can set none of the four** |

#### 3.5 Two shape decisions inside the grant

**IT IS TABLE BY TABLE AND NOT `ON ALL TABLES IN SCHEMA pgboss`, AND THE DECIDING ARGUMENT IS `0079`'s OWN PROPERTY A.** The `DO` block over `pgboss.queue_stats` creates one partition per day named `queue_stats_YYYYMMDD` off the day `0079` is APPLIED. `ALL TABLES` would grant on those, so the resulting privilege set would **differ per install day** and `SUCCESS 5` could not state it. Enumeration also makes the set assertable in both directions, which is what turns a widening into a red diff rather than a comment.

**AND NO `ALTER DEFAULT PRIVILEGES` IS WRITTEN FOR THIS SCHEMA**, which is the opposite of what [`0026:174`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql) does for `public` and is deliberate. The next pg-boss catalog bump arrives as a NEW migration carrying `getMigrationPlans`' output; a table it adds should be **unreachable** until somebody grants it deliberately in that same migration. That is `0026`'s own reasoning for the analytics role, applied to a vendor's schema, and it converts an upgrade's new table from a silent inheritance into a `permission denied` somebody has to rule on.

### 10. Approval

**Status `proposed`. The approval line below is UNSIGNED and merging is not signing.**

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.**

**THE FIRST THING TO DECIDE IS THE WIDENING ITSELF, BECAUSE THAT IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT THIS ROW DOES AND NO GATE CAN JUDGE IT.** Every check here answers "is the grant exactly what was written down". **None of them answers "should the application role reach this schema at all"**, and that is the question. `0026` gives `merit_app` no DDL anywhere and no `DELETE` on eight append-only tables, and `0079` put a vendor's schema inside the same restore window as `ledger_entries` and then deliberately granted nothing, naming the grant a control rather than a detail. This file hands that role `INSERT`, `UPDATE` and `DELETE` on tables that sit beside the ledger in every PITR restore. **A founder who accepts the fifteen privileges is accepting that a compromised application connection can write and delete rows inside the ledger's restore boundary**, bounded to a vendor's job tables, and that the boundary is now shared rather than reserved. The alternative designs are real and this entry did not take either: a SECOND database for the queue, which costs ADR-006's transactional enqueue outright, or a `SECURITY DEFINER` wrapper per statement, which trades a grant for a surface nobody could enumerate.

**THE SECOND IS WHETHER MEASURED-AND-MINIMAL IS THE RIGHT SHAPE, OR WHETHER MINIMAL IS TOO BRITTLE.** Sixteen enumerated terms with no default privileges means a pg-boss upgrade that adds a table produces `permission denied` in a deployable rather than a silent inheritance. **This entry argues that is correct and a founder may reasonably read it as an outage waiting for a Tuesday.** The cheaper shape, `GRANT ... ON ALL TABLES IN SCHEMA pgboss` plus `ALTER DEFAULT PRIVILEGES`, is one line, survives upgrades, and gives the role reach over four tables no code path touches plus partitions whose names depend on the day the schema was installed. **The trade is an outage that names its cause against a widening nobody would notice**, and which of those two costs the firm more is a judgement rather than a measurement.

**THE THIRD IS SECTION 5, BECAUSE IT IS THE ONE PLACE THIS ROW LEFT SOMETHING HALF-DONE ON PURPOSE.** The manifest declares a capability no file uses. [ADR-326](ADR-326.md) refused exactly this shape from the other side, arguing that a line with no working call is not wiring; this row adds the line while the call would now work and the caller does not exist. **A founder who reads that as the same defect wearing the opposite argument is entitled to**, and the answer this entry can give is that the blocker is measured as discharged and the missing half is asserted by a case rather than promised in a comment.

**AND THE FOURTH IS SECTION 9 ITEM 5, THE FENCE.** Two files outside the dispatch's fence were edited because this row's success turned their assertions false. **That is the correct outcome of a red-by-succeeding rule and it is also exactly how a fence erodes**, one defensible exception at a time. A founder who wants those two repairs backed out and dispatched as their own row would be describing a slower and stricter process than this one ran, and the cost of it is a suite that stays red between the two merges.

**AND ONE THING THIS ENTRY IS NOT A JUDGEMENT ABOUT.** Sections 1, 2, 3, 6 and 7 are **FINDINGS**: every claim is a path and a line or a transcript, and any of them is refuted by opening the file or by re-running the measurement rather than by disagreeing. **Section 3.4's refusals, section 3.5's two shape decisions and section 5's half-wiring are the judgements**, and they move if and only if one of those findings is wrong.

- **Decision:** [ADR-305](ADR-305.md) section 7 slice 8's remainder lands as [`0082_pgboss_app_grants.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0082_pgboss_app_grants.sql). `merit_app` holds `USAGE` on `pgboss` and **not `CREATE`**, plus fifteen table privileges over six of the schema's twelve tables, **each one measured load-bearing by revoking it and watching the run fail**, and nothing at all on `bam`, `warning`, `queue_stats` and `subscription`. The grant is enumerated table by table and no default privileges are altered, so a pg-boss upgrade's new table is a refusal somebody rules on. **[ADR-326](ADR-326.md) section 3.3's ruling holds under measurement: no statement this interface can issue needs DDL in that schema**, and the false sentence in `0079` and its probe saying otherwise is superseded in place rather than edited. `REJECTION 5` is rewritten from a deliberate absence into a ceiling and the probe now fails against `0001`..`0081`. **`@merit/queue` joins the worker's manifest and the ONE-DOOR importer is owed.**
