## ADR-122: `input_digest` is taken over the computation's own argument, and it EXCLUDES the answer  (2026-08-27, status: proposed)

**[P4 section 8](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md)'s `P4-g` row names one gap in the M12 machine and does not close it: *"`input_digest` is the reproduction claim and it needs a producer."* This entry is that producer's ruling.** A digest asserting that a published number can be reproduced is a claim about **what is inside it**, and the three questions that decides are what it covers, what it deliberately excludes, and what a reader may conclude from two runs whose digests match. All three are answered below, and the one thing it **cannot** cover is named rather than quietly widened away.

- **Origin:** [P4 section 8](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md)'s `P4-g` row, session **234**. The number was **RESERVED BEFORE DISPATCH and REQUIRED** ([ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md)), and the row is amended in place per [ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3. **MONEY PATH**: `INV-M12-01` binds the machine to closed-session authoritative data, `ST-03` and `ST-04` publish **money on a public surface**, and [ADR-031](ADR-031.md) already ruled that surface `bigint` with a unit. The [E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) line-by-line read is the founder's and is owed on every line this lands.

- **Context:** `published_statistics.input_digest bytea NOT NULL` has existed since [`0021`](../../packages/db/migrations/0021_transparency.sql) with the one-line justification *"makes reproduction VERIFIABLE rather than merely possible"*, and nothing has ever written one. `INV-M12-12` states the property it is supposed to carry: *"every statistic is reproducible: recomputing a published value from the same as-of data yields the same number."* **A column whose meaning is one sentence in a migration header is a column two implementations will spell differently**, and this one is stamped onto an APPEND-ONLY, PUBLICLY RESTATED surface where the first spelling is the only spelling: there is no migration that repairs a `bytea` whose meaning moved.

### 2. THE RULING

> **1. THE DIGEST IS TAKEN OVER THE COMPUTATION'S OWN ARGUMENT, NOT OVER A DESCRIPTION OF THE QUERY.** [`inputDigest`](../../apps/worker/src/batch/statistics.ts) is handed the same value the arithmetic reads, and [`canonicalFact`](../../apps/worker/src/batch/statistics.ts) walks the fact objects with `Object.keys` rather than a written field list. **An input the arithmetic can see and the digest cannot is therefore UNEXPRESSIBLE rather than test-caught**, and a value the framing does not cover is a THROW rather than a skip.
>
> **2. IT EXCLUDES THE ANSWER, AND THAT EXCLUSION IS WHAT MAKES IT WORTH TAKING.** `value`, `numerator`, `denominator` and `sample_size` are outside it. A digest that covered its own answer could not distinguish *"different inputs"* from *"different arithmetic"*, and the second is the failure worth catching. **Two runs whose digests MATCH and whose values DIFFER is an arithmetic regression**, and it is loud only because the value is outside.
>
> **3. IT ALSO EXCLUDES `measure`, THE CLOCK, THE DATABASE'S IDENTITIES, AND THE PORT'S READ ORDER.** `ST-04`'s mean and median are computed from ONE input set and carry ONE digest, which is the pair's reproducibility stated rather than a collision. `computed_at`, `id` and `created_at` are the database's; a digest that moved every night would claim every window was a different window. The read order is defeated by sorting the facts on their own serialization, because a reproduction claim that depends on which rows Postgres returned first is not a reproduction claim.
>
> **4. IT CANNOT COVER THE BUILD THAT COMPUTED IT, AND THAT LIMIT IS PUBLISHED RATHER THAN PAPERED OVER.** Finding 1: there is no column for it and no migration is in `P4-g`'s fence. **Folding a worker version into the digest BYTES is REFUSED** rather than merely unavailable: it would make the digest change on every deploy, conflating a code change with an input change and destroying clause 2 outright. **So what a reader may conclude from two matching digests is that both runs read the SAME DEFINITION OVER THE SAME WINDOW AND THE SAME INPUT FACTS.** Not that they produced the same number. Not that they ran the same code. Both limits belong on the method page.
>
> **5. IMMUTABILITY IS THE DATABASE'S AND THE MACHINE DOES NOT RESTATE IT.** Finding 2. `runStatisticsRun` does not read the published series to see whether a window was already published and does not skip it: it computes, it writes, and `published_statistics_window_uq` refuses the second row. **An application pre-check would be a fourth control that can drift from the other three**, and [ADR-042](ADR-042.md)'s finding that a comment is not a control applies exactly as well to a check that duplicates one.
>
> **6. THE PUBLISHER'S ROUNDING IS ROUND-TO-NEAREST, TIES UPWARD, AND `median` AND `p50` ARE DIFFERENT MEASURES COMPUTED DIFFERENTLY.** [ADR-031](ADR-031.md) made the rounding the publisher's; this is the publisher taking it. `median` is the ORDERED MIDDLE, averaging the two middle observations on an even count, which is `ST-04`'s own wording. `p50` and `p95` are **nearest rank with no interpolation**, because an interpolated percentile is a duration NOBODY EVER WAITED and this surface publishes elapsed times a trader compares against their own.
>
> **7. A DENOMINATOR IS PUBLISHED EXACTLY WHEN THE MEASURE IS A RATIO.** [`0021:256`](../../packages/db/migrations/0021_transparency.sql)'s own rule, applied rather than relaxed: *"ST-03 has NO DENOMINATOR by ruling, because it is a total and the surface says so RATHER THAN IMPLYING A RATE."* An order statistic implies a rate exactly as hard as a total does, so `median`, `p50` and `p95` carry none either and `sample_size` carries the count they were selected from.

### 3. Clause 1, and the two shapes it refuses

**READING 1, A WRITTEN FIELD LIST: refused, and it is the shape everybody reaches for first** because it is what `hash.ts` does for `rule_states` and it works there. It does not work here, and the difference is which side the drift can arrive on. `HASHED_COLUMNS` enumerates nineteen columns of a table whose DDL a migration fixes, so an unlisted column is a migration that a reviewer sees. **A statistics input is a PROJECTION an adapter builds**, and an adapter adding a field to `SettledPayoutFact` next quarter changes what the arithmetic sees with no migration anywhere. Under a written list that field is silently outside the digest, two genuinely different windows carry one digest, and the reproduction claim is false in the direction nobody checks.

**READING 2, HASHING THE ROW THAT WAS WRITTEN: refused, and it is the reading the column's position invites.** `input_digest` sits between `computed_at` and `created_at` on a row carrying `value`, and hashing the row is one line. It would make the column a CHECKSUM OF THE ANSWER, which is a different and much weaker artifact: it detects a row edited after the fact, which `0026` already prevents by revoking the grant, and it detects nothing at all about whether the number can be reproduced. **It would also delete clause 2's property**, because a run with the same inputs and a regressed computation would produce a different digest and read as different data.

**THE THROW IS THE PART THAT MAKES CLAUSE 1 TRUE RATHER THAN HOPEFUL.** `tagged` covers `string`, `bigint`, `boolean` and `null`, and refuses everything else, `number` included. That is [ADR-031](ADR-031.md)'s exclusion carried one layer out: a `number` on a fact would admit `2.5` and admit a value above `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` that has already lost digits by the time the digest sees it. **A skip would have been the dangerous spelling**: it would leave the field out of the digest and let the run publish.

### 4. Clause 2, and the property it buys, stated as the thing a reader gets

| Two runs | Digest | Value | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Same window, same data | **same** | same | The reproduction claim holds |
| Same window, same data, **regressed arithmetic** | **same** | **different** | **AN ARITHMETIC REGRESSION, and it is visible.** This row is the whole reason the value is outside |
| Same window, data corrected underneath | **different** | any | A restatement is owed ([M12](../plans/M12-transparency-platform.md) section 3.3), and the digest is what says so |
| Same window, definition edited in place at an unchanged version | **different** | any | `INV-M12-07`. The prose specs are in the digest, so an in-place edit cannot be quiet |
| Same window, same data, **different build** | **same** | possibly different | **Clause 4's limit.** The digest does not see the build, and this row is why that is published rather than implied |

**The last two rows are the pair worth reading together.** A definition change is detectable because the definition is DATA the machine reads; a code change is not, because the code is not. The corpus already treats those two differently everywhere else -- a definition change needs an ADR and a future-dated `effective_from`, a code change needs a deploy -- and the digest inherits the distinction rather than blurring it.

### 5. What the schema cannot hold, found by building the machine

**THE COMMITMENT: *"each statistic carries BOTH a trailing-window and a lifetime form"*, which is the first of [M12-statistic-definitions](../plans/M12-statistic-definitions.md)' three binding requirements, restated in [M12](../plans/M12-transparency-platform.md)'s registry as `ST-03`'s *"trailing 30, trailing 90, and lifetime"*.**

**IT HAS NO SHAPE IN `statistic_definitions` AS LANDED.** Finding 4: `statistic_definitions_live_uq` gives a statistic exactly one live row, and that row carries exactly one `window_spec text`. A statistic therefore cannot carry a trailing form and a lifetime form at the same time, and a second row for the second window would need a second `version`, which is a DEFINITION version and not a window.

**Separately, a lifetime window's START is an anchor in the DATA** -- *"since first evaluation sold"*, *"since first settled payout"*, *"since the wallet shipped"* -- **that no column carries and no port returns.** A machine that resolved it from whatever calendar happened to be loaded would be choosing where Merit's published history begins, nightly, from an operational decision about slice depth.

**So the machine REFUSES a lifetime `window_spec` with a halt** rather than resolving one, and the refusal names both reasons. **This is reported and not repaired**, because repairing it is a migration and `P4-g`'s fence holds none. Section 9 rows it as owed.

### 6. `INV-M12-06`'s k-anonymity floor, which has no number, and what is done instead

**The invariant reads *"no published aggregate has a cell small enough to identify an account or an identity"*, enforced by *"k-anonymity floor per grain, checked at publish"*. Finding 7: THE FLOOR HAS NO NUMBER ANYWHERE, and `published_statistics` and `statistic_definitions` have no column for one.**

**What exists is `min_sample`, and every definition applies it PER PUBLISHED CELL in its own words** (`ST-01`: *"250 completed evaluations per published cell"*; `ST-04`: *"50 settled payouts per cell"*). The machine suppresses a cell below its own `min_sample`, and at 250, 100 and 50 that floor sits far above any plausible k for six of the seven.

**`ST-03` IS THE ONE IT DOES NOT COVER AND THAT IS SAID RATHER THAN ROUNDED UP.** Its `min_sample` is ruled *"None"* -- *"a total of a small number is still a true total"* -- so no floor applies. It is a lineup total over every identity, which is the grain least able to identify anybody, and its surface publishes the count behind it, so a reader sees the sample. **A separate k floor with a different number is NOT invented here**, because a number chosen by a session is exactly the kind of publication policy `SD-M12-01` puts on a versioned row with a founder's signature.

### 9. What this FORECLOSES, and what it leaves owed

**Forecloses:**

1. **The digest format is fixed forever by its first stored row.** `INPUT_DIGEST_HEADER` carries `.v1` so a successor is expressible, and a successor does not repair the rows already written: two digests under two formats are incomparable, and `published_statistics` is append-only.
2. **A published figure can never be checked against the code that produced it from the row alone.** Clause 4. Closing it is a migration adding an `engine_version` column, and until one lands the method page carries the limit in words.
3. **A definition's arithmetic is CODE and its policy is DATA, permanently.** `numerator_spec` is `text`, so the machine carries a computation per `stat_code` and refuses a code it does not know. A statistic whose method changes without a code change is not expressible, which is the direction the corpus wants: `INV-M12-07` says a definition change is an ADR.
4. **A whole-run write is the only write.** `publishRun` takes every row, because `STAT-C1` is deferred. A caller wanting to publish one statistic at a time cannot, and that is `FM-M12-02` holding.
5. **`median` and `p50` will disagree on even counts forever**, by clause 6, and both are published.

**Owed, and none of it is this session's fence:**

| Owed | Whose |
|---|---|
| **The adapter implementing `StatisticsPorts` over `systemDb('nightly-batch')`**, and the `stats.published` / `stats.run_halted` / `stats.suppressed` emissions | The session that writes `apps/worker`'s first adapter. It is the same gap [`apps/worker/src/index.ts`](../../apps/worker/src/index.ts) has named for `BatchPorts` since session 147 |
| **`apps/worker/src/index.ts` does not export `statistics.ts`**, while it exports `nightly.ts` and `replay.ts` | Outside `P4-g`'s fence, one line, reported rather than reached for |
| **The `nightly.yml` job**, so `CRON_INVENTORY`'s statistics-run row has something scheduled to page about | The session that schedules it. `CI-09`'s fourth leg is a different leg |
| **`statistic_definitions` has no shape for a trailing form and a lifetime form at once** (section 5) | A migration and an ADR. Whether the lifetime form becomes a second `stat_code`, a second column, or a relaxed `live_uq` is a ruling |
| **The four separate publications section 7 names**, each needing a `stat_code` and a definition row | M12's own, and `S-11` and `S-13` are signed off carrying them |
| **`INV-M12-06`'s k floor has no number** (section 6) | The founder. It is a publication policy, like `min_sample` |

### 10. Alternatives considered

- **Hash the written row.** Section 3, reading 2. **Rejected**: it is a checksum of the answer, it detects only an edit `0026` already prevents, and it deletes clause 2.
- **A written field list per fact type.** Section 3, reading 1. **Rejected on where the drift arrives**: a statistics input is an adapter's projection, not a migration's table.
- **Fold the worker version into the digest bytes.** **Rejected, and it is the widening the dispatch asked to see refused rather than merely unavailable.** It makes every deploy look like a data change. The honest form of that claim is a COLUMN, which is a migration.
- **Share `canonicalPayload` with [`provisioning/payload.ts`](../../apps/worker/src/provisioning/payload.ts).** **Rejected**, on that file's own precedent against sharing `hash.ts`'s `frame`: each digest fixes its own stored column forever, and one serializer across three `bytea` columns means a change for any one silently invalidates the other two. M01 section 3.7's *"no second code path"* is about THE FOLD; a serializer for one column is not a second expression of a serializer for another.
- **Use the engine's hand-rolled `sha256`.** **Not reached**: it is not exported (finding 6), and the purity constraints that made it hand-rolled do not bind `apps/worker`, which has `@types/node` and three existing `createHash` call sites.
- **Read the published series and skip an already-published window.** **Rejected by clause 5.** It is a fourth control duplicating three that already work, and the one that would drift.
- **Suppress a cell below an invented k floor.** **Rejected by section 6.** A number a session picks is a publication policy nobody signed.
- **Publish the lifetime form by anchoring on the loaded calendar slice.** **Rejected by section 5.** It would move Merit's published history start with an operational decision about slice depth.

### 11. Consequences

- **`P4-g`'s named gap is closed and the machine exists.** [`apps/worker/src/batch/statistics.ts`](../../apps/worker/src/batch/statistics.ts) reads authoritative closed-session facts, computes all seven signed definitions, and writes one immutable row per window per measure per grain cell.
- **`published_statistics.input_digest` has a MEANING and not only a producer**, and the meaning is publishable: the method page can state what two matching digests prove and what they do not.
- **Three of the six global choices need no code at all**, which is worth recording because it is why they cannot drift: `G-1` (never-traded accounts are in the denominator because nothing removes them), `G-2` (an account still in evaluation has no outcome and so produces no fact), and `G-3` (finding 9). They are properties of the schema rather than branches in a function.
- **A structural gap in `statistic_definitions` is now written down** (section 5), found by building against it rather than by reading it.
- **[CRON_INVENTORY](../ops/runbooks/CRON_INVENTORY.md)'s statistics-run row now names an entry point**, and states the property its dead-man switch cannot distinguish: `stats.published` absent means "did not run" and "ran and halted" alike, which for this job is correct rather than a false alarm, because both mean nothing was published.
- **`ST-07` will publish 100 percent and the machine computes it anyway** (finding 8). A machine that hard-coded the constant would stop being able to report the day it stopped holding, which is the one day the figure matters.

### 12. Approval

- **Approval line: PENDING, UNSIGNED. Two runs over the same closed window produce the same `input_digest`, and ONE row exists rather than two.** Executable, one clause, and scoped to [`statistics.ts`](../../apps/worker/src/batch/statistics.ts) and [`ports.ts`](../../apps/worker/src/batch/ports.ts) rather than to a package-wide test count, which has drifted six times in this corpus while the ruling held every time. Section 8 is it executed, with the second run's refusal coming from the transcribed unique index rather than from the machine.
- **MONEY PATH. The [E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) line-by-line read is owed on every line this lands and is NOT claimed by this entry.** `ST-03` and `ST-04` publish money on a public surface, and a float in this path is a float on the transparency page.
- **Clauses 2 and 4 are the two a later session will want to reverse**, and section 4's table is why neither should be reversed without replacing what it buys.
