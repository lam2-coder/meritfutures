## ADR-093: auth is P3's, because the phase whose title already says "identity" is the one whose own stated contents cannot ship without it  (2026-08-24, status: proposed)

**[P4's build plan](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md) section 4.4 found that [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) specifies auth, the schema carries it, no code implements it, and no phase's contents name it, then declined to rule and put three readings to the founder. Every clause of that finding reproduces here, checked at its source. The ruling is the third reading: AUTH IS P3'S, and [DELIVERY_PLAN](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md) section 4's P3 cell is amended to say so.** The ground is not that P3 already carries `M19`, which is the ground P4 offered and which is the weakest of the three available. It is that **P3's own stated contents contain two endpoints whose contract line reads `Auth: session`**, so P3 cannot ship what it is already committed to without the thing nobody assigned. **A RULING ONLY. No code, no route, no migration, and DELIVERY_PLAN is not edited here.**

- **Origin:** [P4](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md) section 10 item 3, dispatched as session **180**. P4 section 4.4 is the finding; this entry is the decision P4 deliberately did not take.

- **Context:** DELIVERY_PLAN is **frozen**. A done-condition moves by ADR or it does not move, on the precedent [session 136](../sessions/2026-08-22-session-136.md) set and which [P3 section 3](../plans/P3-ledger-billing-identity.md) and [P4 section 4.4](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md) both cite. **That precedent is real and this entry rests on it. The sentence all three sites quote is not in session 136, and section 9 is that finding rather than a footnote.**

- **MONEY PATH.** Auth is one of the four `E2` classes in [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md)'s regime table, and [`0002_identity.sql:4`](../../packages/db/migrations/0002_identity.sql) says it in the schema's own words: *"E2 READ: MONEY PATH. Auth is a money path (CLAUDE.md, constitution E2)."* **The direction this ruling can fail in is a phase gate that passes with the session issuer unbuilt and the negative-authz matrix unwritten**, which is section 11.

### 2. What P4 section 4.4 gets right, and the one inference that does not follow

**Right, and load-bearing:** *"KYC is not auth. [M19](../plans/M19-kyc-identity.md) verifies who a human is; `SC-M4-01` and `SC-M4-11` log them in and elevate them."* That is correct and this entry adopts it. **[Session 168](../sessions/2026-08-24-session-168.md) measured M19 against the tree and its seven deltas, six tables and eight endpoints contain no session, no challenge and no assertion.** M19 is verification. Auth is authentication. Two subjects.

**The inference that does not follow**, recorded in [STATE](../STATE.md) in exactly these words: *"KYC is not auth, so P3 does not carry it."*

The premise is about **M19**. The conclusion is about **P3**. P3 is not M19.

[DELIVERY_PLAN:127](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md) names the phase **"P3 Ledger, billing, identity"**. Its contents cell lists five items and M19 is one of them. **The phase title is wider than its cell**, and the width is the word this ruling turns on: `M19` is not the whole of identity, and nothing in DELIVERY_PLAN says it is.

**The corpus already filed auth under identity, in the schema, before any of this was a question.** [`0002_identity.sql:11-14`](../../packages/db/migrations/0002_identity.sql), inside the file named `identity`, in the founder's own `E2` header:

> **2. THE AUTHENTICATION SURFACE. `passkeys`, `otp_challenges` and `sessions` are the whole of it.** Merit is passwordless by design, so there is no password table anywhere in this schema and the absence is deliberate, not an omission to be repaired later.

`passkeys` is created at [`0002:282`](../../packages/db/migrations/0002_identity.sql), `otp_challenges` at `:303`, `sessions` at `:337`. **Auth's three tables are in the migration called `identity`, and P3 is the phase called identity.** `SD-M4-04` later adds three columns to `sessions` from inside [`0029_phone_identity_and_auth`](../../packages/db/migrations/0029_phone_identity_and_auth.sql), a file whose name carries **both** M19's phone deltas and M4's auth columns, folded into one migration by the same author on the same day.

**This is not a naming coincidence pressed into service.** It is the only reading under which the schema, the migration names, the phase title and the endpoint dependencies all say the same thing, and section 3 is the dependency half.

### 3. THE RULING

**Auth is P3's.** Specifically:

**RULING 1. The authentication SERVICE is P3's stated content.** Sessions, the OTP challenge lifecycle, the two passkey ceremonies, `GET /me`, `POST /auth/logout`, and `C-27` elevation with its per-endpoint required-factor declaration. [DELIVERY_PLAN:127](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md)'s P3 contents cell is amended to name it. Section 4 writes the amendment out.

**RULING 2. The two auth SCREENS stay in P4, and no cell moves for them.** `SC-M4-01` and `SC-M4-11` are two of [M04](../plans/M04-trader-portal.md)'s eleven screens, and *"trader portal"* contains them by DELIVERY_PLAN's own wording. **They were never homeless. P4 section 4.4's framing hides that**: it reads *"P4's first stated content contains two money-path screens whose subject appears in no phase's contents at all"*, which is true of the **subject** and false of the **screens**. What has no phase is the session issuer the screens post to. **Once the issuer is P3's, `SC-M4-01` and `SC-M4-11` are ordinary P4 portal screens whose dependency lands one phase earlier**, exactly as `SC-M4-05` depends on P2's engine and `SC-M4-06` on P3's purchases.

**RULING 3. P3's definition of done gains the `C-27` clause, in both directions.** A contents amendment with no done-clause leaves auth unverified at the gate that is supposed to verify P3. Section 4 writes it.

**RULING 4. P4's cell is not amended, in either column.** Nothing is added to it and nothing is removed from it.

**The ground, stated once.** P3's own stated contents contain endpoints the contract marks `Auth: session`:

| Endpoint | Contract line | Which P3 content owns it |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /checkout` | [API_CONTRACT:285](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md), **`Auth: session`** | *"billing and checkout"* |
| `POST /kyc/session` | [API_CONTRACT:488](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md), **`Auth: session`** | *"M19 KYC with the composite trigger set"* |
| every `/affiliate/*` | [API_CONTRACT:677](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md), section 12's first matrix row: an unauthenticated request to `/affiliate/*` is **401** | *"coupons and affiliate attribution"* |

**And section 12 is not advisory.** It is headed *"Negative-authz test matrix (D5, **required in CI**)"* and its first sentence is *"Every row is a named test that **must exist before the endpoint ships**"*, which is `VG-5`'s subject. `CI-06k` is live in the runner today and reads it: *"Declared authority: every endpoint declares a factor, and no sensitive action accepts a single one."* **So P3 cannot ship `POST /checkout` without a session issuer**, not as a matter of user experience but because the row that must exist before it ships is `Checkout with a client-supplied price field | session | field ignored`, and there is no `session` to establish.

**A dependency shared by two phases belongs to the earlier one.** That is the principle, it is not invented here, and [P3 section 9 item 4](../plans/P3-ledger-billing-identity.md) already applied it to the runtime: *"P1's contents, P2's and P3's each omit a database client, an HTTP surface and a job runner, and all six of P3's contents need all three."* **Auth is that finding one layer up, with one difference that decides it: the runtime is needed by every phase from P3 onward, and auth is needed by P3 first.**

### 4. The amendment DELIVERY_PLAN takes, written out so the applying session transcribes rather than composes

**This entry does not edit DELIVERY_PLAN.** It rules that the file moves, and the session that holds it applies exactly this. Both edits are to [line 127](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md), the P3 row, and to nothing else in the table. **The two blocks below are written with links relative to `docs/DELIVERY_PLAN.md` rather than to this file**, so they transcribe verbatim, which is also why they are fenced rather than quoted: a relative link that resolves from its destination does not resolve from here, and `CI-06a` reads this file.

**Edit 1, the Contents cell.** After *"`M19` KYC with the composite trigger set"* and before *"`ADR-023` enrichment in observe mode"*, insert:

```
**the authentication surface: sessions, the OTP challenge lifecycle, the two passkey ceremonies and `C-27` elevation** ([ADR-039](decisions/ADR-039.md), `SD-M4-04`, [ADR-093](decisions/ADR-093.md)),
```

**Edit 2, the Definition of done cell.** After *"verification firing at each configured trigger"*, append:

```
, and **[API_CONTRACT section 12](architecture/API_CONTRACT.md)'s `C-27` rows green in BOTH directions**: an SMS-established session refused elevation, a non-elevated session refused each of the three sensitive actions, and `GET /sessions` and `GET /phone/change` returning **200** from a single-factor session
```

**The third clause of edit 2 is the one a hurried transcription will drop, and it is the one that matters most.** Section 12 states its own reason: *"a boundary tested only where it refuses is indistinguishable from a boundary that refuses everything"*, and requiring elevation merely to **look** at the session list *"would lock a compromised account's real owner out of the one screen that helps them"*. A done-condition carrying only the refusals passes on an implementation that refuses everything.

**Nothing else in DELIVERY_PLAN moves.** Not P4's row, not the phase order, not the week figures, not the eighteen-week headline. Section 8 is what that last omission costs.

### 5. Reading 1 priced: auth is P4's, and it lands behind the phase that needs it

**Reading 1 is refused on a measurement rather than on taste.** If auth is P4's, then P3's checkout, P3's affiliate surface and P3's KYC session endpoint each need a session issuer that arrives in weeks 7 to 8, and they are scheduled for weeks 5 to 6.

Two exits and both are worse than the ruling:

- **P3 ships those endpoints without sessions.** Section 12 forbids it in the sentence quoted above, `CI-06k` reads the matrix, and `VG-5` is the row whose subject it is. This is not a rule a session can decide to carry as debt, because the gate is already in the runner and already passing.
- **P3 waits on P4.** That inverts two phases for the whole of P3's wave 3, which [P3 section 6](../plans/P3-ledger-billing-identity.md) lists as **six module slices, every one money path except the enrichment call**, and which is the largest undispatched block in the corpus.

**And reading 1 puts the work in the phase with the least room for it.** P4's plan measures its own ten slices and finds that **P4 needs no migration at all**, a property [P4 section 9](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md) says it shares with M19 alone, and that only two of its ten slices are money path. Reading 1 adds the `E2` class the constitution names first to the one phase that had escaped carrying one.

### 6. Reading 2 priced: the tidiest reading blocks the most, and this is the trap the brief names

**Reading 2 is the most attractive on the page and the worst in the schedule.** A phase whose single subject is auth reads clean, fences cleanly, and would let the founder read one `E2` set in one sitting.

It fails on two counts.

**First, it does not fix P3.** A phase *between P3 and P4* is still after P3, so `POST /checkout` and `POST /kyc/session` are exactly as unshippable as under reading 1. **Reading 2 buys the tidiness and does not buy the ordering**, which is the only thing the ruling is for.

**Second, it serializes three P4 contents that do not need auth at all.** Measured against the contract:

| P4's stated content | What it needs from auth |
|---|---|
| **Trader portal** | a session. **This is the one** |
| **Marketing site, config-rendered plans and rules** | **nothing.** `GET /plans` at [API_CONTRACT:249](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) reads `Auth: none`, and `GET /plans/:planId/versions/:version` at `:263` reads `Auth: none` |
| **The M12 machine and the method pages** | **nothing.** The machine is a nightly batch reading through `systemDb('nightly-batch')`, one of the two members of the closed `SystemReason` vocabulary at [`scoped-db.ts:143`](../../packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts). `GET /public/methods/:statCode` is marked **NEW, public** at [M12:218](../plans/M12-transparency-platform.md) |
| **The stats page rendering "not yet meaningful" honestly** | **nothing.** [`apps/site/src/stats/published.ts`](../../apps/site/src/stats/published.ts) reads `published_statistics`, a public surface |

**Three of P4's four stated contents require no session, and reading 2 puts all four behind one.** It also inserts a tenth phase into a nine-phase table, shifting P5 through P9 and the *"eighteen weeks plus 3 to 5 days"* figure that [DELIVERY_PLAN section 4](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md) deliberately writes into *"the two phases it touches rather than into the total alone."*

**A reading that is tidy and blocks everything is worse than one that is untidy and unblocks P4.** Reading 2 is the tidy one.

### 7. What the ruling does to P4's dispatchability: nothing, and that is the point

**Checked slice by slice against [P4 section 8](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md) rather than asserted.**

| P4 wave | What the ruling changes |
|---|---|
| **Wave 1**, `P4-a` to `P4-d`, the four preconditions | **Nothing.** None of the four is auth and P4's own plan says *"none of these four is P4's subject"* |
| **Wave 2**, `P4-e` to `P4-h`, the stated contents | **Nothing.** `P4-h` already reads *"`SC-M4-04`, `SC-M4-10`, `SC-M4-01` and `SC-M4-11` are deliberately NOT in it"*, so no dispatched slice loses a line |

| **Wave 4**, NOT DISPATCHED | **Its auth half leaves P4 and enters P3's undispatched wave 3.** Reset pricing, wave 4's other half, is untouched by this entry and stays where [P4 section 10 item 3](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md) left it |

**So the ruling removes an undispatched item from P4 and changes zero dispatched slices.** Ten slices, three waves, all ten fences and all thirteen per-file collision rows stand exactly as written.

**And it is the reading that lets the most work run at once.** Under the ruling, P4's site, M12 machine, method pages and stats page are concurrent with auth rather than behind it, and the portal's identity-scoped routes find their issuer already built. Under reading 1 auth sits **inside** P4's critical path; under reading 2 it sits **in front of all of it**. Reading 3 is the only one of the three that takes auth off P4's critical path entirely.

### 8. What this costs, named rather than absorbed

**Four costs, and the fourth is the one that could make the ruling worthless.**

1. **P3's weeks 5 to 6 gain the whole authentication service** and DELIVERY_PLAN's week figure for P3 does not move here. **That figure probably should move and this entry does not move it**, because a week count is a scheduling judgement rather than an ordering one, and section 4's fence is the two cells of one row. **Named so it is a decision rather than an oversight.**
2. **`SD-M4-04` is an `M04` delta discharged inside a P3 phase.** Nothing moves in the schema, both migrations are merged and `E2`-read, and the manifest rows stay as they are. A later reader tracing `SD-M4-nn` to a phase will find one of the four outside M04's own phase, which is exactly the shape `0029_phone_identity_and_auth`'s filename already had.
3. **[STATE](../STATE.md)'s recorded sentence is contradicted.** *"KYC is not auth, so P3 does not carry it."* Its premise survives and its conclusion does not, for section 2's reason. STATE is append-only, so this entry appends rather than repairs, and the append says which half moved.
4. **THE RULING IS COHERENT ONLY IF THE RUNTIME IS ALSO P3'S, AND THAT IS [P3 SECTION 9 ITEM 4](../plans/P3-ledger-billing-identity.md), STILL UNANSWERED.** Auth is seven route headings and a middleware, and there is no route registry: `apps/api/src/routes/index.ts` does not exist and is scheduled as `P4-d`. **If the founder answers item 4 by leaving the runtime in P4, then P3 depends on P4 anyway and this ruling buys nothing**, because auth moves to the phase in front and its precondition stays in the phase behind. The two rulings are one decision taken in two entries, and **this one is worth signing only alongside P3's item 4 answered the way P3's plan proposed**: the runtime is P3's wave 1. That is stated here rather than discovered by whoever dispatches P3's wave 3.

### 9. A quotation three documents rest on is not in the document they cite

**What session 136 actually says, at [`:28`](../sessions/2026-08-22-session-136.md):** *"**P1'S DEFINITION OF DONE CONTAINS A FEATURE MERIT HAS RULED OUT.** [DELIVERY_PLAN](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md) section 4 asks for 'every VG gate wired' ... **The two readings and their distances are tabled in the entry; which one binds is not a session's to decide.**"* And at [`:12`](../sessions/2026-08-22-session-136.md): *"Two founder decisions are handed over and neither is taken here."*

**The precedent is real, it is on the same document, section 4 of DELIVERY_PLAN, and it is correctly applied by both plans and by this entry.** What is wrong is only the quotation marks: a paraphrase in P3 acquired them, and P4 inherited it as a quote from a third document. **This is the class [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) names in its own caution** (*"a failure to check a claim against the primary source"*), arriving in the mildest possible form and in three places at once.

**Both citing documents are `draft` and both are outside this session's fence, so neither is repaired here.** The repair is one sentence in each, and it belongs to whichever session next holds those files. **`CI-06a` will not catch it**, because the link resolves; the target simply does not contain the words. **A gate that verifies quoted spans against their cited file is a real proposal and this entry does not write it**, on section 10's rule.
