### 4. Does the leg still do work? Assertion by assertion, because the answer is not uniform

**THE DISPATCH ASKED WHETHER THE LEG IS NOW TRUE FOR A DIFFERENT REASON OR VACUOUS. IT IS BOTH, ON DIFFERENT ASSERTIONS**, and reporting one verdict for the block would be false either way. The block carries five.

| # | Assertion | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `inMigrations - GENERATED_ALWAYS_AS.length` is `1` | **UNIQUE LIVE WORK.** Nothing else in the tree asserts this population difference. A sixth generated column landing in the migrations, or a registration moving one into `GENERATED_ALWAYS_AS`, is red here and nowhere else |
| 2 | `live_account_state` is NOT in `ALL_REGISTERED` | **TRUE FOR A DIFFERENT REASON, AND DUPLICATED.** True because the non-registration is deliberate, not because nothing transcribes the table. **ASSERTION 4 IS NAMED AT ITS SITE AND NOT REWORDED.** A vacuous leg reworded into something that passes is worse than a vacuous leg labelled vacuous, because the label is what a later row needs in order to decide whether to replace it with a real reader. The comment above it now says so in the file, in three lines replacing three.

### 5. What the docblock says now, and why the constants keep their names

**THE REPAIR LANDS ON `:3592` ITSELF**, which is what the fence required. Lines `3589` to `3596` are the docblock body and `3592` is the cited one; the corrected sentence begins on `3592` and the block is 8 lines on both sides.

**THE CONSTANTS ARE NOT RENAMED AND THE REASON IS THAT THEY WERE NEVER WRONG.** The dispatch's own framing is that they are *"named for a condition that no longer holds"*. **Derived, that is not so.** `UNREGISTERED_GENERATED_TABLE` names REGISTRATION, the table is unregistered, and the leg it feeds reads `ALL_REGISTERED`. The name is right and the cost of changing it would be paid for nothing.
