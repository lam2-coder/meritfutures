## ADR-116: Playwright `1.56.1` is admitted under a DELEGATED `VG-12` grant, and `CI-08` becomes a stage that renders fixtures because there is no page  (2026-08-26, status: proposed)

**[STRATEGY:192](../testing/STRATEGY.md) says of a `VG-12` dependency admission that it is one *"a session cannot grant itself"*, and this session did not.** The founder delegated the grant for this slice explicitly. That sentence is the entry: an admission recorded as though the gate did not apply and an admission recorded as delegated are indistinguishable in the lockfile and are opposite in the record, and the second is the only one `VG-12` survives. **What is admitted is `@playwright/test@1.56.1`**, exact per [`.npmrc`](../../.npmrc)'s `save-exact`, in `pnpm-workspace.yaml`'s `catalog:` block. **What it buys is `SS-01` to `SS-08`**, the Appendix F slop score, whose identifier is `M4-F-01` and which [DESIGN_SYSTEM section 8](../design/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md) opens by calling *"A Playwright pass"*.

**`CI-08`'s cell moves in the same commit as the artifact that activates it**, which is [P4 section 11](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md) rule 10 and which [session 183](../sessions/2026-08-24-session-183.md) followed for `CI-07`.

- **Origin:** [P4](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md) section 8's `P4-i` row, dispatched as session **228**. The number was reserved before dispatch by [session 227's allocation commit](../sessions/2026-08-26-session-227.md), so none was chosen here; [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md)'s `116` row is amended **in place** per [ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3.

- **NOT MONEY PATH.** Nothing here touches the engine, the ledger, payout or auth, so [ADR-003](ADR-003.md)'s strict regime does not bind this session and no [E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) read is owed. It carries a founder decision anyway, for the reason section 2 gives: a dependency admission is the one class of change this corpus rules a session may not take alone.

### 1. The delegation, written down rather than implied

[STRATEGY section 4.2](../testing/STRATEGY.md)'s `VG-12` row is the authority and its clause is one sentence long:

> a session cannot grant itself

**`CI-09`'s Stryker leg still waits on that exact artifact today**, phrased in the register as *"the `VG-12` admission, which a session cannot grant itself"*, and [`gates.mjs`](../../scripts/corpus/gates.mjs)'s `UNPROBEABLE_ARTIFACTS` records why no job can ever see it, quoting session 23: *"a job can see the dependency surface changed and cannot see that a human agreed"*.

**So the grant is unobservable to every gate in this repository, and that is precisely why it has to be written.** The delegation for this slice arrived with the dispatch, in the founder's own words: the `VG-12` admission is **delegated** for `P4-i`, and the entry must **record the delegation rather than proceed as though the gate did not apply**. [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md)'s reservation row for this number, written before this session ran, carries the same sentence, so the delegation predates the branch and is not a thing this session decided it had.

**The failure mode this section exists to refuse** is a session that adds a dependency past a gate naming that dependency and writes an entry describing the package. The difference between that session and this one is one sentence, and it is above.

### 3. THE RULING

**RULING 1. `@playwright/test@1.56.1`**, one catalog entry, exact, in `pnpm-workspace.yaml`'s `catalog:` block so `VG-12`'s human approval happens once on a diff to that block rather than once per manifest. Declared as a `devDependency` in **three** manifests and no others: the root, `apps/site` and `apps/portal`. The root holds it because `playwright.config.ts` and the shared pass live there and `node-linker=isolated` resolves only declared dependencies; the two apps hold it because their spec files import it.

**RULING 2. `1.56.1` and not `1.62.1`, and the reason is a measurement rather than caution.** Section 4.

**RULING 3. `CI-08` is IMPLEMENTED, at two of its three projects, over FIXTURES rather than pages.** Section 6. The row says all three of those things in its own cell rather than claiming a stage and leaving a reader to discover the shape.

**RULING 4. The falsification is IN BAND.** Every other stage proves it can fail through `scripts/ci/falsify-ci.mjs`, a separate harness. This one ships, per surface, a compliant fixture **and** a deliberately non-compliant one, and fails unless the first produces no finding and the second produces a finding for **every one of the eight**. [P1](../plans/P1-monorepo-scaffold.md) section 6's requirement is therefore discharged inside the stage, on every commit, in one command.

**RULING 5. One browser, Chromium.** [STRATEGY section 2](../testing/STRATEGY.md) chose Playwright over Cypress because [ADR-012](ADR-012.md) puts the admin console on a separate apex domain and cross-origin is a requirement rather than an edge case. It did not choose a browser matrix. `SS-01` to `SS-08` read **computed style**, which is the part of a rendering engine that agrees across engines, so a three-browser matrix would triple a merge blocker's cost to re-assert twenty color values three times.

**RULING 6. The pass lives at the repository root and the surfaces live in the apps.** The slop score is **one** gate over the whole UI estate. Putting the checks in `apps/site` and importing them from `apps/portal` is a deployable reaching into a deployable, which `RI-04`'s prose refuses in the one sentence it is known for; putting a copy in each is two transcriptions of a twenty-value palette, which is the drift every register in this corpus exists to prevent. So `e2e/` sits beside [`vitest.config.ts`](../../vitest.config.ts) and [`eslint.config.js`](../../eslint.config.js), the other two repository-wide gate implementations, and `apps/*/e2e/` holds only what is genuinely per-deployable: which surfaces that app renders.

### 4. The version is CHOSEN on a measurement, and `latest` was refused

`@playwright/test` pins its browser builds in `playwright-core`'s `browsers.json`, and **the pin is a REVISION rather than a range**. A runner whose pinned revision is absent reports `Executable doesn't exist` and the pass does not run at all.

Read out of each candidate tarball, rather than from a release page:

| Version | chromium revision | Chromium |
|---|---|---|
| `1.55.0` | `1187` | `140.0.7339.16` |
| `1.56.0` | `1194` | `141.0.7390.37` |
| **`1.56.1`** | **`1194`** | **`141.0.7390.37`** |
| `1.57.0` | `1200` | `143.0.7499.4` |
| `1.58.0` | `1208` | `145.0.7632.6` |

**`1.56.1` is the NEWEST version whose chromium pin is `1194`**, and `1194` is the build every Merit build container already carries at `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1194`. Verified by running it: `chromium.launch()` from this tree, with no `executablePath` override and no `playwright install`, reported `141.0.7390.37` and returned a computed style.

**`npm view @playwright/test dist-tags` reports `latest: 1.62.1`, and taking it would have pinned a revision no container holds.** The gate would then be one a session cannot run, and [`repo-invariants.mjs`](../../packages/tooling/checks/repo-invariants.mjs) states the standard this corpus judges that by: **a check that cannot run is not a check that passed.**

**This is a version chosen rather than a version pinned back.** Moving it is a diff to the catalog block that re-runs the table above, and the entry a future session needs is the arithmetic and not the number.

### 6. `CI-08` renders FIXTURES, and that is measured rather than chosen

`ls apps/*/src/app` returns `No such file or directory`. `CI-07` one row up is open on *"a `page`, `layout` or `route` file under `apps/*/src/app/`"* and `CI-06/gate-inventory` probes that path and reports it **absent** on this commit. **There is no page to render.** [`session 183`](../sessions/2026-08-24-session-183.md) measured the adjacent half: `next build` in `apps/site` exits `1` on *"Couldn't find any `pages` or `app` directory"*.

**So the pass renders the design system as the DOM a page produces**, which is exactly what `SS-01` to `SS-08` read: they read computed style and rendered text, and a browser computes those from a document regardless of what produced it. Four fixtures, two per surface, each rendered at 375px and 1280px in light and dark, which is DESIGN_SYSTEM section 8's matrix verbatim.

**THE HONEST LIMIT, STATED HERE SO IT IS NOT DISCOVERED LATER.** A compliant fixture is a document this session wrote, so the compliant direction alone proves that the checks accept what this session believes compliance looks like and nothing about `apps/site`. **What makes the stage a control today is the SEEDED direction**, which is a document written to break every check and which fails the stage if any check stays silent against it. That direction is about the CHECKS rather than about the fixtures, and it is the half that keeps working unchanged when a real page arrives.

**Nothing about the eight checks changes when the first page lands.** What changes is one field: `Surface.path` becomes a URL and the config grows a `webServer`. That is why the fixtures are a fixture list in each app's spec rather than a mode in the pass.

**TWO PROJECTS AND NOT THREE.** `apps/admin` is the third UI surface, is in no P4 slice at all, and [ADR-095](ADR-095.md) declined to rule even its framework for that reason. A fixture for it inside this fence would be this session choosing scope. `CI-06/gate-inventory` **never asks whether a `Contents` cell is TRUE**, which is [ADR-073](ADR-073.md)'s own second limit and which `CI-02` already exercises by promising the `PT-nn` suites against a tree that has none, so the row is implemented at two of three **and the row says so**.

### 7. The probe fired for the right reason, and two seeded cases retire with it

**This is the first slice to make `CI-08`'s needle fire for real, and it fired correctly on the first install.** [ADR-095](ADR-095.md) section 8 had re-anchored it after `next@16.3.2` was admitted: that version declares `@playwright/test` as an **optional peer**, pnpm writes a package's peer block into the lockfile verbatim, and the previous needle read the declaration as an install and failed `CI-08` at **29 of 30** on an artifact that was genuinely absent. The repaired needle reads a **lockfile v9 entry key**, `name@version:` at exactly two spaces.

Verbatim, from `node scripts/corpus/gates.mjs check` on the install this entry admits:

> `docs/testing/STRATEGY.md:191 CI-08: the artifact "@playwright/test present in the lockfile" HAS ARRIVED (pnpm-lock.yaml holds the entry '@playwright/test@1.56.1':)`

**It names the entry key at two spaces and not the peer line at six**, and the peer line is still in the file. The repair is therefore proved by the arrival it was repaired for rather than by re-reading it.

**And then the probe retires, because the register's own rule says it must.** With `CI-08` implemented, no condition in section 4.1 names that artifact, and the stale-direction loop reports it in its own words: *"a register entry that no longer names a real condition is a finding, which is what keeps this register from becoming furniture"*. `playwrightInLockfile` and its `INVENTORY_PROBES` row are removed and a retirement note takes their place.

**`falsify.mjs`'s pair does NOT survive, and it was measured rather than assumed.** Both cases had already stopped working on the arrival commit, reported by `node scripts/corpus/falsify.mjs` before the edit:

| Case | What happened |
|---|---|
| `playwright-peer-declaration-is-not-an-install` | **`CONTROL DID NOT FIRE`**: *"seeding the control failed: seed anchor not found: the lockfile already holds a @playwright/test entry"*. Its control seeds the entry key and asserts the absence it was written against |
| `playwright-near-miss` | **`CONTROL DID NOT FIRE`**: *"Without the exemption this must fail with HAS ARRIVED. It did not, so the PASS below asserts nothing"* |

**What their removal costs, stated rather than absorbed.** They were the only **executable** statement in this repository that a peer declaration is not an install. That statement survives as prose, at the retirement note in `gates.mjs` and in the catalog comment, and **as prose it is weaker**: [ADR-042](ADR-042.md) ruled that prose is not a control. Nothing today re-derives that distinction, and no gate would notice if a future probe got it wrong again. **That is the honest price of an artifact arriving**, it is not repaired here, and it is section 8's second finding rather than a gap left for a reader to infer from a shorter file.

### 8. What this entry does NOT close

**FINDING 1. The token set is transcribed twice and no gate reconciles the two.** [`e2e/tokens.ts`](../../e2e/tokens.ts) holds the twenty color values, the five banned faces and the six banned verbs, because `SS-04` compares a **computed** color (`rgb(7, 12, 10)`) against the set and a computed color is not a token name. DESIGN_SYSTEM section 2 holds the same values as a table. **If the palette moves and the file does not, the pass enforces a palette nobody chose**, and the failure is silent in the direction that reads as working. The file's own header names it; the mitigation today is that the values carry their token names and sit in the document's order, so the two diff by eye. A `CI-06` gate reconciling them is the repair and it is one registry over from `CI-06i`'s shape.

**FINDING 2. The peer-versus-install distinction is now prose only.** Section 7.

**FINDING 3. `SS-05`'s configured number is chosen here and DESIGN_SYSTEM deliberately does not fix it.** The row reads *"fewer than a configured number of elements per viewport"*. Three is written in `e2e/tokens.ts` with its arithmetic: section 4.2 has exactly one shadow and it is for overlays, so a compliant viewport carries a shadowed rounded element only while an overlay is open; zero would fail the surface the system permits and three refuses the fourth. **That is a parameter this entry sets and a founder may move**, and it is the only number in the pass that is a judgment rather than a transcription.

**FINDING 4. DG-13 through DG-16 are not checkable and are not checked.** DESIGN_SYSTEM section 8 says so first: they are judgments, and *"pretending a script could do them would produce a check that fails on good copy and passes on bland copy, which is the wrong direction"*. The seeded fixtures carry the banned section order and the two-abstract-noun feature titles anyway, so a reviewer running the pass has the specimen in front of them.

**Approval line: UNSIGNED. One clause, executable, and scoped to the two files this entry rules rather than to a package-wide count.**

> **With any one of the eight check functions in [`e2e/slop-score.ts`](../../e2e/slop-score.ts) replaced by `return [];`, `pnpm exec playwright test` reports `8 failed, 8 passed`, and the only check named in a `did not fire` message is the one that was blinded. With none blinded it reports `16 passed`.**

Checkable in one command: `pnpm exec playwright test`.

**It was executed in this session, once for each of the eight**, and each run named exactly the blinded check and no other: `SS-01 did not fire` through `SS-08 did not fire`, in eight separate runs. The clean run reports `16 passed`. The seeded direction additionally fired in the other direction on demand: a 4px `#3b82f6` left border added to the compliant site fixture turned four tests red naming `SS-02` **and** `SS-04` at one selector, and the fixture was restored.

**Why THIS clause and not a count.** A clause naming a package-wide test count has drifted six times in this corpus ([ADR-077](ADR-077.md), [078](ADR-078.md), [083](ADR-083.md), [093](ADR-093.md), [096](ADR-096.md), [109](ADR-109.md)) and every time the **ruling held while the enumeration did not**, because the count is a fact about the whole repository on a day and any session may move it. The clause above is a fact about **two files this entry writes**, and the numbers in it are properties of the fixture set rather than of the tree: `8 failed, 8 passed` is two seeded surfaces times four renderings, and it moves only when somebody adds or removes a surface, which is the same diff that would have to update it.

**The direction it fails in.** If the clause is wrong, a check is passing vacuously and this entry has overstated what the stage proves. **That is the expensive failure and it is the one the clause is aimed at**, because a slop-score check that silently stops reading looks exactly like a surface that is clean.

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.** Three things are judgments rather than measurements. **`SS-05`'s configured number is three** and DESIGN_SYSTEM left it open. **`1.56.1` over `latest`** trades currency for a gate that runs today, and section 4's table is the arithmetic but not the trade. **And the whole shape of ruling 3** accepts a merge blocker whose compliant direction is currently self-referential, on the argument that the seeded direction is the part that is a control. Whether a gate worth blocking merges on should ship before there is a page for it to read is the founder's call, and nothing in this session turns it into a number.

**Every load-bearing claim was executed.** Section 2's four claims are the four commands beside them. Section 4's revision table was read out of each candidate's own `browsers.json`, and the launch was run. Section 5's delta is the lockfile's `packages:` keys diffed before and after, and the audit and install-script lines are quoted from their commands. Section 7's finding is quoted from `gates.mjs check` and its two retirements from `falsify.mjs` run before the edit. The approval clause is eight runs and one restore.
