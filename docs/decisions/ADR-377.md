## ADR-377: a citation that carries only a path and a line cannot be made checkable on this corpus, the fifty dead pointers are repaired and the FORM is left alone, and the pointer that died inside the checker itself was true the day it was written and blind for three independent reasons (2026-09-06, status: proposed)

**RULED: OUTCOME (c). THE FIFTY ARE REPAIRED WHERE SOMEBODY OWNS THEM AND THE FORM IS LEFT ALONE.** Every alternative was measured before it was refused, and the measurements are the deliverable rather than the ruling.

### 2. THE RULING

**RULED: (c). REPAIR THE DEAD POINTERS. LEAVE THE FORM ALONE.** Four findings carry it, each measured before it was believed.

1. **A form change is 8,250 sites.** Outcome (a) is a migration of ninety-five percent of this corpus's citations, and it cannot be enforced on new citations only, because `RI-15` never reads git history and says so as its own miss (7). **The alternative to history is a grandfather register of 8,250 entries, and a register that large is an exemption list with a different name.**
2. **Outcome (b) is measurably dead. The content is not there to check against.** Section 3.
3. **Widening `IDENTIFIER_GLUE` is a distraction for the fifty and a bad trade on its own.** Section 4.
4. **The remedy that would have caught the one pointer this row repaired is not a check at all**, because the two files it lives in are legitimately full of false citations. Section 5.

**AND THE RULING CARRIES ONE RULE THAT IS WORTH MORE THAN THE THREE REFUSALS.** A dead pointer is repaired by finding its SUBJECT, never by tracking where the old line's text went. **[ADR-375](ADR-375.md) did the latter and got it wrong**, section 8, and it is the most careful row in this corpus.

### 3. Outcome (b), measured before it was refused: the content is not there

**THE PROPOSAL IS TO VERIFY A PATH-PLUS-LINE CITATION AGAINST CONTENT RATHER THAN POSITION.** The corpus writes quotations beside its pointers, so the obvious second datum is the quoted phrase: assert it appears near the line cited. **It needs no migration, because the quotes are already written.** So it was measured.

Over the 1,558 bare citations in checkable scope, taking the last quoted phrase of twelve characters or more within `CITATION_SUBJECT_SPAN` before each pointer:

```
bare citations in scope                                1,558
carrying a quoted phrase near them                       335   (21.5%)
  quote lands within +-3 lines of the pointer               1
  quote is elsewhere in the cited file                      6
  quote is NOWHERE in the cited file                       58
  not resolvable to one file, or too short                270
```

**ONE.** The quotes this corpus writes beside its pointers are quotations of ADRs, of prose, of its own earlier sentences. **They are not quotations of the cited line**, and the 58 that appear nowhere in the file they sit beside are the proof: a check built on them would raise 64 findings of which 6 are drift candidates and 58 are the check misreading a document written for a reader.

**THIS IS [ADR-371](ADR-371.md) SECTION 5.2's MEASUREMENT ARRIVING AT A DIFFERENT CHECK.** That row refused to lift `RI-35`'s `test/` exclusion because it bought two lines out of thirty-five, and wrote that widening the walk while the needles are empty is motion. **The needles here are emptier.**

**A PATH AND A LINE HAS ONE DEGREE OF FREEDOM AND NOTHING TO CHECK IT AGAINST**, which is the general statement of the whole finding. `RI-15`'s strong leg works because a backticked name is a SECOND, INDEPENDENT statement of the same fact, and the two must agree. Where the subject is prose there is no second statement, and no check can invent one. **Outcome (b) is not a weaker version of outcome (a); it IS outcome (a), asking the citation to carry a second datum, and the corpus's citations do not carry one.**

### 4. `RI-15`'s known hole: is widening the glue part of the answer, or a distraction

**IT IS A DISTRACTION FOR THE FIFTY AND THE ANSWER IS MEASURED IN TWO PARTS.**

**FIRST, IT REACHES NONE OF THEM.** [ADR-357](ADR-357.md) section 10 found that `IDENTIFIER_GLUE` admits at most one linking word from `at|in|is|see`, so no name binds across *"at module scope ("*. That hole is real and it is about a citation that HAS a backticked name a few words away. **The fifty do not have one.** They carry a path and a line inside a markdown table cell, or beside a quoted sentence. Widening the glue to four words binds not one additional citation pointing into `wiring.test.ts`.

**SECOND, ON ITS OWN MERITS IT IS A BAD TRADE, AND THAT WAS RUN RATHER THAN ARGUED.** Admitting up to four ordinary words with no sentence end:

```
citations that bind NO name today and would bind one      49
  of those, would PASS                                    16
  of those, would go RED                                  33
```

**THIRTY-THREE RED, AND THE SAMPLE SAYS WHY.** `schema` bound to `scope.ts:1559`, `affiliate` bound to `checkout.ts:1211` with the nearest occurrence 117 lines away, `ledger` bound to `routes/auth.ts:643` where the word appears nowhere. **These are [ADR-212](ADR-212.md)'s finding recurring**: a wider glue binds the nearest common word rather than the subject, and this file's own header already says a rule that turns honest citations red is a rule somebody deletes.

**SO THE GLUE IS NOT WIDENED HERE AND THE HOLE STAYS NAMED**, which is what [ADR-357](ADR-357.md) and [ADR-369](ADR-369.md) each did with it before this row. **What is new is that it is now measured against the population it would be widened for, and it does not reach it.**

### 5. The second blindfold, which nobody had named: `RI-15` cannot read its own package

**THE POINTER THIS ROW REPAIRED WAS INVISIBLE FOR TWO INDEPENDENT REASONS AND ONLY ONE OF THEM WAS ON RECORD.**

`citedReasonFiles` returns every source file EXCEPT those under the checker's own package. **So `RI-15` reads 753 source files and cannot read the six it is written in.** The exclusion's stated reason is honest and is measured in its own header: those files' pointers are the grammar's worked examples and the suite's seeds.

**AND THE EXCLUSION IS CORRECT AND MUST NOT BE NARROWED, WHICH IS THE OPPOSITE OF WHAT THIS ROW EXPECTED TO FIND.** The header's own numbers reproduce in shape on this tree:

```
source files inside the excluded package                  13
citations inside it                                      218
  in the two files whose subject IS the grammar          212
  in the other eleven files                                6
findings if the exclusion were lifted                    107
  in the two files whose subject IS the grammar          107
  in the other eleven files                                0
```

**NARROWING THE EXCLUSION FROM THE PACKAGE TO THE TWO SPECIMEN FILES WOULD NEWLY GUARD SIX CITATIONS, ALL OF WHICH PASS TODAY.** That is [ADR-371](ADR-371.md)'s two-of-thirty-five one register over, and it is refused on the same ground.

**AND THE DEEPER REASON IS STRUCTURAL RATHER THAN ARITHMETIC.** `repo-invariants.mjs`'s header REPRODUCES FALSE CITATIONS ON PURPOSE, as the specimens the grammar is explained with: it quotes a pointer at `admin-writes.ts:266` for a declaration at `:269` because that is the defect the check exists for. **A file that must contain false citations cannot be checked for false citations.** So the two files are permanently exempt, and every TRUE claim they make about this tree is permanently unguarded.

**WHICH IS WHY THE REPAIR IN SECTION 7 IS A CHANGE OF FORM AND NOT A REPOINT.** Nothing can hold a line number honest in those two files, so those two files must not carry one.

### 6. The third blindfold, found by a seed that went the wrong way

**A SEED WRITTEN TO REDDEN `RI-15`'s STRONG LEG WENT GREEN, AND CHASING IT DOWN PRODUCED A PROPERTY NOBODY HAS RECORDED.**

The seed was the corpus's own citation shape, `` `principal(request)` (`admin-writes.ts:12`) ``, inserted into `apps/api/test/wiring.test.ts`. It bound no name. **The same sentence, inserted one line earlier, binds `principal` and reddens the check; one line later it binds nothing; one line later again it binds.** Measured at six adjacent insertion points, three bound and three did not, with an identical token list beside the pointer in every case.

**THE CAUSE IS THE SCANNER'S RUNNING PAIRING STATE AND IT IS THE DOCUMENTED DESIGN RATHER THAN A BUG.** `citationsIn` walks backticks left to right; a token that is not a citation surrenders its closing backtick to be the next token's opener, and a citation consumes two. **So whether the two backticks immediately before a pointer are the name's own depends on every backtick and every citation earlier in the file.** The header argues for this and the argument is good: read locally, a stray backtick costs one binding instead of every binding below it.

**BUT THE CONSEQUENCE IS WORTH STATING PLAINLY AND HAS NOT BEEN.** The NAMED form is not reliably checkable either. **The same sentence is a control in one position and a reading aid three lines down**, and nothing reports which it became. That is a third reason a citation goes unchecked, alongside the prose subject and the own-package exclusion, and it is the one that most weakens outcome (a): a migration to the named form would not buy what it appears to buy.

**IT IS REPORTED AND NOT REPAIRED.** Changing the pairing re-reads 8,723 citations, which is the widening [ADR-357](ADR-357.md) and [ADR-371](ADR-371.md) each declined for smaller changes.

### 11. Where this entry is most likely wrong

- **The census counts what the checks' reader parses, and that is the point and also the limit.** A pointer written in a shape `citationsIn` refuses is not in the 8,723. The raw-grep count of pointers into `wiring.test.ts` is 185 where the reader sees 168; the 17 are inside fenced blocks, generated spans, and the checker's own package, all excluded by shape. **The true corpus figure is therefore a floor, and the checkable fraction of 5.4% is if anything an over-estimate.**
- **Outcome (b) was measured with one quotation grammar.** Straight and curly double quotes, twelve characters or more, within 400 characters before the pointer, matched case-insensitively after collapsing non-alphanumerics. **A richer extraction would find more than one hit.** It would have to find several hundred more to change the ruling, and the 58 quotes that appear nowhere in the file they sit beside argue that the shortfall is the corpus's and not the probe's.
- **Section 6's instability is characterised by measurement rather than by reading the walk through.** Six insertion points, three bindings, an identical token list beside every one. **The stated cause is the header's own account of the scanner**, and if the real cause is narrower than that, the property still stands and the explanation moves.
- **Section 9 reads obstacle 2 as changed rather than gone, and a founder may read it as gone.** The retired clause is named rather than quoted, so there is no history string; whether that makes a `retired` registration impossible or merely awkward is a judgement about what leg 1 should anchor to, and it is not this row's.

### 14. Open questions, recorded as options because none is this row's to answer

1. **Should the census be re-derived on a schedule, or is knowing it once enough?** This row derived 8,723 / 473 / 8,250 by hand-running the checker's reader. **Options: (a) nothing, and the next row that needs it derives it again, which is what happened here; (b) a gate that reports the fraction without failing on it, which this corpus has no precedent for; (c) a gate that fails when the named fraction FALLS, which would go red on any honest documentation wave and is almost certainly wrong.** **(a) is what shipped**, on [ADR-371](ADR-371.md)'s measure-rather-than-extend.

### 15. Approval

**Status `proposed`. The approval line below is UNSIGNED and merging is not signing.** Inherited as well as owed: [ADR-357](ADR-357.md) and [ADR-360](ADR-360.md) through [ADR-376](ADR-376.md) are all `proposed` and unsigned, and this row settles none of them.

  **What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.**
