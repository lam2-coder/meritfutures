## ADR-205: `detector.run_completed` is blocked by two DIFFERENT defects, and only one of them is its own row's  (2026-08-28, status: proposed)

**[Session 382](../sessions/2026-08-28-session-382.md) transcribed `flag.raised` from [`EVENTS:354`](../architecture/EVENTS.md) and REFUSED `detector.run_completed` at [`EVENTS:358`](../architecture/EVENTS.md), and the refusal is why this entry exists.** It was right to refuse. What it could not do inside its fence was establish **which kind of defect it had hit**, and the answer turns out to be that it had hit two, of two different kinds, stacked. **The first is a plain omission in one row.** The second is not a defect in that row at all: **this catalogue has no way for any row to say that its fact belongs to the firm**, so a producer reading it cannot tell an untenanted firm fact from an untenanted trader fact, and it correctly refuses both.

**THE NUMBER IS SPENT AND THE TEST FOR SPENDING IT WAS THE RIGHT ONE.** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `205` reserved it conditionally and said to return it if the row were transcribable after all or if the gap were an omission an editor could close inside an existing ruling. **It is neither.** [`docs/architecture/EVENTS.md`](../architecture/EVENTS.md) is frozen and must move twice, and the second move introduces a notation the corpus does not have.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `205`, session **391**, dispatched as `claude/detector-run-completed-payload`. The reservation is amended in place under [ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3.

- **NO MIGRATION NUMBER WAS RESERVED, none is needed and none is taken.** Section 8 establishes that rather than assuming it: every column this ruling reaches already exists, `events.identity_id` and `events.account_id` are already nullable, and `detector_runs.id` is already a `uuid` primary key. **No `SystemReason` member, no `SqlExecutorReason` member, no `pg` import, no cast past a key type, no ledger account named, no door widened.**

- **THE ENTRY SHIPS `proposed` WITH AN UNSIGNED APPROVAL LINE.** What is **derived** is the two-defect diagnosis in section 2, the census in section 3, the refutation in section 5 and the citation arithmetic in section 6. What is **judged** is clause 2 -- that a catalogue row may declare itself firm-level at all, and that the declaration is an explicit per-row mark rather than a derivation. Section 11 is that question.

- **A DISPATCH PROMPT IS NOT A SOURCE.** Every claim below was read at its own file. **Two of the premises this session inherited were sharpened rather than repeated and section 9 is both.**

### 1. THE RULING

> **1. `EVENTS:358`'s PAYLOAD GAINS `detector_run_id` AS ITS FIRST FIELD.** It holds [`detector_runs.id`](../../packages/db/migrations/0008_risk.sql), the `uuid PRIMARY KEY` at `0008:61`. The producer's `subject_kind` for the name is `detector_run`, the singular of the table, and its `subjectField` is `detector_run_id`. **The field name is transcribed and not coined**: `detector_run_id uuid NULL REFERENCES detector_runs(id)` is already the spelling this schema uses for a reference to that table, twice, at `0008:134` and `0008:216`.
>
> **2. A CATALOGUE ROW WHOSE FACT BELONGS TO THE FIRM SAYS SO ON ITS OWN ROW, WITH THE MARK `**FIRM**` AFTER ITS EVENT NAME.** A producer may write an `events` row reaching neither `identity_id` nor `account_id` for a marked name, and for no other name. The mark is EXPLICIT, PER ROW, and NEVER INFERRED -- section 5 is the derivation that was available and is refused on evidence.
>
> **3. `detector.run_completed` IS MARKED AND IT IS THE ONLY ROW MARKED TODAY.** The ruling sweeps nothing. **Thirty-three other readable rows in this catalogue name neither tenancy column** and every one of them stays refused, because an unmarked untenanted row is UNDECIDED rather than a trader's, and refusing an undecided row is the honest reading of it.
>
> **4. THE MARK IS NOT A LICENCE TO LEAVE A TENANCY UNRESOLVED.** A row qualifies only where the fact belongs to no identity and no account **and there is no correct one to resolve**. Where a producer holds something that names one, [ADR-191](ADR-191.md)'s rule stands unchanged and unweakened: it resolves the tenancy at write time and the row is a trader's.
>
> **5. `assertTenanted` IS NOT RELAXED. IT LEARNS TO READ A DECLARATION.** Today it refuses every untenanted row because no row declares; after this entry it refuses every untenanted row that does not declare. Its argument, its message and its position inside the caller's transaction are unchanged, and the declaration it reads is the frozen registry's rather than the producer's, on [ADR-159](ADR-159.md) clause 1.
>
> **6. `schema_version` DOES NOT MOVE AND STAYS `1`.** Section 1 of the catalogue keeps two shapes readable forever because a 2031 audit reads a 2027 row, and that rule protects readers of rows that EXIST. **No row of this name has ever been written**, which section 10 establishes at a live column rather than assuming.
>
> **7. THE AMENDMENT IS LINE-COUNT PRESERVING AND THAT IS PART OF THE RULING.** Both edits above line 435 are made IN PLACE at the same line count and the new section 14 is APPENDED. Section 6 is the arithmetic and the reason.

### 2. What is actually missing, which is the question this session was sent to answer

**THE DISPATCH NAMED THREE CANDIDATE DEFECTS -- the payload is ABSENT, or AMBIGUOUS, or names a shape NO DOCUMENT DEFINES -- and said the three take three different repairs.** The row hits two of the three, on two different fields, and telling them apart is what makes the repair two clauses instead of one guess.

| Blocker | Which defect | Repair |
|---|---|---|
| `events.subject_id` has no payload field to read | **ABSENT.** A plain omission, and the subject it omits is a real primary key the producer already holds | clause 1: add the field |
| `assertTenanted` refuses the row | **NOT A DEFECT IN THIS ROW.** The payload is right; the CATALOGUE has no vocabulary for what this row is | clause 2: give the catalogue the vocabulary |

**THE PAYLOAD IS NOT AMBIGUOUS ANYWHERE, and that was checked field by field rather than assumed.** All six fields resolve to a definition: `detector`, `detector_version`, `trading_day`, `rows_scanned` and `flags_raised` are columns of [`detector_runs`](../../packages/db/migrations/0008_risk.sql) at `0008:62`, `0008:63`, `0008:64`, `0008:67` and `0008:68`. `duration_ms` is the one field with no column, and it is not undefined either: [`0064_reconciliation_runs.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0064_reconciliation_runs.sql) states in terms that a run record stores no `duration_ms` because *"`finished_at - started_at` is the same number and a stored copy can disagree with the two instants beside it"*, and [`runner.ts`](../../apps/worker/src/detectors/runner.ts) computes exactly that subtraction. **So no field of this payload names a shape no document defines, and the third candidate defect is refuted rather than left open.**

### 3. The subject, which is one omission in a class of eight

`events.subject_id` is `uuid NOT NULL` at [`0017:47`](../../packages/db/migrations/0017_events_and_audit.sql) and `CatalogueRow.subjectField` names the payload field it is read from. The payload at `EVENTS:358` declared no field ending `_id` at all.

**IT IS NOT ALONE AND THE CENSUS MATTERS, because it is what makes this row's repair a field addition rather than a new escape hatch.** Eight of the 102 readable catalogue rows declare no `_id` field:

| Row | Its subject |
|---|---|
| `identity.linked` | the link. Payload carries `identity_a` and `identity_b`, which are uuids |
| `sms.budget_breaker_tripped` | a budget scope. `scope_key` is a key and not a row |
| `ingest.file_late` | **a file that never arrived.** There is no row and there cannot be one |
| `feed.stalled`, `feed.resumed` | a feed. `feed` is a name |
| `liability.snapshot_taken` | the snapshot. `snapshot_on` is a date |
| `treasury.coverage_changed` | a coverage position. `as_of` is an instant |
| **`detector.run_completed`** | **the run, which IS a row: `detector_runs.id`** |

**`detector.run_completed` is the one of the eight whose subject is a real row in this schema that the payload simply failed to name.** That is why clause 1 is an addition and not a general relaxation of `subject_id`: the other seven raise a question this entry does not answer and must not pretend to, and a nullable subject or a synthetic uuid invented to fill one would have made all eight writable while making none of them true.

**THE PRODUCER ALREADY HOLDS THE VALUE**, which is what makes the addition cheap and is not by itself a reason to make it. [`runner.ts:295`](../../apps/worker/src/detectors/runner.ts) binds `const id = await insertRun(tx, {...})` and `emitRunEvents` is called at `runner.ts:313` inside that same closure, with `id` in scope. **The binding at the emit site is `id`; `runId` is the outer variable the closure's return is assigned to at `runner.ts:294`**, and this entry says so precisely because two earlier records said "the runner holds it as `runId` one line above the emit", which is true of the function and not of the line.

### 4. The tenancy, which is the catalogue's gap and not this row's

**A DETECTOR RUN BELONGS TO NOBODY AND THERE IS NO CORRECT OWNER TO RESOLVE.** [`detector_runs`](../../packages/db/migrations/0008_risk.sql) at `0008:60` declares no column against `identities(id)`; a nightly sweep covers the whole population and the accounts it disagreed with are its OUTPUT, recorded on `risk_flags`, rather than its subject. **The corpus already states that test for this exact table**: [`0064_reconciliation_runs.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0064_reconciliation_runs.sql) says of its own sweep *"There is no identity column and there is no correct one... That is `detector_runs`' sentence and this table is `firm` for the same reason."*

**SO ADR-191'S MECHANISM IS EXHAUSTED HERE RATHER THAN UNUSED.** That entry rules that *"a producer resolves the row's tenancy at write time from what it has, whatever the payload happens to name"*, and it is the right answer for `payout.freeze_expiring`, whose producer holds a `payout_request_id` that names an account. **The producer of `detector.run_completed` holds a `detector_runs` row and a `detector_runs` row names nobody**, so there is nothing to resolve from and the absence is the fact rather than an oversight.

**AND THE SCOPE REGISTRY ALREADY SAYS FIRM ROWS ON THIS TABLE ARE LEGITIMATE.** [`scope.ts`](../../packages/db/src/scope.ts)'s registration of `events` states *"WHAT A SCOPED READ RETURNS IS THIS PERSON'S EVENTS BY EITHER PATH AND THE FIRM ROWS BY NEITHER"* and, of the polymorphic subject, that `subject_id uuid NOT NULL` is *"on every row including the firm ones"*. **So what was missing was never the concept. It was the per-row declaration**, without which `assertTenanted` cannot tell which rows those are -- which is exactly what its own refusal message has been asking for: *"where the event genuinely belongs to the firm rather than to a trader, say so on its row in EVENTS. No row says that today."*

### 5. The derivation that was available, and why it is refused on evidence

**THE TEMPTING RULE IS THAT A ROW WITH NO `TL` CONSUMER IS FIRM-LEVEL.** `TL` is section 2's per-account chronological view, so a row no per-account view reads looks like a row no account owns, and the rule would need no new notation at all. **It is wrong, and the counterexample is a row this corpus has already ruled on.**

`payout.freeze_expiring` at [`EVENTS:255`](../architecture/EVENTS.md) is consumed by `ALERT, FEED` and by no `TL`. It is a fact about **one trader's** frozen payout whose payload merely fails to name a column, and [ADR-191](ADR-191.md) rules it a **producer** defect to be repaired at the producer. **Under the consumer derivation it would be silently admitted as a firm fact and that trader's warning would be written untenanted, forever, on an append-only table.** A rule whose first application writes the wrong answer to the one case the corpus has already decided is not a rule.

**Of the 34 readable rows naming neither tenancy column, 33 have no `TL` consumer.** The derivation would have admitted all 33 in one move, including that one. **The mark is explicit and per row because the corpus can afford to decide 33 rows one at a time and cannot afford to decide them wrong all at once.**

- **Approval:** `___________________________` (UNSIGNED. **`E2` READ OWED** on clause 2, which changes what a producer may write to an append-only table retained forever. **Merging is not signing.**)

  **What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.**

  **THE FIRST THING TO READ IS CLAUSE 2, BECAUSE IT IS THE ONLY THING HERE THAT IS A JUDGEMENT.** Clause 1 is transcription: a column exists, a producer holds it, a field name is already spelled twice in the schema. **Clause 2 invents a notation for a frozen catalogue and hands a producer permission it has never had**, and the permission is to write a row that no per-trader read will ever return. The entry argues that is right for a fact about the firm and that the scope registry already said so. **A founder who disagrees should say so now**, because the alternative is not "no firm events" -- it is that this row, `liability.snapshot_taken`, `treasury.coverage_changed`, `feed.stalled` and every future nightly-job event stay permanently unwritable, and the pressure to invent a fake tenancy for them will arrive one session at a time.

  **THE SECOND IS THAT THE MARK IS A MARKDOWN TOKEN AND NOT A TYPE.** `**FIRM**` in a table cell is read by a test that parses the document as text, which is the same mechanism binding every other field in this producer, and it is genuinely weaker than a column would be. **A reviewer who wants tenancy to be a first-class COLUMN of every catalogue table should commission that instead** -- it is the shape the consumer legend has -- and the reason this entry did not is section 6: a column added to every table in the catalogue rewrites 105 rows and moves every line below the first one, which invalidates 85 live citations, 31 of them in a deployable nobody may currently open. **That is a real cost and it is a scheduling argument rather than a design one**, which is exactly the kind of trade a founder should get to overrule.

  **THE THIRD IS THAT THIS ENTRY DECIDES ONE ROW AND LEAVES 33 UNDECIDED ON PURPOSE.** Every one of them will now be decided by whichever session next needs it, one at a time, from inside a fence that is about something else. **That is slower and it is more likely to be right**; the opposite policy is a sweep, and a sweep here is one session deciding 33 tenancy questions it was not sent to answer. A founder who would rather have the sweep should commission it as its own slice with its own read, not accept it as a side effect of a detector event.

  **WHERE THIS ENTRY IS MOST LIKELY WRONG, named rather than left to be found.** **The mark says a row belongs to the firm and nothing anywhere says who may READ it.** Section 4's registry quote establishes that a scoped read returns firm rows to nobody, and `events` is class `either` rather than `firm`, so `firmDb()` -- whose key type is `FirmTableKey` -- **cannot read this table at all**. The row is reachable through `systemDb(reason)` and through the BI replica its own consumer cell names, and that is enough for the two consumers it declares. **But "reachable by the firm" is being inferred from the absence of an obstacle rather than from a rule**, and the day an admin surface wants to render firm events beside trader events on one screen, somebody will discover there is no door shaped like that question. The entry does not open one, because opening a door is not what a catalogue mark is for; it names the gap here so that the discovery is a citation rather than a surprise.
