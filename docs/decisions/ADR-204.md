## ADR-204: the per-account eligibility forecast is a projection of ONE gate under five stated assumptions, because ten of the eleven conditions that decide eligibility have inputs no stored row can know seven days out  (2026-08-28, status: proposed)

**[Session 380](../sessions/2026-08-28-session-380.md) went looking for `B1`'s field and found the field was two things: the next seven TRADING days, which it built and executed, and a PER-ACCOUNT ELIGIBILITY FORECAST over them. THIS ENTRY RULES THE SECOND HALF.** The dispatch and the allocation row both say the forecast has **no source anywhere**, and **that premise did not survive the check**: [`M01:785`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) names a producer in terms, *"`evaluatePayout` projected forward over the calendar"*. **The finding is worse than an absence and it is why this survived four sessions.** `evaluatePayout` takes a `RuleState`, a `RuleState` for a future day comes only from `advanceDay`, and `advanceDay` requires that day's `DailyMark`. **So the corpus names a function and no argument exists to call it with**, and a named producer reads, to every session that greps for one, exactly like a solved problem. **Section 2 is the arithmetic that replaces it**: of the eleven conditions `evaluatePayout` conjoins, **exactly one -- R-37, the cadence gap -- has an input a stored row already fixes for the whole horizon**, and by this codebase's own pinned analysis that one is dominated or co-binding on every plan in the lineup. **THE FIELD IS NOT RULED OUT**, and section 6 says why [ADR-202](ADR-202.md)'s precedent does not carry: the value set here is not empty. **What is ruled out is the word FORECAST.** What this field may carry is a projection of one gate under five assumptions that are part of the ruling, and section 3 shows -- without data -- that the literal reading of `by_day[]` puts `sum(by_day[].cents)` at up to seven times `total_cents` on a plan in the shipped lineup, that the projection is blind to precisely the scenario `AS-09` created it to make visible, and that the account-level field cannot discharge that launch requirement at all because it carries no identity dimension.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `204`, session **388**, dispatched as `claude/per-account-eligibility-source`. **NO MIGRATION NUMBER WAS RESERVED, none is needed, and none is taken.** Section 8 establishes that rather than assuming it, and it does so by **refuting** the leg the reservation would have been for.

- **MONEY PATH.** `eligible_next_7d` is the input [ADR-011](ADR-011.md)'s same-day top-up trigger reads and the figure the payout wallet is funded against. [**`E2`**](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) **READ IS OWED** on this ruling. The entry ships `proposed` with an **UNSIGNED** approval line. **Merging is not signing.**

- **THIS SLICE RULES AND DOES NOT BUILD.** No producer is written and no half-forecast is shipped. `apps/**` and `packages/**` are **not opened for edit at all**; eleven files under them were READ and every read is reported in section 10. One approved plan moves, additively and on the line it already occupied, and section 9 is why that one and not the others.

- **WHAT IS PROVED, WHAT IS DERIVED, AND WHAT IS JUDGED**, separated before the argument starts. That `M01:785`'s producer cannot be called is **PROVED** in section 1, from three type signatures. That ten of the eleven conditions have inputs unknowable at the horizon is **DERIVED** in section 2, gate by gate, at each gate's own line. That the literal reading of `by_day[]` overcounts by the cadence gap is **PROVED** in section 3a and needs no data. What is **JUDGED** is that the field survives (section 6), the population it carries (ruling 2), the placement rule (ruling 3), and which of the two undeclared readings of "next 7 days" is taken (ruling 5).

- **A DISPATCH PROMPT IS NOT A SOURCE.** Every claim below was read at its own file before it was relied on, and the two that came back different are section 1 and section 8.

### 1. The producer the corpus names, and the three signatures that close it

**THE DISPATCH SAYS THE FORECAST HAS NO SOURCE ANYWHERE. IT HAS ONE.** [`M01:785`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md), section 4's endpoint table, verbatim:

> \| `GET /admin/eligible-forecast` \| `evaluatePayout` projected forward over the calendar \| Projects which accounts clear their gates in the next 7 trading days, aggregated at identity level as well as account level (AS-09) \|

That is a named function, a named input and a named aggregation. **It is the only sentence in the corpus that says how this figure is produced**, and no ADR has ever read it: `ADR-199` section 6 reaches the same field through the schema and lists four tables, and does not cite this row.

**The producer cannot be called, and it takes three signatures to show it.**

1. [`evaluate.ts:63`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/payout/evaluate.ts) declares `evaluatePayout(state: RuleState, plan: ResolvedPlan, ctx: PayoutContext)`. The verdict is a pure function of the `RuleState` it is handed. **There is no day parameter**: "projected forward" cannot mean asking the same state about a later day, because the state IS the day.
2. So a forward evaluation needs a `RuleState` for day `D+k`. The only thing in this estate that produces one is [`advance.ts:179`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/advance.ts), `advanceDay(input: DayInput)`.
3. [`types.ts:762`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts) declares `DayInput.mark: DailyMark`, and it is **not optional**. A `DailyMark` for a day that has not closed does not exist, is not derivable, and is the whole subject of `R-06`: *"Every evaluation is against the last closed day and nothing more recent"* ([`M01:470`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)).

**So `M01:785`'s producer is a call whose argument is the future.** That is a stronger finding than the absence the dispatch carried, and it is the mechanical reason the gap survived: a session grepping for a producer finds one, and the sentence that names it is in an approved plan.

**`R-06` IS NOT AN OBSTACLE THE FORECAST HAS TO ROUTE AROUND. IT IS THE RULE THAT MAKES A FORECAST A DIFFERENT KIND OF OBJECT.** The engine is deliberately incapable of saying anything about a day it has not seen close. Anything this field carries about day `D+3` is therefore produced OUTSIDE the engine, by an adapter, under assumptions the engine does not hold and cannot check. **Naming those assumptions is what this entry is for.**

### 2. The eleven conditions, and the one whose input is already fixed

`engineEligible` is the conjunction of six engine gates ([`gates.ts:280`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/payout/gates.ts), `allGatesPass`, terms listed rather than reduced), and the trader's actual eligibility is that conjunction AND the four context gates `R-40` supplies plus `R-38`. [`0015:116`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql) states the split in the schema's own words: *"The trader's actual eligibility is engine_eligible AND every context gate, and that combined answer is DELIBERATELY NOT STORED HERE, because it is not a property of the day; it is a property of the moment it was asked."*

**A condition is FORWARD-KNOWABLE if a row stored at the last closed day fixes its verdict for every day of the horizon.** Read that way, at each condition's own line:

| # | Condition | Input, at its source | Forward-knowable |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `tradedDays`, `R-33` | `tradedDaysCount >= min_trading_days` ([`M01:526`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)) | **NO.** The counter advances only when the trader takes a fill. Monotone up, so a PASS today is a pass at `D+7`; a FAIL today is undecidable |
| 2 | `winDays`, `R-34` | `winDaysCount >= required_count`, *"counted over trading days strictly after `payoutAnchorDay`"* ([`M01:527`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)) | **NO.** A win day is a future P&L fact, and `R-47` ([`M01:563`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)) resets the counter on settlement |
| 3 | `buffer`, `R-35` | `withdrawable = max(0, balance_cents - size_cents - buffer_cents)` ([`M01:528`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)) | **NO.** `balance_cents` is the daily mark. Moves both ways |
| 4 | `consistency`, `R-36` | best day as a share of period profit ([`M01:529`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)) | **NO.** Numerator and denominator both move with trading, and the share can move DOWN |
| 5 | **`cadenceGap`, `R-37`** | `count(trading days d : cadenceAnchorDay < d <= basisDay) >= cadence_gap_trading_days` ([`M01:530`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)) | **YES, AND IT IS THE ONLY ONE.** The anchor is a stored column that moves only on settlement (`R-46`, [`M01:562`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)), and the days are published calendar data |
| 6 | `minimumAmount`, `R-39` | `min(withdrawable, cap) >= 10000` ([`M01:532`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)) | **NO.** `withdrawable` again |
| 7 | `accountActive`, `R-40` | `accounts.status` and phase ([`M01:533`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)) | **NO.** A breach on any day of the horizon flips it, and `R-24` makes it terminal |
| 8 | `kycVerified`, `R-40` | `KycState`, supplied at read time by `D-M19-1` | **NO.** `expired` is a member of the vocabulary ([`types.ts:895`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts)) |
| 9 | `notFrozen`, `R-40` | `payoutsFrozen`, *"account level OR identity level, RESOLVED BY THE CALLER"* | **NO.** An operator action, and it is not a property of the day at all |
| 10 | `reconClear`, `R-40` | `reconBlocked`, `FM-04` | **NO.** A mismatch found tomorrow blocks tomorrow |
| 11 | `noPayoutInFlight`, `R-38` | an outstanding external-leg withdrawal for this identity ([`M01:531`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)) | **NO**, and it is the one the forecast's own subject matter changes |

**ONE OF ELEVEN.** And what the corpus says about that one, in this codebase's own comment at [`gates.ts:228`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/payout/gates.ts):

> ON EVERY V1 PLAN THIS GATE IS DOMINATED OR CO-BINDING AND NEVER THE REASON A PLAN IS FAST. PW-02b fires on Merit Rapid (gap 1 against 3 win days) and EC-049 stands; PW-02a fires on Core EOD and Direct, where the gap and the win-day gate co-bind at 5.

**So the only condition a projection can advance is, on every shipped plan, not the condition that is holding the account back.** That is the whole difficulty in one line, and it is a fact about the plan lineup rather than about the engine: on Merit Rapid the trader is waiting on win days, and on the two Core plans the cadence gap clears on the same day the win-day gate would if the trader wins every session. **A projection that advances only R-37 therefore advances almost nothing, and the accounts it does advance are the accounts that were going to be eligible anyway.**

### 3. Three things that follow before any data is read

#### 3a. The literal reading of `by_day[]` overcounts by the cadence gap, and it is arithmetic rather than a worry

`ADR-199` section 6 and `M01:785` both read the group the same way: *"which accounts clear their payout gates on each of the next seven trading days"*. **On each.** Nothing removes an account from the eligible set except acting on it, and nothing in this response acts. **So an account eligible at the last closed day clears its gates on all seven days and appears in all seven `by_day` rows**, at the same `cents`, and

> `sum(by_day[].cents) = 7 x total_cents`

for that population. **The factor is not hypothetical and it is not seven everywhere**: it is bounded below by the account's own `cadence_gap_trading_days`, and [`MERIT-RAPID-50K.json:18`](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/plans/MERIT-RAPID-50K.json) declares `"cadence_gap_trading_days": 1`, so on that plan an account can legitimately be eligible on every day of the horizon. `CORE-50K` and `CORE-150K` declare `5`.

**Nothing on this response declares which figure `P-M6-03` shows.** [`API_CONTRACT:904`](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) gives the group as `{ total_cents, account_count, by_day: Array<{ trading_day, cents, accounts }> }` with no comment, and no document anywhere states a relation between `total_cents` and the `by_day` sum. **Two producers reading the same words produce numbers that differ by up to a factor of seven, and both are right.** That is not a shape defect; it is the same figure under one name meaning two things, which is what `EC-074` is entirely about.

#### 3b. The projection is blind to the scenario the field was created for

`AS-09` ([`M01:967`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)) is the attack: *"one identity holding the plan maximum of ten copy-traded accounts will have all ten cross the **win-day gate** on the same trading day, because they trade identically."* Its counter is this field: *"a correlated wave is visible in the Eligible-Next-7-Days number before it lands."*

**The gate the wave crosses is condition 2 in section 2's table, and it is not forward-knowable.** A projection that advances only R-37 sees a correlated win-day wave on the day it lands and not one day earlier. **The control and the mechanism available to serve it do not meet.**

**And `M05` had already said so, on the line that matters most and that no entry has cited.** [`M05:439`](../plans/M05-payout-system.md): the forecast *"depends on trader behavior rather than on gates the engine computes"* and is *"a **harder** prediction, not an easier one"*. That sentence was written about the wallet's external-withdrawal sibling; **it is true of the approval-volume figure too, for the reason section 2 derives**, and reading it as applying only to the sibling is what left the account-level figure looking mechanical.

#### 3c. The account-level field cannot discharge the launch requirement whatever it contains

`AS-09`'s counter and [`M05:429`](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)'s *"launch requirement, not a dashboard nicety"* both require the figure **at identity level**. `LiabilityResponse.eligible_next_7d` carries `total_cents`, `account_count` and a per-day array, and **no identity member**. `ADR-188` clause 5 refused adding one and its finding 6 records that `SD-M6-01`'s `eligible_next_7d_identity_max_cents` and `eligible_next_7d_identity_max_id` **landed in no migration**.

**So the launch requirement is unserved by this field on the wire and by the schema, independently of everything above**, and a perfect account-level projection would not move it. This entry does not repair that -- the field is another fence's and the columns are another entry's -- but it refuses to let a ruling on derivability read as a ruling that the launch requirement is met.

### 4. THE RULING

**1. The field carries a PROJECTION, never a forecast, and the two words are not interchangeable here.** A forecast states what will happen. This states what follows from the last closed day if nothing happens. The distinction is the difference between a number an operator may fund against and a guess wearing a date, and section 2 is why no third option exists.

**2. THE POPULATION IS `E` UNION `C` AND NOTHING ELSE.**

- **`E`**, eligible at the last closed day: `engineEligible` is true and all four `R-40` context gates pass and `R-38` passes, resolved at read time.
- **`C`**, cadence-pending inside the horizon: every engine gate except `cadenceGap` passes, `cadenceGap.pass` is false, all four context gates and `R-38` pass, and `cadenceGap.nextEligibleTradingDay` is a day in the horizon [session 380's `readTradingHorizon`](../../apps/api/src/admin-source/liability.ts) returns.

**Nothing else is admitted, and the exclusion is the ruling rather than a simplification.** An account one win day short is NOT in the population, because admitting it requires predicting a fill. **The cost is stated in ruling 6 rather than hidden.**

**3. `by_day[]` IS FIRST ELIGIBILITY INSIDE THE HORIZON, AT MOST ONE ROW PER ACCOUNT.** Every member of `E` is placed on the horizon's first day; every member of `C` on its own `nextEligibleTradingDay`. **Therefore `sum(by_day[].cents) == total_cents` and `sum(by_day[].accounts) == account_count`, exactly**, and a producer that violates either identity is wrong rather than differently-opinionated. Ruled against the literal reading on 3a's arithmetic and on what the panel is for: `P-M6-03` and `ADR-011`'s trigger want the shape of an arriving wave, and a series that repeats the standing population on every day is a flat line with the wave inside it.

**4. `by_day[].cents` IS `min(withdrawable_cents, cap_for_next_ordinal)` AT THE LAST CLOSED DAY, AND `total_cents` IS ITS SUM.** This is `EC-074`'s arithmetic and `P-M6-02`'s formula, not a new one, and `R-42`'s rung is the one `ordinalForNextPayout` resolves for that account. Integer cents throughout; `withdrawable_cents` is `bigint NOT NULL` on `rule_states` and `INV-05` makes it non-negative in the formula.

**5. "NEXT 7 DAYS" INCLUDES THE ACCOUNTS ELIGIBLE TODAY.** The reading is undeclared on the wire and both readings are available on the words. It is ruled inclusive because `ADR-011`'s trigger measures near-term demand against the wallet balance, and money that can leave today is the first money that can leave in the next seven days. A figure excluding `E` would understate that demand by exactly the accounts most able to draw on it, on the day `AS-M5-03`'s wave is being funded for.

**6. THE FIVE ASSUMPTIONS ARE PART OF THIS RULING AND A PRODUCER MAY NOT CHOOSE THEM.** Section 5.

**7. THE FIGURE IS A LOWER BOUND ON THE POPULATION AND IS NOT A BOUND ON THE MONEY, AND BOTH HALVES ARE STATED WHEREVER IT IS SHOWN.** Section 5's table derives each direction from its own assumption. A panel that prints this number without the second half reproduces `EC-074` inside `EC-074`'s own remedy.

**8. `AS-09`'s CONTROL IS NOT THIS FIELD, AND WHAT WOULD SERVE IT IS NAMED HERE AND NOT BUILT.** What makes a correlated wave visible in advance is not a date. It is that N accounts under one identity sit the same distance from the same gate with identical counters -- a read of `engine_gates` at the last closed day, needing no projection at all and defeating none of section 2. **This entry names it, rules that it is a different figure from `eligible_next_7d`, and takes no field, no column and no number for it.**

**9. WHERE THE HORIZON HAS NO ANSWER, THE FIELD HAS NO VALUE, AND THE SHAPE OF THAT ABSENCE IS `ADR-203`'s.** Section 7.

### 5. The five assumptions, and what each one costs

**A projection under assumptions nobody wrote down is a guess with a decimal point.** These are the assumptions ruling 2's population is exactly the set of accounts for which they are not self-contradictory.

| | Assumption | What it buys | What it costs, and in which direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** | No account trades inside the horizon | Conditions 1, 2, 4 hold their verdicts | **UNDERSTATES THE POPULATION.** Every account that will trade into eligibility is absent, and `AS-09`'s wave is made of exactly those (3b) |
| **A2** | Balances are the last closed day's | Conditions 3 and 6 hold, and `cents` is computable | **NOT A BOUND IN EITHER DIRECTION** on the money. This is `EC-074`'s own design rather than a defect: the figure answers *how much could be claimed*, never *how much will be earned* |
| **A3** | No context gate changes | Conditions 7 to 10 hold | **OVERSTATES.** A breach, a freeze, a KYC expiry or a recon block inside the horizon is invisible, and three of the four are operator or engine events with no forward notice |
| **A4** | No payout is requested or settles inside the horizon | Makes `E` and `C` well defined at all | **OVERSTATES**, and it is the self-referential one. `R-46` moves both anchors on settlement and `R-47` resets the win-day counter; a projection that does not apply the payouts it predicts is describing a world its own output has already left |
| **A5** | The account's pinned `plan_version_id` does not change | `cadence_gap_trading_days` and the ladder caps are fixed | Small, and it is the only one a stored row can verify after the fact |

**A1 AND A4 ARE THE PAIR THAT FORCES RULING 2's NARROWNESS.** Under A1 no account can gain a win day, so an account failing condition 2 today can never enter the population; admitting it anyway would require predicting a fill, which is A1's negation. Under A4 no anchor moves, so `nextEligibleTradingDay` computed at the last closed day is still the right date on day six. **The two together are the largest assumption set under which the population is well defined, and ruling 2's `E` union `C` is exactly the set they define.** Anything wider is not a wider projection; it is a different object with no stated assumptions.

**THE HONEST SUMMARY, WHICH BELONGS ON THE PANEL AND NOT ONLY IN THIS ENTRY:** *the accounts that qualify today, plus those whose only remaining wait is the cadence gap, placed on the day that gap expires.*

### 6. The field is NOT ruled out, and why `ADR-202`'s precedent does not carry

**The allocation row names ruling it out as the option to beat, on a precedent one week old.** `ADR-202` ruled a required object ABSENT rather than inventing one for it. **That precedent does not carry, and the reason is precise rather than a preference.**

`ADR-202` section 2 proves an **empty set**: rule A permits only *absent*, rule B permits only objects of a stated form, the intersection has no member, so every value the field could hold is an invention. **Here the set is not empty.** `E` union `C` is a real, non-empty, checkable population; `min(withdrawable, cap_for_next_ordinal)` over it is a figure `0009`'s own column comment, [`M06:93`](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) and [`EC-074:3`](../edge-cases/EC-074.md) each define independently and identically; and the payout wallet is already funded against exactly that population. **Ruling the field out would delete a figure the corpus computes for another column anyway.**

**Three further reasons, in decreasing weight.**

1. **`P-M6-02`'s stored column already commits to the same predicate.** `bounded_near_term_cents` is a landed `bigint NOT NULL` whose definition is *"accounts eligible now or inside 7 trading days"*, stated identically in `0009`'s column comment, `M06:93` and `EC-074:3`. **A ruling that no such population is definable would make a landed money column undefinable**, which is a far larger claim than this slice holds and one no evidence here supports.
2. **`FM-M6-07`'s test is passed rather than failed.** The allocation row cites *"either constant alarms or none ... is the same as no chart"*. A cadence projection is neither: it moves when anchors expire, it is silent when they do not, and the accounts it names are checkable against `rule_states` the next morning. **What fails `FM-M6-07` is not this figure; it is a figure that claims to see the win-day wave.**
3. **Absence would leave `ADR-011`'s trigger with no input at all**, and that trigger is `AS-M5-03`'s named payout-trust control. Deleting a control's only input to avoid overstating it is the trade `AS-09` already refused when it ruled `OQ-7` visibility rather than a ceiling.

**WHAT IS RULED OUT IS THE CLAIM, NOT THE FIELD.** `M01:785`'s *"`evaluatePayout` projected forward over the calendar"* is ruled unproducible, and section 9 amends it.

### 7. What this entry needs from `ADR-203` and does not take

**Session 386 holds `LiabilityResponse` under `ADR-203` (unlinked until it lands, on `CI-06a`) and this entry does not touch the wire.** No field is added, removed, renamed or retyped by this ruling, and `API_CONTRACT` is byte for byte unchanged.

**One thing is REPORTED to that fence rather than decided here.** Session 380 established that the trading horizon is a discriminated union: it can answer `exhausted` (coverage runs out inside the seven days) or `uncovered` (no load declares coverage over the anchor), and `ADR-042` F-4 is why those are values rather than short arrays. **Under either, `eligible_next_7d` has no value**: `by_day[]` cannot be built over days the calendar cannot speak for, and a `total_cents` folded over a partial horizon is `B2`'s understatement with a different denominator.

**What this entry needs from `ADR-203`, stated so 386 can price it:** that `eligible_next_7d` be able to say *there is no horizon* **with a reason**, from the same vocabulary that carries `payout_velocity`'s *there is no window* and `per_plan[].cusum`'s *uncalibrated*. **Two reasons are needed and they must stay distinct**, on `0032`'s and `ADR-042` F-4's own rule that an exhausted calendar and an uncovered one are different answers and only one is safe to act on. **Whether that is a null, an omitted key or a tagged value is 386's ruling and not this one's**, and if 386 rules a shape that carries no reason, this entry's requirement is the finding rather than the shape.

### 8. `B5` re-derived: leg 1 stands, LEG 2 DOES NOT, and that is why no number is taken

Session 380 measured `B5` as two independent legs, either of which alone blocks the group. **Both were re-derived at their sources. One holds and one does not.**

**LEG 1 HOLDS, AND IT IS NOW THE WHOLE BLOCKER.** `rule_states.engine_gates` is where `cadenceGap.nextEligibleTradingDay` lives and **no adapter writes it**. [`ports.ts:308`](../../apps/worker/src/batch/ports.ts) declares `writeRuleState(row: RuleStateRow): Promise<void>` and [`nightly.ts:307`](../../apps/worker/src/batch/nightly.ts) calls it, so **the call site is real and it is the implementation that is missing**: the only things satisfying that port are test doubles and [`world.ts:559`](../../scripts/demo/world.ts), which refuses by design. **The refinement matters**: session 380's entry says *"nothing in this tree writes it"*, and read strictly that is about implementations rather than call sites. Nothing about the blocker changes; what changes is that the work owed is one adapter and not a call path. **And the stored shape is still declared by no primary source**: `API_CONTRACT` carries no `engine_gates` shape at all, `EngineGateResults` types every cents member `bigint` (which JSON cannot carry), and [`0015:124`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql)'s own column comment names eight gates -- *"profit target, drawdown, win days, minimum days, consistency, cadence, cap, minimum payout"* -- where the engine produces six.

**LEG 2 DOES NOT HOLD, AND THE REFUTATION IS THIS ENTRY'S LARGEST FINDING OUTSIDE THE RULING.** Session 380's leg 2 is that `R-37` counts by `sequence` subtraction and `trading_calendar` declares no such column, so *"the only substitute available to an adapter is the date arithmetic AS-06 forbids"*. **The column is re-confirmed absent and the conclusion does not follow.**

- **`sequence` IS A PROPERTY OF THE SLICE, NOT OF THE TABLE.** [`types.ts:224`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts) documents `CalendarDay.sequence` as *"Dense index into the calendar"*, and `CalendarSlice` is a VALUE the caller constructs (`ADR-049`).
- **EVERY READER OF IT IS SLICE-LOCAL.** [`calendar.ts:213`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/calendar.ts)'s `tradingDaysBetween` returns `to.day.sequence - from.day.sequence` for two days looked up in **one** slice, and [`calendar.ts:245`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/calendar.ts)'s `tradingDayAtSequence` scans **that same slice**. `RuleState` stores no sequence, and `nextEligibleTradingDay` is a DATE. **No sequence is ever compared across two slices, so the base is free and only density inside the slice is load bearing.**
- **TWO CALLERS ALREADY SYNTHESIZE IT AND BOTH SAY WHEN THAT IS SAFE.** [`bridge.ts:112`](../../scripts/demo/bridge.ts) assigns `sequence: sequenceBase + i`; [`calendar.ts:22`](../../packages/golden-loader/src/calendar.ts) states the limit exactly: *"FOR A CONTIGUOUS FIXTURE CALENDAR THE TWO AGREE ... What it cannot do is discriminate ... on a calendar with a hole in it, position and calendar index diverge."*
- **AND A SLICE BUILT FROM `trading_calendar` OVER ONE COVERED INTERVAL CANNOT HAVE A HOLE.** `0032`'s coverage rule is that a day inside coverage which the table does not hold is **positively not a session**, so it is correctly absent from a dense index; a day outside coverage is unknown, and coverage is what bounds the slice. **Position over the ordered, holiday-filtered rows of one covered interval IS the dense calendar index, by construction rather than by luck.** Session 380 already built that walk: [`readTradingHorizon`](../../apps/api/src/admin-source/liability.ts) reads `trading_calendar` and `trading_calendar_loads` together, filters `is_holiday = false`, takes rows in ascending order and refuses to bridge disjoint loads.

**THEREFORE NO MIGRATION IS OWED AND NO NUMBER IS TAKEN.** The row reserved none conditionally and none is needed: what leg 2 actually names is a **`CalendarSlice` loader over the covered interval**, which is code in the `apps/api` fence and not DDL. **Taking a number to add `trading_calendar.sequence` would store, on a published exchange calendar, a value every reader derives and no reader compares across slices** -- and it would fix that value at the moment of maximum ignorance about the loader that has not been written, which is `ADR-199` section 7's own rule for returning a number unspent.
