### 4. THE STOP CONDITION. A RACE THAT IS A 409 AND NOT A 500, RED AND GREEN BOTH WATCHED

**THE CASE LIVES IN [`payouts.test.ts`](../../apps/api/test/payouts.test.ts) AND IT DRIVES TWO CONCURRENT REQUESTS**, through the real route, over a fixture that models exactly two database facts.

**FACT ONE: THE PARTIAL UNIQUE INDEX SPANS TRANSACTIONS.** `payout_requests_no_in_flight_uq` refuses a second row while the first is written and NOT YET COMMITTED, so the fixture stamps its in-flight set at the INSERT and not at the commit. **A fixture that stamped at commit would let both inserts through and would be agreeing with its own fake**, which is [ADR-084](ADR-084.md) section 7's defect.

**FACT TWO: `ScopedTx.lockScope()` IS A ROW LOCK HELD UNTIL THE TRANSACTION ENDS.** It is a FIFO queue in the fixture, joined in `lockScope()` and left when `transact` returns by either path. **IT IS TAKEN ONLY IF THE PORT CALLS IT**, which is the whole of what the case measures.

**THE ONE DESIGN CHOICE THAT COULD HAVE MADE THE CASE A LIE IS NAMED IN THE FIXTURE.** The barrier is *"both requests are inside `transact`"* and **not** *"both have read"*. A barrier on the READ is unreachable under the lock, because the second transaction is parked in `lockScope()` and will never read; the case would hang rather than go green, which is a fixture deciding the answer instead of measuring it. A barrier on ENTRY is reached in both worlds and leaves everything after it to the code under test.

#### 4.1 RED, on the tree before the four lines

The case was written first and run against `origin/main` with no `src/` line moved. **THE FIRST FAILURE IS [ADR-293](ADR-293.md) SECTION 3.3'S SENTENCE, PRINTED BY A TEST RUNNER:**

```
AssertionError: expected [ 200, 500 ] to not include 500

```

**`[ 200, 500 ]` IS THE FINDING.** The loser reached the insert, took a unique violation, and `unwiredOrThrow` rethrew it, because a unique violation is not a `PayoutBackendUnwired`. The contract specifies a named 409 on that request and the deployment answered an internal error.

The last of those is the one worth naming: two inserts against one committed row would mean the INDEX refused the second, which is the backstop doing the work [ADR-293](ADR-293.md) section 3.3 ruled it cannot do.

### 9. Approval

**Status `proposed`. The approval line below is UNSIGNED and merging is not signing. THE `E2` LINE-BY-LINE READ IS OWED AND HAS NOT HAPPENED.**

**THERE IS A MONEY-PATH DIFF TO READ AND IT IS FOUR LINES OF CODE.** Everything else in it is prose, fixtures and cases.

- **Decision (1):** **[ADR-293](ADR-293.md) section 3.5 is BUILT.** The member, the delegation, the call as the first statement, and the docblock. Nothing was re-decided.
- **Decision (2):** **`transact` STILL TAKES NO LOCK**, and the case asserting it is now a refusal with a reason rather than a placeholder for an open question.
- **Decision (3):** **The race is asserted end to end and both transcripts are recorded.** RED `[ 200, 500 ]`, GREEN one 200 and one 409, four seeds watched.
- **Decision (4):** **`RI-35` is LEFT FREE and the corpus gate is refused for the fourth time**, on inherited reasoning rather than new reasoning.
- **Alternatives considered:** taking the lock inside `transact`, where no interface change is needed (**refused**: [ADR-293](ADR-293.md) section 3.4 refused it on the merits and this entry does not reopen a settled ruling to save a line); locking only around the insert (**refused**: it leaves the gate decided outside the lock, which is the arrangement the index already fails to rescue); asserting the race by a second insert alone rather than through the route (**refused**: the finding IS the status code, and a case that stopped at the port would not have seen the 500); rewriting [ADR-295](ADR-295.md)'s D2 repair in this row's own words (**refused**: the repair exists and a diff that changes no fact is noise on a money-path review).
- **Consequences:** slice 6's read-then-write is serialised. Slice 9 loses one of the two preconditions [ADR-295](ADR-295.md) gave it, and the one that remains is the founder read. **Every payout request now takes a row lock on the caller's identity for the life of its transaction, including requests about to be refused**, which is the cost [ADR-293](ADR-293.md) section 3.5 measured and accepted and which `decideWithdrawal` has been paying deliberately for longer.

**WHAT A FOUNDER READ ADDS AND THIS ENTRY CANNOT.**

**THE FIRST QUESTION IS THE COST SENTENCE ABOVE.** A lock held across a refused request is a lock held for nothing, and the payout path refuses more often than it approves. [ADR-293](ADR-293.md) answered it once, on `decideWithdrawal`'s docblock: a gate evaluated before the lock is evaluated against a state another transaction can still change. **The opposite judgement is available and is cheap to take**: move the call below `gateIdentityStatus` and pay the lock only on requests that reach an account read. That would leave `identityStatus()`'s own read outside the lock, and it would need its own entry, because the placement cases in [`payouts.test.ts`](../../apps/api/test/payouts.test.ts) are written to refuse it.

**THE SECOND IS WHETHER THE RACE CASE EARNS ITS COMPLEXITY.** It is the most machinery any single case in [`payouts.test.ts`](../../apps/api/test/payouts.test.ts) carries: a barrier, a queue, and a store that models an index. **The alternative was the text assertion alone**, which is what [`checkout.test.ts`](../../apps/api/test/checkout.test.ts) settles for. This entry's answer is that the text assertion proves the line is there and proves nothing about what it BUYS, and the thing it buys is the difference between a 409 and a 500 on the door where money leaves the firm. **A founder who disagrees deletes one case and keeps three.**

**AND ONE THING THIS ENTRY IS NOT A JUDGEMENT ABOUT.** [ADR-291](ADR-291.md) still asserts the sentence three later documents record as retired (section 1). **Whether it is amended, or left standing with the finding beside it, is the founder question [ADR-299](ADR-299.md) section 14 asked in the same shape**, and it is now asked about a specific line.
