### 9. What the narrowing made harder, priced explicitly because the dispatch asked

**[ADR-426](ADR-426.md) SECTION 9 NAMED THIS TRADE AND THIS ROW MAKES IT TWICE, ON MONEY BOTH TIMES.**

Two cases exist to watch a VALUE refusal fire, and each seeds a row PostgreSQL could not produce:

1. `a number where cents are expected is refused, never coerced` seeds `closingBalanceCents: 5_000_000`, a `number`, into a `bigint` column, to prove `requireCents` refuses rather than coerces.
2. `a row that cannot be addressed is refused rather than guessed at` seeds `id: 7`, a `number`, into `reconciliations.id`.

**Under the narrowed port both columns ARE `bigint`, so both seeds are compile errors and the input each case exists to watch is no longer expressible without casting past the type.**

**THE CASTS STAY, THE CASES STAY, AND THE REFUSALS STAY.** The type says *"this cannot happen"* on the authority of a transcription that nothing verifies. Deleting a cast would delete the case; deleting a refusal would trade a guard that FIRES for a claim nothing checks. **On this slice the guard being traded away is the one standing between a float and a balance**, which is `compareBalances`' own stated reason at [`recon/sweep.ts:331`](../../apps/worker/src/recon/sweep.ts): *"A subtraction on `number` would lose digits above 2^53 silently, and a balance is money."*

**THE COST IS CHARGED ONCE RATHER THAN AT EVERY SITE THAT PAYS IT.** One named helper, `malformed(row, overrides)`, carries the single cast and the whole argument above it, so a reader meets the trade in one place and a future case cannot quietly acquire a bare `as`.

**AND THE PRESSURE IN ITS USEFUL DIRECTION IS WHY THE SUITE'S STORE IS STRONGER, AT A PRICE [ADR-426](ADR-426.md) DID NOT PAY.** That entry's fixtures *"already carried every column, so the cost was one `id`"*. **These carried four of eighteen on `dailyMarks` and two of twenty-nine on `ruleStates`**, so making the store hold `DeclaredRow` cost **41 fixture columns no case reads**, plus a whole `reconciliations` builder. What it buys is that `mark({ closingBalanceCentss: 1n })` is now a compile error; before this row it was a passing test over a row no migration can produce. **That is the trade in one sentence: 41 lines of fixture nobody reads, against a class of green run that means nothing.**
