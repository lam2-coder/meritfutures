### 9. Where this entry is most likely wrong

**Named rather than left for a reader to find.**

**THE CENSUS IN SECTION 3 IS A REGEX OVER `export const UNWIRED_`, AND THAT IS A NAMING CONVENTION TOO.** This row's whole finding is that the previous census counted a suffix; the replacement counts a prefix. **A refusing default named anything else is invisible to both**, and nothing in this tree requires the prefix. The direction of the error is the safe one, since an uncounted default reads as a port with no default rather than as a port that is served, but it is the same class of defect one letter along.

**THE CONSTRUCTOR CENSUS READS RETURN TYPES AND `RuleStateWriterIo` PROVES IT MISSES THINGS.** It is stated in section 3, asserted in case `6.1`, and it is still a census that cannot see a value composed at a call site. A second inline composition would be counted as absent.

**AND SECTION 4.3'S "NONE" IS A JUDGMENT ABOUT WHAT SCHEDULABLE MEANS.** A reader who takes it to mean *the process would start and not immediately throw* would put the replay self-audit in the third bucket, because on a book that agrees it runs and returns. **This entry takes it to mean the job could be given a clock without a decision nobody has taken**, and by that reading the audit fails on all four of section 4.3's items. **If the founder takes the looser reading, the replay row is schedulable and item 2 becomes the whole of the argument against it.**
