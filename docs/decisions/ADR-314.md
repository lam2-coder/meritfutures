## ADR-314: the `LT-06` builder gets a public door, `LT-09` still derives from it so the two cannot part, and the ground the door was shut on had expired three entries later on the same day  (2026-09-04, status: proposed)

**[ADR-305](ADR-305.md) section 7 slice 1, and it is one keyword.** `walletWithdrawalApprovalPosting` is [`packages/ledger/src/reversal.ts:218`](../../packages/ledger/src/reversal.ts) and it now carries `export`, and [`packages/ledger/src/index.ts:77`](../../packages/ledger/src/index.ts) hands it out of the package. **What is not one keyword is the property that survives it**, and sections 3 and 4 are that.

**THE ABSENCE WAS RE-DERIVED BEFORE ANYTHING MOVED AND BOTH DISPATCHED LINE NUMBERS WERE RIGHT.** The declaration was at `:218` and its only caller at `:265`, inside `walletWithdrawalFailurePosting`, which is `LT-09`'s leg. So `@merit/ledger` could build the reversal of a posting it could not hand anybody, and the withdrawal driver four slices downstream had no `LT-06` to compose.

**THE DOOR WAS SHUT DELIBERATELY AND ITS GROUND HAD ALREADY EXPIRED.** [`apps/api/test/lt06-posting-timing.test.ts:72`](../../apps/api/test/lt06-posting-timing.test.ts) said the builder is private *"deliberately, because nothing in this tree rules `LT-06`'s writer"*, and [`reversal.ts`](../../packages/ledger/src/reversal.ts) said the same thing one level down in its own docblock. **[ADR-270](ADR-270.md) clause 2 ruled that writer.** Section 2 dates both sides, and the dating is the reason this is a slice rather than a reversal of anything: nobody overrode a deliberate decision here, because the decision was conditional on its own face and the condition was met.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `314`, session **505**, branch `claude/the-builder-with-no-door`. **NO MIGRATION NUMBER IS TAKEN OR RESERVED** and no `RI-nn` is minted.

- **MONEY PATH: this is the ledger, and `E2` READ IS OWED.** Ships `proposed` with an **UNSIGNED** approval line. **Merging is not signing.** It inherits the `E2` debt on [ADR-189](ADR-189.md), [ADR-267](ADR-267.md), [ADR-270](ADR-270.md) and [ADR-305](ADR-305.md), each `proposed` and each UNSIGNED, because it acts on the last two and moves a line in the first one's file.

- **NOTHING IS WIRED AND THE COUNTS DO NOT MOVE.** No port gains an adapter, no manifest gains a dependency, [`apps/api/src/start.ts`](../../apps/api/src/start.ts) was not opened, and `useWithdrawalBackend` answers `503` after this entry exactly as it did before. Section 5 states what the export makes constructible and, at greater length, what it does not.

- **WHAT IS MEASURED AND WHAT IS JUDGED.** That the builder was module-scoped, that its only caller was `LT-09`'s, that the two comments carried the expired ground, and that the wallet leg occurs exactly once in `packages/ledger/src` are all **MEASURED**, at the lines cited, on this branch. The three seeded defects in section 4 were **RUN**, and the restores checked by `sha256sum`. What is **JUDGED** is that a public builder plus a composed `LT-09` is a safer shape than a public builder plus a second construction, which is section 3.

### 3. THE RULING

> **Clause 1. `walletWithdrawalApprovalPosting` IS EXPORTED FROM `packages/ledger`, UNDER THE NAME IT ALREADY HAD.**
>
> `reversal.ts:218` gains `export` and `index.ts` gains one specifier in the block it already has for that module. **No name is minted.** The corpus, the tree and five documents already spell this builder `walletWithdrawalApprovalPosting`, and renaming it at the moment it becomes public would invalidate every one of those citations to buy nothing.

> **Clause 2. `LT-09` STILL DERIVES FROM IT THROUGH `reversalPosting`, AND THAT IS THE CONDITION OF CLAUSE 1 RATHER THAN A DETAIL OF IT.**
>
> `walletWithdrawalFailurePosting` composes the same builder it always composed. **There is ONE construction of `LT-06`'s arithmetic in this package and there must stay one.** A second, written beside the first so the export could have its own copy, is [ADR-092](ADR-092.md) section 5's two-statements-of-one-fact hazard **arriving on the money path**: two spellings of one posting that agree on the day they are written and are checked by nothing afterwards. `reversalPosting` negates nothing and builds no leg, so an exact `LT-09` is only exact while it reverses the same `LT-06` a caller would post.

> **Clause 3. THE PROPERTY IN CLAUSE 2 IS ASSERTED MECHANICALLY, IN BOTH DIRECTIONS, AND NOT LEFT AS PROSE.**
>
> [`packages/ledger/test/lt06-approval-builder.test.ts`](../../packages/ledger/test/lt06-approval-builder.test.ts), five cases. Two hold the door (the index exports it; the header it builds names the withdrawal and posts under the withdrawal's own bare key). Three hold clause 2: the entries of `LT-09` as the entry-for-entry negation **of the exported builder**, the composition read out of `reversal.ts` as text, and the wallet leg counted across the whole of `packages/ledger/src` by walking the directory rather than by a file list. **Section 4 is the transcript of watching each of them fail.**

> **Clause 4. `lt06-posting-timing.test.ts` PIN 1 IS UNCHANGED IN EVERY ASSERTION, AND NO CASE IS WRITTEN THAT WOULD MAKE IT LOOK REDUNDANT.**
>
> PIN 1 reads `LT-06`'s signs through `LT-09`'s negation and is [ADR-267](ADR-267.md) clause 1's whole evidence for the fact that entry rests on. It is the check that an export altering the arithmetic turns red, so it is the check this slice was measured by. **Only its `:72` comment moved.** `lt06-approval-builder.test.ts` deliberately does NOT re-assert `LT-06`'s direction, and says so in its header, because a second case asserting the same thing another way is how a load-bearing case comes to look like a duplicate and gets deleted by a later tidy.

> **Clause 5. NOTHING IS WIRED, AND THE EXPORT IS NOT A STEP TOWARDS WIRING THAT ANYBODY MAY TAKE HERE.**
>
> Section 5 enumerates it. The counts stand where [ADR-312](ADR-312.md) left them.

### 4. What was watched red, rather than argued

**Three defects, each seeded into the tree, run, and restored.** `sha256sum -c` confirmed both source files byte-identical after the pass, and `git status` showed no seed left behind.

| # | The defect | What fired |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A second construction of the wallet leg added in a new `packages/ledger/src/` module (`identityAccount('trader_wallet', ...)` against `firmAccount('withdrawals_in_flight')`) | **`the wallet leg is stated ONCE`**: *"expected [ …(2) ] to have a length of 1 but got 2"*. The directory walk found it in a file no list names, which is why the walk is a walk |
| 2 | `walletWithdrawalFailurePosting` stops composing the builder and restates the two legs inline | **`the composition is one call`** and **`the wallet leg is stated ONCE`**, both red |
| 3 | The exported builder's two legs swapped | **`lt06-posting-timing.test.ts` PIN 1**: *"expected 25000n to be -25000n"*. The other nine cases in that file stayed green, which is the case being specific rather than the file being sensitive |

**DEFECT 2 IS THE ONE WORTH READING, BECAUSE ONE CASE STAYED GREEN UNDER IT.** The entry-for-entry negation case passed, because the forked legs were byte-identical to the builder's on the day the fork was written. **That is precisely the hazard**: a behavioural case catches two copies only once they have already parted, which is one commit too late on the money path. The textual case catches the fork itself. **Neither is sufficient and the pair is why there are two**, and this is the honest limit of what a suite can hold about a duplicated statement.

### 8. Approval

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.**

**THE FIRST THING IS WHETHER CLAUSE 2 IS A CONSTRAINT OR A PREFERENCE, BECAUSE THE WHOLE SHAPE OF THIS SLICE RESTS ON IT.** The alternative was available and is ordinary: give the export its own construction of `LT-06`'s two legs and leave `LT-09` composing the private one, which is simpler to read at each site and needs no directory walk to police. **This entry refuses it on [ADR-092](ADR-092.md) section 5**, and section 4's defect 2 is the evidence that the refusal is not theoretical: two copies that agree today are caught by nothing behavioural until they disagree, and by then a withdrawal has been reversed by the wrong amount. **A founder who reads the duplication risk as acceptable would take a smaller diff and a weaker property**, and the case against that reading is that this is the ledger and the copies would be in different packages within two slices.

**THE SECOND IS THE TEXTUAL CASE, WHICH IS THE ONE MOST LIKELY TO BE WRONG HERE.** `the wallet leg is stated ONCE` counts lines containing `identityAccount('trader_wallet'` across `packages/ledger/src`. **It is a syntactic check standing in for a semantic property**, and it is evadable: a second builder taking the account as a parameter, or spelling the code through a variable, passes it. It is also a check a legitimate future change trips, because a second posting that genuinely debits `trader_wallet` (`LT-08` is one, and it lives in `apps/api` today) would have to be admitted deliberately. **That admission cost is the point rather than a defect**, on the constitution's own preference for a mechanical assertion over a bigger model, but a founder may reasonably rule that a check with a known evasion buys less than it costs to maintain. **If it is removed, clause 2 keeps only its behavioural half and section 4 says exactly what that half misses.**

**THE THIRD IS THAT THIS ENTRY MOVES A LINE IN A FILE WHOSE `E2` READ IS STILL OWED.** [`reversal.ts`](../../packages/ledger/src/reversal.ts) is [ADR-189](ADR-189.md)'s, `proposed` and UNSIGNED; [`lt06-posting-timing.test.ts`](../../apps/api/test/lt06-posting-timing.test.ts) is [ADR-267](ADR-267.md)'s, the same; the authority this slice acts on is [ADR-270](ADR-270.md)'s, the same. **Four unsigned entries now stack on one builder**, and this one is the first that changes what a deployable outside the package can reach. That is not an argument against merging it, and it is the reason the `E2` read on this diff should be read against `reversal.ts` whole rather than against the four lines the diff shows.

**THE FOURTH IS THE DATING CORRECTION IN SECTION 2, WHICH IS SMALL AND SHOULD NOT BE SILENT.** The dispatching row and the session prompt both say the ground expired eight entries later. It is three, on the entries' own dates, and the same calendar day. **The substance is unaffected and the row's finding stands.** It is surfaced because a count nobody re-checked is the failure this project has named as its own worst, and a session that quietly wrote "eight" into a comment because its prompt said so would be repeating it.
