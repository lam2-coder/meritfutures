## ADR-179: the redaction vocabulary is closed at two members, it was open at exactly one line rather than at a call site, and each member's promise is written per member because the corpus states it three times in three different lengths (2026-08-28, status: proposed)

**`evidence_packs.redaction_profile` is the column a later argument about what was disclosed is answered from**, in [M06:301](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md)'s own words: *"Every export records which profile was used, so a later argument about what was disclosed is answered by a row rather than by memory."* [`0008_risk.sql:257`](../../packages/db/migrations/0008_risk.sql) makes it `text NOT NULL` with **no `CHECK`**, and [ADR-166](ADR-166.md) clause 6 left its vocabulary deliberately open because `P7-j` had not been built yet. `P7-j` ([#423](https://github.com/lam2-coder/meritfutures/pull/423)) then built it, wrote the two strings, **labelled them CHOSEN rather than cited, and took no number**.

**THIS ENTRY CLOSES THE SET AT TWO AND SAYS WHAT EACH MEMBER PROMISES.** `full-detail` and `trader-facts-only`, in the shape `SystemReason` and `SqlExecutorReason` already have in this tree: a union declared in one place, a source-reading case that says how many members it has, and a widening that is therefore a diff on a test which says why the set is closed.

**AND THE OPENNESS WAS NOT WHERE THE DISPATCH SAID IT WAS, WHICH IS THE FINDING THIS ENTRY WOULD KEEP IF IT KEPT ONE.** The allocation row describes *"a redaction profile somebody invents at a call site"*. Measured in the tree, no call site could: `EvidenceRedactionProfile` was DERIVED from the audience map, so at every call site it was exactly the two values and a third string was already a type error. **It was open at exactly one line, and that line is the line a vocabulary change is made on.** `regulator: 'regulator-detail'` in the map type-checked GREEN, because a derived vocabulary cannot refuse a member: it is whatever its map says.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `179`, session **327**, dispatched as `claude/redaction-vocabulary`. Reported as needed by `P7-j` ([session 319](../sessions/2026-08-28-session-319.md) section 5), which held no number and did not take one.
- **NOT A MONEY PATH.** No migration number is allocated and none is taken. Clause 5 carries the DDL for the constraint this ruling wants and explains why it is not written here.
- **`SystemReason` gains no member, `SqlExecutorReason` gains no member, no `pg` import, no cast past a key type, `packages/db` untouched, `0008_risk.sql` byte for byte unchanged.**
- **No port is wired.** [`apps/api/test/wiring.test.ts`](../../apps/api/test/wiring.test.ts) is untouched and its `{declared, wired, blocked}` triple is unmoved. `apps/api/src/admin-source/index.ts` is untouched: this entry adds no member to the composition.
- **`INV-M7-10`'s computed strip list is not reopened**, and nothing in the mechanism moved. Clause 3 describes what it strips; it does not change what it strips.
- **Every claim below was read at its own source on this branch**, and one claim the record carried came back different. Finding 8.

### 2. THE RULING

> **Clause 1. THE SET IS CLOSED AT TWO MEMBERS: `full-detail` AND `trader-facts-only`.**
>
> Joining it is a diff on one line with an argument attached, in the shape finding 4 measures. **The ground is not that an open string is untidy; it is that this column is the RECORD OF A PROMISE and not an input to one.** M06:301 makes the row the answer to a later argument about what was disclosed. A free-text label lets two exports that performed the SAME redaction be labelled differently, and lets one row name a redaction **no code in this tree performs**. Neither is visible until the argument the column exists for is actually happening, which is the worst moment for a record to be unreadable.
>
> **AS-M6-01 is why the bar is closure rather than convention.** `EC-071` prices the disclosure at one burned evaluation, the dossier says these groups coordinate, and the damage is permanent on a single occurrence. A vocabulary that is closed by habit is closed until the first hurried session.

> **Clause 2. IT IS CLOSED BY A DECLARED UNION, AND THE MAP IS ANNOTATED BY THE UNION RATHER THAN BY `string`. THIS CLAUSE IS THE WHOLE OF THE CODE CHANGE.**
>
> Finding 2. A derived vocabulary **cannot refuse a member**, because it is whatever its map says: it is the map wearing a type's clothes. Finding 3 is that stated as a compiler result, in both directions.
>
> **THE MAPPING AND THE VOCABULARY ARE NOW SEPARATE THINGS AND THAT SEPARATION IS THE POINT.** Finding 11: `OQ-F4-03` may yet give `regulator` its own profile. Under the derived shape that ruling was a one-line map edit that turned two assertions red, and both of those assertions are ABOUT THE MAPPING and read as "update me" to a session whose ruling is about the mapping. **The vocabulary would have grown as a side effect of a change that was legitimately ruled, with nobody having argued the new member.** Under this shape the same edit is a compile error naming the type, plus a case that says the set is closed. The session then adds a member deliberately, or does not.
>
> **NO PARALLEL ARRAY OF THE NAMES IS DECLARED**, which is [ADR-092](ADR-092.md)'s landmine avoided rather than repeated: a `readonly EvidenceRedactionProfile[]` beside the union catches a member the union does not have and **never** a union member the array is missing. That entry executed exactly that omission on `DDL_NAMES` and got `tsc` exit 0 with the suite green at one test fewer. Totality is asserted instead between the declaration and its one consumer, in both directions.

> **Clause 3. WHAT EACH MEMBER PROMISES, PER MEMBER, AND THE PROMISE IS THE UNION OF THE THREE APPROVED STATEMENTS RATHER THAN THE SHORTEST OF THEM.**
>
> Finding 8. `GS-112`'s line is the shortest of the three and it is the one the code was built from.
>
> **`trader-facts-only`**
>
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **CARRIES** | The account's own facts whole: the `accounts` and `identities` rows, every fill, every mark, every rule state, the pinned plan version and its `rule_text`, and per flag the FACT that it exists: `flag_id`, `flag_type`, `status`, `first_detected_on`, `tos_clause`. **A "gate result" is `rule_states.engine_gates` and `rule_states.context_gates`** after `SD-06` split it, and not a table |
> | **STRIPS** | Four things by four mechanisms: **(1)** every top-level parameter name of every `is_sensitive` registry row, swept recursively, computed and never listed (`INV-M7-10`); **(2)** the flag's `evidence` bag, `severity`, `detector` and `detector_version`, by the allowlist; **(3)** the `detectors` section, **absent rather than empty**; **(4)** any identifier belonging to another subject, anywhere inside any value, including inside free text |
> | **THE FOUR NEGATIVES MAP ONTO THE FOUR MECHANISMS** | No detector parameter is 1 and 3. No threshold is 1, because **a threshold is a threshold by the NAME it arrives under and not by its value**. No other identity is 4. **No comparison against a population is 2**, because such a comparison can only reach a pack through the `evidence` bag or a `detectors` section and the allowlist admits neither |
> | **`includes_detector_detail`** | `false`, always |
>
> **`full-detail`**
>
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **CARRIES** | Everything `trader-facts-only` carries, plus the whole flag record and a `detectors` section holding every registry row with its parameters and its `is_sensitive` |
> | **STRIPS** | Nothing, and the name is M06:300's own phrase for that |
> | **WHAT IT DOES NOT PROMISE** | **Everything the firm holds.** The subject is ONE account: `readSubject` takes an account id and `exportEvidence` refuses a subject whose `account_id` is not the one requested. **`full-detail` bounds the DETAIL and never the SCOPE**, and a reader of the name is likelier to get that wrong than to get the detector question wrong |
> | **`includes_detector_detail`** | `true`, always |

> **Clause 4. `includes_detector_detail` STAYS DERIVED FROM THE PROFILE AND IS NOT A THIRD PARAMETER.**
>
> It already was; this clause pins it, because a closed vocabulary with a free boolean beside it is half a control. The DDL's constraint makes `audience = 'trader' AND includes_detector_detail = true` unrepresentable and this file has no argument that produces it. **The OTHER lie is the one nothing else catches**: a `full-detail` pack recorded with detail `false` is a row the database accepts and that misdescribes its own bytes.

> **Clause 5. THE `CHECK` IS REPORTED WITH ITS DDL AND NO MIGRATION NUMBER IS TAKEN, AND THE CLOSURE'S LIMIT IS STATED RATHER THAN LEFT TO BE FOUND.**
>
> **This ruling closes the vocabulary at the layer that COMPOSES the value and not at the layer that STORES it.** Two layers below it are open and both are named:
>
> | Layer | State | Repair |
> |---|---|---|
> | [`0008_risk.sql:257`](../../packages/db/migrations/0008_risk.sql) | `text NOT NULL`, no `CHECK` | The DDL below, in a new migration |
> | [`schema.ts:1836`](../../packages/db/src/schema.ts) | `text('redaction_profile').notNull()`, so `string` | A `$type` narrowing, which needs a vocabulary `packages/db` can see without depending on `apps/api` |
>
> **The migration is not written here for two reasons and neither is scheduling.** A merged migration is never edited and only superseded (constitution E2), so this is a new file rather than a change to `0008`; and `packages/db/migrations` is another session's this wave. The DDL is therefore stated exactly, for whoever takes the number:
>
> ```sql
> -- SD-M6-04, ADR-179. The redaction profile is a CLOSED vocabulary of two and
> -- the column recorded it as free text. A profile name that nothing refuses is
> -- a redaction that nothing performs.
> ALTER TABLE evidence_packs
>   ADD CONSTRAINT evidence_packs_redaction_profile_is_ruled
>   CHECK (redaction_profile IN ('full-detail', 'trader-facts-only'));
>
> -- And the profile FOLLOWS FROM the audience (M06:248), which is a second
> -- property and not the same one: the constraint above admits a `trader` pack
> -- labelled `full-detail`.
> ALTER TABLE evidence_packs
>   ADD CONSTRAINT evidence_packs_profile_follows_audience
>   CHECK (
>     (audience = 'trader' AND redaction_profile = 'trader-facts-only')
>     OR (audience <> 'trader' AND redaction_profile = 'full-detail')
>   );
> ```
>
> **`ADD CONSTRAINT` is safe today for a measured reason and it will not stay safe**: finding 7, the table has no rows, because the writer is a port nothing implements. The day it has rows, the second constraint needs a backfill argument.
>
> **The second constraint is the one worth arguing about**, and it is written here rather than silently included: it makes the MAPPING a database rule, and finding 11 says the mapping may legitimately change. That is a cost, and it is the ordinary cost of a constraint, paid by a superseding migration rather than by leaving the rule unwritten.

> **Clause 6. THE TWO NAMES ARE CONFIRMED RATHER THAN RE-CHOSEN, AND THE `CHOSEN` LABEL IS NARROWED RATHER THAN REMOVED.**
>
> Finding 6: each string is assembled from the phrasing of the document that rules the behaviour, so a reader who meets `full-detail` in a row can find the sentence it came from. **`P7-j` labelling them CHOSEN was correct and this entry does not overturn it**: an identifier is not a sentence, and picking a phrase to be an identifier is a choice even when the phrase is quoted. What this entry adds is that the choice is now MADE, with its ground written down, and the ground is that the name quotes its own ruling.
>
> **Two alternatives were available and both are refused with their reasons.**
>
> | Shape | Refused because | What it would cost |
> |---|---|---|
> | **A profile per audience**: `internal-full`, `counsel-full`, `regulator-full`, `trader-facts-only` | M06:300 collapses three into one deliberately and the gate closure calls it *"two tiers"*. Four profiles over four audiences makes `redaction_profile` a second copy of `audience` | **A column that can disagree with itself.** Two names for one state, and every future reader asking which one is authoritative |
> | **Naming by mechanism**: `detector-internals-included` / `detector-internals-withheld` | It names ONE of the four things the trader profile withholds, so the other three read as outside the promise | A name that is accurate and incomplete is worse than one that is vague, because it invites a session to add a threshold on the ground that a threshold is not a detector internal |
>
> **The rename escape is cheap by construction and it is the reason this clause is not a bigger decision.** The profile is recorded ON the pack and a pack is never rewritten, so a superseding name applies to new rows the way `SD-M7-03` supersedes a threshold. Finding 7 measures that the cost is currently zero.

> **Clause 7. THREE THINGS ARE REPORTED AND NOT BUILT, EACH NAMED SO THAT ITS ABSENCE IS A DEBT RATHER THAN A SILENCE.**
>
> 1. **THE COMPUTATION TRACE.** Finding 10. M06:299 and EC-071 both put it in the trader pack's positive list and `GS-112`'s own line does not. The pack today carries the trace's INPUTS, and a trace is a replay through `advanceDay` rather than a column anyone can read. **Building it here would be reopening `GS-112`'s content**, which this fence forecloses, and it is larger than a section: it is the rules engine invoked from an export path.
> 2. **THE FREE-TEXT CHANNEL IS CLOSED FOR ONE NEGATIVE AND NOT FOR THE OTHERS.** `tos_clause` is prose an operator writes and `GS-112` REQUIRES the pack to carry it. Mechanism 4 reaches an identifier inside it because a uuid has a shape a runner can match, which is the defect `P7-j` found in its own control and repaired. **A population comparison written in prose has no such shape**, and neither does a threshold quoted in a clause. This entry does not claim the sweep covers them; it says which negative is mechanically enforced in free text and which are enforced only by the operator writing the clause.
> 3. **`evidence.pack_exported` STILL CARRIES `redaction_profile` AND IS STILL NOT EMITTED.** M06 section 6. The event catalogue is outside this fence, as it was outside `P7-j`'s. The audit is not lost: `0008_risk.sql` calls export *"ITSELF AN AUDITED ACT"* about the ROW.

### 6. Approval

**Approval line: PENDING, UNSIGNED.**

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.**

**MOST OF THIS ENTRY IS A DERIVATION AND SAYS SO.** Findings 1 through 5 are `grep`s, `sed`s and one compiler run. The mapping is a transcription of three approved documents. Clause 2 is a type-system argument that either holds or does not, and finding 3 settles it mechanically. **Three things are not derivations, and they are the read.**

1. **CLOSING THE SET AT TWO IS A BET THAT THE THREE-INTO-ONE COLLAPSE SURVIVES `OQ-F4-03`.** Finding 11: whether an impersonation record appears in a regulator pack is open, it is counsel's rather than the founder's, and a ruling either way may give `regulator` its own profile. **This entry judges that closure is right precisely BECAUSE it makes that moment loud**, but that judgement trades a cheap future edit for a noisy one, and a founder who expects the regulator profile to diverge within weeks is choosing differently. **No file can answer how likely that is; it is a reading of how the counsel sitting will go.**
2. **THE TWO NAMES ARE THE FIRST TWO VALUES THIS COLUMN WILL EVER HOLD, AND THE ONLY FREE MOMENT TO CHANGE THEM IS NOW.** Finding 7 measures the cost at zero: no row exists and nothing cites the strings. Clause 6 confirms them on the ground that each quotes its own ruling and refuses two alternatives. **That is a naming judgement and naming is a product decision.** The specific thing worth a founder's second: `full-detail` is M06's own phrase and it also reads, to somebody who has not read M06, as *"everything we hold about this person"*. Clause 3 says in terms that it bounds detail and not scope. **A founder who thinks that name will be misread in a dispute should say so while the rename is still free.**
3. **CLAUSE 5's SECOND CONSTRAINT IS PROPOSED AND NOT OBVIOUS.** Making "the profile follows from the audience" a database rule is stronger than the first constraint and is the half that would have to be superseded if the mapping moves. **This entry recommends it and does not write it**, and whether a control that must later be superseded is better than a rule left in code alone is a judgement this corpus has made both ways.

**Nothing else here is waiting on a decision.** The seeds, the counts and the two misses are facts, and section 4.1 is the part a founder can read fastest for whether this session did the work.
