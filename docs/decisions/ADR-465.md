### 3. The definition of a forcing constraint, stated so a script could apply it

**THIS IS THE ROW'S FIRST DELIVERABLE AND EVERYTHING BELOW DEPENDS ON IT.**

> **A FORCING CONSTRAINT is a claim, made in a section where an entry names work it did not take, that attributes the not-taking to a stated property of the world rather than to the entry's preference.**

Mechanically, over the 444 numbered ADR files, the unit is a **SENTENCE** and a sentence qualifies when all four gates pass.

**GATE 1, PLACE.** The sentence sits under a heading matching an owed-work **PHRASE**, not an owed-work token: `what is owed`, `what is still owed`, `what is still open`, `what this (row|entry|session|read|census) (did not|deliberately did not)`, `what this (row|entry|session) leaves (owed|open)`, `what (was|is) found and (not) repaired`, `found and (not) repaired`, `reported and (not) repaired`, `left uncorrected`, `landmine`, `owed outside`, `not taken here`, `deliberately did not`.

**The phrase requirement is load-bearing and it is the repair to the failure [ADR-461](ADR-461.md) section 8 names.** That entry reports that B's first pass *"returned an entry whose heading merely contained the word 'refused'"*. The same defect reproduced here on the token `owed`: [`ADR-404.md:145`](ADR-404.md) sits under the heading *"REFUTED: [ADR-398]'s seam has ZERO surviving instances, and the reason is that an owed list cannot see inside its own wave"*, which is a findings heading containing the word `owed` inside a noun phrase. **A token gate admits it and a phrase gate does not.**

**GATE 2, THE BLOCKING RELATION, WHICH HAS FOUR KINDS AND EACH KIND IS ITS OWN TEST.** At least one must match.

| Kind | What it asserts | The surface test, in words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **F1 FENCE** | the work is somebody else's to do | `out of fence`, `outside (this\|the\|its\|that) fence`, `not in (this\|the) fence`, `(this\|that\|its) (row\|entry\|session)'s fence`, `is not (this\|the) (row\|entry\|session)'s`, `no row holds`, `the fence does not reach`, `beyond (this\|the) fence` |
| **F2 RULE** | a named rule or authority forbids it | `forbid(s\|den)`, `prohibit(s\|ed)`, `is refused by`, `no row can substitute`, `requires a founder ruling`, `a founder ruling`, `governs` |
| **F3 COST** | a quantified consequence of doing it | a cardinal bound to a unit of work (`cited lines`, `lines`, `files`, `call sites`, `callers`, `columns`, `cases`, `rows`, `entries`, `tests`, `sites`, `legs`, `migrations`, `guards`, `builders`) within 80 characters of `would`, `move`, `change`, `break`, `touch`, `cost` or `invalidate` |
| **F4 CAPABILITY** | it cannot be done or checked from here at all | `cannot be (checked\|repaired\|done\|built\|enforced\|expressed\|reached)`, `unbuildable`, `is not buildable`, `nothing (in this repository\|in the tree\|here) can`, `not checkable from here` |

**GATE 3, THE REFERENT.** The sentence names something a reader could go and look at: an inline-code path ending `.ts`, `.tsx`, `.js`, `.mjs`, `.cjs`, `.sql`, `.json`, `.yml`, `.yaml` or `.md`, or an `ADR-nnn`, or a `PR #nnn`, or an identifier of the form `RI-`, `CI-`, `CV-`, `SD-`, `MZ-`, `VG-`, `OI-`, `LT-` or `INV-`.

**GATE 4, THE UNIT.** Sentences are split on terminal punctuation followed by an opening character, **with inline code masked first**, so that a colon or a period inside `` `scoped-db.ts:2211` `` never splits a claim in half. Fenced code blocks are excluded and headings are carried as context rather than scanned.

**WHY A SENTENCE AND NOT A LINE, WHICH IS WHERE THE PRECISION COMES FROM.** Derivation B's unit was a line and Merit prose puts three to six sentences on one line. **A line-scoped filter therefore reports one candidate for a paragraph in which one clause is a forcing constraint and five are not**, and it cannot say which. Sentence scope is what makes a hand-read verdict transferable to the thing the filter returned, and without it the precision measured in section 4 would be a measurement of nothing.

**THE FOUR GATES ARE NOT ONE KEYWORD UNION WITH MORE WORDS IN IT.** Gate 2 and gate 3 must be satisfied by the same sentence, which is what forces the obstacle and the not-taking to be asserted together rather than merely to co-occur in a section.

### 4. The precision measurement, its sample, its selection method and its result

**PRECISION IS 26 OF 30, WHICH IS 86.7 PERCENT.**

**THE METHOD, STATED SO THE NUMBER CAN BE DISCOUNTED IF THE METHOD IS BAD.**

1. **The definition was tuned against a first version and then frozen.** A 12-claim eyeball read of version 1 drove three changes: the heading gate went from tokens to phrases, the fence kind lost six weak alternatives (`whoever holds`, `belongs to`, `the next row`, `a later row`, `another row`, `out of scope`), and the cost kind was tightened to require the cardinal and the unit of work adjacent rather than anywhere in the sentence.
2. **The measurement sample was drawn AFTER the freeze, from the frozen definition, with a seed not used during tuning.** Tuning used version 1 at seed 7. The measurement used the frozen version at seed `20260908` over a linear congruential shuffle of the whole population. **No claim read during tuning was scored in the measurement**, and the four version-1 hits reread here were reached by the seed rather than carried.
3. **The sample is 30 of 105, which is 28.6 percent, drawn uniformly over the population and not stratified by kind.** Every drawn claim was read against its own file. None was replaced and none was skipped.
4. **A claim scores TRUE when it states an obstacle as the reason work was not taken.** It scores FALSE when it states scope without an obstacle, when it reports another document's argument rather than making one, or when it asserts compliance with a fence rather than citing a fence as a reason.

**THE FOUR FALSE POSITIVES, EACH WITH THE REASON IT FAILED.**

| Site | Why it is not a forcing constraint |
| --- | --- |
| [`ADR-440.md:217`](ADR-440.md) | *"`scoped-db.ts`'s OTHER 4,549 LINES WERE NOT TOUCHED"* is a scope statement with no obstacle. The **F3 COST** test matched a cardinal beside `lines` where no consequence was being priced |
| [`ADR-444.md:197`](ADR-444.md) | *"NO FILE OUTSIDE THE FENCE WAS EDITED"* asserts compliance with a fence. It names no repair and cites the fence as a boundary observed, not as a reason a repair was withheld |
| [`ADR-434.md:181`](ADR-434.md) | the `forbids` sits inside quoted material the entry is reporting as **measured wrong**. The constraint belongs to the quoted argument and not to the entry |
| [`ADR-335.md:200`](ADR-335.md) | a staleness fact under a `Found and NOT repaired` heading. It states what is wrong and never says why it was left |

**TWO OF THE FOUR ARE THE SAME DEFECT AND IT IS NAMED RATHER THAN SMOOTHED.** [`ADR-440.md:217`](ADR-440.md) and the F3 COST kind generally are the weakest part of this definition: **cost accounts for only 4 of the 105 and its numeric branch is the one that misfires on section and item numbers.** A reader who wants a higher-precision filter should delete F3, which costs 4 claims and removes the commonest false-positive shape. **It is kept here because deleting a kind after measuring it is how a precision figure gets manufactured**, and the four are reported instead.

**WHAT THE 86.7 PERCENT DOES AND DOES NOT LICENSE.** It licenses calling the 105 a population, which is the thing [ADR-461](ADR-461.md) section 8 said B was not. **It does not license reporting a census fraction over the 105**, and section 6 is why, and the reason has nothing to do with this number.
