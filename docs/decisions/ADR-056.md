## ADR-056: `INV-07` gains a stated `R-31` exception, and the lock is cleared rather than carried  (2026-08-18, status: proposed)

**[M01](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)'s `INV-07` is amended by this ruling, together with `R-15`'s permanence clause and `R-31`'s effects list. [ADR-050](ADR-050.md) is the precedent and gave `INV-06` the same shape at the same transition; it deliberately did not touch `INV-07`, and [STATE](../STATE.md) has carried that as an open founder item since. `RE-P-02` is what it unblocks.**

**This ruling RATIFIES shipped engine behaviour rather than reversing it, which makes it the opposite of [ADR-052](ADR-052.md) and [ADR-054](ADR-054.md) and changes where the risk sits.** The engine already clears the lock. Nothing written down says it may, so the exposure is not that the code is wrong: it is that **a session reading `INV-07` and `R-15` literally would "correct" the engine toward the document**, and the correction would breach every funded account.

- **Context:** **`INV-07` as frozen reads "a locked floor never changes again for the LIFE OF THE ACCOUNT" ([M01 section 1.5](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md), enforced by `RE-P-02` and `GS-016`). The `R-31` funded reset clears the lock.**

  `R-15`'s Effect column is where the permanence is stated: "`floor = floor_lock_floor_at_cents`, `floorLocked = true`, `hwb` frozen, **all permanently**". `INV-07` is that sentence promoted to an invariant, in the same words `INV-06` used for "no exception, no phase qualifier".

  **`R-31` does not name `floorLocked` at all.** Its effects list is explicit and finite — phase, `balance`, `hwb`, `floor`, traded days, win days, the consistency accumulators, `consistencyPeriodStartDay` — and the lock is not among them. [`progression.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/progression.ts) writes `floorLocked: false` at `DO-8` and says so in its own comment. **That derivation is sound and it is not the point**; it is a derivation, in a source comment, about a frozen invariant that says the opposite.

  **The interlock is what makes the literal reading untenable, and it is two rules deep.** `R-13` reads "`hwb` stops updating once `floorLocked`". So a funded account arriving locked has `hwb` frozen at the `size_cents` `R-31` just wrote, `R-13` guarded off for the rest of its life, a floor that can never trail, and a funded lock that can never re-engage because its trigger is a profit condition and `R-31` set profit to zero. **That is not a constrained funded floor. It is the funded floor deleted.**

  **This is a live path on every v1 plan and not a hypothetical.** CORE-50K locks at 260,000c of profit against an eval target of 300,000c, so **every v1 eval pass is also a lock day** — the same fact [ADR-050](ADR-050.md) turns on. Every account that ever reaches the funded phase reaches it locked.

### Why [ADR-052](ADR-052.md) makes this sharper than it was

**The lock is now an ASSIGNMENT rather than a term, and that converts a frozen floor into a breach.** Under the superseded `max` reading the carried lock would have left the floor at `max(trailed, locked)`, which with `hwb` frozen is merely stuck. Under ADR-052 `DO-7` **assigns** `floor = floor_lock_floor_at_cents` on every day the account is locked. Walk the ordering out on CORE-50K:

| Day | What runs | Floor at close |
|---|---|---|
| The pass day | `DO-7` assigns the locked floor; `DO-8`'s `R-31` then overwrites it, because `DO-8` is later | **4,750,000** = `size` − funded drawdown |
| First funded day | Floor **at open** is 4,750,000, so `DO-4` finds no breach. `DO-7` then assigns `floor_lock_floor_at_cents` again | **5,010,000** = `size` + 10,000 |
| Second funded day | Floor at open is now 5,010,000 against a balance of 5,000,000 | **Breach.** The day's low cannot clear a floor 10,000c above the opening balance |

**So under the literal reading every funded account breaches on its second funded day**, on all three v1 plans, because [ADR-014](ADR-014.md) sets `floor_lock_floor_at_cents = size_cents + 10,000c` on all three while `R-31` sets the balance to exactly `size_cents`. **This ADR does not ask to be believed on that.** It is a claim about execution and [ADR-053](ADR-053.md)'s precedent is that a defect of this shape is **executed rather than argued**; the founder should require the walk above reproduced against the real fold before signing, and the fixture named below is where it would live.

### The ruling

> **`INV-07`: a locked floor never changes again, EXCEPT at the `R-31` funded reset, where `floorLocked` returns to `false` and the floor is re-initialised to `size_cents` minus the funded drawdown (`R-12`, `R-31`, `GS-019`).**

**This is [ADR-050](ADR-050.md)'s exception, at the same transition, for the same reason, and the two now pair exactly.** `INV-06` gained a stated `R-31` exception for the floor's **value**; `INV-07` gains one for the floor's **lock**. One rule, one transition, two invariants — and stating the second is what stops a reader concluding that ADR-050 considered the lock and left it permanent on purpose. It did not consider it: `ADR-050`'s own allocation row says `INV-07` "is deliberately NOT covered by it" and is a separate open item.

**"For the life of the account" exists to prevent UNSTATED exceptions**, and ADR-050 already ruled on this exact clause in its `INV-06` form: a single named, cited, testable exception "is the opposite of what that language guards against". The wording was written to stop the invariant being eroded by additions nobody wrote down. Writing one down, with its rule, its second rule and its scenario beside it, honours the clause rather than weakening it.

**Scoping `INV-07` per `(account, phase)` is rejected on ADR-050's reasoning verbatim.** It makes the invariant technically true by redefining its domain, and the lock still clears at the transition. A reader who wants to know what happens to the lock at the pass finds an invariant that no longer speaks to it and has to reconstruct the answer from `R-13`'s guard — which is precisely the reconstruction this session had to perform. **An invariant that is true because its domain was narrowed to exclude the case is not stronger, it is quieter.**

**`R-15`'s "all permanently" is about the settlement path, not the phase transition.** It is [ADR-014](ADR-014.md)'s concern in `R-15`'s words: a payout must never reset the floor or release the lock, so loss room can never widen after an extraction. `R-31` is a **phase transition** and not a settlement, and at it the balance falls to `size_cents` in the same step. **Nothing about the trader's loss room widens**: it goes from 10,000c under the eval lock to the funded drawdown measured from `size_cents`, which is the funded contract the trader was sold. `GS-065` and `GS-081` are the settlement fixtures and neither is touched by this ruling.

### The fixture this ruling needs, NAMED and deliberately not written

**No fixture in the directory pins `floor_locked` on any day after an eval pass**, verified rather than assumed. Every fixture that pins the field — `GS-015`, `GS-016`, `GS-054`, `GS-055`, `GS-056`, `GS-061`, `GS-064`, `GS-065`, `GS-070` — **starts in the funded phase**, so none of them crosses the reset. The two that do cross it, `GS-044` and `GS-242`, pin **no floor field at all**, and the fixtures README says why: while both readings were live, a fixture crossing the pass could not pin `floor_cents`, `floor_locked` or `high_water_balance_cents` without ratifying a ruling nobody had made.

**What is owed on acceptance is one fixture that starts in eval, crosses the pass, and pins `floor_locked: false` with the funded floor on the day after.** It is named here and not written, because writing it now would pin the outcome of a ruling that is still `proposed` — the exact thing those two fixtures correctly declined to do. **This ADR is what unblocks it**, in the same way ADR-050 unblocked the three that pin `floor_cents: 4750000`.

- **Alternatives considered:**

  **Carrying the lock into the funded phase, which is the literal reading** (rejected: it deletes the funded floor via `R-13`'s guard, and under [ADR-052](ADR-052.md)'s assignment it breaches every funded account on its second funded day. It is not a conservative reading of a frozen document, it is a different product).

  **`INV-07` scoped per `(account, phase)`** (rejected on [ADR-050](ADR-050.md)'s reasoning verbatim: true by redefinition, silent about the transition it excludes, and it costs every future reader the reconstruction).

  **Amending `R-15` alone to make the lock per-phase, leaving `INV-07` untouched** (rejected: it moves the exception into the rule and away from the invariant, so `INV-07` would go on saying "life of the account" while a rule two sections away contradicted it. That is the drift [ADR-034](ADR-034.md) exists to end, and it is worse here because the invariant is the thing `RE-P-02` is generated from).

  **Leaving `INV-07` unruled and letting `progression.ts`'s comment stand** (rejected: a decision recorded only in a source comment is what this week's `CI-06q` session was commissioned by. It is also actively dangerous rather than merely untidy, because the document says the opposite, so the next reader to notice the disagreement has every reason to change the code).

  **Re-locking immediately at the funded `floor_lock_at_profit_cents`** (rejected: not a real alternative. `R-15`'s trigger is `closing_balance_cents - size_cents >= floor_lock_at_profit_cents` and `R-31` sets the balance to exactly `size_cents`, so the funded lock cannot trigger at the reset on any plan with a positive trigger).

- **Founder approval, as written when proposed: PENDING.** This amends a frozen invariant, a frozen rule's effects list and a frozen rule's permanence clause, all on the money path, so it is not taken on a session's authority ([E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md)). **No document was amended by the session that proposed it**, and no engine file and no fixture was edited. **Recommended for acceptance**, with one condition stated rather than implied: the second-funded-day breach in the table above is a **derivation from the DO ordering, not an execution**, and [ADR-053](ADR-053.md)'s precedent is that a defect of this shape is executed rather than argued. **The signature should follow a run, not this paragraph.**
