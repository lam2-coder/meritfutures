## ADR-056: `INV-07` gains a stated `R-31` exception, and the lock is cleared rather than carried  (2026-08-18, status: proposed)

**[M01](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)'s `INV-07` is amended by this ruling, together with `R-15`'s permanence clause and `R-31`'s effects list. [ADR-050](ADR-050.md) is the precedent and gave `INV-06` the same shape at the same transition; it deliberately did not touch `INV-07`, and [STATE](../STATE.md) has carried that as an open founder item since. `RE-P-02` is what it unblocks.**

**This ruling RATIFIES shipped engine behaviour rather than reversing it, which makes it the opposite of [ADR-052](ADR-052.md) and [ADR-054](ADR-054.md) and changes where the risk sits.** The engine already clears the lock. Nothing written down says it may, so the exposure is not that the code is wrong: it is that **a session reading `INV-07` and `R-15` literally would "correct" the engine toward the document**, and the correction would freeze every funded account's floor at its opening value for life, **without a breach, an assertion or a refused row to announce it**.

**REWRITTEN 2026-08-18, still `proposed` and still unsigned.** [PR #90](https://github.com/lam2-coder/meritfutures/pull/90) executed the claim this ADR led with and **it does not reproduce**. The ruling, its wording and its consequences are unchanged; the argument supporting them is not. The section headed "Why ADR-052 makes this sharper than it was" and its three-row breach table are **deleted**, the refuted sentence is quoted where they stood, and the argument that reproduced is now the primary one. Three narrowings from the same run are folded in. **This ADR was not withdrawn, because its conclusion survived its own table.**

- **Context:** **`INV-07` as frozen reads "a locked floor never changes again for the LIFE OF THE ACCOUNT" ([M01 section 1.5](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md), enforced by `RE-P-02` and `GS-016`). The `R-31` funded reset clears the lock.**

  `R-15`'s Effect column is where the permanence is stated: "`floor = floor_lock_floor_at_cents`, `floorLocked = true`, `hwb` frozen, **all permanently**". `INV-07` is that sentence promoted to an invariant, in the same words `INV-06` used for "no exception, no phase qualifier".

  **`R-31` does not name `floorLocked` at all.** Its effects list is explicit and finite — phase, `balance`, `hwb`, `floor`, traded days, win days, the consistency accumulators, `consistencyPeriodStartDay` — and the lock is not among them. [`progression.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/progression.ts) writes `floorLocked: false` at `DO-8` and says so in its own comment. **That derivation is sound and it is not the point**; it is a derivation, in a source comment, about a frozen invariant that says the opposite.

  **The interlock is what makes the literal reading untenable, it is two rules deep, and it is this ruling's PRIMARY argument.** `R-13` reads "`hwb` stops updating once `floorLocked`" ([M01 section 3.5](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)). So a funded account arriving locked has `hwb` frozen at the `size_cents` `R-31` just wrote, `R-13` guarded off for the rest of its life, a floor that can never trail, and a funded lock that can never re-engage because its trigger is a profit condition and `R-31` set profit to zero. **That is not a constrained funded floor. It is the funded floor deleted.**

  **It has been folded, and it reproduced exactly.** Across 2,000 generated streams the shipped account re-locked **232 times** and the carried-lock account **not once**, and the carried account's high-water balance sat below its own balance on every profitable funded day. The measurement is [PR #90](https://github.com/lam2-coder/meritfutures/pull/90), reported in [session 57's log](../sessions/2026-08-18-session-57.md) and standing in the tree as [`adr-056-carried-lock.test.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/test/adr-056-carried-lock.test.ts) and [`adr-056-carried-lock.property.test.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/test/adr-056-carried-lock.property.test.ts). **The section below records what the same run did to this ADR's OTHER argument**, which was the one it led with.

  **This is a live path on TWO of the three v1 plans and not a hypothetical.** CORE-50K locks at 260,000c of profit against an eval target of 300,000c, so **every eval pass on it is also a lock day**, which is the fact [ADR-050](ADR-050.md) turns on, and Merit Rapid carries both figures unchanged (Appendix A.2 overrides neither). **DIRECT-50K is the exception and it is an exception by ABSENCE**: `phase_eval.enabled` is `false` with a null `profit_target_cents` (Appendix A.3), so it resolves with no eval rules, opens **funded**, and `R-31` never runs on it at all. The transition this ruling amends `INV-07` against does not exist on Direct. On the two plans that have an eval phase, every account that ever reaches the funded phase reaches it locked.

### The argument this ruling LED WITH was executed, and it is FALSE

**What stood here was a section headed "Why [ADR-052](ADR-052.md) makes this sharper than it was" and a three-row table walking CORE-50K to a breach on its second funded day. Both are deleted. The sentence they rested on is kept, verbatim, rather than quietly dropped, because a ruling that removes a wrong argument without saying it was wrong teaches its next reader nothing:**

> under ADR-052 `DO-7` **assigns** `floor = floor_lock_floor_at_cents` on every day the account is locked

**That sentence is false.** This ADR asked for it to be executed before the signature, [PR #90](https://github.com/lam2-coder/meritfutures/pull/90) executed it, and it does not reproduce: not on any v1 plan, not on any funded day, and not for the reason given. [`floor.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/floor.ts) guards the `R-15` block with `!floorLocked` at line 116, and line 99 opens `advanceFloor` with `floorCents = input.priorFloorCents`. **The locked floor is assigned ONCE, on the day the lock engages, and CARRIED on every locked day after it.** So a carried-lock funded account leaves `R-31` with the floor `R-31` wrote, `size_cents` minus the funded drawdown, and keeps it: **4,750,000c on CORE-50K, not the 5,010,000c the deleted table put there.**

**The wrong claim was a correct reading of the file's header and a wrong reading of its code, which is why it survived a session and a review.** The header states the floor as a **stateless expression** over the day, `floor(d) = floorLocked ? floor_lock_floor_at_cents : max(...)`; the implementation is a **stateful carry**. The two forms agree on every account the corpus admits, because nothing between the lock day and today ever moves the floor, and they part company at exactly one state: one whose floor was moved by something other than the lock while `floorLocked` stayed true. That state is this ADR's counterfactual and it exists nowhere else, so no fixture, no property and no gate could have told the two forms apart.

**AND THE SIGN WAS BACKWARDS**, which is the part of the run worth reading twice. On the walked stream, with a funded day two whose low is 4,800,000c:

| | Carried lock, the literal `INV-07` | Shipped, what the engine does |
|---|---|---|
| Funded day 1 floor | 4,750,000c, **frozen** | 4,850,000c, trailed by `R-13` |
| Funded day 2, low 4,800,000c | **survives**, 4,800,000 >= 4,750,000 | **BREACHES**, 4,800,000 < 4,850,000, shortfall 50,000c |

**The carried lock is strictly MORE PERMISSIVE than the engine it was argued to be more dangerous than.** Over **3,442 folded funded days across 2,000 generated streams**, with **1,790 shipped breaches and 1,485 carried breaches**, the carried lock breached on **zero** days the shipped fold survived. The reason is structural rather than statistical: both accounts leave `R-31` with the same floor, the carried one is frozen there by the two `!floorLocked` guards, and the shipped one is non-decreasing from there by `R-13` and `R-14`, so the floor `R-18` judges the day against is at or below the shipped one on every day. Those counts are the run's, asserted in the sweep as `> 0` rather than pinned, because pinning a generator's yield makes a property test a change-detector.

**So the defect is gentler than the one this ADR led with, and it is permanent where that one was immediate.** The literal reading does not breach every funded account on its second funded day. **It hands every funded account a floor frozen at its opening value for the life of the account**, on a drawdown sold to the trader as trailing that then trails nothing. That is the argument the ruling rests on, and it is the argument it should have led with.

| **Even granting the refuted mechanism, "every funded account on its SECOND funded day" was CONDITIONAL.** Measured under the temporarily reintroduced mutant, on CORE-50K: a funded day one closing flat or **+9,999c** breaches on day two, **+10,001c does not**, because the balance has cleared the locked floor and the day's low can stay above it | The deleted table assumed the balance was still `size_cents` on day two, which is true of an account that made no money on its first funded day and of no other. **Moot while the mechanism is false**, and recorded because a re-derivation on other grounds would inherit it |
| **The carried lock FAILS SILENTLY.** `R-13`'s guard freezes `hwb` at `size_cents`, so every profitable funded day leaves the high-water balance **below the account's own balance**, and [`0037`](../../packages/db/migrations/0037_supersede_rule_states_high_water_bounds_balance.sql) replaced `0015`'s unconditional bound with `floor_locked OR high_water_balance_cents >= balance_cents`, which **exempts every locked row** | Its own consequence line below. Under the literal reading there is no breach, no assertion, no refusal and no rejected row |

**A STATE THE SCHEMA CANNOT DETECT IS A DIFFERENT CLASS OF PROBLEM FROM ONE IT REJECTS**, and it is the reason the last narrowing is not a footnote. **`0037`'s exemption is correct for the shipped engine**: a legitimately locked account really does freeze `hwb` below its later balances, which is `GS-016`'s expected end state, and that is what `0037` was written to stop refusing. Its side effect is that a carried-lock row is **indistinguishable from a legitimate one**. So the literal reading, if a session ever "corrected" the engine toward the frozen document, would be **stored, published and settled against for the life of every funded account**, in the trader's favour and against the house, with the first evidence a payout nobody could account for. A defect that trips a constraint on the day it ships is bounded by its own noise. This one has none. **[`0037`'s item 4](../../packages/db/migrations/0037_supersede_rule_states_high_water_bounds_balance.sql) names the reason no cross-row `hwb` freeze trigger was written**: "`INV-07` versus that reset is an OPEN FOUNDER ITEM. A trigger would have to take a side on it." This ruling is what lets that trigger be written, and that trigger is what would make the state detectable if it ever arose.

### The ruling

> **`INV-07`: a locked floor never changes again, EXCEPT at the `R-31` funded reset, where `floorLocked` returns to `false` and the floor is re-initialised to `size_cents` minus the funded drawdown (`R-12`, `R-31`, `GS-019`).**

**This is [ADR-050](ADR-050.md)'s exception, at the same transition, for the same reason, and the two now pair exactly.** `INV-06` gained a stated `R-31` exception for the floor's **value**; `INV-07` gains one for the floor's **lock**. One rule, one transition, two invariants — and stating the second is what stops a reader concluding that ADR-050 considered the lock and left it permanent on purpose. It did not consider it: `ADR-050`'s own allocation row says `INV-07` "is deliberately NOT covered by it" and is a separate open item.

**"For the life of the account" exists to prevent UNSTATED exceptions**, and ADR-050 already ruled on this exact clause in its `INV-06` form: a single named, cited, testable exception "is the opposite of what that language guards against". The wording was written to stop the invariant being eroded by additions nobody wrote down. Writing one down, with its rule, its second rule and its scenario beside it, honours the clause rather than weakening it.

**Scoping `INV-07` per `(account, phase)` is rejected on ADR-050's reasoning verbatim.** It makes the invariant technically true by redefining its domain, and the lock still clears at the transition. A reader who wants to know what happens to the lock at the pass finds an invariant that no longer speaks to it and has to reconstruct the answer from `R-13`'s guard — which is precisely the reconstruction this session had to perform. **An invariant that is true because its domain was narrowed to exclude the case is not stronger, it is quieter.**

**`R-15`'s "all permanently" is about the settlement path, not the phase transition.** It is [ADR-014](ADR-014.md)'s concern in `R-15`'s words: a payout must never reset the floor or release the lock, so loss room can never widen after an extraction. `R-31` is a **phase transition** and not a settlement, and at it the balance falls to `size_cents` in the same step. **Nothing about the trader's loss room widens**: it goes from 10,000c under the eval lock to the funded drawdown measured from `size_cents`, which is the funded contract the trader was sold. `GS-065` and `GS-081` are the settlement fixtures and neither is touched by this ruling.

### The fixture this ruling needs, NAMED and deliberately not written

**No fixture in the directory pins `floor_locked` on any day after an eval pass**, verified rather than assumed. Every fixture that pins the field — `GS-015`, `GS-016`, `GS-054`, `GS-055`, `GS-056`, `GS-061`, `GS-064`, `GS-065`, `GS-070` — **starts in the funded phase**, so none of them crosses the reset. The two that do cross it, `GS-044` and `GS-242`, pin **no floor field at all**, and the fixtures README says why: while both readings were live, a fixture crossing the pass could not pin `floor_cents`, `floor_locked` or `high_water_balance_cents` without ratifying a ruling nobody had made.

**What is owed on acceptance is one fixture that starts in eval, crosses the pass, and pins `floor_locked: false` with the funded floor on the day after.** It is named here and not written, because writing it now would pin the outcome of a ruling that is still `proposed` — the exact thing those two fixtures correctly declined to do. **This ADR is what unblocks it**, in the same way ADR-050 unblocked the three that pin `floor_cents: 4750000`.

- **Alternatives considered:**

  **Carrying the lock into the funded phase, which is the literal reading** (rejected: it deletes the funded floor via `R-13`'s guard. The floor freezes at `size_cents` minus the funded drawdown for the life of the account, trails no part of the funded profit, and the lock can never re-engage: 232 shipped re-locks against **zero** carried, over 2,000 streams. **It does NOT breach every funded account on its second funded day**, which is what this ADR first argued and what [PR #90](https://github.com/lam2-coder/meritfutures/pull/90) refuted; the carried lock is strictly **more permissive** than the shipped engine on every day of every generated stream. It is not a conservative reading of a frozen document, it is a different product, and it is one whose difference nothing in the fold and nothing in the schema would report).

  **`INV-07` scoped per `(account, phase)`** (rejected on [ADR-050](ADR-050.md)'s reasoning verbatim: true by redefinition, silent about the transition it excludes, and it costs every future reader the reconstruction).

  **Amending `R-15` alone to make the lock per-phase, leaving `INV-07` untouched** (rejected: it moves the exception into the rule and away from the invariant, so `INV-07` would go on saying "life of the account" while a rule two sections away contradicted it. That is the drift [ADR-034](ADR-034.md) exists to end, and it is worse here because the invariant is the thing `RE-P-02` is generated from).

  **Leaving `INV-07` unruled and letting `progression.ts`'s comment stand** (rejected: a decision recorded only in a source comment is what this week's `CI-06q` session was commissioned by. It is also actively dangerous rather than merely untidy, because the document says the opposite, so the next reader to notice the disagreement has every reason to change the code).

  **Re-locking immediately at the funded `floor_lock_at_profit_cents`** (rejected: not a real alternative. `R-15`'s trigger is `closing_balance_cents - size_cents >= floor_lock_at_profit_cents` and `R-31` sets the balance to exactly `size_cents`, so the funded lock cannot trigger at the reset on any plan with a positive trigger).

- **Founder approval: STILL PENDING, and the condition it was proposed under is now DISCHARGED.** This amends a frozen invariant, a frozen rule's effects list and a frozen rule's permanence clause, all on the money path, so it is not taken on a session's authority ([E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md)). **No document has been amended by any session that has touched this ADR**, and no engine file and no fixture has been edited by any of them.

  **As proposed, this ADR refused to be believed on the claim it led with**: "the second-funded-day breach in the table above is a derivation from the `DO` ordering, not an execution ... the signature should follow a run, not this paragraph." **The run happened, in [PR #90](https://github.com/lam2-coder/meritfutures/pull/90), and it REFUTED the claim.** That is the condition working rather than the condition failing, and it is [ADR-053](ADR-053.md)'s precedent doing what it was cited for.

  **What a signature would now ratify is a smaller, better supported ruling.** The ruling wording is unchanged and the run reversed no consequence in the table above. What changed is the argument underneath it: the immediate breach is gone, and what remains is a funded floor frozen for the life of the account, **measured rather than derived**, which nothing in the fold and nothing in the schema would report. **Still recommended for acceptance**, and the fixture named above stays unwritten until the line is signed.
