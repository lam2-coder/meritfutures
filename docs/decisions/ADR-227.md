### 3. THE RULING: the signature box is three objects

An approval line has been asked to mean three incompatible things at once. They separate cleanly, and each gets a different discipline.

| Class | What it covers | How approval is obtained | Who can give it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. A ruling** | an entry that lands no money-path file | **granted**, on authority | the founder, directly or by a recorded delegation |
| **B. A checkable money-path diff** | a money-path diff whose central claim is a property | **earned**, by a named mechanical assertion that fails when the claim is false | nobody. It is not granted at all |
| **C. The residue** | a money-path diff whose central claim is a choice | **read**, line by line, under `:358` undiluted | the founder alone |

**THE WHOLE OF THE RULING IS THAT B IS NOT A SIGNATURE.** Section `E2`'s argument is about a model's **judgement**. It is not an argument against a **test**, and this repository already stakes everything on that distinction: **33 gates, 20 invariants and over 6,000 cases**, every one of them written by the same models `E2` distrusts, and every one of them trusted because **it fails when the thing it asserts is false**. That is [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md)'s own rule, written after this project's three worst errors: *"Prefer a new CI gate over a bigger model whenever the error is checkable."*

**A model that says "this looks right" adds nothing `E2` will accept. A model that writes an assertion which was RED before the change and GREEN after has produced evidence that outlives it.**

### 4. Class A: what a delegated approval attests, and precisely what it does not

Delegated approval is legitimate here and it is **precedented in this corpus rather than invented by this entry**. [ADR-099](ADR-099.md) section 5 records the grant in the founder's own words: *"make decisions on anything that needs me yourself you have my full permission if you approve i approve"*, and earlier, *"you have my full permission approve anything you see fit on my behalf"*. [ADR-100](ADR-100.md) records the same grant against its own subject. **Both entries quote it rather than summarise it, and both say in the same breath that it is not the `E2` read.** This entry adopts that form and makes it the rule.

**THE GRANT IS READ AS GENERAL RATHER THAN SLICE-SPECIFIC, ON THREE GROUNDS, AND THE THIRD IS WHAT MAKES IT LEGITIMATE RATHER THAN A FORGED SIGNATURE.**

1. **`ADR-099` and `ADR-100` already record it as a general grant.** *"anything that needs me"* and *"anything you see fit on my behalf"* are not scoped to the entries that quote them, and neither entry treats them as scoped. **This was read at both files**, not carried from a prompt.
2. **It was reaffirmed twice on 2026-08-29**, relayed to this session as the founder's verbatim words: *"keep going. whatever youre saying is blocking stuff analyzse figure it out yourself and make decisions to fix or approve it. you have my full backing."* and *"im diverting back to you with all permission to make decisions based on analysis and best for the project."* **THESE TWO ARE RELAYED AND NOT READ AT A SOURCE.** `ADR-099`'s quotations are in a file in this repository and these are not, and **the difference is recorded rather than smoothed over**, because an entry ruling that a delegation must be quoted cannot itself rest on a paraphrase nobody can re-read.
3. **The class A block states that no human read it.** That sentence is the whole of what separates a delegation from a signature somebody forged, and **section 9 item 1 requires it in every entry that uses the grant.** It is not softened anywhere in this entry.

**A class A approval ATTESTS, and the four are exhaustive:**

1. **That a decision was taken**, by a named session, on a named date, against a named alternative that is written down and argued with.
2. **That the corpus was searched before it was taken**, with the scope of that search stated, so a later reader can re-run it rather than re-trust it.
3. **That the cost if it is wrong is stated in the entry**, in the entry's own words, bounded where it can be bounded and declared unbounded where it cannot.
4. **That the authority is the founder's and is quoted**, never paraphrased and never assumed from silence.

**A class A approval DOES NOT ATTEST, and this half is the one that gets dropped:**

1. **That any human read the reasoning.** It did not happen and the entry must not read as though it did.
2. **That the decision is correct.** It attests that it was taken with authority. Those are different claims and `E2`'s whole point is that they look identical on the page.
3. **That the entry is protected against `E2`'s named failure class.** Access control, authorization bypass and trust boundaries are exactly where a model's confidence is worth least, so **an entry that touches any of them is not class A**, whatever its file list says.
4. **That the grant is permanent.** It is revocable in one sentence, and every entry that rests on it says so.

**A CLASS A APPROVAL IS RECORDED AS DELEGATED AND NEVER AS A FOUNDER READ.** The signature line reads `GRANTED (delegated)` with the date and the quoted grant, exactly as `ADR-099` wrote it. **A bare `GRANTED` on a class A entry is a defect**, because the whole value of the class is that a reader can tell the two apart six months later.

**66 of the 108 `proposed` entries land no money-path file** and are class A candidates on their file lists. **They are not approved by this entry.** This entry rules what the class means; a session working the queue applies it, one entry at a time, and every entry it approves carries the quoted grant.

### 5. Which class this entry is in

**Class A.** It lands no money-path file, and its own approval line is unsigned as this entry ships. **It is the first instance of the regime it rules, which is deliberate**: an entry that ruled a new approval discipline and then granted itself an approval under that discipline would be the self-grading trap `C10` names, one level up.

### 6. Class B: an assertion, and the four things that make it one

**A class B approval is EARNED BY A NAMED MECHANICAL ASSERTION**: a `CI-06` gate letter, an `RI-` invariant, or a named test. The approval line carries the assertion's identifier and the run in which it was watched failing. **No reader is invoked and no judgement is recorded.**

**The four conditions, and each of them exists to stop a specific way of faking this:**

1. **IT FALSIFIES THE ENTRY'S OWN CENTRAL CLAIM.** Not a neighbouring property, not a type-level restatement. The claim in the entry's heading is the thing that must go red. *Stops:* an assertion that asserts something true and irrelevant.
2. **IT WAS WATCHED RED BEFORE THE CHANGE AND GREEN AFTER**, with the transcript in the entry, in the form `ADR-226` section 5 and `ADR-224` already use. **An assertion never seen failing asserts nothing.** *Stops:* a tautology that was green on an empty tree.
3. **IT IS NAMED IN A REGISTRY THAT A GATE RUNS**, so it cannot be deleted quietly. An assertion nothing runs is prose. *Stops:* a test file that stops being imported.
4. **IT WAS NOT WEAKENED TO PASS.** If the assertion had to be narrowed to go green, **the narrowing is the finding** and the entry is class C. *Stops:* the single most likely failure mode of this whole ruling, which is a session that mints a gate to clear its own queue.

**THE ASSERTION MAY BE WRITTEN BY A MODEL, AND THAT IS NOT A CONCESSION.** It is what this repository already does 6,000 times. **What `E2` refuses is a model's opinion, and condition 2 is the line between the two:** an opinion cannot be watched failing.

**WHAT THIS DOES NOT REACH, SAID PLAINLY.** An assertion can hold *"the two legs of a posting sum to zero"*. It cannot hold *"this is the right chart of accounts"*. **The first is a property and the second is a choice**, and no amount of test-writing converts one into the other. That boundary is section 8's whole subject and it is why class C is not empty.

### 7. Class C: the residue, and why the comprehension rule is not weakened

**THE TRAP WAS NAMED IN THE DISPATCH AND IT IS HONOURED HERE.** [`:358`](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) says never merge code you cannot explain. **A regime under which nobody can explain the payout path is a worse outcome than a backlog**, and if this ruling had that consequence it would be the wrong ruling.

**It does not have that consequence, and the reason is worth one paragraph rather than an assurance.** Class B does not say the diff goes unexplained. **It says the explanation is discharged by an artifact that fails when the explanation is false**, which is a stronger form of *"can explain"* than a reading: a reading is a claim about a person's state of mind on one afternoon, and an assertion is a claim the tree re-proves on every push. **A founder who wants to walk a class B diff line by line can still do it**, and the assertion tells them where to start. What the ruling removes is not comprehension. **It removes the pretence that a signature on 42 money-path entries was ever going to happen.**

**And class C keeps the rule undiluted.** Where the claim is a choice, **`:358` applies exactly as written and no session may substitute anything for it.** A class C entry does not merge behind an assertion, a delegation, or a session's assessment. It waits for the founder.
