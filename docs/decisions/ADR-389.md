## ADR-389: option (d) is right about the page and is not takeable by a row that owns the runbook, because the fifth column is read out of the table row by eleven assertions in files no such row may write (2026-09-06, status: proposed)

**[ADR-388](ADR-388.md) SECTION 15 ITEM 1 PRICED THIS RESTRUCTURE AT "FIFTEEN POINTERS MOVE, TWELVE ARE ALREADY WRONG, NINE SIT IN DATED RECORDS" AND SAID THE ROW THAT TAKES IT "NEEDS `docs/ops/runbooks/**` PLUS THE CITATION-REPAIR RIGHT".** This row had exactly that, built the restructure, and measured what it does: the scheduled table falls from 31,301 characters to 2,700 and its tallest row from 47 rendered lines to 5, with columns one to four byte-identical across the move.

**RULED: THE PAGE SHOULD BE RESTRUCTURED AND THIS ROW MAY NOT BE THE ONE THAT DOES IT.** The runbook is reverted byte-identical to its base. What ships is the measurement, the corrected specification, and the seven pointer repairs that do not depend on the move.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `389`, branch `claude/the-runbook-table-restructured`, session **584**, off the wave integration branch rather than off `main`.
- **CONTROL OVER AN OPERATIONS DOCUMENT A HUMAN READS UNDER PRESSURE. Ships `proposed` with an UNSIGNED approval line. Merging is not signing. `E2` read owed.**
- **THE DELIVERABLE IS A REFUSAL WITH THE RESTRUCTURE MEASURED RATHER THAN ESTIMATED, SEVEN POINTER REPAIRS, FIVE SEEDS AND A HAZARD FOUND BY ONE OF THEM.** No invariant was minted, no gate added, widened or weakened, no test skipped, disabled or quarantined, no register grown, and no file owned by row `390` or row `391` was written.

### 4. Seed E, which is the hazard nobody taking (d) should discover the way this row did

**THE NOTES WERE FIRST WRITTEN AS A `###` SUBSECTION INSIDE `## Scheduled work`, WHICH IS WHERE A READER WANTS THEM AND WHERE `cronRows` IS STILL LOOKING.** That scan takes every line starting with `|` between `## Scheduled work` and the next `## `, and neither `###` nor `####` terminates it: `after.search(/\n## /)` needs a space in the fourth position and `\n### ` has a `#` there.

**SO A NOTE LINE THAT OPENS WITH A PIPE IS READ AS A SCHEDULED JOB.** The `scheduled` set only ever grows and `CI-06l`'s assertion 4 asks whether a coverage row's job is IN it, so **a phantom job manufactured out of note prose can SATISFY a coverage row whose real scheduled row is gone.**

**MEASURED RATHER THAN REASONED. A job renamed out of `CI-06l`'s reach is RED at three findings. The same rename plus ONE pipe-leading note line spelling the old name is GREEN at 33 of 33.**

**THE FIX IS ONE HEADING LEVEL AND IT IS IN THE SPECIFICATION IN SECTION 12.** Promoting the notes to a peer `## Severity if absent, by job` terminates the scan before them, and the same seed pair is then RED at 32 of 33 in both halves. **This row shipped nothing, so the hazard is recorded rather than avoided; whoever takes (d) gets it for free.**

**AND IT WAS FOUND BY A PREDICTION THAT WAS WRONG.** Seed D, a lone pipe-leading note line, was predicted RED and came back GREEN. Seed E exists because of that, and it is the only seed here that found a defect this row had written itself.
