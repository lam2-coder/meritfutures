## ADR-208: `GET /admin/eligible-forecast` declines with a body and never with a 404, because on its own heading a 404 already means a route nobody has built  (2026-08-29, status: proposed)

**[ADR-203](ADR-203.md) ruling 8 named this session's edit and did not make it, so the expected shape of session 397 was a transcription of a ruling already made and an unused ADR number.** The number is taken, and **the part `ADR-203` does not cover is stated first because it is the whole of why**: that entry's own section 7a asks *"what does `GET /admin/eligible-forecast` SERVE when there is no horizon?"*, answers *"the corpus leans one way and this entry does not take it"*, and lists `EligibleForecastResponse` under **REFUSED rather than taken**. It also names the consequence exactly: *"what the transcribing slice owes is one `| null`, one gap entry at the producer, and one ruling on a sibling body."* **This entry is that ruling.** The `| null` itself is `ADR-203` ruling 8 transcribed and is not re-decided here.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `208`, session **397**, dispatched as `claude/eligible-next-7d-nullable`. The row reserved the number **conditionally** and instructed that it be taken *"only if the edit needs a ruling that does not exist"*. **It does, in one place and not in three**: the same `| null` is a compile error inside a SECOND response body whose empty-horizon shape no entry had fixed, and neither of the two answers available to it is derivable from `ADR-203`. **NO MIGRATION NUMBER WAS RESERVED AND NONE IS NEEDED**: `ADR-203` ruling 6 already refused a column for an absence, on the reasoning that *"a stored 'this figure is absent' flag beside the `null` that says so"* is drift with an extra column, and nothing here changes that.

- **MONEY PATH.** `eligible_next_7d.total_cents` is the figure the payout wallet is funded against ([EC-074](../edge-cases/EC-074.md), `P-M6-02`, [ADR-011](ADR-011.md)'s top-up trigger), and this entry decides what an operator sees when it cannot be computed. [**`E2`**](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) **READ IS OWED.** The entry ships `proposed` with an **UNSIGNED** approval line. **Merging is not signing.**

- **WHAT IS MEASURED AND WHAT IS JUDGED**, separated before the argument. That the atomic three-copy move is forced, and what it costs `RI-18`, is **MEASURED** in section 3. That `ProjectionGap` cannot carry this absence is **DERIVED** from two declarations in section 5. What is **JUDGED** is the body over the 404 (ruling 2), and section 4 gives the argument rather than the precedent, because the precedent alone is evidence and not a rule.

- **A DISPATCH PROMPT IS NOT A SOURCE, AND ONE PREMISE OF AN APPROVED ENTRY DID NOT SURVIVE.** Section 3 records it: `ADR-203` section 7a states that `RI-18` stays green under the partial move, and on this tree it is **RED on six findings**. **RULING 1. `eligible_next_7d` TAKES `EligibleNext7d | null` IN ALL THREE DECLARATIONS, AND THAT IS A TRANSCRIPTION.** [ADR-203](ADR-203.md) ruling 8 ruled the shape; [ADR-204](ADR-204.md) ruling 9 required it -- *"WHERE THE HORIZON HAS NO ANSWER, THE FIELD HAS NO VALUE, AND THE SHAPE OF THAT ABSENCE IS `ADR-203`'s"*; and `ADR-203` section 6's vocabulary already holds both causes the field needs. Nothing below reopens any of that.

**THE TWO CAUSES ARE `insufficient_history` AND `estate_uncovered` AND THEY STAY DISTINCT.** `ADR-204` section 7 asked for exactly that, on [`0032`](../../packages/db/migrations/0032_trading_calendar_holidays_coverage_revisions.sql)'s and [ADR-042](ADR-042.md) F-4's rule that an exhausted calendar and an uncovered one are different answers and **only one of them is safe to act on**. `readTradingHorizon` in [`liability.ts`](../../apps/api/src/admin-source/liability.ts) already answers three ways -- covered, `exhausted`, `uncovered` -- and the wire carried one, which is `B2`'s situation on a third field.

**NEITHER CAUSE IS `awaiting_dependency`, SO `awaiting` IS `null` ON THIS FIELD'S GAP**, which `ADR-203` ruling 4 makes a pairing rather than a convention and which `assertLiabilityGapsPaired` enforces. Section 5 shows that this is the fact the sibling projections' gap type cannot represent.

### 4. THE RULING ON THE SIBLING BODY

**RULING 2. `GET /admin/eligible-forecast` SERVES A BODY WITH A `null` AND ITS REASON. `adminNotFound` IS REFUSED.**

The endpoint exists to carry this one figure, so the absence has exactly two shapes available. **The precedent points one way and is not the argument**: `admin-breaker.ts`'s two sibling projections both answer with a `null` at the site and a `gaps` array on the body, which `ADR-203` section 7a correctly calls *"evidence rather than a rule"*.

**THE ARGUMENT IS THAT ON THIS HEADING A 404 IS ALREADY SPOKEN FOR.** [`API_CONTRACT`](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) section 8's own paragraph over these three paths records that `GET /admin/loss-ratios` and `GET /admin/cusum` are *"registered by nothing"*, that *"today they answer 404 on the admin origin as well as on the public one"*, and -- in the sentence that decides this -- that **"the two failures look identical from outside the tree"**. A 404 for *there is no horizon* would be a third meaning under a status code this document already says is ambiguous here, and the three would then be: a route nobody built, a route this deployment withholds, and **a live answer an operator has to act on today**. `estate_uncovered` means the estate records no opinion about the period, which [ADR-042](ADR-042.md) F-4 makes somebody's job **now**; encoding it as the same byte as a backlog item is how it waits.

**AND THE 404 THIS ROUTE ALREADY SERVES STAYS**, which is what makes the two separable rather than a judgement call: `readLiability()` returning `null` means **no snapshot has ever been written**, and that is not this figure declining. One route, two absences, and after this entry they are distinguishable.

**RULING 3. THE BODY CARRIES `LiabilityGap` AND NOT `ProjectionGap`, AND THE REASON IS A DECLARATION RATHER THAN A PREFERENCE.** [`admin-breaker.ts`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-breaker.ts) declares `ProjectionGap.awaiting` as a required `string`; [`admin-reads.ts`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-reads.ts) declares `LiabilityGap.awaiting` as `string | null` and `LiabilityGap.cause` as the closed set of three. **This field's two causes are `insufficient_history` and `estate_uncovered`, and `ADR-203` ruling 4 makes `awaiting` non-`null` EXACTLY when the cause is `awaiting_dependency`.** So the sibling projections' own gap type cannot express this absence without naming a deliverable that does not exist, which sends an operator to read a document nobody wrote. **`ADR-203` finding 2 refused to unify the two types and this entry does not unify them either**: it picks the one that can carry the value.

**RULING 4. THE GAP IS FORWARDED FROM THE LIABILITY BODY AND NEVER REBUILT.** This handler already derives from the same `readLiability()` call as `GET /admin/liability`, on section 8's *"focused projections of the same underlying data"* and on the reason [`admin-reads.ts`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-reads.ts) writes at the site: *"two queries are two answers to 'what is eligible in the next 7 days', and the chart and the dashboard disagreeing is how a number stops being believed"*. **A reason composed here would be that same defect one level down**, on the explanation instead of the figure. The handler filters the array to the entries naming its own field and carries those; `gaps` is `[]` on a complete forecast and is never omitted, on `ADR-203`'s rule that an optional key makes *"this figure is blocked"* and *"this deployment did not fill the field"* the same body.

**RULING 5. THE BODY IS NOT WRITTEN INTO `API_CONTRACT`.** It is `as_of`, the projected field and `gaps`, declared once in `admin-reads.ts`. A second copy in a frozen document would be a shape no invariant binds -- which is `RI-18`'s own subject and [ADR-185](ADR-185.md)'s general form, *generate the value or delete it and point at the source, there is no third* -- and the heading already points at `LiabilityResponse.eligible_next_7d`, which now carries the `| null`. **What the contract gains is two paragraphs of prose and no type**: the projection can decline, the 404 is refused, and the reason. [ADR-166](ADR-166.md) clause 3 is untouched; its finding `F5` names `loss-ratios` and `cusum` and not this path.

### 5. The control that was on no served path, which is a repair rather than a precaution

**`assertLiabilityGapsPaired` RUNS INSIDE `projectLiability`, AND THE FORECAST HANDLER DOES NOT CALL `projectLiability`.** It reads the same source and builds its own body. Three route cases watch the ruling itself: a declining forecast is `200` with the `null` and the reason, another field's gap does **not** ride along, and a bare `null` reaching this route is a `500`.
