## ADR-054: `R-35` does not run on the row that closes an account, and `GS-064`'s `20,000` is right  (2026-08-17, status: proposed)

**This ruling amends nothing in [M01](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)'s arithmetic. It states a scope `R-35` never carried and reverses the reading [`advance.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/advance.ts) implements today, so approving it changes engine behaviour on the money path.**

- **Context:** **`GS-064` pins `withdrawable_cents: 20000` and the engine produces `0`.** [STATE.md](../STATE.md) records it as a new founder item and as a **three-way** disagreement: the fixture pins `20,000` from the pre-breach balance, the engine produces `0` and states its reading in a code comment, and `R-35`'s arithmetic on the pinned closing balance would give `40,000`. **What `R-35` measures on the row that CLOSES an account is undecided in the corpus**, and picking a side by editing either file is the `TR-01` failure the stage exists to catch.

  **It is the last red fixture once [ADR-052](ADR-052.md) is applied**, which has not happened: `neverRetreats('R-15', ...)` is still in [`floor.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/floor.ts) and `GS-024` is still red.

  **This ADR decides against the engine. The fixture is right, and it is right for a reason neither of the other two readings can reach: `R-35` does not run on that row at all.**

---

### 1. `R-35` is a `DO-9` gate and `DO-9` never runs on a breach day

**Three binding statements say it and they do not need to be reconciled, because they agree.**

| Where | What it says |
|---|---|
| [M01 section 3.1](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md), the `DO-5` row | "On breach: phase to `closed`, `breached = true`, emit `breach.detected`. **Nothing after this runs.** Breach beats every pass, target, and eligibility condition that the same day might also satisfy" |
| M01 section 3.1, the `DO-9` row | "Evaluate every engine gate, compute `engineEligible`, compute `stateHash`, emit `day.closed` with the full payload \| **`R-33` to `R-41`**" |
| `R-24` | "Breach is terminal and immediate ... **no further state is ever written for the account**" |

`R-35` is one of `R-33` to `R-41`. `DO-9` is where they run. `DO-5` returns before `DO-9`. **So the question "what does `R-35` measure on the closing row" has the answer "it does not measure anything on that row", and the field carries forward like every other field the breach block does not name.**

**Section 3.6's breach block is an explicit list of eight fields and `withdrawableCents` is not one of them.**

```ts
s = { ...s, tradingDay: mark.tradingDay, phase: 'closed', breached: true, breachKind: kind,
      floorOpenCents: floorOpen, balanceCents: mark.closingBalanceCents,
      engineEligible: false, engineVersion };
events.push(breachDetected(s, mark, floorOpen, kind));
return { state: withHash(s), events, assertions };            // R-24, terminal
```

Everything else is `...s`, the previous close. `withdrawable_cents` carries at day three's value by exactly the same construction that carries `win_days_count`, `traded_days_count`, `high_water_balance_cents`, `floor_cents` and the two consistency accumulators, all of which `GS-064` also pins and all of which the engine already matches. **The disputed field is the only one where the engine departs from the spread, and it departs because a line was added, not because a line was read differently.**

**`R-25` and the registry row are about eligibility and this is consistent with them.** `R-25` reads "No `phase.passed`, **no eligibility**, no graduation", and the [registry row](../testing/golden-scenarios/05-gs-052-to-gs-070-adversarial-scenarios-m1-section-7.md) reads "Breach wins. **An eligibility** that existed at the previous close does not survive the breach check". What does not survive is `engine_eligible`, and section 3.6 writes it `false` outright. Neither sentence says an input to the eligibility decision is rewritten, and the fixture's own note is built on that distinction: "**Every input the decision reads is pinned at the value it held at the previous close**, where all six engine gates passed, and `engine_eligible` is `false` beside them."

### 2. The four days, computed by hand

`MERIT-RAPID-50K` ([Appendix A.2](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) at 50K): `size_cents 5,000,000`, funded `drawdown_cents 250,000`, `buffer_cents 100,000`, `win_day_floor_cents 15,000`, `floor_lock_at_profit_cents 260,000`. Funded from day one, opened `2026-10-30`.

| Trading day | Close | Low | `floorOpen` (`R-18`) | `R-21`, strict `<` | `hwb` after `DO-7` | Floor after `DO-7` | `R-35` at `DO-9` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-02 | 5,040,000 | 4,995,000 | 4,750,000 | no | 5,040,000 | 4,790,000 | `5,040,000 - 5,100,000` -> **0** |
| 2026-11-03 | 5,080,000 | 4,800,000 | 4,790,000 | no | 5,080,000 | 4,830,000 | `5,080,000 - 5,100,000` -> **0** |
| **2026-11-04** | 5,120,000 | 5,075,000 | 4,830,000 | no | 5,120,000 | **4,870,000** | `5,120,000 - 5,100,000` -> **20,000** |
| **2026-11-05** | 5,140,000 | **4,869,999** | **4,870,000** | **`4,869,999 < 4,870,000`, breach** | never runs | never runs | **never runs** |

**Day three is where every engine gate passes**, which is the antecedent the scenario exists to state: `R-33` skipped at `min_trading_days 0`, `R-34` at `3 >= 3`, `R-35` at `20,000 > 0`, `R-36` at `40,000 * 10000 = 400,000,000 <= 4000 * 120,000 = 480,000,000`, `R-37` skipped with no anchor, and `R-39` at `min(20,000, 100,000) >= 10,000`.

**Day four carries all of it.** The reference algorithm reproduces every one of `GS-064`'s fourteen pinned fields, `withdrawable_cents: 20000` included. **The lock never engages** (120,000c of profit against a 260,000c trigger), so [ADR-052](ADR-052.md) does not touch this fixture and neither does [ADR-053](ADR-053.md), whose `hwb` bound only binds after a lock.

### 3. The engine's `0` comes from a second call site, and the reasoning for it is a comment doing a document's job

[`advance.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/advance.ts) calls `withdrawableCents(closedState, plan)` inside the breach block, after setting `phase: 'closed'`, and [`gates.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/payout/gates.ts) returns `0n` for any phase that is not `funded`. **The function is a faithful transcription of M01's `withdrawable()`, including the guard. The defect is the new call site.** Its stated reason is a comment:

> R-35 on a closed account is `0n`, and it is recomputed rather than carried from `prior`: a breached account that was withdrawable-positive yesterday must not present a positive withdrawable on the row that closed it.

**Four things put it wrong, in the order they bind.**

**First, the guard's `closed` arm is unreachable in M01's own algorithm, so it cannot be the guard's purpose.** `withdrawable()` is called at exactly one site in section 3.6. At that line the phase can only be `eval` or `funded`: `DO-1` refuses a `closed` or `graduated` prior, `DO-2` returns on `graduated`, and `closed` is set only by the breach block, which returns. **The guard exists for the eval phase**, where an account can hold 300,000c of profit that `R-31` is about to take back and that no gate may treat as extractable. The engine made the `closed` arm reachable by adding a second call site and then read a policy out of the arm it had just reached. (It is separately reachable through `R-32` expiry, which does fall through to `DO-9`, so this ruling does not make the guard dead code.)

**Second, the reason given is a context concern living in a hashed, replayed field.** "Must not present" is about what a reader is shown. [`0015_rule_states.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql) puts `withdrawable_cents` at **position 9 of the `state_hash` input list** and excludes `context_gates` in the same comment block, citing `INV-23`. `INV-23` is the rule that keeps context out of the replayed state, and `SD-06` exists because mixing the two "guarantees nightly false divergences". **Encoding a presentation rule into hash input 9 is that boundary crossed from the other side.** Constitution E2 makes `0015` the strongest source available here, since a merged migration is never edited.

**Third, the protection the comment wants already exists, three times, stated.** `R-40` is a context gate requiring account `active` and phase `funded`. `R-41` is the conjunction, "with no shortcut path and no override anywhere in the codebase". [M01 section 2.2](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) declares `PayoutEvaluation.maxPayoutCents` as "`min(withdrawable, cap)`, **0 when not eligible**". The [GLOSSARY](../GLOSSARY.md#open-liability)'s open liability is the sum "across all **funded** accounts", so a closed row never enters the firm's liability number either. **And `engine_eligible` on the row is already `false`.** A positive `withdrawable_cents` on a closed row authorises nothing, and the corpus says so in three places rather than relying on the field.

**Fourth, the corpus states the opposite direction on the underlying question.** [M01 section 3.3](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md): "**`in_flight` to `breached` still pays.** ... the eligibility snapshot was true when it was taken, **the money was already the trader's under the rules as published**, and clawing it back would be exactly the behavior that kills payout trust. The account closes; the transfer completes." [`FM-M5-10`](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) names `GS-064` on that row and `INV-M5-09` restates it. **A breach closes the account. It does not retroactively unwind what the rules had already granted**, and the comment's premise is that it should.

**The database does not decide this, and that is stated so nobody looks for a tiebreak there.** `0015`'s only constraint on the column is `withdrawable_cents >= 0`, which both readings satisfy.

### 4. `40,000` is produced by no reading at all

It requires **both** running `DO-9`'s `R-35` inside `DO-5` **and** discarding the phase guard M01 states. It is `R-35`'s arithmetic applied to a balance `DO-9` never saw. **No document and no code path produces it**, and it is recorded here only because STATE named three readings and a ruling that dismissed one silently would leave the third alive.

### 5. What this amends

| Where | From | To |
|---|---|---|
| [M01 section 3.6](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md), the commentary on `withdrawable()` | Silent on where the function is called and why the guard exists | `withdrawable()` is called at **one** site, `DO-9`. The phase guard is the **eval** phase's, and a breach row never reaches it: `DO-5` returns and the field carries |
| [`advance.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/advance.ts), the breach block | `withdrawableCents(closedState, plan)`, recomputed | Deleted. `withdrawableCents` carries through the spread, as the other six carried fields already do |

**`R-35`, `R-21`, `R-24`, `R-25`, `INV-05` and `INV-23` are unchanged.** This ruling makes the engine agree with them rather than moving any of them, which is the argument for it: **the arithmetic was never in dispute, only the question of whether it runs, and section 3.1 already answered that in four words.**

- **Alternatives considered.** **The engine's `0`, on the reading that a `closed` row must show nothing extractable**: rejected on all four grounds in section 3, and the strongest form of it is worth naming because it is not silly. A reader who asks "what does `R-35` evaluate to against this row" gets `0`, because the row's phase is `closed` and the guard is real. **The answer is that `R-35` is never asked**, and a function's return value on an argument it is never given is not a fact about the row. **`40,000`**: rejected in section 4. **Adding a phase-conditional `CHECK` to `rule_states` so the database rules it**: rejected, because it would need a superseding migration to encode a reading no document states, and it would put the same context concern one layer lower.

- **Consequences:**
  - **`GS-064` is confirmed rather than edited, and no fixture changes.** It is the only fixture in the directory that pins `engine_eligible`, and the fields it pins beside that flag are the reason the assertion bites: `engine_eligible: false` alone is satisfied by an engine that never computed it.
  - **[`advance.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/advance.ts) changes on approval**, and its three-line comment is the argument being overruled. `gatesAfterBreach` then reports `minimumAmount.withdrawableCents` at the carried value rather than at zero. **That field is nested inside `engine_gates`, which [`compare.ts`](../../packages/golden-loader/src/compare.ts) does not diff**, so no fixture can pin it and the change is invisible to `CI-03`. It is named here because a reader of the diff will see it move.
  - **`GS-064` goes green and `CI-03`'s red set empties.** With [ADR-052](ADR-052.md) applied, `GS-024` and `GS-064` are the two remaining failures. **Whoever applies both rulings should expect the count to reach zero**, and should treat a third failure appearing as evidence that one of the two fixes is wrong rather than as a new discovery.
  - **The mechanical remedy is `RE-U-025`, and it is cheap.** [M01 section 8](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) requires 50 unit tests, "one per rule `R-01` to `R-50`". `R-25`'s asserts the **carried-field set** directly: on a breach row, every field outside section 3.6's eight equals the prior state's. That is a mechanical check on the exact class of error this ruling exists to settle, and per [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md)'s own caution it is worth more than the ruling: "prefer a new CI gate over a bigger model whenever the error is checkable."
  - **No `SD-nn`, no `rule_states` column and no migration number.** `0038` stays free, per [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md)'s rule that a number reserved against work that should not yet be written is worse than no number.
  - **Trader-facing copy is unaffected.** No published sentence describes the withdrawable on a closed account, because there is no screen on which a closed account offers a withdrawal.
  - Net liability direction: **none.** The field is not payable through any path, so neither reading moves a cent of firm exposure. **That is precisely why it had to be decided on the ordering law rather than on consequences**, and why an engine session was free to pick either one without noticing.

- **Founder approval: PENDING.** This reverses shipped engine behaviour on the money path and states a scope in a frozen document, so it is not taken on a session's authority ([E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md)). **The engine's reasoning was weighed as a comment and not as a document**, on [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md)'s record that this week's worst errors were failures to check a claim against its primary source. **Recommended for acceptance.**
