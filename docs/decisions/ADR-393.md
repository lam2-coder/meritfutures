### 11. Where this entry is most likely wrong

1. **THE 84 IS A COUNT OF POINTERS WHOSE CITED LINE CHANGED, NOT OF POINTERS THAT ARE WRONG ABOUT THEIR SUBJECT.** The two coincide wherever the subject was on the cited line, which is what G1 and G2 test per pointer, and this row ran that test on 21. **For the other 63 the 84 is an upper bound on the wrong ones and a lower bound on nothing**, because a pointer whose line never changed can still have been born wrong, as section 6's third case was.
2. **SECTION 5's CLASS COULD BE ARGUED INTO CLASS TWO BY A READER WHO DEFINES THE SUBJECT AS THE REGISTER RATHER THAN THE ENTRY.** `INVENTORY_PROBES` still exists and is still declared; only the entry inside it is gone. **The reason this row did not take that reading is that the citing sentences name the ENTRY and the PROBE, not the register**, so repairing to the register's declaration would answer a question neither entry asked. A founder may disagree, and the repair would then be one line each.
3. **THE THREE CLASS-TWO VERDICTS REST ON `git blame -w` PICKING THE RIGHT COMMIT.** Where a line has been reflowed rather than authored, blame can name a later commit than the one whose author made the claim, and the corpus has had at least one whitespace sweep across the runner directory. **The counter for the two in [ADR-330](ADR-330.md) is that the blamed commit is the commit that created the file**, so there is no earlier candidate, and for the third the subject was 150 lines away, which no reflow explains.
4. **SECTION 8's TWO REPAIRS ASSUME THE THREE REGISTRY ROWS SURVIVE THE MERGE IN THE POSITION THIS ROW PUT THEM.** If a sibling's row lands between them, the offset changes and both pointers are wrong again by a different amount. **That is not hypothetical: two sibling rows are writing rows into the same table in the same wave.** The repair is right on this branch and the merge can falsify it, which is [ADR-390](ADR-390.md) section 10's measurement arriving at this row's own diff.
5. **THE FOURTH OUTCOME MAY ALREADY BE COVERED AND THIS ROW MAY HAVE MISSED IT.** [ADR-388](ADR-388.md)'s census counted 2 pointers *false then, true now, by coincidence* and did not report a bucket for *true then, subject since deleted*. **The absence of the bucket in its table is the evidence used here, and an absence in a table is weaker evidence than a sentence.**
