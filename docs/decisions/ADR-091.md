## ADR-091: analytics is granted `rule_states`, because denying it FORCES the attack the denial cites, and M13's claim that the thresholds are unreachable does not survive `engine_gates`  (2026-08-24, status: proposed)

**[Session 164](../sessions/2026-08-24-session-164.md) found that the role implementing M13's one money-path invariant cannot read the table that invariant names as its source. The finding reproduces. It is not the whole finding.** Re-derived here against a PostgreSQL 16 database built from all 47 migrations, the exclusion is **an omission rather than the revoke session 164 named**, and the sentence M13 uses to justify the whole arrangement -- *"the thresholds are unreachable"* -- is **false about the table this entry grants**. Both halves move, in opposite directions: the grant is too narrow and the invariant's enforcement clause is overclaimed. **A ruling only. No migration, no grant change, no `packages/db` change.**

- **Origin:** [session 164](../sessions/2026-08-24-session-164.md)'s first landmine, dispatched as `M13-a`'s first half. This entry takes the first half only; `GS-174`'s missing fourth role is named in section 7 and left.

- **Context:** [`0026_roles_and_grants.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql) is merged and `E2`-read. **It is never edited, only superseded** (constitution E2), so nothing here proposes changing it. What this entry decides is the SHAPE the superseding migration must have, and that migration is a later session with its own number and its own read.

### 1.1 Session 164's sentence is right in its conclusion and wrong in its mechanism, and the difference decides the remedy

Its title reads *"the role ... **revokes** the table that invariant reads."* **The revoke is not what does it.**

`0026` builds the analytics surface by enumeration, and says so in its own words at [`0026:124-126`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql): *"Explicitly enumerated rather than granted-then-revoked, because the risk here is a table added later becoming readable by default, and the default should be that it is not."* `merit_analytics` therefore never held anything on `rule_states`, and `:157` revokes a privilege that was never granted.

**Measured rather than reasoned about.** A second database was built from the same 47 files with **line 157 deleted**, and read back:

```
DB A (as merged):        merit_analytics SELECT rule_states = false
DB B (line 157 deleted): merit_analytics SELECT rule_states = false
```

**Deleting the line changes nothing.** `0026:157` is declaratory; the operative cause is the ABSENCE of `rule_states` from the enumerated `GRANT SELECT` at `:127-137`. This matters for exactly one reason and it is not pedantry: **the remedy is an addition, not a deletion.** A session that read session 164's sentence and set out to "move the grant" would look for a line to remove, and removing it -- which E2 forbids anyway -- would leave the defect standing.

**The remedy shape was proven too, on DB B**: a `GRANT SELECT ON rule_states TO merit_analytics` issued **after** the set flips the privilege to `true`. A later migration works. That is the whole of what the superseding migration has to do.

### 1.2 The reason `0026` gives is in no corpus document, and it argues against a threat M13 does not have

[`0026:150-152`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql) states it: *"rule_states is excluded for the same reason: it is the engine's verdict, and an analytics surface that reads verdicts will eventually be asked to explain them."*

`grep -rn` over the whole of `docs/` finds that sentence, and any paraphrase of it, in **no document**. The nearest corpus text points the other way in three places:

- [`0026:14-18`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql)'s own `E2` header names item 3 as *"THE ANALYTICS ROLE CANNOT READ **PLAN CONFIG** AT ALL (M13)"*. It does not mention `rule_states`.
- `DEP-M13-05` ([M13:363](../plans/M13-trader-analytics-journal.md)) enumerates what the role must **not** hold -- *"`plan_versions`, `plan_version_sizes`, or the indicative cache"* -- and `rule_states` is not on it.
- [`rule_states.md:35`](../architecture/data-model/rule_states.md) reads *"Append-only. Retention: forever."* and says nothing about analytics, while **seven other [data-model](../architecture/data-model/README.md) pages carry the exact line** *"Not readable by `merit_analytics`"* (`economic_calendar`, `economic_calendar_loads`, `impersonation_page_views`, `report_deliveries`, `report_schedules`, `trading_calendar_loads`, `trading_calendar_revisions`) and two more say it in other words ([`impersonation_sessions.md:25`](../architecture/data-model/impersonation_sessions.md), *"holds no grant at all"*; [`data-model/README.md:517`](../architecture/data-model/README.md), *"`merit_analytics` holds nothing"*). **The convention for recording this exclusion exists, nine pages use it, and `rule_states` does not carry it.**

**And the reason is self-defeating on the module's own adversarial scenario.** `AS-M13-01`'s attack ([M13:187](../plans/M13-trader-analytics-journal.md)) is stated in one sentence: *"`rule_states` has it, but joining is awkward, and the fills are right there. So the page sums them."* Its counter 2 ([M13:195](../plans/M13-trader-analytics-journal.md)) is *"Every engine value is **read from `rule_states`**"*. **The attack is what happens when `rule_states` is hard to reach; the revoke makes it impossible to reach.** Denying the table does not mitigate `AS-M13-01`. It removes the only defence M13 offers against it and leaves the demand for the number in place, which is the second rulebook arriving by construction rather than by carelessness -- the phrase `DEP-M13-01` ([M13:359](../plans/M13-trader-analytics-journal.md)) already uses for this exact outcome: *"`AN-M13-04` has to recompute, which is `AS-M13-01` by force rather than by carelessness."*

`0015` says the same thing from the other side, in the migration that created the column. [`0015:121-122`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql): *"Gate-by-gate booleans plus the numbers behind them, **so the portal renders truth rather than recomputing it**."* **`0026` revoked the table whose stated purpose is to make recomputation unnecessary.**

### 1.3 The half nobody had found: `engine_gates` carries eight plan thresholds

This is where the entry stops agreeing with session 164 and stops agreeing with M13.

`INV-M13-01`'s enforcement cell ([M13:50](../plans/M13-trader-analytics-journal.md)) reads *"The module has no access to plan config **at all**"*, and `AS-M13-01` counter 1 ([M13:194](../plans/M13-trader-analytics-journal.md)) reads *"It is not possible to evaluate a gate here, because **the thresholds are unreachable**."*

**`rule_states.engine_gates` is `jsonb` ([`0015:129`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql)) and [`hash.ts:421-476`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/hash.ts) declares its twenty-five leaves. Eight of them are plan thresholds**, read from the leaf list in this session:

| Leaf | At | The plan parameter it carries |
|---|---|---|
| `tradedDays.need` | [`hash.ts:427`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/hash.ts) | `min_trading_days` |
| `winDays.need` | [`hash.ts:432`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/hash.ts) | required win days |
| `winDays.floorCents` | [`hash.ts:433`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/hash.ts) | `win_day_floor_cents` |
| `buffer.needCents` | [`hash.ts:438`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/hash.ts) | the permanent buffer |
| `consistency.maxDayShareBp` | [`hash.ts:448`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/hash.ts) | `ConsistencyRules.maxDayShareBp` ([`types.ts:322`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts)) |
| `cadenceGap.need` | [`hash.ts:463`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/hash.ts) | the cadence gap |

| `minimumAmount.minPayoutCents` | [`hash.ts:476`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/hash.ts) | `min_payout_cents` |

**So the moment `rule_states` is granted, "no access to plan config at all" and "the thresholds are unreachable" both stop being true**, and they stop being true whatever this entry rules, because they are claims about a table M13 itself requires the module to read. **The two sentences were never jointly satisfiable with `AN-M13-04`.** That is the finding session 164 did not reach, and it is the reason this ruling amends M13 rather than only the grant.

**It does not, however, reinstate `AS-M13-01`.** What `engine_gates` hands over is a threshold **pinned to one account on one trading day, sitting in the same row as the engine's `have`, its `pass`, and `engine_eligible`** -- all inside the state hash ([`0015:139-151`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql), ordinal 19). The second-rulebook failure needs a **current, free-floating** parameter applied to a period the engine scored differently, and that is `plan_versions`, which stays revoked (measured `false`, and it is `DEP-M13-05`'s actual list). **Recomputing from `engine_gates` is not dangerous; it is pointless, because the answer is already in the row.** That is the distinction `0026:150-152` collapses when it treats reading a verdict as explaining one.

### 2. The ruling

> **1. `merit_analytics` is granted `SELECT` on `rule_states`.** The exclusion at [`0026:153-167`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql) is wrong on M13's own text and on `0015`'s own stated purpose for the column. `INV-M13-01` is the invariant and the grant is the defect.
>
> **2. It is a NEW migration and never an edit to `0026`** (constitution E2). It is a `GRANT`, not the removal of a `REVOKE`: `0026:157` is declaratory and deleting it changes nothing, measured in section 1.1.
>
> **3. The plan-config revoke STAYS, and it is what makes clause 1 safe.** `plans`, `plan_versions` and `plan_version_sizes` remain unreadable by `merit_analytics`. Clause 1 does not reopen [`0026:143-148`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql), and `DEP-M13-05`'s list is discharged exactly as written.
>
> **4. `SELECT` only, and `rule_states` only.** Not `INSERT`, not `UPDATE`, not `DELETE` -- `rule_states` is in `0026:88`'s append-only revoke and stays there. The other twelve tables at `0026:154-166` are untouched by this entry.
>
> **5. `INV-M13-01`'s enforcement cell and `AS-M13-01`'s counter 1 are AMENDED**, because clause 1 makes them false. *"No access to plan config at all"* and *"the thresholds are unreachable"* become: **no access to the plan config TABLES, and no access to any threshold except the one the engine pinned into the row it is rendering.** The enforcement is still a permission and still not a convention; it is now stated at the precision the schema actually has.

**Clause 5 is the one a reader should be slowest to accept, and it is the reason this entry exists as a ruling rather than a migration.** Amending the enforcement text of the corpus's only invariant held to money-path standard outside the money path is a founder's call, not a session's.

### 3. The alternative, and what it gives up

**"The invariant is wrong, not the grant."** M13 amended so `AN-M13-04` never existed and analytics never reads `rule_states`. The dispatching brief named this as possibly the better answer, so it is stated at its strongest and then measured.

Its strength is real: it needs no migration at all, it leaves `0026` saying exactly what it says today, and it makes `INV-M13-01`'s *"no access to plan config at all"* literally true, which clause 5 gives up.

**Its cost was measured, and it is not one surface.**

| What it removes | Measured how |
|---|---|
| **`AN-M13-04` entirely** | its data floor **is** `rule_states` ([M13:30](../plans/M13-trader-analytics-journal.md)) |
| **`AN-M13-01`'s equity surface, which is the `daily_marks` floor `OQ-M13-02` proposes shipping on** | [`API_CONTRACT:364`](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md)'s `MarkListItem` carries `floor_cents` and `withdrawable_cents`. `select table_name from information_schema.columns where column_name in ('floor_cents','withdrawable_cents')` returns **`rule_states` for both**, plus `price_floors.floor_cents`, which is `SD-M17-02` offer pricing ([`0024:82`](../../packages/db/migrations/0024_offers.sql)) and a different subject. **`daily_marks` has neither column.** The endpoint M13 calls the equity curve's source already joins the revoked table |
| **`GS-172`, whose whole subject is consistency share against the engine's value** | `consistency_best_day_cents` and `consistency_period_profit_cents` exist on **`rule_states` and nowhere else**, by the same query |
| **`OQ-M13-02`'s launch scope** | it proposes `AN-M13-01` and `AN-M13-04` as the surfaces that ship regardless of the vendor call ([M13:347](../plans/M13-trader-analytics-journal.md)). Both are in the two rows above |

**And it does not buy the safety it is bought for.** Section 1.2: the demand for "profit this consistency period" does not leave when the table does, and `fills` and `daily_marks` stay granted (measured `true`). The alternative deletes the compliant path and leaves the non-compliant one, which is `AS-M13-01` with its counter removed. **It loses on its own terms, not on convenience.**

### 4. The foreclosure: the column-level grant, available and not taken

Postgres can grant `SELECT` per column, and it was tested rather than assumed. On DB B:

```
GRANT SELECT (account_id, trading_day, floor_cents, balance_cents, withdrawable_cents,
              win_days_count, consistency_best_day_cents, consistency_period_profit_cents,
              consistency_period_start_day, engine_eligible) ON rule_states TO merit_analytics;

has_table_privilege (...,'rule_states','SELECT')            = false
has_column_privilege(...,'rule_states','floor_cents',...)   = true
has_column_privilege(...,'rule_states','engine_gates',...)  = false
```

**This is a genuine third option**: it grants everything `AN-M13-01` needs and withholds the eight thresholds of section 1.3, which would let clause 5 be dropped and `INV-M13-01`'s text stand unamended.

**It is not ruled here, and what it costs is stated rather than waved at.** `AN-M13-04` is *"floor distance, buffer progress, win-day accumulation, and consistency share"* ([M13:30](../plans/M13-trader-analytics-journal.md)). Without `engine_gates`: **buffer progress has no source at all** (`buffer.haveCents` and `needCents` are leaves, not columns), and win-day accumulation and consistency share lose their **targets** -- the surface can render `have` and cannot render `need`, which is a progress bar with no end. A trader shown 7 win days with no idea that 10 is the number is the `INV-M13-05` failure (*"absent and explained, never approximated"*) arriving as a half-rendered metric.

**It is left open deliberately and named so it is not rediscovered**, because it trades a real surface for a real narrowing and that trade is the founder's. **Whichever way it goes, one warning belongs with it:** a probe written against `has_table_privilege` reports `false` on a correctly column-granted database. **The checkable clause in section 8 is written on `has_column_privilege` for that reason**, and it is the form the eventual probe should take.

### 8. Founder approval

- **Founder approval: PENDING. This entry is `proposed` and the approval line below is UNSIGNED.** It is **money path**: `INV-M13-01` is held to money-path standard by [M13:50](../plans/M13-trader-analytics-journal.md) despite the module's `ADR-003` classification, and clause 5 amends the enforcement text of that invariant. This branch waits for the line-by-line read.

  **One checkable clause, written in the direction the ruling could FAIL in rather than the direction it was written for.** It is executable SQL against a database built from [`packages/db/migrations/`](../../packages/db/migrations), and every conjunct is `false` on the tree as merged today:

  ```sql
  SELECT
    has_column_privilege('merit_analytics','rule_states','floor_cents','SELECT')
    AND has_column_privilege('merit_analytics','rule_states','consistency_best_day_cents','SELECT')
    AND has_column_privilege('merit_analytics','rule_states','engine_gates','SELECT')
    AS renders_without_recomputing,
    NOT has_table_privilege('merit_analytics','plan_versions','SELECT')
    AND NOT has_table_privilege('merit_analytics','plan_version_sizes','SELECT')
    AS cannot_reach_current_config,
    NOT has_table_privilege('merit_analytics','rule_states','UPDATE')
    AND NOT has_table_privilege('merit_analytics','rule_states','DELETE')
    AS append_only_survives;
  ```

  **All three must be `true` together, and the ruling is wrong if any one of them can be `false` while the other two hold.** `has_column_privilege` rather than `has_table_privilege` on the first, so that a column-level grant under section 4 satisfies it and a table-level one does too.

  **Cost if wrong, in the three directions the clause can break.**

  **If `renders_without_recomputing` is false**, `AN-M13-04` has no source and `AN-M13-01` loses `floor_cents` and `withdrawable_cents`, which live only in `rule_states`. The demand for the number does not go away, so the page sums `fills` -- which stay granted -- and Merit ships `AS-M13-01`: a trader reads a consistency share, requests a payout, and the engine disagrees. **That is today's tree, and it is why this entry rules the way it does.**

  **If `cannot_reach_current_config` is false**, clause 1 was written too wide and `DEP-M13-05` is undischarged. This is the direction the founder's read is actually for: an analyst holding `plan_versions` can evaluate a gate against a version the account was never sold under, and `0026`'s item 3 becomes documentation. Section 1.3 concedes the narrower version of this openly -- **eight pinned thresholds do arrive with clause 1** -- and clause 5 is the entry saying so in the invariant's own text rather than letting the discrepancy sit. **A reader who thinks the pinned thresholds are already too many should reject clause 1 and take section 4's column grant, whose cost is stated there.**

  **If `append_only_survives` is false**, an analytics surface can write the engine's verdict. `rule_states` is one of the eight tables [`0026:19-21`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql) calls *"the financial and evidentiary spine"*, its rows are hashed and replayed nightly, and a writable verdict makes the replay self-audit compare the engine against something that is no longer only the engine's.

  **What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.** Every fact here is measured, and the judgement is not. **Clause 5 amends the enforcement sentence of the one invariant M13 raises to money-path standard**, and it amends it in the direction of admitting that a control claims more than the schema delivers. The alternative reading -- that the sentence was the point and the surface should shrink to fit it -- is section 4, priced at buffer progress and two missing targets. **Which of those two is the right thing to pay is not a measurement, and nothing in this session can make it one.**

  **Every load-bearing claim above was executed in this session, not recalled.** The privilege rows come from `has_table_privilege` against a PostgreSQL 16.13 cluster carrying all 47 migrations applied in order under `ON_ERROR_STOP=1`. **Section 1.1's claim that `0026:157` is inert is a SECOND database built with that line deleted and read back**, rather than an inference from the enumerated grant. Section 4's column-level behaviour is `GRANT SELECT (...)` executed and read back, not a citation of the Postgres manual. The eight threshold leaves are counted from [`hash.ts:421-476`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/hash.ts) with `ConsistencyRules` confirmed at [`types.ts:322`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts). The absence of a grants probe is `grep -rl` over three directories plus a read of all fifteen files in [`scripts/db/`](../../scripts/db). `0026:150-152`'s reason being in no corpus document is `grep -rn` over `docs/`, and the seven data-model pages that DO carry the *"Not readable by `merit_analytics`"* line, plus the two that say it otherwise, are the control that makes its absence from [`rule_states.md`](../architecture/data-model/rule_states.md) evidence rather than silence.

  **THIS ENTRY WAS `ADR-090` UNTIL THE COLLISION IT PREDICTED ACTUALLY HAPPENED, AND IT IS `ADR-091` BECAUSE OF HOW THAT RESOLVED.** `090` was verified free against [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) on a `git fetch origin main` at `c1acfa7`, where `089` was the highest row and nothing claimed `090`. **[Session 174](../sessions/2026-08-24-session-174.md) was dispatched from the same reservation table in the same wave under the same *"lowest free number"* sentence**, read its ALLOCATION at the same commit, and took the same number.
