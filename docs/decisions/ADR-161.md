## ADR-161: the live tier enters `API_CONTRACT` as a PAYLOAD rather than as an endpoint, its degradation is one object rather than two messages, and `feed.*` is produced by the sweep and never by the feed  (2026-08-27, status: proposed)

**[`161`](ALLOCATION.md) was reserved REQUIRED rather than conditionally, because [P6](../plans/P6-live-tier.md)'s wave-1 table marks `P6-d` *"ADR required"* in the plan's own words and [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) makes an edit to a document the freeze covers an entry rather than a commit, regardless of size.** [ADR-153](ADR-153.md) took that route this morning for one wrong verb.

**What the contract gains is a surface it has no shape for.** [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) specifies requests and responses, and section 1's conventions each presume a client that asked: a base path, a content type, an `Idempotency-Key`, a cursor, and a rate limit stated per endpoint. **[ADR-020](ADR-020.md)'s tier 2 is server-initiated delivery of a value nobody requested**, the registry admits five verbs and keys on `METHOD /path`, and the surface list is closed at two. So the live tier does not fit inside what the contract already says, and the choice is between an entry that says what it does fit inside and a slice inventing the answer where nobody can cite it.

- **Origin:** [P6](../plans/P6-live-tier.md) `P6-d`, branch `claude/p6d-contract-catalogue`, session **272**. The number was **RESERVED BEFORE DISPATCH and REQUIRED** ([ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md)), so none was chosen here; the `161` row is amended **in place** per [ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3.

- **MONEY BY CONTENT AND NOT BY FILE.** No migration, no `SD-nn`, no ledger, no engine, no payout, no auth, and no code at all: three documents move. **It is worth a founder read anyway**, because a payload shape is what every later slice transcribes, and the two clauses that carry the safety are clauses 3 and 4 rather than the type block.

### 2. THE RULING

> **1. THE LIVE TIER ENTERS THE CONTRACT AS A PAYLOAD, AND THE TRADER'S SUBSECTION CARRIES NO `METHOD /path` HEADING.** Findings 1 and 2. Section 1's conventions presume a request, the verb list is closed at five and a socket upgrade is none of them. **Where the channel lives is `P6-b`'s and how it is framed is `P6-g`'s**, and this entry takes neither: what it fixes is the payload, the labeling and the degradation, which are the parts [ADR-020](ADR-020.md)'s hard rule, `INV-M4-11` and `INV-M4-12` are about and which no transport ruling changes.
>
> **2. THE SECTION IS THE SURFACE, SO THE OPERATOR'S LIVE DELIVERY CARRIES AN OPERATOR PREFIX OR IT IS SERVED ON THE PUBLIC ORIGIN.** Finding 3. The split is a prefix rule over `['/admin', '/internal']` and not an endpoint list, so a live path outside those prefixes is not withheld from the public deployment by anything: it is served by both. **The trader's channel is specified in section 6.1 and the operator's live figure in section 8**, and this clause binds the PATH SHAPE of whatever `P6-g` registers without touching the mechanism it rules on. [P6 section 10 item 2](../plans/P6-live-tier.md) names the failing direction as *"the founder's numbers get served on the public origin by a route [ADR-083](ADR-083.md)'s 404 never saw"*, and this is that direction closed at the contract rather than at the review.
>
> **3. THE TIER AND THE NUMBERS ARE ONE OBJECT, AND THE TWO ARMS DO NOT SHARE A FIELD NAME FOR THE SAME QUANTITY.** Finding 8 requires the fallback and the relabel to be one render, and finding 11 is the estate's own way of forcing that: an `indicative` frame carries `live_pnl_cents` and an `authoritative` frame carries `pnl_cents`, so a component cannot read a live field off a fallback frame and cannot render either number without having narrowed past the tier. **A separate message carrying the label is refused**: two messages are two renders, which is the implementation `GS-133` exists to fail and the one a careless test passes.
>
> **4. THE FIRST FRAME ON ANY SUBSCRIPTION IS AN `authoritative` FRAME, BECAUSE A FALLBACK NEEDS SOMETHING TO FALL BACK TO.** A client that has been sent only live frames and then loses its transport holds no last-closed values, so it either renders nothing or holds the last live number, which is `INV-M4-12`'s named failure exactly. **The one thing a client may act on by itself is its own transport closing**, which is an observation rather than a computation; a timeout is a computation over a clock and clause 5 forbids it.
>
> **5. FRESHNESS IS THE SERVER'S CLAIM, COPIED FROM `EconomicCalendarPanelResponse.freshness` RATHER THAN INVENTED, AND `sequence` IS ORDERING ONLY.** Findings 5 and 6. `stale` is the server's own answer against its own threshold. **A consumer may discard a frame older than one it holds and may conclude nothing about freshness from the ordinal**, because the ordinal is per account per trading day and a quiet market and a dead feed produce the same absence of a successor. Finding 16 is why the ordinal exists and it is the SERVER that reads it that way.
>
> **6. THE OPERATOR'S LIVE FIGURE IS A SEPARATE SURFACE AND NEVER A FIELD ON `GET /admin/liability`.** Section 8 gains `AdminLiveLiability`, transcribing finding 10's shape onto the wire including the `suppressed` arm. **A live field on the authoritative response makes the number an operator opens during an incident depend on a feed that is down**, and `INV-M6-12`'s *"no breaker, alarm, or task threshold reads it"* is the same coupling stated one level up. **It is a payload here for clause 1's reason applied to BOTH surfaces rather than to one**: whether it arrives on a route or on the channel the trader's frames use is the transport ruling clause 1 refuses to take, and finding 19 is what caught this entry taking it for the operator while refusing it for the trader. Clause 2 binds the path either way.
>
> **7. `feed.*` JOINS `EVENTS` AS SECTION 5.4, AND ITS PRODUCER IS THE EXPECTATION SWEEP RATHER THAN THE FEED OR THE INGEST.** Findings 12, 13 and 15. **The sweep's own expectation row changing state IS the transaction the event is written in**, so section 1's universal delivery rule holds unchanged rather than being amended for one family, and the streaming ingest keeps writing nothing but the live cache, so `INV-M2-14` stays true as written. **This is the shape the corpus has already used twice**, at `report_deliveries.due_at` and at `economic_calendar_loads`' coverage bound, and the expectation row itself is `P6-f`'s to write.
>
> **8. THIS ENTRY DESIGNS NEITHER THE CACHE, NOR THE CONTAINER, NOR THE SOCKET, NOR THE WIN-DAY PROJECTION.** [P6 section 10](../plans/P6-live-tier.md) items 1, 2, 3 and 4 carry them. **Live win-day and consistency tracking is deliberately absent from the payload**: finding 9 rows it as indicative and no document says what it is a projection OF, the two available readings are a second implementation of a rule the engine owns or a synthetic day assembled from ticks, and **the second is [ADR-020](ADR-020.md)'s hard rule crossed in the one direction that type-checks.** When it is ruled it arrives as a field on the indicative arm and not as a third arm, which is why clause 3's union is two.

| **Carry the fallback values in every live frame**, so a client always holds both | **It makes the authoritative numbers arrive on the feed's cadence**, which is the coupling clause 6 refuses on the admin side wearing the trader's clothes, and it puts a last-closed number on the wire many times a second for a value that changes once a day |

### 6. Founder approval

**One checkable clause, and it names an invariant rather than a count.** The count-shaped clause was available and is refused deliberately: an approval clause naming a number is one that drifts, and [P6](../plans/P6-live-tier.md)'s own registries have paid for that class of clause repeatedly.

> **Every `METHOD /path` [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) defines under section 8 or section 9, other than the liveness path, is classified `operator` by `classifyPath()` and is served by no public deployment**, checked with `classifyPath` and `surfaceServes` IMPORTED from [`surface.ts`](../../apps/api/src/surface.ts) together with `LIVENESS_PATH`, so neither the rule nor its one exception is restated here. `RI-09`'s own reason for reading that file rather than copying it is that a second copy of the prefix rule goes stale the first time the rule moves.
>
> **The direction it fails in is the one that matters.** A live operator path written as `/live/liability` passes every other check in this repository: it type-checks, it registers, it answers, and it is served identically by both deployments, because the withholding is a prefix match and nothing else. **The clause fails on it immediately**, and it fails the same way for any future row, which is what a count-shaped clause could not do.

**THE FIRST FORM OF THIS CLAUSE WAS FALSE AND RUNNING IT IS WHY THIS ONE IS NOT.** It read *"begins with one of `OPERATOR_PREFIXES`"* and it fails on `GET /health`, which section 9 rows in its own table as *"Public, returns `{ status: "ok" }` and nothing else"* and which [`surface.ts`](../../apps/api/src/surface.ts) registers on BOTH surfaces deliberately, because a constant response *"discloses nothing the origin's own reachability does not already disclose"*. **A clause naming a prefix could not see a deliberate exception; the application's own classifier already carries it as a third path class.**

**Executed, and the output is in the pull request.** The comparison in finding 18 was run against `CompositionReport.registered` on both surfaces rather than against a grep over route files, because [P6 section 5.1](../plans/P6-live-tier.md) records two greps getting the same figure wrong on the same day.

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.** Every fact in section 1 is a file's own words. **Clauses 3 and 4 are not.** Clause 3 rules that two arms of one union may not share a field name, which buys `INV-M4-12` at compile time and costs every consumer a narrowing it would otherwise not write; clause 4 rules that a subscription opens with a frame carrying yesterday's numbers, which is a wire message whose only purpose is to make a later failure honest. **Both are judgements about how much structure is worth spending to make a display obligation mechanical**, and section 4's last row is the alternative that spends more.

**Approval line: UNSIGNED.**
