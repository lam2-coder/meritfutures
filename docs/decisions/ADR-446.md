### 5. The sample: size, selection, and precisely what is and is not claimed

**METHOD, STATED RATHER THAN DESCRIBED.** Population `1,017`, stratified by [`isDatedRecord`](../../packages/tooling/checks/absence-grammar-census.mjs) into **721 dated-record** and **296 live**. **n = 60, thirty per stratum**, drawn by a Fisher-Yates shuffle over a seeded LCG at **seed 446**, without replacement, reproducible on any host and pinned by four cases in the test file. Every member was read at full sentence length and adjudicated by hand into four classes.

| Class | What it is | Dated (n=30) | Live (n=30) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **COVERAGE** | a claim about what the check population does not do | **21** | **21** |
| **QUOTATION** | the sentence is quoted from somewhere else | **4** | **2** |
| **FENCE** | a claim about what a ROW did, not what the SUITE does | **2** | **0** |
| **INCIDENTAL** | the negation does not govern a check claim at all | **3** | **7** |

**PRECISION IS 42 of 60, which is 0.700, Wilson 95% `[0.575, 0.801]`.** Projected onto the population that is **712 coverage claims, interval `[585, 815]`**. Per stratum both are 21 of 30, Wilson 95% `[0.521, 0.833]`, and **the two intervals overlap almost entirely, so this row claims NO difference in precision between the strata and the sample is not powered to find one.**

**WHAT IS CLAIMED:** that roughly three in ten members of this population are not coverage claims, and that the three failure classes are the ones named above. **WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED:** any per-file rate, any rate for a grammar other than the shipped one, and any claim that the dated and live strata differ in precision. **A single adjudicator classified all sixty and no second reader checked them**, which is the largest uncontrolled term in this section and is stated rather than left to be discovered.

### 6. The failure classes are manufactured by document conventions the corpus imposes on purpose

**THIS IS THE FINDING, AND IT IS ONE LEVEL EARLIER THAN THE DISPATCH'S HYPOTHESIS.** The dispatch expected the difference to be about TRUTH VALUE: a source sentence saying "nothing compares X" is usually stale, the same sentence in an ADR may be an accurate record of a hole. **The sample says the difference bites at REFERENCE instead.** In a document, thirty percent of the grammar's population is not an assertion about check coverage at all, and each of the three ways it fails is produced by a convention this corpus enforces deliberately.

1. **QUOTATION, 6 of 60.** Citation grammar REQUIRES an entry ruling on a sentence to quote it. `RI-35` already met this in source and registered two quoted error strings as `unbindable` rather than narrowing them away, [`absence-claims.mjs:2463`](../../packages/tooling/checks/absence-claims.mjs). In source those two are a residue. **In a document quotation is not a residue, it is the convention working correctly**, and no scan over raw text can tell a quotation from an assertion.
2. **FENCE, 2 of 60, both in dated records.** *"Nothing in this entry touches any of the three"*, [`ADR-230.md:119`](ADR-230.md). That is a claim about what a ROW DID. It is true forever and by construction, because the diff is immutable. **It is structurally identical to a coverage claim and semantically its opposite**: a coverage claim is a defect report, a fence statement is a completeness report, and this estate requires one in every entry.
3. **INCIDENTAL, 10 of 60, concentrated in live documents.** *"a token nothing checks is not a control"*, [`ALLOCATION.md:213`](ALLOCATION.md), is an aphorism about controls in general. *"Nothing in this rule grants a trader a read"*, [`2026-08-25-session-192.md:79`](../sessions/2026-08-25-session-192.md), is about a database GRANT and matches only because `read` is a checking verb. Neither says anything about the suite.

### 8. The ruling

**THE CENSUS IS SPLIT INTO TWO POPULATIONS WITH TWO DUTIES, AND THE SPLIT IS NOT SOURCE VERSUS DOCUMENT.** It is **STANDING CLAIM versus DATED OBSERVATION**, and that line runs THROUGH documents rather than around them.

- **A STANDING CLAIM** is read as current every time the file is opened. Source comments are standing claims. So are `docs/architecture/`, `docs/plans/`, `docs/testing/` and `docs/edge-cases/`. **What is owed is what is owed in source: the claim is true, or the file says it was wrong.**
- **A DATED OBSERVATION** is indexed to its own date. [`ADR-386.md:169`](ADR-386.md) rules that every pointer in a dated record is NAMED because it cannot be repaired by a commit, and that ruling is not about pointers alone. **What is owed is NAMING, by a later entry, and never repair.** `ADR-403` naming `ADR-392`'s false sentence is the whole remedy, correctly executed, and it is already in the tree.

**NAMING IS NOT A WEAKER DUTY THAN REPAIR. IT IS A DIFFERENT ONE**, because repair is unavailable. A rate computed over a population most of whose members are unrepairable by construction is not a defect rate. It is a census of the corpus's own recorded blind spots, which is what a dated record is FOR.

**THE OTHER THREE OPTIONS ARE REFUSED, EACH WITH ITS REASON.** *Tightening the grammar for documents* fails because the three failure classes are not phrasings, they are conventions, and section 6 shows a tighter regex cannot see a quotation. *Exempting documents entirely* fails because 55 standing claims live in `docs/` and section 9 shows they are auditable. *Reporting the question unanswerable* fails because it was answered, at a cost this section states.
