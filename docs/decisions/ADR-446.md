### 8. What that population actually contains, by hand

**METHOD, STATED RATHER THAN DESCRIBED.** Stratified by `isDatedRecord`. **n = 60, thirty per stratum**, drawn by a Fisher-Yates shuffle over a seeded LCG at **seed 446**, without replacement, over the FROZEN census after both section 5 and section 6 edits. Every member was read at full sentence length with its matched subject and verb, and adjudicated into four classes.

| Class | What it is | Dated (n=30) | Live (n=30) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **COVERAGE** | a claim about what the check population does not do | **15** | **13** |
| **FENCE** | a claim about what a ROW did, not what the SUITE does | **5** | **2** |
| **QUOTATION** | the sentence is quoted from somewhere else | **2** | **0** |
| **INCIDENTAL** | the negation does not govern a check claim at all | **8** | **15** |

**POOLED PRECISION IS 28 of 60, which is 0.467, Wilson 95% `[0.346, 0.591]`.** Dated is 0.500 `[0.332, 0.668]`, live is 0.433 `[0.274, 0.608]`. **This row claims NO difference between the strata**: a two-proportion z test gives `z = 0.518`, which is nowhere near significant. The first attempt reached the same conclusion on a different grammar over a different population, which is the one finding that has now survived both.

> **SO THE 1,302 IS A CEILING ROUGHLY TWICE THE REAL COUNT. Projected coverage claims among the live and unframed: 608, interval `[451, 770]`.** Over the whole document half of 4,849 it is 2,263, interval `[1,679, 2,866]`. The instrument's own closing line says it *"cannot tell a TRUE claim from a FALSE one"*; this section adds the prior step it also could not take, which is how many of its sentences are claims about coverage at all.

**A CAUTION MEASURED RATHER THAN ASSUMED, AND IT IS AN ARGUMENT AGAINST THIS SECTION'S OWN PRECISION.** An earlier draw of the same design, on the population as it stood before section 6's 23-sentence fix, adjudicated to **18 of 30 dated and 9 of 30 live**. The final draw gives 15 and 13. **A 23-sentence change in a 4,872-sentence population moved a stratum estimate by a sixth**, because it reshuffles the draw entirely. At thirty per stratum this design resolves the pooled rate and does not resolve a difference between strata. **One adjudicator classified all sixty and no second reader checked them.**

### 9. The ruling, which the reconciliation did not change

**THE CENSUS OWES A DOCUMENT SENTENCE TWO DIFFERENT DUTIES, AND THE SPLIT IS NOT SOURCE VERSUS DOCUMENT.** It is **STANDING CLAIM versus DATED OBSERVATION**, a line that runs THROUGH `docs/` rather than around it.

- **A STANDING CLAIM** is read as current every time the file is opened. Source comments are standing claims; so are `docs/architecture/`, `docs/plans/` and `docs/testing/`. **What is owed is what is owed in source: the claim is true, or the file says it was wrong.**
- **A DATED OBSERVATION** is indexed to its own date. [`ADR-386.md:169`](ADR-386.md) rules that a pointer in a dated record is NAMED because it cannot be repaired by a commit. **What is owed is NAMING BY A LATER ENTRY AND NEVER REPAIR.**

**THE MEASUREMENT THAT DECIDES IT IS AN INVERSION, AND IT IS STRONGER ON THIS TREE THAN ON THE LAST ONE.** The sentence *"no check in this tree compares a `schema.ts` column TYPE against the DDL"* now has **20 document members**, up from 16 a wave ago. **Four are inside [ADR-440](ADR-440.md), the entry that REPAIRED the source copies, and two more are inside [ADR-441](ADR-441.md).** It reproduces independently: [`ADR-392.md:236`](ADR-392.md) states *"nothing checks that the target file exists"*, which is FALSE and which [`ADR-403.md:3`](ADR-403.md) refutes with `CI-06a`; `ADR-392.md` carries **5** members and `ADR-403.md`, the entry correcting it, carries **12**.

> **A FALSITY RATE OVER `docs/` MOVES THE WRONG WAY WHEN THE CORPUS BEHAVES CORRECTLY.** Every repair of a source claim, and every naming of a false document one, ADDS members. **Naming is not a weaker duty than repair. It is a different one, because repair is unavailable**, and a rate over a population most of whose members are unrepairable by construction is not a defect rate.

### 10a. A NEW defect found by running the generator this row was required to run

**`gates.mjs generate` REWROTE A SPAN INSIDE A DATED RECORD, AND NO STATE OF THIS TREE SATISFIES BOTH RULES AT ONCE.**

[`ADR-447.md:219`](ADR-447.md) QUOTES a generated span in its prose. It is arguing that a figure it was handed was wrong, and to do that it reproduces the opening and closing markers verbatim, spelled `gen:adr_count` between angle-bracket comment delimiters. **The generator cannot tell a quotation from a live span**, so it rewrites the quotation on every wave that changes the count. This row's own `ADR-446.md` moved the count by one and that quotation went from `429` to `430`.

**AND THIS PARAGRAPH PROVED IT ON ITSELF, WHICH WAS NOT PLANNED.** The sentence above originally reproduced the markers verbatim in order to name them. **`gates.mjs generate` immediately rewrote THIS ENTRY's copy too**, reporting `docs/decisions/ADR-446.md: adr_count ... -> 430`, and `CI-06g` went RED until it did. **BOTH DISPOSITIONS WERE TRIED AND MEASURED, AND EACH FAILS A RULE.**

| Disposition | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Keep the generator's output | `33 of 33` gates PASS, and a **dated record has been edited** |
| Revert it | **`CI-06g` FAILS with 1 finding**, and `32 of 33` gates pass |

**THE GENERATED OUTPUT IS KEPT**, because a green tree with the defect NAMED is worth more than a red tree with it unnamed, and because the alternative is weakening a gate to pass it. It is the disposition [ADR-432](ADR-432.md) section 10 item 5 took for the same generator and the same cause.

> **THIS IS A CATEGORY [`ADR-386.md:169`](ADR-386.md) DOES NOT COVER.** That ruling says a pointer in a dated record **cannot be repaired by a commit**, so it is NAMED. This defect is the opposite failure: a dated record that quotes a generated span **is rewritten by a commit, silently, by a generator every row is required to run.** The quotation is not merely unrepairable, it is unstable. **The `429` this row found there was itself a rewrite**, because `ADR-447`'s surrounding prose argues about `426`, so the span has not held what the sentence says it holds since before this row existed.

**IT IS NOT REPAIRED HERE.** The repair is to escape the markers inside the quotation, which edits a dated record deliberately rather than mechanically, and that is not this row's call. Section 12 item 7 states it.

### 13. What a founder read adds and this entry cannot

**THE FIRST DECISION IS WHETHER A DISPATCH'S CITATIONS SHOULD RESOLVE BEFORE IT IS SENT.** This row was written twice for one reason: three artifacts existed on a branch the container could not see. The verification that failed was not sloppy, it was **three independent checks, all correct**. `RI-15` and `RI-16` hold documents to resolving pointers while a dispatch is held to nothing. **The cost is now measured at one wasted row**, and whether that buys a control is a judgement about the build loop rather than about this file.

This entry keeps the ceiling. **It is a presentation decision with a factor of two in it and a founder should make it.**

**THE THIRD IS WHETHER THE 21 SENTENCES `control` ADDS SHOULD NOW BE ANSWERED.** Section 5 adjudicated all 21 as genuine absence-of-check claims, and several are money-adjacent: [`SECURITY.md:295`](../architecture/SECURITY.md) on cross-firm hedging is a stated risk control that does not exist. **The census reports; it does not decide which holes matter.** That ranking is a founder's and no runner can take it.
