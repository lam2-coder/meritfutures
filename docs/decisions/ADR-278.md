## ADR-278: the rename ADR-272 specified, and the reader that could not have seen it land (2026-08-30, status: proposed)

**[ADR-272](ADR-272.md) section 5 specified this repair, fenced it out, and named the thing that would tell the next session it was finished: *"the `AT_SUFFIX_EXCEPTIONS` entry in `RI-26`, which turns RED on the day the rename lands and is what will tell that session it is not finished."* That sentence is FALSE, and finding out why is most of this entry.**

**THE RENAME LANDED AND `RI-26` REPORTED PASS.** Not because the repair was wrong, but because `RI-26` reads `CREATE TABLE` bodies and constitution E2 makes `0045`'s body permanent. The check was watching the declaration, the repair moved the schema, and the two stopped being the same thing at the exact moment the repair worked. An exception that cannot go stale is a suppression list with a stated ground, and it would have outlived its defect silently.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `278`, session **469**, dispatched as `claude/the-rename-adr-272-specified`. **MIGRATION `0075` IS TAKEN**, [`0075_calibration_observed_on.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0075_calibration_observed_on.sql).

- **NOT THE MONEY PATH, AND THE GROUND IS STATED RATHER THAN ASSUMED.** No value moves, no constraint or grant changes, no row is written, and the column keeps its type, its `NOT NULL`, its ordinal position, its comment and every byte it holds. `0045` was read as money path because it decides what a publish is allowed to be; this file does not touch that decision. `E2` is not claimed and not discharged.

- **`RI-26`'s LAST `_at` EXCEPTION CAME OUT IN THE COMMIT THAT LANDED THE REPAIR**, as the row required, and the reader learned to fold renames in the same commit because without that the removal is a false alarm rather than a repair. Section 4.

### 2. THE RULING

> **1. `simulation_runs.calibration_observed_at` IS RENAMED TO `calibration_observed_on` BY A NEW MIGRATION**, [`0075`](../../packages/db/migrations/0075_calibration_observed_on.sql), carrying `ALTER TABLE simulation_runs RENAME COLUMN`. `0045` is not edited, its header stays exactly as written, and the three sentences in it that argue the `date` type under the old name stay too: **a merged migration records what it did and is not a description of the schema today** (constitution E2).
>
> **2. `_on` AND NEVER `_day`**, on [ADR-272](ADR-272.md) section 5's reasoning taken unchanged. [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) section 1 makes `*_day` an EXCHANGE trading day and a calibration vendor publishes on no exchange calendar, so `_day` would trade one false assertion for a second one.
>
> **3. `RI-26`'s `AT_SUFFIX_EXCEPTIONS` IS EMPTY AS OF THIS COMMIT.** The entry did not expire, it was retired by the repair, and the two land together because either one alone is a defect: the exception alone excuses a defect that no longer exists, and the removal alone reports a repaired tree as broken.
>
> **4. AND THIS IS THE CLAUSE THAT IS NOT ABOUT ONE COLUMN. A DDL READER'S POPULATION IS THE SCHEMA THE MIGRATION SET INSTALLS, NOT THE UNION OF ITS `CREATE TABLE` BODIES.** Those were the same answer on every tree this repository has ever had, because it held no `RENAME COLUMN` and no `DROP COLUMN`. E2 makes the divergence permanent from here: a superseded declaration stands forever in a file nobody may edit. **A reader that sums declarations is now stating something false, and the fact that it was true until today is exactly why nobody was checking.**
>
> **5. A `DROP COLUMN` HAS NO FOLD AND MUST THROW RATHER THAN BE IGNORED.** There are none in the estate. `RI-26` and the `ADR-216` fold each refuse one loudly, so the first one to land arrives as a ruling to be made rather than as a reader quietly asserting about a column that is gone.

### 10. Where this entry is most likely wrong

**CLAUSE 4 IS A RULE INVENTED FROM ONE STATEMENT.** One `RENAME COLUMN` landed and this entry generalised from it to every DDL reader in the repository. The generalisation is sound as a statement about what a reader's population IS, and it is unproven as a claim about how much of this tree gets it wrong: four readers were found because they turned red, and a reader that is wrong and quiet was not looked for. Section 5 owes the sweep and this entry did not take it.

**THE `CI-06m` WORKAROUND IS THE CLAUSE TO ATTACK HARDEST.** The design record now carries both column names in one cell so that a gate reading declarations still resolves the old one. That is true content and it is also, read unkindly, a document shaped around a reader's defect. The defence is that the alternative was editing a file in no part of the fence, and that the cell states its own reason in the open where the next session will read it. **A reviewer who thinks the fence should have been widened for one function should say so**, because that is the disagreement, not whether the fold is correct.

**THE THREE TEST-FILE EDITS MAY BE ONE TOO MANY.** Section 4 argues each was forced by a fenced act. The weakest of the three is [`ledger-posting-authority.test.ts`](../../apps/api/test/ledger-posting-authority.test.ts), where a one-word edit to `0075_calibration_observed_on.sql` would also have gone green and would have handed the identical breakage to the next migration author. This entry rewrote the assertion instead, which is a larger diff and a smaller claim, and a reviewer may reasonably prefer that a stale assertion belonging to ADR-270 be repaired by ADR-270's own author.

**AND THE RENAME ITSELF IS THE PART LEAST LIKELY TO BE WRONG**, which is worth saying plainly: it was specified by a prior entry, the target name was ruled there against a named alternative, it installs, sixteen probe assertions run against it twice over, and no dependency in the catalogue names the old column.

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.** **Clause 4 is the one to read hardest**, because it is a rule about every DDL reader in the repository derived from a single statement landing, and section 10 says where it is thin. **The fence judgement in section 4 is a judgement**, made against three files the row does not name, on [ADR-272](ADR-272.md) section 7's precedent, and a reviewer who reads the fence more narrowly should say so: the three test edits are separable from the repair and the repair stands without them. **And the sentence to read most carefully is in section 5**, where this entry declines to fix `CI-06m`'s identical blindness and works around it in a document instead.
