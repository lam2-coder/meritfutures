## ADR-273: the coverage check lives in the caller and that is safe exactly where a TYPE makes forgetting a compile error, so the split is ruled rather than moved and the census is closed by `RI-27`  (2026-08-30, status: proposed)

**Row `273` asked whether a door that consults no coverage is right only if every caller remembers, and said in terms that a ruling the current design is correct and needs only a check would be a complete outcome. IT IS THAT OUTCOME, AND THE REASON IS SHARPER THAN THE QUESTION.** The split is safe in `apps/api` and it is safe **for a reason nobody wrote down**: `lastClosedDay` is module-private with exactly one caller, and that caller returns a discriminated union whose anchored day is unreachable without narrowing past an `uncovered` arm. **A consumer that forgets the coverage check does not compile.** That is a fence, not a memory, and it is the property this entry names and enforces.

**AND THE ROW'S PREMISE IS A SPLICE OF TWO SEPARATE SENTENCES ABOUT TWO SEPARATE FUNCTIONS, WHICH MATTERS BECAUSE THE HALVES ANSWER DIFFERENTLY.** The row read [`scoped-db.ts:3046-3050`](../../packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts) as saying that `readLastClosedTradingDay` consults no coverage and that its caller `anchorCalendar` then checks `trading_calendar_loads`. **The comment says those of two different functions.** `readLastClosedTradingDay` lives in `apps/worker/src/batch/adapter.ts`; `anchorCalendar` is `lastClosedDay`'s caller in `apps/api/src/admin-source/liability.ts` and has never called the worker's fold. So the question "is that caller the only caller" has **two answers**, and only one of them is reassuring.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `273`, session **464**, branch `claude/the-coverage-check-lives-in-the-caller`. **NO MIGRATION NUMBER IS TAKEN OR RESERVED.** The highest on disk is `0074`, read at the moment of branching, `0075` is still next, and `git diff origin/main -- packages/db/migrations` is empty on this branch. Sessions 463 and 465 own the migrations and the runbooks and neither is touched here.

- **MONEY PATH. An unknown trading day answered as a known one is a wrong payout basis**, and the day this fold returns SELECTS the `rule_states` row every gate is read from. The [**`E2`**](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) read is **OWED**. Ships `proposed` with an **UNSIGNED** approval line. **Merging is not signing.** It inherits the `E2` debt on [ADR-268](ADR-268.md), which built the door this row asks about, and on [ADR-271](ADR-271.md), which repaired the driver defect that entry found.

- **`RI-27` IS MINTED IN THIS COMMIT**, taking the next free number, with its row in the register in the same commit. **Section 7 records the two seeds that proved its first version wrong and the third that the full suite found after the seeding was done.**

- **A DISPATCH PROMPT IS NOT A SOURCE.** Every line row `273` supplied was re-derived at this commit. **One did not hold** and it is the splice above.

### 2. THE RULING

> **1. THE SPLIT IS SAFE WHERE THE CALLER'S RETURN TYPE MAKES FORGETTING A COMPILE ERROR, AND NOWHERE ELSE.** A fold may leave the coverage read to its caller only when the caller hands back a value from which the day is UNREACHABLE without discriminating on a coverage verdict. `anchorCalendar` does: `CalendarAnchor` declares `anchorDay` on the `anchored` arm alone, and all three walks narrow. **The rule is about the TYPE and not about the count of callers**, because a count is true on the day it is measured and a union is true on the day somebody adds the fourth walk.
>
> **2. THE COVERAGE READ IS NOT MOVED INTO `lastClosedDay`, AND THE REASON IS NOT [ADR-268](ADR-268.md)'s.** That entry's grounds are about a CATALOGUE admission and this is a private function inside one module, so they do not reach. The reason here is narrower and is measured: `anchorCalendar` reads both tables ONCE and shares one parse across three walks, so moving the coverage read one frame down would either re-read `trading_calendar_loads` per walk or pass the intervals in, **which is the same split with more steps and one more place to get it wrong.** Section 4.
>
> **3. THE CATALOGUE ADMISSION AND THE TWO-TRANSACTION CROSSING STAY REFUSED, RE-DERIVED AT SOURCE RATHER THAN CARRIED.** `CATALOG_TABLE_KEYS` is unchanged at five members, neither calendar table is among them, `scoped-db.ts`'s door still reads both tables itself, and nothing in this diff touches `scope.ts` or any key set. Section 5.
>
> **4. THE SECOND TRAP HAS A MEASURED ANSWER AND IT IS THAT NO SUCH CALLER EXISTS, WHICH IS A MEASUREMENT AND NOT A LICENCE.** Row `273` required the cost of a total refusal to be established before making it total. **Every caller of every last-closed-day fold in this tree needs coverage**, and the reader that legitimately needs none, `databaseEconomicCalendar`, **selects no day and calls no fold**. That is not an accident: SELECTING A DAY is what creates the need. Section 6.
>
> **5. THE ONE PLACE NOTHING HOLDS THE SPLIT IS `apps/worker`, AND IT IS REGISTERED RATHER THAN REPAIRED.** `readLastClosedTradingDay` is EXPORTED, consults no coverage, and its caller `resolveTradingDay` consults none either: `calendarCarriesDay` asks a coverage-shaped question of `trading_calendar`, **which is the wrong table.** `apps/worker` is outside this row's fence, so this is finding 1 and not a diff.
>
> **6. `RI-27` CLOSES THE CENSUS, BECAUSE A RULING ABOUT THREE FOLDS DOES NOT SURVIVE A FOURTH LANDING IN A PACKAGE NOBODY WAS LOOKING AT.** Every source file whose CODE handles a session close is registered with its coverage disposition, and each disposition is asked of the file rather than of the register. Section 7.
>
> **7. NO DOOR CHANGED, NO KEY SET MOVED, NO PORT WAS WIRED, NO MIGRATION WAS WRITTEN AND NO GATE WAS WEAKENED TO PASS IT.** The diff is one new suite, one new invariant, this entry and the registers. `scoped-db.ts` is READ and not written.

### 4. Ruling 1, and what actually holds the `apps/api` split

**THE ROW'S LITERAL QUESTION, ANSWERED BY COUNTING RATHER THAN BY READING.** In `liability.ts`, with comments stripped:

| Asked | Measured |
| --- | --- |
| Is `lastClosedDay` exported? | **NO.** No `export` before the declaration and no re-export clause names it |
| How many call sites? | **ONE.** Two occurrences of `lastClosedDay(` in code: the declaration and one call, and the call falls between `anchorCalendar`'s opening line and the next declaration in the file |
| How many callers does `anchorCalendar` have? | **THREE**, and there are **THREE** `anchor.kind === 'uncovered'` guards. The counts are asserted equal rather than each pinned, so a fourth walk without its guard moves one and not the other |
| Does anything ENFORCE it? | **YES, AND IT IS NOT THE COUNT.** `CalendarAnchor` is a discriminated union carrying `anchorDay` on the `anchored` arm ALONE. Reading the day without narrowing is a type error |

**SO THE ANSWER TO "IS THAT CALLER THE ONLY CALLER" IS YES, AND IT IS THE LESS INTERESTING HALF.** A count is a fact about today. **What makes the split safe next month is that `anchorCalendar`'s three consumers cannot skip the check even if they want to**, because the value they are handed does not carry a day until they have looked at `kind`. `readTradingHorizon`, `readTradingLookback` and `readCalendarSlice` each narrow, and each propagates the `uncovered` verdict outward: `payout-velocity.ts` and `eligible-next-7d.ts` both discriminate again at their own layer.

**WHERE THE FENCE IS THINNEST, STATED RATHER THAN HIDDEN.** The union protects `anchorCalendar`'s CONSUMERS. It does not protect `lastClosedDay` from a second caller **inside its own 2,000-line module**: such a caller compiles, receives a `string | null`, and nothing raises. That is the one place the row's fear is live in `apps/api`, and it is why the one-caller property is asserted mechanically in section 8 rather than left as a count in this prose.

**AND RULING 2'S REASON IS MEASURED RATHER THAN INHERITED.** [ADR-268](ADR-268.md)'s grounds are about a CATALOGUE key, which grants a whole table to every holder of a scoped transaction forever; a private helper inside one module grants nothing to anybody. So the door-change question has to be re-asked here on its own facts, and the answer is `anchorCalendar`'s own stated reason one scale down: it reads both tables ONCE, parses the calendar ONCE, and hands one anchor to three walks. **Moving the coverage read into `lastClosedDay` would put the loads read inside a function that receives already-parsed rows**, so either the intervals become a fourth parameter (the same split, with the caller now responsible for passing what it used to be responsible for checking) or the function opens a second read per walk. **Neither is a fence and one is slower.**

### 6. The second trap: what a total refusal would cost, measured before it was refused

**THE ROW REQUIRED THIS BEFORE ANY DOOR COULD BE MADE TOTAL: "establish what the refusal costs the callers that legitimately do not need coverage, if any, before making it total."**

**THE SET IS EMPTY AMONG CALLERS OF A FOLD, AND IT IS NOT EMPTY IN THE TREE, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS THE RULE.**

| Reader | Needs coverage? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `readTradingHorizon` | **YES.** It walks forward and stops at `covering.to`; coverage is the walk's own bound | Selects days |
| `readTradingLookback` | **YES**, and most acutely: [ADR-201](ADR-201.md) ruling 3 scales a DENOMINATOR from the window, and a denominator short by days nobody loaded is a pager firing on an ordinary week | Selects days |
| `readCalendarSlice` | **YES.** The covered interval IS the slice's declared coverage | Selects days |
| `resolveTradingDay` (worker) | **YES, AND IT DOES NOT HAVE IT.** It folds a day and then WRITES `rule_states` rows stamped with it | Selects a day |
| `databaseEconomicCalendar` ([`routes/economic-calendar.ts`](../../apps/api/src/routes/economic-calendar.ts)) | **NO, LEGITIMATELY** | **AND IT CALLS NO FOLD.** It counts sessions still ahead for a freshness badge and names no session close at all, which is why it is not among section 3's seven files |

**SO THE COST OF A TOTAL REFUSAL IS ZERO TODAY, AND THAT IS THE WEAKER OF THE TWO REASONS TO BE CALM ABOUT IT.** The structural reason is that the exempt reader is exempt **because it selects no day**, and a fold that selects no day is not a fold. [ADR-268](ADR-268.md) section 5 drew exactly this line for the same table one door over: *"the same table on the same door answering a different question is not the same read."* **This entry generalises it: coverage is owed by the act of returning a DAY, and by nothing else.**

**WHAT THAT DOES NOT LICENSE.** It does not license making `lastClosedDay` total, for ruling 2's reason, and it does not license making the scoped door looser: [ADR-268](ADR-268.md) section 6's adjacent-load refusal still costs a 503 at a load boundary and that judgement is untouched here.

### 10. Where this entry is most likely wrong

**RULING 1 IS A RULE ABOUT TYPES AND THIS REPOSITORY HAS NO MECHANICAL CHECK FOR IT.** `RI-27` asks whether coverage is READ, not whether the value handed out makes skipping it impossible, and the union's shape is pinned by a source-parsing case in one suite over one file. **A fifth fold could satisfy `RI-27` completely while handing its caller a bare `string | null`**, which is precisely the shape finding 1 describes. The honest position is that ruling 1 is enforced where it was measured and asserted everywhere else.

**THE ONE-CALLER CASE PINS A NEIGHBOUR.** It bounds the call site between `anchorCalendar`'s opening line and `readTradingHorizon`'s declaration, so reordering the module turns it red without anything being wrong. That is the fail-closed direction and it is cheap to repair, but a reviewer who thinks a suite should not know its module's layout should say so.

**AND THE ASYMMETRY [ADR-268](ADR-268.md) SECTION 10 FLAGGED IS NOW WIDER RATHER THAN NARROWER.** That entry worried that two answers to `R-06` in one estate is the condition its own finding 2 exists to end, and asked whether it should have stopped and rowed the merge. **There are four.** This entry did not merge them either, and its defence is that a census that turns red is a better custodian of four statements than a prose sentence is of two. **A reviewer may hold that the fourth statement is the point at which the merge stops being deferrable, and that view is not answered here.**
