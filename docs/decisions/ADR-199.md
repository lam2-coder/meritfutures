## ADR-199: `reserve_coverage_snapshots` is `firm` and registering it is the whole of what `packages/db` owed `readLiability`, because the other three figures are DERIVABLE and `0062` returns unspent  (2026-08-28, status: proposed)

**[Session 363](../sessions/2026-08-28-session-363.md) measured `readLiability` as unwritable in four parts and this entry clears three of them, two by building and one by ruling.** `LiabilityResponse` projects six groups. `reserve` was a COMPILE ERROR rather than a design gap: [`0049:154`](../../packages/db/migrations/0049_reserve_coverage_snapshots.sql) creates `reserve_coverage_snapshots` and no file of `packages/db/src/` registered it, so a `Tx` naming it was `TS2322` against `TABLE_KEYS`. **It is registered here, `firm`, on the DDL rather than on the shape of its name.** And `per_plan[].cusum`, `integrations.batch` and `eligible_next_7d` were read as needing columns no migration declares. **The census is re-derived and confirmed -- 58 migrations, 735 distinct column names, none of the three among them -- and the INFERENCE from it is refused: all three are derivable from columns that already exist, so the absence is correct rather than owed.** `0062` is therefore **RETURNED TO THE POOL UNSPENT**, which is the eighth time.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `199`, session **372**, dispatched as `claude/reserve-coverage-registration`. Migration `0062` was reserved **CONDITIONALLY** in the same file and its row is amended in place under [ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3.

- **Context:** it runs after [ADR-191](ADR-191.md), which is the precedent for what a registration costs and the entry that made `events` readable, which turns out to be load-bearing for section 5; after [ADR-167](ADR-167.md), which already ruled the CUSUM recomputed and returned its own conditional number; after [ADR-092](ADR-092.md), whose rule that a table is registered by the FIRST SESSION THAT NEEDS IT is why this table waited; and after [ADR-188](ADR-188.md), whose clause 5 -- no field is added for a figure no column produces -- is the rule this entry has to distinguish from *no field is added for a figure no column STORES*.

- **MONEY PATH BY SUBJECT AND NOT BY FILE, AND NO `E2` READ IS OWED.** No migration is written, no migration is edited, no transaction is opened, no ledger account is named, no `SystemReason` and no `SqlExecutorReason` member is added, no `pg` is imported, and no cast past a key type is written. Every merged migration, `0049` included, is byte for byte unchanged.

- **THE ENTRY SHIPS `proposed` WITH AN UNSIGNED APPROVAL LINE.** What is **derived** is the class (section 2), the census (section 3), and the three derivations (sections 4 to 6), every one read at its own primary source. What is **judged** is clause 6 -- that a derivable figure is not owed a column -- and section 9's answer about whether one migration should carry `withdrawals_in_flight_cents` too. Its ONE foreign key is a COMPOSITE edge to `treasury_balances(account_code, as_of)`, so a `derived` rule is not expressible in this vocabulary at all, would multiply rows if it were narrowed to one column, and would terminate at a `firm` table in any case. Section 2.

> **2. REGISTERING IT MAKES IT READABLE AND NOTHING ELSE.** [M12](../plans/M12-transparency-platform.md)'s `AS-M12-04` rules the coverage ratio unpublishable, because a falling RCR is a bank-run mechanic. >
> **3. `per_plan[].cusum` NEEDS NO COLUMN AND ALREADY HAD A RULING SAYING SO.** [ADR-167](ADR-167.md) clause 1 takes reading 3: `S_t` is folded at read time and *"no column, table or row anywhere holds it"*. Section 4. Its remaining blocker is `DEP-M6-05`'s CALIBRATION, which clause 5 of that entry says is rendered ABSENT rather than manufactured, and a calibration is not a column.
>
> **4. `integrations.batch` NEEDS NO COLUMN, BECAUSE THE BATCH ALREADY REPORTS THROUGH AN EVENT.** [EVENTS section 5](../architecture/EVENTS.md) declares `batch.started` / `batch.completed` with payload `{ run_id, trading_day, accounts_total, accounts_done, duration_ms }`, and `events` has been a `TableKey` since [ADR-191](ADR-191.md). `last_success_at` is the `occurred_at` of the latest `batch.completed` row and `last_duration_ms` is that row's `payload->>'duration_ms'`. Section 5.
>
> **5. `eligible_next_7d` NEEDS NO COLUMN, BECAUSE EVERY INPUT OF THE FOLD IS LANDED.** `{ total_cents, account_count, by_day[] }` is a projection over `rule_states`, `accounts`, `plan_versions.rules` and `trading_calendar`, all of which exist. ### 2. THE CLASS, ARGUED ON THE PREDICATE

**The question `scope.ts` partitions is HOW DOES A ROW REACH AN IDENTITY?** Read against `0049`'s eight columns, taken from `pg_attribute` on a database installed from `0001` forward rather than from the file:

| Column | Type | Reaches an identity? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `id` | `bigint GENERATED ALWAYS AS IDENTITY` | surrogate key |
| `as_of` | `timestamptz NOT NULL` | an instant |
| `reserve_cents` | `bigint NOT NULL` | the rail's balance |
| `treasury_account_code` | `text NOT NULL` | half of the anchor edge |
| `treasury_as_of` | `timestamptz NOT NULL` | the other half |
| `cvar99_cents` | `bigint NOT NULL` | the floor |
| `rcr_bp` | `integer GENERATED ... STORED`, nullable | the ratio |
| `created_at` | `timestamptz NOT NULL` | a clock |

**Zero columns are declared `REFERENCES identities(id)` and zero are declared against `accounts(id)`.** `pg_constraint` reports exactly one foreign key on the table.

| Member | Refused because |
| --- | --- |
| `root` | `identities`' alone, by that rule's own `column: 'id'` |
| `owned` | needs an identity column ON THE ROW. There is none, and the suite's `every owned rule names a column the DDL declares REFERENCES identities(id)` is what says so mechanically |
| `pair` | needs TWO identity columns. There are zero |
| `either` | needs ONE identity column beside one path to another row that carries one. The first leg does not exist |
| `derived` | **the only member with a candidate, and it fails three separate ways.** Section 2.1 |
| `firm` | **TAKEN.** No path, and the suite's `no firm table carries a column referencing identities` reads the same fact from the migration text |

#### 2.1 `derived` is the available mistake, and it is refused three times

**FIRST, IT CANNOT BE WRITTEN.** `reserve_coverage_snapshots_anchor_fk` is `FOREIGN KEY (treasury_account_code, treasury_as_of) REFERENCES treasury_balances (account_code, as_of)`. `DerivedRule` names ONE `localColumn` against ONE `foreignColumn`. A two-column edge has no expression in this vocabulary -- which is `correlation_groups`' `uuid[]` objection arriving from the other direction, and its `why` already says the reason it MUST NOT be traversed is stronger than the reason it cannot.

**SECOND, THE ONE-COLUMN VERSION MULTIPLIES ROWS.** `account_code` alone is half of `treasury_balances`' primary key, one row per attestation instant. A `hop` through it returns this snapshot once per attestation ever recorded against the account, which is `ledger_transactions`' arithmetic objection on a table where no join is needed at all. Seeded and watched: the suite goes red on `every hop names the via table primary key, and every semi-join does not`.

**THIRD, THE CHAIN TERMINATES NOWHERE.** `treasury_balances` is itself `firm`, and a derivation chain terminates at `owned` or at `root`. Seeded and watched: `every derivation chain ends at an identity, so none of them ends at a firm table` goes red.

**AND THE STRONGER REASON IS NOT MECHANICAL AND IS RECORDED ANYWAY.** A per-identity slice of a firm-wide coverage RATIO is not a smaller version of it: the denominator is one CVaR99 computed over the whole book, and dividing one person's share by it produces a number nothing in the corpus defines. That is `liability_snapshots`' own `why` arriving on a second surface, and it is the sentence that would still refuse this table if somebody added an identity column to it tomorrow.

#### 2.2 What the registration touched, and what it did NOT

[ADR-191](ADR-191.md)'s registration changed `scope.ts` alone. **THIS ONE COULD NOT, AND THE DISPATCH'S PREMISE THAT IT COULD IS SECTION 11 FINDING 1.** `TABLES` is built from `schema.ts`'s declarations, and `schema.ts` had no `reserveCoverageSnapshots` to import: `events` was already transcribed when `ADR-191` registered it, and this table was not. So the registration is two files and not one: the drizzle transcription and the rule. **[`packages/db/src/index.ts`](../../packages/db/src/index.ts) and [`scoped-db.ts`](../../packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts) are session 367's and were not opened**, and nothing here needed them.

### 4. `per_plan[].cusum`: ruled RECOMPUTED a session before this one

[ADR-167](ADR-167.md) is session 281's and it took exactly this question for exactly this field. Its ruling clause 1: *"`S_t` is computed at read time, by folding the pass-rate series in `evaluated_on` order with the floor at zero, and no column, table or row anywhere holds it."* Its clause 6: *"`0051` is not spent."*

**So the absence the census finds is a RULING HOLDING and not a gap.** Its finding 8 supplies the series -- `accounts.plan_version_id`, `opened_on`, `funded_on`, `closed_on`, `phase`, plus `account_status_history`'s transitions, plus `plan_versions.plan_id` -- and states that *"a per-plan-per-day pass rate is derivable from landed columns and nothing else is needed to fold it"*.

**WHAT STILL BLOCKS THE FIELD IS NOT A COLUMN.** `mu_0` and `sigma` are `DEP-M6-05`'s, in Wave 4, and `FM-M6-07` reads that an uncalibrated CUSUM is *"either constant alarms or none, which is the same as no chart"*. ADR-167 clause 5 rules that `P7-k` renders the field ABSENT until then. **A migration taken for this figure would put a column behind a panel this repository already, correctly, refuses to draw** -- which is that entry's ground (c), and taking `0062` for it would be overturning a ruling by writing DDL rather than by writing an entry.

### 5. `integrations.batch`: the two figures are an EVENT, and the table is registered

**This is the derivation the dispatch did not anticipate and it is the strongest of the three, because it needs no arithmetic at all.**

[EVENTS section 5](../architecture/EVENTS.md) declares, in the approved catalogue:

> `batch.started` / `batch.completed` | Worker | `{ run_id, trading_day, accounts_total, accounts_done, duration_ms }` | ALERT, BI

and beside it `batch.failed | Worker | { run_id, stage, account_cursor, error }`.

**The operational corpus already reads the batch's success off that event rather than off a row.** [RB-01](../ops/runbooks/RB-01-nightly-batch-failure.md): *"Trigger. `batch.failed`, or the dead-man switch firing because `batch.completed` did not arrive by 06:00 CT."* The dead man watches the EVENT.

`events` carries `event_name text NOT NULL`, `occurred_at timestamptz NOT NULL` and `payload jsonb NOT NULL` ([`0017`](../../packages/db/migrations/0017_events_and_audit.sql)), and is a `TableKey` as of [ADR-191](ADR-191.md). So:

| Field | Source |
| --- | --- |
| `batch.last_success_at` | `occurred_at` of the latest `events` row with `event_name = 'batch.completed'` |
| `batch.last_duration_ms` | that row's `payload->>'duration_ms'` |

**AND A COLUMN WOULD BE WORSE THAN NO COLUMN, ON `0049`'s OWN ARGUMENT.** Its founder-read item 1 refuses *"a ratio stored beside its own numerator"* because the two can disagree in one row. A `batch_runs.last_success_at` beside a `batch.completed` event is that hazard with two writers instead of one column, and there is no `GENERATED` remedy available across tables.

**THE COUNTERFACTUAL DERIVATION IS NAMED AND REFUSED, so the next session does not reach for it.** `max(rule_states.computed_at)` for a trading day looks like a batch clock and is not one: [OVERVIEW section 5.2](../architecture/OVERVIEW.md) says *"any stage failing leaves prior stages committed per account and the batch resumable at the account boundary, so a crash at account 2,341 of 5,000 resumes without double-applying a day"*. A fold over per-account clocks therefore reports a SUCCESS for a run that crashed, which is `INV-M6-04`'s *"a figure whose freshness is unstated is a figure that will eventually be quoted stale in a decision that mattered"* with the freshness stated and wrong. **The event is the only thing in this schema that distinguishes a completed run from an interrupted one, and it exists.**

**WHAT IS NOT RULED HERE.** Whether the adapter reads that row by ordering and limit, or by a filter this accessor does not offer, is [`scoped-db.ts`](../../packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts)'s question and [ADR-157](ADR-157.md)'s ruling, and it is IDENTICAL under a stored column and under this derivation. It is not a cost that separates them and it is not resolved here.

### 6. `eligible_next_7d`: a fold whose every input is landed

**The response's group is `{ total_cents, account_count, by_day: [{ trading_day, cents, accounts }] }`.** It is a forward-looking projection: which accounts clear their payout gates on each of the next seven trading days, and for how much.

**THE CORPUS ALREADY DEFINES AN ADJACENT FIGURE OVER THE SAME HORIZON AND STORES IT.** [EC-074](../edge-cases/EC-074.md): bounded near-term liability is *"the sum of `min(withdrawable, cap_for_next_ordinal)` over accounts eligible now or inside 7 trading days"*, and that is `liability_snapshots.bounded_near_term_cents`, a landed column. **So a producer of `liability_snapshots` must already compute the per-account "eligible inside 7 trading days" predicate in order to write a column that exists.** The `by_day` breakdown is a refinement of a computation the schema already commits to, not a new one.

The inputs, each read at its migration:

| Input | Where |
| --- | --- |
| `withdrawable_cents`, `engine_eligible`, `engine_gates`, `payout_anchor_day`, `cadence_anchor_day`, `payouts_settled_count`, `traded_days_count`, `win_days_count`, `trading_day` | `rule_states` ([`0015`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql)) |
| `plan_version_id`, `phase` | `accounts` ([`0007`](../../packages/db/migrations/0007_accounts.sql)) |
| `rules jsonb`, holding `cadence_gap_trading_days` and the ladder caps | `plan_versions` ([`0004`](../../packages/db/migrations/0004_catalog.sql)) |
| the next seven trading days, with `is_holiday` and `halted` | `trading_calendar` ([`0004`](../../packages/db/migrations/0004_catalog.sql)) |

**`SD-M6-01`'s TWO ABSENT COLUMNS ARE A DIFFERENT FIGURE AND CONFLATING THEM IS WHAT MADE THIS LOOK BLOCKED.** [M06:69](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) asks `liability_snapshots` for `eligible_next_7d_identity_max_cents` and `eligible_next_7d_identity_max_id`; [M06:94](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md)'s `P-M6-03` is *"Account-level total, **plus the largest single identity's share**"*. **The largest single identity's share is on NO field of `LiabilityResponse` and no field of `EligibleForecastResponse`** -- [ADR-188](ADR-188.md) clause 5 refused it and finding 9 recorded why. So the two absent columns bear on the identity maximum, which stays unproducible and unclaimed, and say nothing about the group this response actually projects.

**ONE INPUT IS NOT YET A `TableKey` AND IT IS NAMED RATHER THAN TAKEN.** `trading_calendar` is unregistered; `trading_calendar_loads` and `trading_calendar_revisions` are registered and are not it. That is an [ADR-092](ADR-092.md) registration for the session that needs it -- it is `firm` by inspection, a published exchange calendar being the same calendar for everybody -- and it is **not a migration**. This entry does not take it, on that entry's own rule: the session that NEEDS it registers it, and this one does not read it.

### 12. THE FENCE, AND WHERE IT WAS WIDENED

**The registration made an `apps/api` test go RED, by design, and green was the stop condition.** [`admin-source-liability.test.ts`](../../apps/api/test/admin-source-liability.test.ts)'s blocker-1 case asserted `expect(TABLE_KEYS).not.toContain('reserveCoverageSnapshots')` under a comment that reads *"THE CLEARING CONDITION. The three sibling cases stayed green and their COMMENTS became false -- they claimed the three figures *"have nothing behind them"* -- so they are repointed at sections 4 to 6 and each gains a positive assertion naming the derivation's own columns.
