## ADR-188: the liability response is one snapshot row column for column, the shared name is KEPT, and the total is the reader's  (2026-08-28, status: proposed)

**[`W6-d`](../plans/WAVE-06-admin-console-transport.md) landed the liability home and then found that the response contracted to feed it cannot produce its input. The finding is real and its statement was not: the response did not carry `open_liability_cents` and nothing else, it carried three of [`liability_snapshots`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql)' figures and four of them were missing.** This entry rules the four in, rules that `open_liability_cents` KEEPS the name it shares with the panel it is one component of, rules that `INV-M6-11`'s *"separately as well as summed"* is satisfied by the two components with NO total on the wire, and rules that `reserve` carries its own `as_of` and its anchor because it is a different table on a different clock. **No migration number is taken and none is needed. No code is edited, and the reason is measured rather than asserted: the type this ruling changes cannot move alone.**

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `188`, session **345**, dispatched as `claude/liability-response-shape`. **The reservation's premise is confirmed in substance and corrected in one figure**, and the row is amended in place under [ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3 rather than answered with a second row.

- **NOT THE MONEY PATH BY FILE AND ARGUABLY ON IT BY CONTENT.** This entry opens no transaction, posts nothing and claims no migration number. What it specifies is the shape of the numbers an operator reads during an incident, and `AS-M6-04` is the registered scenario for getting exactly that wrong. The [`E2`](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) read is owed on the code that transcribes these rows and is **not** discharged here, because that code is not written here.

- **THE ENTRY SHIPS `proposed` WITH AN UNSIGNED APPROVAL LINE.** What is DERIVED is the field list, its completeness rule, and the fact that no landed column can produce `P-M6-03`'s identity maximum. What is JUDGED is clause 2, keeping a name that means one thing to the schema and another to a panel, and clause 3, declining to send a total that a client would otherwise be free to compute wrongly. Section 11 is that question.

- **A DISPATCH PROMPT IS NOT A SOURCE.** Every claim below was read at its own file before it was relied on, and the two that came back different are findings 2 and 9.

### 2. THE RULING

> **1. THE TOP-LEVEL FIELDS OF `LiabilityResponse` ARE ONE `liability_snapshots` ROW, COLUMN FOR COLUMN.** It gains `wallet_balances_cents`, `bounded_near_term_cents`, `remaining_ladder_exposure_cents` and `absorbed_corrections_cents`. The completeness rule is the projection itself and it is checkable rather than enumerated: the only columns of that table the response does not carry are `id`, a surrogate key, and `computed_at`, which records when a batch ran rather than what any figure describes.
>
> **2. `open_liability_cents` KEEPS ITS NAME, AND NO FIELD ON THIS RESPONSE IS NAMED FOR A PANEL.** The wire speaks the schema's vocabulary in both directions. The ambiguity is closed by clause 1 putting the second component beside the first and by clause 3 refusing the panel's name to any field, not by renaming a column halfway across the boundary.
>
> **3. NO TOTAL IS SENT. `INV-M6-11`'s "separately as well as summed" is satisfied by the two components, and the sum belongs to the reader.** A total on the wire would be a third number, computed by a handler, that can disagree with the two beside it, and it would be the one field a client can render alone under the panel's name.
>
> **4. `reserve` CARRIES ITS OWN `as_of` AND ITS ANCHOR.** It gains `as_of`, `treasury_account_code`, `treasury_as_of` and `treasury_source`. `breaker_armed` is the one member that is not a column and it stays, because removing a field from a served shape is a break rather than a repair.
>
> **5. NO FIELD IS ADDED FOR A FIGURE NO COLUMN PRODUCES.** `P-M6-03`'s identity maximum is named by `SD-M6-01`, has landed in no migration, and gains nothing here.

**`GET /admin/liability` is the only endpoint this entry touches. No endpoint is added, none is removed, and no path changes.**

### 4. Clause 2, and what a rename actually costs on both sides

**The case FOR renaming is real and it is the reason this clause exists.** A field named `open_liability_cents` sitting on the same response as `wallet_balances_cents` invites a reader to render the first under the panel's name, and `INV-M6-11` would then be broken by a field name rather than by a decision. [`liability.ts:30`](../../apps/admin/src/liability.ts) says so in terms and its own field is `withdrawableAcrossFundedCents` for that reason.

**It is refused on four grounds, and the fourth is the one that is not obvious.**

| Ground | |
|---|---|
| **A renamed field disagrees with the column** | The schema keeps `open_liability_cents` and a merged migration is never edited ([`E2`](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md)). A reader tracing `withdrawable_across_funded_cents` back to `0009` finds no such column, which is finding 8's defect pointed the other way: one thing under two names instead of two things under one |
| **The response is served and copied** | The name is in the contract block, in [`admin-reads.ts:464`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-reads.ts), in [`api/types.ts:127`](../../apps/admin/src/api/types.ts), in `projectLiability` at [`:1093`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-reads.ts) and in two typed fixtures. A rename is a break in a published shape bought to reduce a risk clause 1 already reduces |
| **Clause 1 removes the affordance the name creates** | The panel's second component is now on the response. A client that renders `open_liability_cents` alone is not reading a name ambiguously; it is ignoring a field that is right beside it, which is a different and more visible error |
| **A rename would DELETE the console's one stated mapping site** | [`liability.ts:40`](../../apps/admin/src/liability.ts): *"the mapping from the column is stated once at the call site that builds the row"*. Kept as `open_liability_cents`, the adapter's line is a RENAME, which is a place a reader stops. Renamed on the wire, that line becomes `withdrawableAcrossFundedCents: response.withdrawable_across_funded_cents`, an identity assignment nobody reads twice, and the boundary where the column stops being the panel would no longer be marked anywhere |

**The adapter is [`P5-l`](../plans/P5-payouts-and-wallet.md)'s and is not written here.** The fourth ground is the constraint this entry hands it: the mapping from `open_liability_cents` to `withdrawableAcrossFundedCents` is a **documented rename at a boundary**, and a later slice that "simplifies" it by aligning the two names has removed the control rather than the ceremony.

### 5. Clause 3, and why a total the server sends and a total the reader computes are different designs

**Both are available and the corpus already contains both patterns on this one response**, which is why the choice is argued rather than assumed. `payout_velocity.alarm` is a verdict the server sends; `breaker_armed` is a verdict [`0049:114`](../../packages/db/migrations/0049_reserve_coverage_snapshots.sql) refuses to store and [`liability.ts`](../../apps/admin/src/liability.ts) computes at render time.

**The rule that separates them: a value is SENT when the server knows something the reader does not, and is COMPUTED by the reader when it is a pure function of fields already on the response.** `payout_velocity.alarm` reads a ratio threshold the reader has no source for. The open liability total is `open_liability_cents + wallet_balances_cents`, two fields of one row at one `as_of`, and there is nothing behind it a client cannot see.

**Three consequences follow and each is a reason.**

1. **A sent total is a third number that can disagree with the two beside it.** [`data-model/liability_snapshots.md`](../architecture/data-model/liability_snapshots.md) gives that as its third reason for a separate coverage table, *"a ratio stored beside its own numerator invites recomputation drift"*, and [`0049`](../../packages/db/migrations/0049_reserve_coverage_snapshots.sql) answered it with a `GENERATED` column so that *"a ratio the database computes cannot disagree with its own inputs"*. **There is no generated column for this sum.** A handler-computed total has exactly the drift the generated column exists to remove, on a response nobody can constrain.
2. **A sent total is the one field a client can render alone.** [`liability.ts`](../../apps/admin/src/liability.ts) refuses to offer one on purpose: *"there is no exported function that returns one of them ... a caller cannot reach for the convenient figure because there is no convenient figure to reach for"*. A supplier that puts the convenient figure back on the wire has undone that structurally, one layer up, where the console's own type cannot reach.
3. **`INV-M6-04` is satisfied without it.** Both components come from one row at one `as_of` and one source, so the sum inherits both. [`liability.ts:184`](../../apps/admin/src/liability.ts) already builds it that way, as a `Reading` carrying the definition, the as-of and the source, which is the form `INV-M6-04` actually requires and a bare number on the wire is not.

**What is given up is stated rather than hidden.** A client that computes the sum and omits the wallet term breaks `INV-M6-11` and nothing on the wire refuses it. That risk is real and it is smaller than the alternative: the same client, handed a total, renders it under the panel's name and never reads the components at all, which is the same invariant broken with the components present and unread. **Neither design is enforced by the payload, so the choice is which failure is visible**, and a missing addition in a reader is a diff a reviewer sees where a total quietly preferred to its own parts is not.

### 6. Clause 4, and the second table this response was already carrying badly

**`reserve` was a projection of `reserve_coverage_snapshots` with the two fields that make it readable left off.**

| Field | Column | What needs it |
|---|---|---|
| `as_of` | [`0049:159`](../../packages/db/migrations/0049_reserve_coverage_snapshots.sql) | `INV-M6-04` ([M06:48](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md)) and [M06:247](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md)'s *"Every field carries its own `as_of`"*. Dating it with the top-level `as_of` would put the liability snapshot's clock on the rail's figure, which is the second of the three reasons [`data-model/liability_snapshots.md`](../architecture/data-model/liability_snapshots.md) gives for the two tables being two: *"one row forces one `as_of` on two sources that do not move together"*. **A response that carried one `as_of` for both would re-collapse in the payload exactly what `ADR-128` separated in the schema** |
| `treasury_account_code` | [`0049:174`](../../packages/db/migrations/0049_reserve_coverage_snapshots.sql) | The anchor `RESERVE-C1` asserts the numerator equals. `P-M6-07`'s panel names which row it read |

| `treasury_source` | [`0009:147`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql), `treasury_balances.source` | `P-M6-07` ([M06:97](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md)) requires *"attestation staleness shown when the balance is a manual attestation"*. **Nothing else on this response answers which of the two it is**, and a panel that cannot tell a provider reading from a human statement cannot satisfy that clause at all |

### 9. What this entry does NOT decide

- **Where the numbers come from.** No producer writes `liability_snapshots` or `reserve_coverage_snapshots` in this tree, and `0049`'s own header says *"no producer, no rows"*. This entry rules the shape of a response, not the read that fills it.
- **How the read is expressed.** [ADR-157](ADR-157.md) section 5 REFUSES a scalar aggregate on the accessor and clause 1 admits a range term and `IS NULL` on the read path only. **Nothing here needs an aggregate**: every figure is a stored column of one row. What the producing slice still owes an argument for is selecting the LATEST row, which is neither an aggregate nor a term.
- **`rcr_bp` crossing the boundary as a `number`.** The wire is JSON and [`liability.ts`](../../apps/admin/src/liability.ts) types it `bigint` on purpose. That conversion is the adapter's and is named here so it is not discovered.
- **Whether `breaker_armed` should be on the response at all.** Registered as item 3, not ruled: removing a served field is a break and the divergence is not one this entry created.
- **Anything about `eligible_next_7d`, `payout_velocity`, `per_plan` or `integrations`.** Other tables feed them and no claim here reaches them.

### 11. Alternatives considered

- **Rename `open_liability_cents` on the wire.** Refused on section 4's four grounds. The fourth is decisive on its own: it would delete the boundary rename that is currently the only marked place where the column stops being the panel.
- **Send a computed `open_liability_total_cents`.** Refused on section 5. It is a handler-computed third number with no generated column behind it and it is the one field a client can render alone under the panel's name.
- **Send the total INSTEAD of `open_liability_cents`, with the wallet term folded in.** Refused hardest of the three: `P-M6-01` requires the components *"shown separately as well as summed"* because *"they behave differently"*, and it is the shape `EC-095` and `AS-M6-04` are both about. It also makes the response no longer a projection of a row, which is clause 1's whole check.
- **Put the coverage fields on a second endpoint.** Refused: it adds a `METHOD /path`, which moves `RI-17`, needs its own reservation, and is a transport ruling this entry has no mandate for. `reserve` is already on this response and clause 4 completes it rather than moving it.
- **Add `eligible_next_7d.identity_max_cents` now, typed nullable.** Refused. It is [ADR-158](ADR-158.md) clause 6's `available_at` move, a contract row stating a value the tree cannot produce, and that entry's own approval block names it as one of the four things a founder read should weigh. Registered as item 6 with the migration it actually needs.
- **Edit the type in `admin-reads.ts` and the two suites too.** Refused: two of the three files are outside this fence. *"Never weaken a gate to pass it and never widen a fence to finish. They are the same move."*

### 12. Consequences

- **The console can be fed.** `LiabilityHomeInput`'s `snapshot`, `absorbedCorrectionsCents` and `reserveCoverage` all have suppliers on the wire after this ruling, which they did not before. What remains between the page and a number is a producer for the two tables and the adapter, both [`P5-l`](../plans/P5-payouts-and-wallet.md)'s.
- **`P-M6-01` becomes computable and `INV-M6-11` becomes checkable.** Before this entry the second term of the sum did not exist on the wire, so the invariant could not be satisfied by any client at all.
- **The completeness rule is now a sentence a reviewer can execute.** *"One `liability_snapshots` row, column for column, less `id` and `computed_at`."* A future column on that table is either on this response or has an argument written against it.
- **Three copies of one type still diverge silently.** Finding 14 is unrepaired and item 1 is how it gets worse: the ruled shape lands in the document today and in the code later, and nothing in this tree reds while they disagree. **The check that would close it compares the contract's own `type` block against the exported interface**, which is `RI-17`'s method applied to fields instead of paths, and it is a check rather than a ruling.
- **A merged migration is untouched.** `0009`, `0038` and `0049` are byte for byte unchanged and no migration number is taken.

### 13. Approval

**Approval line: UNSIGNED.**

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.** Four of the decisions here are judgements rather than derivations, and each fixes a public shape.

**Clause 2, keeping a name that means one thing to the schema and another to a panel**, is the one this entry is least able to settle alone. It is the exact shape `ADR-158` is the standing example of, and the argument for keeping it turns on a fourth ground that is a claim about how people read code: that a marked rename at a boundary is a control and an identity assignment is not. If that is wrong, the rename is right and it is cheaper today than after an adapter exists.

**Clause 3, declining to send the total**, chooses which failure is visible rather than eliminating one. The payload enforces neither design. A founder who weights "a client silently omits the wallet term" above "a client renders the total and ignores the components" should reverse it, and the reversal is a field addition rather than a rewrite.

**Clause 4 is the widest part of this ruling and it was not in the reservation.** The four `reserve` members are a second table's worth of fields added to an endpoint reserved for a different defect. They are derived from `0049`'s columns and from `INV-M6-04`, and they are still a widening this entry took rather than proposed.

**And clause 5 declines to write a field `M06` section 4 asked for by name.** `P-M6-03`'s identity maximum is the number `ADR-011`'s same-day top-up trigger reads. This entry leaves it off on the ground that no column produces it, which is right and which also means a plan obligation stays undischarged with only a register row pointing at it.

**What would make this safe to sign without a line-by-line read:** nothing here is enforced by a gate. The completeness rule is prose, the three copies are unbound, and section 10's green results prove only that nothing went red. **A check binding the contract's `LiabilityResponse` block to the exported interface field for field is what would make clause 1 load bearing before it is signed**, and it is one file in a fence nobody holds.
