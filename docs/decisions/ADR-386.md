### 6. THE RULING ON LINE-COUNT-NEUTRAL EDITING: AN EXPLICIT NON-RULE, AND THE PROPERTY THAT REPLACES IT

**IT IS NOT MADE A RULE, AND THE GROUND IS NOT COST. IT IS THAT WHOLE-FILE LINE-COUNT NEUTRALITY IS NEITHER NECESSARY NOR SUFFICIENT FOR THE THING EVERY ROW HOLDING IT WANTED.**

- **NOT NECESSARY**, and section 5 is the proof from inside the practice's most careful entry: `ADR-385` grew two files by five lines, believed it had not, and moved no citation.
- **NOT SUFFICIENT**: a diff that adds three lines high in a file and deletes three low in it reports equal insertions and deletions and re-points every pointer between them. Nothing in the corpus checks for that and no entry has named it.

> **THE PROPERTY IS THAT NO CITED LINE MOVES. Where one must, every pointer in a LIVE file is repaired and listed, and every pointer in a DATED RECORD is NAMED, because that one cannot be repaired by a commit.**

**THIS IS NOT NEW LANGUAGE. IT IS [ADR-385](ADR-385.md) SECTION 4's OWN QUALIFIER**, *"line-count neutral ACROSS ITS OWN REGION"*, which is correct, and which the next sentence of that entry generalised into the `numstat` claim the corpus has been quoting. **The practice has been holding the generalisation and paying for it in clauses.**

**IT IS STRICTLY LOOSER AND THE DIFFERENCE IS LARGE.** Growth below a file's deepest cited line is free. Between two cited lines a row has the whole span to work in and owes only that the span's net delta is zero. Whole-file neutrality gives a row neither.

**FOUR REASONS IT IS NOT MADE A GATE, EACH ONE MEASURED RATHER THAN ASSERTED.**

1. **THE CHECKS ARE NOT THE ENFORCER AND EVERY ROW THOUGHT THEY WERE.** 10.2 percent at one line, under 28 at any drift, 0.0 on two of this corpus's most-cited files. Six of the nine sessions in section 4 justified the practice by keeping them green.
2. **THE REAL REASON IS THE ONE SESSION 176 GAVE FIRST AND NOBODY REPEATED**: a signed or dated record cites a line, and `RI-15`'s miss (4) and `RI-16`'s exclusion 1 both rule that repairing that pointer *"would rewrite the record to say something it did not say."* **7,751 of 9,614 edges are in that position. A moved line rarely costs a repair. It costs a pointer nobody may fix**, which is worse and not better.
3. **A GATE OVER IT CANNOT BE WRITTEN BY EITHER CHECK, AND THE REASON IS STRUCTURAL RATHER THAN A COST.** *No cited line moved* is a property of a DIFF. `RI-15`'s miss (7) and `RI-16`'s miss (5) each say in their own words that the check never reads git history. **Both are tree readers by construction**, and a check that cannot see two refs cannot see this property at all. That is a stronger refusal than [ADR-377](ADR-377.md)'s, [ADR-380](ADR-380.md)'s or [ADR-385](ADR-385.md)'s, all three of which turned on a finding count.
4. **AND A GATE ON LINE COUNT WOULD ENTRENCH THE COST.** Four clauses in `ADR-385`, one in `ADR-378`, two phrases in `ADR-381`, and a first clause lost in [session 440](../sessions/2026-08-29-session-440.md). **A rule whose only enforcement is arithmetic is satisfied by deleting meaning, which is exactly what has been happening.**

**WHAT A ROW ACTUALLY NEEDS IS NOT A RULE. IT IS THE FAN-IN NUMBER FOR THE FILE IT IS ABOUT**, which is one derivation away and which no runner reports today. Section 11 owes it.

### 9. What is bound mechanically, and what is deliberately not

**TWO CASES ARE ADDED TO [`packages/tooling/test/repo-invariants.test.ts`](../../packages/tooling/test/repo-invariants.test.ts) AND THEY ARE THE ONLY THING THIS ROW BINDS.** Neither pins prose. Both assert BEHAVIOUR of a check, in the shape the file already uses, and both were watched failing before they were trusted.

1. **`RI-15` stays GREEN when one inserted line makes a nameless citation point somewhere else.** The seed is the case above it moved by one line: `identityScope` at `:1527` with the pointer right, then one line inserted so the pointer is wrong. **The check reports nothing either way.** *A green invariant run is NOT evidence a citation survived* has been in three dispatches and three headers as prose since [ADR-380](ADR-380.md), and it is a control now.
