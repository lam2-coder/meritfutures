## ADR-053: the high-water bound holds only while the floor is unlocked, and what it stops asserting is the ruling  (2026-08-17, status: proposed)

**No frozen document is amended by this ruling. [`0015_rule_states.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql) asserts something `M01` never said, and this ADR decides what it should have asserted instead. It is money path: the constraint refuses to write state rows the engine is specified to produce, so the signature is the founder's.**

- **Origin:** [ADR-052](ADR-052.md)'s consequences bullet, which names this defect and rules that it gets its own ADR, its own migration and its own money-path session rather than being fixed in passing. See section 5.

- **Context:** [`0015_rule_states.sql:208`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql) carries

  ```sql
  CONSTRAINT rule_states_high_water_bounds_balance CHECK (
    high_water_balance_cents >= balance_cents
  )
  ```

  unconditionally, under the comment "the high-water mark is a maximum and never falls below the balance it tracks". **[M01](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) `R-15` freezes `hwb` permanently at the floor lock**, and `R-13` says the same thing from the other side: "`hwb` stops updating once `floorLocked`". **A locked account that then makes a new closing high therefore has a state row the database refuses to write.**

---

### 1. It is not an edge case, and the fixtures already state the rejected row

**The lock is enabled on all three v1 plans** ([Appendix A.1, A.2, A.3](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)), and it fires below every eval target, so **every account that passes an evaluation crosses it**. At 50K the trigger is `260,000c` of profit on Core EOD and Merit Rapid and `210,000c` on Direct; the per-size figures are `110,000` to `760,000` and the trigger is `drawdown_cents + 10,000c` at every one of them by `CV-12`. **After that day, every new closing high is a row this constraint rejects.**

Two golden scenarios state the rejected row outright, and one of them exists for nothing else:

| Fixture | The row | `hwb` | `balance_cents` |
|---|---|---|---|
| [`GS-016`](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/GS-016-locked-floor-does-not-move-on-a-later-high.expected.json), whose entire subject is a new closing high after the lock | day two | **5,260,000** | **5,400,000** |
| [`GS-024`](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/GS-024-consistency-one-cent-over-the-threshold-defers.yaml), day three | after the day-two lock | 5,300,001 | 5,450,001 |
| `GS-024`, day four | frozen | 5,300,001 | 5,500,000 |

**`GS-016`'s expected end state was executed against the schema as it stands** on a PostgreSQL 16 with `0001` through `0036` applied forward-only. It is rejected, by name:

```
NOTICE:  GS-016 day two REJECTED by rule_states_high_water_bounds_balance
```

**The constraint is not wrong about the pre-lock world.** `DO-7` computes `hwb' = max(hwb, closing_balance_cents)` before the lock test, so on every unlocked day, and on the lock day itself, `hwb >= balance` holds by construction. **The defect is the absence of a qualifier, not the presence of an assertion.**

---

### 2. The ruling

**`0015`'s constraint is superseded by [`0037`](../../packages/db/migrations/0037_supersede_rule_states_high_water_bounds_balance.sql), and the replacement is scoped to the unlocked state:**

```sql
CONSTRAINT rule_states_high_water_bounds_balance_unlocked CHECK (
  floor_locked OR high_water_balance_cents >= balance_cents
)
```

`0015` is merged and is never edited (constitution E2), so it is superseded rather than repaired.

**The predicate can never evaluate to NULL, and the reason is three `NOT NULL`s rather than anything in the predicate.** `floor_locked`, `high_water_balance_cents` and `balance_cents` are all declared `NOT NULL` in `0015`. This matters more than it reads: **a `CHECK` that evaluates to NULL PASSES in PostgreSQL**, so if a later migration drops one of those `NOT NULL`s, this constraint silently degrades into one that admits everything, with its text unchanged and every existing test still green. **The probe therefore asserts the three `NOT NULL`s from the catalogue**, because they are load-bearing for a constraint that does not mention them.

---

### 3. What this deliberately stops asserting, which is the half worth the founder's attention

**Post-lock, the database now asserts no relation at all between `high_water_balance_cents` and `balance_cents`.** That is a real loss of coverage and it should be recorded as one rather than discovered later. `GS-016`'s own note names the field as the one that catches a half-implemented lock:

> THE FROZEN HIGH-WATER BALANCE IS THE FIELD THAT CATCHES A HALF-IMPLEMENTED LOCK. An engine that stopped moving the floor but kept updating `hwb` reports 5,400,000 here and still shows the right floor

**Under this ruling the database accepts that wrong row.** The fixture still rejects it; the schema no longer does.

**Three stronger designs were considered and each is refused for a stated reason.**

**(a) A cross-row trigger enforcing the freeze**, comparing each row against the prior trading day's row for the same account and requiring `hwb` to be unchanged once `floor_locked`. This is the one that would recover `GS-016`'s coverage exactly, and it is refused because **it would encode a ruling that does not exist and would break a rule that does**:

- **`R-31` resets `hwb` downward, explicitly.** The eval-pass effects are "`balance = size_cents`; **`hwb = size_cents`**; `floor = size_cents - funded drawdown_cents`". **On Core EOD every eval pass is also a lock day** (the target is `300,000c`, the trigger `260,000c`), so a freeze trigger would reject the funded reset on the ordinary path, which is the same class of defect as the one this ADR repairs.
- **Whether the lock even survives the reset is an open founder item.** [STATE.md](../STATE.md) records it: `INV-07` says a locked floor never changes "for the life of the account", [`progression.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/progression.ts) writes `floorLocked: false` at `DO-8`, and [ADR-050](ADR-050.md) deliberately did not cover it. **A trigger would have to take a side.**

**(b) A post-lock bound against the floor**, `high_water_balance_cents > floor_cents`, which is derivable from `CV-12` and `CV-02` and holds under both readings of the floor expression. **Refused because it is derived rather than stated.** No row of `M01` asserts it, and **this session exists because somebody wrote a `CHECK` stronger than the rule it was checking.** Writing a second one in the same migration repeats the error while repairing it. It also would not catch the failure mode that motivates it: `GS-016`'s half-implemented lock makes `hwb` too **high**, and this bounds it from below.

**(c) Dropping the constraint outright.** Refused: the pre-lock invariant is real, holds by construction, and covers every row of every account before its lock day.

**What is left holding the post-lock behaviour is the fixture layer**, `GS-016` and `RE-P-02`, and that is stated here so the next reader knows the schema is not a second opinion on it.

---

### 4. Why this ruling does not depend on the open `INV-07` question, and does not pre-empt it

The founder has an unruled item on whether the funded reset clears the lock. **This predicate is correct under both answers**, which is the property that makes it safe to land before that item is decided:

| If the reset **clears** the lock | If the lock **persists** across the reset |
|---|---|
| The post-reset row has `floor_locked = false`, `hwb = size_cents` and `balance_cents = size_cents` by `R-31`. `hwb >= balance` holds with equality | The post-reset row is exempt from the constraint entirely |

**Neither answer makes a legitimate row unwritable, and neither is asserted here.**

---

### 5. This ruling removes one of [ADR-052](ADR-052.md)'s stated rejection reasons, and that has to be said out loud

**[ADR-052](ADR-052.md) is `accepted`, founder-approved 2026-08-17, and its consequences bullet is where this session comes from.** It names this defect in its own words, declines to fix it in passing, and says why: "**The remedy is a superseding migration, never an edit** (constitution E2), so it is its own ADR, its own migration and its own money-path session under [ADR-003](ADR-003.md). **No number is claimed for it here and `0037` stays free.**" This ADR and `0037` are that follow-through.

**But ADR-052 also uses the constraint as an argument, and this ruling weakens it.** Rejecting the alternative of freezing `hwb` at the trigger, ADR-052 reasons that a `hwb` of `5,260,000` or `5,150,001` "**store a `hwb` below that day's `balance_cents 5,300,001`, and the constraint rejects the row**". **Under `0037` that row is a locked row, so the constraint no longer rejects it and that argument no longer holds.**

**The alternative stays rejected, on ADR-052's second and independent reason**, given in the same sentence: both freezes "would also make `high_water_balance_cents` stop being the account's high-water balance". That one is about what the column means and nothing here touches it. **Nothing in this ADR reopens a signed ruling**; what it does is remove a support that ruling no longer needs, and the removal is recorded here so that a later reader re-deriving ADR-052 from its own text does not find an argument that has quietly stopped working.

**One factual note on the signed text, reported rather than edited.** ADR-052's consequences bullet says the lock "fires at `size + 260,000c` on all three v1 plans". At 50K that is Core EOD and Merit Rapid; **Direct is `210,000c`**, because its drawdown is 400bp rather than 500bp ([Appendix A.3](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md), and `CV-12` checks `210,000 = 200,000 + 10,000` in the validation walk). The blast-radius claim is unaffected and is if anything stronger, since Direct locks earlier. Section 1 above states the per-plan figures.

---

### 6. Consequences

- `0037` drops `rule_states_high_water_bounds_balance` and adds `rule_states_high_water_bounds_balance_unlocked`. The retired name is **gone from the catalogue** rather than quietly redefined, on `0036`'s precedent, so a reader who greps it finds nothing instead of finding a statement whose meaning changed underneath them.
- [`probe_rule_states_high_water_bound.sql`](../../scripts/db/probe_rule_states_high_water_bound.sql) is wired into the migrations CI job. **It leads with five success cases**, the first of which is `GS-016`'s day two, because the defect being repaired was a constraint refusing a legitimate row and no inventory of rejections can see one.
- **No engine change and no fixture change.** `GS-016` and `GS-024` are already correct; it is the schema that could not hold them.
- **[`DATA_MODEL`'s `rule_states` entry](../architecture/data-model/rule_states.md) carries the constraint by name in its `Constraints:` line**, so the retired name is replaced there in the same commit rather than left pointing at a constraint the database no longer has.
