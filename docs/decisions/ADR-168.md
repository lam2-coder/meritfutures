## ADR-168: three reads `apps/portal` names and `API_CONTRACT` does not define, and they do not have the same answer (2026-08-27, status: proposed)

**[ADR-153](ADR-153.md) repaired a contract row naming an endpoint the contract defines nowhere, and its section 4 says running that comparison is how the next one is found. Run in the other direction it finds three, and this entry rules them separately because they are three different shapes.** Two are **missing rows** and land: [M11 section 4](../plans/M11-certificates-social-proof.md) is `approved`, so the endpoints it marks NEW are commitments the interface contract never grew. One is a **refusal**: [M08 section 4](../plans/M08-affiliate-system.md) enumerates the affiliate surface and adds exactly one endpoint, and a read of the creatives collection is in no approved document.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `168`, session **287**, dispatched as `claude/contract-certificates-and-creatives`. **Not money path by content.** It writes no code, registers no route, opens no transaction and claims no migration number. The [`E2`](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) read is owed on the handlers these rows produce and is not discharged here.
- **A DISPATCH PROMPT IS NOT A SOURCE, and two of its claims did not survive.** Both are in section 1. Every figure below is a `grep`, a `sed` over a migration, or a composed server, quoted at the file it came from.

### 2. THE RULING, three clauses because there are three questions

> **Clause 1. `GET /certificates` is a MISSING ROW. It lands, in `API_CONTRACT` section 6.3.**
>
> [M11](../plans/M11-certificates-social-proof.md) is `approved` and marks the endpoint **NEW** and **Owns**. Under FREEZE a behavior in the corpus is a commitment, so the endpoint is committed scope and the interface contract simply never grew it. **The portal is not reaching for anything**: finding 6 shows it declines to use the endpoint and says why.

> **Clause 2. `GET /certificates/:code/image.png` is a MISSING ROW and lands with its security posture STATED rather than assumed.** Same source, same status, and one thing more: `INV-M11-08` makes the re-render the only mechanism by which a revocation reaches an image already in circulation, so without this endpoint `AS-M11-02`'s answer does not exist. The path carries the `.png` that `M11` wrote.

> **Clause 3. `GET /affiliate/creatives` is REFUSED. The row does not land.**
>
> [M08 section 4](../plans/M08-affiliate-system.md) enumerates the affiliate surface, says in its own prose that it *"adds one"*, and the one is the `POST`. No approved document states a read of that collection, a shape for it, an auth rule or an error set. **Inventing it would be inventing scope**, and the gap `referrals/screen.ts` reports is real and is not this endpoint's to close: `M08` routes the required disclosure through the `POST` response, and finding 15 shows that reading is coherent.

**And no portal comment is corrected except one, because only one becomes false.** `ports.ts`' *"is in no API_CONTRACT section today"* is falsified by clause 1 and is replaced in place, with the superseded sentence quoted rather than deleted. `model.ts:182` and `referrals/screen.ts:307` were both re-read against all three clauses and **survive verbatim**: the first attributes the image endpoint to `M11` and does not claim the contract lacks it, and the second states an absence clause 3 confirms. **A comment edited to look responsive to a ruling that did not touch it is drift.**

### 3. What this entry FORECLOSES

1. **It forecloses reading `M11`'s certificate surface as CLOSED at two rows.** Finding 3: four of the six stay undefined, and one of them is named by a field this contract already ships. `verify_url` at section 6's singular certificate row is commented *"public verification page"*, and `GET /verify/:code` is defined nowhere. **That is ADR-153's disease found a second time, inside the section this entry edits, and it is deliberately NOT repaired here**: a public oracle over Merit's own payout book is its own ruling with its own session, and `AS-M11-04` is the reason it does not get taken in passing.
2. **It forecloses `withheld` entering any response before a column can hold it.** Findings 9 and 10. The union is three members and a fourth is a client branch that never fires ([ADR-040](ADR-040.md)'s own lesson on `PayoutListItem`, one section up).
3. **It forecloses `revoked_reason` reaching a trader surface.** Finding 11. The class is published, the free text is not, and the response allowlist is where that is enforced rather than in a reviewer's memory.
4. **It forecloses a deferred certificate handing out a shareable token.** `code`, `verify_url` and `image_url` are `null` while deferred, against a `NOT NULL` column, because a deferral is a claim Merit has not made yet.
5. **It forecloses `INV-M11-05` being read as one uniform rule over every public certificate surface.** Finding 13: the image endpoint keeps the entropy, the rate limit and the anomaly signal and cannot keep the clock. **It also forecloses treating a timing difference on that endpoint as a defect**, which is the direction an implementer would otherwise chase forever.
6. **It forecloses the image endpoint being a static asset on the public-catalog rate class.** It writes `certificate_verifications` on every fetch and carries its own section 11 row, because a public read keyed on `code` is one oracle however it is dressed.
7. **It forecloses "the application named it, therefore it is owed".** Clause 3 is the counter-example and it is the reason the three were ruled apart. **A comment in a segment is not a commitment**, and the direction that makes it one is how a portal's prose becomes Merit's scope.
8. **It forecloses building any of this.** No route is registered, no handler is written, no `SD-nn` is claimed. The rows are the specification a later slice transcribes.

### 4. What this entry does NOT do

- **It registers nothing.** Finding 7's count moves from 14 contract rows registered by nothing to **16**, and that is stated rather than left for the next reader to count.
- **It does not touch section 12.** `GET /certificates` and the image row each owe a negative-authz row, and section 12 was held by session **280** while this branch ran. Named as owed, and a row written into a file another session holds is a merge conflict dressed as diligence.
- **It does not touch `apps/api`, `M11`, `M08`, or the `certificates` migration.** `0020` is merged and sacred under [E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md); findings 10 and 12 are contradictions inside `M11`'s own prose and are recorded, not repaired.
- **It does not settle what a deferral reason SAYS.** `INV-M11-09` makes the reason visible and no approved document constrains its wording, which matters because the trigger is an open severity 4+ flag and [M07](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md) does not publish detector detail. The field is typed; the copy rule is owed.
- **It does not rule `M08` self-contradictory.** Finding 15.

### 5. Approval

**Approval line: PENDING, UNSIGNED.** Not money path: two contract rows, no route, no migration, no code path that moves cash. The `E2` read is owed on the handlers.

**One checkable clause, and it is a comparison rather than a count**, because [ADR-153](ADR-153.md) section 4 is what produced this entry and a count would go stale on the next merge.

> **Every `METHOD /path` heading in `API_CONTRACT` resolves to an endpoint an approved document commits Merit to, and every certificate endpoint an approved document commits Merit to is either defined in `API_CONTRACT` or named in section 3 clause 1 above as owed.**
>
> ```
> grep -cE '^#+ (GET|POST|PUT|PATCH|DELETE) .*/certificates' docs/architecture/API_CONTRACT.md
> grep -n '/affiliate/creatives' docs/architecture/API_CONTRACT.md
> ```
>
> **The first must print `2`, the two headings this entry wrote and no third. The second must still return exactly one line, and it must be the `POST`.** The first is scoped to HEADINGS rather than to the string, because the prose around these rows names the path a dozen times and a bare `grep -c` would count the argument for the row as if it were the row. Then, from the API rather than from a grep, because a grep over route files has been wrong twice in this repository: `discoverRouteModules()` composed on both surfaces, and no `/certificates` path in either `CompositionReport.registered`, because clause 1 and clause 2 admit rows and build nothing.
>
> **The direction it fails in is a THIRD shape neither half catches**: an endpoint named by a module plan, absent from the contract, and named by no portal segment at all. Four of those are live right now and finding 3 lists them. What generalises is the comparison, not these two greps.

**Cost if wrong.** If clause 3 is wrong, an affiliate learns a disclosure requirement one step later than `M08` wanted and the remedy is a later row. **If clause 1 or 2 is wrong, Merit's interface contract commits it to an endpoint no approved document asked for**, and the honest response to a defined endpoint is to build it. Clause 2 is the expensive half: it is public, unauthenticated, and it renders a real trader's real result to whoever holds the code.

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.** Two of the decisions here are judgements rather than derivations. **That two endpoints LAND in a frozen contract and one is REFUSED** turns on a distinction the entry draws itself: an approved module plan marking an endpoint `NEW` is a commitment the interface contract never grew, and a portal segment naming a read is not. Foreclosure 7 states the rule in one line and the cost of getting it backwards is that a portal's prose becomes Merit's scope. **The refusal is the half to weigh**, because the gap the portal reports is real and clause 3 leaves it open on the ground that inventing the shape would be inventing scope. **The second judgement is clause 2's security posture**, stated rather than assumed on a public, unauthenticated endpoint keyed on a code: the entry says plainly that clause 2 is the expensive half and that the honest response to a defined endpoint is to build it, so the row is a commitment to publish a real trader's real result to whoever holds the code.
