## ADR-177: the chart of accounts is seeded and four of its seven kinds are ruled, and `firm_treasury` is refused because the corpus contradicts itself about it rather than because it is silent  (2026-08-28, status: proposed)

**[ADR-174](ADR-174.md) recorded finding (C) as an ABSENCE and inferred no direction from it. Re-derived here at source and executed against a database, (C) is not one absence. It is three different things wearing one label, and only one of them is silence.** Four of the seven codes have a `kind` DERIVABLE from a posting the corpus already states, read through the one sign convention. `psp_clearing` and `reserve` are silent. **`firm_treasury` is not silent at all: it is contradicted**, and the contradiction is unanimous on both sides. So this entry rules four kinds, seeds the one row a migration can seed, and refuses three, and migration `0052` IS TAKEN.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `177`, session **321**, dispatched as `claude/chart-of-accounts`. **`0052` is taken.** [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) returned that number to the pool unspent and named this session's ordering: *"the next session on this row is one that first rules the chart of accounts and only then rules the posting."*

- **MONEY PATH. `E2` READ IS OWED**, on [`0052`](../../packages/db/migrations/0052_chart_of_accounts.sql), which writes four `kind` literals into a CHECK and one into a row. A merged migration is never edited, only superseded, so a wrong literal here is permanent by construction. **`0009`, `0027` and `0038` are byte for byte unchanged** and the seven-code vocabulary is untouched.

- **THE OBLIGATION [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) SECTION 4 LEFT TO THIS ENTRY IS DISCHARGED IN SECTION 3**, which reads `LT-06`'s row rather than only `LT-07`'s, and finds the defect is larger than that entry measured: `LT-02` reads backwards too, on the same account, in the same direction.

### 1. The four derivations, each with its elimination step written out

**The convention, read and not trusted.** [`posting.ts:235-236`](../../packages/ledger/src/posting.ts) pushes `{account: t.debit, amountCents: t.amountCents}` and `{account: t.credit, amountCents: -t.amountCents}`, and its own header calls itself *"THE ONE PLACE A SIGN IS APPLIED IN THIS PACKAGE"*. [`0009:118`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql) states the same from the schema side. **So a DEBIT is `+amount_cents` and a CREDIT is `-amount_cents`**, and a debit increases an asset or an expense and decreases a liability, a revenue or an equity.

| Code | Ruled | The posting, and the step |
|---|---|---|
| **`fees_revenue`** | **`revenue`** | `LT-01` CREDITS it `firm_cents` and [M05:147](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) says *"`firm_cents` becomes recognized revenue"*, so the credit increases it: liability, revenue or equity. **The other direction is posted on the same account and agrees**: [ADR-067](ADR-067.md) section 2.3 and [`0038`](../../packages/db/migrations/0038_account_adjustments.sql) header item 4 DEBIT `fees_revenue` and both say in terms that it *"reduces revenue"*. [`checkout.ts:1671`](../../apps/api/src/routes/checkout.ts) states the conclusion outright in shipped source: `fees_revenue` is *"the only one of the seven whose `kind` is `revenue`"* |
| **`trader_wallet`** | **`liability`** | `LT-01` CREDITS it `trader_cents`; [M05:145](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) calls what it credits *"a firm obligation to pay"* and [`0009:25`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql) says *"Wallet is what Merit already owes them"*. The credit increases it: liability, revenue or equity. It is per identity, so it is neither the firm's revenue nor the firm's equity. **[`0038`](../../packages/db/migrations/0038_account_adjustments.sql) header item 4 names the class directly** for a posting against this account: it *"books a liability and reduces revenue in ONE moment, its own"* |
| **`trader_withdrawable`** | **`liability`** | `LT-01` DEBITS it `approved_cents` and [M05:147](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) says that is *"what leaves their claim on the firm"*; [`0009:24`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql) says *"Withdrawable is what the engine says the trader may draw"*. The debit decreases it: liability, revenue or equity. It is per identity and it is a trader's claim, so it is neither of the other two |
| **`promotional_credit`** | **`liability`** | **DERIVED FROM AN ENFORCED CONSTRAINT RATHER THAN FROM PROSE, which is why it is settleable while two codes beside it are not.** [`0038:146`](../../packages/db/migrations/0038_account_adjustments.sql) CHECKs `destination IN ('trader_wallet','promotional_credit')`; [`0038:420-425`](../../packages/db/migrations/0038_account_adjustments.sql) writes the sign mapping as a table, a credit posting `-amount_cents` on *"the identity's destination class"* and `+amount_cents` on `fees_revenue`; and `assert_adjustment_posting_matches` ENFORCES that mapping on every row, to the account, the identity, the cent and the sign. **Header item 4 describes what that posting does ONCE, for both destinations**: it *"books a liability"*. So the credit increases `promotional_credit` and the same file names what is increased. [M17](../plans/M17-offers-engine.md) `OF-M17-04` corroborates the meaning, a grant *"spendable at checkout"*, which is an entitlement Merit owes |

**The probe is a CHECK on this and deliberately not its source.** [`probe_ledger_constraints.sql`](../../scripts/db/probe_ledger_constraints.sql) happens to seed `trader_withdrawable` and `trader_wallet` as `liability` and `fees_revenue` as `revenue`, which is three of the four above. **[ADR-174](ADR-174.md) section 4 is right that a fixture chosen to make a constraint reject something is not a statement of the chart**, so the derivations were taken from the postings and the agreement was noticed afterwards. It is worth one line because the agreement is evidence that the fixture's author reasoned the same way, and worth no more than one because two readers reaching one answer is not a source.

### 2. THE RULING

> **1. FOUR KINDS ARE RULED AND WRITTEN INTO THE SCHEMA.** [`0052`](../../packages/db/migrations/0052_chart_of_accounts.sql) adds `ledger_accounts_kind_matches_code`, a CHECK binding `kind` to `code` for `fees_revenue`, `trader_wallet`, `trader_withdrawable` and `promotional_credit`. **[ADR-174](ADR-174.md) finding 9 EXECUTED the fact that this database accepted `firm_treasury` as `'asset'`, as `'liability'` and as `'revenue'` in turn**; for these four codes that is no longer true, and the refusal was watched rather than assumed (section 5).
>
> **2. THE CONSTRAINT ENDS IN `ELSE true` AND THE HOLE IS THE RULING'S OTHER HALF.** `firm_treasury`, `psp_clearing` and `reserve` fall through it. A constraint naming all seven would be this entry guessing with a constraint wrapped round the guess. **The hole is where the open question is stored, in the schema, where the next session cannot miss it**, and [`chart-of-accounts-kinds.test.ts`](../../packages/ledger/test/chart-of-accounts-kinds.test.ts) asserts the absence as well as the four so a code cannot leave the list quietly.
>
> **3. ONE ROW IS SEEDED, AND ONE IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT A MIGRATION CAN SEED.** `LEDGER_ACCOUNT_SCOPE` makes four codes firm-scoped and three per identity; a migration cannot seed a per-identity account because there are no identities. So the seedable set is the four firm codes, and of those only `fees_revenue` has a settled kind. **`ledger_accounts` goes from 0 rows to 1**, which is the first row it has ever held.
>
> **4. `firm_treasury` IS REFUSED ON A CONTRADICTION AND NOT ON SILENCE, AND THAT IS THIS ENTRY'S CENTRAL FINDING.** Section 3. Five documents call it the account where cash books; every posting the corpus writes against it reads as a liability. **Both sides are unanimous**, so this is a defect in the corpus rather than a gap in it, and no literal written here could be right about both halves.
>
> **5. NO DOCUMENT IS AMENDED AND NO CODE IS MINTED.** The vocabulary stays closed at seven. [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) section 2.1's rows stand as written, on [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) clause 5's ground: rewriting half a defective row is how a reader stops being able to see that it was defective.

### 3. `LT-06`'s row, read as [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) section 4 requires, and the defect is bigger than that entry measured

[ADR-174](ADR-174.md) measured that `LT-06` credits `firm_treasury` `amount_cents`, `LT-07` debits it, and the external leg therefore moves it by ZERO. **That is confirmed here at source.** What this entry adds is that the zero is a symptom of something with a third instance, and the third one is in the INTERNAL leg, which [ADR-174](ADR-174.md)'s fence excluded.

| Posting | What [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) writes | Signed | Under `asset` | Under `liability` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`LT-02`** `payout_settlement` | *"debit `firm_treasury` `trader_cents`"* ([M05:131](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)) | `+trader_cents` | **BACKWARDS.** A debit increases an asset, so cash ARRIVES at the moment cash departs | Correct. The obligation to pay is extinguished at settlement |
| **`LT-06`** `wallet_withdrawal_approval` | *"credit `firm_treasury` `amount_cents`"* ([M05:135](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)) | `-amount_cents` | **BACKWARDS.** A credit decreases an asset, so cash DERECOGNIZES AT APPROVAL, the exact timing [ADR-027](ADR-027.md), [ADR-033](ADR-033.md), [ADR-067](ADR-067.md) and [`0038:48-49`](../../packages/db/migrations/0038_account_adjustments.sql) each refused in turn on `LT-01` | Correct. The wallet claim, debited in the same posting, becomes a firm-level obligation to remit |
| **`LT-07`** `wallet_withdrawal_settlement` | *"debit `firm_treasury`"* ([M05:136](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)) | `+amount_cents` | **BACKWARDS**, for `LT-02`'s reason | Correct. The obligation is extinguished when the cash leaves |

**Against that, five documents, none of which is a `kind`:** [M05:141](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) *"it books a cash movement at approval"*, [ADR-027:48](ADR-027.md), [ADR-033:20](ADR-033.md), [ADR-067:77](ADR-067.md) and [`0038:48-49`](../../packages/db/migrations/0038_account_adjustments.sql) *"`firm_treasury` was the available mistake"*. **Every one of them treats it as cash, which is an asset.**

**So the arithmetic is unanimous on `liability` and the prose is unanimous on `asset`, and the two cannot both be right.** [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) section 4 refused to write `asset` from the five prose sites and was correct to; this entry refuses to write `liability` from the three postings for the mirror-image reason. **Writing either literal into `0052` would harden one half of a contradiction against the other, permanently, under [`E2`](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md).**

**What the liability reading implies, recorded because it is the cheapest thing this entry can leave the next session.** If `firm_treasury` is the obligation rather than the cash, then `LT-07`'s missing credit slot is **the cash account, and the chart has no cash account**: `psp_clearing` and `reserve` are the only remaining firm codes and neither has a posting or a stated meaning. That is [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) section 3 shape (i) or (iii) reached from the other end, and it is a finding rather than a proposal. **If instead the five prose sites are right and `firm_treasury` is cash, then `LT-02`, `LT-06` and `LT-07` are three rows written with their legs on the wrong sides**, which is [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) clause 2 widened by one row. **This entry does not choose**, and section 8 names the choice as the founder's.

### 4. The two silent codes, refused for two different reasons

- **`psp_clearing`. REFUSED FOR LACK OF A POSTING.** Nothing in this tree posts against it; session 157 recorded *"ACCOUNTS EXIST, PATH DOES NOT"* and it is still true. **The one statement of its nature is [`checkout.ts:1680`](../../apps/api/src/routes/checkout.ts)**, that crediting it *"would book a receivable from a processor that was never asked for money"*. A receivable is an asset. **That sentence's own verb is backwards for one**, because crediting an asset reduces a receivable rather than booking one, so it cannot be read as a sign-checked derivation. It is a correct instinct about a leg nobody has posted, and it is not a chart. **The transfers in [`post.test.ts`](../../packages/ledger/test/post.test.ts) are not evidence either**: `psp_clearing` is debited against `firm_treasury` in one case and credited against it in another, which is a fixture exercising the posting machinery in both directions.
- **`reserve`. REFUSED FOR LACK OF A POSTING AND FOR LACK OF A READER.** Nothing posts against it and nothing reads it. **`SD-M5-03` and `INV-M5-11` anchor the reserve figure to `treasury_balances`, deliberately OUTSIDE this ledger**, because *"computing it from our own ledger makes it a number that agrees with itself"*, and [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) finding 10 EXECUTED that `treasury_balances.account_code` carries no key into the chart. [GLOSSARY](../GLOSSARY.md) calls the reserve *"funds set aside"*, which is asset-shaped prose of exactly the kind that turned out to be contradicted for `firm_treasury` one row up.

### 6. THE DEFECT THIS SESSION FOUND, WHICH WAS NOT IN THE DISPATCH

**Seeding `fees_revenue` breaks the CI probe, and it breaks it by going quiet.** [`probe_ledger_constraints.sql`](../../scripts/db/probe_ledger_constraints.sql) seeded its own `fees_revenue` row under `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` with a pinned uuid. **Once the seed exists, `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` skips that row**, the pinned uuid names nothing, and the `LEDGER-C1` block raises `LEDGER-C2` on a dangling account **instead of probing `LEDGER-C1` at all**. Executed: `INSERT 0 2` where the file expects three, and the job exits 3.

**The repair makes the probe READ the chart rather than assert itself onto it**, and it was falsified in both directions: with the seed deleted the new guard fails the build by name, and with `ledger_entries_no_opposite_signs` DISABLEd the probe still reports *"PROBE FAILED: LEDGER-C1 admitted opposite signs"*, so the repair did not neuter the gate it was adapting. **The file's own header is the argument for checking this**: *"A constraint nobody has watched reject anything is a constraint nobody knows is wired up."* A probe that stops probing while still passing is the same object.

### 7. What this entry deliberately does NOT rule

> **1. `LT-07`'s repair.** [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) section 3's three shapes each need `firm_treasury`'s kind, and section 3 above refuses it. **The interlock that entry named is real and is now one question rather than two**: not *"what is `firm_treasury`"* but *"which of the two unanimous halves of this corpus is wrong about it"*.
>
> **2. THE CHART OF ACCOUNTS IS MUTABLE BY THE APPLICATION ROLE AND THIS ENTRY LEAVES IT THAT WAY.** Measured on this tree: `merit_app` holds `SELECT`, `INSERT`, `UPDATE` and `DELETE` on `ledger_accounts`, from `0026`'s default grant, **and nothing in the application writes the table at all** (`LedgerReadKey` is a read key; [`chart.ts`](../../packages/ledger/src/chart.ts) only reads). So the seeded row can be deleted and an account reclassified by the role that never writes either. `REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE` is what `0032`, `0039`, `0049` and `0051` each did on their own tables and would cost nothing today. **It is refused here because [`assert_append_only_grants.mjs`](../../scripts/db/assert_append_only_grants.mjs) asserts in BOTH directions that the append-only set [DATA_MODEL](../architecture/data-model/README.md) section 1 declares equals the set the database installs**: the revoke makes `ledger_accounts` append-only by that gate's own definition, so it requires amending a FROZEN document, which is an ADR and a slice of its own. **Widening this session's fence to reach it is the thing that is not permitted**, and the gate refusing the widening is the gate working.
>
> **3. NOTHING CREATES A LEDGER ACCOUNT FOR AN IDENTITY, AT ANY POINT.** There is no INSERT against `ledger_accounts` anywhere in the application, so the three per-identity classes have no writer and `postTransaction` REFUSES rather than creating one ([`chart.ts`](../../packages/ledger/src/chart.ts)). **The money path is blocked on that absence independently of this entry's**, and this seed does not unblock it. Recorded here because it is the next thing a session reading [`0052`](../../packages/db/migrations/0052_chart_of_accounts.sql) will assume was handled.

### 8. Approval

NOBODY GRANTED IT AND NO SIGNATURE IS RECORDED HERE.** Clause 1, that four kinds are ruled and written into the schema, is a PROPERTY. The assertion is [`packages/ledger/test/chart-of-accounts-kinds.test.ts`](../../packages/ledger/test/chart-of-accounts-kinds.test.ts). **Watched RED on the real tree and GREEN after a byte-identical restore**, transcript at [ADR-244](ADR-244.md) section 3.12: `0052`'s `trader_wallet` arm flipped from `liability` to `asset` turned 2 of 14 red, on *"0052 still carries its own four arms, unedited, now that 0053 supersedes it"* and *"a superseding constraint carries every arm the one before it carried"*.

  **AND THIS ENTRY'S OWN DECLARED CENTRAL FINDING IS SUPERSEDED RATHER THAN EARNED.** Clause 4 reads *"`firm_treasury` IS REFUSED ON A CONTRADICTION AND NOT ON SILENCE, AND THAT IS THIS ENTRY'S CENTRAL FINDING"*, and [ADR-180](ADR-180.md) subsequently ruled `firm_treasury` an `asset` in [`0053`](../../packages/db/migrations/0053_firm_treasury_kind.sql). **No assertion can falsify that clause, because the property it describes is deliberately gone.** So **the residual choice did not vanish, it moved**: [ADR-180](ADR-180.md) took the decision this entry declined, that entry is class C on its own words, and the `E2` read owed on `0053` is owed there and not here. **WHAT THE ASSERTION DOES NOT COVER.** Section 8's second judgement, that four kinds are enough to write while three are not, with `promotional_credit` named as the one to check. And the probe repair in section 6, which this entry itself flags as a fence a founder may prefer to see reviewed separately.

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.**

**The load-bearing judgement is section 3, and it is a judgement about which half of the corpus to disbelieve rather than about `firm_treasury`.** This entry establishes that the arithmetic and the prose are each unanimous and opposite. It then refuses to choose, and a founder may reasonably hold that the choice is obvious in one direction: **`firm_treasury` is cash, the five documents are right, and `LT-02`, `LT-06` and `LT-07` are three rows with their legs on the wrong sides.** That reading is coherent, it is arguably the likelier of the two given that `LT-01`'s row was already found defective in exactly this way by [ADR-027](ADR-027.md), and taking it would close [ADR-174](ADR-174.md)'s interlock in one move instead of leaving a third session to it. **What goes wrong if this entry's refusal is over-cautious is a wave of sessions each blocked on a question a founder could have answered in a sentence.** What goes wrong if a founder answers it too quickly is a `kind` literal in a merged migration that three approved postings contradict, and `E2` makes that permanent.

**The second judgement is that four kinds are enough to write while three are not**, and the line between them is *"is there a posting"* rather than *"is there prose"*. **`promotional_credit` is the one to check**: its derivation runs through [`0038`](../../packages/db/migrations/0038_account_adjustments.sql)'s header sentence *"books a liability"*, which is written about a posting whose destination may be either `trader_wallet` or `promotional_credit`, and this entry reads that sentence as covering both. **If it was written with only `trader_wallet` in mind, then `promotional_credit` belongs with the refused three** and `0052`'s CHECK names one code too many.

**The third is that the probe repair in section 6 is a repair rather than a fence widened.** [`probe_ledger_constraints.sql`](../../scripts/db/probe_ledger_constraints.sql) is not in this session's named ownership, and the alternative to touching it was to seed nothing and leave the objective undone. **A founder may prefer that the seed and the probe change land in separate reviews**, since one of them edits a CI gate on the money path.
