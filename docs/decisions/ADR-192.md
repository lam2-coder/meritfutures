## ADR-192: the thirteen keep their 503 and stop disclosing it before authenticating, because ADR-190's ground is two grounds and only one of them reaches here (2026-08-28, status: proposed)

**[ADR-190](ADR-190.md) registered three items and called them one question. They are one question, and the answer is that its clause 2 does NOT reach the thirteen and its clause 3 does.** Clause 2 refused a 503 for `AdminReadError` because that class carries two different facts; the four write modules carry four DEDICATED classes and **17 throw sites, 17 of 17 inside the unwired default constant and not one of them refusing data a wired backend returned**, so the fact each carries is exactly *"no backend is installed"* and the 503 is the honest answer to it. **The status does not move and no code is minted.** What moves is the ORDER: clause 3 refused to tell an unauthenticated caller which of this deployment's ports are uncomposed, and the thirteen were telling them before authenticating. `principal`'s refusal by an unwired backend now answers **401**, because a deployment that cannot authenticate anyone has authenticated nobody, and that sentence is true of every caller and discloses nothing. **The 503 did not leave the surface; it moved behind the 401**, and each of the four suites asserts it there. **Measured over a real `compose()` and Fastify's own `inject`: all 23 `/admin/*` routes now answer 401 `unauthenticated` to an anonymous caller and no registered operator route answers 503 before authenticating.**

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `192`, session **352**, dispatched as `claude/operator-error-surface`. The row is amended in place under [ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3.

- **NOT THE MONEY PATH.** No migration number is reserved and none is taken, no transaction is opened, no ledger account is named, no `SystemReason` or `SqlExecutorReason` member is added, no `pg` is imported, no cast past a key type is written, and `API_CONTRACT` is not edited because the ruling moves no declared code. What moves is one `catch` in each of four route modules, their four suites, one assertion in a fifth, and one citation.

- **THE ENTRY SHIPS `proposed` WITH AN UNSIGNED APPROVAL LINE.** What is **derived** is the surface measurement on both sides, the 17-of-17 throw-site partition that decides item 1, and the five seeds. What is **judged** is clause 2, which spends an operator's ability to tell *"I am not logged in"* from *"this deployment cannot log anyone in"* to buy the disclosure's closure; and the decision in section 7 to touch one file the dispatch did not list. Section 9 is that question.

- **A DISPATCH PROMPT IS NOT A SOURCE.** Every figure below was re-derived at its own file at the moment of reporting. **Two came back different and one of them is a finding**, which is section 1.

### 2. THE RULING

**Clause 1. THE THIRTEEN KEEP THEIR 503, AND [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) CLAUSE 2's GROUND DOES NOT REACH THEM.** That clause refused a 503 for `AdminReadError` because the status is a property of the CLASS and the class carries thirteen refusals that are not about composition. **The four write modules carry four classes that carry one fact each, measured 17 of 17 at finding 5.** A 503 from any of them says *no backend is installed*, and that is what happened. Section 3.

**Clause 2. THE 503 MOVES BEHIND THE 401, AND [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) CLAUSE 3's GROUND IS WHAT REACHES HERE.** `principal`'s refusal by an unwired backend answers **401 `unauthenticated`**; every other port member's refusal still answers 503. **A deployment that cannot authenticate anyone has authenticated nobody**, so the 401 is true of every caller, needs no guess about what a credential looks like, and discloses nothing. Section 4.

**Clause 3. THE DISCRIMINATION GOES IN THE LOG, WHICH IS THE TRADE [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) CLAUSE 3 ALREADY MADE.** An operator meeting a deployment whose `principal` port is unwired is told 401 and cannot tell that from being logged out. Each `*Unwired` message names the port member it could not serve and each catch logs it under a message ending `: principal`. Section 5 states the cost rather than hiding it.

**Clause 4. THE VOCABULARY IS NOT REPAIRED AND THE REASON IS NOT DEFERRAL.** `service_unavailable` stays out of [`STATUS_CODE:67`](../../apps/api/src/server.ts) and [`TITLE:77`](../../apps/api/src/server.ts) on finding 9: the hand-built title is a stated design, and the table entry is the mechanism that would let a future class set a status nobody meant to send. **`server.ts` is in this session's fence and is byte for byte unchanged, which is a ruling rather than an omission.** Section 6.

**Clause 5. THE PROPERTY IS ASSERTED OVER THE SURFACE AND NOT OVER FOUR MODULES.** Four modules each behaving well is not the surface holding the property, and the split [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) found survived three sessions precisely because nobody swept the whole of it. Section 8 is the control.

### 3. Clause 1, and the count that decides it

**The naive reading is that the thirteen are wrong and should join the ten**, and [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) clause 4 says so in terms: *"On this ruling's ground the ten are right and the thirteen are answering the wrong question."* **That sentence is true of its own ground and its own ground is narrower than the sentence.**

What clause 2 actually reasoned was never *"503 is the wrong status for an unwired port."* It was: the status is a property of the CLASS, `AdminReadError` is one class carrying two facts, and a status set on it answers all fifteen throw sites including the twelve that refuse a float in a cents field. **That reasoning is about a class with mixed contents. It says nothing about a class with one content.**

| | class | throw sites | *"no backend is installed"* | refusing data a wired source returned |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the ten | `AdminReadError` | **15** | **2** | **13** |
| the thirteen | `AdminWriteUnwired`, `AdminWalletUnwired`, `AdminPayoutUnwired`, `AdminCertificateUnwired` | **17** | **17** | **0** |

**Every one of the 17 is a member of its module's `UNWIRED_*` default object and there is no other way to construct one.** So the sentence [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) section 4 rejected for the read side -- *"a 503 answers try again shortly to a float in a cents field"* -- has no instance here. The data refusals in these modules are `Refusal`s carrying their own contract codes, caught one branch earlier at each module's `err instanceof Refusal`.

**[ADR-190](ADR-190.md) SECTION 8 NAMED THIS EXACT REVERSAL AND ITS CONDITION**: *"A reader who weights an operator's ability to tell unfinished from broken above one class keeping one status should reverse clause 2 for the WRITE modules only, and nothing here forecloses it."* This entry reverses it for the write modules only, and **not** on that weighting, which is a preference. It reverses it on the count above, which is not.

**And the class split is priced rather than adopted.** The dispatch is right that it is the obvious shape. It is also unnecessary: a class split exists to separate two facts inside one class, and these four classes each hold one fact already. **The estate has ALREADY done the split** -- `AdminReadError` versus these four is the split, drawn at the module boundary rather than inside one module -- and item 2 of [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) remains open for `admin-reads.ts` alone, where the mixing is. A fifth class here would be a second thing four handlers must choose between correctly, to distinguish facts that are not currently mixed.

### 4. Clause 2, the disclosure, and why the repair is order rather than status

**[`admin-writes.ts:844`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-writes.ts) and its three siblings are the argument this entry adjudicates**, and they win it. Their claim is that an unauthenticated caller learns *existence* and no more, which is why 401 precedes 403. The 503 gave them one fact more.

**What that fact is, precisely, is finding 7.** Not existence -- 401 discloses existence and the contract intends it to. **Deployment wiring state**, unauthenticated, and refreshed as slices land. [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) clause 3 refused exactly this fact on the wire for the read side: *"A response that distinguished 'this deployment composed no session source' from 'it composed no read source' would tell an unauthenticated caller which of this deployment's ports are uncomposed."* **The thirteen were doing the coarser version of that to a caller who had sent nothing at all.**

**THE REPAIR IS NOT TO CHANGE WHAT THE 503 MEANS BUT TO PUT IT AFTER THE 401**, and the shape is [`admin-reads.ts`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-reads.ts)'s, which [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) finding 11 already identified as the reason that module escapes: it reads the credential before consulting the source. These modules cannot do that literally. Their `principal` port takes the whole `FastifyRequest` and resolves it however the deployment chooses -- [`admin-writes.ts:262`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-writes.ts) calls hardware-key SSO and the IP allowlist edge concerns -- so **the module does not know what a credential looks like and must not guess.** A check for a cookie name would be this module asserting a credential shape no backend has promised.

**So the answer is the one that needs no guess.** `principal` throwing `*Unwired` means this deployment can authenticate nobody. **Then nobody is authenticated, and `unauthenticated` is true of every caller** -- the one with a valid credential included, because there is nothing here that could have honoured it. The 401 is not a euphemism for the 503; it is a different true statement, and it happens to be the one that carries no deployment fact.

**Measured both sides over a real `compose()` and Fastify's own `inject`, every registered operator route one at a time:**

| | anonymous | with a cookie |
|---|---|---|
| the ten, `admin-reads.ts` / `admin-breaker.ts` / `admin-feed.ts` | **401** `unauthenticated`, unmoved | **500** `internal_error`, unmoved |
| the thirteen, before | **503** `service_unavailable` | **503** `service_unavailable` |
| the thirteen, after | **401** `unauthenticated` | **401** `unauthenticated` |

**All 23 `/admin/*` routes now answer 401 to an anonymous caller and no registered operator route answers 503 before authenticating.** `admin-reads.ts` is byte for byte unchanged and its two answers were re-measured rather than assumed.

**THE 503 DID NOT LEAVE THE SURFACE, AND THAT IS ASSERTED RATHER THAN CLAIMED.** Each of the four suites gained the leg that an authenticated operator whose deployment wired no `operator` (or no `reconcile`, or no `presentation`) still receives 503 `service_unavailable`. **That leg exists because a module that simply stopped sending 503 at all would pass every other case in this entry**, and seed B in section 8 is that counterfactual executed.

### 5. The cost of clause 2, stated rather than hidden

**An operator on a deployment whose `principal` port is unwired is told 401 and cannot tell that from being logged out.** Before this entry they were told 503, which named the deployment as the problem. That discrimination is now only in the log, where [`AdminWriteUnwired:311`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-writes.ts)'s message says *"no backend is installed. `useAdminWriteBackend` was never called"* and reaches [`:836`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-writes.ts)'s log line inside `err`, under a message ending `: principal` so the port member is nameable without reading the stack.

**A monitor loses the ability to page on an unwired `principal` from the wire.** It keeps it for every other port member, which still answers 503.

**This is the same trade [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) clause 3 made and it is made here for the same reason**, which is the only thing that makes it consistent rather than merely convenient: a status that lets an unauthenticated caller read deployment state is worth less than the log line that says the same thing to somebody who can already see the process.

**Whether that trade is right is the founder's**, and section 9 says so.

### 6. Clause 4, and why `server.ts` is in the fence and unedited

**Item 3 of the dispatch is the vocabulary, and it dissolves under examination in one direction and hardens in another.**

**It dissolves as a defect about titles.** [`admin-writes.ts:381`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-writes.ts)'s docstring states the arrangement deliberately: `server.ts`'s tables are closed over what the TRANSPORT can produce, and `service_unavailable` is a handler code built at the handler. **21 call sites spell the title by hand because 21 call sites are handlers**, and a module reaching into `server.ts` to borrow a sender is the thing that docstring declines. The re-derived 21 (11 declarative, 10 positional) is a bigger number than [ADR-190](ADR-190.md)'s 18 for the same reason: three more handlers were written.

**It hardens as a defect about THROWN statuses, which is [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) measurement one and is real.** A thrown error carrying `statusCode = 503` is silently rewritten to `internal_error` at [`server.ts:150`](../../apps/api/src/server.ts) with the true status logged at [`:154`](../../apps/api/src/server.ts). Nine of section 2's sixteen codes are in that position.

**The tempting repair is to add the two entries. This entry refuses it, and the refusal is argued from [ADR-190](ADR-190.md)'s own experiment.** Adding `503: 'service_unavailable'` to `STATUS_CODE` is exactly what let a class-level `statusCode` reach the wire in that session, for twelve cases that should never have carried it. **Nothing in this tree throws a 503 today.** So the entry would change no response and would arm the next class that sets one, in a file every route module shares. **A table entry that is inert until it is wrong is worse than an absence that fails loudly**, and the loud failure -- a 500 with the real status in the log -- is at least visible.

**So `server.ts` is byte for byte unchanged, and it is in the fence.** The residue is registered as item 1 with the shape of the check that would close it.

### 7. The one file outside the dispatch's list, and why

**`apps/api/test/admin-reads.test.ts`'s *"ADR-190 ruling 2"* case asserted `expect(unavailable.length).toBeGreaterThan(0)`, and clause 2 empties that set.** The dispatch's fence names `apps/api/src/routes/admin-reads.ts` as byte for byte unchanged and it is; it does not name that suite, and it names *"their suites"* meaning the four write modules'. **This entry read the ambiguity as a gap rather than a licence and states the decision here instead of in a commit message.**

**The assertion pinned a state its own entry called wrong.** [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) clause 4 says the thirteen *"are answering the wrong question"* and item 3 registers the disclosure for repair. **A control asserting that at least one route still answers 503 anonymously is a control asserting the defect persists**, and it could not survive its own item being discharged. It refused to pin the count 13 for a good reason and pinned the set's non-emptiness for no reason it stated.

**The change is a strengthening and not a relaxation, which is the only form this session would take.** `toBeGreaterThan(0)` becomes `toEqual([])`, a bound becomes an equality, and a second line asserts `authenticating` holds every `/admin/` route. **Seed A in section 8 confirms it: reintroducing the disclosure in one module fails four cases across two files, that one among them.** Everything else in the case is untouched, including its assertion that every 503 carries `code: service_unavailable` and `title: Service unavailable`.

**`apps/api/src/routes/payouts.ts` is the second file outside the list, and it is one line.** `RI-15` caught [`:87`](../../apps/api/src/routes/payouts.ts) citing `admin-payouts.ts:1192` for `ledger` after this session's insertion moved it 18 lines to `:1210`. Repointing it is the mechanical consequence the invariant exists to force; leaving it would have shipped a red gate. **No prose in that file changed.**

### 9. Approval

**Approval line: UNSIGNED.**

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.**

**Clause 2 spends something real and the entry cannot decide it is worth it.** An operator meeting a half-wired deployment is now told 401 and cannot tell that from being logged out; a monitor cannot page on an unwired `principal` from the wire. **The entry argues this is the same trade [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) clause 3 already made**, and consistency with an unsigned entry is a weaker argument than it reads. A founder who thinks an operator's ability to diagnose their own deployment outweighs an unauthenticated caller's ability to enumerate its unwired ports should reverse clause 2, and the reversal is the four `catch` blocks, in one commit.

**Clause 1 reverses a clause of an unsigned entry, which is the widest thing here.** [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) section 8 named this reversal and its condition, so the reversal is licensed by that entry rather than taken against it, and the ground is a count rather than a weighting. **But 17 of 17 is a count of what the classes do TODAY.** The day somebody throws an `AdminWriteUnwired` for a data refusal, the four classes acquire `AdminReadError`'s problem and clause 1 is retroactively wrong. **Nothing checks that**, and the check that would is a sweep asserting every `new *Unwired` sits inside an `UNWIRED_*` constant. It is item 3.

**Section 7 is a fence judgement and it is the one this entry least wants to make alone.** `admin-reads.test.ts` is not in the dispatch's list either way. The entry read *"admin-reads.ts is byte for byte unchanged"* as naming the source file, which it does, and changed one assertion in the suite because discharging [ADR-190](ADR-190.md)'s registered item makes it fail. **The alternative was to leave `main` red or to not discharge the item**, and neither is obviously worse than touching an unlisted file. A founder who reads that as a widened fence is reading it the way the constitution's sentence invites, and the entry would rather be told now.

**Clause 4 declines a repair that a plain reading of [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) item 4 asks for.** That item calls the missing `STATUS_CODE` entry a latent defect; this entry calls it a stated design plus a real but different residue, and refuses the two-line fix on the ground that it arms rather than disarms. **That is a judgement about a file every route module shares**, and if it is wrong the cost is that nine of sixteen codes stay unreachable by a throw for another wave.

**What would make this safe to sign without a line-by-line read:** clause 2 and clause 5 are executed by a suite over a real `compose()`, so *"no operator route discloses its deployment state"* is checkable in one command and five seeds show it failing. **Clause 1 is not enforced by anything**, and the check that would make it load bearing is item 3.

### 11. Alternatives considered

- **Move the thirteen to 500, joining the ten.** Refused on the count at finding 5, which is [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) clause 2's own reasoning applied to classes that do not have the property it reasoned about. It would answer *this process is broken* about a deployment that is merely not finished, which is [`kyc.ts:337`](../../apps/api/src/routes/kyc.ts)'s sentence and right there.
- **Split the class.** Refused, section 3. The split already exists at the module boundary: these four classes hold one fact each. A fifth class would be a second thing four handlers must choose between correctly, to separate facts that are not mixed.
- **Leave the disclosure and register it again.** Refused. [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) registered it because four modules were outside its fence; they are inside this one, and re-registering an item whose files you hold is the move [ADR-190](ADR-190.md) item 5 shows compounding.
- **Read a cookie before consulting the backend, as [`admin-reads.ts`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-reads.ts) does.** Refused, section 4. It would be four route modules asserting a credential shape their own port deliberately abstracts, and it would answer 503 to a caller who authenticates by a header this guess did not know about.
- **Answer 401 only when the request carries no `Cookie` and no `Authorization` header, keeping the 503 for callers who present something.** Refused, and it is the closest rejected alternative. It preserves the operator's diagnostic and closes the anonymous case. **But it makes the disclosure conditional on sending one junk header**, which is not a control, and it still guesses at credential shape by picking two header names.
- **Add `service_unavailable` to `STATUS_CODE` and `TITLE`.** Refused, clause 4 and section 6. It changes no response today and arms the next class that sets a status.
- **Declare `service_unavailable` on the sixteen operator endpoints in `API_CONTRACT`.** Refused: outside the fence, which licenses that file only if the ruling moves a declared code. Registered as item 2 with the endpoints named.
- **Revert [ADR-190](ADR-190.md)'s partition assertion instead of strengthening it.** Refused, section 7. A control that pins a defect must move when the defect is repaired, and the move must be to a stronger claim or it is a gate weakened to pass.
