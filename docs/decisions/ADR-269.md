## ADR-269: a blocker whose every term is spent while its figure stays absent was stated one layer too high, the fold that names the layer underneath, and a liability figure that says which of its terms are measured  (2026-08-30, status: proposed)

**[ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `269` sent this session to re-derive one sentence before building anything.** `test/wiring.test.ts`'s `setAdminReadSource` entry said `readLiability` is *"UNBUILT rather than blocked: its book reader, its seven-day horizon and its payout-velocity evaluator all exist and nothing folds them into one body, and the figure holding that fold is `eligible_next_7d`, whose last term is a `writeRuleState` implementation under `apps/worker/**` or `packages/**`."*

**THE LAST CLAUSE IS FALSE.** It is false by measurement rather than by argument, and section 1 is the measurement. **AND THE FIGURE IS STILL NOT ON THE WIRE**, which is the finding this entry exists for: a clearing condition whose every term is spent while its figure stays absent was stated one layer too high.

- **Origin:** row `269`, session **460**, branch `claude/the-figure-nobody-folds`. **NO MIGRATION IS WRITTEN, RESERVED OR SPENT.** The highest is `0074` and it stays there; `git diff origin/main -- packages/db/migrations` is empty on this branch.

- **MONEY PATH BY WHAT IT REPORTS: this is the firm's liability, the number that says what Merit owes.** `eligible_next_7d.total_cents` is the figure the payout wallet is funded against ([EC-074](../edge-cases/EC-074.md), `P-M6-02`, [ADR-011](ADR-011.md)'s top-up trigger). A zero read off an unrun batch says the firm owes nothing on the morning it may owe the most. The [**`E2`**](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) read is **OWED** on this diff. Ships `proposed` with an **UNSIGNED** approval line. **Merging is not signing.**

- **AND IT INHERITS FIVE `E2` DEBTS AS WELL AS OWING ONE.** [ADR-204](ADR-204.md), [ADR-206](ADR-206.md), [ADR-208](ADR-208.md), [ADR-250](ADR-250.md) and [ADR-264](ADR-264.md) are each `proposed` and UNSIGNED, and the fold in section 3 stands on all five: 204 ruled the projection, 206 declared the stored encoding, 208 gave the response its `| null`, 250 wrote the codec and 264 built the reader. **A founder who moves any of them moves this one with them.**

- **THE PORT IS NOT WIRED AND NOTHING HERE MOVES IT.** `setAdminReadSource` still reduces to `setAdminSessionSource`, [`apps/api/src/start.ts`](../../apps/api/src/start.ts) is untouched, `IMPLEMENTED_ADMIN_READS` does not gain `readLiability`, and `composeAdminReadSource` still fills the gap with `AdminSourceNotComposed('readLiability')`. **The row said narrow the entry and do not wire the port, and the honest blocker in section 4 would have forced that even if it had not.**

- **WHAT IS MEASURED, WHAT IS DERIVED, AND WHAT IS JUDGED**, separated before the argument starts. That the `writeRuleState` term is spent is **MEASURED**, at four files, in section 1. That `projectPayout`'s five inputs reduce to one unproducible term in this fence is **DERIVED**, from two closed lists, in section 4. That `EligibleNext7d` declares no measured term is **MEASURED**, off the contract, in section 5. What is **JUDGED** is that an empty `rule_states` is a refusal and a partial fold is not (section 6), that the plan decoder is registered rather than taken (section 4), and that the basis rides on the module's value because the wire has no field for it (section 5).

### 2. The finding, which is what a fully spent condition means

`RI-19` exists because `B5`'s clearing condition was once stated twice with two of its three terms each. That defect is fixed. **THIS IS A DIFFERENT ONE AND NO CONTROL IN THIS TREE LOOKS FOR IT:** every term of a clearing condition can be spent while the thing it was blocking is exactly as absent as it was.

**ALL THREE TERMS WERE ABOUT GETTING A ROW.** Write the row, declare how the bag inside it is encoded, and let the response decline where there is no answer. Not one of them was about what stands between a row and a rendered number. A condition written from inside the blocker sees the next obstacle and calls it the last one; there is no vantage point inside the module from which the layer underneath is visible.

**THE CHEAPEST CONTROL IS THE ONE THIS SESSION ALREADY PAID FOR, AND IT FAILED.** `test/admin-read-constructibility.test.ts` carried a case whose own comment read *"the day a real implementation lands, this case goes red and says which figure it unblocks."* It did not go red. What it asserted was `expect(liability).toContain('writeRuleState')` -- **a string inside a comment of the module it was reading**. `writeRuleStateVia` landed in session 395 and the case stayed green for five sessions, because it pinned the blocker's prose rather than the tree. That case is repointed in this diff and both halves are now read at their own sources, and section 8 records the class.

### 3. The fold, built

[`apps/api/src/admin-source/eligible-next-7d.ts`](../../apps/api/src/admin-source/eligible-next-7d.ts) is the body the entry said did not exist. `readEligibleNext7d(tx, calendarTx, io, asOf)`:

1. **The calendar first, and the order is the dependency order.** `readTradingHorizon` and `readCalendarSlice`, both already in `liability.ts`. An uncovered estate refuses `calendar_uncovered` and an exhausted one refuses `horizon_exhausted`, **before a single account row is read**, so an unloaded calendar can never be reported as an unrun batch. Those are two different mornings and only one is an engineering problem.
2. **The funded population**, at the accessor: `rowsWhere('accounts', { phase: 'funded' })`.
3. **The state**, through `ruleStateOn` ([ADR-264](ADR-264.md)). `RuleStateAbsent` takes the account out of the covered population; `RuleStateUnreadable` is rethrown, because a malformed row silently skipped is a liability quietly missing an account whose row exists.
4. **The gates**, through the engine's own `resolveExternalGates` over five raw columns and two whole chains. Every value handed over is the raw column, on `adapter.ts`'s stated reason: narrowing `accounts.status` here would be a second place the seven-versus-six question is answered.
5. **`projectPayout`**, per account, over the horizon and slice. **Nothing in this module recomputes a gate**, which is `admin-source/account.ts`'s directory rule; the engine decides and this module folds what comes back.
6. **[ADR-204](ADR-204.md) ruling 3 is honoured by the shape**: one bucket per horizon day, and an account lands in at most one of them. `sum(by_day[].cents)` equals `total_cents` and the suite asserts it, because the literal reading puts the series at up to seven times the total and turns an arriving wave into a flat line.

**CENTS ARE `bigint` FROM THE ROW TO THE WIRE.** `centsOf` deliberately does not return a `number` where `liability.ts`'s `cents` does, and the header says why: this value is summed across a whole funded population before anything renders it, so a fold that narrows first is a fold that rounds first. `toWireEligibleNext7d` is the one narrowing and it refuses past 2^53 rather than rounding.

### 4. What actually blocks it, and it is one input of five

`PayoutProjectionInput` takes `state`, `plan`, `gates`, `calendar` and `horizon`. **Four are producible in this fence today and the fifth is not.**

| Input | State |
|---|---|

| `horizon` | `readTradingHorizon`, session 377 |

**A SECOND ONE WRITTEN HERE WOULD BE `FM-16` ON THE BLOB THAT FIXES EVERY CENTS VALUE AND EVERY GATE THRESHOLD A PAYOUT IS DECIDED AGAINST**, with nothing comparing the two. **SO THE TERM IS A PORT.** `EligibleFoldIo.resolvePinnedPlan`, injected, whose unwired default `UNWIRED_ELIGIBLE_FOLD_IO` throws `EligibleFoldUnwired` naming the member and citing the ruling. That is `RuleStateWriterIo.encodeEngineGates`'s own shape, chosen for its own reason: **a term that cannot be taken inside a fence is visible as an injected function and invisible as a paragraph.** A default `ResolvedPlan` would have been the worst value in the file, because every cents threshold a projection clears sits on it.

### 5. The trap the row named, and the wire cannot carry the answer

**`eligible_next_7d` IS A FORECAST OVER SEVEN TRADING DAYS AND EVERY INPUT IS A PROJECTION.** [ADR-204](ADR-204.md)'s five assumptions are what make it computable at all, and its own caveat says what the figure is: *"A LOWER BOUND ON THE POPULATION, AND NOT A BOUND ON THE MONEY."* The row's words for the failure are the right ones: a liability number that reads as measured when it is projected is worse than no number, on a surface an operator uses to decide whether the firm can pay.

**AND THE WIRE TYPE HAS NOWHERE TO PUT THAT.** `EligibleNext7d` declares exactly three members -- `total_cents`, `account_count`, `by_day[]` -- and **not one of them is a measurement**. There is no measured term on that type, so there is no field a basis could ride on and no pair of fields a reader could tell apart. **This is not an omission this fence may repair**: `RI-18` binds that shape atomically across `API_CONTRACT.md`, `routes/admin-reads.ts` and `apps/admin/src/api/types.ts`, all three outside the row's fence, and widening a fence to finish is the thing that is never done.

**SO THE BASIS RIDES ON THE MODULE'S OWN VALUE, WHICH IS THE FIGURE AT THIS FENCE, AND IT RIDES AS DATA RATHER THAN AS PROSE.** `ELIGIBLE_FIGURE_TERMS` carries fourteen entries, each a term with a `basis` of `measured` or `projected`, the row or function it came from, and the `ADR-204` assumption ids that make a projection one.

| MEASURED | PROJECTED |
|---|---|
| `as_of_trading_day` (`R-06`'s last closed day) | `total_cents` |
| `population.funded`, `.covered`, `.uncovered` | `account_count` |
| `by_day[].trading_day` (rows of `trading_calendar`) | `by_day[].cents`, `by_day[].accounts` |
| `input.state`, `input.plan`, `input.gates`, `input.calendar` | `input.gates_forward` (A3) |

**THE TABLE IS BIDIRECTIONAL OR IT IS DECORATION.** The suite walks the produced figure's own leaves against these entries in both directions, so a field added to the figure and not to the table is a red case and never a number with no stated basis. `toWireEligibleNext7d` drops all of it, and that function's docblock says at its own declaration that a caller rendering its output alone is rendering a forecast as a measurement.

**RULING 7 IS WHY THIS IS A TERM OF THE CLEARING CONDITION AND NOT A PREFERENCE.** [ADR-204](ADR-204.md) ruling 7 requires both halves of the figure to be stated wherever it is shown. A wire that cannot state them cannot show the figure, so `B5`'s replacement condition has two terms and this is the second.

### 6. Two zeroes that are not the same zero

**THE ROW'S SECOND TRAP: `rule_states` may hold no rows in a given deployment, and an empty table is a refusal and never a zero liability.**

Folding an empty table sums to `0`. That is a number an operator funds a payout wallet against. So `readEligibleNext7d` answers `refused` with cause `no_folded_state`, `awaiting` naming the run that would clear it, and a detail carrying the count of funded accounts that have no folded state on the anchor day. **A row that exists for a DIFFERENT day is the same refusal**, because `R-06` permits one day and there is no `latest` arm anywhere on this path.

**A GENUINELY EMPTY FUNDED POPULATION IS A MEASURED ZERO AND IS PRODUCED.** `accounts` was read and it held none; `population.funded` says `0` and the term is `measured`. An operator reading "0 of 0 funded accounts" cannot mistake it for an unrun batch. **The two produce the same `0` and mean opposite things, and what tells them apart is a measured term on the figure rather than a comment.**

**A PARTIAL FOLD IS PRODUCED AND THIS IS A JUDGEMENT.** An account funded today has no `daily_marks` row, so the nightly batch does not consider it and no `rule_states` row exists for it; a deployment where every funded account is covered is the exception rather than the rule. **Refusing the whole figure for one uncovered account would leave the operator with nothing on every ordinary day**, which is `apps/admin/src/figure.ts`'s bar failed from the other side. So the figure is folded over the covered population and `population.uncovered` is a measured term of it. `EC-074`'s complaint is about a number that does not say it is short; this one says so.

**THE ALTERNATIVE AND ITS COST, STATED RATHER THAN HIDDEN.** Refusing on any shortfall is the safer direction on a liability figure and a founder may prefer it. What it costs is a permanently refusing panel in any live deployment, which is a different way of telling an operator nothing.

### 8. The class of defect, which is the part worth carrying forward

**A CASE CAN PIN A BLOCKER'S PROSE INSTEAD OF THE BLOCKER, AND IT READS AS A CONTROL FROM EVERY ANGLE.** `test/admin-read-constructibility.test.ts` promised a red on the day the writer landed and asserted a string in a comment of the module it read. It passed for five sessions after the thing it watched had happened.

**THIS IS `RI-15` AND `RI-16`'s CONCERN ONE LEVEL UP.** Those checks catch a `file:line` pointer that has drifted off what the sentence names. What neither catches is a citation to a CLAIM rather than to a line: `expect(liability).toContain('writeRuleState')` is true of a file that says the term is missing and of a file that says it landed. **A sentence and its subject are different objects, and a case that greps the sentence is measuring the wrong one.**

The general form, offered rather than ruled: **a case pinning a clearing condition should assert the TERM at its own source and never the condition's text.** Whether that is checkable mechanically is a question for a session with a fence over `packages/tooling`, and this entry does not take it.

**AND THE INSERTION LANDMINE FIRED AGAIN, FOR THE FOURTH SESSION RUNNING.** Adding paragraphs to `liability.ts`'s header moved `rowsWhere('reconciliations', ...)` from `:1193` to `:1212` and turned `RI-15` red on a citation in `wiring.test.ts`. It is repaired as a NAME rather than a number, on [ADR-212](ADR-212.md), because that pointer has now been broken twice by lines inserted above it.
