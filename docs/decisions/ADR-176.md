## ADR-176: the request path records the approval and does not post it, and the key it must store to make that safe (2026-08-28, status: proposed)

**[ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `176` says the ruling is SETTLED and the work is the verification. It is, and the verification found one thing the ruling needed and [ADR-172](ADR-172.md) did not see.** Clause 2 says `PayoutTx` must not carry a `LedgerTx`. Removing that field alone would have committed an approval carrying nothing a later door could post it under: **`PayoutRequestInsert` had no `idempotencyKey` member, and `payout_requests.idempotency_key` is `text NOT NULL`.** While the posting stood inside the handler the key reached it out of local memory, so the gap was invisible. **The removal and that field are one change, and either one without the other is worse than neither.**

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `176`, session **316**, dispatched as `claude/payout-tx-ledger`. **MONEY PATH.** [`E2`](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) read owed. **No migration number was allocated and none is needed**; section 5 checks that at `0010`.
- **NOTHING BELOW IS INHERITED FROM [ADR-172](ADR-172.md) OR FROM THE DISPATCH.** Every premise in section 1 was re-derived at its own file and line in this tree, on this branch, and two of them came back different from the summaries that carried them. A settled ruling is still a ruling about a tree, and the tree is what is read.
- **No port is wired by this entry.** `apps/api/test/wiring.test.ts`'s `{declared, wired, blocked}` triple stays `{21, 6, 15}`. No `SqlExecutorReason` member, no `SystemReason` member, no `pg` import, no cast past a key type, and `packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts` is untouched.

### 2. THE RULING

> **Clause 1. `PayoutTx` CARRIES NO `LedgerTx`, AND THE POSTING ARM IS REMOVED FROM `decidePayout`.**
>
> [ADR-172](ADR-172.md) clause 2 applied where it was reported. Findings 2 to 6 compose to the same conclusion independently of that entry: the only value satisfying `LedgerTx` is `insert<K extends TableKey>` over every table Merit holds, so a door in `apps/api` returning one is `systemDb` renamed and hands the trader surface estate-wide write authority. **The request path RECORDS the approval. The posting is performed at a system authority.**

> **Clause 2. `PayoutRequestInsert` GAINS `idempotencyKey`, AND THIS CLAUSE IS THE PRICE OF CLAUSE 1 RATHER THAN AN ADDITION TO IT.**
>
> Findings 10, 11 and 12. `ledger_transactions.idempotency_key` is `text NOT NULL UNIQUE` and every door that posts `LT-01` composes `` `${PAYOUT_ENDPOINT} ${key}` `` from `payout_requests.idempotency_key`. While the posting stood in the handler, the key reached it from a local variable and the insert shape never needed the field; `admin-payouts.ts` was already reading a column nothing on this path wrote. **Remove the posting without adding the field and an approval commits that NO door can ever post, and the failure is silent.**
>
> **IT IS STORED UNPREFIXED.** The column is the client's token, on `payout_requests_account_idempotency_uq`'s own sentence: *"the same key on the same account is the same request"*. The `PAYOUT_ENDPOINT` prefix belongs to the LEDGER key each door composes, and storing the prefixed string would double-prefix every posting.

> **Clause 3. `INV-M5-06` SURVIVES BY CONSTRUCTION, AND THAT IS SHOWN AT THREE FILES RATHER THAN ASSERTED.**
>
> Finding 10 read all three doors and both remaining call sites. The key is a property of the APPROVAL: it is minted before the first send, persisted in a column, and re-derived identically by whichever door reaches the row. **Removing one of three doors that mint the identical string cannot produce a second posting**, and the DATABASE refuses a duplicate rather than application memory that forgets on a restart. Clause 2 is what keeps that true, which is why the two clauses are one change.

> **Clause 4. `usePayoutBackend` STAYS BLOCKED, AND ITS REASON IS REWRITTEN RATHER THAN SHRUNK.**
>
> The recorded reason was `PayoutTx.ledger` and clause 1 deleted it, so the reason is now false. **The port did not become wireable; a different obstruction was underneath the one that moved, and it is a READ rather than a write.** Findings 14 to 16: `RuleState` is not persistable in this schema, which a WIRED sibling already refuses on for the same three fields, and `ResolvedPlan` is behind the `firm` door while `PayoutTx` runs every method on one transaction.
>
> **A PARTIAL BACKEND IS REFUSED RATHER THAN OVERLOOKED.** `listPayouts` and `idempotency` are both constructible today. Installing them beside a `transact` that rejects would put a live-looking route in front of the arm that approves payouts, which `UNWIRED_PAYOUT_BACKEND`'s own docstring already refuses: *"a backend that returned plausible values would be a fixture serving real traffic, and on THIS route it would be a fixture approving payouts"*.

> **Clause 5. THE `LT-01` DRIVER IS OWED, IS NAMED, AND IS NOT BUILT HERE. THE GAP IT LEAVES IS UNREACHABLE, AND THAT IS A CONTROL RATHER THAN A HOPE.**
>
> The two remaining doors both fire on a HELD request being released, so **nothing in this tree posts `LT-01` for a request approved with no hold.** [ADR-172](ADR-172.md) section 5 names that driver and checked at `0010` that it needs no DDL. This entry does not build it: it is `apps/worker/src/sweeps/`, a different deployable, and [ADR-003](ADR-003.md)'s money-path regime is one objective per session.
>
> **The gap is not reachable in any deployment.** `usePayoutBackend` is unwired by clause 4, `wiring.test.ts` asserts `start.ts` does not call it, and the route answers 503 before it reaches a transaction. **The route answered 503 before this ruling and answers 503 after it**, so nothing a running Merit can do changed. What changed is that the port stopped asking for a handle no deployment can supply.
>
> **`INV-M5-21`'s ENFORCEMENT MOVES WITH THE POSTING AND IS STATED WHERE THE DRIVER WILL BE WRITTEN.** A held request must post nothing. At request time nothing posts at all now, so the invariant holds here trivially and **binds on the driver, which must select on `status = 'approved'`**. That is recorded in `decidePayout` and in the suite rather than left to be rediscovered.

### 7. Approval

**Approval line: PENDING, UNSIGNED. MONEY PATH, [`E2`](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) READ OWED.**

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.** Every fact in section 1 is a `grep` or a `sed` and needs no judgement, and section 3's seeded mutations were watched failing. **The three questions below are judgements**, and the first two are choices about what a stored value MEANS rather than defects to repair.

1. **Clause 2: is the client's token stored unprefixed?** This entry says yes, on the unique index's own sentence. **A founder who wants the prefixed string stored instead is choosing to make `payout_requests.idempotency_key` mean something different from `wallet_withdrawals.idempotency_key`**, and every door would then have to stop composing and start trusting.
2. **Section 3: does `API_CONTRACT` section 6's ordered sentence get amended?** The wire shape does not change and the prose now names a step this route does not perform. **Amending a frozen document is an ADR, and this one asks rather than takes it.**
3. **Clause 5: is the `LT-01` driver allocated a number now?** It is named, unbuilt, and unreachable. **The day `usePayoutBackend` becomes wireable it stops being unreachable**, and findings 14 to 16 are what stand between here and there.
