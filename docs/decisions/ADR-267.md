## ADR-267: the remedy does not transfer, because LT-01 credits the wallet and LT-06 debits it, and the corpus separates the two legs by exactly that fact  (2026-08-30, status: proposed)

**[ADR-232](ADR-232.md) section 6 named the obstruction, [ADR-263](ADR-263.md) took the lockout half of it off `useWithdrawalBackend`'s entry, and what is left is one edge: nothing drives `requested --> approved`, the approval posts `LT-06`, and no door in `apps/api` may open the ledger authority a posting needs.** A session arriving here reads that sentence and reaches for [ADR-176](ADR-176.md), which cleared an obstruction of the same shape on the payout route by **deleting `PayoutTx.ledger`, recording the approval on the request path and posting `LT-01` later at a system authority**. This entry is the ruling on whether that reach is sound.

**IT IS NOT, AND THE ANSWER IS NOT AN ANALOGY IN EITHER DIRECTION.** [ADR-238](ADR-238.md) section 6 already found the same remedy does not transfer to `LT-08`, because [M20](../plans/M20-wallet.md) pins that posting to the purchase transaction **by name** and `DEP-M20-02` states the consequence of moving it. `LT-06` has no `DEP-M20-02`. What it has is a different pin in the same two documents, and it is stronger rather than weaker: **`LT-01` CREDITS the wallet and `LT-06` DEBITS it**, `INV-M20-01` binds every wallet debit to the transaction that checks it against the live position, and M20 section 3.3a separates the payout hold from the wallet halt by whether the posting has happened and calls that separation *"the whole of the difference between the two legs"*.

**THE PORT STAYS BLOCKED AND THE HONEST `503` STAYS.** That is the deliverable rather than a shortfall against it: the row that dispatched this session said so in terms, and a withdrawal approval that posts nothing is a trader told their money is approved while the ledger says it never moved.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `267`, session **458**, dispatched as `claude/does-the-lt-01-remedy-transfer`. **NO MIGRATION NUMBER IS TAKEN OR RESERVED.** The highest on disk is `0074`, this ruling adds no column and no constraint, and section 5 is the honest account of the one place the database is silent.

- **MONEY PATH: this is a trader's money leaving the wallet. `E2` READ IS OWED AND NOTHING HERE DISCHARGES IT.** Ships `proposed` with an **UNSIGNED** approval line. **Merging is not signing.** It inherits the `E2` debt on [ADR-176](ADR-176.md), [ADR-232](ADR-232.md) and [ADR-263](ADR-263.md), each `proposed` and each UNSIGNED, because it rules on the seam all three meet at.

- **NOTHING BELOW IS INHERITED FROM THE DISPATCH.** Every premise in section 1 was re-derived at its own file and line on this tree. **Two came back different from the sentences that carried them**, and both are recorded rather than quietly used: the row dispatching this session cites the `LT-08` finding as *"[ADR-238](ADR-238.md) ruling 3"*, and it is **ruling 7, in that entry's section 6**; and `wallet-withdrawals.ts`'s own header stated `LT-06` *"credits `firm_treasury`"*, which [ADR-180](ADR-180.md) had already ruled false before that line was written.

- **WHAT IS MEASURED, WHAT IS DERIVED, AND WHAT IS JUDGED.** That `LT-06` debits `trader_wallet` and `LT-01` credits it, that `INV-M20-01` carries the words *"inside the same transaction"*, that M20 section 3.3a separates the two holds by the posting, that `wallet_withdrawals.idempotency_key` is stored by the create path, that `0057`'s `WD-C1` watches only the three terminal statuses, and that `gateNoInFlight` appears in one file and not on the wallet-spend path are all **MEASURED**, by [`apps/api/test/lt06-posting-timing.test.ts`](../../apps/api/test/lt06-posting-timing.test.ts), watched red under five seeded defects. That the deferral window is reachable by a wallet spend is **DERIVED** from M20 section 3.1's diagram, `INV-M3-13` and `lockScope`'s implementation. What is **JUDGED** is that these pins bind (section 2 clauses 2 and 3), that the shape which does work is a transition and a posting together at a system authority rather than a split (clause 5), and that the port stays blocked (clause 6).

### 2. THE RULING

> **Clause 1. [ADR-176](ADR-176.md)'s REMEDY DOES NOT TRANSFER TO `LT-06`, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS THE DIRECTION OF THE WALLET LEG.**
>
> Findings 1, 2 and 3. `LT-01` credits `trader_wallet`; `LT-06` debits it. **A deferred CREDIT understates a position and a deferred DEBIT overstates one**, and every gate in this estate that lets money out reads the position to decide. An understated wallet refuses more than it should, which is a delay. An overstated wallet lets out money that has already been promised elsewhere, which is a second payment.
>
> **THAT IS THE WHOLE ARGUMENT AND IT IS AN ARGUMENT ABOUT THE POSTINGS RATHER THAN ABOUT THE ENTRIES THAT RULED THEM.** "It worked for `LT-01`" is not available, and neither is "it failed for `LT-08`": [ADR-238](ADR-238.md) ruling 7 failed on a pin `LT-06` does not have, and finding 15 is that even the pointer to it was wrong. The pins that bind here are clauses 2 and 3.

> **Clause 2. `INV-M20-01` IS `LT-06`'s PIN, IT NAMES THE TRANSACTION, AND ITS CONSEQUENCE IS ITS OWN FIRST WORDS.**
>
> Findings 4 and 5. *"Every debit is checked against the live position inside the same transaction"*, and `LT-06` is a wallet debit on one of the position's two drawn exits. Under the remedy, `gateFunds` runs on the request path inside `lockScope()` and the debit posts from a second transaction at a second authority. **The check and the debit are then in two transactions, which is the thing the invariant forbids in the words it forbids it in.**
>
> **THE CONSEQUENCE IS STATED WHERE `DEP-M20-02` STATES `LT-08`'s, IN THE SAME SENTENCE AS THE RULE**: *"a wallet balance is never negative"*. Finding 6 makes the failure concrete rather than notional, because the lock is real and it is released at `COMMIT`. A trader with 20,000 cents requests a 20,000 cent withdrawal, which is approved and unposted; a wallet-funded purchase takes the same lock, reads a position that still holds the cents and debits them under `INV-M3-13`; the deferred door then finds nothing to debit. **Either the wallet goes negative or the approval is unpostable forever, and the second is the trap the dispatching row named.**

> **Clause 3. AND THE CORPUS ALREADY DREW THIS DISTINCTION, BY HAND, IN A TWO-COLUMN TABLE.**
>
> Finding 8. M20 section 3.3a compares the payout hold with the wallet halt and separates them on a row called `Ledger at entry`: **nothing posted** against **`LT-06` already posted**. [ADR-176](ADR-176.md)'s remedy IS *"a decision that has posted nothing"*. M20 names that shape, pins it to the internal leg, and calls the separation *"the whole of the difference between the two legs"*.
>
> **APPLYING THE REMEDY HERE DOES NOT BEND THAT SENTENCE, IT DELETES IT.** And the deletion is not decorative: the same table's next two rows are `INV-M20-14`, *"Release means: **resume the rail.** Never re-pay"* and *"Enforcement means: the money is gone; recovery is a dispute, not a reversal"*. **The halt rides on any pre-terminal status** ([STATE_MACHINES section 3.2](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md)'s *"any pre-terminal --> halted in place"*), so `approved` is exactly where it acts. A halt landing in the deferral window would be holding a **claim** while every rule written about it says it is holding a **transfer**.
>
> **AND FINDING 9 IS THE OTHER HALF OF THE SAME DRAWING.** The corpus contains one sentence blessing a deferred ledger posting, *"Only the ledger posting is deferred"*, it is in M05's hold branch, and [ADR-176](ADR-176.md)'s remedy is that existing shape widened from the hold branch to the request path. **M20 has no such sentence and has the opposite one.**

> **Clause 4. WHAT REFUSES IS THE POSTING'S TIMING, AND THIS IS SHOWN BY THE HALF THAT IS ALREADY PAID.**
>
> Finding 12. [ADR-176](ADR-176.md) was two clauses because one without the other was worse than neither: deleting the handle while `PayoutRequestInsert` carried no `idempotencyKey` would have committed approvals no door could ever post, silently. **On this leg that half is already done.** The column has been `NOT NULL` since `0011`, `decideWithdrawal` writes it, and `reversal.ts` already names it as what `LT-06` posts under, bare.
>
> **SO THE REMEDY FAILS HERE WITH ITS PRICE ALREADY PAID, WHICH IS THE CLEANEST EVIDENCE AVAILABLE THAT THE OBSTRUCTION IS NEITHER A KEY NOR A HANDLE.** A successor reaching for a third door, a widened `SystemReason` or a stored key is reaching past the thing that refuses.

> **Clause 5. THE SHAPE THAT DOES WORK IS NAMED AND IS NOT BUILT HERE, AND IT IS A MOVE RATHER THAN A SPLIT.**
>
> The driver performs `requested --> approved` **and** posts `LT-06` **in one transaction, at a system authority**. That is not [ADR-176](ADR-176.md)'s remedy with different nouns: ADR-176 SPLIT the approval from the posting and left the approval in `apps/api`. **This moves both out.** `decideApproval` is already total and pure and `driveApprovals` already runs under `lockScope()`, so what is owed is a caller in `apps/worker/src/sweeps/` holding a port in `ExpiryLedgerPort.postLt01`'s shape, on the handle [ADR-165](ADR-165.md) already granted that deployable.
>
> **NO NEW `SystemReason` MEMBER IS NEEDED AND NONE IS TAKEN.** Finding 14. A sweep is `'nightly-batch'` exactly, on clause 3's own words.
>
> **IT IS NOT STARTED HERE.** `apps/worker` is a different deployable, the slice needs `decideApproval`'s six inputs reachable from a system handle, and [ADR-003](ADR-003.md)'s money-path regime is one objective per session. **This entry rules the question and cuts no code in that deployable.**

> **Clause 6. `useWithdrawalBackend` STAYS BLOCKED, AND ITS ENTRY IS NARROWED RATHER THAN LENGTHENED.**
>
> The route answered `503` before this ruling and answers `503` after it, so nothing a running Merit can do changed. **What changed is what the entry is waiting for.** Its approval clause read as an absence, a door nobody had built, which is a clause a wiring slice could hope to discharge. It is now a ruling: **no `apps/api` door can discharge it at all**, because the discharge is a driver in another deployable. That is the seventh correction to that entry and the first that makes it smaller.

### 5. Where the database is silent, stated rather than left to be found

  **What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.**

  1. **IS `INV-M20-01` A RULE ABOUT THE WITHDRAWAL LEG, OR ONLY ABOUT THE SPEND LEG?** This is the single judgement the whole ruling rests on.
