IT IS NOT A PURCHASE.**

**TERM 1 IS A COMPOSITION, IT IS SMALLER THAN EVERY EARLIER RECORD PRICED IT, AND ITS CHEAPEST HOME IS STILL NOT HERE.** What it needs is a supplier of `EligibleFoldIo.resolvePinnedPlan`, which is the account's pinned `plan_versions.rules` decoded and its `plan_version_sizes` row read, resolved through the engine's `resolvePlan`. **SO TERM 1 IS ANOTHER ROW'S, ITS HOME IS A SMALL RULING RATHER THAN A LARGE ONE, AND IT IS NOT A PURCHASE.** [ADR-407](ADR-407.md) section 11 item 2 said its home is arguable and declined to rule it; this row agrees, adds the measurement that makes option 2 the cheap one, and declines it for the same reason that entry did.
