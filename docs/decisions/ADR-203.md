### 3. The absence cannot sit on a member, and this is PROVED by a guard rather than argued

**RULING 1. THE ABSENCE IS A PROPERTY OF THE FIGURE AND SITS AT THE OBJECT. No `*_cents` or `*_bp` member on this response becomes nullable, now or by any later ruling that does not first move the scalar sweep.**

`assertContractScalars` runs over every admin body at the shared handler, and its money branch admits a `*_cents` or `*_bp` key only when the value is a `Number.isSafeInteger`. **`last_7d_cents: null` throws.** Seeded into the sweep directly and watched: `{ payout_velocity: { last_7d_cents: null } }` and `{ payout_velocity: { ratio_bp: null } }` each raise `AdminReadError`, and `{ payout_velocity: null, per_plan: [{ cusum: null }] }` passes.

**This is worth stating as a ruling rather than as a note, because it eliminates a whole family of options before any preference is expressed.** The obvious minimal answer to `B2` -- null just the two numbers that need a window and keep `alarm` -- **is not a shape this contract can serve at all**, whatever an entry rules about it. It would pass `RI-18`, pass the typechecker, and produce a 500 the first time a calendar was uncovered. **That is the same conclusion `ADR-202` reached for `cusum` by argument, arrived at here by a guard, and the two agreeing is evidence rather than a coincidence:** the corpus decided years of sessions ago that a money field is an integer, and "absent" is not one.

**It also settles the `absorbed_corrections_cents` question nobody asked.** That field is signed and could in principle want an absence of its own. It cannot have one in this form, and it does not need one: it is a column of a row that either exists or does not, and when the row does not exist `readLiability` returns `null` and the route answers 404.

### 4. The form, and the null that cannot say why is beaten rather than adopted

**RULING 2. `payout_velocity` and `per_plan[].cusum` become `{...} | null`, the key is ALWAYS PRESENT, and `null` is the absence.** The members of each object are unchanged in name and in type, so [ADR-167](ADR-167.md) clause 4's integer basis points and `API_CONTRACT`'s *"neither cents nor basis points"* note on the CUSUM both stand exactly as written. For `cusum` this is `ADR-202` ruling 3 transcribed; for `payout_velocity` it is new and it is the same form for the same reasons.

**An omitted key is refused, on `ADR-198` clause 2 one type over**: *"encoding an absence as a number would make 'no figure' and 'zero cents' the same row forever."* A `null` is a value the response asserts; a missing key is a value it withholds, and a client cannot tell *"this figure is blocked"* from *"this deployment did not fill the field."*

**RULING 3. NO `null` TRAVELS ALONE. `LiabilityResponse` gains `gaps`, a required array carrying one entry per absent figure, and a `null` is admissible only when `gaps` names its path.** Pairing is enforced in BOTH directions, at the one function that builds this body:

> A `null` with no entry naming it is **refused**. A `gaps` entry naming a path that is NOT null is **refused**. `gaps` is `[]` on a complete response and is never omitted.

**THE BARE NULL IS THE OPTION THIS RULING EXISTS TO BEAT AND IT IS BEATEN ON `B2`'s OWN EVIDENCE RATHER THAN ON PRINCIPLE.** A bare `null` on `payout_velocity` is strictly better than `0 / false`, so it clears the bar `B2` actually failed. **It then fails a second bar that `B2` sets in the same breath**: `exhausted` and `uncovered` are two different facts, they demand two different acts by whoever reads the panel, and a bare `null` makes them one. `ADR-042` F-4 is the corpus's ruling that an uncovered day is UNKNOWN rather than a not-holiday, and collapsing the two here would re-lose exactly what that entry preserved, one layer up and on a panel with a pager attached. **A shape that cannot distinguish "the estate has no opinion" from "the estate is young" has beaten the zero and reproduced the silence.**

**THE SECOND DIRECTION OF THE PAIRING IS THE ONE NOBODY ASKS FOR AND IT IS THE WORSE FAILURE.** A gap naming a figure that is present tells an operator, during an incident, that a number they are reading off the screen is missing. Both directions are seeded and both go red; removing either loop turns the case red on its own.

### 5. Where this ruling and the console's shape DIVERGE, and why that is correct rather than tolerated

**The console tags the absence AT THE SITE. This ruling tags it ON THE BODY.** [`figure.ts`](../../apps/admin/src/figure.ts) declares `Reading` as `{ kind: 'figure' } \| { kind: 'absent', absent: AbsentFigure }`, and `AbsentFigure` at [`figure.ts:127`](../../apps/admin/src/figure.ts) carries `origin`, `label`, `definition` and a required non-blank `reason`. **Three differences, each with its own reason, and none of them is a compromise.**

**1. THE SITE VERSUS THE BODY.** A `Reading` is held one figure at a time by a caller that has no document to hang anything on; there is no body in `figure.ts` to put a gap list on. A response IS a document, and it has a once-per-body place. **`ADR-202` already ruled that the reason for a repeated absence belongs there rather than on each row, and `CUSUM_GAPS` at [`admin-breaker.ts:187`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-breaker.ts) and `LOSS_RATIO_GAPS` already sit there on two sibling endpoints.** Copying the console's shape onto the wire would put the identical sentence on every plan.

**2. `origin`, `label` AND `definition` DO NOT CROSS.** Those three are the CONSOLE's page vocabulary: `requireOrigin` admits `P-M6-01` to `P-M6-10` and `AS-M6-04` and refuses everything else, and `label` and `definition` are what `INV-M6-04` requires printed beside a number ON A PAGE. **Putting them on the wire would move a rendering roster into an API contract, and that is the one thing [ADR-188](ADR-188.md) kept out of this response: *"no field is named for a panel"*.** The wire carries the PATH; the console supplies the label and the definition, as it already does for every present figure.

**3. THE CONSOLE'S `reason` IS FREE TEXT AND THIS RULING'S `cause` IS CLOSED, AND BOTH ARE RIGHT WHERE THEY SIT.** `reason` is RENDERED to a human and never branched on, so a vocabulary would buy nothing and cost expressiveness. `cause` is BRANCHED ON by a machine, which is section 6.

**SO THE TWO LAYERS DRAW THE SAME DISTINCTION IN TWO VOCABULARIES, AND THE MAPPING IS TOTAL IN THE DIRECTION THAT MATTERS.** Every `gaps` entry becomes exactly one `absent()` call: `detail` is the `reason`, and the console supplies from its own roster the three fields the wire deliberately does not carry. **Nothing on the wire is unrenderable and nothing the console must print is unavailable**, which is the test a boundary shape has to pass and is the reason this entry claims agreement rather than mere coexistence.

### 6. `cause` is a CLOSED vocabulary of three, and the three are kinds of absence

**RULING 4. `LiabilityGapCause` at [`admin-reads.ts:600`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-reads.ts) is `'awaiting_dependency' \| 'insufficient_history' \| 'estate_uncovered'`, DECLARED in one place, published as data, and watched by a source-reading case. `detail` is REQUIRED FREE TEXT and may not be blank. `awaiting` is non-`null` EXACTLY when the cause is `awaiting_dependency`.**

**THE SHAPE IS [ADR-179](ADR-179.md)'s, taken deliberately and not by habit.** That entry closed `redaction_profile` at two and stated the precedent as *"a union declared in one place, a source-reading case that says how many members it has, and a widening that is therefore a diff on a test which says why the set is closed"*, and its finding 2 is the trap avoided here: **a DERIVED vocabulary cannot refuse a member, because it is whatever its map says.** `LiabilityGapCause` is declared and `LIABILITY_GAP_CAUSES` is checked against it by `satisfies`, so the data cannot drift from the type and neither can grow silently.

**WHY CLOSURE IS WORTH ITS COST HERE, in `ADR-179`'s own terms rather than in tidiness.** That entry's ground was that `redaction_profile` is *"the RECORD OF A PROMISE and not an input to one"*. **The ground here is the mirror image: this field is an INPUT to a decision, and the decision is a person's.** An operator meeting `insufficient_history` waits; an operator meeting `estate_uncovered` goes and loads a calendar, today. **A free-text reason makes that branch a substring match**, which is the failure `ADR-042` F-4 is about arriving as a client-side regex, and it lets two deployments describe one cause in two sentences that no console can group.

**WHY THREE, AND WHY THE THREE ARE KINDS RATHER THAN FIGURES.** [ADR-165](ADR-165.md) refused a third `SystemReason` member because *"`SystemReason` DOES NOT NAME SERVICES, IT NAMES KINDS OF ACCESS"* and *"the vocabulary that moves is the TABLE and never the REASON"*. **The same rule fixes this set's grain: a member is minted for a KIND OF ABSENCE and never for a FIGURE.** `awaiting_dependency` covers the CUSUM's calibration and would cover any later figure waiting on a named deliverable; a member called `cusum_uncalibrated` would be the vocabulary joining itself. Each of the three is a different act, which is what makes three the number:

| Member | What it says | What the reader does | Who has it today |
|---|---|---|---|
| `awaiting_dependency` | a named deliverable is outstanding, and `awaiting` names it | reads the deliverable | `per_plan[].cusum`, awaiting `DEP-M6-05` |
| `insufficient_history` | the estate is correct and does not reach back far enough for the window | waits, and nothing is wrong | `payout_velocity`, the evaluator's `exhausted` |
| `estate_uncovered` | the estate records NO OPINION about the period | loads what is missing, today | `payout_velocity`, the evaluator's `uncovered` |

**`detail` IS FREE TEXT AND THAT IS THE OTHER HALF OF THE RULING RATHER THAN A CONCESSION.** A closed vocabulary cannot hold *"four trading days short of thirty"* or *"no load covers 2026-08-26"*, and the evaluator carries exactly those quantities on `exhausted` and `uncovered`. **A cause that closed the whole message would force every producer to round its own finding to the nearest enum member**, which is the calibration defect `FM-M6-07` names in a different register. The bar the text must clear is `figure.ts`'s and it is enforced rather than stated: blank is refused, and the check is `.trim()` because whitespace is that sentence's loophole.

### 10. Findings outside the ruling: reported, not repaired, each with the fence that owns it
