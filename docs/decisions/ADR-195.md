## ADR-195: the in-flight obligation is a TERM IN Open Liability and not a figure beside it, `INV-M5-15` does not move, and the column the term needs does not exist  (2026-08-28, status: proposed)

**[ADR-181](ADR-181.md) section 7 recorded a consequence it could not fix and [ADR-187](ADR-187.md) made it writable. This entry rules it.** `withdrawals_in_flight` is `P-M6-01`'s **third component**, summed into Open Liability rather than reported beside it, and [`M06:59`](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md)'s new `INV-M6-15` is the rule that follows: **Open Liability does not move when a wallet withdrawal is approved, and it falls when that withdrawal's cash leaves.** `INV-M5-15` stays exactly as it is and [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) is not opened. **What this entry does NOT hold is the thing the term needs in order to be rendered**, and section 6 is that list: no column of `liability_snapshots` holds this obligation, so the field [ADR-188](ADR-188.md)'s projection rule would put on the wire has nothing behind it.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `195`, session **361**, dispatched as `claude/open-liability-obligation`. The row is amended in place under [ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3, which `CI-06f` enforces, rather than answered with a second row.

What is JUDGED is minting `INV-M6-15` rather than widening `INV-M6-11`, and declining to open `apps/admin/src/liability.ts` to carry the assertion. Section 9 is that question.

### 2. THE RULING

> **1. THE OBLIGATION IS A TERM IN OPEN LIABILITY AND NOT A FIGURE BESIDE IT.** `P-M6-01` has **three** components, not two: `sum(withdrawable)` across funded accounts, `sum(wallet balances)` across identities, and the firm-scoped `withdrawals_in_flight` obligation. All three are shown separately and all three are summed, on the panel's existing rule.
>
> **2. `LT-06` IS A TRANSFER BETWEEN TWO TERMS OF ONE PANEL AND NEVER A CHANGE TO THE PANEL.** [`M06:59`](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md)'s `INV-M6-15`. `LT-07` is the only external-leg posting that moves Open Liability, and it moves it in the moment the cash moves. `LT-09` returns the amount to the wallet component and leaves the total where it was.
>
> **3. THE TERM'S VALUE IS THE MAGNITUDE OF THE `withdrawals_in_flight` BALANCE AT THE PANEL'S OWN `as_of`, AND IT HAS A SECOND PRODUCER THAT A MERGED CONSTRAINT ALREADY BINDS TO IT.** By [`0057:231`](../../packages/db/migrations/0057_terminal_withdrawal_obligation.sql)'s `WD-C1` every terminal withdrawal contributes exactly `0` to that account, so the balance is the sum of `amount_cents` over withdrawals standing at `approved` or `transferring`. **Two derivations of one integer, and a divergence between them is confined by construction to the open set.**
>
> **4. THE TERM DOES NOT ENTER `P-M6-07`'s FLOAT.** [`liability.ts:554`](../../apps/admin/src/liability.ts) reads `floatCents` from `wallet_balances_cents` deliberately, and an in-flight withdrawal is money already being withdrawn rather than float. **Whether it enters that panel's exposure denominator is a SECOND question and is not ruled here**, section 8.
>
> **5. `INV-M5-15` DOES NOT MOVE AND [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) IS NOT OPENED.** Its words are true, its subject is wallet balances, and what was missing is a rule about a panel. Section 5.

**Every amount in this entry is integer cents. The term is a magnitude and never a signed ledger net**, which section 4 states because the two differ in sign and both are correct answers to different questions.

### 3. Clause 1, and why a figure beside cannot repair the defect

**THE ARGUMENT FOR A FIGURE BESIDE IS REAL AND IT IS THE PANEL'S OWN WORDING.** [`M06:92`](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) defines `P-M6-01` as *"what traders could claim if every gate vanished"*. A trader whose withdrawal is approved can no longer claim that money; they have already claimed it and Merit has acted. On the wording alone, the obligation is not what a trader could claim, it is what Merit has committed to pay, and a different question deserves a different figure.

**IT IS REFUSED ON THREE GROUNDS AND THE THIRD IS DECISIVE.**

| Ground | |
|---|---|
| **The counterfactual describes the POPULATION, not the obligation** | *"If every gate vanished"* is there to explain why this figure exceeds near-term cash: it admits claims that caps and cadence still block. The panel's own gloss offers *"has already cleared them all and is owed unconditionally"* as the reason a wallet balance BELONGS, never as a reason a settled claim might not. An in-flight withdrawal has cleared every gate and been acted on, so the gloss admits it a fortiori |
| **Money that can return to Open Liability without a new obligation being created never left it** | `LT-09` credits `trader_wallet` and posts no new obligation ([`M05:138`](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)). If the amount were outside the panel between `LT-06` and `LT-09`, a rail failure would create liability out of nothing. **Nothing in the corpus says a rail failure increases what Merit owes**, because it does not: it changes where the same debt sits |
| **THE DEFECT IS IN THE TOTAL, AND A FIGURE BESIDE THE TOTAL LEAVES THE TOTAL WRONG** | Finding 9. The three wrong movements are properties of the sum, and no number placed next to a wrong sum makes it right. `INV-M6-11`'s own failure mode is *"liability blindness introduced by a feature intended to reduce it"*; a figure beside is that sentence with a footnote |

**THE FOURTH SHAPE WAS AVAILABLE AND IT IS REFUSED SEPARATELY, because it is the cheapest one and it looks like it costs nothing.** Fold the obligation into `wallet_balances_cents` and read *wallet balance* as *what Merit owes this trader out of the wallet, wherever it currently sits*. **It needs no column, no field and no migration.** It is refused because that column is read in two places on purpose: [`liability.ts:534`](../../apps/admin/src/liability.ts) states that `floatCents` is *"the same column `P-M6-01`'s wallet component reads"*, on the ground that *"one quantity read from one column and shown in two places is a quantity that cannot drift between them"*. **Folding a second quantity into it makes that column mean two different things in the two places, which is the exact drift the shared read exists to prevent**, and it would overstate the float by every cent already in transit. It also contradicts [`M20:64`](../plans/M20-wallet.md)'s `INV-M20-08`, which requires wallet balances be *"segregated in reporting and in fact"*, and the ledger has already segregated them in fact: after `LT-06` the amount is in a firm-scoped account that no identity holds a position in.

### 4. Clause 2, and what an operator actually sees between the two postings

**THE PANEL IS INVARIANT ACROSS THE APPROVAL AND THAT IS THE WHOLE ANSWER.** Write `W` for withdrawable across funded accounts, `B` for wallet balances, `F` for the obligation, all integer cents and all magnitudes.

| Posting | `W` | `B` | `F` | `P-M6-01 = W + B + F` | What an operator reads |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `LT-06`, approval | unchanged | falls by `amount_cents` | rises by `amount_cents` | **UNCHANGED** | The debt did not shrink. It changed form, at one `as_of`, between two components on one page |
| `LT-07`, settlement | unchanged | unchanged | falls by `amount_cents` | **FALLS by `amount_cents`** | Merit paid. This is the only external-leg posting that moves the panel, and it moves it when the cash moves |
| `LT-09`, rail exhausted | unchanged | rises by `amount_cents` | falls by `amount_cents` | **UNCHANGED** | The transfer failed and the trader's claim came back. Nothing new is owed |

**AND WHAT AN OPERATOR NEEDS TO KNOW ABOUT THE THIRD COMPONENT SPECIFICALLY IS THAT IT IS THE ONLY ONE WITH TWO EXITS.** Withdrawable becomes a wallet balance or expires against a cap. A wallet balance is payable on demand forever ([`M20:65`](../plans/M20-wallet.md)'s `INV-M20-09`). **The obligation leaves either as cash or as a reversal back into the wallet**, and a panel that showed it without saying so would invite an operator to read it as money already gone.

**THE SIGN IS STATED BECAUSE THE TWO CORRECT ANSWERS DIFFER BY ONE.** [`posting.ts:235-236`](../../packages/ledger/src/posting.ts) makes a credit `-amountCents`, so a standing obligation of 25,000c is a ledger net of **-25000** in `withdrawals_in_flight`, which is what [ADR-187](ADR-187.md) and [ADR-189](ADR-189.md) both measured against a real database. **The panel term is the magnitude, 25000**, on `wallet_balances_cents`' own precedent: that column is `bigint NOT NULL` holding a positive balance for a liability whose ledger net is negative, and [`liability.ts:156`](../../apps/admin/src/liability.ts) refuses a negative one. **A term written as the ledger net would subtract the obligation from the panel instead of adding it**, which is a sign error inside a figure whose whole purpose is that it not fall wrongly.

### 5. Clause 5, and why `INV-M5-15` neither covers this nor needs to move

**ITS WORDS DO NOT COVER THE OBLIGATION.** [`M05:98`](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)'s subject is *wallet balances*. After `LT-06` the amount is not a wallet balance: `trader_wallet` no longer carries it, a firm-scoped account does, and [`M20:64`](../plans/M20-wallet.md)'s `INV-M20-08` requires the two be *"segregated in reporting and in fact"*. **Reading "wallet balances" to include an amount the ledger has moved out of the wallet would require the phrase to mean what that invariant forbids.**

**AND IT STILL DOES NOT MOVE, BECAUSE NOTHING IN IT IS WRONG.** It says wallet balances are in Open Liability and in the reserve coverage ratio. Both remain true after this entry, unamended and unqualified. What was missing was never a correction to that sentence; it was **a rule about the panel**, and the panel is [M06](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md)'s: [`M06:35`](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) says M6 *"aggregates numbers other modules computed"*, and [`M20:50`](../plans/M20-wallet.md) routes reserve and liability reporting to M6 by name. **A reporting rule written into `M05` would put it in the module whose own boundary table gives it away.**

**`INV-M6-11` WAS THE OTHER CANDIDATE HOME AND IT IS ALSO LEFT UNTOUCHED, WHICH IS A JUDGEMENT.** It reads *"Every liability figure includes wallet balances, and no panel reports a liability number that excludes them"*, and its second clause is nearly general enough. **Appending to it would expand an approved invariant's SUBJECT** from wallet balances to every amount owed, which is a larger act than adding a row, and it would make one invariant carry two rules: an inclusion rule and a conservation rule. **`INV-M6-15` carries the conservation rule and `INV-M6-11` is not edited at all**, which is the strongest form of the additive discipline [ADR-180](ADR-180.md) and [ADR-181](ADR-181.md) established in [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) section 2.1.

### 7. What was measured rather than argued

**IT DID NOT, AND WHAT KEEPS THE SERIES PENDING IS A DUPLICATE RATHER THAN A GAP.** Run as a trial edit before this entry was written, and again on the real one: the gate PASSES both times. **THE TERM'S SECOND PRODUCER WAS READ AT THE CONSTRAINT AND NOT INFERRED.** [`0057:231`](../../packages/db/migrations/0057_terminal_withdrawal_obligation.sql) sums `ledger_entries.amount_cents` over `withdrawals_in_flight` for one withdrawal's transactions and raises unless it is exactly `0`, at `settled`, `failed` and `cancelled` alike. **What that buys clause 3 is a bound rather than an identity**: it forces the terminal set's contribution to zero, so any disagreement between the ledger balance and the sum over open withdrawals is confined to rows at `approved` or `transferring`. **It does not assert that an `approved` row has posted `LT-06`**, and nothing does, which is why clause 3 states a reconciliation and not an equality.

### 9. Approval

- **Approval line: PENDING, UNSIGNED.** **MONEY PATH.** No migration lands and no code is edited, so the [`E2`](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) line-by-line read is owed on the diff that adds the column and on the renderer that sums the third term. **Neither is discharged here, and merging is not signing.**

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.**

**THE QUESTION, PLAINLY.
