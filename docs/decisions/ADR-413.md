## ADR-413: `B5` term 1 is not one export, its home is a `packages/db` door, and the fact every record of it has missed is the handle rather than the fence  (2026-09-07, status: proposed)

**THE ROW WAS DISPATCHED TO TAKE `B5` TERM 1 AND IT IS NOT TAKEN.** The dispatch held both files [ADR-411](ADR-411.md) named as the whole of the obstacle, `apps/api/src/admin-source/**` and `apps/api/src/payout-backend.ts`, and it asked for the claim to be re-derived before it was acted on. **RE-DERIVED, THE CLAIM IS FALSE, AND IT IS FALSE ON A FACT NO ENTRY IN THIS CHAIN HAS EVER MEASURED.**

[ADR-411](ADR-411.md) section 12 item 2 prices term 1 as ONE EXPORT AND ONE CALL, and its approval note says the only reason that row did not take it is that the cheap version lives one file outside its fence, which is *"a dispatch shape rather than a cost"*. **THE FENCE WAS NOT THE OBSTACLE.** The composition that entry names, `planLeg`, reads through `catalogRowAt`. `packages/db` declares `catalogRowAt` on `ScopedTx` and on no other handle. An admin read holds `SystemTx`, which has no catalogue accessor at all. **SO THE EXPORT WOULD COMPILE AND NO CALLER ON THIS READ PATH COULD PRODUCE ITS FIRST ARGUMENT.**

**THE ROUTE THAT REMAINS IS THE ONE [ADR-411](ADR-411.md) SECTION 10 REFUSED**, and it is refused again here on its own ground: the rows ARE reachable from an admin handle, as `unknown`, so a supplier written on this side maps them onto the engine's `PlanVersionSizeRow` a second time in this deployable, on the blob that fixes every cents threshold a payout is decided against.

**SO TERM 1'S HOME IS NEITHER THIS FENCE NOR `payout-backend.ts`. IT IS A `packages/db` DOOR**, and no wave that hands one row those two paths clears it, however the fence is drawn.

### 4. WHAT THE PORT REQUIRES, AND WHAT `planLeg` SUPPLIES

Both read at source rather than taken from any entry.

`EligibleFoldIo.resolvePinnedPlan` is declared in `apps/api/src/admin-source/eligible-next-7d.ts` as `resolvePinnedPlan(planVersionId: string, sizeCents: Cents): Promise<ResolvedPlan>`. **IT TAKES NO TRANSACTION.** The port is injected beside the fold's handle rather than through it, so the unit of work is the supplier's to hold and to open.

`planLeg` is declared in `apps/api/src/payout-backend.ts` as `async function planLeg(handle: ScopedTx, account: Record<string, unknown>): Promise<ResolvedPlan>`. It reads two keys off the account row, addresses `plan_versions` and `plan_version_sizes` through `handle.catalogRowAt`, decodes the blob with the engine's `decodePlanRules`, maps the size row onto the engine's `PlanVersionSizeRow` and returns `resolvePlan(rules, sizeRow)`.

**THE TWO DIFFER IN TWO PLACES AND ONLY ONE OF THEM IS COSMETIC.**

| | Port | `planLeg` | Reconcilable? |
|---|---|---|---|
| The pair | Taken as two parameters | Decoded out of an `accounts` row | **YES.** The fold already decodes the pair out of the account row before it calls the port, so both sides hold it as typed values and the seam is `planLeg`'s own first two statements |
| The handle | Captured by the supplier | `ScopedTx`, declared | **NO, AND SECTION 5 IS WHY** |

### 5. THE HANDLE, WHICH IS THE FACT EVERY EARLIER RECORD MISSED

**`catalogRowAt` IS DECLARED ON ONE HANDLE IN `packages/db` AND IT IS `ScopedTx`.** Derived over the interface declarations in `packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts` rather than read off any one of them, and held as a control rather than as this sentence (section 9, case 1). `TxCommon`, `SystemTx`, `FirmTx`, `EstablishmentTx` and every `*Db` door declare none.

**AND THE ADMIN LIABILITY READ HOLDS `SystemTx`.** `apps/api/src/admin-source/index.ts` declares `AdminSourceBackend` with one method, `operator<T>(fn: (tx: AdminSourceTx) => Promise<T>): Promise<T>`, whose authority its own docblock names as `systemDb('operator-console')` on [ADR-102](ADR-102.md). `AdminSourceTx` is an intersection of the five module handles and not one of them declares a catalogue accessor.

**THE CONSEQUENCE IS THAT [ADR-411](ADR-411.md) SECTION 5's OPTION 1 IS NOT MERELY AN `FM-16`. ITS FIRST CLAUSE IS UNBUILDABLE AS WRITTEN.** That entry offered *widen the handle and write the mapping again*, reading `LiabilityTx`'s missing `catalogRowAt` as something this fence could add. A `LiabilityTx` carrying `catalogRowAt` is a handle `SystemTx` does not satisfy, so widening it does not open the read, it closes the door the directory is served through.

**AND THE PROPERTY THAT `SystemTx` SATISFIES `AdminSourceTx` IS PROSE AND NOT A CONTROL**, which is reported here and not repaired: nine docblocks under `apps/api/src/admin-source/` state it, nothing in this deployable binds it, and `apps/api/src/db.ts` is the one file that may name `@merit/db` and declares no `system` door for it to bind against. That is [ADR-171](ADR-171.md) clause 1's refusal working as ruled, and its cost is that the sentence every module in this directory rests on is checked by nobody.

### 6. THE ROWS ARE REACHABLE, AS `unknown`, AND THAT IS THE REFUSED ROUTE

**A READER WHO STOPS AT SECTION 5 WOULD CONCLUDE THE ADMIN PATH CANNOT SEE THE CATALOGUE AT ALL, AND THAT IS FALSE.** `CATALOG_TABLE_KEYS` is declared `satisfies readonly FirmTableKey[]`, `planVersions` and `planVersionSizes` are both members, `ScopedTableKey` is `Exclude<TableKey, FirmTableKey | PairTableKey>` so `FirmTableKey` is inside `TableKey`, and `SystemTx.rowAt` is generic over `TableKey`. **SO `SystemTx.rowAt('planVersions', { id })` COMPILES AND RETURNS `unknown`.**

**THAT IS THE WHOLE OF WHAT IS AVAILABLE ON THIS SIDE, AND IT IS EXACTLY THE ROUTE [ADR-411](ADR-411.md) SECTION 10 REFUSED.** A supplier written against it decodes `unknown` into the engine's `PlanVersionSizeRow` a second time in this deployable and a third in this tree, beside `planLeg`'s and `apps/worker`'s `toSizeRow`. [ADR-303](ADR-303.md) limit 2 registers that mapping as a per-caller `FM-16` rather than forgiving it, and `apps/api/src/payout-backend.ts`'s own docblock says what the typed row buys and that a driver-side caller still writes the mapping once per caller. **THE BLOB IT MAPS FIXES EVERY CENTS THRESHOLD A PAYOUT IS DECIDED AGAINST. THE REFUSAL STANDS AND THE DISPATCH FORBIDS THE ROUTE INDEPENDENTLY.**

### 7. THE TWO OPTIONS, AND THE ONE THAT LOSES

The dispatch asked which of *export `planLeg`* and *lift it beside the fold* is the better shape, and for the losing option to be named.

**THE LOSING OPTION IS EXPORTING `planLeg` AS IT STANDS, AND IT LOSES ON TWO GROUNDS THAT ARE INDEPENDENT OF EACH OTHER.**

1. **ITS PARAMETER IS AN `accounts` ROW AND THE PORT HOLDS A PAIR.** A supplier would synthesise a record carrying two keys and hand it to a function whose parameter is typed `Record<string, unknown>` and whose error strings name `accounts` as the table the values came from. The fold has already decoded that pair with its own `text` and `centsOf`; `planLeg` would decode it again out of the synthetic record. That is one pair decoded twice through an `unknown` hop that exists only to fit the signature, and the second decode's refusals would name a row nobody read. **THE WINNING VARIANT OF THE SAME IDEA IS A SPLIT AT `planLeg`'s OWN FIRST SEAM**, an inner function over `(handle, planVersionId, sizeCents)` with `planLeg` as its caller, which is the shape `apps/worker`'s `resolvePinnedPlan` already has one deployable over.
2. **AND THE SPLIT LOSES TOO, ON SECTION 5.** Either form is a function whose first parameter is a `ScopedTx`, and a `ScopedTx` is bound to ONE identity. The fold walks the whole funded population across identities and the liability read resolves no identity at all, so there is no identity to open one against. Opening one per account would be a transaction per account, on a tenancy-bound door, to read rows that belong to nobody. **THE EXPORT IS CHEAP, CORRECT AND UNCALLABLE FROM HERE.**

**SO NEITHER OPTION IS TAKEN, AND `payout-backend.ts` IS UNCHANGED.** An export whose first argument no caller on the admin read path can produce is a widened money-path surface for nothing, which is `packages/db`'s own rule that a primitive admitted before a caller exists is a primitive nobody can remove. **THE REFUSAL IS HELD BY A CASE AND NOT BY THIS PARAGRAPH** (section 9, case 2).

**LIFTING THE COMPOSITION BESIDE THE FOLD, READ AS A MOVE RATHER THAN A REWRITE, LOSES ON A THIRD GROUND.** It would put a money-path resolver the payout transaction depends on inside the admin READ directory and make `payout-backend.ts` import from `admin-source/`, inverting producer and consumer, and it would not solve the handle either.

### 8. SO THE HOME IS A `packages/db` DOOR, AND NAMING IT IS THE ROW'S DELIVERABLE

**WHAT IS MISSING IS A TYPED CATALOGUE READ ON THE HANDLE AN OPERATOR CONSOLE HOLDS.** `CatalogRow<K>` is handed out by `catalogRowAt` alone, `catalogRowAt` sits on `ScopedTx` alone, and every other reader of these two tables in this tree writes the mapping itself. [ADR-286](ADR-286.md) section 5 named this remedy in its own words for the payout path, *"`packages/db` handing out a typed catalogue row instead of `unknown` ... whoever takes it is amending a door, not writing a decoder"*, and [ADR-303](ADR-303.md) took it for `ScopedTx` only. **THE SECOND HALF OF THAT SAME DOOR IS TERM 1.**

**IT IS NOT TAKEN HERE AND THE REASON IS THE FENCE THIS TIME RATHER THAN THE COST.** `packages/db` is not in this row's fence, row 415 holds `packages/tooling` and nobody in this wave was given `packages/db`. **THIS IS THE FIRST RECORD IN THE CHAIN TO NAME THAT ADDRESS, AND A ROW HOLDING `packages/db` PLUS THIS DIRECTORY IS THE DISPATCH THAT CLEARS TERM 1.** Whether the door is `catalogRowAt` lifted onto `TxCommon`, or a narrower one, is a `packages/db` ruling and not this row's.
