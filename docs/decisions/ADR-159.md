### 2. THE RULING

> **1. A NAME BECOMES A ROW ONLY WHERE EVERY FIELD IS A COLUMN A MIGRATION DECLARES OR A FIELD OF A MIRROR A PLAN NAMES.** Those are the two authorities and there is no third. [EVENTS](../architecture/EVENTS.md) section 6's refusal to invent three shapes was right about the danger and wrong only about the remedy: the remedy is not a later slice, it is a RULE about where a field may come from. Every field folded by this session resolves to [`0011`](../../packages/db/migrations/0011_wallet.sql), to [`0001`](../../packages/db/migrations/0001_extensions_and_enums.sql), or to [M05 section 5](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)'s named `payout.transfer_*` mirror. **A field that resolves to neither is a refusal and not a guess**, which is why `wallet.withdrawal_cancelled` is reported in clause 5 rather than written.
>
> **2. THE REGISTRY MAY AMEND A PAYLOAD AT THE FOLD, AND EVERY AMENDMENT IS NAMED IN ONE TABLE.** [EVENTS](../architecture/EVENTS.md)' own precedent is the M1 gate, where `payout.win_days_reset` *"gains `anchor_trading_day`, because 'reset to zero' without the anchor does not explain the next cycle"*. A fold that could only transcribe would have to install measurement 3's unproducible payload and measurement 7's two breaches of section 1. **What the registry may NOT do is amend silently**: section 3's table is the whole permission, and an amendment absent from it is a defect.
>
> **3. A CATALOGUE ROW CARRIES THE DISCRIMINATOR OF THE TABLE IT IS WRITTEN FROM, AND FIELDS BELOW THAT DISCRIMINATOR ARE CONDITIONAL ON IT.** `wallet.credited` gains `provenance`, and `account_id`, `payout_request_id` and `basis_trading_day` become present only where `provenance` is `payout`. This is section 1's *"a payload may add optional fields at the same version"* used for the purpose it exists for, and it is the alternative to three events for three provenances, which would put the storage layer's closed list into the event vocabulary and make every consumer subscribe three times.
>
> **4. A GUARD IS NAMED IN THE DRAWING AND AN EVENT IS NAMED IN THE REGISTRY, AND THE TWO DO NOT HAVE TO RHYME.** `G-WITHDRAWAL-HALT-CLEARED` stays spelled as it is beside `wallet.withdrawal_halt_released`. Renaming the guard to match would have made this fold a change to a machine, which is the exact widening [P5 section 9](../plans/P5-payouts-and-wallet.md) fenced against when it held this drawing for one row.
>
> **5. A NAME THIS CATALOGUE CANNOT SOURCE IS REPORTED WHERE THE MACHINE IS DRAWN, NOT INVENTED WHERE THE NAMES ARE KEPT.** The two `cancelled` edges get no event here. The repair is a row in a transition table, and a session holding that table writes the row and the name together, in that order, because a name without a transition is a row nobody ruled on and a transition without a name is what measurement 5 found.
>
> **6. `payout.freeze_expiring`'s LEAD IS TWELVE WALL-CLOCK HOURS AND THE PAYLOAD CARRIES IT.** The value is set here because `OQ-M5-07` says it is set here. It is in the payload because the corpus calls it a launch parameter rather than a constant, and section 1's own reason applies: *"consumers include a 2031 audit reading a 2027 row"*, and that audit cannot tell a twelve-hour warning from a re-tuned one without the field.

### 6. Alternatives considered

**Take the number and record nothing, on the ground that the reservation's condition cannot fire.** The closest call in this entry, and it is refused by [P5 section 8](../plans/P5-payouts-and-wallet.md), whose `P5-d` row marks the ADR **REQUIRED** in the plan's own words while [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md)'s row marks it CONDITIONAL. **Two registries disagree and the plan is the one that describes the work**; the allocation row describes a condition about a different document. Recording nothing would also leave the five amendments in section 3 as unexplained differences between `approved` plans and an `approved` registry, which is the shape a later session repairs in the wrong direction.

**Transcribe M05 and M20 verbatim and report every difference instead of amending.** This is [ADR-141](ADR-141.md) section 4's posture and it was right there, because those repairs each edited a document outside the fence. **It is wrong here, because the document the repair belongs in IS the fence**: the registry is the thing that makes a name real, so a registry row that cannot be produced is not a report about a defect, it is the defect.

**Split `wallet.credited` into three events, one per provenance.** Refused. It puts a storage discriminator into the event vocabulary, makes every consumer subscribe three times to learn that a balance moved, and breaks [M11](../plans/M11-certificates-social-proof.md)'s single trigger, which fires the payout certificate on `wallet.credited` precisely because [ADR-019](ADR-019.md)'s wallet credit is the moment a trader experiences as being paid.

**Fold `wallet.withdrawal_cancelled` too, so the machine has no silent edge.** Refused by clause 5 and by the paragraph in [EVENTS](../architecture/EVENTS.md) this entry's subject grew out of. The missing artifact is a transition row, and inventing an event to cover a transition nobody drew leaves the drawing exactly as unmet and adds a name to the registry with no producer.

**Rename `G-WITHDRAWAL-HALT-CLEARED` to match the event.** Refused by clause 4. It is a guard, guards are named in the drawing, and the fence over that drawing is one row.
