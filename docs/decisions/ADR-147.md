## ADR-147: `M04` section 3.3's consistency meter names five figures, three of them reach no client, and two of those three are `R-29`'s own operands  (2026-08-27, status: proposed)

**[M04 section 3.3](../plans/M04-trader-portal.md) rules the consistency meter visible AT ALL TIMES and says exactly what it shows: *"The meter shows three numbers, always: best day, period profit, and the resulting share in bp against the limit. When the share is under the limit it also shows the headroom."* Rendering that meter is this session's work, and TWO OF THE THREE ALWAYS-NUMBERS AND THE CONDITIONAL HEADROOM ARE ON NO RESPONSE IN [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) AND ON NO FIELD OF THE ENGINE'S OWN `ConsistencyGate`.** The portal cannot close the gap, and the reason is not a fence: the headroom is `max_bp - best_day_share_bp`, which is arithmetic on two `_bp` fields and is precisely what `INV-M4-01` bans, and the other two are magnitudes it does not hold in any form.

**The gap does not begin at the contract.** [`packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts:834`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts)'s `ConsistencyGate` carries five fields and the contract projects all five faithfully. What both drop are `best_day_cents` and `period_profit_cents`, which are **the two operands of [`R-29`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)'s ruled expression**, consumed inside the comparison and never surfaced. So this is not a computation nobody has. It is two halves of an arithmetic the engine performs on every funded account, discarded one layer below the screen that an approved plan requires them on.

- **Origin:** [M04 section 3.3](../plans/M04-trader-portal.md) and `SC-M4-03`, session **259**, branch `claude/portal-accounts`. The number was **RESERVED BEFORE DISPATCH and CONDITIONAL**, against the case that rendering found a view module asking a question the API cannot answer; [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md)'s `147` row is amended **in place** per [ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3. **The condition fired.**

- **Context:** two view modules recorded this absence independently before any page existed. [`view/accounts.ts`](../../apps/portal/src/view/accounts.ts): *"the headroom is `max_bp - best_day_share_bp` ... and no endpoint returns it ... Recorded in this session's log as an owed field rather than closed with a `-`."* [`view/eligibility.ts`](../../apps/portal/src/view/eligibility.ts): *"THE HEADROOM IS OWED AND IS NOT COMPUTED ... the subtraction belongs to the engine beside `profit_needed_to_dilute_cents`, which is the harder half of the same rule and is already computed there."* **Neither could see the other and both stopped at the headroom.** Rendering the screen is what widened it: a meter has to show every figure section 3.3 lists, and two more of them turn out to be missing for the same reason.

- **NOT MONEY PATH.** No migration, no route, no engine change, no `SD-nn`, and nothing here moves a cent. **It is still consequential**, because what the meter exists to prevent is a trader concluding the rules moved (`AS-13`), and a meter missing three of its five figures is the version of that meter a later session will call finished.

### 2. THE RULING

**RULING 1. The portal computes none of the three, and the ban is not a preference.** `INV-M4-01` is *"no money value displayed anywhere is computed client side"*, enforced by *"a lint rule banning arithmetic operators on any field whose name ends `_cents` or `_bp`; a formatting helper is the only permitted consumer"*. The headroom is a subtraction of two `_bp` fields and is the ban's exact shape. `best_day_cents` and `period_profit_cents` are not derivable from the share at all, and the only expression that would try is a division on money, which [`M01`](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) `INV-03` refuses even inside the engine.

**RULING 2. The screen renders what it holds and states no figure it lacks.** [`app/accounts/account-detail.ts`](../../apps/portal/src/app/accounts/account-detail.ts) shows `best_day_share` and `max` and emits no headroom row, no dash and no zero. A `0.00%` headroom is the failure [`format/money.ts`](../../apps/portal/src/format/money.ts) already names one field over: *"rendering a null as `0.00` would state a fact the server did not send, and it would do it in the one place where zero is the most consequential possible number."* Under a consistency limit, a headroom of zero reads as "you are exactly at the cap", which is a different account from the one being looked at.

**RULING 3. The remedy is three fields on `ConsistencyGate` and its projection, and it is NAMED here rather than written.** `bestDayCents`, `periodProfitCents` and the headroom, surfaced on the gate and carried through `EligibilityResponse.gates.consistency`. **The eligibility endpoint and not the account detail**, on [M04 section 4](../plans/M04-trader-portal.md)'s own words about it: *"this endpoint is cheap by design and is called on every dashboard render"*. Splitting one meter across two responses is how a card and a gate list end up disagreeing on one screen, which is the failure [`view/accounts.ts`](../../apps/portal/src/view/accounts.ts) already refuses for `blocked`.

**RULING 4. Whether the headroom is a FIELD or is dropped in favour of the two magnitudes is left open, deliberately.** Once `bestDayCents` and `periodProfitCents` are on the wire, the headroom is the only one of the three that a client could still be tempted to derive, and it is also the only one section 3.3 asks for conditionally. **An entry that settled it here would be ruling engine output from a rendering session.** What is ruled is that the portal does not compute it under either answer.

**RULING 5. [M04 section 3.3](../plans/M04-trader-portal.md) is NOT amended.** It is `approved`, and an entry that narrowed an approved plan until it matched an under-specified contract would be weakening a requirement in order to pass it. The plan is right and the mechanism is short.

**RULING 6. No route, no wire type and no engine file is edited by this entry.** [`api/types.ts`](../../apps/portal/src/api/types.ts) transcribes the contract and must keep transcribing it, so it may not gain a field the contract does not have. Session **251** holds the account routes and is writing them now; the engine is `M01`'s.

### 3. What this FORECLOSES, and the four repairs refused by name

| # | Refused | Why, and what it would have cost |
|---|---|---|
| **F1** | **Subtracting `max_bp - best_day_share_bp` in the portal** | `INV-M4-01`, verbatim. It is also the cheapest of the four to write and the hardest to spot in review, which is why the ban is structural: [`test/inv-m4-01.test.ts`](../../apps/portal/test/inv-m4-01.test.ts) reads this application's source for exactly this expression and names `const headroom = max_bp - best_day_share_bp;` as one of its four seeded violations |
| **F2** | **Deriving period profit from the share and the best day** | The portal holds neither operand, and the expression is a division on money. `M01` `INV-03` bans division inside the engine, where the numbers are `bigint`; doing it in a browser on two `number`s is the same rule broken with worse arithmetic |
| **F3** | **Rendering the share alone and calling section 3.3 met** | `AS-13`'s defence is *"the shape of the rule was visible before it bit"*. A share against a limit does show the shape, and it is what this screen renders today, and it is **three figures short of the ruling**. Recording the shortfall is what stops the next session reading a rendered meter as a discharged obligation |
| **F4** | **Widening `GET /accounts/:accountId`'s `progress.consistency` instead** | It already carries three of the meter's fields, so it is the obvious place and it is the wrong one. The gate's other four fields are on the eligibility response, `profit_needed_to_dilute_cents` among them, and section 3.3's meter is one object. **A meter assembled from two endpoints has two `as_of_trading_day`s**, which `INV-M4-02` makes a required field precisely so that cannot be papered over |

### 7. Approval

**One checkable clause, carrying the direction it fails in.**

> **`grep -rc headroom docs/architecture/` reports zero in every file, and `grep -n 'best_day\|period_profit' docs/architecture/API_CONTRACT.md` returns exactly two lines, both of them `best_day_share_bp`. `ConsistencyGate` at [`packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts:834`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts) declares five fields and none of them is a magnitude.** So of the five figures [M04 section 3.3](../plans/M04-trader-portal.md) names, three cannot be rendered by any client from any response this corpus specifies.

**Executed in this session, on this branch, and all three came back as stated.**

**The direction it fails in.** If the clause is wrong, some response carries the figures under a name this entry did not search for, and the cost is an entry that reported a gap the contract had already closed. **If it is right, which is what the run says, then the meter [M04](../plans/M04-trader-portal.md) makes visible AT ALL TIMES has been unbuildable since the contract was written**, and the first thing that would have discovered it is the first screen that tried to draw it. **That screen exists as of this branch and it draws two figures out of five.** The failure is quiet in the worst way available: the meter renders, it renders true numbers, and it is missing precisely the numbers `AS-13` says a trader needs before the rule bites.

- **Approval line: PENDING, UNSIGNED.** **NOT MONEY PATH**: no migration, no route, no engine file, no `SD-nn`, and no gate is added, moved or weakened by this entry.
- **The ruling a later session will want to reverse is RULING 2**, the empty row, and it is the one to reverse deliberately: rendering a placeholder would make the screen look complete, and section 3's `F3` is the argument against it.

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.** Three of the decisions here are judgements rather than derivations. **RULING 2, the empty row,** is named above as the one to reverse deliberately: the screen omits the figures it lacks rather than rendering a placeholder, so a meter an approved plan requires at all times ships showing two of five figures and nothing says which three are missing. The opposite reading, that a dash tells a trader more than an absence, is coherent and section 3's `F3` is the argument against it. **RULING 4 leaves open whether the headroom is a field at all**, on the ground that settling it here would be a rendering session ruling engine output; what that costs is that the remedy this entry names is three fields under one answer and two under the other, and neither is written. **RULING 5 refuses to amend the approved plan to match the under-specified contract**, which is the version of this entry that would close the finding by deleting the requirement. That is the right instinct and it is still a choice about which of two approved documents is wrong, taken by a session that holds neither.
