## ADR-088: the ADR registry and the session index are generated from the files they index, and the CLAIM table never will be  (2026-08-24, status: proposed)

**Two hand-transcribed registry tables become generated spans. This is [ADR-034](ADR-034.md) applied to the two tables it never reached: "either generate the fact or delete it and point at the source". It is non-money, it moves no rule, and it touches no migration.**

- **Context:** every session appends a row to a registry table describing a file it has just written. The row is a copy, the file is the source, and a copy maintained by hand drifts from its source. **[Session 149](../sessions/2026-08-23-session-149.md) found 27 ADR rows carrying a stale status word**, four of them signed the day before.

---

### 1. The measurement, and the derivation it needs to survive

**Over the last 25 FIRST-PARENT merges, which is one merge per session:**

| File | Merges touching it |
|---|---|
| [`sessions/README.md`](../sessions/README.md) | **25 of 25** |
| [`STATE.md`](../STATE.md) | 18 of 25 |
| [`decisions/README.md`](README.md) | 10 of 25 |
| [`INDEX.md`](../INDEX.md) | 10 of 25 |
| [`gates.mjs`](../../scripts/corpus/gates.mjs) | 4 of 25 |
| [`STRATEGY.md`](../testing/STRATEGY.md) | 2 of 25 |

**The derivation is stated because the obvious command gives a different answer.** Counted over the last 25 *raw* commits the figure is 9 of 25; over the last 25 *non-merge* commits, 11. **A later reader running `git log -25` will get 9 and conclude the premise collapsed.** The unit that matters for contention is the session, and one session is one merge, so `--first-parent` is the correct denominator and the only one that reproduces.

**The two tables generated here are the first and the third.** They are not chosen for being the worst prose; they are chosen for being the two most contended files whose contents are **derivable**. `STATE.md` at 18 of 25 is second and is not derivable, which is why it is untouched.

### 1.1 The motivating defect is REPAIRED, and the entry does not claim otherwise

**Session 149 repaired all 27 rows.** Checked before this was written: today all 85 ADR files are `accepted` and **zero** README rows disagree with their own heading. **Generation therefore prevents a recurrence; it does not fix a live defect**, and an entry implying otherwise would be overclaiming, which this corpus has been bitten by before.

**Nothing compared the row to the heading, and nothing was going to.** [`CI-06r`](../../scripts/corpus/gates.mjs) says so in its own `covers` line: it *"COMPARES A FILE WITH ITSELF AND CAN CHECK NEITHER HALF AGAINST THE GATE RECORD"*. After this ruling nothing needs to, because the status word is no longer copied.

### 1.2 The live drift, which is in the other table and is worse than a count

**`2026-08-20-session-95.md` holds three `##` sections and the entry table carried one row.** Across the directory: 145 files, **185 sections, 183 rows**.

**The two unindexed sections produced a wrong belief in a live reader.** The founder read session 95's first heading, concluded the file was only the impersonation session, and **doubted a correct claim that it had signed fourteen ADRs**. Section 3's heading is, verbatim, *"fourteen signatures, and one of the nine declines was a live hole rather than a missing signature"*. The registry indexed one of three sections and the reader believed the registry. **That is what an under-indexed registry costs, and it is better evidence than the count.**

### 2. RULING 1. The ADR table is a generated span, sourced from each entry's own heading

Reproduced against the captured baseline before the ruling was written: **85 files, 85 rows, none missing, none extra, and one row differing** — `ADR-080`'s initial capital, where the row now adopts its own document's convention (52 of 68 headings open uppercase). Every other row, including the ten changed below, is byte for byte.

**The status column cannot drift after this, by construction.**

### 2.1 Ten headings moved first, and generating is lossless only because they did

Eleven rows differed from their headings. **Five carried a RICHER summary in the row than in the ADR's own heading**, so generating from headings as they stood would have deleted text a human wrote. This ruling authorises the edit that makes each heading say what its row already said. **Only the title text moves. The `status:` token is untouched in all ten and `CI-06r` is unaffected.**

### 2.2 One pre-existing defect the generated table repairs by construction

**[ADR-079](ADR-079.md)'s row was inside the `Gate closures` table, not the ADR table.** 84 rows sat in the real table and one sat below the wrong heading. **This is the third occurrence of that defect**: [the unsigned-ADR audit](gates/unsigned-adr-audit-2026-08-18.md) recorded it for `ADR-056` and `ADR-057`, noting *"No gate reads which table a row is in"*. Nothing caught the third either, and it was this session's own row from [session 135](../sessions/2026-08-22-session-135.md). **A generated table cannot append to the wrong table.**

### 2.3 Order

**Numeric ascending**, replacing order-of-append, which is not an order a reader can scan for a number. `ADR-D1` carries no three-digit number and is **pinned immediately after `ADR-015`**, where the registry has always carried it, by an explicit rule rather than by accident.

### 3. RULING 2. The session ENTRY table is a generated span, keyed by ADR-064's own identity

**[ADR-064](ADR-064.md) section 1 is signed and states the key outright: identity is `(log file, section heading)`.** So a row is one `##` **section**, not one file, which is what makes session 95's two orphans appear.

**The rows are NOT derivable from the headings, and this was checked at source rather than assumed.** Session 49's four rows differ from its four headings in every one, and carry text no heading has: *"`CV-01` to `CV-19`, and R-17 declared (45 of 50)"* appears in no heading. Generating from headings would have rewritten 183 rows to say less.

**So the row text moves INTO the file it describes.** Each `##` section carries one `<!--index: … -->` line, and **183 of the 185 are the existing row verbatim**. The two exceptions are session 95's unindexed sections, which take their own heading subject because no row has ever existed to copy.

**The backfill is asserted byte for byte, two independent ways.** The writer strips every inserted line back out and demands the original before it writes; and `git` reports **185 lines added, 0 deleted, across 145 files, with 0 additions that are not an index marker**. A backfill that silently reflows a historical log is the one way this goes wrong quietly.

**`<!--index:` is deliberately not a `gen:` span.** It is an input to one. `spansIn` cannot see it and `CI-06t` does not count it.

**A section with no marker is a named error, not a skipped row**, because a silently omitted row is the defect this span exists to end.

### 3.1 The CLAIM table is NOT generated, and it must never be

**A claim precedes its file by design** ([ADR-064](ADR-064.md)), and the README's own preamble says it: *"A number is free when no row claims it, and **a row here is the only claim there is**."* Generating the claim table from the files would delete the reservation mechanism and reinstate the 110/111/112 collision it was built after. **This session hit the same defect twice while being dispatched**: `151` was dispatched to two sessions and `088` to two, neither claimed first, which is `OI-27` again.

**One thing IS derivable and is deliberately refused.** The claim row's **State** column reads `WRITTEN. Struck` exactly when an entry exists for that number, so it could be generated. **It is not**, because deriving it would pull half the claim table into the generator and blur the line this section draws. **Named here so the refusal is a decision rather than an oversight, and so a later session does not finish the job.**

---

### 4. RULING 3. What a session's per-session write reduces to, stated honestly

**Three writes, not one.** Its own new file, one `STATE.md` append, and **striking its own claim row**. The entry row goes; the strike does not.

| File | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `sessions/README.md` | 25 of 25 merges | the claim and the strike only |
| `decisions/README.md` | 10 of 25 merges | **zero** |

**Contention on the top file is reduced, not eliminated**, and this entry says so rather than claiming a halving it does not deliver.

---

### 5. What this deliberately stops asserting

- **`CI-06n` goes vacuous for two of its five registries.** It asserts that every entry file has a README row and every row resolves, in both directions. Once the rows are generated from the files, **both directions hold by construction** for `decisions/` and `sessions/`. It still covers `data-model/`, `golden-scenarios/` and `edge-cases/`. **It earned its keep on the way in**: the first wrap over-reached, `generate` deleted the whole `Gate closures` section, and `CI-06n` is what reported it.
- **`CI06U_REGISTER` gains one key and loses none.** `2026-08-20 - session 95` was not a duplicate while the table carried one row for its three sections; generating surfaces the other two. **It is [ADR-061](ADR-061.md)'s T3 shape the other twenty keys already have**, and the register entry says so rather than presenting it as new damage.

---

### 6. Consequences

- **[`gates.mjs`](../../scripts/corpus/gates.mjs) gains two `SPAN_QUERIES` and three helpers.** **The span mechanism does not change**: `spansIn` already matched with the `s` flag, so a multi-line span body was expressible for as long as the mechanism has existed and no query had used it.
- **A span's value is compared trimmed and written verbatim**, a two-line change. `CI-06v` is why: a `<!--gen:` line terminates a table for its parser, so an opener between `|---|---|` and the first row leaves 85 pipe lines as an orphan fragment. **Each span therefore owns its header and delimiter rows too**, which is the only arrangement the generator cannot split.
- **No `CI-06` letter, and `x` stays unclaimed.** If the rows are generated the status-drift gate has nothing left to check. [ADR-082](ADR-082.md) already says `x` is free and this entry does not change that.
- **No migration, no `SD-nn`, no engine change, and nothing under `packages/` is touched.**

- **Founder approval: PENDING.** **Unsigned by design.** It authorises edits to ten `accepted` ADR headings and changes how two registries are maintained, so it is not taken on a session's authority.

  **One checkable clause:** *the generated ADR table reproduces all 85 rows with one capitalisation change and no loss, the generated session table adds exactly two rows and removes none, and both spans have been watched reporting a seeded drift by name.* **Cost if wrong: a registry that reads as derived while carrying something nobody wrote.**
