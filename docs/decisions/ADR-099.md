## ADR-099: `ADR-083`'s build-script clause is superseded, and the VG-12 admission for the UI framework is recorded  (2026-08-25, status: accepted)

**[ADR-083](ADR-083.md)'s signed approval clause has two halves and the second one expires the day `ADR-095` merges.** It reads *"no `package.json` under `apps/` declares a `build` script: the count is **0**"*. On the branch behind [#240](https://github.com/lam2-coder/meritfutures/pull/240) the count is **2**, `apps/portal` and `apps/site`. **A signed entry moves by a superseding ADR and never by a session**, so this entry is a precondition of that merge rather than a follow-up to it.

**It supersedes ONE CLAUSE AND NOTHING ELSE.** `ADR-083`'s ruling stands whole: Fastify 5 still runs `apps/api`, still under `node --experimental-strip-types`, still with no build step and no bundler in **that** deployable. The first half of the clause stands unamended. `ADR-083` is not edited.

- **Origin:** [ADR-098](ADR-098.md)'s approval line, which found the collision and named this supersession *"a precondition of #240 ... named here rather than discovered after the merge"*. Session 189 found it; this entry discharges it.

- **NOT MONEY PATH.** No ledger, no payout, no engine, no migration, no `SD-nn`, no route. It supersedes one sentence and records one admission.

---

### 1. The clause was RIGHT when it was written, and `ADR-083` named the day it would expire

**This is not a clause that turned out to be wrong.** It was true on 2026-08-23 and it is true on `main` today: the count is 0, verified in this session.

**`ADR-083` itself wrote down the expiry, in two places, before anyone needed it.**

> [ADR-083:80](ADR-083.md): *"`CI-06/gate-inventory` probes every `apps/*/package.json` for a `build` script and requires it **ABSENT**, because `CI-07`'s whole row waits on an artifact that does not exist; **the day a build script lands, that gate fails on good news**."*

> [ADR-083:138](ADR-083.md), inside the signed clause's own cost statement: *"the second half failing means `CI-06/gate-inventory` fails on good news the day the script lands, and closing `CI-07` in the same breath needs [STRATEGY](../testing/STRATEGY.md) section 4.1 and [`gates.mjs`](../../scripts/corpus/gates.mjs)."*

**So the entry predicted its own clause's expiry, named the mechanism that would fail, and named the two files whose repair would be needed.** All three were correct. What it could not do was supersede itself in advance, which is what this entry is for.

### 2. The probe MOVED rather than being relaxed, and that distinction is the whole ruling

**Weakening a gate to pass it is the one move this project does not make.** The absent-`build` probe could have been deleted, or its assertion inverted, or `apps/portal` and `apps/site` exempted from it. `ADR-095` does none of those.

`CI-07`'s row waits on an **activation artifact**. The old probe used "no app declares a `build` script" as the proxy for "no bundle exists". `ADR-095` replaces the proxy with the thing itself: the artifact is a **built bundle**, and `next build` exits **1** on *"Couldn't find any `pages` or `app` directory"*. **There are no pages in this repository yet**, so the artifact is still genuinely absent and `CI-07` still correctly waits. The gate asserts the same fact through a probe that survives the build script existing.

**RULED: the second half of `ADR-083`'s clause is superseded and replaced by the sentence below**, which asserts what the old one was a proxy for.

> **No built bundle exists under `apps/`.** A `build` script may be declared; running it must not produce a bundle, because no `pages` or `app` directory exists to build. `CI-07`'s activation artifact is therefore still absent, and `VG-2`'s chain still holds.

**What this does NOT license.** It does not permit a build step in `apps/api`. `ADR-083` section 5's constraint on the API's framework was argued from the runtime as well as from the probe, and only the probe half moves here. **A future entry wanting `apps/api` to build must argue it on its own merits** and will not find support in this one.

### 3. The VG-12 admission, recorded with the check the gate exists to perform

**[STRATEGY:218](../testing/STRATEGY.md) wires `VG-12`'s mechanical legs into `CI-05` and leaves the human half *"discharged outside Actions"*.** [STRATEGY:192](../testing/STRATEGY.md) says of that half that it is *"which a session cannot grant itself"*. It is granted here by the founder and not by a session, and the grant is quoted in section 5.

**The gate exists for a stated reason and a blanket grant does not discharge that reason by itself.** [STRATEGY:62](../testing/STRATEGY.md): *"the build method is AI-assisted and hallucinated package names recur predictably"*. So the substantive check was performed rather than waved through.

**The admission is 15 packages.** The install closure moves **179 to 230**, `+51`, of which **36 are per-platform binaries** (`@next/swc-*`, `@img/*`, `@emnapi/runtime`) that no single host installs in full.

Each is either the framework itself, one of its declared peers, or a documented transitive dependency of `next@16` or `react@19`.

**`pnpm audit --audit-level=low` reports `No known vulnerabilities found`, exit 0**, run in this session against the admitted lockfile rather than quoted from the branch. **`pnpm install --frozen-lockfile` ignores no build script**, so `onlyBuiltDependencies: []` still means what it says.

**`16.3.2` rather than an older major is itself a refusal worth preserving.** That version declares `@playwright/test` as an **optional peer**; pnpm writes a package's peer block into the lockfile verbatim, and `CI-08`'s probe read that declaration as an install. **An older major that happened not to declare it would have kept the gate quiet, which is choosing a version to hide a defect.** `ADR-095` repairs the probe to read an entry KEY instead, and `CI-08`'s row is untouched because nothing about it was wrong.

### 4. A SIXTH approval clause has drifted on its enumeration, and the pattern is now the finding

**This is the sixth signed or proposed clause whose RULING held and whose ENUMERATION did not**: `ADR-077`, `ADR-078`, `ADR-093`, `ADR-096`, `ADR-097`, and now `ADR-083`. In every one the decision was right and a **count, a list, or a grep** written beside it went stale.

**Five were caught at signing and amended. This one was caught by a different session, three entries later, at a merge.** That is the first time the drift outlived the signature, and it outlived it because **nothing reconciles an approval clause against the tree once the clause is signed.** A clause is prose, and [ADR-042](ADR-042.md) already ruled prose is not a control.

**This entry does not build that check**, and says so rather than leaving the omission to be read as an oversight. The remedy belongs with the other owed gate of the same class -- nothing derives `schema.ts`'s drifted-table sentence either, and both are checkable. **Naming the class is what this section is for; a session that writes the check should supersede this paragraph.**

### 5. Founder approval

**Approval line: GRANTED 2026-08-25**, by the founder directly, as an **explicit delegated approval and NOT the [E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) line-by-line read.** This entry writes no money-path file.

**The `VG-12` human admission is granted in the founder's own words**, recorded here because `STRATEGY:192` requires that it not be a session's: *"make decisions on anything that needs me yourself you have my full permission if you approve i approve"*, and earlier in the same engagement, *"you have my full permission approve anything you see fit on my behalf"*. **The grant is the founder's; the verification in section 3 is this session's, and neither substitutes for the other.**

**The clause was executed before signing, on the merge result rather than on a transcript:**

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.** Every fact in section 3 is a command's output. **The judgement is not.** The framework is a choice the project lives with for years, and `ADR-083` said the same thing about Fastify in its own signature: *"what a founder read adds is the years-long half."* **That half is granted here on a blanket authority rather than on a line-by-line read of `ADR-095`**, and the distinction is recorded rather than absorbed.

**One checkable clause, written in the direction that fails.**

> **`grep -l '"build"' apps/*/package.json` returns exactly two paths, `apps/portal/package.json` and `apps/site/package.json`, and `pnpm run gates` is 30 of 30 on the same tree.** And **no built bundle exists**: there is no `apps/portal/src/app` or `apps/site/src/app` directory, so `next build` cannot produce one, and `CI-07`'s activation artifact is still absent.

**Cost if wrong.** If the supersession is wrong, a signed clause was replaced by a weaker one and `CI-07` activates on an artifact that never arrives, which the second half of the clause above is written to catch. **If the `VG-12` admission is wrong, a package nobody vetted is in the closure** -- bounded by section 3's fifteen named resolutions and the audit, and unbounded beyond them, because an audit reports known vulnerabilities and says nothing about a package that is merely a bad idea. **That residue is real and is the founder's, which is why the grant is quoted rather than summarised.**
