### 4. What was re-derived, and the one thing every entry so far has missed

`catalogRowAt` was declared on `ScopedTx` and on nothing else. Derived by grep over `packages/db/src/` rather than read off [ADR-413](ADR-413.md): `catalogRow` occurs ten times in that directory and every occurrence is in `scoped-db.ts`. `SystemTx` declared `rows`, `insert`, `rowsWhere`, `rowAt`, `lockAt`, `updateAt` and `deleteAt`, all generic over `TableKey`, all returning `unknown`. `AdminSourceTx` is an intersection of five module handles and `AdminSourceBackend.operator` hands one out. Every one of those is [ADR-413](ADR-413.md)'s claim and every one of them holds.

**THE MEASUREMENT [ADR-413](ADR-413.md) DID NOT MAKE IS THAT THE DOOR ADDS NO REACH AT ALL, AND IT CHANGES WHAT THE DOOR COSTS.** That entry treated the missing accessor as an absent capability, and read the remaining route as "the rows are reachable, as `unknown`". Both are true, and together they mean something stronger than either: `SystemTx.rowAt('planVersionSizes', at)` and a catalogue read at the same address are THE SAME SELECT.

| | `systemTx.rowAt` | `scopedTx.catalogRowAt` |
|---|---|---|
| Arity guard | `refuseUnaddressed(key, at)` | `refuseUnaddressed(key, at)` |
| Predicate | `unscopedAddressPredicate(key, at)` | `unscopedAddressPredicate(key, at)` |
| Statement | `selectStatement(source, key, predicate)` | `selectStatement(source, key, predicate)` |
| Arbitration | `oneOrNone(key, found)` | `oneOrNone(key, found)` |
| Vocabulary | `TableKey`, every table in the estate | `CatalogTableKey`, five |
| Return | `unknown` | `CatalogRow<K>` |

**SO THE DOOR IS A NARROWING OF AN EXISTING `SystemTx` READ AND NOT A WIDENING OF THE OPERATOR CONSOLE.** It admits no row a `SystemTx` could not already read, it takes the key vocabulary from every registered table down to five, and it declares what comes back. That is [ADR-303](ADR-303.md) limit 4 taken for ONE VERB ON ONE HANDLE: that entry named the `unknown` on every `SystemTx` read as a much larger surface with real callers and left it unruled, and this takes only the slice with a caller.

**IT IS HELD AS A RUN AND NOT AS THAT TABLE.** `packages/db/test/catalog-read.test.ts` drives both methods over every member of `CATALOG_TABLE_KEYS` at the same address and asserts the SQL and the binds are identical. The day they diverge, the argument for admitting the method has stopped being true and its reader finds out there.

### 5. The door, and why it carries one verb

`CatalogReadTx` is the shape, `catalogRowAt` is its one member, and `ScopedTx` and `SystemTx` both satisfy it.

**`catalogRows` AND `catalogRowsWhere` ARE ABSENT RATHER THAN UNUSED.** `ScopedTx`'s own docblock states the rule that admitting them would break, in `job-queue.ts`'s words: a primitive admitted before a caller exists is a primitive nobody can remove. A whole-table catalogue read at `'operator-console'` is a separate admission with no caller, and a case asserts the system handle carries exactly one catalogue verb so that taking it is a decision somebody sees.

**THE SAME RULE IS HONOURED FOR THE VERB THAT WAS ADMITTED AND THAT IS WORTH SAYING PLAINLY.** `catalogRowAt` on `SystemTx` has a caller in this commit, two files away.

### 7. THE EXPORT IS [ADR-413](ADR-413.md) SECTION 7'S OWN WINNER, TAKEN ONCE ITS OBJECTION MOVED

That entry weighed exporting `planLeg` as it stood against splitting it at its own first seam. **It ruled the split better, on a reason this row did not re-litigate and agrees with**: `planLeg`'s parameter is an `accounts` row and the port holds a PAIR, so a supplier would synthesise a record to fit the signature, decode the pair a second time through an `unknown` hop, and refuse in the name of a row nobody read.
