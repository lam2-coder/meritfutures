## ADR-169: the payout-destination cooling window gets a registry, and `cooling_until` is `NOT NULL` because a nullable one fails OPEN  (2026-08-27, status: proposed)

**`C-11` has been a shipped control with no input since the schema was written.** [SECURITY](../architecture/SECURITY.md) section 4 item 1 says a destination change *"starts a 48 hour cooling window during which payouts cannot settle"*, `C-24` extends it to affiliate destinations, [STATE_MACHINES section 3.2](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) draws `requested --> cooling` on `G-DESTINATION-COOLING`, `0001` carries the `cooling` enum value and `0011` carries a partial index that scans for it -- and **nothing in the database could answer when a destination changed, because `destination_ref` on [`payout_transfers`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql) and [`wallet_withdrawals`](../../packages/db/migrations/0011_wallet.sql) is the destination OF A TRANSFER.** This entry closes `OI-06 (payout destinations)` by landing [`0051`](../../packages/db/migrations/0051_payout_destinations.sql). **It TAKES [DELTA_MANIFEST](../../packages/db/DELTA_MANIFEST.md)'s recommendation on the key and the two columns and AMENDS it on the one thing the recommendation does not say**, which is nullability, and nullability is the whole control.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `169`, session **293**, dispatched as `claude/p5e-destinations` against [P5](../plans/P5-payouts-and-wallet.md) section 8's `P5-e` row. **REQUIRED rather than conditional, and the reservation says why**: the shape is a recommendation the slice *"MAY REFUSE WITH AN ARGUMENT"*, so a number was going to be spent whichever way the argument came out. It **re-takes migration `0051`**, returned to the pool unspent by [ADR-167](ADR-167.md) the same afternoon when `P7-c` took the reading that needs no table.

- **Context:** `OI-06 (payout destinations)` was raised by [FOLD-01](../plans/FOLD-01-phone-identity.md) finding 5 on 2026-08-15 and **found by trying to model [ADR-039](ADR-039.md)'s (c) on the control (c) says to copy**: the phone ceremony was told to carry *"the same 48 hour hold as a destination change"*, and the session went looking for the destination change's storage and found none. It was left **OPEN and deliberately undecided** for eleven days, on the ruling that folding an unasked-for change into the diff the founder reads line by line is how a review stops being a review. [`0029`](../../packages/db/migrations/0029_phone_identity_and_auth.sql) accordingly built the phone hold on its own storage and did not touch this.

### 2. The recommendation is TAKEN on the key and the two columns, and the argument is not deference

[DELTA_MANIFEST](../../packages/db/DELTA_MANIFEST.md)'s `OI-06` row recommends *"a `payout_destinations` registry keyed on `(identity_id, destination_ref)` carrying `first_seen_at` and `cooling_until`, read by both payout legs and by the affiliate rail under `C-24`."* Three things had to hold for that to survive contact, and each was checked rather than assumed.

**The key spans both rails only because of finding 5.** `C-24` requires affiliate destination changes to carry the same window, and if an affiliate were not an identity the recommendation's key would need a discriminator, a second table, or a nullable second column -- all three of which would put [ADR-017](ADR-017.md)'s *"one rail, one destination table, one detector"* back to being a convention. `affiliates.identity_id` is what makes the two-column key sufficient, and it has been there since `0005`.

**One row per pair is what makes the gate readable through the one door.** This is the finding that turned a plausible shape into the only available one. `G-DESTINATION-COOLING`'s read is *"is this destination cooling for this identity right now"*. With one row per pair that is a keyed lookup. **With more than one row per pair -- which is what an append-only event log is -- it becomes `max(cooling_until)`, and [ADR-157](ADR-157.md) REFUSED THE SCALAR AGGREGATE ON THE ACCESSOR**, on measured grounds this entry does not reopen. So an append-only registry would leave `P5-h`'s gate unreachable through `scopedDb` and would put the external leg back where `P5-a` found it. **The recommendation's key is not merely acceptable, it is the shape the accessor can serve.**

**The two columns are different kinds of fact and neither is derivable from the other.** `first_seen_at` is when the destination appeared; `cooling_until` is the window that was applied. They are equal in duration terms only while the configured duration never moves, and section 4 is why that is not a reason to store one.

### 3. THE AMENDMENT: `cooling_until` is `NOT NULL`, and this is the entry's ruling

**The recommendation names the column and says nothing about nullability, and nullability is the entire difference between a control and a record of one.**

Take the recommendation literally, as `cooling_until timestamptz NULL`. The gate then reads `cooling_until > now()`. **A NULL compares to nothing, so the predicate is not true, so the destination is not cooling.** An `INSERT` that omits the column -- a handler bug, a partial write, a second code path that registers a destination without arming it, a fixture -- produces a row that is **usable the instant it exists**. And the row it produces is not a random one: it is the row written when a destination is registered, which is the moment `C-11` exists to slow down.

**Under `NOT NULL` that row cannot exist.** Watched failing, on PostgreSQL 16.13, against `0001..0051` applied from empty:

```
INSERT INTO payout_destinations (identity_id, destination_ref) VALUES (...);
ERROR:  null value in column "cooling_until" of relation "payout_destinations"
        violates not-null constraint
```

This is [SECURITY](../architecture/SECURITY.md) section 4 item 9's own transferable lesson applied **before** rather than after: *"columns that record a control are not the control ... a control that exists as columns and not as a constraint reads as done in every review and stops nothing at runtime."* That sentence was written about `wallet_withdrawals` carrying `frozen_at`, `freeze_flag_id` and `freeze_expires_at` from `0011` while a halted withdrawal still matched the open index and nothing refused settlement -- **representable and unenforced, in a shipped control, for the whole life of the table.** A nullable `cooling_until` is that same shape, one table over, and it is cheaper to refuse now than to supersede later.

**It also settles a question the recommendation leaves open, and settles it fail-closed.** `C-11` says a *change* triggers cooling, and the first destination an identity ever registers is not a change. But [SECURITY](../architecture/SECURITY.md)'s own threat row is *"attacker with a stolen session requests a payout to their own destination"*, beaten by *"KYC verification, the 48 hour destination-cooling window (C-11), name matching (C-12)"* -- and **on an identity that has never withdrawn there is nothing to change from**, so a first-destination exemption is a hole that opens on every account that has not yet used the rail, which at launch is all of them. `NOT NULL` implements the fail-closed reading structurally: every row carries a window because no row can be written without one, so *"is this a change?"* is never a question a handler gets to answer wrongly.

### 4. `cooling_until` is STORED rather than computed, and that is the one derivable-looking value this table accepts

**The duration is not in the schema and that is [ADR-037](ADR-037.md)'s rule**, applied exactly as [`0029`](../../packages/db/migrations/0029_phone_identity_and_auth.sql) applied it to the phone ceremony: 48 hours is a launch candidate that lives in config, and a shorthand may not restate a value the config owns. What the database asserts is the **ordering** -- `payout_destinations_cooling_follows_first_seen` -- which is the part a config cannot get wrong.

**So why store the window at all rather than derive it from `first_seen_at` plus the configured duration?** Because [`0049`](../../packages/db/migrations/0049_reserve_coverage_snapshots.sql)'s objection is the right question to ask and its answer here comes out the other way. That header says *"a ratio stored beside its own numerator invites recomputation drift ... a ratio the database computes cannot disagree with its own inputs"*, and bought its way out with a `GENERATED` column.

**A cooling window is not a ratio, it is a PROMISE MADE AT A MOMENT.** If the configured duration moves from 48 hours to 72, a derived window would **retroactively re-cool every destination ever registered**, including ones whose windows elapsed months ago; a move the other way would retroactively release destinations that are cooling right now. Neither is a recomputation of the same fact -- each is a different fact silently replacing the stored one. And `0049`'s remedy is **structurally unavailable**, for [session 281](../sessions/2026-08-27-session-281.md)'s reason on a different table: a `GENERATED` column is a **row-local expression** and the duration is not in the row.

**Storing it is therefore not accepting drift, it is refusing a rewrite.** The drift `0049` warns about needs two values that must agree; here the second value would not exist until somebody asked for it, and the answer would depend on when they asked.

### 5. `PAYOUT-DEST-C1`: `UPDATE` survives and is bounded by a trigger rather than by a grant

Section 2 rules out the append-only shape, so `UPDATE` has to exist: [STATE_MACHINES:420](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) glosses the gate as *"destination changed **inside** the 48 hour cooling window"*, so a running window can be extended, and an extension of a single row is an `UPDATE`.

**But the only `UPDATE` anybody wants to make wrongly is one that SHORTENS the window, and a PostgreSQL grant cannot tell a lengthening from a shortening.** That is [`0033`](../../packages/db/migrations/0033_trading_calendar_revision_required.sql)'s own argument for `CALENDAR-C1` -- *"a revoke does not bind the table owner and a trigger does"* -- applied one table over. `0051` therefore grants `UPDATE` and bounds it:

| The trigger refuses | Because |
|---|---|
| `cooling_until` moving **backward** | Shortening a running window is `C-11` defeated by one statement. Equality is **permitted**, so a re-registration inside a still-longer window is a no-op rather than an error, which removes a read-then-decide race the caller would otherwise have to run |
| `identity_id` or `destination_ref` **changing** | Repointing a row moves a cooling window from the destination that earned it onto one that did not. The remedy is to register the new destination, which starts its own window |
| `first_seen_at` **changing** | Rewriting when a destination first appeared makes an established destination look new or a new one look established. It is falsifying the security record the table exists to be |

All three were seeded and **watched firing**, along with the `NOT NULL`, the ordering `CHECK`, the empty-ref `CHECK` and the `DELETE` revoke: seven rejections, seven refusals, and the two legitimate writes (a forward re-arm and an equal write) accepted.

### 6. The grants are a FOURTH shape, and the `REVOKE` was proved load-bearing in both directions

Finding 8 is the sentence this file starts from: `0026`'s `ALTER DEFAULT PRIVILEGES` means the table is **fully deletable by the application role the instant it exists**.

**The departure from every precedent is the verb set.** [`0032`](../../packages/db/migrations/0032_trading_calendar_holidays_coverage_revisions.sql), [`0039`](../../packages/db/migrations/0039_economic_calendar.sql) and [`0049`](../../packages/db/migrations/0049_reserve_coverage_snapshots.sql) each revoke `UPDATE, DELETE`, because each implements an **append-only** table. [`0050`](../../packages/db/migrations/0050_live_cache_and_role.sql) revokes `ALL`, because its sentence is about **reading**. This table is neither: it must be **readable** by `merit_app` (both payout legs and the affiliate rail run there, [INFRA section 5](../architecture/INFRA.md)) and **updatable** for the re-arm. What may never happen is that a row **vanishes**, because the row is the memory of the window and a `DELETE` then re-`INSERT` is a fresh `first_seen_at`, a fresh window, and no trace that the destination was ever here. So: `REVOKE DELETE ON payout_destinations FROM merit_app, PUBLIC`.

**Proved in both directions rather than asserted**, which is `0050`'s discipline and the reason this entry can claim the line is a control:

| Run | `merit_app` privileges | `DELETE FROM payout_destinations` as `merit_app` |
|---|---|---|
| `0001..0051` with the `REVOKE` line **removed** | `SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE` | **`DELETE 1`.** A running cooling window removed by the application role |
| `0001..0051` **as written** | `SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE` | `ERROR: permission denied for table payout_destinations` |

`merit_analytics`, `merit_dispatcher` and `merit_live` hold nothing, and only the first of those needed a decision: `0034` and `0050` gave their roles **no default privileges at all**, so a table created later is invisible to both by construction. That is the direction `0050` item 5 chose and this file inherits it by doing nothing.

### 7. WHAT THIS ENTRY FORECLOSES, which P5 rule 8 requires it to state

1. **A `provider` column, and its cost is named rather than hidden.** `destination_ref` is a **provider-side** id, so the namespace is strictly a provider's and two rails could in principle issue the same string. A provider column is refused anyway, because **the reading leg could not form the key**: `payout_transfers` carries `provider text NOT NULL DEFAULT 'rise'` and `wallet_withdrawals` carries no provider column at all, so `P5-h` would supply a hardcoded `'rise'` to look a row up -- a literal in the gate, which is the drift `E2` exists to catch. [ADR-017](ADR-017.md) rules one rail and one destination table; **the day a second rail lands, the destination namespace needs a ruling and a SUPERSEDING migration, never an edit to `0051`.**
2. **`citext`, and the obligation the refusal creates.** A provider-side opaque id may legitimately be case-sensitive, and folding case would collide two genuinely different destinations onto one row -- handing the second a window it never earned, which is the worse failure. Byte-exact is fail-closed **only if** the reader treats an **absent row** as *"register it and cool it"* and never as *"not cooling"*. **That obligation is `P5-h`'s and it is recorded here because this entry's choice of `text` is what creates it.**
3. **`verified_at` and `notified_at`.** `C-11` is *"48 hour cooling AND re-verification"* and SECTION 4 item 1 adds the notification. Both are real; neither is stored here, because `kyc_verifications` (`0003`) holds the verification and `0034`'s contact tables hold the notification, and a second copy of a fact is a second thing that can disagree with it. **`0029` could assert all three legs of `ADR-039` (c) in one `CHECK` because the CEREMONY is a row in that table. A destination registry is not a ceremony**; the ceremony's row is the withdrawal, and that is `P5-h`'s.
4. **A second registration for the same pair.** Section 2's aggregate argument. If a future reader needs the full history of when each window was armed, that is a **new table** or a superseding key change with `ADR-157`'s refusal re-argued, and it is not a quiet second row.
5. **A `DELETE`-refusing trigger, which is available and deliberately unspent.** `CALENDAR-C2` has the precedent and `0033` states why a revoke is weaker. It is affordable on `trading_calendar` because that table has a **ruled correction path** that leaves a prior image, so every legitimate need has another door. This table has no ruled procedure for a row written in error, and refusing `DELETE` from the **owner** as well would make a superseding migration the only remedy -- an operational decision no document in this corpus makes. Named, priced, not taken.
6. **An index on `destination_ref` alone.** [M07](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md)'s `D-09` destination-concentration detector reads across identities and the composite key's second column does not serve it -- but `D-09` is specified against `payout_transfers`, the detector does not exist, and `P7` owns detectors. **An index whose query nobody has written is a guess at its shape that a sacred file can never take back.** `0029`'s partial `live_hold_idx` has no analogue for a second reason worth recording: **that index exists to skip rows whose hold column is NULL, and section 3's ruling means this table has none.**
7. **An `updated_at`.** Finding 9. Nothing maintains one, so it would be a value a handler is trusted to write, which is item 9 of section 4 again.

### 8. What is OWED after this entry, stated rather than implied

- **`INV-M8-11` still has no column to read.** Finding 6. The affiliate rail can now be *told* about a destination change, and `affiliates` still carries no destination, so nothing knows what the current one is. **`M08-9` is downstream of this entry and of the rail ruling both**, and this entry does not touch `0005`.
- **`POST /wallet/withdrawals` is unblocked and unwritten.** `G-DESTINATION-COOLING` now has an input; the gate, the `cooling` status write and the `cooling_until` field on the wire are `P5-h`'s, and the absent-row obligation in foreclosure 2 is the one thing that slice must not get wrong.
- **`API_CONTRACT:722` is now stale in one direction and this entry does not edit it.** It says the field is `null` *"until that registry exists"*, and the registry exists as of this entry. [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) is `approved` and is `P5-c`'s and `P5-h`'s file, so the correction is **named here and left where the contract lives**.
- **No `SD-nn` is claimed.** This table is not a schema delta from an approved module document; it is an `OI-nn` closure, which is the disposition [DELTA_MANIFEST](../../packages/db/DELTA_MANIFEST.md) section 16 already carries a namespace for.

---

- **Alternatives considered:** an **append-only registration log** keyed `(identity_id, destination_ref, first_seen_at)`, refused in section 2 on the accessor's own refused aggregate rather than on taste; **a nullable `cooling_until` exactly as recommended**, refused in section 3 with the fail-open transcript; **a computed window** from `first_seen_at` plus config, refused in section 4 because it lets a config change rewrite promises already made; **`REVOKE UPDATE, DELETE`** on the three append-only precedents, refused in section 5 because it forecloses the re-arm `STATE_MACHINES` describes; **a `provider` column** and **`citext`**, refused in section 7 items 1 and 2 with their costs named.

- **Consequences:** `P5-h` gains its gate's input and inherits one obligation it cannot get wrong (foreclosure 2). The affiliate rail gains a registry it still cannot populate (section 8). The migration set moves 113 tables to 114 and 20 triggers to 21. `packages/db`'s registry moves 105 declared tables to 106 and 61 served by `scopedDb` to 62. **`0051` is spent, the pool is empty again, and the sequence has no hole**, which is what the conditional reservation in [ADR-167](ADR-167.md) was for.

- **Founder approval:** PENDING. **MONEY PATH. `E2` line-by-line read owed on every line of [`0051`](../../packages/db/migrations/0051_payout_destinations.sql).** Six header items name what needs reading and why; item 1 is the amendment this entry exists for.

- **What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.** Three of the decisions here are judgements rather than derivations, and all three land in a migration that can never be edited. **That `cooling_until` is `NOT NULL`** amends a recommendation that named the column and said nothing about nullability, on the argument that a NULL compares to nothing so the gate would read a missing value as "not cooling". That is fail-closed and it is right; what it costs is that every writer of this table must have a window in hand at insert time, forever, with no partial registration available. **That the FIRST destination cools too** settles a question the recommendation left open, in the direction `C-11`'s own wording does not require: the control says a CHANGE triggers cooling and this entry cools a registration. The threat row is the argument and the price is paid by every honest trader's first payout. **That the window is STORED rather than computed** refuses to let a config change rewrite promises already made, and fixes the value at the moment of registration where a later correction cannot reach it.
