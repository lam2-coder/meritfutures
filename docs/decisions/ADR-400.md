## ADR-400: `G2` is NOT enforced, and the reason [ADR-388](ADR-388.md) gave for refusing it does not hold: `RI-15` reads no dated record at all, the reachable red set is NINE, every one of the nine is ALREADY a registered finding, and two defensible instruments disagree about their class on 23 of 493; and `G1` is read by a PERSON, at 56 of 60 against the binder's 3.8 percent (2026-09-07, status: proposed)

**[ADR-388](ADR-388.md) SECTION 16 QUESTION 1 ASKED WHETHER `G2` SHOULD EVER BE ENFORCED BY A CHECK RATHER THAN MERELY REQUIRED, AND THREE ROWS HAVE NOW DEFERRED IT.** [ADR-397](ADR-397.md) section 12 item 1 handed it on again and added the condition that nobody should re-price anything about repairability *"without first deciding whether `G1` is read by a person or by `citationsIn`"*. **Both answers ship here.**

**RULED: `G2` IS NOT ENFORCED, AND NOT FOR THE REASON ON RECORD.** [ADR-388](ADR-388.md) refused option (c) because it *"would go red on 42 dated records nobody may repair"*. **A leg on `RI-15` cannot go red on a dated record at all.** That check's input is 754 source files, **NOT ONE of them a `.md`**, and its own miss (4) says it reads `docs/decisions` *"NOT AT ALL, deliberately"*. `RI-16` excludes a dated record by shape. **So the 43 born-wrong pointers in dated records on this base are in NO check's scope, and the population a `G2` leg could actually redden is NINE.**

**AND THE NINE BUY NOTHING, WHICH IS THE MEASUREMENT THAT DECIDES IT.** Every named pointer in `RI-15`'s and `RI-16`'s scopes that is wrong today is **ALREADY AN ENTRY IN THOSE CHECKS' OWN EXEMPTION REGISTERS**: 16 distinct keys, 9 of them `RI-16`'s and 7 `RI-15`'s, reconciled key by key. **A `G2` leg would detect NOTHING those checks do not already flag.** Its whole contribution is to re-label 16 known findings, and that label is the one thing this corpus cannot compute stably.

**RULED: `G1` IS READ BY A PERSON.** The binder binds a name on **290 of 7,665** pointers in dated records, 3.8 percent. A systematic one-in-123 sample of 60 drawn from the 7,375 it reads NO name for finds **56 that name a subject a later reader could search the cited file for**. The two readings are not two settings of one test; they are a check and a person, and they differ by a factor of about twenty-five.

- **THE DELIVERABLE IS TWO RULINGS, A CORPUS-WIDE CENSUS UNDER TWO INSTRUMENTS, AND TWO ANNOTATIONS AT THEIR SITES.** **No invariant number was minted, no gate was added, widened or weakened, no leg was written, no register grew, no `CI-06` letter was spent, no migration number was taken or reserved, no `status` field was moved, no test was skipped, disabled or quarantined, and no port was wired.** **`scripts/corpus/**` was never opened for writing and `packages/tooling/**` was READ ONLY**, though this row holds it.
