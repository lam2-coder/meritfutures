### 4. What was executed, and how the population was made observable

**EVERY GENERATED COLUMN WAS GIVEN ROWS THAT MAKE ITS EXPRESSION'S BEHAVIOUR OBSERVABLE, INCLUDING ITS EDGES.** The dispatch named the edges to reach: nulls where the column permits them, zero, negative where the type allows, and the boundary the expression's own arithmetic turns on.

**NULLS ARE UNREACHABLE ON FIVE OF THE SIX AND THAT IS A DERIVED FACT RATHER THAN AN OMISSION.** `mutable` and `rate_limit_exempt` are `IN` predicates over a `NOT NULL` `class`, so both are total and neither can be NULL. `delta_cents` and `intraday_movement_cents` subtract two `NOT NULL` `bigint` columns. `cadence` is the one that CAN evaluate to NULL, by a `CASE` with no `ELSE`, and it is `NOT NULL`, which is why REJECTION 5 exists at all. `rcr_bp` can evaluate to NULL through its `NULLIF`, and [`probe_reserve_coverage.sql`](../../scripts/db/probe_reserve_coverage.sql) REJECTION 1 already holds that arm.

**THE FIXTURE IS ONE FUNDED ACCOUNT AND THREE RAIL ATTESTATIONS.** [`probe_reconciliation_run.sql`](../../scripts/db/probe_reconciliation_run.sql) builds three accounts because a sweep needs a population to disagree with; nothing here sweeps, and every assertion is arithmetic on one row. **RESERVE-C1 is load-bearing on the fixture**: a coverage row's `reserve_cents` must EQUAL the attestation it names, so each coverage figure needed its own anchor carrying that same number, which cost one wrong attempt before it was read.

### 8. What EXECUTING them still leaves unproven

**THIS SECTION IS [ADR-443](ADR-443.md) SECTION 8's SHAPE APPLIED TO THIS ROW, AND IT IS NOT SHORTER.**
