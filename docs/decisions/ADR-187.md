### 2. The class, the scope, and why the two literals in the migration are not this entry's opinion

**The sign, READ and not trusted.** [`posting.ts:235-236`](../../packages/ledger/src/posting.ts) pushes `{ account: t.debit, amountCents: t.amountCents }` and `{ account: t.credit, amountCents: -t.amountCents }`. **A DEBIT is `+amount_cents` and a CREDIT is `-amount_cents`**, so a credit INCREASES a liability and a debit DECREASES one.

**The two ruled slots fix the two open ones**, and both are read at [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) section 2.1 rather than out of an entry:

| Row | The ruled slot | The open slot | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`LT-06`** [M05:135](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) | DEBITS `trader_wallet` (identity) `amount_cents` | a **CREDIT** | the account RISES when the trader's claim is extinguished |
| **`LT-07`** [M05:136](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) | CREDITS `firm_treasury` `amount_cents` | a **DEBIT** | the account FALLS when the cash leaves |

**An account that rises when a claim is extinguished and falls when the cash leaves is an obligation.** `kind` is a five-member CHECK ([`0009:40`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql)) and the elimination is short: not `revenue`, because nothing is earned when a trader moves money already theirs; not an `expense`, because no posting in this corpus writes one; not `equity`, because it moves on a rail's settlement rather than a capital event; not an `asset`, because an asset credited at `LT-06` would SHRINK at the moment a withdrawal is approved. **So `liability`.**

**And FIRM-SCOPED, which is cited rather than chosen.** [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) clause 3 rules that *"`LT-07` stays firm-only"*, refusing `trader_wallet` on arithmetic. An identity-scoped in-flight position would make `LT-07` visible to an identity-scoped check, which is the property [ADR-124](ADR-124.md) clause 3's conclusion depends on. **[`0056`](../../packages/db/migrations/0056_eighth_ledger_code.sql) writes `'firm'` for that reason and the CI probe asserts that no identity-scoped row in this code ever exists** (`LEDGER-K3`, section 6).

### 3. The spelling, and a THIRD refused class that no entry lists

**[ADR-181](ADR-181.md) section 4 refused two spellings in advance so this session would not have to rediscover them, and both refusals were checked at source.**

- **Any `payouts_*` spelling**, because *payout* is the INTERNAL leg's word -- `LT-01` is `payout_approval` and `LT-02` is `payout_settlement` ([M05:130-131](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)) -- and `SD-M5-07` retired a pooled `payouts` class, so the name would read as that class returning.

> **THE THIRD REFUSAL IS ARMED IN THIS TREE, NO ENTRY NAMES IT, AND IT REFUSES THE SPELLING TWO ENTRIES ALREADY EXECUTED.**

[`lt-07.test.ts:199`](../../packages/rail/test/lt-07.test.ts) asserts, over the declared vocabulary, `expect(codes.some((code) => code.includes('payable') || code.includes('payout'))).toBe(false)`. **That refuses `withdrawals_payable`**, which is the spelling [ADR-181](ADR-181.md) section 5 row 1 and [ADR-186](ADR-186.md) section 7 row 6 each fired at a real database AS the eighth code, and which [`probe_ledger_constraints.sql:299`](../../scripts/db/probe_ledger_constraints.sql) still uses as `LEDGER-K1c`'s live undeclared-code fixture.

**Two things follow and the entry takes the harder one.** The assertion could be narrowed to admit the name, and that is **weakening a gate to pass it**, which this corpus refuses. So the NAME moved. **The second is that `withdrawals_payable` was doubly disqualified**: it is also a live negative fixture, which is [ADR-181](ADR-181.md)'s own stated ground for refusing `firm_payable`, applied to a fixture written after that entry by [ADR-186](ADR-186.md).

**`withdrawals_in_flight` TRANSCRIBES THE CORPUS'S OWN WORDS FOR THE ROLE.**

- **`withdrawal` is the EXTERNAL leg's word**: `LT-06` is `wallet_withdrawal_approval` and `LT-07` is `wallet_withdrawal_settlement` ([M05:135-136](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)). That is what separates it from the retired pooled class, and it is the distinction the `payouts_*` refusal turns on.
- **`in_flight` is what the corpus calls the role**: [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) clause 4, [ADR-181](ADR-181.md)'s own title, [M05:135](../plans/M05-payout-system.md), and the file that prices the mint is named [`in-flight-obligation.test.ts`](../../packages/ledger/test/in-flight-obligation.test.ts). [STATE_MACHINES section 3.2](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md)'s middle state is `transferring`, which is the same interval under the rail's word.
- **It carries neither refused substring, it is not `firm_payable`, and it leaves `K1c`'s fixture undeclared**, so that probe still asserts exactly what it says and needed no repair to accommodate this ruling.
- **AND IT MATCHES HOW THIS CHART NAMES THINGS.** Six of the seven existing codes name what the account HOLDS or where it sits -- `firm_treasury`, `psp_clearing`, `reserve`, `trader_withdrawable`, `trader_wallet`, `promotional_credit` -- rather than their accounting class. Only `fees_revenue` carries a class word. A `*_payable` spelling would have been the second, and it would have been the only one whose name asserted a `kind` that a CHECK one column over already enforces.

### 4. The price, measured with BOTH tools, and the two defects in the registry that measured it

**[`in-flight-obligation.test.ts`](../../packages/ledger/test/in-flight-obligation.test.ts) registers every site and SCANS for them in both directions, so a partial transcription cannot land and a site cannot be discovered by being told about it. The price was NOT re-counted by hand.** The method was [session 341](../sessions/2026-08-28-session-341.md)'s: seed the change into one file, then read what goes red.

**THE MEASUREMENT. The eighth code was seeded into [`accounts.ts`](../../packages/ledger/src/accounts.ts) alone and nothing else was touched.**

| Tool | Result |
|---|---|
| `pnpm run typecheck` | **ZERO ERRORS.** Nothing in this tree is exhaustive over `LedgerAccountCode` in a way `tsc` refuses |
| `pnpm vitest run` | **3 files, 10 cases**, and the registry's scan named every remaining site in its failure output |

**THAT ASYMMETRY IS THE OPPOSITE OF [SESSION 341](../sessions/2026-08-28-session-341.md)'s AND IT IS WORTH RECORDING.** That session found three of six red files were invisible to `vitest` because they were compile errors, and measured with one tool. Here `tsc` sees **nothing at all**: [`pt-03`](../../packages/ledger/test/pt-03-ledger-zero-sum.property.test.ts)'s `FIRM_CODES` is typed `readonly FirmAccountCode[]`, and a list missing a member of a union is a perfectly well typed shorter list. **A code absent from that generator is a code the property test never generates, and only the registry would have said so.** Both tools, every time; which one is blind changes with the diff.

> **DEFECT 1 IN THE REGISTRY: IT REQUIRED FIVE MERGED MIGRATIONS TO MOVE, AND `E2` FORBIDS EDITING THEM.**

`ENUMERATES_ALL` held [`0009`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql), [`0027`](../../packages/db/migrations/0027_triggers_invariants.sql), [`0052`](../../packages/db/migrations/0052_chart_of_accounts.sql), [`0053`](../../packages/db/migrations/0053_firm_treasury_kind.sql) and [`0055`](../../packages/db/migrations/0055_last_two_ledger_kinds.sql), and its case *"every registered site still states exactly the set it is registered for"* asserted that each contains **every code in `LEDGER_ACCOUNT_CODES`**. A merged migration is never edited, only superseded, **so that assertion was satisfiable only by breaking `E2`**. The contradiction was inside one object: `0009`'s own `why` string read *"Superseded, never edited"* while the case beside it demanded it be edited.

**THE REPAIR IS A PARTITION AND IT IS STRICTLY STRONGER THAN WHAT IT REPLACES.** A migration states the vocabulary AS OF ITS OWN TIME. What a mint owes it is a SUPERSESSION, and what the registry owes it is the OPPOSITE assertion: **that it still says what it said, unedited, and names no code minted after it.** That is checkable, it catches an edit to a merged migration -- which the old shape could not see at all -- and it is why `0056` is the only migration in the live list.

> **DEFECT 2: THE SCAN IS BLIND TO A NEGATIVE FIXTURE, AND ONE OF THEM NAMED THE SPELLING TWO ENTRIES PLANNED TO MINT.**

`enumerating` finds files stating the WHOLE vocabulary or the FIRM subset. **A file naming an UNDECLARED code states neither and is invisible to both directions.** [`probe_ledger_constraints.sql`](../../scripts/db/probe_ledger_constraints.sql) holds two of them, and section 3 is what one of them cost. Minting such a spelling does not make the file incomplete; **it makes the file assert the opposite of what it says, silently**, because the guard it watches refuse a row would now accept it. `NEGATIVE_FIXTURES` is that third category and it goes red the day a registered fixture becomes a declared code.

**THE REGISTRY AFTER THIS ENTRY, derived by a case in the file itself rather than typed here:** **7** live enumeration sites, **5** superseded migrations, **3** firm-subset sites, **2** negative fixtures and **6** cardinality claims -- **23 entries over 18 distinct files.** The size is now asserted, so a session quoting the price is quoting something a control checked.

**AND THE PRICE WENT DOWN BY ONE SITE, which is the only part of this that was not owed.** [`accounts.test.ts`](../../packages/ledger/test/accounts.test.ts) was a cardinality claim, quoted as `'0009 declares seven codes').toBe(7)`. It now reads `.toBe(LEDGER_ACCOUNT_CODES.length)`, derived from the package, so there is no number left in it to keep true and it leaves the registry entirely. **A ninth code does not have to move it.**

### 5. Whether to seed, which is argued rather than assumed, because the two entries before this went opposite ways

**[ADR-180](ADR-180.md) seeded and [ADR-186](ADR-186.md) deliberately did not. Both reasons are good and neither is a rule.** [`0052`](../../packages/db/migrations/0052_chart_of_accounts.sql) header item 4's stated RULE is that the seedable set is the firm codes with a settled kind, and this code passes it: it is firm-scoped and its kind is ruled in the same file. **The rule is not what decides.**

**WHAT DECIDES IS `0052`'s ARGUMENT, WHICH `0055` FOLLOWED AND DECLINED ON.** [`0052`](../../packages/db/migrations/0052_chart_of_accounts.sql) seeded `fees_revenue` and [`0053`](../../packages/db/migrations/0053_firm_treasury_kind.sql) seeded `firm_treasury` **because a posting the corpus STATES resolves against each**. [ADR-186](ADR-186.md) seeded nothing because nothing posts against `psp_clearing` or `reserve`. **TWO postings the corpus states resolve against this one**: [M05:135](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)'s `LT-06` credit leg and [M05:136](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)'s `LT-07` debit leg, both ruled to this exact role by [ADR-181](ADR-181.md), and both UNPOSTABLE until the row exists.

**[ADR-183](ADR-183.md) SECTION 7 ROW 3 IS THE STANDING MEASUREMENT OF WHAT AN UNSEEDED ROW COSTS.** [`chart.ts:149`](../../packages/ledger/src/chart.ts) throws *"A posting never opens an account"*, and `LT-01` and `LT-08` had never posted in this repository's history for exactly that reason until `0054` wrote their rows. **A mint that widened the vocabulary at `E2`'s price and seeded nothing would leave both `M05` rows exactly as unpostable as it found them**, which is [ADR-181](ADR-181.md) section 4's third ground surviving in a new form after [ADR-183](ADR-183.md) had killed it.

**AND [ADR-186](ADR-186.md)'s REASON FOR NOT SEEDING DOES NOT TRANSFER.** Its hazard was that a `reserve` ROW is the first object in this tree a reader could sum and mistake for the figure `SD-M5-03` anchors OUTSIDE this ledger on purpose. **Nothing anchors this obligation outside this ledger.** Its balance is meant to be summed, and [ADR-181](ADR-181.md) section 7 already names the reader who owes it a term: `INV-M5-15`'s Open Liability, which must not quietly fall when a withdrawal is approved and the cash is still Merit's.

**SO: SEEDED. `ledger_accounts` goes from 2 rows to 3, by a PLAIN INSERT.** The first draft carried `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, which is `0054`'s idiom, and [`chart-of-accounts-kinds.test.ts`](../../packages/ledger/test/chart-of-accounts-kinds.test.ts) refused it -- section 7 finding 3.

**FINDING 6. [ADR-181](ADR-181.md) SECTION 4's `probe_ledger_constraints.sql:84` CITATION IS STALE AND ITS CLAIM IS TRUE.** `firm_payable` is at **:128** today, moved by [ADR-183](ADR-183.md) section 8.1's repair.
