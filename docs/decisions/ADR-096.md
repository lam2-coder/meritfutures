## ADR-096: an unauthenticated public read goes over HTTP to the public API, and `apps/site` never links `packages/db` at all  (2026-08-24, status: proposed)

**[P4's build plan](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md) section 10 item 6 asks what `apps/site`'s adapter reads through, offers three readings and takes none. This entry takes the third, and the finding that decides it is one P4 did not have: READINGS 1 AND 2 DO NOT REACH A SINGLE TABLE THE SITE NEEDS.** `systemDb(reason).rows(key)` at [`scoped-db.ts:155`](../../packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts) is generic over `TableKey`, `TableKey` is exactly the seven keys of `TABLES` at [`scope.ts:40-48`](../../packages/db/src/scope.ts), and **none of the eight tables [M09](../plans/M09-marketing-site.md) names is one of them.** Adding a third `SystemReason` member is not a small change; it is a **no-op** on the question that was asked. **A RULING ONLY. No `apps/site/src/catalog/adapter.ts`, no change of any kind to [`packages/db`](../../packages/db), no route, and no endpoint written.**

- **Origin:** [P4](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md) section 10 item 6, dispatched as session **185**. P4 calls it *"small, real, and `P4-e` cannot start without an answer"*, and `P4-e` is blocked on this entry alone.

- **Context:** [ADR-084](ADR-084.md) section 5 named *"the legitimate unscoped reader"* and closed the vocabulary at two members, `'nightly-batch'` and `'operator-console'`, on the argument that **"forgot to scope" becomes "wrote the word system"**. It did not anticipate a reader with no identity that is also not a Merit process. That is why this is a ruling and not a patch.

- **NOT MONEY PATH.** No ledger, no payout, no engine, no auth. It touches a money-path *property* -- the BOLA blast radius [ADR-008](ADR-008.md) scoped `scopedDb` to bound -- from outside, by deciding that the most exposed surface in the estate never acquires the ability to reach the database at all. **The direction it can fail in is a silent one and section 9 measures it.**

### 3. THE RULING

**Reading 3, and what does not exist yet is not in `packages/db`.**

1. **`apps/site` reads over HTTP, against the public API, and holds no database connection of any kind.** The adapter `P4-e` writes implements the four ports in [`ports.ts`](../../apps/site/src/catalog/ports.ts) as HTTP calls. It opens no pool, holds no credential, and imports nothing from [`packages/db`](../../packages/db).

2. **`apps/site/package.json` declares no dependency that reaches `@merit/db`, and that absence is a DECLARED property rather than a state of affairs.** The idiom already exists in this workspace and this entry only extends it: [`packages/queue/package.json:13`](../../packages/queue/package.json) states, in its own `//` key, *"IT DECLARES NO WORKSPACE DEPENDENCY, AND `@merit/db`'s ABSENCE IS THE DESIGN RATHER THAN AN OMISSION."* `apps/site` gets the same sentence for a different reason, and section 9 is why the sentence is not enough on its own.

3. **`SystemReason` stays at two members.** No third member is added by `P4-e` or by any later site work. A marketing page is not a legitimate unscoped reader; it is **not a reader of this database at all**.

4. **The one write path M9 owns, `POST /internal/revalidate`, is NOT `apps/site`'s to serve.** [`ports.ts:127-141`](../../apps/site/src/catalog/ports.ts) declares its contract and says in its own words that it *"does not run on this origin"*. It is a route on the **`api-admin`** surface by [INFRA section 2.1](../architecture/INFRA.md)'s `/internal` path prefix, and it is the only one of the five endpoints that writes. **Declaring a contract in `apps/site` and serving it from `apps/api` is not a contradiction and this clause exists so nobody later resolves it as one.**

**What this ruling is silent on**, so that a later session does not read it wider than it is: **the rendering technology and the build-versus-request timing are `P4-c`'s** ([ADR-095](ALLOCATION.md), session 183, running concurrently), and nothing here constrains either. The transport is HTTP; when the fetch happens is not this entry's.

### 4. What happens to `ADR-084`'s brand disjointness: PRESERVED, and the site's refusal deliberately does not rest on it

**The property.** [ADR-084](ADR-084.md) section 6 bought a compile-time refusal: `systemDb(...)`'s return carries `__brand: 'SystemDb'` ([`scoped-db.ts:153`](../../packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts)) and `ScopedDb`'s is `'ScopedDb'` ([`:132`](../../packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts)), so the two are not assignable in either direction. The refusal is watched failing to compile at `CI-01` with `TS2322` naming `__brand`, **with `identityId` present on the seeded value so that the brand and not a missing property is what refuses**. That last detail is the whole reason the case proves anything, and this entry does not touch it.

**PRESERVED. Not strengthened, and not spent.**

- **Not spent:** the entry adds no `SystemReason` member, exports nothing new, relaxes no brand, and makes no change to any file under [`packages/db`](../../packages/db). `git status --porcelain packages/db` reports zero on the branch that carries it. Reading 1 was the reading that would have spent it -- not by breaking the brand, but by growing the list the brand exists to keep somebody accountable for joining -- and reading 1 is refused on section 2 for a reason that has nothing to do with the brand, which is the honest ordering.
- **Not strengthened, and this is the part worth saying plainly:** a control is strengthened when it refuses something it previously admitted. **This ruling puts `apps/site` where the brand can never fire**, because a package that does not link `@merit/db` cannot name `ScopedDb` or `SystemDb` at all. The refusal for the site is **module resolution**, one level above the type system, and the brand is simply never consulted.

**A control that never has to fire is not evidence that it works, and that is a cost rather than a bonus.** [ADR-084](ADR-084.md)'s design turns *"forgot to scope"* into *"wrote the word system"*, **a diff a reviewer reads.** This ruling turns it into *"added one line to a `package.json`"*, which is also a diff a reviewer reads and is **shorter, less obviously about the database, and reviewed by whoever reviews manifests rather than by whoever reviews queries.** Section 9 measures exactly how quiet that line is, and the checkable clause in section 11 is aimed at it. **The entry states this against itself rather than claiming a strengthening it did not buy.**

### 5. Reading 1 priced: a third `SystemReason` member, and what it would actually have cost

Section 2 establishes it buys nothing. It is worth stating what it would have cost anyway, because a no-op that also costs something is the worst of the three.

- **It would have required eight `SCOPE_RULES` entries to become useful**, every one of them `firm` -- a `plans` row belongs to no identity -- so the site would read through the class [ADR-084](ADR-084.md) section 3 declares the scoped accessor **refuses**, and `TABLES` would grow from 7 to 15 for a consumer that is not the application.
- **It would have put a `pg` pool on `meritfutures.com`.** [ADR-084](ADR-084.md) section 9 records that `pg.Pool` *"needs a long-lived process and TCP"* and forecloses any HTTP or serverless deployment of **any surface** that holds it. The marketing site is the one surface in the estate where that foreclosure costs the most and buys the least.
- **It would have made the vocabulary's argument false in its own file.** [`scoped-db.ts:26-28`](../../packages/db/src/scoped-db.ts) says the reason for a reason is that *"the legitimate unscoped readers are a list somebody has to join"*. A list whose third member is *"the public website"* is a list nobody has to justify joining.

### 6. Reading 2 priced: it is the reading that reverses `ADR-008`

*"A scoped accessor with no identity"* is a contradiction in the accessor's own terms, and section 2 shows the type refuses it twice. The reason to price it rather than dismiss it is what a session under a deadline would write instead: a cast. [ADR-084](ADR-084.md) section 6 states that the brand is passable *"only by a cast somebody has to write"*, and reading 2 is the one reading that makes writing that cast the **specified** thing to do. **That is the BOLA blast radius [ADR-008](ADR-008.md) scoped the wrapper to bound, arrived at through the front door with a plan citing it.**

### 8. There is no database role a site reader could hold that is not the application's own

The strongest argument against readings 1 and 2 is not in the type system. It is in [`0026_roles_and_grants.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql), which is `E2` money path and already signed.

**Three roles exist**, at [`:40`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql), [`:46`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql) and [`:52`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql): `merit_app`, `merit_analytics`, `merit_migrator`. **There is no read-only role, no public role, and no site role.**

- **`merit_migrator`** owns DDL. Not a candidate.
- **`merit_analytics`** reads ten tables: nine enumerated at [`:127-137`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql) and `journal_entries` at [`:141`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql). **Not one of the site's eight is among them.** The file's own comment at [`:143`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql) says why in capitals: *"THE RULEBOOK IS NOT READABLE BY ANALYTICS."* The site's entire subject **is** the rulebook, so this role is refused by the ruling that created it, not merely by its grant list. [ADR-091](ADR-091.md) is the entry that widened that role most recently and it widened it toward `rule_states`, in the opposite direction from here.
- **`merit_app`** is therefore the only remaining role, and it is the one that holds `INSERT` and full DML on the mutable tables ([`:8-10`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql)).

**So a site that reads the database holds a write-capable credential on the most-attacked origin in the estate.** [`M09:57`](../plans/M09-marketing-site.md) states `INV-M9-10` as *"the marketing origin holds no session, no trader data, and no write path"*, with the rationale *"the most-attacked and least-privileged surface in the estate is also the one with nothing to steal."* **A `merit_app` connection string is something to steal and it is a write path**, and neither reading 1 nor reading 2 can avoid it without a fourth role that nobody has proposed and that would be a money-path migration with its own `E2` read.

**The corpus had already answered the transport question and the answer is one sentence long.** [API_CONTRACT:249](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md), under `GET /plans`:

> Auth: none. Cacheable (60s). **This is the endpoint the marketing site renders from**, which is how "marketing equals implementation to the tick" is structurally true rather than a promise.

**And M9's own test discipline is unwritable under either other reading.** [`M09:339`](../plans/M09-marketing-site.md), section 8.3: *"Every value rendered on a public page is asserted equal to the same value fetched from the API in the same test run. Not a snapshot of an expected number: a comparison against the source."* If the page were rendered from a database read, that assertion compares a row with the same row through two spellings of one query. **It would pass on a wrong page**, which is [ADR-084](ADR-084.md) section 7's defect exactly: a suite asserting the code against itself, agreeing with its own expectation, and unable to see the failure it was written for. **The coverage rule is only a control if the two sides come from different places, and HTTP is what makes them different places.**

### 9. The hole this ruling leaves, WATCHED GREEN rather than predicted

**Nothing in this repository stops `apps/site` importing `systemDb` today, and this was measured rather than reasoned.**

[`eslint.config.js:41-45`](../../eslint.config.js) attaches `merit/no-raw-db-client` to `apps/**/*.ts` with `packages/db/**` the single `ignores` entry, and the rule bans **client** specifiers -- `drizzle-orm`, `pg`, `postgres`, `@neondatabase/serverless` and the rest of `CLIENT_FAMILIES` at [`no-raw-db-client.js:57`](../../packages/eslint-plugin-merit/rules/no-raw-db-client.js). **`@merit/db` is not on that list and is not meant to be**, because reaching the database *through the accessor* is the sanctioned idiom the rule exists to funnel everything into. The rule is working as designed. It simply has nothing to say here.

**The seed, in a throwaway `git worktree`.** `"@merit/db": "workspace:*"` added to [`apps/site/package.json:15-17`](../../apps/site/package.json), plus one file in `apps/site/src` calling `systemDb('nightly-batch').rows('accounts')` -- a **firm-wide unscoped read of the account table, on the marketing origin**:

```
$ (cd apps/site && pnpm exec tsc --noEmit)    ->  exit 0
$ pnpm exec eslint apps packages              ->  exit 0
$ pnpm run check:invariants                   ->  7 of 7 invariants hold
$ node scripts/corpus/gates.mjs check         ->  30 of 30 gates pass
```

**Four green results on a tree that does the one thing this entry rules out.** The worktree was removed and `git status --porcelain` on the working tree reports clean; [`pnpm-lock.yaml`](../../pnpm-lock.yaml) is session 183's and was never written here, the local install being `--frozen-lockfile`.

**What DOES refuse it today is exactly one thing, and only until somebody adds one line.** Without the manifest entry, `tsc --noEmit` in `apps/site` reports `TS2307: Cannot find module '@merit/db'`. **The control is an ABSENT dependency declaration, which is a control that announces nothing when it stops holding.** That is the difference between this ruling's enforcement and [ADR-084](ADR-084.md)'s: a brand refuses at the moment of the mistake and prints the reason; a missing manifest line refuses right up until the line is added, and then refuses nothing, silently, forever.

**The remedy is one invariant and its model is already in the tree.** [`repo-invariants.mjs:155`](../../packages/tooling/checks/repo-invariants.mjs) is `RI-01`, *"packages/rules-engine declares no workspace dependencies, in any dependency field"*, whose `covers` line names it *"the MANIFEST half of the purity boundary"* and says plainly that it does **not** read the source, the ESLint half being the other. **An `RI-08` over `apps/site`'s four dependency fields, refusing any specifier that resolves to `@merit/db`, is that check pointed one directory over.** It is named here and **deliberately not written**: this session is `RULING ONLY` and [`packages/tooling`](../../packages/tooling) is outside its fence. It belongs to `P4-e`, in the same commit as the adapter, and the checkable clause in section 11 is what makes its absence visible until then.

**Stated in the direction that costs something:** as of this entry the ruling is enforced by a convention and a comment. `packages/queue` has held the same shape since [ADR-086](ADR-086.md) with the same absence of a check, so this is a second instance rather than a new class, and **an `RI-08` written to cover both is the better version of it.**

### 10. What this entry does NOT rule

- **Whether a gate reconciles a module plan's endpoint table against API_CONTRACT.** [P4 section 10 item 2](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md), session **187**, measuring concurrently. Section 7 above is one more instance for that measurement and is not an attempt to pre-empt it.
- **Where `geo_restrictions` is served from.** Section 7 establishes that the port has no endpoint anywhere. **Writing one is an API_CONTRACT amendment and this entry does not write it**, on the same ground `DEP-M9-07`'s owed amendment is not written in M9: [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) is frozen and a contract moves by ADR or by the session that holds it.
- **Anything about `apps/portal` or `apps/admin`.** Both are authenticated surfaces with an identity in hand, which is the case `scopedDb` was built for. **This ruling is about the surface with no identity and it does not generalise upward.**
- **Whether `packages/queue`'s identical undeclared-dependency shape should also be checked.** Named in section 9, not ruled.
