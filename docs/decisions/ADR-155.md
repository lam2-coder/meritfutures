## ADR-155: `ADR-022`'s v1 tier names a LINK CLASS, and "auto-enforce" is the edge being written without review rather than a transition on the flag machine  (2026-08-27, status: proposed)

**[DELIVERY_PLAN](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md)'s `P7` row says [ADR-022](ADR-022.md)'s **v1 tier only**, twice. That tier is two things, *"hard links plus KYC dedupe"*, and the corpus answers two different ways about what the second one DOES: `ADR-022`'s Behavior column says **auto-enforce**, [ADR-029](ADR-029.md) says a hard link *"bans an account without human review"*, and [STATE_MACHINES:301](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) says *"Binding: no automatic transition into `enforced`."* A `P7` detector slice cannot begin without choosing, because the choice IS the first line it writes to `risk_flags.status`. This entry rules WHICH DOCUMENT A SLICE READS and refuses the policy underneath it, because whether Merit ever bans a human on a face match without review is a decision about a false-match rate [M19](../plans/M19-kyc-identity.md) `AS-M19-05` records as demographically uneven, and that decision is the founder's.**

- **Origin:** session **266**, [`claude/p7-planning`](../plans/P7-risk-and-abuse.md), writing `P7`'s phase plan. The number was **RESERVED BEFORE DISPATCH and CONDITIONAL**, and the reservation row in [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) is amended in place under [ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3 rather than joined by a second row.

- **THE RESERVATION'S STATED CONDITION DID NOT FIRE, AND THIS ENTRY SAYS SO BEFORE IT SAYS ANYTHING ELSE.** The row reserved the number against *"an evidence pack whose redaction is BY AUDIENCE, which is the shape [ADR-068](ADR-068.md) met from the impersonation side and answered for one audience only."* **All four audiences already have a defined redaction profile**, and section 5 is the measurement. What fired is the other half of the same `P7` row. **A CONDITIONAL reservation firing on a different finding is what a CONDITIONAL reservation is for**, and [ADR-124](ADR-124.md) section 1 records the identical shape one phase over.

- **NOT MONEY BY FILE, and it decides what a detector may do to a human.** No migration, no `SD-nn`, no `packages/db` change, no route, no dependency and no gate. It moves nothing and it forecloses one line of code in every slice `P7` dispatches.

### 2. THE RULING

> **1. `ADR-022`'s v1 tier names a LINK CLASS, and "auto-enforce" is the EDGE being written without review.** What a hard signal auto-enforces is the `identity_links` row at the hard-link confidence ceiling: no operator confirms the edge, no queue holds it, and the graph carries it the moment the signal lands. **That is a real and meaningful automatic act** and it is what distinguishes `ADR-022`'s Hard row from its Soft row, whose edge queues a pre-funding review. **It is not a transition on the `risk_flags` machine.**
>
> **2. NO DETECTOR AND NO RESOLVER WRITES A `risk_flags.status` OTHER THAN `open`.** [STATE_MACHINES:301](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) states it in the negative, is `approved`, is dated after `ADR-022`, and no entry has amended it. [ADR-040](ADR-040.md) reaffirmed it a day after `ADR-022` was accepted, and `INV-M7-02` and `INV-M19-04` are the two module invariants that read it. **A `P7` slice reading `ADR-022`'s Behavior column as a state transition is reading the older of two readings, and this clause is what a prompt cites instead of re-deriving it.**
>
> **3. THIS ENTRY DOES NOT ANSWER `OQ-M7-05` AND MAY NOT.** What a biometric dedupe hit should MEAN, and whether the corpus should ever admit an automatic ban, is a decision about restricting a human on a false-match rate [M19](../plans/M19-kyc-identity.md) `FM-M19-02` and `AS-M19-05` record as producing false matches on siblings, twins, poor captures and **at demographically uneven rates**. **That is the founder's under [E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md), and a phase-planning session choosing it would be exactly the move `M07` refused when it declined to extend `ADR-039`'s phone reasoning to a face.** What this entry settles is which document a slice reads **while `OQ-M7-05` stands open**, and the answer is the frozen state machine because that is what a frozen document is for.
>
> **4. THE ROUTE FROM AN EDGE TO AN ENFORCEMENT IS THE FLAG AND THE HUMAN, AND IT IS UNCHANGED.** A hard link writes the edge and opens a flag; the flag's severity decides the queue and the clock; `investigating` is a human act with a written reason and a ToS clause; `enforced` requires an exported evidence pack id on the transition. **[ADR-041](ADR-041.md)'s identity restriction and a closure for cause both enter through that path and through no other**, on [M07](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md) section 3.3a's *"Entry points, both of them v1 ... the flags queue and the identity drill-down"*.

### 3. Why the frozen document wins, stated as an argument rather than as a date

**The date is the weakest of the three reasons and it is given first so it is not mistaken for the strongest.**

**Reason 1, the shape of the sentences.** `ADR-022`'s *"Auto-enforce"* is a two-word cell in a Behavior column whose subject is **how enforcement grades by confidence**. [STATE_MACHINES:301](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) is a paragraph headed **Binding** whose subject is **which transitions exist**. A table cell describing a class and a paragraph enumerating a machine's edges are not equally specific about the machine, and **the more specific one governs its own subject.**

**Reason 2, the invariants that would have to be false.** On the auto-enforce reading, `INV-M7-02` is false, `INV-M19-04` is false, `INV-M19-13` is false, [M07](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md) section 1.2's *"Enforcing anything automatically | admin, via M6"* row is false, and the `risk_flags` drawing is missing an edge. **On this entry's reading, one cell in one accepted entry means something narrower than a reader assumed.** A ruling that makes four approved invariants false is not a reading, it is an amendment, and an amendment to a frozen document is an ADR somebody has to write with the counter-argument in it.

**Reason 3, and it is the one that decides it.** `ADR-022`'s own **Alternatives considered** rejects *"auto-enforce on soft links to cut review load"* **outright**, and the reason it gives is the false-accusation cost: *"[AS-M19-05](../plans/M19-kyc-identity.md) already establishes that the fleet-killer is also a false-accusation engine."* **The entry's own reasoning is about who gets wrongly enforced against**, and `M19`'s measurement is that biometric dedupe produces false matches on siblings, twins, poor captures and at demographically uneven rates. **So the population `ADR-022` protects from soft-link auto-enforcement is substantially the population a hard-link auto-ban would hit**, and the entry does not notice it. That is a gap in `ADR-022`'s reasoning rather than a decision it took, which is precisely why `OQ-M7-05` exists and why this entry refuses to close it in either direction.

**The counter-argument, at full strength, because a reader will raise it.** *"Hard links auto-enforce"* is **load bearing in `ADR-022`'s economics**: the whole point of grading by confidence is that facts need no review, and a v1 tier where every fact still queues is a tier that buys less than the entry costed. **`M07` states this counter itself** and adds the one asymmetry that matters: *"a face is not reassigned by a carrier, so the phone's specific rescue mechanism has no analogue."* **Both halves belong in the ruling that closes `OQ-M7-05` and neither is settled here.** What this entry preserves is that the tier still buys something: the edge is written without review, the graph is complete at funding time, `D-12` can form candidate clusters from graph priors on day 0, and a severity-5 flag opens against both identities immediately. **The difference between that and an auto-ban is one human, and it is the human `AS-M7-04` exists for.**

### 4. `INV-M7-02`'s permission does not exist, and this entry reports it rather than repairing it

The invariant's enforcement column is precise and it is currently false:

> *"Enforced by the writer: the detector service has no grant to write `status` values other than `open`. **Not a convention, a permission.**"*

**Finding 8 is the measurement.** Three roles exist, one of them is usable by an application, and it holds full DML on `risk_flags` by [`0026`](../../packages/db/migrations/0026_roles_and_grants.sql)'s default-privileges grant. **There is no detector role, and there is no revoke.** So the control today is the code path this entry's clause 2 governs, which is a convention wearing the word permission, and [ADR-096](ADR-096.md)'s finding one origin over is the same shape: *"the remaining role is the write-capable one."*

**It is not repaired here for two reasons.** A grant is a migration and this session may not take a migration number; and **the grant's shape depends on `OQ-M7-05`'s answer**, because a role that can never write `enforced` is the wrong grant if the founder rules that a biometric hit enforces. **Naming it is the cheapest available correction** and it is [P7](../plans/P7-risk-and-abuse.md) section 10 item 2, owned by nobody today. **A finding recorded and unowned is on its way to being treated as handled**, which is [ADR-068](ADR-068.md) section 7's sentence and this is its third instance.

### 5. WHY THE RESERVED CONDITION DID NOT FIRE, measured rather than asserted

The reservation's premise was that a `regulator` audience has no defined redaction profile and that `P7` would have to invent one. **It is defined, in three places.**

| Source | Says |
|---|---|
| [M06:300](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) | *"**`internal`, `counsel`, and `regulator`**: full detail including detector internals."* |
| [The Wave 3 batch 1 gate closure](gates/wave-3-batch-1-gate-closure-2026-08-14.md) | *"A trader-facing pack shows **conduct, rule text, and the trader's own trades**. Thresholds, detector internals, parameters, and population comparisons are **internal and counsel tier only**. **The `regulator` audience follows the internal profile.**"* |
| [EC-071](../edge-cases/EC-071.md) | *"`internal`, `counsel`, and `regulator` profiles carry full detail. Every export records the profile used."* |

**So four audiences, two profiles, and `GS-112` is writable exactly as it is stated.**

**`OQ-F4-03` is still open and it asks a different question about the same column.** [ADR-068 section 3](ADR-068.md) asks whether an **impersonation record** appears in a regulator pack, and rules only the trader case: *"A pack that omits impersonation for a trader audience and a pack that omits it for a regulator are different decisions, and this entry rules only the first. The second is a legal question and belongs with the counsel sitting."* **Whether a regulator pack carries detector internals is closed; whether it carries impersonation is not.** Two questions about one column, and the closure of one is not evidence about the other, **which is `OQ-M7-05`'s own lesson arriving on a second identifier.** [P7](../plans/P7-risk-and-abuse.md) section 5.2 separates them so a slice reading `SD-M6-04` does not treat the closed one as open.

**What the audience question actually leaves is a contract row rather than a ruling.** [API_CONTRACT:706](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) requires `?reason=` and names no audience; [M06:248](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) says the route *"Requires `reason` **and** now `audience`"*; [`0008:254`](../../packages/db/migrations/0008_risk.sql) makes `audience`, `redaction_profile` and `includes_detector_detail` all `NOT NULL`; and the route [ADR-144](ADR-144.md) landed carries an `EvidenceExportRequest` of `{ accountId, reason, actor }`. **Three `NOT NULL` columns with no input.** That is [P7](../plans/P7-risk-and-abuse.md)'s `P7-b` and it is that slice's entry, on [P4](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md)'s `P4-f` precedent that a contract row enters an `approved` document through an ADR. **This number is not spent on it.**

---

- **Decision:** **`ADR-022`'s v1 tier names a LINK CLASS. "Auto-enforce" is the edge written at the hard-link confidence ceiling without review, and it is NOT a transition on the `risk_flags` machine.** No detector and no resolver writes a `status` other than `open`, on [STATE_MACHINES:301](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md), which is `approved`, dated after `ADR-022`, unamended, and reaffirmed by [ADR-040](ADR-040.md). **`OQ-M7-05` is NOT answered and is the founder's**; this entry rules which document a slice reads while it stands open. **`INV-M7-02`'s stated permission does not exist and is reported rather than repaired.** The reserved condition, the evidence-pack audience, **did not fire**, and section 5 is the measurement that refutes it.

- **What this FORECLOSES**, and it is a real cost:

  - **A `P7` slice may not grant the detector service a write path to `enforced`**, so the v1 tier ships with every hard link queued behind a human and `ADR-022`'s review-load saving is not realised at launch. **That is a cost and it is stated rather than hidden.**
  - **`ADR-022`'s Behavior column may not be cited as authority for a state transition** anywhere in `P7`, which means an author who wants one has to write the amendment to [STATE_MACHINES](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) with the counter-argument in it.
  - **It forecloses the reading in the other direction too.** A slice may not conclude from clause 2 that a hard link is *merely* a soft link, or skip writing the edge at the ceiling, or route it through a pre-funding review. `ADR-022`'s Hard row still auto-enforces something and clause 1 says what.

- **Alternatives considered:**

  - **Answer `OQ-M7-05` here, in the flag-and-review direction** (rejected, clause 3). It is the direction [ADR-039](ADR-039.md), [ADR-040](ADR-040.md), `INV-M19-04` and `INV-M19-13` all point, and taking it would still be a session deciding whether Merit bans people automatically. **[ADR-122](ADR-122.md) section 6 refused to invent a k-anonymity number on the ground that *"a number chosen by a session is exactly the kind of publication policy `SD-M12-01` puts on a versioned row with a founder's signature."* An enforcement policy is worse than a publication policy and the same refusal applies.**
  - **Answer it in the auto-enforce direction, on `ADR-022`'s own words** (rejected, section 3 reason 2). It makes four approved invariants false and removes an edge the state machine draws, which is an amendment and not a reading.
  - **Rule nothing and leave the plan to name it** (rejected, and this is the closest call). `P7`'s wave 2 is four detector slices and each one's first write is `risk_flags.status`. **A phase plan that dispatches four concurrent sessions against a question the corpus answers two ways is dispatching a coin flip four times**, and the two outcomes are "a flag" and "a ban".
  - **Amend `ADR-022`'s Behavior cell to read "auto-link"** (rejected). `ADR-022` is `accepted` and its words are the record of what was decided. **The entry is not wrong; a reader's extension of it is**, and the repair for that is a ruling naming the extension, not a rewrite that makes the misreading unavailable to a historian.
  - **Repair `INV-M7-02`'s grant in this entry** (rejected, section 4). It is a migration, and its shape depends on the answer clause 3 refuses to give.

- **Consequences:**

  - **[P7](../plans/P7-risk-and-abuse.md) section 11 rule 11 carries clause 2 into every slice prompt**, beside the accessor rule it sits next to, because both are one-line reach-arounds under deadline.
  - **`P7-h` is the slice this binds hardest**: `D-09`, `D-08` and `D-16`'s v1 half all produce hard-link signals and all three write `open`.
  - **`GS-235`'s subject line, *"v1 tier: a hard link auto-enforces"*, is now a scenario whose subject the corpus does not support**, and it is `blocked / no-fixture-format` today, which is the only thing preventing it from settling `OQ-M7-05` by being written. **A fixture is a control**, which is [ADR-042](ADR-042.md)'s ruling read forwards. The row's file is outside this session's fence; it is [P7](../plans/P7-risk-and-abuse.md) section 10 item 5 and it belongs to whoever holds the golden-scenario register.
  - **Nothing in the rules engine moves.** No gate, no rule, no migration, no schema delta, no route.
  - **`OQ-M7-05` stays open and now has a named consequence** rather than only a name: until it is ruled, the v1 tier ships flag-and-review.

- **Founder approval: NOT GIVEN. `status: proposed`, and this line stays UNSIGNED.** The entry rules a reading and refuses the policy, but the reading it takes has a cost `ADR-022` did not price, which is section 3's counter-argument, and **the founder should read clause 3 and section 3 together before `P7-h` is dispatched.**
