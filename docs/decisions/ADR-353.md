### 2. RULING ONE: all three members are served, and a refusal here would have had to be manufactured

**THE DISPATCH SAID A REFUSAL WOULD BE THE SURPRISING OUTCOME HERE RATHER THAN THE EXPECTED ONE, AND IT IS RIGHT.** Three rows this wave served four of five ports and refused the fifth, and in every one of those three the refused member was a capability this repository does not contain. **THIS PORT CONTAINS NO SUCH MEMBER**, and the reason is a property of the alarm rather than luck:

| Member | Served by | Why nothing is missing |
|---|---|---|
| `read` | `WorkerDb.batch`, narrowed | `report_schedules` and `report_deliveries` are both `firm` in `scope.ts`, so no identity cast, no second door and no scope predicate is in play. `SystemTx` is the handle `src/db.ts` already opens |
| `terms` | `atLeast`, re-exported by `src/db.ts` | `DigestTerms` declares ONE constructor, [ADR-157](ADR-157.md) clause 1 admits it, and [ADR-349](ADR-349.md) already carried it through the one door |
| `now` | the injected process clock | `job.ts` already spends `() => new Date()` at the wiring for the nightly fold |

**THE ALARM WRITES NOTHING, EMITS NOTHING, AND READS NO TRADER-BEARING TABLE**, which is why there is no fourth thing to withhold. `expiry-adapter.ts` takes its fifth member as an argument nothing can be passed for because every leg of that sweep emits; `detectors/adapter.ts` defaults its fifth to a refusal for the same reason; `batch/statistics-adapter.ts` refuses three of nine because a published statistic would otherwise be computed over a fact this estate cannot produce. **None of those shapes has an analogue here, and inventing one would have been a refusal written to look rigorous.** The dispatch put the burden of proof on a refusal and no refusal met it.

**THE ADAPTER HOLDS NO CAST PAST A KEY TYPE, AND THAT IS MEASURED RATHER THAN CLAIMED.** `detectors/adapter.ts` calls its `tx.rowsWhere(key as never, where as never)` *"THE ONE CAST IN THIS DEPLOYABLE PAST A KEY TYPE"* and buys back what it gave up with a conditional-type alias and a run-time membership set. **Neither is needed here.** `DigestReadTable` is two string literals and `DigestFilter` is `Readonly<Record<string, unknown>>`, and `SystemTx.rowsWhere` accepts both as written. A `'notATable'` member added to `DIGEST_READ_TABLES` is `tsc` error **`TS2345` at the call site**, which is the same protection the alias buys, obtained from the call instead. **Watched red before the adapter was written, and restored.** `test/digest-alarm-adapter.test.ts` case 5.4 keeps the file free of `as never` and `as unknown` so the property cannot be quietly lost.
