## ADR-181: the external leg's in-flight obligation is a firm-scoped liability and none of the seven codes can hold it, so the shape is ruled and `0054` returns to the pool unspent  (2026-08-28, status: proposed)

**[ADR-174](ADR-174.md) section 3 declined to rule which account carries the external leg's in-flight obligation and named three shapes, each of which needed an answer to its finding (C). [ADR-180](ADR-180.md) supplied that answer and this entry uses it. The role's CLASS and SCOPE are derivable and are derived here: a FIRM-SCOPED `liability`. No code in the chart of accounts can hold one.** That is [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) section 3 shape (i), and shapes (ii) and (iii) are refused in section 3 with their costs stated rather than left unmentioned. **What this entry does NOT do is mint the code**, and migration `0054` is RETURNED TO THE POOL UNSPENT, because the eighth code's price is larger than [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) finding 3 measured and seven of the thirteen sites it must move are outside this session's fence.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `181`, session **330**, dispatched as `claude/lt07-repair`. **`0054` was CONDITIONAL on the chosen shape needing one AND the ruling being settled enough to seed.** The shape needs one; section 4 is why the ruling is not settled enough to seed today and what makes it settled. [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) and [ADR-177](ADR-177.md) each returned a number unspent and the pool is the mechanism rather than the exception.

- **MONEY PATH. NO MIGRATION LANDS, SO NO [`E2`](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) READ IS OWED ON ONE.** `0009`, `0027`, `0038`, `0052` and `0053` are byte for byte unchanged, the vocabulary stays closed at seven, and `ledger_accounts` still holds exactly two rows. The `E2` read is owed on the diff that mints the code and is not discharged here.

- **THE SHAPE IS A JUDGEMENT ABOUT WHERE AN IN-FLIGHT OBLIGATION LIVES AND THIS ENTRY SAYS SO PLAINLY.** What is DERIVED is that the role is a firm-scoped `liability` and that no declared code is one; sections 1 and 2 are that derivation and it is executed against a database in section 5. What is JUDGED is that a new class is the right home for it rather than a re-reading of one of the two silent codes, and section 8 states that question for the founder. The entry ships `proposed` with an UNSIGNED approval line.

### 1. The role's class and its scope, derived from the two slots [ADR-180](ADR-180.md) already ruled

**The convention, read and not trusted.** [`posting.ts:235-236`](../../packages/ledger/src/posting.ts) pushes `{account: t.debit, amountCents: t.amountCents}` and `{account: t.credit, amountCents: -t.amountCents}`, under a header at [`posting.ts:29`](../../packages/ledger/src/posting.ts) calling signs the thing *"THIS CORPUS HAS GOT BACKWARDS FOUR TIMES IN ONE DAY"*. [`0009:118`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql) states the same from the schema side. **A DEBIT is `+amount_cents` and a CREDIT is `-amount_cents`**, so a debit increases an asset or an expense and decreases a liability, a revenue or an equity.

**[ADR-180](ADR-180.md) left one slot open in each row and did not leave it free.** Each row's OTHER slot is ruled, and the ruled slot plus the one sign convention fixes which side the open slot sits on.

| Row | The ruled slot | Therefore the open slot | What a movement on that side means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`LT-06`** `wallet_withdrawal_approval` | DEBITS `trader_wallet` (identity) `amount_cents`, unchanged since `SD-M5-07` | a **CREDIT** of `amount_cents` | a credit INCREASES a liability, a revenue or an equity |
| **`LT-07`** `wallet_withdrawal_settlement` | CREDITS `firm_treasury` `amount_cents` ([ADR-180](ADR-180.md)) | a **DEBIT** of `amount_cents` | a debit DECREASES a liability, a revenue or an equity |

**So the same account rises when the trader's claim is extinguished and falls when the cash leaves.** [`0052`](../../packages/db/migrations/0052_chart_of_accounts.sql) rules `trader_wallet` a `liability` and [`0053`](../../packages/db/migrations/0053_firm_treasury_kind.sql) rules `firm_treasury` an `asset`, and both are read here out of the constraint the migration set INSTALLS rather than out of either file by name, because a CHECK cannot be extended in place and the last one added is the one the database has.

**The elimination over the five kinds, each step written out.** `kind` is a five-member CHECK ([`0009:40`](../../packages/db/migrations/0009_ledger.sql)). It is not `revenue`: nothing is earned when a trader moves money that is already theirs, and [`checkout.ts:1673`](../../apps/api/src/routes/checkout.ts) states in shipped source that `fees_revenue` is *"the only one of the seven whose `kind` is `revenue`"*. It is not an `expense`: no posting in this corpus writes one. It is not `equity`: it moves on a rail's settlement rather than on a capital event. It is not an `asset`: an asset credited at `LT-06` would SHRINK at the moment the withdrawal is approved, which is nothing happening to the firm's holdings, and it would then be debited back at settlement, which reproduces exactly the zero [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) finding 6 measured on the wrong account. **So it is a `liability`**, which is what [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) clause 4 named it in words: *"an obligation exists between the two moments and must be recorded somewhere."*

**And it is FIRM-SCOPED, which is cited rather than chosen.** [ADR-174](ADR-174.md) clause 3 rules that *"`LT-07` stays firm-only"*, refusing `trader_wallet` on arithmetic rather than on scope. This entry does not reopen that, and a reader should know what reopening it would cost: an identity-scoped in-flight position would make `LT-07` visible to an identity-scoped check, which is the exact property [ADR-124](ADR-124.md) clause 3's conclusion depends on.

### 6. THE FINDING THIS SESSION FOUND, WHICH WAS NOT IN THE DISPATCH

**`wallet_withdrawals` has a terminal `failed` state with no ledger posting anywhere in the corpus, and naming the in-flight obligation is what makes that visible.** [STATE_MACHINES section 3.2](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) draws `transferring --> failed: G-TRANSFER-EXHAUSTED` and rows it with the event `wallet.withdrawal_failed`. [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) section 2.1 declares eight transactions, `LT-01` through `LT-08`, and **not one of them is a wallet withdrawal that failed on the rail.**

**Under the shape this entry rules, a rail-exhausted withdrawal leaves the obligation standing forever**: `LT-06` posted, so the trader's wallet claim is extinguished; `LT-07` never posts, so no cash leaves; the obligation carries a credit balance against a withdrawal that will never settle, and the trader's money is in neither place they could be shown it. **The repair is a ninth transaction that reverses `LT-06`, and [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) `SD-M5-05` already carries the mechanism for it** (`reversal_of`, corrections are compensating entries and never updates).

**This is not created by this entry and it is not evidence against its ruling.** `LT-06` posts at approval today, under an approved plan, so the gap exists on any answer to `LT-07`'s credit slot and existed before [ADR-174](ADR-174.md). **It IS the one genuine argument for shape (ii)**, where nothing posts until settlement and a failed withdrawal therefore strands nothing, and section 3 refuses (ii) on a cost that is larger rather than on that argument being weak.

**No ninth transaction is minted here, no id is claimed for one, and no edge case is filed.** A transaction id is a member of a declared identifier series, which `CI-06/identifier-series` refuses to let a citation invent, and a new posting is a design; both are larger than *"which account carries the in-flight obligation"*, and this entry's fence is that question. **It is recorded in [STATE](../STATE.md) as owed** rather than left in an entry a later session would have to find.
