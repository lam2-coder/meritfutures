## ADR-137: `OI-16` never held `GS-004` and `GS-031`'s expected end state, and the blocker on both is the calendar record's missing `halted` key  (2026-08-27, status: proposed)

**Two rows have sat on `open-question` for seven days against an item whose own register says it does not hold them.** [`OI-16`](../STATE.md) asks a question about a VENDOR FEED. `GS-004` and `GS-031` ask a question about the ENGINE. The first is open, is real, and stays open; it is not what stops either fixture from being written. **What stops them is that a golden fixture cannot say a session was halted**, and the fixture calendar's own record field names those two scenario ids while saying so.

- **Origin:** session **249**, [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row 137, **RESERVED BEFORE DISPATCH and CONDITIONAL**: *"reserved because `open-question` is a term whose two members may each need an answer rather than a disposition."* **The condition is met, both members needed an answer, and section 3 is it.**

- **NOT MONEY PATH.** Nothing here reads or writes a money field, changes a rule, or touches a migration. It moves a blocker term on two rows of [section 39](../testing/golden-scenarios/39-fixture-status-and-blockers.md) and it amends nothing frozen except that table's own cells, which [ADR-072](ADR-072.md) makes the live record by design.

- **It amends a founder-approved assignment and says so first.** [ADR-072 section 3](ADR-072.md) put these two rows on `open-question` in its own reconciliation table (*"3 held on an open question | 2, `GS-004` and `GS-031` on `OI-16`"*), and that entry is signed. **This entry does not dispute the count and does not retire the term.** It disputes which of two true statements belongs in a column whose rule admits exactly one, and it applies ADR-072's own tie-break to decide.

---

### 1. The question, named exactly, because naming it is half the ruling

[`OI-16`](../STATE.md), opened by [session 75](../sessions/2026-08-19-session-75.md) under [ADR-059](ADR-059.md) recommendation 3, reads in full:

> **Does any market-data or platform feed Merit will hold report a whole-session halt or limit lock, at the granularity `R-04` consumes it (one boolean per trading day per product)?** If one does, name the feed and the field. If none does, `halted` has no publisher and the answer is a decision about whether Merit sources one.

Its own register cells answer three things about it before this entry says anything:

| Cell | What it says |
|---|---|
| **State** | *"BLOCKED ON A RETRIEVAL. Not blocked on a design, not blocked on a type"* |
| **Who** | *"The founder or the vendor call. There is no engineering content in it"* |
| **What it does NOT hold** | *"`R-04` itself. The rule is implemented, declared, published in the constitution's B4 answer and restated in [GLOSSARY](../GLOSSARY.md), [EC-013](../edge-cases/EC-012-to-033-appendix-b4-battery.md) and [M14](../plans/M14-loyalty-retention.md)'s `GS-185`"* |

**The third cell is the finding.** [ADR-072 section 2](ADR-072.md) admits a row to `open-question` on one condition: *"A named `OI-nn` or `OQ-nn` holds the expected END STATE, and the item is open."* `GS-004`'s expected end state is *"halted session advances day counters but not win days"* and `GS-031`'s is *"calendar trading days advance, win days do not. Cadence gap and min-day counters progress."* **Both are `R-04`, and `OI-16`'s register says in its own words that it does not hold `R-04`.** The term's condition is unmet on the register's own text.

### 2. Is the question answerable here? NO on the retrieval, YES on the hold

**The retrieval is out of reach and this entry does not attempt it.** [ADR-059 section 6](ADR-059.md)'s honest limit is carried forward unchanged: *"'I could not find it' and 'it does not exist' are different claims, and only the first is proven."* No vendor specification is in this repository, [M02](../plans/M02-rithmic-bridge.md) holds at `status: review` under [ADR-005](ADR-005.md), and a session cannot make a vendor call.

**What deciding it would cost, stated because the dispatch asked and because nobody has costed it:**

| Path | Cost |
|---|---|
| **A feed reports it** | The retrieval is one field lookup on the Rithmic session or account API during the call that already gates [M02](../plans/M02-rithmic-bridge.md). Zero engineering; it rides an event already on the calendar |
| **No feed reports it** | A DECISION, not a build: either Merit sources a halt calendar (the exchange publishes halts; the transcription shape already exists at [`packages/db/src/seed/calendars/`](../../packages/db/src/seed/calendars/README.md), whose generator writes `halted: false` twice by constant and carries session 75's two comments saying why), or `R-04` stays implemented and unreachable and Merit says so |

**Neither path is a fixture-format question, and that is the whole point.** A golden fixture states its own inputs by hand. **Whether production will ever supply a halted day has no bearing on whether the engine, GIVEN a halted day, advances counters and withholds the win day.** That is what the two scenarios assert and it is decided.

### 3. The tie-break, run in both directions at the source

[ADR-072 section 2](ADR-072.md): *"When more than one condition fails, the row carries the blocker that WOULD STILL HOLD IF EVERY OTHER WERE CLEARED."*

| Direction | Result |
|---|---|
| **`OI-16` is answered tomorrow. Can the fixture be written?** | **NO.** The calendar record has no key for `halted` and the loader writes `false` unconditionally. **W2 still holds** |
| **The record grows a `halted` key tomorrow. Can the fixture be written?** | **YES.** `CalendarDay.halted` already exists, `advanceDay` already reads it, `R-04` is already declared and implemented, and the end state is already decided. **W3 does not still hold** |

**Only one survives its counterfactual and it is W2.** Four primary sources, each read rather than quoted from a prior session:

| # | Fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The loader reduces a calendar session to AT MOST TWO KEYS**, `trading_day` and an optional `kind` | [`loader.ts:569`](../../packages/golden-loader/src/loader.ts) declares the row type and [`:596`](../../packages/golden-loader/src/loader.ts) builds it. Nothing else on a session survives `loadCalendar` |
| 2 | **`halted` is written `false` on every day, by a constant, and the file refuses to invent a key** | [`calendar.ts:158`](../../packages/golden-loader/src/calendar.ts). Its header at [`:46`](../../packages/golden-loader/src/calendar.ts): *"`halted` HAS NO SOURCE AND IS `false` ON EVERY DAY ... The record has no key for it; inventing one here would be this file deciding a session was halted"* |
| 3 | **THE FIXTURE CALENDAR NAMES THESE TWO ROWS ITSELF, IN THE TERM'S OWN LANGUAGE** | [`cme-2026.json:5`](../../packages/rules-engine/fixtures/calendars/cme-2026.json), field `what_this_format_cannot_carry`: *"A record key plus a loader mapping would be needed to change that, which is `halted`'s blocker exactly (GS-004, GS-031)."* **A record field, on disk, states the blocker this entry rules, and section 39 rowed the other one** |
| 4 | **The engine side is complete**, so nothing beyond the format is missing | [`types.ts:222`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/types.ts) declares `CalendarDay.halted` with `R-04` on its doc line, and [M01](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) section 3.5 files `R-04` as **Engine** and implemented |

**Fact 3 is the one that makes this a mis-filing rather than a judgement call.** The two candidate blockers were both written down, in two files, and the table took the weaker one.

### 4. The ruling

1. **`OI-16` is ANSWERED for the purpose of `GS-004` and `GS-031`, and the answer is that it never held them.** The item stays **OPEN** as a production-data question and is not closed, narrowed or reworded by this entry.
2. **Both rows move from `blocked | open-question` to `blocked | format-cannot-express`**, with citations to facts 1, 2 and 3. Neither row moves status: both stay `blocked`, and no fixture becomes writable.
3. **`open-question` stays in the closed vocabulary and holds ZERO rows.** It is not retired. Section 39 already carries the precedent in its own words for `writable`: *"a term in the vocabulary with a count of zero, which is what it should read"*, and the next scenario a named open item genuinely holds reads `open-question` again.
4. **What would clear these two rows is now one sentence and it is a record key plus a loader mapping.** `halted` on the calendar record, read by `calendarRowsFromRecord` the way `kind` already is. **This entry does NOT propose it**, on ADR-072's own rule that a term *"is a statement about the format as it stands, never a proposal that the format should grow."*

### 5. What `OI-16` holds after this entry, which is nothing, and why that is worth recording

[`OI-16`](../STATE.md)'s **What it holds** cell reads *"`GS-004` and `GS-031`, both of which stay in group A and stay in the denominator."* **After clause 2 it holds neither**, and no other row in [section 39](../testing/golden-scenarios/39-fixture-status-and-blockers.md) names it.

**[STATE](../STATE.md) is outside session 249's fence and is NOT edited.** The correction is recorded here and in the pull request rather than performed, on the precedent [ADR-059](ADR-059.md)'s own application set: *"reported as a finding rather than acted on, exactly as the ruling instructs."* **An open item that holds nothing is still open**; what it loses is the claim that two fixtures are waiting on it, which is the claim that made it look like fixture work rather than a line on a vendor call agenda.

### 7. Alternatives considered

- **Leave both rows on `open-question` and rule nothing.** Rejected, and the reason is the dispatch's: a scenario blocked on an open question is not blocked on a fixture, so filing it under a term nobody queries is how a decision stays invisible. **It stayed invisible for seven days here and the record that would have caught it was a JSON field naming the two ids.**
- **Close `OI-16`.** Refused. This session cannot perform a retrieval and *"I could not find it"* is not *"it does not exist"* ([ADR-059](ADR-059.md) section 6). **Closing an item on a session's own authority is exactly the move ADR-072's citation rule exists to make impossible.**
- **Retire `open-question` from the vocabulary now that it holds nothing.** Rejected. The vocabulary is closed and shrinking it costs the next row that needs it; `writable` already sits at zero without being retired, and section 39 argues that case in its own text.
- **Split the two rows, ruling `GS-004` and holding `GS-031`.** Rejected. Both fail on the same key in the same file, and [session 75](../sessions/2026-08-19-session-75.md) already recorded what filing rows with different blockers together costs. These two have the SAME blocker, checked separately and found identical.
- **Propose the `halted` record key here, since the fix is one mapping.** Rejected on ADR-072's rule quoted in clause 4, and on fence: the calendar record and the loader are both outside session 249's fence, and a term that proposes its own repair stops being a statement about the format as it stands.

### 8. Consequences

- **`open-question` reads zero and `format-cannot-express` gains two**, which are the counts a wave schedules against. **The queue did not grow**; two rows moved from a term that reads *"waiting on a decision"* to one that reads *"waiting on a format"*, and only the second is engineering work anybody in this repository can do.
- **`OI-16` becomes a line on the vendor-call agenda and stops looking like fixture backlog.** Its **Who** cell always said so.
- **One correction is owed in [STATE](../STATE.md) and it is outside this fence**: `OI-16`'s **What it holds** cell. It is named in section 5, in the pull request and in the session entry so it is not discovered a fourth time.
- **The `no-module-code` lesson repeats itself at two rows.** [ADR-072](ADR-072.md) refused a vocabulary term that *"names the half which clears first"*. `open-question` on these two rows was that defect arriving anyway, one term over: `OI-16` is the half that clears first, and the format objection is the half that survives it.
