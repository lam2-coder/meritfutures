## ADR-152: an as-of stamp's staleness is a claim the SERVER makes and the portal ORDERS, never one the portal computes, and no account-state contract publishes the fact today  (2026-08-27, status: proposed)

**[`152`](ALLOCATION.md) was reserved CONDITIONALLY, for the case where rendering forced a ruling on the trading day or on the as-of stamp. It forced one, on the second.** [INV-M4-02](../plans/M04-trader-portal.md) puts the day on the screen and **it does not put the STALENESS on the screen**, and the distance between those two sentences is where a transparency surface tells its first lie. A correctly labelled `as_of_trading_day` that is four sessions behind renders identically to one computed an hour ago: same field, same wording, same confidence. [`view/as-of.ts`](../../apps/portal/src/view/as-of.ts) already states the general form of the failure, one step short of this one: a trader "has been told something false BY A SCREEN THAT CONTAINS NO FALSE STATEMENT."

**The trading day forced no ruling and the reason is worth recording rather than leaving as a silence.** Every fact the calendar and timeline screens need is already a stored column that arrives answered: `release_trading_day` is stored and never derived, and `TimelineItem.trading_day` is nullable and the server decides the null. Rendering them required a component that cannot accept an instant, which is a design decision inside a fence and not a ruling. **The as-of stamp is different because the fact it needs does not exist.**

- **Origin:** [P4](../plans/P4-portal-and-site.md)'s portal-page wave, dispatched as session **264**, branch `claude/portal-calendar`. The number was **RESERVED BEFORE DISPATCH and CONDITIONAL** ([ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md)), so none was chosen here; the `152` row is amended **in place** per [ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3.

- **NOT MONEY PATH.** Nothing here touches the engine, the ledger, payout or auth, so [ADR-003](ADR-003.md)'s strict regime does not bind and no [E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) read is owed. It rules on what a read surface may say about its own freshness, which is a display obligation.

### 1. The findings, every one re-derived at source before anything was ruled

| # | Claim | Verified |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **`INV-M4-02` requires the DAY and says nothing about whether the day is CURRENT** | [M04 section 1.3](../plans/M04-trader-portal.md): "Every screen showing account state labels the last closed day it is as of \| `as_of_trading_day` is a required prop on every account-state component." The enforcement is a required prop, and a required prop carrying a stale value satisfies it exactly as well as one carrying a fresh value |
| 2 | **The portal is forbidden to derive a day from an instant, and the refusal is already written down** | [`view/as-of.ts`](../../apps/portal/src/view/as-of.ts): "THE DAY IS A STRING AND IS NOT PARSED ... the portal has no trading calendar. A `Date` here would be the client deciding what day a timestamp falls in" |
| 3 | **There is nothing for the portal to import even if it wanted to** | [`packages/rules-engine/src/calendar.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/calendar.ts) exports `buildCalendarSlice`, a lookup, `nextTradingDayAfter` and a gap count over a slice THE CALLER SUPPLIED. Its header refuses to make the slice a capability by name, and `CalendarSliceIsData` makes the alternative a compile error. **No function anywhere in the workspace maps an instant to a trading day** |
| 4 | **The engine's own day comparison is LEXICOGRAPHIC and involves no arithmetic** | [`calendar.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/calendar.ts): "every day comparison in the engine is lexicographic on a zero-padded ISO day, which is chronological order with no arithmetic". This is what makes clause 2 below a permitted operation rather than a calendar in disguise |
| 5 | **Exactly one contract in the portal's wire surface carries a freshness fact, and it is not an account contract** | [`api/types.ts`](../../apps/portal/src/api/types.ts): `EconomicCalendarFreshness` carries `stale`, "the server's answer, evaluated against its own threshold", and `covered_through_day`. `grep` over that file for `as_of`, `last_closed`, `closed_through` and `stale` returns **four sites**: two `as_of_trading_day` fields and the two freshness fields. **No account-state contract publishes the day the firm has closed through** |
| 6 | **`API_CONTRACT` ASSERTS that `as_of_trading_day` IS the last closed day, in a comment, and that assertion is exactly what makes a confident render feel safe** | [API_CONTRACT section 6](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md), `AccountListItem`: `as_of_trading_day: string;  // the last closed day; every number above is as of this date`. **The comment is true when the response is composed and says nothing about when it is read.** A cached body, a batch that did not run, and a degraded window all leave it literally true and leave the trader reading it as "today's close" |
| 7 | **The concept the missing field would carry is already DEFINED in the corpus** | [GLOSSARY](../GLOSSARY.md), `last closed day`: "The most recent trading day for which the nightly batch has completed and marks are final." **It is defined, it is what every gate is evaluated against, and no endpoint serves it as a firm-level fact** |
| 8 | **`ADR-122` is a reproduction claim over a published figure and it is NOT about this surface** | [ADR-122](ADR-122.md) rules `published_statistics.input_digest`, on M12's PUBLIC transparency surface, `status: proposed`. Its subject is whether a published statistic can be RECOMPUTED from the same inputs, and its clause 2 excludes the answer from the digest. **The dispatch that commissioned this session cited it as ruling "over exactly this kind of published figure"; that is an analogy and not an application**, and section 4 below takes what carries across and names what does not |

### 2. THE RULING

> **1. AN AS-OF STAMP'S FRESHNESS IS A CLAIM THE SERVER MAKES. The portal never computes it, from a clock or from a calendar.** Finding 2 forbids the calendar and finding 3 removes the tooling; a clock would additionally make the answer move at midnight in a timezone nobody chose. **The portal's whole job on this surface is to render a fact and to say plainly when it has not been given one.**
>
> **2. THE ONE OPERATION THE PORTAL MAY PERFORM IS ORDERING TWO TRADING DAYS THE SERVER SUPPLIED, AS STRINGS.** Finding 4: that is the engine's own day comparison, it consults no calendar and reads no clock, and it is the only way a client can check a freshness claim rather than take one. `freshnessAgainst(as_of_trading_day, closed_through_day)` in [`as-of-stamp.tsx`](../../apps/portal/src/app/calendar/as-of-stamp.tsx) is the only producer.
>
> **3. THE FRESHNESS IS A REQUIRED PROP AND `unstated` IS ONE OF ITS ARMS.** Enforced the way `INV-M4-02`, `INV-M4-11` and `INV-M4-14` are all enforced: a screen that renders an as-of day without a freshness claim does not compile. **A two-valued fresh-or-stale union would be worse than nothing**, because every caller holding no fact has to pick one and the one they will pick is the one that renders cleanly. `DEP-M4-09`'s rule, quoted by [`view/economic-calendar.ts`](../../apps/portal/src/view/economic-calendar.ts), is the general form: "the dangerous failure is not the empty panel, it is the confident one."
>
> **4. A FIGURE DATED AFTER THE DAY THE FIRM SAYS IT HAS CLOSED THROUGH IS A REFUSAL, NOT A THIRD RENDERING.** The server has said two things that cannot both be true and every available rendering of that is a guess about which one is wrong. `AsOfContradictionError` names both days and stops. This is the idiom the module already uses twice, at [`view/economic-calendar.ts`](../../apps/portal/src/view/economic-calendar.ts) on an unparsable instant and at [`view/rules.ts`](../../apps/portal/src/view/rules.ts) on a version that published no copy.
>
> **5. THE GAP IS NAMED AND NOT CLOSED HERE. No account-state contract publishes the firm's closed-through day (finding 5), so EVERY ACCOUNT-STATE SCREEN IN THIS APPLICATION RENDERS `unstated` TODAY, and that is the correct render rather than a defect.** Adding the field is a ruling on [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) and belongs to whoever owns that surface, not to a portal session. **What this entry refuses is the alternative**: inventing the field in the portal's own type file so a screen can look current.
>
> **6. A DIVERGENCE BETWEEN TWO ACCOUNTS' `as_of_trading_day` VALUES IS NOT A FRESHNESS FACT AND MAY NOT BE USED AS ONE.** A trader holding two accounts whose days differ has evidence that a batch is behind on one of them. That is a finding about the batch and the portal is not the place it is adjudicated, so `freshnessAgainst` takes the firm's day and never a sibling account's.

### 3. What clause 5 costs, stated rather than softened

**Until an endpoint publishes the firm's closed-through day, the honest rendering of every account-state as-of stamp is the sentence "Merit has not published which trading day it has closed through, so this screen cannot tell you whether that is the most recent one."** That is a worse-looking screen than the one that says nothing, and it is the trade this entry takes deliberately.

The argument is the one finding 6 makes. The screen that says nothing is not neutral: it renders a labelled day under a heading the trader has every reason to read as current, and `API_CONTRACT`'s own comment says it IS the last closed day, so the confident reading is the one the corpus itself teaches. **A surface whose differentiator is that the trader can check the firm's work has to be checkable on this too**, and the only checkable form is two days beside each other. Where the second day does not exist, saying so is the only remaining honest move.

**It is also the cheapest possible pressure toward the repair.** A gap recorded in a document is a gap nobody is reminded of; a gap that renders a sentence on every account screen is one the next reader of that screen asks about.

---

### 4. `ADR-122`, what carries across and what does not

**Finding 8: it is a different surface and this entry does not inherit it.** [ADR-122](ADR-122.md) rules a `bytea` on `published_statistics`, an append-only public table, and its subject is REPRODUCTION: whether the same inputs recompute to the same number. An as-of stamp makes no reproduction claim at all. It makes a RECENCY claim, and the two fail differently: a reproduction claim fails when an input the arithmetic saw was outside the digest, and a recency claim fails when nothing on the screen distinguishes a figure computed tonight from one computed last week.

**What does carry across is the POSTURE, in one clause and in the direction that constrains rather than excuses.** ADR-122 clause 4 publishes a limit rather than papering over it: two matching digests mean both runs read the same definition over the same window and the same inputs, and **not** that they produced the same number or ran the same code, and "both limits belong on the method page". Clause 5 above is that sentence applied to a screen: **the limit of what Merit can currently say about an as-of stamp goes on the stamp**, not into a document a trader will never read.
