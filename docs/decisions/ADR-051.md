## ADR-051: `R-32` anchors at `opened_on`, and `phase_eval.max_days` is the column that binds  (2026-08-17, status: accepted)

**[M01 section 2.1](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)'s `DayInput` is amended by this ruling. The refusal in [`progression.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/progression.ts) has stood on these two questions since session 47 and this ADR closes both.**

- **Context:** **`R-32` is the last rule blocked on anything other than the calendar.** [M01 line 473](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) states it as "elapsed trading days `>` `phase_eval.max_days` expires the account", [STATE_MACHINES](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) draws it as `active --> expired: G-EXPIRED`, and **neither names the day the trading days elapse from, nor says whether the count or the stored date is authoritative.** The engine therefore **refuses** any eval day on a plan that sets `max_days` rather than folding it and expiring nothing, which would be an account trading past its own expiry with a green state row.

  `max_days` is `null` on all three v1 plans, so **nothing in the lineup reaches this line.** That is what makes now the right moment to rule it: the choice costs nothing today and constrains what a fourth plan may do.

---

### The ruling, part 1: `phase_eval.max_days` is authoritative and `accounts.expires_on` is derived

**Four facts on the tree decide this, and none of them is a preference.**

**`accounts.expires_on` has no writer.** `grep` over every `.ts`, `.sql` and `.mjs` in the repository finds the column declared in [`0007`](../../packages/db/migrations/0007_accounts.sql) and discussed in four comments, and **written nowhere.** A column nothing populates is `NULL` on every row, so a rule reading it expires nobody. Making it authoritative would ship the house defect by construction: a control that is present, valid, and enforcing nothing.

**`max_days` is immutable once published and `expires_on` is not.** `max_days` lives in `plan_versions.rules`, which [`0027`](../../packages/db/migrations/0027_triggers_invariants.sql) and [`0028`](../../packages/db/migrations/0028_supersede_plan_version_immutability.sql) make immutable on a published row. `expires_on` is an ordinary mutable column on an ordinary mutable row. **An expiry that an `UPDATE` can move per account is not a rule, it is an override**, and an override on a money path with no ADR behind it is the thing this corpus exists to prevent.

**Replay refuses it.** `INV-04` is "replaying every mark from day one reproduces stored state byte-identically". A limit derived from the account's pinned plan config replays. **A stored date somebody edited last March does not**: replay would reproduce an expiry different from the one that actually fired. This is the identical reasoning [`state-hash.ts`](../../apps/worker/src/batch/state-hash.ts) already applies to `INV-23`, that a fact which "may not be true now" is not replayable and must not enter the replayed state.

**The engine already has `max_days` and does not have `expires_on`.** It arrives through `ResolvedPlan`. Binding to `expires_on` would add a per-account date to `DayInput` for strictly less determinism than the field already there.

> **`phase_eval.max_days` binds. `accounts.expires_on` is a DERIVED materialisation for display and query, never an input to `R-32`.**

**`expires_on` is not dropped**, and that is deliberate rather than lazy: dropping it needs a superseding migration, constitution E2 makes migrations sacred, and the column is genuinely useful to an admin console that wants to sort by expiry without resolving plan config per row. **Its design record must state that it is derived**, so the next reader does not find an unwritten column and conclude the rule is unimplemented.

---

### The ruling, part 2: the count anchors at `opened_on`, and `opened_on` means the first tradeable day

**The objection to `opened_on` is real.** [STATE_MACHINES](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) enters `provisioning_pending` on `purchase.paid` and leaves it on `G-PROVISIONED`, so an account exists before it is tradeable. **An eval clock anchored at a purchase date burns days the trader could not have traded on**, and on a slow provisioning day that is the firm taking eval time for its own latency.

**Anchoring at activation is not available.** The `accounts` table carries `opened_on`, `funded_on`, `closed_on`, `expires_on`, `graduated_at`, `created_at` and `updated_at`, and **no column records the transition out of `provisioning_pending`.** Adding one is a migration, an `SD-nn` and a manifest row: precisely the weight the refusal's own analysis established `R-32` does **not** need.

**But the objection does not require a column, because `opened_on` is not yet defined.** No approved document says whether `opened_on` is the purchase day or the tradeable day. [DATA_MODEL](../architecture/data-model/accounts.md) declares only its unit ("**Unit: trading day** ... the calendar is authoritative"), [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) exposes it as a string, and the fixture format carries it as data. **Nothing is being overruled by fixing its meaning now**, and nothing writes it yet either, because the provisioning path is M02 and M02 is not built.

> **`R-32` counts elapsed trading days from `accounts.opened_on`, and `opened_on` is THE FIRST TRADING DAY THE ACCOUNT IS TRADEABLE: it is set when the account reaches `active`, not when the purchase is paid.**

This removes the burned-days problem with **no column, no migration and no `SD-nn`**, and it puts the cost of slow provisioning where it belongs. An account that takes three days to provision does not silently spend three days of its evaluation; it also does not hide the three days, because `created_at` still records the purchase and the gap between the two **is** the provisioning latency, measurable by anyone who wants to page on it.

**The binding consequence for M02:** the provisioning path sets `opened_on` at `G-PROVISIONED`, from the trading calendar, and never from `purchase.paid`. A `provisioning_pending` account has `opened_on` unset until it activates, which the `NOT NULL` constraint currently forbids — so **M02 either inserts the row at activation or the column relaxes to `NULL` with a `CHECK` tying it to status.** That is M02's to design and this ADR does not decide it; what it decides is which day the value means.

---

### What this unblocks, and what it deliberately does not

**`DayInput` gains one field: `openedOn: TradingDay`.** That is an amendment to [M01 section 2.1](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) and the reason this is an ADR rather than a commit. **No `rule_states` column, no `SD-nn`, no `DELTA_MANIFEST` row, and no migration number is reserved.** `0037` stays free. A number reserved against a migration that should not be written is worse than no number, because a merged migration can only be superseded (E2).

**The fencepost is NOT ruled here and must be pinned by a fixture rather than by prose.** `R-32` says elapsed trading days `>` `max_days`; whether the opening day is elapsed day 0 or day 1 is an off-by-one on a money path, and this corpus has ruled repeatedly that the way to settle one is an executable pin. **The session implementing `R-32` writes a golden fixture at the boundary — `max_days` exactly reached, and exactly exceeded — and the fixture is the answer.** A sentence here would be a sixth artifact for a fact that already has five.

**`R-32` stays undeclared until that session runs.** This ADR removes what blocked it; it does not implement it, and `IMPLEMENTED_RULES` stays at 45 until the code and the fixture land together.

### Consequences

| | |
|---|---|
| **M01 section 2.1** | `DayInput` gains `openedOn: TradingDay`. Amended by this ADR |
| **`accounts.expires_on`** | Its [design record](../architecture/data-model/accounts.md) states that it is **derived** and is not an input to `R-32` |
| **`accounts.opened_on`** | Its design record states that it is the first **tradeable** trading day, set at `G-PROVISIONED` |
| **M02** | Sets `opened_on` at activation from the calendar, never from `purchase.paid`. How the `NOT NULL` constraint is satisfied before activation is M02's design call |
| **`0037`** | Remains free. No migration is implied by this ruling |
| **The fencepost** | Unruled by design, pinned by a golden fixture at the boundary when `R-32` is implemented |
| **v1 impact** | **None.** `max_days` is `null` on Core EOD, Merit Rapid and Direct, which is why this could be ruled cheaply rather than under pressure |
