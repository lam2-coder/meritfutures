### 8. ADR-370 section 9's hole, measured, and it is not the shape that entry gives it

**THAT ENTRY NAMED ITS OWN CENSUS'S HOLE AND STATED TWO THINGS ABOUT IT.** That a refusing default named anything else is **invisible to both** censuses, and that **the direction of the error is the safe one**, because an uncounted default reads as a port with no default rather than as a port that is served. **THREE SEEDS SAY THE FIRST IS TOO WIDE AND THE SECOND IS BACKWARDS FOR THE CASE THAT OPENS.**

**`constructorsOf` EXCLUDES ONLY NAMES BEGINNING `UNWIRED_`, SO IT CATCHES THE RENAMED DEFAULT AS AN INHABITANT.** Seed 10a added `REFUSING_EXPIRY_EVENT_SINK: ExpiryEventPort` and `6.1` went RED reporting `ExpiryEventPort` as having a constructor at `./sweeps/ports.ts`. **A REFUSING VALUE UNDER ANOTHER NAME IS COUNTED AS A CONSTRUCTOR**, so a port served only by a refusal reads as a port a job could be handed a live value for. That is the UNSAFE direction and it is the opposite of what section 9 predicts.

**THE HOLE THAT DOES OPEN IS NARROWER AND SEED 10c IS IT.** The same value declared for a port that ALREADY has a constructor is invisible to both censuses at once: absent from the prefix scan, and lost in a list its port was already in. **The suite stayed entirely GREEN.**

**NO CENSUS GRAMMAR WAS WIDENED AND NO CHECKER WAS OPENED FOR WRITING.** There is no syntactic difference between a refusing value and a live one, so any repair here is a naming convention replacing a naming convention, which is the defect one letter along and is section 9's own point. **This is recorded, not fixed**, on [ADR-369](ADR-369.md)'s precedent for `RI-15`'s second hole and [ADR-371](ADR-371.md)'s for `RI-35`.

### 10. Where this entry is most likely wrong

**THE INSERTION INTO `schedule.ts` MOVES THREE CITATIONS THIS ROW CANNOT REACH, AND THAT IS A COST AND NOT A ZERO.** All three were stale before it and none of them reddens anything, which is why the repair was made rather than declined; but *already broken* is a reason and not a licence, and a reader who takes the three as evidence that citations into this file do not matter would be drawing the wrong lesson from them. **The alternative was leaving a false clause standing in a `src/` register on the ground that correcting it costs line numbers**, and section 5's argument is that the register is exactly where the clause does harm.

**AND `7.2` ASSERTS THE ROWS RATHER THAN THE HEADER, SO THE HEADER'S CORRECTION IS HELD BY NOTHING.** It is prose beside prose, exactly like the five census paragraphs above it. **The defence is that the FACT it states is derived in `6.1` and in `7.2` and cannot drift**, so a future degradation of the header can be wrong but cannot be believed for long. That is weaker than an assertion and it is stated as weaker.

**SECTION 8'S MEASUREMENT IS THREE SEEDS AND NOT A SWEEP.** It establishes that the hole is narrower than section 9 says and that its direction is the unsafe one; it does not establish that no third shape exists. **A row that enumerated every value of a port type under `apps/worker/src` and partitioned them by whether they refuse would be measuring the real population**, and this row measured three points on it.
