## ADR-163: the live tier's address is `apps/api` on both existing surfaces, so the estate gains no sixth deployable and no seventh service, and the container diagram gains three arrows and no live-cache node  (2026-08-27, status: proposed)

**[`163`](ALLOCATION.md) was reserved REQUIRED rather than conditionally, and the reservation says why: [P6](../plans/P6-live-tier.md) RECOMMENDS `apps/api` on both surfaces rather than a sixth deployable and PERMITS this slice to refuse it with an argument.** A recommendation that may be refused is a ruling whichever way it goes, so taking it silently is the one disposition the number forecloses. **This entry takes it, and section 2 is the argument rather than the assent.**

**What made the argument worth running is that the plan's own wording presumes the branch it does not recommend.** [P6 section 8](../plans/P6-live-tier.md)'s `P6-b` row says *"[INFRA](../architecture/INFRA.md) section 2.1 gains its service"* and *"section 3's table gains its row or rows"*. **Taking the recommendation means neither happens**: no container is new, so [OVERVIEW section 3](../architecture/OVERVIEW.md)'s table gains no row, and no service is new, so [INFRA section 2.1](../architecture/INFRA.md)'s table gains no row and its heading still reads six. **The documents gain amended cells and three arrows.** That is a smaller diff than the row this slice was dispatched to write, and it is the whole finding rather than a shortcut: the live tier's address already exists and the work was establishing that nothing else may hold it.

- **Origin:** [P6](../plans/P6-live-tier.md) `P6-b`, branch `claude/p6b-container-address`, session **277**. The number was **RESERVED BEFORE DISPATCH and REQUIRED** ([ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md)), so none was chosen here; the `163` row is amended **in place** per [ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3.

- **NO CODE, and money by content rather than by file.** No migration, no `SD-nn`, no `SystemReason`, no `SqlExecutorReason`, no dependency, no gate and no test. Three documents move. **It is worth a founder read anyway**, because [P6 section 10 item 2](../plans/P6-live-tier.md) names the failing direction as *"the founder's numbers get served on the public origin by a route [ADR-083](ADR-083.md)'s 404 never saw"*, and an address is what decides which process can answer at all.

### 2. THE RULING

> **1. THE LIVE TIER'S ADDRESS IS `apps/api`, AND THE ESTATE GAINS NO SIXTH DEPLOYABLE AND NO SEVENTH SERVICE.** Findings 1, 2 and 3. **The 404 that [P6 section 10 item 2](../plans/P6-live-tier.md) names as the thing the live tier must not evade is produced by a process registering nothing for a path**, and the process that decides what it registers is the one that reads `MERIT_API_SURFACE`. A live channel served by any other process is served by a process that never resolves that value, so its withholding is either re-derived somewhere or absent, and [ADR-161](ADR-161.md) `F5` already prices the re-derivation: an endpoint list rather than a prefix, *"a second transcription of the contract, going stale the first time a route is added"*.
>
> **2. THE ADDRESS IS AN ORIGIN AND NOT ONLY A PROCESS, AND THAT IS WHERE THE SIXTH DEPLOYABLE COSTS THE MOST.** Findings 9 and 10. The operator's live figure is served by `api-admin`, on `ADMIN_ORIGIN`, under the `/api/v1` path rule that already routes there, so **it inherits the C-08 IP allowlist and the origin's Cloudflare rules by running where they already point.** A sixth deployable is a new origin or a new path rule or both; under hard rule 3's own wording the controls follow the ORIGIN, so a live operator channel on an origin nobody added to the allowlist is outside C-08 while every check in this repository stays green. **This corpus has already made the smaller version of that mistake once**, naming one service where two run, and finding 10 is [ADR-089](ADR-089.md) correcting it.
>
> **3. THE LIVE CHANNEL'S CONTRACT PATH SITS UNDER `BASE_PATH` AND IS CLASSIFIED BY `classifyPath` AT STARTUP, IN THE PROCESS THAT RESOLVED THE SURFACE.** Findings 7 and 8. **This is the constraint this entry imposes on `P6-g` and it is the only one**: whatever shape the registry learns, the decision *does this deployment serve this channel* is `surfaceServes` evaluated on the contract path, written as [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) writes it, without the base path, before the server accepts a connection. **A socket registered beside `compose` rather than through the same classification is served identically by both deployments**, which is [P6 section 9](../plans/P6-live-tier.md)'s named risk for those two files, *"the risk is not a merge conflict, it is a later slice reaching around it"*, and finding 7 is why the path may not carry `/api/v1` in the string classification reads.
>
> **4. `apps/worker` KEEPS `none, no ingress`, AND THAT CELL IS A FORECLOSURE FROM HERE RATHER THAN A DESCRIPTION.** Findings 12 and 13. The worker is the one existing service that already runs a long-lived process and already consumes the feed, which is exactly what makes it the tempting home. **Giving it ingress to serve a socket puts a request path on the deployable whose row exists to say there is not one**, and it would put the trader's channel and the operator's channel in one process with no `MERIT_API_SURFACE` to separate them. **The worker keeps the INGEST and never the CHANNEL**, and the two halves are two deployables for the reason `INV-M2-14` is a permission rather than a convention.
>
> **5. THE CONTAINER DIAGRAM GAINS THE STREAMING ARROW AND THE TWO PUSH ARROWS, AND IT GAINS NO LIVE-CACHE NODE.** Finding 14. Tier 2 is drawn as three dotted edges, `Rithmic` to `Worker` for the indicative ticks, and `API` to `Portal` and `API` to `Admin` for the frames, **and the dotted edges are the diagram's only new vocabulary: server-initiated and indicative, against solid edges that are requested and authoritative.** The leg from the ingest to the channel is a STORE whose medium is [P6 section 10 item 1](../plans/P6-live-tier.md)'s and is [ADR-164](ALLOCATION.md)'s to rule, and two of its three readings draw nothing at all. **Drawing it would be this entry deciding a ruling it does not hold, and leaving it out is stated under the diagram rather than left to be noticed.**
>
> **6. [OVERVIEW SECTION 3](../architecture/OVERVIEW.md)'s TABLE GAINS NO ROW AND [INFRA SECTION 2.1](../architecture/INFRA.md)'s TABLE GAINS NO ROW.** The live tier has no container of its own: the channel is `apps/api`, the ingest is `apps/worker`, the adapter is `packages/rithmic` and its row landed with [ADR-154](ADR-154.md), and the cache is `P6-c`'s. **Both tables gain amended cells instead, in the rows that already name those containers**, and INFRA section 2.1's heading still reads six services because six is still the count. **This is the recommendation's consequence and not an omission**, and it is written here so that a later reader who finds `P6-b`'s dispatch and no new row does not read the absence as work that was skipped.
>
> **7. WHAT THIS ENTRY LEAVES TO `P6-g`, AND THE ONE THING THE ADDRESS SETTLED IN PASSING.** **The MECHANISM is `P6-g`'s entirely**: whether `HTTP_METHODS` gains a member, whether the registry keys on something other than `METHOD /path`, whether the channel is composed or registered beside `compose`, what the framing is, and the rate-limit row [ADR-161](ADR-161.md) section 5 item 1 hands it. **This entry is silent on `HTTP_METHODS` and says so rather than implying it.** On `API_SURFACES` it is not silent and it is not a prohibition either: **the address creates no requirement for a third member**, because the channel is served by the two deployments that already exist and withheld by the classification that already runs. So a third surface added by `P6-g` is buying something the address does not need, and that slice's entry owes the reason under [P6 section 11](../plans/P6-live-tier.md) rule 8 rather than owing this entry an exemption.
>
> **8. THE ADDRESS CONSTRAINS THE MEDIUM WITHOUT CHOOSING IT.** [P6 section 10 item 1](../plans/P6-live-tier.md)'s second reading is *"a process-local store in the socket server"*, and clause 1 has now named which processes those are: **two services, and [INFRA section 2.1](../architecture/INFRA.md) already reasons about a service running more than one instance**, in the sentence about a shared `MERIT_API_SURFACE` reaching *"both of its instances"*. Nothing in [INFRA](../architecture/INFRA.md) pins an instance count for `api` or `api-admin`. **So a process-local live cache is per-instance rather than per-firm, and two clients of one account can hold different numbers with nothing wrong anywhere**, which is a fact `P6-c` should weigh and is not a ruling this entry takes. The plan already knew half of it: that reading *"makes `GS-132`'s poisoning step untestable from outside"*.

### 6. Founder approval

**One checkable clause, and it is the check `RI-04` says in its own `covers` that it does not perform and that [session 153](../sessions/2026-08-24-session-153.md) refused as a separate one.** It is run here by hand, against this ruling, and **nothing in the tree is changed to run it**: turning it into a standing check is minting `RI-12`, which is a check to write rather than a clause to sign, and this fence holds no file it could live in. A count-shaped clause was available, *"[INFRA](../architecture/INFRA.md) section 2.1 rows six services"*, and it is refused for [ADR-034](ADR-034.md)'s reason: a clause naming a number is a clause that drifts, and this document's own registries have paid for that repeatedly.

> **`apps/` holds exactly the directories `DEPLOYABLES` names, and the two API services this ruling serves the live tier from are exactly the values of `SERVICE_BY_SURFACE`**, with `DEPLOYABLES` imported from [`repo-invariants.mjs`](../../packages/tooling/checks/repo-invariants.mjs) and `SERVICE_BY_SURFACE` and `API_SURFACES` imported from [`surface.ts`](../../apps/api/src/surface.ts), so no list is transcribed into this entry. `RI-09`'s own reason for reading `surface.ts` rather than copying it is that a second copy of the prefix rule drifts SILENTLY, and the same argument covers a second copy of a service list.
>
> **The direction it fails in is the one that matters, and it was watched failing rather than asserted.** `apps/live/package.json` plus `apps/live/src/api/v1/admin/liability.ts`, a sixth application directory holding a path that spells an operator route, **takes `node packages/tooling/checks/repo-invariants.mjs` to 11 of 11 and exit 0**, because `RI-04` and `RI-09` both iterate `DEPLOYABLES` and neither reads the directory. **The clause exits 1 on it immediately**, and it fails the same way for any future sixth directory, which is what a count-shaped clause could not do.

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.** Every fact in section 1 is a file's own words, and clause 1 follows from them. **Clauses 2 and 8 do not.** Clause 2 rules that the address is an ORIGIN, which converts a hosting choice into a security-control question and is the reason a sixth deployable is refused on more than tidiness; clause 8 tells `P6-c` that one of its three available media is per-instance under this address, which is a constraint derived from a sentence [INFRA](../architecture/INFRA.md) wrote about a different failure. **Both are judgements about how far an address ruling reaches into rulings it does not hold**, and section 5 items 1 and 3 are the two places this entry deliberately stopped short.
