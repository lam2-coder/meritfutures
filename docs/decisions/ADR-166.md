## ADR-166: the evidence export's audience is a required parameter with no default, and section 8's three-path heading becomes three rows because one of the three is built and two are not  (2026-08-27, status: proposed)

**[`evidence_packs`](../../packages/db/migrations/0008_risk.sql) makes `audience`, `redaction_profile` and `includes_detector_detail` all `NOT NULL`, and the endpoint that writes those rows accepts no audience at all.** So the generator would supply one the caller never named, which [M06 section 4](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) already forbids in words. This entry adds the parameter, refuses a default, and rules the response echo. **Its second half is a reconciliation rather than an addition**: section 8 heads three endpoints with one line, one of the three is registered and two are registered by nothing, and the two failures look identical from outside the tree.

- **Origin:** [P7](../plans/P7-risk-and-abuse.md) `P7-b`, branch `claude/p7b-contract-audience`, session **280**. The number was **RESERVED BEFORE DISPATCH and REQUIRED** ([ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md)), so none was chosen here; the `166` row is amended **in place** per [ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3.
- **[API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) is `approved`**, so [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) makes this an entry rather than a commit regardless of size. [ADR-153](ADR-153.md) took that route this morning for one wrong verb and [ADR-161](ADR-161.md) took it yesterday for a payload.
- **NOT MONEY BY FILE.** No migration, no `SD-nn`, no ledger, no engine, no payout, no auth, no scope rule. **It is worth a founder read anyway, and clause 1 is the reason**: `AS-M6-01` is that the audience decides what leaves the building, and the parameter this entry adds is the only place a human states it.

### 2. THE RULING

> **1. `?audience=` IS REQUIRED BESIDE `?reason=`, AND THERE IS NO DEFAULT.** Findings 1 and 5. An absent, blank or unrecognised audience is `validation_failed` and the generator is not reached. **The narrowest available default, `trader`, is the one that would look safest and it is refused too**: a pack silently narrowed is one somebody hands to counsel believing it is complete, and finding 3 shows the schema already treats `trader` as the single combination worth making unrepresentable rather than as a safe floor. **The audience is a declared, audited property of the export** ([`0008_risk.sql:250-252`](../../packages/db/migrations/0008_risk.sql)) *"rather than a judgment made in the moment by whoever is answering the ticket"*, and a default is that judgment made by a process instead of by a person.
>
> **2. THE RESPONSE ECHOES THE AUDIENCE, AND A PACK BUILT FOR ANOTHER ONE IS REFUSED RATHER THAN RELABELLED.** `EvidencePackResponse` gains `audience` and nothing else. **The echo exists so a caller can tell from the response what the bytes behind `download_url` were built as** rather than from what it remembers sending, and the comparison against the requested audience is the assertion that carries it: both values are valid members, so a mismatch produces a well-formed response carrying a real audience and no type check anywhere would see it. **Overwriting the pack's audience with the requested one is refused explicitly**, because that is the same disclosure with a truthful label removed.
>
> **3. SECTION 8'S THREE-PATH HEADING BECOMES THREE HEADINGS, AND THE TWO UNBUILT BODIES ARE DELIBERATELY NOT SPECIFIED.** Findings 10 to 12. One heading over three paths reads as one endpoint to a human, while the document's own test already reads it as three, and the divergence is what let two unregistered paths sit inside a row that looked served. **The bodies are left to `P7-k`** for findings 15 and 16: a projection cannot be specified over an evaluator that does not exist or a table nobody has chosen, and a shape written here is one that slice would have to break. **What IS fixed here is what does not depend on either**: all three carry the `/admin` prefix, so [ADR-161](ADR-161.md) clause 2 classifies them `operator` and no public deployment serves them.
>
> **4. THE FOUR AUDIENCE NAMES ARE A TRANSCRIPTION OF A MERGED `CHECK`, NOT A VOCABULARY THIS ENTRY CHOOSES.** Finding 2. The contract and the route carry the same four names for one reason: [`0008_risk.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0008_risk.sql) is merged and sacred, so a fifth name added anywhere else type-checks, passes every route test, and fails at the database on the first export that uses it. **The migration is the source and the suite asserts against the migration** rather than against a copy of it.
>
> **5. THIS ENTRY DOES NOT RULE `OQ-F4-03`, AND SAYING WHICH HALF IS ALREADY CLOSED IS THE POINT.** Finding 18. **Whether a regulator pack carries detector internals is CLOSED**, by three approved documents that collapse `internal`, `counsel` and `regulator` into one profile. **Whether an IMPERSONATION RECORD appears in a regulator pack is OPEN, and it is COUNSEL's rather than the founder's**, in [ADR-068](ADR-068.md)'s own words. They are two questions about one column and the closure of one is not evidence about the other. **A slice reading `SD-M6-04` and finding a required `audience` parameter must not read the open half as settled by its presence**: this entry makes the audience statable and rules nothing about what an impersonation record does inside any profile.
>
> **6. THIS ENTRY DOES NOT RULE `redaction_profile`'S VOCABULARY EITHER, AND IT IS NOT ECHOED FOR THAT REASON.** Finding 4: the column carries no `CHECK`. `INV-M7-10` makes the strip list **computed** from `detector_definitions.is_sensitive` rather than hand-listed, and [P7 section 8](../plans/P7-risk-and-abuse.md) gives `P7-j` an entry *"only if `redaction_profile`'s vocabulary needs a ruling"*. **Publishing the profile on the wire here would fix a vocabulary that is deliberately still open**, so the response carries the audience and stops.

### 6. Founder approval

**One checkable clause, and it is a command rather than a count**, on [ADR-161](ADR-161.md)'s reasoning that an approval clause naming a number is one that drifts.

> **NO EVIDENCE PACK IS GENERATED WITHOUT AN AUDIENCE A HUMAN NAMED.** `GET /admin/evidence/:accountId` reaches `exportEvidence` only when `audience` parses to a member of `evidence_packs.audience`'s merged `CHECK`, and the four names the route admits are read **from [`0008_risk.sql`](../../packages/db/migrations/0008_risk.sql) itself** rather than from a copy, so the vocabulary cannot drift from the database that stores it.
>
> **The direction it fails in is the one that matters, and it was watched failing.** A route that defaulted a missing audience to `trader` type-checks, answers `200`, writes a valid row, and passes every test that supplies an audience. **Seeded, it turns `an evidence export without an audience is refused and no default is chosen` red**, on the assertion that the generator is reached zero times. The relabel defect was seeded the same way and turns clause 2's test red; a fifth audience name was seeded and turns the migration comparison red.
>
> **What a count-shaped clause could not do here:** the number of audiences is four today and is a merged `CHECK`'s to change, so a clause naming four would have to be edited by the migration that changes it, which is the drift [ADR-034](ADR-034.md)'s rule is about.

**What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.** Every fact in section 1 is a file's own words or a report the application built. **Clause 1's refusal of a default and clause 2's refusal to relabel are not.** Clause 1 says an operator who forgets a parameter gets an error rather than the narrowest pack, which costs a round trip on every export and buys the property that no disclosure decision is ever made by a process; clause 2 says a mismatched pack is a `500` rather than a corrected `200`, which costs an operator a confusing failure and buys the property that a label on an evidence pack is never a claim the API invented. **Both are judgements about how much friction is worth spending to keep a disclosure decision attributable to a person**, and section 4's first row is the alternative that spends less.

**Approval line: UNSIGNED.** Not money path by file: no migration, no `SD-nn`, no ledger, engine, payout, auth or scope rule.

**Cost if wrong:** if clause 1 is too strict, every evidence export costs one extra parameter and the reversal is one line. If it is too loose, a pack built for the wrong audience is a disclosure that cannot be recalled, and [`0008_risk.sql:247`](../../packages/db/migrations/0008_risk.sql) states what it discloses, in capitals: *"A PACK GIVEN TO A TRADER IN A DISPUTE IS A CHANNEL THAT DISCLOSES DETECTOR THRESHOLDS TO THE ADVERSARY WHO TRIGGERED THEM."*
