## ADR-052: the locked floor is an assignment, and the engine is wrong because a test was made stricter than the rule it was testing  (2026-08-17, status: proposed)

**[M01 section 3.4](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)'s binding floor expression and the `CV-12` row of [section 2.4](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) are amended by this ruling. It reverses the reading [`floor.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/floor.ts) implements today, so approving it changes engine behaviour on the money path.**

- **Context:** **`GS-024` pins `floor_cents: 5010000` and the engine produces `5050001`.** [STATE.md](../STATE.md) has carried "M01 disagrees with itself on `R-15`'s lock effect" as an open founder item since [session 45](../sessions/2026-08-16-session-45.md), where the engine was written to take section 3.4's `max` and the disagreement was recorded rather than edited into a frozen document. **This ADR is that item's ruling, and it decides against the engine.**

  Section 3.4 states the floor as `floor(d) = max( hwb(d) - drawdown_cents, floorLocked ? floor_lock_floor_at_cents : size_cents - drawdown_cents )` and calls the `max` "redundant given the update order". Section 3.6's `DO-7` **assigns** `floor = rules.floorLockFloorAtCents!`. **The redundancy claim is what makes the two agree, and the redundancy claim is false.**

---

### 1. The divergence day, computed by hand under both readings

`MERIT-RAPID-50K` ([Appendix A.2](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) at 50K): `size_cents 5,000,000`, eval `drawdown_cents 250,000`, `floor_lock_at_profit_cents 260,000`, `floor_lock_floor_at_cents 5,010,000`. `CV-12` holds exactly: `260,000 == 250,000 + 10,000`.

| Trading day | Close | `hwb` after `DO-7` | 3.4, the `max` | 3.6, the assignment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-02 | 5,150,001 | 5,150,001 | 4,900,001 | 4,900,001 |
| **2026-11-03** | **5,300,001** | **5,300,001** | **5,050,001** | **5,010,000** |
| 2026-11-04 | 5,450,001 | 5,300,001, frozen | 5,050,001 | 5,010,000 |
| 2026-11-05 | 5,500,000 | 5,300,001, frozen | 5,050,001 | 5,010,000 |

**The divergence day is 2026-11-03**, the day the closing balance clears the lock trigger by 40,001c instead of landing on it. `R-15`'s trigger is `>=`, so a day that jumps past the trigger engages the lock exactly as a day that lands on it does, and on that day `hwb - drawdown_cents` is already above `floor_lock_floor_at_cents`. **The `max` therefore selects the trailed floor and the lock does nothing at all**, on this day or on any day after it, because `hwb` is frozen where the overshoot left it.

**`CV-12` makes the two readings agree only where the balance lands on the trigger to the cent**, which is `GS-015`'s and `GS-016`'s lock day and is why neither of them detects this. It is not where a real account locks: on all three v1 plans the lock fires at `drawdown_cents + 10,000c` of profit and the eval target is above it, so **every eval pass in the lineup overshoots.**

**The 40,001 is not a rounding remainder, and there is no unwritten rounding decision behind it.** It is exactly the overshoot above the trigger, `5,300,001 - (5,000,000 + 260,000)`. The odd cent is `GS-023`'s moved cent, which `GS-024`'s own header states in its own words ("THIS IS GS-023 WITH ONE CENT MOVED FROM THE LAST DAY TO THE FIRST"): day one's `realized_pnl_cents` is `150,001` where `GS-023`'s is `150,000`, and it is still in the balance on day two. **No division exists anywhere in the floor path**, so no remainder could arise there. Under `GS-023`'s stream the same divergence is 40,000c exactly.

### 2. The ruling: `floor = floor_lock_floor_at_cents`, and `GS-024`'s `5,010,000` stands

**`CV-11`'s derivation of `INV-21` decides this, and it is the one argument that is not repairable.**

`CV-11` reads `buffer_cents > (floor_lock_floor_at_cents - size_cents)`, and its stated reasoning is that "the floor never exceeds `floor_lock_floor_at_cents` while the lock is enabled (pre-lock it is strictly below by `CV-12`, **post-lock it equals it**), and a post-payout balance is always at least `size + buffer`". **Under the `max` the post-lock floor is the lock-day close minus `drawdown_cents`, which exceeds `floor_lock_floor_at_cents` by the overshoot. The premise is false and `INV-21` no longer follows from it.**

**The counterexample is complete and needs no losing day.** On `MERIT-RAPID-50K`, funded, at a closing balance of `5,350,001`:

| Step | Arithmetic | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Lock engages, `max` reading | `hwb 5,350,001`, floor `5,350,001 - 250,000` | floor **5,100,001** |
| Withdrawable (`R-35`) | `5,350,001 - 5,000,000 - 100,000` | 250,001 |
| Payout 1 (`R-43`, cap 100,000) | `min(250,001, 100,000)` | balance 5,250,001 |
| Payout 2 | `min(150,001, 100,000)` | balance 5,150,001 |
| Payout 3 | `min(50,001, 100,000)`, and `50,001 >= min_payout 10,000` | balance **5,100,000** |

**The balance is one cent below the account's own floor, on three of a five-rung ladder, with no losing day anywhere in the stream.** The next mark's low is at or below the balance, so `R-21`'s strict `<` breaches. That is **`INV-21` violated** ("a settled payout can never breach the account that earned it"), and it is `AS-04`'s "Counter, as built. **Config validation, not runtime logic**" collapsing with it.

**Generally, the `max` reading breaches whenever a funded lock-day close exceeds `size_cents + buffer_cents + drawdown_cents`**, since `size + buffer < size + (P - drawdown_cents)` reduces to `P > buffer_cents + drawdown_cents`. **All three v1 plans reach it at 350,000c of profit at 50K, and they reach the same number through different parameters**, which is worth stating so it is not read as one plan's arithmetic generalised: Core EOD and Merit Rapid are `100,000 + 250,000`, and Direct is `150,000 + 200,000` ([Appendix A.1, A.2 and A.3](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) at 50K). **Every one of them locks below that threshold** and Core EOD's eval target alone is 300,000c, so no plan in the lineup keeps an account on the safe side of it by construction.

**Why no new validation can rescue the `max`.** `CV-11` and `CV-17` are publish-time checks, and [ADR-014](ADR-014.md) deliberately made `INV-21` derive from publish-time validation rather than from runtime logic: "`INV-21` now derives from `CV-11` and `CV-17` rather than from the reset." **Under the `max` the post-lock floor depends on the lock-day closing balance, which is runtime data.** A publish-time check cannot bound a quantity the trader chooses after the plan is published. The `max` does not make `INV-21` harder to enforce; it makes it **unenforceable in the layer ADR-014 assigned it to.**

**And nothing in the tree would have caught it.** `GS-065`, `GS-081` and `GS-083`, the three fixtures [M01](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md) names as `INV-21`'s, are all unwritten.

**The supporting grounds, in the order they bind:**

- **`R-15`'s Effect column is an assignment**: "Effect: `floor = floor_lock_floor_at_cents`, `floorLocked = true`, `hwb` frozen, all permanently." Section 3.5 makes the operator column the contract: "it is what the engine executes, what `copy_blocks` publishes, and what the fixture asserts. All three or none."
- **The [GLOSSARY](../GLOSSARY.md#floor-lock) states the assignment outright**, and it is the clearest sentence on this question in the corpus: the lock "stops a trailing floor from trailing ... **fixing the floor at [account size](../GLOSSARY.md#account-size) plus a configured amount**", and the frozen high-water balance "is what makes the floor **immutable rather than merely capped**". Under the `max` the floor is neither fixed at `size + X` nor immutable at it; it is whatever the trader's best close left behind. [ADR-014](ADR-014.md)'s own title calls the lock "a permanent **stop**", and a stop that can be outvoted by the thing it stops is not one.
- **Precedent, and it is exact.** `R-22` had this shape: section 3.6's pseudocode wrote `>=` where the operator column, `OQ-6`'s ruling and section 10.1 all wrote `>`. **The table won and the pseudocode was recorded as the defect.** This ruling is that one applied to `R-15`.

### 3. The `INV-06` objection is a sub-step artifact, and the engine is wrong because a test was made stricter than the rule it was testing

**This is the part of the ruling worth reading, because the engine's reasoning is otherwise sound and the flaw is one level below it.**

[`floor.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/floor.ts) argues that the assignment "drops the floor by 40,000c on the account's best day, which is `INV-06` ... and `RE-P-01` violated on a reachable input". **It is not.** `INV-06` is a property of the **stored day-over-day floor series**: `rule_states.floor_cents`, one row per `(account_id, trading_day)` under [`0015`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql)'s unique index, and its three enforcement citations (`RE-P-01`, `GS-010`, `GS-081`) are all day-level. Under the assignment that series on `GS-024` is `4,900,001 -> 5,010,000 -> 5,010,000 -> 5,010,000`: **monotone non-decreasing, no violation, at the only granularity any stated invariant samples.**

**The retreat exists solely in a local variable inside `DO-7`**, between the trail and the lock, and the corpus defines no floor there. **Section 3.6's own tripwire agrees**: `if (floor < s.floorCents) throw new EngineInvariantError('INV-06')` compares the day's final floor against `s.floorCents`, the **prior day's** floor. That is the check M01 specifies.

**[Session 45](../sessions/2026-08-16-session-45.md) tightened that tripwire to fire after each sub-step**, and STATE.md records the tightening as "the more useful half of the finding". **It is the cause of the defect rather than a discovery about it.** A monotonicity check that samples sub-steps asserts a stronger property than `INV-06` states, that stronger property is violated by the assignment `R-15` specifies, and the engine resolved the contradiction by changing the rule to satisfy the test. **The engine is wrong because a test was made stricter than the rule it was testing.**

**The floor still never falls day-over-day under the assignment, and this is provable rather than asserted.** Pre-lock, `hwb` is the maximum closing balance and the lock triggers on the closing balance, so `hwb < size_cents + floor_lock_at_profit_cents` on every day before the lock. By `CV-12` that is exactly `hwb - drawdown_cents < floor_lock_floor_at_cents`. **The prior day's floor is therefore strictly below the locked value**, and the lock day raises the floor. `INV-06` holds with no new exception, and [ADR-050](ADR-050.md)'s `R-31` exception remains the only one.

### 4. Why the corpus cannot be re-read into agreement, and a merged migration is what settles it

Two readings would make `CV-12`'s redundancy claim literally true, and **[`0015_rule_states.sql:208`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql) refutes both**:

```sql
CONSTRAINT rule_states_high_water_bounds_balance CHECK (
  high_water_balance_cents >= balance_cents
),
```

Freezing `hwb` at the trigger (`5,260,000`) or before the lock day's update (`5,150,001`) would make `max(hwb - drawdown_cents, floor_lock_floor_at_cents)` resolve to `5,010,000` and every sentence in the corpus true at once. **Both store a `hwb` below that day's `balance_cents 5,300,001`, and the constraint rejects the row.** `hwb` must reach the lock-day close.

**So `hwb` freezes at `5,300,001`, `hwb - drawdown_cents` is `5,050,001`, and the redundancy claim is false as a matter of arithmetic rather than in this scenario only.** [ADR-014](ADR-014.md)'s own sentence, that `hwb` stopping "is what makes the `max` above resolve to the locked value forever after", fails for the same reason and is amended by the same ruling. One of the two formulations must move; neither survives a re-reading.

### 5. What this amends

| Where | From | To |
|---|---|---|
| [M01 section 3.4](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md), the binding expression | `floor(d) = max( hwb(d) - drawdown_cents, floorLocked ? floor_lock_floor_at_cents : size_cents - drawdown_cents )` | `floor(d) = floorLocked ? floor_lock_floor_at_cents : max( hwb(d) - drawdown_cents, size_cents - drawdown_cents )` |
| M01 section 3.4, the paragraph after it | "The `max` is redundant given the update order" | The `max` is **not** redundant, it is **wrong on any day that overshoots the trigger**, and the lock is a branch rather than a term |
| [M01 section 2.4](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md), `CV-12`'s "Rejected because" | "Forces the lock to engage exactly where the trailing floor already sits, so the floor never jumps" | Forces the locked value to sit **at or above** the trailing floor of the day before the lock, so the lock day never lowers the floor. It does **not** make the two equal on the lock day itself |
| [ADR-014](ADR-014.md)'s decision block | The same `max`, and "`hwb` stops updating ... which is what makes the `max` above resolve to the locked value forever after" | The same amendment. `hwb` stops updating, and the **branch** is what makes the floor resolve to the locked value forever after |

**`R-15`, `R-14`, `INV-06`, `INV-07`, `CV-11` and `CV-12`'s operator are unchanged.** This ruling makes them consistent rather than moving any of them, which is the argument for it: **every other reading requires amending an invariant, and this one requires amending an expression that was already wrong.**

- **Alternatives considered.** **The `max`, as `floor.ts` implements it today**: rejected, because it makes `INV-21` unenforceable at publish time and no runtime substitute exists that ADR-014 has not already ruled out. **Freezing `hwb` at the trigger, or before the lock day's update**: rejected by `0015`'s check constraint, above, and both would also make `high_water_balance_cents` stop being the account's high-water balance. **Adding an `INV-06` sub-step exception**: rejected, because there is nothing to except; `INV-06` is not violated at the granularity it is stated at, and writing an exception would ratify session 45's tightening as the rule.

- **Consequences:**
  - **`GS-024` is confirmed rather than edited, and no fixture changes.** It is the **only** fixture in the corpus whose pinned `floor_cents` differs between the two readings: of the seventeen that pin a floor, `GS-055` and `GS-061` stop one rung below the trigger and never lock, `GS-015` and `GS-016` land on it to the cent, `GS-020` and `GS-023` overshoot but end after `R-31` has rewritten the floor, and `GS-069` overshoots and pins none. **STATE.md's catalogue of files this defect touches does not name `GS-024`**, and this ADR corrects that.
  - **[`floor.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/floor.ts) changes on approval, and its 25-line comment is the argument being overruled.** The `max` becomes the branch, and the sub-step `neverRetreats('R-15', trailedFloorCents, floorCents)` call is deleted rather than kept, because it asserts a property no document states and it is what forced the `max`. The `R-13` sub-step check may stay. `CI-02/engine-R-15`'s seeded mutant **inverts**: the assignment stops being the mutant and the `max` becomes it.
  - **`GS-024` goes green, and a red stage moves. ~~Six become five~~ TWO BECOME ONE** (corrected 2026-08-17). This clause was written against a `CI-03` baseline of **six** failures and [PR #72](https://github.com/lam2-coder/meritfutures/pull/72) merged between the writing and the reading: the calendar was extended to seven sessions and four funded fixtures re-anchored, so `GS-020`, `GS-054`, `GS-055` and `GS-061` now pass and **`CI-03` stands at two failures, `GS-024` and `GS-064`.** **Whoever applies this ruling should expect two to become one**, and should treat the count not moving as evidence the fix is incomplete. **`GS-024`'s diff is byte-for-byte unchanged across that merge** (`floor_cents: expected 5010000, engine produced 5050001n`) and is still its only one, so nothing above this line moved. `GS-064`'s remaining diff is `withdrawable_cents` and is a **different, unruled three-way disagreement** about which balance `R-35` measures on the row that closes an account; it is not this ADR's.
  - **`INV-21`'s counterexample above is `GS-065`'s content.** The arithmetic in section 2 is a complete fixture stream. The next session transcribes it rather than re-deriving it, per `TR-01`.
  - **A merged money-path migration refuses to store the ordinary success case, and it is a separate ruling.** `rule_states_high_water_bounds_balance` asserts `hwb >= balance_cents` unconditionally, while `R-15` freezes `hwb` "permanently" at the lock. **Every locked account that then makes a new closing high therefore has a state row the database rejects.** That is not an edge case and not a consequence of this ruling: the lock fires at `size + 260,000c` on all three v1 plans, so it is **every successful funded account**, under the `max` and the assignment alike. `GS-016`'s entire subject is that scenario, and its end state cannot be stored; `GS-024`'s days three and four cannot be stored either (`hwb 5,300,001` against balances `5,450,001` and `5,500,000`). The constraint encodes the pre-lock invariant and was never checked against the lock. **The remedy is a superseding migration, never an edit** (constitution E2), so it is its own ADR, its own migration and its own money-path session under [ADR-003](ADR-003.md). **No number is claimed for it here and `0037` stays free**, per [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md)'s rule that a number reserved against work that should not yet be written is worse than no number.
  - **Trader-facing copy is unaffected**, which is worth stating because it is the case that would have made this expensive. The published fact is "after a payout, your loss room is your buffer" ([ADR-014](ADR-014.md), `AS-04`), and the assignment is the reading that fact was written against: post-lock the floor is `size + $100` on every plan, exactly as the rules page says. **The `max` is the reading that would have required a copy change**, because under it the post-lock floor depends on the trader's best close and no sentence in the corpus describes it.
  - Net liability direction: the assignment leaves the trader **more** loss room after the lock than the `max` does, by the overshoot. Unlike [ADR-013](ADR-013.md) and [ADR-014](ADR-014.md), **the error here does not run conservative**, and it is chosen anyway because `INV-21` is a promise and the room is a parameter.
