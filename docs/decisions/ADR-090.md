## ADR-090: "one rail" binds on the destination and the detector, and the affiliate settlement leg is a sibling table rather than a widened `payout_transfers`  (2026-08-24, status: accepted)

**[Session 162](../sessions/2026-08-24-session-162.md) measured [M08](../plans/M08-affiliate-system.md) and found that an affiliate settlement is not writable as a row in either `payout_requests` or `payout_transfers`. This entry rules the shape and writes no DDL.** The migration, if the founder wants one, is a later session with its own number and its own `E2` read, on [ADR-087](ADR-087.md)'s own sentence: *"a money-path migration that installs an unruled constraint because it was cheap is how a schema acquires rules nobody decided."* **MONEY PATH: it decides the shape of an outbound payment path, and the direction it can fail in is a mule detector that returns clean.**

- **Origin:** [session 162](../sessions/2026-08-24-session-162.md)'s first landmine and its slice `M08-2`, which says in its own words that this *"widens the meaning of a merged money-path table, which is the restraint `OQ-M8-05` names"* and is *"not a thing a non-money session grants itself."* Every clause of that finding was re-derived at its source in this session rather than inherited, and section 1 records where each was read.

- **Context:** [ADR-017](ADR-017.md) is `accepted` and signed with a founder addition. [M08](../plans/M08-affiliate-system.md) and [M07](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md) are frozen and `approved`. What moves is one premise row in M08 section 3.4, one closing sentence of `AS-M8-05`, and `D-09`'s Source cell in [M07:116](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md). **No migration, no `packages/db` change, no engine change, and `OQ-075-01` is not touched.**

**This entry's half is the MECHANISM, and section 3.4 asserted it rather than checking it.** Rows one and three were performed against the tree. Row two was accepted from four documents. **Checked here at the migration, row two is FALSE**: settlement does not ride M5's transfer machinery, because there is no row an affiliate statement can occupy in either table that machinery writes.

> **That is section 3.4's own next paragraph arriving inside section 3.4's own evidence column.** It reads: *"A control that exists in the paragraph explaining why the attack fails, and nowhere that binds, is the attack succeeding on schedule."* It wrote that about `INV-M8-12`, having just accepted `INV-M8-10` and `INV-M8-11` on exactly that footing one row above. **The gap is not that section 3.4 was careless; it is that it checked the row it was dispatched against and cited the row it was not.**

### 3. The premise is false, and a merged table already broke it on purpose

**`payout_transfers` has not been the only outbound leg since [`0011`](../../packages/db/migrations/0011_wallet.sql), and the second leg was RULED rather than allowed.**

| | `payout_transfers` | `wallet_withdrawals` |
|---|---|---|
| Declared at | [`0010:233`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql) | [`0011:126`](../../packages/db/migrations/0011_wallet.sql) |
| Reaches `payout_requests` | `NOT NULL` FK, [`0010:235`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql) | **No FK at all.** Its only two references are `identities(id)` and `risk_flags(id)` |
| Its destination | `destination_ref text NOT NULL`, [`0010:243`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql) | `destination_ref text NOT NULL`, [`0011:132`](../../packages/db/migrations/0011_wallet.sql) |
| Its idempotency | `idempotency_key text NOT NULL UNIQUE` | `idempotency_key text NOT NULL` |
| Its name-match columns | `SD-M5-02` | the same four, carried by `SD-M5-06` |
| Who ruled it | [ADR-028](ADR-028.md) | [ADR-028](ADR-028.md), `SD-M5-06` |

**[`0011:24`](../../packages/db/migrations/0011_wallet.sql) states the reason in the migration's own `E2 READ` header:** *"`wallet_withdrawals` OWNS `'transferring'` ([ADR-028](ADR-028.md)). `payout_requests` gave it up. **Two tables tracking one transfer is how they disagree.**"* And [M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)'s `SD-M5-06` gives the general form: *"The external leg is a different object from a payout request and modelling it as one would be the mistake... Conflating them means the engine's gates and the rail's gates share a status column, and the first person to add a state breaks the other one."*

**The corpus has already absorbed the consequence on the detector side, twice, and neither absorption reached ADR-017.** [M06:402](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) rows the shared-destination view as reading **`payout_transfers.destination_ref` AND `wallet_withdrawals.destination_ref`**, and says why in the next paragraph: *"a view reading `payout_transfers` alone would show a clean board while a destination collected wallet withdrawals from four identities."* [Session 91](../sessions/2026-08-20-session-91.md) established the same thing from the other side. **`D-09`'s Source cell at [M07:116](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md) still reads `payout_transfers` alone**, because `D-09` was written before the split.

**So the phrase that is false in the merged tree is [ADR-017](ADR-017.md)'s TITLE, and the phrase that is true is its DECISION line.** The title says *"one rail and one transfer table"*. The decision says *"**One rail, one destination table, one detector.**"* Session 162 quoted the title and recorded, correctly, that it *"reads as `payout_transfers` widened"* and that it could not determine which reading governs. **It is the decision line, and section 4 rules that rather than leaving it derivable.** A title is a shorthand for a ruling the entry's own body owns, which is [ADR-037](ADR-037.md)'s shape one level up from a parameter.

### 4. RULING 1. One rail is one destination namespace and one detector over it

> **[ADR-017](ADR-017.md) binds on the DESTINATION and the DETECTOR. It does not bind on the transfer table, and it has not since [`0011`](../../packages/db/migrations/0011_wallet.sql) merged.** An outbound leg complies with ADR-017 by contributing its destination to one namespace that one detector reads, not by writing a row into `payout_transfers`.

**What the ruling preserves is exactly what `AS-M8-05` is afraid of.** That scenario's attack is *"points the affiliate destination at the same account that receives trader payouts for several unrelated traders"*, and its counter is *"`D-09`'s input is every outbound destination regardless of which module produced it."* **Neither sentence mentions a table.** The control is a `GROUP BY` over a repeated destination identifier, and a `GROUP BY` does not care how many tables its input arrives from; it cares that every outbound destination is in the input and that the identifier repeats.

**`AS-M8-05`'s closing sentence is amended** from *"One rail, one destination table, one detector"* to **one rail, one destination namespace, one detector**, matching what the merged schema does and what `INV-M8-10` already says in words that never named a table.

### 5. RULING 2. The affiliate settlement leg is a sibling table, and `payout_transfers.payout_request_id` is NOT relaxed

> **The affiliate settlement leg is a new table on M5's provider machinery, on `SD-M5-06`'s precedent. `payout_transfers.payout_request_id` stays `NOT NULL`. `payout_requests` gains no nullable `account_id` and no nullable `plan_version_id`.**

**Three reasons, in order of weight.**

1. **`SD-M5-06`'s stated reason transfers to this table without a word changed.** An affiliate statement is *"a different object from a payout request"* in precisely `SD-M5-06`'s sense: a payout request is a claim against an **account** evaluated by the **rules engine**, and an affiliate statement is a period total over commissions evaluated against a **chargeback clock** and a **ToS version**. `payout_requests` carries `eligibility_snapshot jsonb NOT NULL`, `basis_trading_day date NOT NULL`, `payout_ordinal integer NOT NULL CHECK (> 0)` and the split legs `trader_cents` and `firm_cents`. **An affiliate settlement has a value for none of them**, and every value it would have to invent is an input to a gate that would then be evaluating a fiction.

2. **Nulling `payout_request_id` destroys the one thing [`0010`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql) says the table exists for.** Its header at [`0010:231`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql) reads *"Separates 'we approved' from 'the rail moved money', so a Rise outage never looks like a payout problem."* **A `payout_transfers` row with a null request is money that moved with no approval decision behind it**, which is the separation collapsing in the direction that leaves no record of the half that authorises.

3. **The `E2 READ` header names the predicate cost directly.** [`0010:17`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql) item 3 is a whole paragraph on why a predicate that silently stops matching is worse than an absent one: *"A gate that silently stops gating is worse than one that is absent, and nothing in the test suite would fail."* Every partial index and constraint on `payout_transfers` and `payout_requests` was written against rows that have an account, a plan version and an ordinal. **Admitting a row that has none of them re-runs `0010`'s own item 3 across the whole file.**

**What this ruling gives up, stated rather than glossed.** A sibling table is a second place to look, a second migration to review, a second set of provider columns to keep in step, and a second table for a later reader to forget. `wallet_withdrawals` is the standing evidence that this cost is real: it is the leg `D-09` forgot for four days, and [M06:404](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) is the repair. **The ruling accepts that cost and pays for it with RULING 4**, which is the clause that makes forgetting the third leg a gate failure rather than a discovery.

### 6. RULING 3. The binding obligation is one column, and `paid_transfer_ref` is not it

> **The affiliate settlement leg declares `destination_ref text NOT NULL`, to the same contract as the other two legs: the provider-side destination id, never bank details. `affiliate_statements.paid_transfer_ref` is NOT renamed into it, NOT widened, and NOT given a foreign key by this ruling.**

**This is where this entry corrects one clause of session 162, and the correction matters more than the clause it replaces.** Session 162 calls `paid_transfer_ref` *"the second spelling [ADR-017](ADR-017.md) exists to refuse."* **It is not a second spelling of the destination. It is not a destination at all**, and the same log proves it three landmines later: `grep -in destination` over [`0005`](../../packages/db/migrations/0005_affiliate_program.sql) and [`0012`](../../packages/db/migrations/0012_disputes_and_affiliate_settlement.sql) returns zero lines, so there is no first spelling for it to be the second of. **Both halves of session 162 are true and they name two different objects**, and which object the rail defect is decides what a migration would write.

**The two objects, and why a query cannot substitute one for the other.**

| | A destination ref | A transfer ref |
|---|---|---|
| Names | a payee endpoint | one movement of money |
| In the tree | `payout_transfers.destination_ref`, `wallet_withdrawals.destination_ref` | `payout_transfers.provider_transfer_id` [`0010:237`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql) |
| Repeats across rows | **yes, and that is the point** | **no.** [`0010:290`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql) declares `payout_transfers_provider_transfer_uq` UNIQUE on `(provider, provider_transfer_id)` |
| `D-09` reads | this one | never |

**`D-09` is a concentration query, and concentration is only expressible over an identifier that repeats.** [M07:116](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md) defines it as *"one `destination_ref` receiving payouts from more than one unrelated identity"*, which is a `GROUP BY destination_ref HAVING count(distinct identity) > 1`. **Grouped by a per-transfer identifier, every group has size one, on every input, forever.**

> **That is the direction this ruling exists to foreclose, and it is worse than the absence it replaces.** An affiliate leg outside `D-09` is a **visible** hole: the Source cell says `payout_transfers`, the affiliate table is not it, and anybody who reads both notices. **An affiliate leg wired into `D-09` through `paid_transfer_ref` reports NO CONCENTRATION on every input**, including the input `AS-M8-05` describes, and it reports it from inside the detector with the row present and the query green. **`AS-M8-05` succeeding with a detector watching is the one outcome strictly worse than `AS-M8-05` succeeding with none.**

**`paid_transfer_ref` is left exactly as it is**, and this entry states what it is for so the next session does not repair it into something else: it is the human-readable *"which transfer paid this statement"* pointer, the affiliate-side twin of `affiliate_commissions.paid_in_statement_id` at [`0012:188`](../../packages/db/migrations/0012_disputes_and_affiliate_settlement.sql), whose own comment three lines above it says it makes *"when did we pay this, and on what statement"* **a lookup rather than a reconstruction**. **Whether it gains a foreign key to the new sibling table is the migration session's question and is not ruled here**, because a foreign key is DDL and this entry writes none. What is ruled is that **giving it one would not discharge `INV-M8-10`**, and a migration session that added the key and stopped would have satisfied [ADR-017](ADR-017.md)'s title while leaving its decision line unmet.

### 7. RULING 4. The admission rule, which is what keeps RULING 1 from being a licence

**RULING 1 is a WIDENING.** It replaces *"write into `payout_transfers`"*, which a session either does or does not do, with *"contribute to the destination namespace"*, which a session can believe it has done. **The blind spot [ADR-017](ADR-017.md) refused comes back through exactly that gap**, and M08 is the proof: `grep -c "INV-M8-10\|INV-M8-11"` over [M08](../plans/M08-affiliate-system.md) returns **nine**, and not one of M08's six tables declares a destination column.

> **A new outbound leg is admissible under [ADR-017](ADR-017.md) only if, in the SAME migration that creates it, it declares `destination_ref` to the shared contract AND `D-09`'s source list gains it.** A leg that lands with the column and without the detector row is `wallet_withdrawals` between [`0011`](../../packages/db/migrations/0011_wallet.sql) and [M06:404](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md). A leg that lands with neither is `affiliate_statements` today.

**This binds every module ADR-017 already binds**, which is its own stated scope: *"refunds, bonuses, promotional credit, live-program payments, and anything M11 through M19 invents."*

**The structural form of it is a gate and this entry does not write one**, on the same restraint as the migration. **The shape is stated so the gate session inherits a specification rather than an intention:** a check that every table declaring a `destination_ref` column is named in `D-09`'s source list, and that every table in that list declares the column, **failing in both directions**, seeded from the migration directory. That is [`CI-06l`](../../scripts/corpus/gates.mjs)'s shape over a different column, and `CI-06l`'s own note records why the both-directions half is the one that matters: *"Stale entries in either list are findings, which is the direction an allowlist decays in."* **`x` is not a free `CI-06` letter** ([ADR-088](ADR-088.md)), so the gate session claims its own identifier and this entry claims none.

### 8. Does this withhold earned money? `INV-M20-09` answered directly rather than by assurance

**[M20](../plans/M20-wallet.md)'s `INV-M20-09` says a forfeiture clause on money already earned is *"the single most brand-destroying term available to this firm"*, and [ADR-075](ADR-075.md) section 3 refused the commerce half of the extraction ruling on exactly that ground.** An affiliate statement is money already earned. **A ruling touching this path owes that sentence an answer, and here it is in three parts.**

**1. This entry moves no gate and no predicate.** `INV-M8-12` is untouched. `OQ-075-01`, which asks *"must an affiliate statement settle to a `closed` identity"*, is **open, unchanged, and still owned by [ADR-075](ADR-075.md)**. Nothing here admits or refuses any `identities.status` value on any door. **A shape ruling is not a gate**, and the distinction is the whole reason ADR-075 could rule three doors while declining two.

**2. The one direction it makes settlement harder is a DELAY WITH A CLOCK, and [ADR-017](ADR-017.md) already accepted it for this table.** A settlement leg with a real `destination_ref` is a leg on which `INV-M8-11`'s 48 hour cooling window can bind, and a cooling window delays a payment to a newly changed destination. **`INV-M20-09` is about forfeiture and not about delay**, and [ADR-040:34](ADR-040.md) already drew that line one door over: *"A held request that reaches auto-release **pays**, even if the account breached during the hold... the alternative is that Merit's own hold cost the trader money, which is the exact shape zero denial exists to make impossible."* **This entry creates no state in which affiliate money stops being owed.** The thing that would trip `INV-M20-09` is a hold with no clock, which is `OQ-M8-05`'s second reading, which is **open, is the founder's, and is not closed here.**

**3. The honest direction of the current tree is the opposite of the fear, and it is measured rather than argued.** `affiliate_statements.status` can reach `'paid'` and `paid_transfer_ref` can be set to any string. **No row anywhere records a movement, no idempotency key protects it from a retry, and no destination exists to send it to**, since `grep -in destination` over both affiliate migrations returns nothing. **A settlement path that does not exist withholds 100 percent of earned affiliate commission**, and it withholds it silently, with `status = 'paid'` available to say otherwise. **This ruling is what makes settlement possible; the cooling window is the only friction it adds, and that friction was signed by the founder in [ADR-017](ADR-017.md)'s own approval line.**

### 10. `OI-06` is compatible with this ruling, and that was checked rather than assumed

**[FOLD-01](../plans/FOLD-01-phone-identity.md)'s recommendation for `OI-06` is *"a `payout_destinations` registry keyed on `(identity_id, destination_ref)` carrying `first_seen_at` and `cooling_until`, read by both payout legs and by the affiliate rail under C-24."*** That registry is the stronger form of RULING 1: it makes the destination namespace a **table** rather than a convention over three columns.

**The key works on the affiliate leg unchanged, and the reason is a merged `NOT NULL`.** [`0005`](../../packages/db/migrations/0005_affiliate_program.sql) declares `affiliates.identity_id uuid NOT NULL REFERENCES identities(id)` with the comment *"An affiliate is an identity. That is what makes the self-deal check possible at all."* **So `(identity_id, destination_ref)` is derivable for an affiliate settlement the moment the leg has a `destination_ref`**, with no second key shape and no nullable half. **This ruling therefore does not foreclose `OI-06` and is not blocked by it**, which is the one interaction session 162 recorded as unresolved between `M08-9` and `M08-2`.

**What stays open on both readings**: `INV-M8-11`'s 48 hour cooling window has **no storage on any of the three legs**, which is `OI-06` and not this entry.

---

- **Decision:** **[ADR-017](ADR-017.md) binds on the destination namespace and the detector, not on the transfer table** (RULING 1). **The affiliate settlement leg is a sibling table on M5's provider machinery**, and `payout_transfers.payout_request_id` stays `NOT NULL` (RULING 2). **Its one binding obligation is `destination_ref text NOT NULL` to the shared contract**; `paid_transfer_ref` is not that column and is not renamed into it (RULING 3). **A new outbound leg is admissible only if its own migration declares the column and adds the leg to `D-09`'s source list** (RULING 4). **No migration, no `packages/db` change, no engine change, and no gate is written here.**

- **Amendments this ruling makes to frozen documents, and the fence.** [M08](../plans/M08-affiliate-system.md) section 3.4's second premise row moves from **TRUE** to **TRUE OF THE DESTINATION AND FALSE OF THE MECHANISM**; `AS-M8-05`'s closing sentence moves from *"one destination table"* to *"one destination namespace"*; [M07:116](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md)'s `D-09` Source cell moves from `payout_transfers` to the destination columns of every outbound leg, which [M06:402](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) already reads as two. **All three files are OUTSIDE this session's fence and are NOT edited here**, so each will read its old text beside a ruling that moves it until a session holding the file amends it. **This is [ADR-082](ADR-082.md)'s disposition of `OI-28` taken deliberately rather than by omission**, and it is recorded here because an amendment nobody applies is an amendment nobody made.

- **Alternatives considered:**

  - **Widen `payout_transfers` by making `payout_request_id` nullable** (rejected, section 5). It is the reading [ADR-017](ADR-017.md)'s title invites and session 162 named first. It admits a transfer row with no approval decision behind it, which is the separation [`0010:231`](../../packages/db/migrations/0010_payouts.sql) says the table exists to make, and it re-runs that file's own `E2 READ` item 3 across every predicate written against rows that have an account and an ordinal.
  - **Widen `payout_requests` so an affiliate statement is one** (rejected, section 5). It requires nulling `account_id` and `plan_version_id` and inventing `eligibility_snapshot`, `basis_trading_day` and `payout_ordinal` for an object that has none. It is the shape `SD-M5-06` refused by name for `wallet_withdrawals`, and refusing it twice for the same reason is consistency rather than repetition.
  - **Rename `paid_transfer_ref` to `destination_ref` and wire it into `D-09`** (rejected, section 6, and **this is the dangerous one**). It is the cheapest change in the set, it makes every citation read correctly, and it makes the strongest mule detector in the system return **no concentration on every input** because the column it now reads is unique per transfer by construction.
  - **Rule nothing and let the migration session decide** (rejected). It is the option [ADR-087](ADR-087.md) argues for on DDL and against here: the shape is not a constraint installed because it was cheap, it is the choice between three tables and three foreign-key regimes, and a session dispatched to write a migration will take it in passing. Session 162's `M08-2` exists to stop that.
  - **Wait for `OI-06`'s registry and rule the leg against it** (rejected, section 10). The registry is the stronger form and it is a founder ruling with its own migration. Ordering this behind it leaves `M08-4` and `M08-8` blocked on an item deliberately undecided since 2026-08-16, and section 10 shows the two compose rather than compete.
