### 6. RULED: the cardinal is not the repairable half, and the coordinate is

The dispatch asked whether **restating a register's cardinal in prose** is repairable at all, or whether it should be deleted and replaced by a pointer to the register, as [ADR-383](ADR-383.md) section 8 and [ADR-386](ADR-386.md) section 8 ruled for the `covers` cardinals. **The question is asked about the wrong half of the token, and both of its offered answers are wrong for this shape.**

**THE `covers` PRECEDENT DOES NOT TRANSFER, AND THE REASON IS A PROPERTY OF THE SENTENCE RATHER THAN OF THE NUMBER.** A `covers` line is the **live self-description of a running check**. It is present tense, it carries no date, it is read as a statement about the tree the runner is standing in, and a stale cardinal in it is therefore a false claim being made continuously. Deleting it and naming the ADR that measured it loses nothing, because the sentence was never a record.

**THESE SIX SENTENCES ARE THE OPPOSITE SHAPE.** Five of the six sit in a table headed *"The pre-edit grep, and what already pins this subject"*, whose third column is literally **"Moved?"** and whose answer is **"No."** The table exists to record **what the row measured before it edited, and why its diff did not disturb it**. The cardinal is not decoration on that record. **It is the measurement.** Section 3 proves each one was accurate.

So:

- **Rewriting `7` to `10` is refused.** It would make [ADR-335](ADR-335.md) section 9 claim it measured 10 on `27115f60`, where the register read 7. That manufactures a false measurement, and it is the thing [ADR-367](ADR-367.md) forbids and [ADR-393](ADR-393.md) section 7 and [ADR-396](ADR-396.md) already declined twice.
- **Deleting the cardinal and pointing at the register is also refused**, and this is the half the dispatch did not anticipate. The pointer would resolve to **today's** value, and the sentence would then read as though the row had checked something it did not check. **A pointer is not a substitute for a record: it answers a question about now, and the sentence is asking a question about then.**
- **What IS repairable is the tense**, and it is the actual defect. *"registers this document at `sites: 7`"* reads as live. *"registered this document at `sites: 7` as the register read on this row's base commit"* reads as what it is. **The figure is untouched and the reader can no longer mistake a record for a claim.**

**THE RULING, STATED SO IT CAN BE APPLIED WITHOUT THIS ENTRY.** A cardinal inside a dated record is **not a restatement of a register and never was**, so [ADR-034](../decisions/ADR-034.md)'s prohibition does not reach it and [ADR-383](ADR-383.md)'s remedy does not apply to it. **The prohibition reaches the undated present tense, not the number.** Date the sentence and the same digits stop being a restatement.

**AND THE COORDINATE GOES THE OTHER WAY.** `:8579` is a live pointer, it claims something about the tree as it stands, it has been wrong on three of the eight refs in section 3's table, and it has cost two rows a repair each and is about to cost a third. **It is deleted and replaced by a name.** `RETIRED_REGISTER`'s entry for the document cannot drift, and this row does not own `gates.mjs`, which is precisely the case its own fence rule describes: a citation into a file you do not own is a citation into a moving target.
