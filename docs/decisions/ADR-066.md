## ADR-066: Five vendor-parity surfaces admitted, two excluded, and the largest of the five is not new scope but an outstanding commitment nobody had noticed was unsatisfied  (2026-08-20, status: accepted)

**A baseline check against the PropAccount vendor demo referred six items. This entry admits five surfaces into five frozen module plans, excludes two candidates by founder ruling, sizes every admitted component, and rules on nothing on the money path. Its sharpest content is that the item which looked most like new scope is not scope at all: [M07](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md) `DEP-M7-06` has declared a maintained Tier-1 economic calendar as a data dependency since M07 was written, `FM-M7-08` has required a staleness alarm for it, and no table in this corpus satisfies either, so `D-04` news-window clustering is not implementable today and has never been. The parity check found a hole in the corpus, not a hole against a competitor. Four of the six referred items were mis-scoped against the tree; every correction is in section 0 rather than absorbed silently.**

- **Context:** [FOLD-03](../plans/FOLD-03-vendor-parity-gap-fill.md) refers six items from a vendor baseline check. Five of them add scope to [M04](../plans/M04-trader-portal.md), [M06](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md), [M07](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md), [M16](../plans/M16-notification-center.md) and [PROP_TECH_LANDSCAPE](../../research/PROP_TECH_LANDSCAPE.md). [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) and [STATE](../STATE.md) both say **"Changing a frozen document requires an ADR, not a commit"**, so none of it lands until this entry does. **Every FOLD-03 session cites this ruling**, which is why it runs first and alone: a fold that landed ahead of it would amend a frozen document on a session's own authority. The sixth item, manual balance adjustment, is money path and is **not ruled here** (section 7).

### 2. The calendar: the commitment is the dataset, and that settles the source question without arguing it

**`DEP-M7-06` assigns the dataset to "M6 admin, seed" and `FM-M7-08` puts its freshness on the same footing as `contract_specs` and `trading_calendar`.** Two frozen rows, written when M07 was, describing a table that does not exist. **`D-04` has therefore been unimplementable for the whole life of the corpus and no gate noticed**, because a declared dependency with no satisfying object is invisible to every check this repository runs.

**The ruling is an ingested feed into a Merit-owned table, and an embed is refused on mechanical grounds rather than on preference.** An embed cannot carry a **revision**, cannot be **staleness-monitored**, and cannot be **joined to `fills`. `D-04` needs all three**: it clusters entries against a *scheduled release instant*, `FM-M7-08` demands the staleness clock, and a release time that moves must move `D-04`'s window with it. **An embed rendered beside the table would be a second source of truth for "when was the news", which is the failure `FM-M7-08` already guards.**

**The declining behaviour is the load-bearing acceptance and it is a ruling, not a detail.** `FM-M7-08` says a stale calendar makes `D-04` fire *"on the wrong windows or not at all"*. **Firing on wrong windows is strictly worse than not firing: it manufactures evidence against a trader.** So a stale calendar makes `D-04` **decline**, and the alarm is what carries the failure to a human. `GS-287` pins it.

**The vendor is not chosen here.** `OQ-F3-03` stands: the shape is ruled, the procurement is the founder's and carries a cost line.

### 3. The digests: the alarm fires on the delivery record, and that is why any of it is MUST

**Two of the four digests are MUST and the reason is not the report, it is the ritual.** The C8 weekly risk ritual's input is **currently a human remembering to look**. The daily liability digest (Open Liability, Eligible-Next-7-Days, reserve coverage ratio per [M06:90](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) `P-M6-07`) and the weekly plan loss-ratio and CUSUM digest are the ritual's input, so a control that exists and does not arrive is a control that enforces nothing. **The flag-queue and monthly cohort digests are SHOULD**: they are useful and nothing depends on them.

**The delivery-failure alarm is MUST and it earns it the same way [M05:91](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) `INV-M5-18` does. The alarm reads `report_deliveries`, never the job's own report.** `INV-M5-18` is asserted *"independently of whether the hourly sweep reported success"* on the stated ground that **"a job that reports success is not evidence that the work happened."** A second sweep with the same shape gets the same control rather than a new one. `GS-288` pins the case where the job reports success and nothing arrived.

**`INV-M6-10` is not weakened by any digest.** [M06:54](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) permits trader-identifying data only when the query names a specific subject. **No digest is a bulk identity export**: the liability digest is aggregate, and the flag-queue digest carries counts and links rather than trader-identifying rows.

**`OQ-F3-04` stands and the founder closes it at signature.** Email is the MUST channel. **SFTP push is admitted as SHOULD and is a second credential surface**, which makes "is email alone acceptable for v1" a security question rather than a convenience one. It reuses **no [M02](../plans/M02-rithmic-bridge.md) code path**: M02's SFTP is a vendor wire format under [ADR-005](ADR-005.md), and coupling them would make a reporting change a provisioning incident.

### 4. The duplicate-signal views: sorted by liability, because sorted by count teaches the wrong lesson

**Nothing new is detected and no migration is needed.** [M07:84](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md)'s approved signal model is *"normalized email, device fingerprint, IP and ASN, payment fingerprint, verified KYC identity, and settlement-rail identity"*, and phone is already a signal: [M07:123](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md) `D-18` reads `identity_phones` carrier metadata at capture (`SD-M19-05`). **Every signal the six views need already exists.** M07 gains **one sentence** recording that these are views and that no detector is duplicated; a test asserts they read `identity_signals` and the link tiers and compute no confidence of their own.

**The default sort is aggregate open liability, descending, and it is the ruling rather than a preference.** [M07:94](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md) says *"A shared IP is a coffee shop; a shared device is a household; a shared card is a family. None of them is a person."* **A view sorted by signal count therefore ranks the coffee shop first and teaches the operator to chase it.** Sorting by liability is what makes the surface a risk tool instead of a curiosity. `GS-291` asserts it.

**The link tiers are respected and this is the safety property.** A **soft link renders as a soft link and changes nothing a trader may buy**, per [M07:94](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md)'s *"Only a hard merge changes what a trader may buy."* `GS-292` pins it. **`INV-M6-10` holds**: naming a signal and its identities is a specific-subject query.

### 5. Communications: five classes, and most of the item already exists

**Cited, not added.** `GET /admin/notifications/:identityId` already exists at [M16:197](../plans/M16-notification-center.md) and already answers *"What was sent, when, to which channel, and its delivery status. The proof-of-notice query."* `SD-M16-02` at [M16:87](../plans/M16-notification-center.md) already carries `rendered_body text`. `FM-M16-05` at [M16:223](../plans/M16-notification-center.md) already separates `sent_at`, `delivered_at` and `read_at`, and the machine at [M16:113](../plans/M16-notification-center.md) already carries `dispatched --> delivered: provider confirms`.

**Genuinely new, and this is all of it: `bounced` and `spam_complaint` as outcomes, the alert they feed, admin resend, and admin download.** `0041` is **contingent** and the folding session confirms the delta before spending it, per [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md).

**The bounce alert is MUST because OTP login depends on deliverability.** [M16:25](../plans/M16-notification-center.md)'s five classes make the **security class exempt from rate limits and preference opt-outs**, which is exactly why **a bounce on it is an incident and not a preference**: a silently bounced OTP is a locked-out trader with no signal anywhere. `GS-293`.

**Resend re-renders nothing.** It re-sends the stored `rendered_body`, so proof of notice survives a template change. A resend that re-rendered would destroy the evidence `SD-M16-02` exists to preserve. `GS-294`.

**`GS-295` is where the five-class ruling earns itself.** A spam complaint suppresses **marketing** class and leaves **security** class untouched. A complaint that suppressed every class would silence the freeze notice; the class table already forbids it and the fixture pins it.

### 8. Alternatives

  **ADMIT NONE OF IT: the vendor baseline is not our roadmap** (rejected, and it is the strongest objection to this entry rather than a straw man). The position is coherent and section 7 of [FOLD-03](../plans/FOLD-03-vendor-parity-gap-fill.md) is its best evidence: **Merit is at or above vendor baseline on every demoed surface**, with instant auto-approval against a human queue ([M05](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) `INV-M5-01`, no `denied` value), liability plus CVaR reserve coverage plus CUSUM against an open-positions tab, and a scored identity graph against duplicate-IP lists. **A roadmap that admits scope because a competitor demoed it has outsourced its prioritisation**, and four of the six referred items were mis-scoped against our own tree, which is precisely the evidence that the referral was reading the demo more carefully than the corpus. **It is rejected on one ground and not on parity: the two MUSTs do not depend on the vendor at all.** The calendar closes `DEP-M7-06`, a commitment made in 2026 and unsatisfied since, and the bounce alert closes a path where a trader is locked out of OTP login with no signal. **Both would be MUST if no competitor existed.** What the parity check did was cause somebody to look; the case for the MUSTs is internal. **The four SHOULDs are exactly where this objection lands, and they are sized SHOULD for that reason** rather than being talked up to MUST to justify the exercise.

  **ADMIT THE FIVE AND SIZE THEM ALL MUST** (rejected: it would be the same error in the opposite direction. [DELIVERY_PLAN](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md) sizes by launch-blocking test rather than by appetite, and **an M04 calendar panel, six admin views and a download button do not block a launch**. A MUST set that contains things nobody would delay a launch for is a MUST set that stops meaning anything, and the next real MUST arrives into a list that has been devalued).

  **A THIRD-PARTY CALENDAR EMBED INSTEAD OF AN INGESTED TABLE** (rejected on mechanics, section 2: an embed carries no revision, cannot be staleness-monitored and cannot be joined to `fills`. It would satisfy the *panel* and satisfy **none** of `DEP-M7-06`, `D-04` or `FM-M7-08`, which is the entire commitment. It is cheaper precisely because it does not do the work).

  **SPECIFY A REPORT BUILDER WITH SAVED VIEWS AND SCHEDULE THOSE** (rejected: it is what the referral described and it is a module, not a fold. **Nothing in the corpus specifies a reporting layer**, so admitting item 2 as written would mean designing one inside a parity gap-fill. **The four digests are all derivable from panels [M06](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) already has**, so the named-set scope delivers the operator value at a fraction of the surface, and a builder remains available later as its own module with its own ruling).

  **FOLD THE FIVE SURFACES IN THIS SESSION AND SKIP THE SEPARATE RULING** (rejected: it inverts the rule this corpus runs on. Five frozen documents would be amended on a session's authority, and **every FOLD-03 session cites this entry**, so the ruling has to exist before the first fold rather than be reconstructed from five diffs afterwards).
