### 7. One cause, and why exactly the two cases that assert a WRITE SUCCEEDS are the two that fail

**BOTH REDS ARE `23503` ON `admin_actions_actor_is_an_operator`.** The constraint is declared at [`packages/db/migrations/0073_operator_directory.sql:274-277`](../../packages/db/migrations/0073_operator_directory.sql):

```sql
ALTER TABLE admin_actions
  ADD CONSTRAINT admin_actions_actor_is_an_operator
    FOREIGN KEY (actor) REFERENCES operators(actor)
    ON UPDATE RESTRICT ON DELETE RESTRICT;
```

Its comment at [`0073:279-283`](../../packages/db/migrations/0073_operator_directory.sql) states its whole purpose: *"An action whose ACTOR resolves to no row is unexplained in the way that matters most, and this constraint is the half 0017 could not write because no directory existed."*

**THE SUITE'S `seed()` AT [`admin-writes.test.ts:708-774`](../../apps/api/test/admin-writes.test.ts) INSERTS SEVEN ROWS AND NONE OF THEM IS AN OPERATOR**: `identities`, `users`, `plans`, `planVersions`, `purchases`, `accounts`, `riskFlags`. Its `liveBackend` at [`admin-writes.test.ts:794`](../../apps/api/test/admin-writes.test.ts) supplies `principal: () => Promise.resolve({ actor: 'sso:owner@merit', role })`, and `sso:owner@merit` is in no directory because there is no directory.

**WHY THREE OF THE FIVE STILL PASS, DERIVED AT THE DATABASE RATHER THAN REASONED ABOUT.** Three probe inserts were run directly through `psql` against the same schema:

| Probe | Refused by |
|---|---|
| `reason` omitted, actor unknown | `null value in column "reason" of relation "admin_actions" violates not-null constraint` |
| `reason` present, actor unknown | `insert or update on table "admin_actions" violates foreign key constraint "admin_actions_actor_is_an_operator"` |

**POSTGRES CHECKS `NOT NULL` AND `CHECK` BEFORE IT FIRES A REFERENTIAL TRIGGER.** So each of the three passing cases raises its own violation (`23502` on `reason`, `23502` on `initiative`, `23514` on `admin_actions_on_behalf_matches_initiative`) **before the foreign key is ever consulted**, and each therefore gets the `400` its assertion wants, **for a reason one layer shallower than the one it names**. The two failing cases are the only two that require an `admin_actions` row to actually land, and they are the only two that reach the foreign key.

**THIS FILE PREDICTED ITS OWN FAILURE MODE IN ITS FIRST PARAGRAPH.** [`admin-writes.test.ts:4-8`](../../apps/api/test/admin-writes.test.ts) reads: *"an admin write with no reason is refused, THE REFUSAL COMES FROM THE DATABASE, and the same call carrying a reason succeeds. **Both halves, because a guard that refuses everything passes every refusal test**."* **Today the database refuses everything and every refusal case passes.** The suite's own stated reason for having a success half is the exact reason it is not green, and the success half is the half that failed.
