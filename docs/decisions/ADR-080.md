## ADR-080: A `VG` row closes on its NEXT link and never on its whole chain, and two of the twelve are unwired for no reason at all  (2026-08-22, status: proposed)

**[ADR-073](ADR-073.md) section 8 named this ruling and refused to take it: *"a `VG` row's condition is its STAGE, which makes the conditions CHAIN, and a chained condition is a shape nobody has ruled."* This entry rules that shape. A chained condition names ONE row of [STRATEGY section 4.1](../testing/STRATEGY.md) and never that row's own artifact, and it is available only while the row it names is NOT implemented. That second clause is the whole of the ruling's value, because it is what turns `VG-5` from a row that would have read "waiting on `CI-06`" forever into what it actually is: a gate whose stage has run since P1 and which nobody wired.**

**Two of the twelve rows are findings on the day this is signed, and neither is waiting on anything.** `VG-5` has its stage and its subject and no implementation. `VG-8`'s `PreToolUse` half is named as its implementation in three documents and does not exist in `.claude/settings.json`. **Every figure below was measured on this tree on 2026-08-22 rather than carried from the referring plan**, and the independently derived count of wired rows reproduces [STATE](../STATE.md)'s three of twelve.

- **Context:** [ADR-073](ADR-073.md) ruled the ten **stage** rows of [STRATEGY section 4.1](../testing/STRATEGY.md) and stated its own boundary twice, at [ADR-073:42](ADR-073.md) (*"Section 4.2's twelve rows are **not** in this ruling's scope"*) and again at [ADR-073:191](ADR-073.md) after its signature. `CI-06/gate-inventory` was built to that ruling and repeats the boundary in its own `covers` line: it *"rules on the STAGE row and never on the `VG` rows of section 4.2, whose conditions would chain."* The ruling has been named and unowned since 2026-08-20. **This entry takes it, writes no gate, and rules nothing on the money path.**

### 1. The ruling

> **A row of [STRATEGY section 4.2](../testing/STRATEGY.md) is CLOSED when every leg it carries is well formed, and a row that carries no leg is a finding.** The unit is the leg, not the row, on `CI-06/gate-inventory`'s own precedent.

| | Disposition | What the leg must carry | What makes it checkable |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | **Wired** | The workflow, the **job**, and the **step name or rule id** that runs it | All three resolve on disk. **This is strictly stronger than section 4.1's (a)**, and section 3 is why |
| **(b)** | **Waiting on an artifact** | A date and **one artifact**. Unchanged from [ADR-073:35](ADR-073.md) | The artifact probes to **absent**, so the leg fails on good news |
| **(c)** | **Discharged outside Actions** | The register that carries the obligation | The register resolves and **rows the work** |
| **(d)** | **Chained** | **One `CI-nn` row of section 4.1**, named as the row and never as that row's artifact | The named row resolves in 4.1 **and is not at disposition (a)**. The day it reaches (a), the chained row is a finding |

**(d) is new and its second clause is the entire ruling.** A chained leg is not a place a row rests. It is a claim that the work is impossible today, and that claim expires the moment the stage exists.

### 2. The chain: the next link, and what the whole chain would have cost

**The question is real and both answers are defensible.** `VG-2`'s condition is `CI-07`; `CI-07`'s condition is a `build` script in any `apps/*/package.json`. So `VG-2`'s chain is two links long and something has to say which link the row names.

> **RULED: a chained condition names its NEXT LINK ONLY.** `VG-2` waits on `CI-07`. It does not wait on a `build` script, and its row does not say `build` script.

**Three reasons, and the second is decisive.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **One source of truth** | Naming the whole chain copies `CI-07`'s artifact into two other rows. `CI-06/gate-inventory` states in its own `covers` line that its unprobeable register *"shrinks as ADR-073 re-rules an artifact into a path or a manifest key"*, so **those artifacts are expected to be re-ruled**. Every re-ruling would strand two copies, and nothing joins a 4.2 row to a 4.1 row to notice |
| **It fires when the work becomes possible, not before** | Under the whole chain, `VG-2` reopens the day a `build` script lands. **On that day `CI-07` does not exist and `VG-2` cannot be wired**, so the gate produces a finding nobody can act on. [STRATEGY section 1](../testing/STRATEGY.md) forbids exactly that: *"A test suite whose failures are not trusted is worse than a smaller suite whose failures are, because the first one trains its own reader to click through red."* Under the next link, `VG-2` reopens the day `CI-07` reaches (a), which is the first day the work exists |
| **It makes the chain a graph rather than prose** | Two tables joined on one key. A cycle is detectable, and the chain's length is derived rather than transcribed |

**The cost of the next link, stated so it is not discovered by the session that builds the gate.**

- **The condition is not directly probeable.** A 4.2 row's state now depends on a 4.1 row being well formed, so **a malformed stage row makes several `VG` rows unreadable rather than one stage row**. That is a real widening of the blast radius and it is the price of the join.
- **Reopening is deferred, and the deferral is unbounded.** `VG-2` is invisible for the whole period between a `build` script landing and `CI-07` being written. **If `CI-07` is never written, `VG-2` never reopens** and this ruling buys nothing for those rows. It is the same dependency [ADR-073:62](ADR-073.md) admits about itself: *"the ruling is worth exactly what that gate is worth."*
- **It requires the conjunctive-leg rule**, section 4, because one row has a chain and an independent subject.

**A chained leg may name a section 4.1 row only.** A `VG` row chaining to another `VG` row is refused: one link of that kind and never a chain of them, so the join stays a join.

### 3. Why the stage existing is not sufficient, which is `VG-5`

**`CI-06` is implemented. `VG-5` is assigned to `CI-06`. `VG-5` is not wired.** [STRATEGY:178](../testing/STRATEGY.md) already states this boundary for the stage table in its own words, and [ADR-073:42](ADR-073.md) repeats it. **What neither of them does is say what disposition `VG-5` then takes**, and that is the hole this entry is here to close.

> **Under (d)'s second clause `VG-5` cannot be closed, because the row it would chain to is implemented. `VG-5` is a FINDING.**

**This is the ruling paying for itself on its first row.** Had (d) been written to let a `VG` row name its stage regardless of that stage's state, `VG-5` would read *"waiting on `CI-06`"* and be closed forever, against a stage that has run since P1. **That is precisely the marker no gate reads which [ADR-073:167](ADR-073.md) rejects by name**, reintroduced one table lower.

**And `VG-5` is not waiting on anything.** Its subject is [API_CONTRACT section 12](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md)'s matrix, present at line 658 and headed *"required in CI"*. Its stage runs on every push. **No honest condition is available to it**, so none is invented.

**`VG-5` is also why (a) is strengthened for a `VG` row.** Section 4.1's (a) asserts that a job exists, and [ADR-073:43](ADR-073.md) is explicit that it *"asserts that a job exists, never that its contents cell is true"*. **A `VG` row inherited that reading unchanged would count `VG-5` as wired the moment `CI-06` was**, because `VG-5`'s stage is named and its job resolves. [DELIVERY_PLAN:125](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md) asks for *"every VG gate wired and failing correctly on a seeded violation"*, so a `VG` row's (a) names the **step or rule** and not only the job, which is a string that resolves for `VG-1`, `VG-4` and `VG-12` and resolves to nothing for `VG-5`.

### 4. Why a `VG` condition can be an artifact, which is `VG-11`, and the conjunctive rule, which is `VG-10`

**`CI-02` runs and `VG-11` is assigned to it, so `VG-11` has no chain. Its subject is what is missing.** No upload path exists in the tree, and the absence is a ruling rather than a backlog: [VIBE_FAILURE_POSTMORTEMS:66](../../research/VIBE_FAILURE_POSTMORTEMS.md), which is where the `VG` series is defined, says it in its own parenthesis, *"(v1 has minimal uploads; the rule stands so it is never improvised.)"* and [API_CONTRACT:488](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) rules that Merit never proxies documents.

> **RULED: `VG-11` takes (b) and not (d). A `VG` row's condition is its stage BY DEFAULT and not BY RULE**, and the referral's framing that "a `VG` row's condition is its stage" is true of nine rows and false of this one.

**`VG-11`'s artifact is a content condition, and that is stated rather than dressed up as a path.** The artifact is **an endpoint in [API_CONTRACT](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) whose request body is not `application/json`**. [ADR-073:35](ADR-073.md) requires an artifact *"stated as a path or as a manifest key"* and this is neither. **The phrasing is extended here rather than stretched**, to admit a named property of a named file, and the honest cost is that a content probe is weaker than an existence probe: a media type spelled differently defeats it. **`CI-06/gate-inventory` already reports that four of the six stage conditions name neither a path nor a manifest key**, on every run, so this entry is widening a form the existing gate has been reporting against rather than inventing a loophole. The alternative, a multipart parser in the lockfile, was rejected for firing **late**: a hand-rolled parser would never trip it, and a condition that never fires is the row [ADR-073](ADR-073.md) exists to end.

**`VG-10` carries two legs, and it is the only row that does.** Its stage is `CI-07`, so it chains; its subject is a live bucket policy, and no object storage exists. Those are independent, and [ADR-073:44](ADR-073.md)'s *"one artifact per condition"* forbids folding them into one leg.

> **RULED: in section 4.2 the legs of a row are CONJUNCTIVE prerequisites for one implementation, so the row reopens only when EVERY leg is satisfied.**

**This is the inverse of section 4.1's rule and the inversion is the trap.** `CI-06/gate-inventory` states that a stage row *"is OPEN for each activation condition it carries"*, per leg, and that is right there: `CI-09`'s four legs are four independent deliverables and one of them shipped alone. **`VG-10`'s two legs are not deliverables. They are both prerequisites for a single test that cannot be written until both hold.** A gate that inherited 4.1's semantics would report `VG-10` open the day a `build` script landed, with no bucket to test against.

**Exactly one row exercises this today**, and that is said plainly so the rule is not read as a general shape. `VG-2`'s subject is the built bundle, which is `CI-07`'s artifact and not an independent one, so `VG-2` carries one leg.

### 7. What closing P1 would mean, which is the stake

**[DELIVERY_PLAN:125](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md) gives P1's definition of done as *"Every VG gate wired and failing correctly on a seeded violation. VG-12 is not deferred"*, and [ADR-073:155](ADR-073.md) is emphatic that this and not the stage inventory is what P1 closes on.** Read at its word, "every VG gate wired" means twelve rows at disposition (a).

> **Twelve (a)s is not reachable, and not because of effort.** It requires `CI-04` and `CI-07`, so it transitively requires a provisioned Neon branch, a bundler nobody has chosen and a production build. **It further requires an object store and a file-upload path**, and `VG-11`'s own defining document says v1 has minimal uploads while [API_CONTRACT:488](../architecture/API_CONTRACT.md) rules that Merit never proxies documents. **P1's definition of done, taken literally, contains a feature Merit has ruled it will not have at v1.**

**So the founder has a choice and this entry states it rather than making it**, because [DELIVERY_PLAN](../DELIVERY_PLAN.md) is outside this session's fence and P1's definition of done is not a session's to rewrite.

| Reading | What it means | Distance |
|---|---|---|
| **P1 closes when every `VG` row carries a well-formed leg** | The disposition table of section 6 is complete and no row is a finding | **Two findings away.** `VG-5` and `VG-8`'s hook |
| **P1 closes when twelve rows read (a)** | Every stage exists and every gate inside it is wired | **Unreachable at v1** for the reasons above |

**The first is recommended and the honest argument for it is not convenience.** `VG-2`, `VG-10` and `VG-11` are gates on surfaces that do not exist. **A foundation milestone that cannot close until the product has an upload path is not measuring the foundation**, and the three rows are exactly the ones this ruling gives a condition that fires on the day their surface arrives. **That is what a chained condition buys and it is the whole of what it buys.**

### 8. What a later gate must read, and why this entry writes none

**[ADR-073:183](ADR-073.md) wrote no gate and stated the parse so its implementer would not have to infer it. This entry does the same**, and states one thing more, because there is a way to get this wrong that reads well.

1. **Every row of section 4.2 carries at least one leg, and every leg is well formed.**
2. **An (a) leg names a workflow, a top-level job key in that file, and a step name or rule id that resolves.** The third is the addition and section 3 is its reason.
3. **A (d) leg names exactly one `CI-nn` row that exists in section 4.1, and that row is NOT at disposition (a).** It may not name a `VG` row.
4. **A (b) leg is [ADR-073](ADR-073.md)'s unchanged**: dated, one artifact, probing to absent.
5. **A row carrying more than one (b) or (d) leg reopens only when EVERY such leg is satisfied.**
6. **A (c) leg links a register that resolves and rows the work.**

> **The third and the fifth are the assertions that do the work. The fifth is the one an implementer will get wrong**, because `CI-06/gate-inventory` already asserts the opposite for section 4.1, in a sentence that is correct there and reads perfectly well.

**And the third is the one that fails on good news**, which is [ADR-073:161](ADR-073.md)'s own observation about its gate: the assertion most likely to be left out is the one whose failure is a report that something got better.

**Two rows will fail assertion 1 on the day that gate is written.** That is stated here so it is not discovered as a surprise: `VG-5` and `VG-8`'s hook half carry no honest leg, and the gate cannot be turned on until they are wired or re-ruled. **This entry does not soften either into a condition to make the gate green**, which is the trade [ADR-073:166](ADR-073.md) rejects as *"the same thing wearing the disposition it is trying to earn."*

- **Alternatives considered.**

  - **A chained condition names its whole chain, so `VG-2` waits on a `build` script.** Rejected in section 2 on three grounds, of which the second is decisive: the row would reopen on a day when the work is still impossible, producing a finding nobody can act on, which [STRATEGY section 1](../testing/STRATEGY.md) forbids by name. It also duplicates an artifact the existing gate's own register expects to be re-ruled.
  - **A chained condition names its stage regardless of that stage's state.** Rejected in section 3. It is the reading that closes `VG-5` forever against a stage that has run since P1, and it reintroduces the unread marker [ADR-073:167](ADR-073.md) rejects, one table lower.
  - **Rule that a `VG` row's condition IS its stage, uniformly.** Rejected in section 4. It is true of nine rows and false of `VG-11`, whose stage runs and whose subject does not exist, and a rule stated uniformly would have given `VG-11` a chained leg to a stage that cannot make it implementable.
  - **Give `VG-5` and `VG-8`'s hook a dated condition so the table has no findings.** Rejected as the entry's worst available move. Nothing is absent that either is waiting for: `VG-5`'s matrix is on disk and the hook is a key in a settings file. **A condition invented to make a gate green is the marker this whole line of rulings exists to end.**
  - **Wire `VG-5` and the `PreToolUse` hook in this session.** Rejected on the fence and on [ADR-073](ADR-073.md)'s precedent. This entry writes no gate and no hook; both are outside the fence and each is a session with its own falsification.
  - **Adopt the referral's approval clause as offered.** Rejected on measurement rather than on taste, and the arithmetic is in the approval line below.
  - **Inherit section 4.1's per-leg reopening rule for section 4.2.** Rejected in section 4. The legs of a stage row are independent deliverables and the legs of a `VG` row are conjunctive prerequisites, and the difference is invisible until a row carries both kinds.

- **Consequences.**

  - **[STRATEGY section 4.2](../testing/STRATEGY.md) gains a `Disposition` column and every one of its twelve rows carries a leg or is named a finding.** No row is added and no assignment is changed; [ADR-073](ADR-073.md) section 7's move of `VG-11` to `CI-02` is inherited and not re-litigated.
  - **Two findings are on the record**, `VG-5` and `VG-8`'s `PreToolUse` half, and neither was known to be a finding before this measurement. Both were counted among the *"nine assigned to stages that do not exist"*, and **neither is**.
  - **`VG-7`'s (c) is admitted and named as the weakest disposition in the table**, with its repair stated so the next reading is not a rediscovery.
  - **`VG-8`'s grants half is discharged and unasserted**, and the probe that would assert it is named and not written.
