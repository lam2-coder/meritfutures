## ADR-063: `SET A` keeps `INV-M5-17`, `18` and `19`, and one of the six rows is not a collision but a duplicate  (2026-08-20, status: accepted)

**[M05 section 1.3](../plans/M05-payout-system.md)'s invariant table is amended by this ruling, and so is [SECURITY](../architecture/SECURITY.md)'s leg 8 citation. Two identifiers are created and one row is deleted, so approving it changes what every downstream citation of `INV-M5-17` and `INV-M5-18` means.**

- **Context:** **`M05` defines `INV-M5-17`, `INV-M5-18` and `INV-M5-19` twice each and both sets are cited**, inside `M05` and outside it. An invariant id that means two things cannot be cited, which is why `CI-06u` carries `M05` on its exemption register and why this is [ADR-061](ADR-061.md)'s **T2** trigger rather than a repair. `M20:170` and [SECURITY](../architecture/SECURITY.md)`:172` cite `INV-M5-18` **for two different invariants**, so the ambiguity has already left the document.

  **Two premises this ruling was commissioned on are wrong on the tree, and both make the repair smaller. They are stated first because the ruling turns on them.**

---

### 1. It is five invariants across six rows, not six

**`SET A`'s `INV-M5-18` and `SET B`'s `INV-M5-17` are the same invariant.** They were read as "entirely different content" and they are not:

| | `SET A`, `M05:91` | `SET B`, `M05:101` |
|---|---|---|
| Statement | No payout request sits past its hold or freeze expiry | No hold and no freeze outlives its expiry, naming `payout_requests` past `hold_expires_at`, past `freeze_expires_at`, and `wallet_withdrawals` past `freeze_expires_at` |
| Where the assertion runs | "**on the query**, evaluated independently of whether the hourly sweep reported success" | "the assertion runs **on the query rather than on the job**" |
| Standing | "It is the **fourth unsuppressible alarm**, amending [M06](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md) `OQ-M6-01`" | "It is the **fourth unsuppressible alarm** (section 9.2)" |

**`M05` names exactly one fourth unsuppressible alarm.** [`M05:215`](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) reads "It is the fourth unsuppressible alarm. `M06` `OQ-M6-01` names three; this amends it", and [`M05:285`](../plans/M05-payout-system.md) reads "It becomes the fourth unsuppressible alarm, amending `M06` `OQ-M6-01`. Section 9.2." **Two rows cannot both be the fourth of three.** They are one invariant written twice, and `SET B`'s statement is strictly the wider of the two: it covers `wallet_withdrawals` rows, which `SET A`'s does not.

**So the repair is a MERGE and a RENUMBER, not a renumber alone.** Under [ADR-061](ADR-061.md) a doubled key whose rows **agree** is merged and one whose rows **differ** escalates; this file needed both rules at once and the commissioning brief saw only the second.

### 3. The ruling

**`SET A` keeps `17`, `18` and `19`. `SET B`'s `17` is absorbed into `18`. `SET B`'s `18` and `19` become `21` and `22`.**

| Final | Invariant | From | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| `INV-M5-17` | a held request reaching auto-release pays, even after a breach | `M05:90` | unchanged |
| `INV-M5-18` | **no hold and no freeze outlives its expiry** | `M05:91` **+** `M05:101` | **merged.** The statement is `SET B`'s, being the wider; the enforcement column is the **union**, keeping `SET B`'s `payout_requests_hold_is_complete` and the hourly sweep beside `SET A`'s `M06` `OQ-M6-01` amendment and the `M02` `FM-M2-11` idiom. Nothing either row asserted is dropped |
| `INV-M5-19` | a hold consumes no ladder rung | `M05:92` | unchanged |
| `INV-M5-20` | a restriction cannot hold a held payout past its own 48 hours | `M05:93` | untouched |
| `INV-M5-21` | a held request has posted nothing | `M05:102` | renumbered from `18` |
| `INV-M5-22` | a withdrawal carrying a live freeze cannot settle | `M05:103` | renumbered from `19` |

**THE MERGED `INV-M5-18` KEEPS `SET A`'s ID AND TAKES `SET B`'s STATEMENT, AND THREE DOCUMENTS DEPEND ON THAT ASYMMETRY. This is the one line in the ruling a later session can break by accident.**

The id survived so that `M05:214`, `M20:170`, `FOLD-03:98`, `FOLD-03:133` and `GS-288` keep resolving. But **they do not cite the statement, they cite the enforcement clause**: every one of them names the on-the-query-not-the-job idiom, which was `SET A`'s half. The statement widened to `SET B`'s and the idiom lives on only because **the enforcement column is the union rather than a choice**.

| What moved | Where it came from | What depends on it |
|---|---|---|
| The **id**, `18` | `SET A` | five citations resolving at all |
| The **statement** | `SET B`, being the wider | nothing cites the statement |
| The **enforcement clause**, on the query and never on the job | `SET A`, retained in the union | **all five citations, for their actual content** |

**So narrowing that column to `SET B`'s enforcement alone would leave every citation resolving and every one of them wrong**, which is the failure this ADR exists to end reappearing one layer down: an identifier that still points somewhere, at prose that no longer says what was attributed to it. Three of the five sit in files outside the repairing session's fence and would not be re-read. **The on-the-query clause is load bearing in `INV-M5-18` and may not be dropped without re-pointing `FOLD-03:98`, `FOLD-03:133` and `GS-288` in the same commit.**

**The arithmetic is the confirmation and it is worth stating.** The table held **24 rows and 20 identifiers**. Merging `INV-M5-01`'s pair and the expiry pair gives **22 rows carrying `01` to `22`, gapless, each defined exactly once.** A ruling that left a hole or a spare would be a ruling that had miscounted the invariants.

**Why `SET A` and not `SET B`, stated as a count rather than a preference.** Six sites cite `SET A`'s three and five cite `SET B`'s. Three of `SET A`'s six sit in files outside the repairing session's fence, against one of `SET B`'s. Keeping `SET A` therefore leaves **six citations correct with no edit** and needs **three re-points plus one out-of-fence record**; keeping `SET B` would have needed **six re-points, three of them in files that could not be reached.**

**`INV-M5-01` is a plain merge under [ADR-061](ADR-061.md).** Its two rows at `M05:80` and `:81` agree: both are `ADR-040` amendments of the same zero-denial invariant, both say the mechanism moved from the absence of a state to the expiry of one, and both say the amendment is written out rather than absorbed. **`:80` is kept**, because it states "amended in both halves" explicitly and carries the forward reference to `INV-M5-17`, which this ruling leaves meaning what it meant.

**`INV-M5-20`'s number, statement and enforcement are unchanged**, which is worth stating because it sits between the two sets and a renumbering that moved it would have broken `M05:217` for no reason.

- **Alternatives considered.** **`SET B` keeps the numbers** (rejected in section 3 on the count: six re-points against three, and three of them unreachable from this fence). **Renumber both sets and leave `17`, `18`, `19` unused** (rejected: it invalidates all eleven citations to buy tidiness, and `ADR-034`'s lesson is that a number nobody can cite is worse than a number that moved). **Merge the expiry pair and renumber nothing else** (rejected: it leaves `SET B`'s `18` and `19` colliding, which is the defect). **Delete `SET B` as a duplicate of `SET A`** (rejected, and it is the one worth naming: only ONE of the three pairs is a duplicate. `SET B`'s `18` and `19` state invariants nothing else in the corpus states, and `SET B`'s `19` is enforced by a named constraint in a merged migration).

- **Consequences:**
  - **`M05` defines each `INV-M5` identifier exactly once**, and `CI-06u` loses its `M05` and `M20` exemptions. The register is the measure of this repair and it shrinks by two entries.
  - **`INV-M5-22` is cited nowhere**, before or after. It is the one invariant of the six that no document outside `M05` has ever named, and that is recorded rather than treated as a reason to doubt it: it is enforced by `wallet_withdrawals_live_freeze_blocks_settlement` in [`0031`](../../packages/db/migrations/0031_payout_hold_and_identity_restriction.sql), which is a merged migration and a stronger enforcement than a citation.
  - **`INV-M5-18`'s enforcement column may not be narrowed without re-pointing three out-of-fence documents in the same commit**, per the table in section 3. It is the only edit to this ruling that would break citations while leaving every one of them resolving.
  - **No migration, no `SD-nn` and no engine change.** Nothing in `packages/` names any of these identifiers.
  - **[M20](../plans/M20-wallet.md)'s `INV-M20-06` is merged in the same session** under [ADR-061](ADR-061.md)'s agreement rule, keeping the **enumerated** row at `:62` which carries `identities.status = 'restricted'` and the `ADR-041` shape, over the prose row at `:63`. A duplicated mermaid edge at `M20:114` is deleted with it; **no gate can see that one**, because `CI-06u` skips fenced blocks by design.
  - **`ADR-060`'s fold item 6 and session 75 were talking past each other and neither needs correcting.** `M05:88` cites `SD-09` for the partial unique index, which is session 75's finding, **and** `SD-06` for the separate claim that `R-38` is a context gate rather than a term in `engineEligible`, which is `ADR-060`'s. Both citations are in the cell today and both are right.

- **Founder approval: GRANTED 2026-08-21.** Given by the founder in session 95 as an **explicit delegated approval**, not as a line-by-line read. It amends a frozen document on the money path, so **[E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md)'s read applies and has not happened**, recorded rather than glossed. **What makes the grant defensible is that the exposure is the NUMBERING and not the behaviour**: `INV-M5-17`, `18` and `19` keep their meanings, and the ruling decides which of two colliding definitions survives rather than moving any predicate a trader can reach. The two premise corrections in sections 1 and 2 were each verified against the primary source before the ruling was chosen: `M05:215` and `M05:285` both name the fourth unsuppressible alarm, and the three unlisted citations were read in full rather than counted by grep.
