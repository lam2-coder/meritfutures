### 13. Where this entry is most likely wrong

- **"CARRIES A LIMITATION CLAUSE" IS A REGEX STANDING IN FOR A READING.** Section 4 gives two grammars and one counterexample precisely because the property is not mechanical. **A third grammar would give a third number**, and a founder who reads `CI-06/vg-inventory`'s *"a probed artifact must resolve to ABSENT, so the gate FAILS ON GOOD NEWS"* as a stated limitation gets 32 rather than 31.
