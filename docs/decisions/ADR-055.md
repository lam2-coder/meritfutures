## ADR-055: a holiday pauses the session belonging to the next trade date, and the exception carries that session's bounds  (2026-08-17, status: accepted)

**[ADR-042](ADR-042.md)'s `OQ-SE-02` is amended by section 4 of this ruling, and the exception shape [`cme-2026-2028.source.json`](../../packages/db/src/seed/calendars/cme-2026-2028.source.json) declares is widened by section 2. It is settled now because two blind readers are about to fill a schema we already know is missing a field, and a transcription checked against the wrong shape is a transcription that has to be done twice.**

- **Origin:** `FINDING 3` of [the calendar README](../../packages/db/src/seed/calendars/README.md), which recorded the defect and explicitly declined to rule it: "Deciding it amends [ADR-042](ADR-042.md)'s F-series and belongs to a plan session with the four artifacts open." This is that session, and every claim below is re-derived from the artifact bytes rather than from that README.

- **Context:** `session_rule` states that a trade date runs **17:00 CT on the prior calendar day to 16:00 CT on the trading day**. The file carries `holidays` and `early_closes` and **no third list**. `early_closes` corrects the close end. **Nothing corrects the open end.**

  The consequence is on the money path. Merit's computed session for `2026-09-08` is a strict **subset** of the real one, so a fill on the Sunday evening or Monday morning of Labor Day weekend falls inside **no Merit session at all**. `R-01` is fill containment, and a fill in no session is the condition it exists to detect. It arrives on ordinary trading, on roughly three holidays a year, at the moment volume returns.

---

### 1. What the artifacts say, re-derived from the bytes

Every cell below was parsed out of the committed `.xlsx` XML, not read off the README's table.

**The ordinary day confirms `session_rule` exactly**, which matters because it establishes that the rule is right in general and wrong in one named place. `ES` (`E-mini S&P 500 Futures`) in the all-products export:

| Calendar day | Cell |
|---|---|
| `2026-08-16` | `16:00 Trade Date: 2026-08-17 (PREOPEN)`, `17:00 Trade Date: 2026-08-17 (OPEN)` |
| `2026-08-17` | `16:00 Trade Date: 2026-08-17 (CLOSED)`, `16:45 Trade Date: 2026-08-18 (PREOPEN)`, `17:00 Trade Date: 2026-08-18 (OPEN)` |

Open at 17:00 on D-1, close at 16:00 on D. `open_day_offset: -1` is correct.

**After a mid-week holiday it is wrong at the open end, by twenty-four hours.**

| Trade date | What CME shows | What `session_rule` computes |
|---|---|---|
| `2026-09-08` | opens **Sun `09-06` 17:00**, halts Mon 12:00 to 17:00, closes Tue 16:00 | opens Mon `09-07` 17:00 |
| `2026-11-27` | opens **Wed `11-25` 17:00**, halts Thu 12:00 to 17:00, closes Fri 12:15 | opens Thu `11-26` 17:00 |

**A holiday does not remove a session. It pauses the session belonging to the next trade date, and that session opened before the holiday began.**

---

### 2. The finding is narrower than it was recorded, and the narrowing is what decides the shape

`FINDING 3` generalised from two artifacts. **The other two committed artifacts show the opposite behaviour, and they show it as a positive fact rather than as missing coverage.**

Reading the day-before column across every asset class in all four holiday exports:

| Holiday | Day | Evening open on the preceding trading day? |
|---|---|---|
| Labor Day `2026-09-07` | Mon | **yes.** Sun `09-06 17:00` opens trade date `09-08`, in all nine futures classes |
| Thanksgiving `2026-11-26` | Thu | **yes.** Wed `11-25 17:00` opens trade date `11-27`, in all nine |
| Christmas `2026-12-25` | Fri | **no.** `12-24` carries `12:15 (CLOSED)` and **nothing after it**, in all nine |
| New Year `2027-01-01` | Fri | **no.** `12-31` carries `16:00 (CLOSED)` and **nothing after it**, in all nine |

`Cryptocurrencies` is the lone exception in both Friday cases and trades its own `16:01`/`16:02` schedule. **No product in [`contract_specs`](../architecture/data-model/README.md) is in that class**, so it does not bear on Merit's calendar; it is recorded here because a later reader will see it and needs to know it was looked at.

**The absence in the Friday cases is evidence, not a gap.** The export lists evening opens in the prior calendar day's column, and it does exactly that for Thanksgiving and Labor Day in files of the identical format. An empty evening on `12-24` is the exchange saying the market does not reopen that evening.

**This is what rules out a derived open.** The natural derivation, "walk back through the run of non-trading days and open at 17:00 on the day before it", computes a Thursday `12-24 17:00` open for trade date `12-28`. **The artifact positively contradicts it.** A correct derivation would have to distinguish a holiday adjacent to a weekend from one inside the week, and would have to know that a Friday holiday simply ends the week. That is not one rule; it is at least three interacting cases, and the committed artifacts already falsify the first attempt at it.

---

### 3. The ruling: `absorbs_into`, transcribed and then cross-checked

**Every holiday entry carries one new required key**, and the founder's recommendation is adopted: the entry states the **explicit bounds of the session that absorbs it**, rather than a second list of early opens.

```json
"holidays": [
  { "date": "2026-09-07", "name": "Labor Day",
    "absorbs_into": {
      "trading_day": "2026-09-08",
      "session_open_day": "2026-09-06", "session_open_ct": "17:00",
      "session_close_day": "2026-09-08", "session_close_ct": "16:00"
    } },
  { "date": "2026-12-25", "name": "Christmas", "absorbs_into": null }
]
```

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **key absent** | `reject('absorbs-into-not-transcribed')`. Nobody has read the artifact for this holiday |
| **`null`** | This holiday absorbs no session. The next trade date opens by `session_rule` |
| **object** | These are the bounds, read off the artifact under `TR-01`, never computed |

**`null` is a positive statement and the distinction from an absent key is the whole design.** This is `F-1`'s lesson, "a holiday is a positive fact rather than an absence", applied one layer deeper into the file, and it is the same distinction the file already draws between `holidays: null` and `holidays: []`.

**The close is carried as well as the open, and carrying it is deliberate.** It is independently derivable: 16:00, or the `early_closes` entry when the absorbing day is an early close, as Thanksgiving's `11-27 12:15` is. **Two independent statements of one fact is this file's existing `declared` pattern**, whose own note says "two independent statements of one number is the point of stating it twice".

**Neither statement is authoritative alone. The agreement is the check.** The transcriber states the bounds from the exchange; `generate.mjs` derives what it expects; a disagreement is a named rejection. That answers the "a derived open is a second rule to get wrong" objection without throwing the derivation away: **the derivation stops being a source and becomes a cross-check**, which is the one role it can hold safely.

The rejections this shape makes possible, each owed a seeded violation alongside the existing eighteen:

| Finding | Condition |
|---|---|
| `absorbs-into-not-transcribed` | the key is absent |
| `absorbed-open-not-before-holiday` | `session_open_day` is not strictly before the holiday |
| `absorbed-trading-day-not-next` | `trading_day` is not the next weekday that is not itself a holiday |
| `absorbed-close-disagrees` | the stated close differs from `session_rule` / `early_closes` for that trade date |
| `absorbed-null-but-next-day-trades` | `null` while the next calendar day is a trade date, which is the Labor Day shape claiming to be the Christmas one |
| `absorbed-session-not-claimed` | a trade date follows a holiday and no holiday names it |

### 4. The intraday pause is deliberately NOT recorded

Trade date `2026-09-08` is not one contiguous interval: it halts Monday 12:00 to 17:00, expressed in the artifact as `12:00 (PREOPEN)` then `17:00 (OPEN)`. **It is not recorded, and this is a decision rather than an omission.**

- **No fill can occur inside it.** A `PREOPEN` accepts orders and matches none. A session recorded as the outer span is a **superset** of the tradeable time, and `R-01` only ever asks whether a fill fell **outside**, so a superset cannot produce a false containment failure. This is `F-3`'s reasoning exactly, which took the **latest** close across product groups because "the only thing at stake is whether a fill can fall outside every session".
- **Ordinary days already carry unrecorded pauses.** `Grains` shows `07:45 (PAUSED)` on an ordinary trading day, and `session_rule` records no intraday structure whatever. Recording a pause only on holidays would make the calendar inconsistent with itself, not more accurate.
- **A field nothing reads is the worse outcome.** [Session 49](../sessions/2026-08-17-session-49.md) recorded `sessions[].kind` as "the one place this format can silently drop a stated condition", and named the failure: "the fixture states a condition, the engine never sees it, and the scenario passes while pinning something else." A recorded-but-unenforced pause is that, on the money path.

### 5. Coverage: declared from evidence, which makes `OQ-SE-02`'s premise moot

`OQ-SE-02` rules coverage at **current year plus two** and gives as its reason a fact about the publisher: "the current year plus two is about as far as CME publishes, so it is the honest maximum rather than a chosen one." `FINDING 2` put that premise in question: every schedule table on the holiday page is **2026**, ending `1 January 2027`, with no 2027 and no 2028 table.

**This ruling does not decide how far CME publishes, and deliberately so.** That premise is legible only from a **paste**, the page carries a date picker and a `Holidays / Full Calendar` toggle a paste cannot exercise, and the calendars README already names ruling from a rendering as the mistake this desk made once this week. Ruling it here would repeat that.

**So the ruling changes the mechanism instead of the number, and the premise stops mattering.**

> **`coverage.to` may not exceed the last date whose exceptions are transcribed from a committed artifact.**

That is true whatever CME publishes. It is also what `F-4` already implies: `trading_calendar_loads` records coverage as a **fact**, and declaring coverage through 2028 while filling only 2026 makes it assert knowledge nobody has, which is precisely the state `F-4` exists to make impossible. **A horizon formula and an evidence bound cannot disagree if the evidence bound is the one that binds.**

**What can honestly be declared today: nothing, and that is the honest answer rather than a shortfall.** The five committed artifacts are three-day windows covering four holidays. **The holiday list itself has still not been retrieved**; the calendars README says so twice, and names the page. Until it lands, `coverage` stays `null` alongside `holidays` and `early_closes`, and `generate.mjs` keeps refusing the file.

**The horizon alarm gains its purpose from this.** At six months against an evidence-bounded horizon it fires when the transcribed calendar actually runs short, which is a retrieval that needs doing. Against a declared-but-empty two-year tail it would never fire at all, because coverage would claim to run to 2028 while the exception lists ended in 2026.

`OQ-SE-02` is amended on its mechanism, not on its number.

---

### 6. Consequences

- **No transcription in this ruling.** `holidays`, `early_closes`, `coverage` and `provenance` stay `null`. Two blind readers fill them under `OQ-SE-04`, and a table in a README they both read is the first reader's answer handed to the second.
- **No `generate.mjs` change and no shape edit to `cme-2026-2028.source.json` in this commit.** The shape and its six named rejections land together, in the session that writes both. Shipping a schema without its validator is the `sessions[].kind` failure this ADR's own section 4 rejects, and doing it in the same document that rejects it would be the corpus disagreeing with itself inside one file.
- **`FINDING 3` is amended in place** to carry the mid-week versus Friday split, because it currently generalises from two artifacts to a rule the other two contradict, and it is the document both blind readers are pointed at.
- **The transcriber's instructions grow one line**, alongside the existing `INCLUDES THE FOLLOWING DATES` and Columbus Day / Veterans Day warnings: for every holiday, read the **evening of the preceding trading day** and state whether a session opened there.
- **`R-01`'s containment gap closes** for the mid-week holidays, which is the money-path reason this is an ADR and not a note.

---

- **Alternatives considered.** **A second list of early opens** (rejected: it puts the correction in a different place from the fact that causes it, and leaves the two lists free to disagree about which trade date a session belongs to). **A derived open with no transcribed value** (rejected in section 2, on artifact evidence: the natural derivation is falsified by the Christmas export, and the correct one is three interacting cases). **A transcribed open with no derivation** (rejected: it discards a free cross-check, and `declared` already establishes that this file states load-bearing facts twice on purpose). **Recording the intraday pause** (rejected in section 4). **Ruling that CME publishes one year and setting coverage to 2026** (rejected in section 5: it is a fact about the publisher read off a paste, and the evidence bound makes it unnecessary to decide).

- **Founder approval: GRANTED 2026-08-17.** **This ruling corrected the review desk's own finding and that is why it was accepted quickly.** `Finding 3` was written by the desk from two of the four committed artifacts, and it does not generalise: the desk re-checked all four before signing and confirms a mid-week holiday is absorbed (Labor Day, Thanksgiving, evening open present in every futures class) while a **Friday** holiday is not (Christmas `12-24` closes `12:15` and New Year `12-31` closes `16:00`, nothing after, every futures class). **The contradicting artifacts were committed by the desk that wrote the finding and were not read against it.** The obvious walk-back derivation computes a Thursday `12-24 17:00` open for trade date `12-28` and the artifact positively contradicts it, which turns the explicit-bounds recommendation from a preference into a finding.

- **Founder approval, as written when proposed: PENDING.** This amends a frozen ADR and settles the shape of a money-path input before two blind readers fill it, so it is not taken on a session's authority ([E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md)). **Recommended for acceptance.** The three load-bearing claims were each re-derived from the artifact bytes before the recommendation was made: the ordinary-day `ES` cells confirm `session_rule`, the Labor Day and Thanksgiving exports show the absorbed open in every futures class, and the Christmas and New Year exports show an empty evening in every futures class, which is the observation that turns the recommendation from a preference into a finding.
