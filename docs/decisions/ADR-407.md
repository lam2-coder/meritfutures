### 5. THE FIFTH BLOCKER, WHICH THE DISPATCH DOES NOT NAME AND WHICH IS THE WHOLE ANSWER

`liability.ts` enumerates FIVE blockers and not four. The fifth was stated after the dispatch's list was drawn, by the sessions that lifted the others, and it is a second blocker on the same group blocker 1 held.

**`B5` IS `eligible_next_7d`'s PER-ACCOUNT HALF.** The group is a forecast of which accounts clear their payout gates on each of the next seven trading days and for how much. The horizon is produced, the calendar slice is produced, the fold is BUILT in `eligible-next-7d.ts`, and it has one input that refuses.

**TERM 1: `EligibleFoldIo.resolvePinnedPlan`, INJECTED AND REFUSING BY NAME.** `projectPayout` takes five inputs. Four are built. `plan` is a `ResolvedPlan` over the account's pinned `plan_versions.rules` blob and its `plan_version_sizes` row. **THE HALF EVERY EARLIER RECORD BLAMED IS DISCHARGED**: [ADR-239](ADR-239.md) slice A ruled the decoder's shared home, [ADR-283](ADR-283.md) landed it, `decodePlanRules` is re-exported from the engine's index, this deployable has declared `@merit/rules-engine` since session 252, and `payout-backend.ts` already calls it. **WHAT IS UNSUPPLIED IS THE COMPOSITION OF THAT DECODE WITH THE SIZE READ ON A TRANSACTION THIS FOLD HOLDS**, and `UNWIRED_ELIGIBLE_FOLD_IO` throws `EligibleFoldUnwired('resolvePinnedPlan')` rather than returning a plausible plan. **A DEFAULT `ResolvedPlan` WOULD BE THE WORST VALUE IN THAT FILE**, because every cents threshold a projection clears sits on it.

**TERM 2: THE WIRE CANNOT SAY THE FIGURE IS A FORECAST.** [ADR-204](ADR-204.md) ruling 7 requires both halves of the figure to be stated wherever it is shown. `EligibleNext7d` declares `total_cents`, `account_count` and `by_day` and NOT ONE MEASURED TERM, so there is no field a basis can ride on. The fold carries the basis on its own value instead. The shape is bound in three copies, so it moves atomically and by a ruling.

**THE GROUP GOES WHOLE OR NOT AT ALL.** `EC-074` and `P-M6-02` fund the payout wallet against this number, so producing only the accounts eligible today would understate it. Either term alone leaves the group unproducible.

### 6. SO `readLiability` IS NOT COMPOSED, AND THE REFUSAL IT ALREADY ANSWERS IS THE RIGHT ONE

The dispatch's middle branch requires that a method which cannot fill some of its own paths still answer a named, synchronous "this deployment is not finished" rather than a 500. **THAT IS ALREADY WHAT THIS TREE DOES AND NOTHING NEEDED BUILDING FOR IT.** `composeAdminReadSource` fills every unsupplied method with `AdminSourceNotComposed`, which names the method in its message and throws at the first request. This row asserts that behaviour rather than reimplementing it.

**AND THE HONEST ANSWER TO "COULD IT BE COMPOSED ANYWAY" IS NO, ON THE GAP VOCABULARY RATHER THAN ON THE TYPE.** The response can now carry `eligible_next_7d: null`, so a composition that returned the book plus a `null` would TYPE-CHECK and would pass `assertLiabilityGapsPaired`, which enforces one entry per absent figure, a non-blank `detail`, and `awaiting` non-null exactly when the cause is `awaiting_dependency`. **IT DOES NOT CONSTRAIN WHICH CAUSE A GIVEN FIELD MAY TAKE, AND THE FIELD'S OWN RULING DOES.** [ADR-203](ADR-203.md) ruling 8 and [ADR-204](ADR-204.md) ruling 9 fix this field's two causes as `insufficient_history` and `estate_uncovered`, with `awaiting` null, because the absence they rule is a HORIZON with no answer.

**NEITHER IS TRUE OF AN UNWIRED PORT.** `insufficient_history` tells an operator to wait and `estate_uncovered` tells them to load a calendar today; the actual remedy is to supply a port. Serving either would send somebody to do the wrong thing during the incident the panel exists for. Serving `awaiting_dependency` would pass every check in the tree and contradict the ruling that fixed the field's causes, in three copies. **BOTH ROUTES ARE THE WIDENING THE DISPATCH FORBIDS, so the composition is not taken and this section is why.** The choice between them is a ruling and it is section 11's first owed item.

### 7. THE DEFECT: THE COMPOSITION FILE STATES ALL FOUR SPENT BLOCKERS AS LIVE

`apps/api/src/admin-source/index.ts` is where a reader asking why `readLiability` is missing from `IMPLEMENTED_ADMIN_READS` looks first. On `43ae94a0` its answer named the four blockers of section 4, in the present tense, as the reason. **All four clauses are measured false and were false before this wave opened.** A reader following that paragraph would go and register a calendar key that is registered, look for a window definition that [ADR-201](ADR-201.md) states, invent a wire form the contract already carries, or ask for a run record `0064` already creates.

**AND THE STANDING BLOCKER WAS NOT NAMED THERE AT ALL**, which is the worse half. A stale reason can be checked. An absent one cannot, and a reader who discovered the four were spent would have found no blocker in front of an uncomposed method and would reasonably have composed it, into section 6's dishonest gap.

**THE PARAGRAPH ALSO CARRIED TWO HAND-TYPED COUNTS**, a produced count over a declared count and a count of the paths a composed method could not fill. Both are stale, and the second is stale in both of its terms. `test/admin-source-liability-book.test.ts` DERIVES declared, blocked and produced from `API_CONTRACT` on every run and asserts all three, which is the only honest statement of the position: two of the recent lifts moved the declared count without moving production, so a falling blocked count read alone reports figures arriving that did not arrive.

**`liability.ts` CARRIED THE SAME SHAPE IN FOUR MORE PLACES**, found while deriving section 4: two statements of how many tables the module reads, against an array that has grown twice since; one statement of how many leaves the book subtracts; one of how many it produces. **This is [ADR-034](ADR-034.md)'s remedy case exactly**, and its two branches are to generate the value or delete it and point at the source. The arrays and the deriving suite are the sources, so the numerals are deleted and the sources named. The file's own header already refuses a numeral beside a derivation for this reason, which is the finding: **the rule was written in that file and broken in that file.**

### 8. `RI-14` CANNOT SEE THIS AND THE REASON IS STRUCTURAL, NOT AN OVERSIGHT

`RI-14` holds that no reason claims a named thing does not exist while the tree exports it, and it was green over all four false clauses on every run for four sessions.

**IT ASKS ABOUT EXPORTS AND NONE OF THE FOUR SUBJECTS IS ONE.** A `TableKey` is a key of a registry object, not an exported binding. A ruling is a document. A migration is a file. A wire form is a union arm inside an interface. **So the invariant that exists for this defect passed over four instances of it**, and it passed for a reason no amount of care inside the fence would have changed.

**REPORTED AND NOT REPAIRED.** Widening `RI-14` is `packages/tooling/checks/repo-invariants.mjs`, which is outside this row's fence and is a control over every reason in the corpus rather than over this one file. The property is held locally instead, in `test/admin-source-liability.test.ts`, from both ends: each of the four is asserted spent AT ITS PRIMARY SOURCE, and the composition file is asserted not to restate any of them and to name the blocker that stands. **The primary-source half is what makes it a control rather than a spell-check**: it goes red if a registration, a ruling, a contract line or a migration regresses, which is the direction that matters.

### 9. WHERE THIS ROW WAS TEMPTED TO WIDEN A DEFINITION AND REFUSED

**ONE PLACE, AND IT IS SECTION 6.** Composing `readLiability` with `eligible_next_7d: null` and an `awaiting_dependency` gap naming `EligibleFoldIo.resolvePinnedPlan` would have satisfied every check in this repository, would have moved `IMPLEMENTED_ADMIN_READS`, would have unblocked the admin liability home and the worker's digests, and would have been the dispatch's own first branch delivered.

**IT IS REFUSED BECAUSE THE FIELD'S CAUSES ARE RULED IN THREE COPIES AND `awaiting_dependency` IS NOT ONE OF THEM.** The check does not enforce that pairing; the ruling does. **A gate that would not have caught it is the reason to be careful and never the licence**, and this corpus has a name for the alternative: clearing a blocker by widening its definition. The window definition the dispatch singles out is the same class of thing, and it was not invented either; [ADR-201](ADR-201.md) states it and this row read it rather than assuming it.
