## ADR-356: the operator console's one read door serves ZERO of its seven reads today and FIVE on the day the door opens, the composition the dispatch says nothing joins is joined and green in two suites, and `start.ts` does not install the port because there is no expression it could write (2026-09-06, status: proposed)

**THE DISPATCH SAID THE COMPOSITION FUNCTION EXISTS, THE SETTER EXISTS, AND NOTHING JOINS THEM. THE FIRST TWO REPRODUCE AND THE THIRD IS FALSE.** `composeAdminReadSource`, `composeImplementedAdminReads` and `setAdminReadSource` are joined, in one expression, in two suites, and the join runs end to end through the real router and answers **200**. What is missing is not a join. It is ONE VALUE: an `AdminSourceBackend` a deployment could hand in. **The join is proven and the door is not, and those are different absences with different remedies.**

**RULED: ZERO of the seven can be served on this tree, and the blocker is ONE and it is not a shape.** `AdminSourceBackend.operator` takes a `SystemTx`, `systemDb` is the only name in the accessor that yields one, and [`apps/api/src/db.ts:224`](../../apps/api/src/db.ts) closes `ApiDb` at five doors with no `operator(fn)` among them. [ADR-171](ADR-171.md) clause 1 refuses to add it and section 9 states the one condition that makes it takeable. **Every one of the seven reduces to that, and two of them carry a second blocker of their own that the door would not discharge.**

**RULED: `start.ts` does NOT install the port, and the honest reason is that it CANNOT rather than that it should not.** Every install in that file takes `LIVE_DB`, which is an `ApiDb`, and no expression over an `ApiDb` produces an `AdminSourceBackend`. **And the dispatch's harder question was still answered rather than dodged**: a partial install, were the door open, would be a WORSE failure mode and not an improvement, and section 4 measures the pair rather than arguing it.

**RULED: `composeImplementedAdminReads` is NOT dead.** It is called six times, in four suites, and it is the exact function the wiring slice calls on door day. What it is FOR is the compile-time half of a two-part defence against a merge that drops a leg, and that defence has a named prior incident behind it.

- **Origin:** [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) row `356`, branch `claude/the-operator-door-nobody-opens`, session **551**.
- **THE DELIVERABLE IS A RULING AND ONE CONTROL.** No port is installed, no door is opened, no producer is written. What lands is the ruling, a refutation of the dispatch's central premise, one stale-citation repair kept beside its original under `RI-14`, and a suite binding the arithmetic nobody had written down.
- **MONEY PATH, `E2` READ OWED.** `readLiability` is the liability dashboard and `exportEvidence` is a disclosure `AS-M6-01` names. A ruling about what an operator is told when the console cannot read is a ruling on the surface a founder looks at to decide whether the numbers are believed. Ships `proposed` with an **UNSIGNED** approval line. **Merging is not signing.**
- **NO MIGRATION WAS WRITTEN, TAKEN OR RESERVED.** `git diff --stat origin/main -- packages/db/migrations scripts/db` is **EMPTY**.
- **A DISPATCH PROMPT IS NOT A SOURCE.** Five claims were dispatched. **Three reproduce exactly, two are false as worded**, and section 7 records both with what is true in their place.

### 2. THE RULING ON HOW MANY CAN BE SERVED

**The question has TWO honest answers and collapsing them into one is the defect this entry exists to name.**

#### 2.1 Today: ZERO

Not one of the seven. **The producers are not the problem and six of them exist.** The problem is that no producer can be handed a unit of work.

`composeImplementedAdminReads` takes an `AdminSourceBackend` ([`admin-source/index.ts:217`](../../apps/api/src/admin-source/index.ts)), whose one method takes an `AdminSourceTx`, which `SystemTx` satisfies structurally. `systemDb` is the only name in the accessor that yields a `SystemTx`. And:

- [`apps/api/src/db.ts:224`](../../apps/api/src/db.ts) closes `ApiDb` at **five** members: `scoped`, `firm`, `resolution`, `establishment`, `publicLookup`. **There is no `operator(fn)` and no `system(reason, fn)`.**
- [`db.ts:79-85`](../../apps/api/src/db.ts) states the absence is the point, in its own words, and names the clearing condition: ADR-171 section 9 makes the door takeable by *"the slice that lands an `AdminSessionSource` a deployment can install"*.
- `test/db.test.ts` pins that no file but `db.ts` may take a handle off the accessor at all, so no producer can open one for itself.

**So the refusal is total and it is measured at a line rather than inferred from a missing feature.**

#### 2.2 On door day: FIVE, and the five are named

The day ADR-171 section 9 is met and a deployment can build one `AdminSourceBackend`, `composeImplementedAdminReads` returns these and nothing else ([`admin-source/index.ts:233-239`](../../apps/api/src/admin-source/index.ts)):

**`listEvents`, `listFlags`, `readAccount`, `readIdentityGraph`, `searchAccounts`.**

**FIVE. Not six, and not seven.** That figure is this entry's finding and section 3 is why nobody had written it down.

#### 2.3 The two that refuse anyway, each with its blocker at a line

| method | blocker, at source | is it the door? |
|---|---|---|
| `exportEvidence` | `EvidenceExporterDeps` names THREE ports ([`evidence.ts:756-760`](../../apps/api/src/admin-source/evidence.ts)). `evidenceReadPort` supplies one ([`evidence.ts:1787`](../../apps/api/src/admin-source/evidence.ts)). **`EvidencePackStore` ([`evidence.ts:723`](../../apps/api/src/admin-source/evidence.ts)) is object storage and nothing in this repository is** ([`evidence.ts:862-868`](../../apps/api/src/admin-source/evidence.ts)); **`EvidencePackWriter` ([`evidence.ts:748`](../../apps/api/src/admin-source/evidence.ts)) is an insert this read-only directory refuses to mint an arm for** ([`evidence.ts:870-880`](../../apps/api/src/admin-source/evidence.ts)) | **NO.** Two independent absences the door does not touch |

**Neither is this row's to clear, and neither is a founder purchase either.** They are unbuilt slices with named conditions, which is a different disposition from the door and is recorded as one.

### 4. THE RULING ON `start.ts`, AND THE PARTIAL INSTALL MEASURED RATHER THAN ARGUED

**RULED: `start.ts` does not install the port. It CANNOT, and the distinction is the whole of this section.**

Every install in [`apps/api/src/start.ts`](../../apps/api/src/start.ts) takes `LIVE_DB`. `LIVE_DB` is an `ApiDb`. `ApiDb` has no `operator`. **There is no expression `start.ts` could write**, so this is not a preference being exercised and recording it as one would misdescribe what a future session has to do to change it. Three suites already assert `start.ts` does not call the setter and none is weakened here.

#### 4.1 The dispatch's harder question, answered

The dispatch asked whether a partial install would move an operator from "nothing" to "some of it", and whether that is an improvement or a worse failure mode. **It is worse, and the pair was RUN rather than reasoned about.**

| install | `GET /admin/accounts?query=` | `GET /admin/liability` |
|---|---|---|
| **unwired** (today) | **503** | **503** |
| **half-wired** | **200** | **500** |

Both rows are read off `inject` through the real router in `admin-read-door-day.test.ts` section 2.

**THE 503 IS THE BETTER ANSWER AND ITS MESSAGE IS WHY.** `READ_SOURCE_UNWIRED` ([`admin-reads.ts:1156-1163`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-reads.ts)) tells an authenticated operator, in their own console, that *"this is a deployment which has not been finished rather than a request that failed"*, and then names the door, `systemDb('operator-console')`, and the ADR section that makes it takeable. **A 500 says none of that.**

**AND THE PARTIAL INSTALL SILENCES THAT MESSAGE FOR ALL SEVEN WHILE FIXING FIVE.** `adminHandler` reaches its refusal branch only when the source IS null ([`admin-reads.ts:1305-1309`](../../apps/api/src/routes/admin-reads.ts)), so a non-null partial source means the `request.log.error({ port: 'setAdminReadSource' }, ...)` line never runs again. **That log line is the only operator-facing statement of the blocker anywhere in the running system.** What the two unfilled methods throw in its place is `AdminSourceNotComposed`, **synchronously**, and a sweep over every `src/` file in both workspaces finds **nothing that catches it**, so it surfaces as an unhandled 500. That sweep is a case, so the day a handler maps it to a status this ruling needs re-reading.

**THE TRADE, STATED PLAINLY: a partial install exchanges a truthful, actionable 503 across all seven for a truthful 200 on five and an unactionable 500 on the exact two whose blockers a deployment cannot fix.** And it does it on the money surface: `readLiability` is the liability dashboard and `M06` section 1's success condition is *"the founder looks at it and believes it"*. **A 500 on the dashboard is indistinguishable from a defect in the numbers**, which is the confusion `AdminSourceNotComposed`'s own message was written to prevent one layer down and which a partial install re-creates one layer up.

**THE COUNTER-ARGUMENT IS RECORDED BECAUSE IT IS NOT SILLY.** Five working screens is genuinely more console than none, and an operator who never opens the liability tab never sees the 500. It is refused because the two that break are not a random five-two split: they are the two carrying the disclosure and the money figure, and the failure mode is worst exactly where the surface matters most. **If the founder wants the five, the shape that gives them without the harm is a source that refuses the unfilled two with the SAME 503 and the SAME message**, which is a change to `composeAdminReadSource`'s gap-filling and is a slice, not a wiring decision. It is named here and not taken.

### 5. WHAT `composeImplementedAdminReads` IS FOR

**It is not dead, and the census that would suggest otherwise was run at the wrong scope.**

Under every `src/` in both workspaces, comments stripped, each of the three names occurs **exactly once and that occurrence is its own declaration**. That much of the dispatch is true. **But the suites call all three**, and "every other occurrence is a comment" is false:

| name | call sites under any `src/` | call sites under `test/` |
|---|---|---|
| `composeImplementedAdminReads` | 0 | **6**, in 4 files |
| `composeAdminReadSource` | 0 | **11** |
| `setAdminReadSource` | 0 | **63** |

**AND THE JOIN THE DISPATCH SAYS DOES NOT EXIST IS WRITTEN, TWICE, AND IT IS GREEN.** [`test/admin-source-search.test.ts:753`](../../apps/api/test/admin-source-search.test.ts) and [`test/admin-source-account.test.ts:740`](../../apps/api/test/admin-source-account.test.ts) both do exactly this:

```
setAdminReadSource(composeAdminReadSource(composeImplementedAdminReads({ operator: (fn) => fn(tx) })))
```

and the first is titled *"ANSWERS 200 WHERE IT ANSWERED 500, which is the whole of this slice"*. **The wiring is not unproven. It is proven, through the real router, against a fake backend.** The only thing a deployment lacks that a suite has is a value for `operator`.

**SO WHAT THE FUNCTION IS FOR, ANSWERED:**

1. **It is the compile-time half of a two-part defence, and the other half is a different function on purpose.** [`admin-source/index.ts:5-47`](../../apps/api/src/admin-source/index.ts) records that two sessions wrote this file independently and the merge kept both: `IMPLEMENTED_ADMIN_READS` is data and `PartialAdminReadSource` is a `Pick` over it, so a merge that drops a leg **does not compile**; `composeAdminReadSource` is the run-time half and fills gaps with a refusal that names the method. **Neither subsumes the other and the header argues that explicitly.** The hazard is not hypothetical: a keep-both merge dropped a leg from `apps/worker/src/index.ts` on 2026-08-28 and `pnpm run typecheck` passed over it.
2. **It is the function door day calls.** One required parameter, an `AdminSourceBackend`. That is the whole of the wiring slice's work for five of the seven, and `admin-read-door-day.test.ts` asserts the parameter is still one, because a second required parameter would mean the door alone no longer buys the five.

**A function with no production caller and six suite callers is not dead code. It is a finished part waiting on an unfinished one**, and the difference matters because deleting it would delete the compile-time defence and the door-day path in one move.

### 8. Open questions, which are the founder's

**NO VALUE BELOW WAS INVENTED. Each is recorded as options rather than answered.**

2. **Does the SSO purchase get scheduled, or does the console wait?** **EIGHT** `BLOCKED` entries reduce to `AdminSessionSource`, and the figure is DERIVED rather than quoted: `admin-read-constructibility.test.ts:371` names them over the whole list, agreeing with [ADR-312](ADR-312.md). **The `setAdminReadSource` entry's own prose says SIX and is narrower than the derived set**, which is recorded here rather than repaired, because that entry is `wiring.test.ts`'s and row `357` is in it. That is a founder decision about money and a vendor, not a session.
3. **Which of `exportEvidence`'s two missing ports is wanted first, and where does the store live?** `evidence_packs.storage_ref` carries *"Private object storage, signed URL only. Never a public path"* in the DDL's own comment, and **no bucket, hostname or vendor is named anywhere in this corpus** ([ADR-012](ADR-012.md) forbids inventing one).
