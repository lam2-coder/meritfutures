## ADR-074: A definition site is a row or a heading inside a DECLARED register, and `CI-06/identifier-series` reads a written scope rather than a discovered one  (2026-08-20, status: proposed)

**[WAVE-04 section 1](../plans/WAVE-04-fixture-backlog-and-gate-inventory.md) says a gate cannot read a property nobody has stated. This ADR states the property.** It is ruled on [the identifier-series survey](../reviews/2026-08-21-identifier-series-survey.md), which measured all 215 series under `docs/` before any of this was drafted, and every number below is that survey's.

- **Context:** `CI-06/identifier-series` was briefed as cheap and slug-shaped. **It is the widest-scoped gate this repository has proposed**, and three findings from the survey are why.

  **One. There is no such thing as "the" definition site, because four shapes are in live use.** A filename (`ADR-070.md`), a table row (`R-nn` in M01), a heading (`### AS-M14-01: ...`), and a bold lead (`**OQ-M5-01 (RULED, ...)**`). Taking the union of all four and asserting one site per identifier fails on **364 members**, and most of those failures are correct: every `EC-nnn` and every `ADR-nnn` has both a document and a register row, and [`CI-06n`](../testing/STRATEGY.md) requires exactly that pairing in both directions. **A rule that cannot tell a register row from the document it points at calls the corpus's own discipline a violation.**

  **Two. Taking the narrow shape instead fails in the other direction**, on **349 members** across the 74 series that have no first-cell row anywhere. Those series are not undisciplined: `AS-*` is defined by heading, consistently, in the module plan that owns it.

  **Three. The scope cannot come from the pattern.** The census matches `SHA-256`, the external citation `NFA I-26-12`, and `PRE-ADR-014`, which is prose meaning "before ADR-014". It also cannot see `D0-1`, because its digit floor is two. **A gate whose scope is a regular expression is asserting that a hash algorithm needs an allocation table, and is silent about a series whose members are single-digit.**

  **The `SD` case is the one that decides the search space, and it was run rather than reasoned.** [ADR-026](ADR-026.md) requires a [`DELTA_MANIFEST`](../../packages/db/DELTA_MANIFEST.md) row for every `SD-` identifier appearing under `docs/`. An unallocated `SD` number was appended to a document, `ADR-026` failed with *"cited in `docs/` but has no DELTA_MANIFEST row"*, and the file was reverted. **So an `SD` allocation cannot live in a document: writing the number to reserve it creates the obligation the reservation cannot discharge.** The survey cannot even quote its own probe token without failing the gate it is describing.

---

### 1. What a definition site is

> **A definition site is a table row whose first cell LEADS with the identifier, or a markdown heading whose text LEADS with the identifier, occurring inside the series' DECLARED REGISTER.**
>
> **Every member of an in-scope series has exactly one. Every other occurrence anywhere in the repository is a citation and is unconstrained.**

Four things in that sentence are load-bearing and each is a measurement rather than a preference.

**"Leads with", not "equals".** [STATE](../STATE.md) writes its finding rows as `| **`OI-06`. The 48 hour payout-destination cooling window has no storage.** | ...`: identifier, full stop, then the finding, all in the first cell. **An equality rule reports the six rows that exist as six rows that do not**, which is the direction that gets a gate switched off. Stripping is [`CI-06u`](../testing/STRATEGY.md)'s `firstCellKey` and not a second copy of it.

**A row OR a heading, and NOT a bold lead.** The heading shape carries 42 series that have no rows at all and is unambiguous. The bold lead is rejected: **a bold span opening a line is this corpus's ordinary emphasis idiom**, present thousands of times, and admitting it makes the rule match prose. A series defined only by bold lead is therefore not in scope, and its remedy is stated in section 5 rather than left implicit.

**"Inside the declared register", which is the whole ruling.** A first cell is not always an identity. `ADR-014` has four rows leading with it: its register row, and three rows in [ADR-052](ADR-052.md) and [ADR-057](ADR-057.md) whose first column is **the source being quoted**. That is `CI-06u`'s dimension problem in a new place, and scoping the search to a named register removes the class instead of enumerating it.

**A register is a file, or a file and one `##` section.** Section granularity is not a convenience: it is what separates `INV`'s definition table from M01's coverage table, and it moves six series from failing to clean. **It is [ADR-043](ADR-043.md)'s own reading** -- a registry may be split, and a row is not a document.

**A register must be a corpus document or a package README.** A [review record](../reviews/2026-08-21-identifier-series-survey.md) is expressly outside the corpus and binds nothing by existing, so it cannot be the thing an identifier is defined by. `OQ-P` is the one series this excludes today.

**The search space is the repository, not `docs/`.** Registers already live under `packages/`: `L-nn`'s is the fixtures README, `RI-nn`'s is the tooling README, and `OI-nn`'s is `DELTA_MANIFEST`. **A gate that looked only under `docs/` would report three series as unregistered and would be wrong about all three.**

### 2. The scope is WRITTEN, never discovered

> **`CI-06/identifier-series` reads a hardcoded list of declared series and their registers. It must NOT compute its scope from the tree.**

**This is the assertion the gate is worth nothing without.** A gate whose scope is "every series that currently satisfies the rule" passes forever by construction: the day a series breaks, it leaves scope, and the gate reports 118 of 118. **A control that cannot fail is [STRATEGY section 4](../testing/STRATEGY.md)'s advisory gate wearing a blocking gate's name.**

The declaration is a table in [`gates.mjs`](../../scripts/corpus/gates.mjs), not in a document, for [ADR-065](ADR-065.md)'s reason and `CI-06w`'s: **the registration and the implementation are then the same line and cannot drift apart.** It is the idiom `CI-06u` already uses for its dimension headers -- a closed list, one argued entry each, and an entry matching nothing is itself a finding.

### 3. In scope on arrival: 118 series, 1,086 members

**Enumerated in [section 3 of the survey](../reviews/2026-08-21-identifier-series-survey.md), which is where the list lives rather than here**, because a 118-row table copied into an ADR is a second copy of a registry. The count is stated so `W8` can check that its transcription reproduced it.

| Shape of the register | Series | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **The module plan that owns the series** | 96 | `INV-M*`, `AS-M*`, `FM-M*`, `DEP-M*` and each module's own specials, in `M02` through `M21` |
| **M01, four series in three sections** | 5 | `INV`, `CV`, `RE-P`, `AS`, `FM` |
| **A testing or architecture document** | 10 | `C` in SECURITY, `PT`, `TR`, `VG` in STRATEGY, `RE-S` and `PP` in SIMULATION_HARNESS, `S`, `D`, `RB`, `FOLD` |
| **A design or fold document** | 7 | `DG`, `SS` in DESIGN_SYSTEM; `OQ-F3` to `OQ-F6`, `OQ-SE`, `OQ-P2`, `OQ-FREEZE` in their own plans |

**Seven `OQ-*` sub-series are in scope and twenty are not, and the split is a finding rather than an inconsistency.** The **fold and phase** plans register their open questions in the document that raises them. The **module** plans do not. Same prefix, two conventions, and only one of them is checkable.

### 4. What is exempt, and the reason for each class

**Four classes, each with an argument. A list without one is what this section refuses to be.**

| Class | What it holds | Why it is exempt, not pending |
|---|---|---|
| **X1. Not a Merit identifier** | `SHA`, `I-26`, `PRE-ADR`, and the `I` row this survey's own table created | The census pattern matches any capitalised hyphenated token. **A hash algorithm and an external regulator's interpretive notice are not series**, and a gate demanding registers for them asserts something false about the corpus rather than finding something true |
| **X2. Already asserted, and harder, by a purpose-built gate** | `ADR`, `GS`, `EC`, `SD` and its module-scoped forms, the unnumbered delta series, migration numbers, and the `CI` stage rows | Each existing gate asserts **strictly more** than one definition site: gaplessness over allocated plus reserved, a disposition from a closed vocabulary, an ownership partition covering every scenario exactly once, a heading status agreeing with its own body. **A second gate over a property a purpose-built gate already checks is the second copy of a rule [ADR-034](ADR-034.md) exists to end**, and here it would be the weaker copy. `CI` is in this class because `CI-06/gate-inventory` is the gate that reads those rows |
| **X3. A name minted once, in place** | 22 prefixes with one member and one or two mentions: `M4-F`, `M2-I`, `M9-K`, and nineteen more | **A register with one row is not a register.** `M4-F-01` is defined by the table row it sits in -- in the second cell, under a first cell reading "Appendix F slop score" -- and that definition is real to every reader and unreachable to every parser. **Demanding 22 one-row registries buys a gate nothing and costs the corpus 22 tables** |
| **X4. `SD`, restated because it is the case that shaped the rule** | `SD-nn` and `SD-M*-nn` | It is in X2 already. It is named separately because **its register is not under `docs/` and cannot be**, and because its reservation mechanism is the opposite of what this wave assumed: **the register carries the `reserved` row and the plan document cites it**, never the reverse. Seven manifest rows carry that disposition today |

### 5. What is pending, and a pending register that cannot become furniture

**44 series are neither in scope nor exempt, and calling them exempt would be the dishonest move this section exists to avoid.** They are **pending**: a real defect, named, with the artifact it waits for.

| Pending class | Series | Members | The artifact it waits for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Has a register with holes or doubles** | 18 | 190 | A repair to the register. `R`'s 13 doubles and `ST`'s 7 are a coverage table sharing a section with a definition table; `RE-U`, `L`, `RI`, `HO`, `M6-N`, `INV-M6` and `PW` are members with no row; `OI` is section 7 |
| **`OQ-M*`, no register at all** | 20 | ~100 | A ruling naming which end of an open question's lifecycle owns it: the module plan that raises it, or the gate-closure document that disposes of it. **Not this ADR's to make**, because it is a question about how rulings are recorded and not about identifiers |
| **No register, small** | 6 | 29 | `RE-C`, `RE-R`, `SF-M21`, `DT`, `PG-M9`, `OQ-P`. Each needs a table in the document that owns it, or an argued move into X3 |

**The pending list is a register in `gates.mjs` with `CI06U_REGISTER`'s defining property**: *a register entry that no longer names a real defect is a finding.* **So it shrinks as repairs land and cannot become furniture**, and its size is **printed on every run** rather than stated in prose, because a repair moves the number and this document does not.

**Undeclared series -- anything in neither list -- are claimed as nothing and counted on every run.** That count is the honest measure of what the gate does not cover, and printing it is what stops "not in the table" from becoming a hole nobody can see.

### 6. Does the gate pass the tree as it stands

> **YES. On arrival, `CI-06/identifier-series` passes: 118 series, 1,086 members, zero findings.** [`falsify.mjs`](../../scripts/corpus/falsify.mjs) makes a gate that cannot pass an ERROR, and this one is not that.

**`W8` must not have to decide this at implementation time, so the argument is given here in full, including the part that is uncomfortable.**

**The two options WAVE-04 named were "narrow the scope" or "arrive with a registered exemption set". The ruling takes both, and the distinction between them is what makes it defensible rather than convenient.**

**What would have been narrowing to pass, and is refused.** Dropping a series **because it fails** is weakening a gate to pass it, which [the constitution](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) forbids in the same words the working agreements use. **Not one of the 44 pending series is exempt.** Each is named, counted, and carries the artifact it waits for, and the register that holds them fails the day one of them is quietly repaired without being promoted.

**What is not narrowing, and is what section 4 does.** The four exempt classes are excluded **by argument about what they are**, not by whether they pass. X1 is not identifiers. X2 would be a second copy of an existing rule. X3 is a name that exists once. **Every one of those exclusions would stand even if the series passed**, which is the test for whether an exemption is a reason or an excuse.

**Why an all-series gate was rejected outright.** It would report **349 findings** under the narrow rule or **364** under the broad one, on arrival, most of them false. `CI-06a` once reported 109 phantom anchors and the corpus was right and the gate was wrong; **a gate that arrives with hundreds of false findings is a gate somebody switches off**, and `CI-06q` is already declared-and-not-written in this repository for exactly that reason at a scale of 28.

**The one thing this ruling cannot claim.** A gate over 1,086 members that passes on the day it is written has proven nothing yet. **Its value is entirely in what it refuses tomorrow**, and the two mechanisms that make that real are section 2's written scope, so a breaking series fails rather than leaving, and section 5's pending register, so a repair cannot land without the series being promoted into scope.

### 7. Whether `OI` gets an allocation table

**`OI` already has one, and correcting that premise is the whole of this section.** The brief states `OI` has no registry table at all. It has [`DELTA_MANIFEST`](../../packages/db/DELTA_MANIFEST.md) **section 16**, headed *"Allocation: `OI-nn` identifiers and section numbers"*, written under [ADR-034](ADR-034.md) after two collisions in one day, and **deliberately** placed there rather than in [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) on a stated argument: *"`OI-nn` and the section numbers are this document's own namespace."*

> **The ruling: that premise has expired, and `OI`'s allocation moves to [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) as a fourth table, superseding section 16.**

**The argument is the manifest's own.** `OI-16` onward were opened by sessions about engine purity, gate coverage, lint scope and the fixture inventory. **None of that is the manifest's namespace**, so the reasoning that put the table in `packages/db/` is the same reasoning that keeps ten of the twenty-five members out of it -- `OI-16` to `OI-23`, and `OI-25` and `OI-26`, which WAVE-04 allocates in a plan document and says so about itself. **A register whose declared subject excludes 40 percent of its series is not that series' register**, and filing those ten into a schema-delta manifest to satisfy a gate would be filing a finding by where the check can see it.

**ALLOCATION is where it belongs and the reason is mechanical.** Its three tables are in one document *"because each is read AS A TABLE: `CI-06f`, `CI-06h` and `CI-06p` call one shared parser over the first cell of every row."* **A fourth table there is read by that parser on the day it lands.**

**`OI-06` is allocated twice and the manifest records it against itself** -- *"CLAIMED TWICE, 2026-08-16, and left that way"* -- and the repair is not ruled here. **It is a disposition question about two live findings and it belongs to the session that moves the table**, with the manifest in its fence.

**The move is NOT in this session's fence and the gate does not wait on it.** `OI` is a pending entry naming the artifact: an `OI` allocation table in `ALLOCATION.md`. **The day that table lands, the pending entry stops naming a real defect and the gate fails until `OI` is promoted into scope in the same commit** -- which is [WAVE-03](../plans/WAVE-03-duplicate-registry-keys.md)'s rule that a register entry goes with its repair, in the same commit.

---

- **Alternatives considered:**

  - **One shape for every series, whichever it is.** Rejected by measurement: the union of four shapes double-counts 364 members and the narrow shape misses 349, and both sets are large enough that the gate's first run would be an argument about the gate.
  - **Derive the scope from the tree at run time.** Rejected in section 2. It is the version of this gate that can never fail.
  - **Put the series declaration in a document rather than in the runner.** Rejected on `CI-06w`'s precedent: read out of the runner, the registration and the implementation are one line and cannot drift.
  - **Exempt `OI` and ship the rest.** Rejected. `OI` is the series that commissioned the gate; exempting it makes the gate pass by omitting its subject, which is the move [WAVE-04 section 3](../plans/WAVE-04-fixture-backlog-and-gate-inventory.md) rejects for `CI-07` in its own words.
  - **Widen `DELTA_MANIFEST` section 16's declared scope instead of moving the table.** Rejected in section 7: it keeps findings about lint scope and gate coverage inside a schema-delta manifest, which is filing by where the check can see it.
  - **Rule the `OQ-M*` convention here and take the twenty series with it.** Rejected as out of scope. Which end of an open question's lifecycle owns its identifier is a question about how rulings are recorded, and answering it inside an identifier ADR would be deciding it by side effect.

- **Consequences:**

  - **`W8` implements one gate with two hardcoded tables and no derivation**: the declared scope, and the pending register whose entries fail when they stop naming a defect. Both counts print on every run.
  - **Nothing in the corpus is repaired by this ADR.** 44 series stay broken and are now counted rather than unknown, which is the same trade `ADR-072` is reserved to make for the fixture backlog.
  - **`ALLOCATION.md` gains a fourth table and `DELTA_MANIFEST` section 16 is superseded**, in a session that holds both in its fence. **`OI-06`'s double allocation is that session's to dispose of.** Neither is this session's, and neither blocks the gate.
  - **`docs/reviews/` can never be a register.** The one series this costs today is `OQ-P`, which becomes pending.
  - **No migration, no engine behaviour, no schema, and no change to any gate that runs today.** The 24 gates that pass at this commit are untouched.

- **Founder approval: PENDING.** This sets the scope of a blocking gate over 1,086 identifiers and rules that a registry moves between two documents. It is not taken on a session's authority ([E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md)). **Recommended for acceptance.**
