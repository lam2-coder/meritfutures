## ADR-071: `M21`, the Plan Designer and Simulation Console, admitted as a new module after FREEZE  (2026-08-20, status: proposed)

**[STATE](../STATE.md) says "a behavior not in the corpus is not in scope", so a twenty-first module is a ruling and not a plan.** This ADR is that ruling. It admits the module, states the intent it is admitted for, and **names the dependency it cannot be built without** rather than assuming it exists.

- **Context:** FREEZE's operative sentence cuts both ways and this ADR takes the harder direction of it. **A behavior in the corpus is a commitment; a behavior not in it is not in scope.** Twenty module plans were frozen on 2026-08-14 and every one of them was written before FREEZE. **`M21` is the first module proposed after it**, which makes the question not "is this a good feature" but "what is the standard for adding one at all".

  **The standard this ADR applies is that a new module must justify itself by what it changes about how the business is run, not by what it lets a user do.** A feature request does not clear FREEZE. A change in the loop by which the firm's core economics are decided might.

---

### 1. The intent, which is the ruling's whole content

> **Plan and pricing design becomes an in-product experiment rather than a code round trip.**

**Today every plan change is a migration, a deploy, and a founder reading a spreadsheet.** That is three artifacts, two of them irreversible in the sense [E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) means -- a merged migration is never edited, only superseded -- and one of them, the spreadsheet, is outside the corpus entirely and leaves no record that any gate can read.

**The last clause is the one that carries the ruling.** The parameters that decide Merit's liability, its margin and its pass rates are currently chosen in a document nothing versions, nothing validates and nothing can reconstruct after the fact. **The plan version records what was chosen; nothing records what it was chosen against.** `M21` is admitted because it moves that decision inside the system that already versions everything else, and **not** because a form is more convenient than a migration.

**What is being bought is a shorter loop on the numbers that price the product.** `plan_versions.rules` is a `jsonb` blob precisely so a plan shape can change without a migration (`ADR-070` section 1 rules that it stays one; **it is deliberately NOT linked here, because it lands on a sibling branch and `CI-06a` fails on a link to a file this ref does not carry**), and that flexibility is worth nothing while the only way to exercise it is a deploy.

### 2. `M21` is admitted, with six requirements it is admitted subject to

**The module is admitted. Its scope is these six and nothing wider**, because "a plan designer" is the kind of name that grows a module by implication.

| | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **(a)** | **A parameter form over every `plan_version` field**, with the existing publish-time checks rendered **live**. `PW-01` to `PW-04`'s severity model is **displayed as it will fire, never re-implemented** |
| **(b)** | **Simulation preview on demand**: eval pass rate, funded-to-payout rate, average payouts per payer, liability per funded account, contribution and margin at the entered price, per-day extraction ceiling, lifetime liability bound. **The calibration source and sample size are shown on the result, always** |
| **(c)** | **Comparison**, a draft against any published version and against modelled competitor configs |
| **(d)** | **Sensitivity**, sweeping one parameter and charting pass rate, liability and margin, **so the binding constraint is visible** |
| **(e)** | **Draft, review, publish**, with dual control on publish per [ADR-010](ADR-010.md), a **diff against current shown before publish**, and versioning semantics preserved: a new version applies to **new sales only** |
| **(f)** | **Never mutates live accounts.** Simulation is read-only compute; publishing creates a version and touches no existing account |

**(a) says "displayed, never re-implemented" because that is the failure this module would otherwise introduce.** A console that re-implements the publish-time severity model is a second copy of a rule set, and a second copy is the drift [ADR-034](ADR-034.md) exists to end. **`M21` renders what `validate.ts` returns.**

**(f) is what keeps this module off the money path.** A designer that could touch a live account would be a plan-parameter mutation reaching accounts that were sold under a different version, which is the retroactive-change hole the immutable published version exists to close.

### 3. The harness, stated as a dependency because it does not exist

**This is the part of the referral that was wrong, and it is stated plainly rather than inherited.**

The referral spoke of "the ported Monte Carlo harness". **There is no Monte Carlo harness in this repository to port.** [`packages/rithmic/src/simulator/session.ts`](../../packages/rithmic/src/simulator/session.ts) opens, in its own words:

> `THE DAY MODEL. One account, one session, in integer cents and integer ticks.`

and it exists for a stated reason that is not economics: *"`daily_marks` needs `high_balance_cents` and `low_balance_cents`, and the low is THE BREACH COMPARISON INPUT."* **It produces equity paths to test the bridge and the engine. It produces no pass rate, no liability figure and no margin.**

**The news is much better than "it does not exist", and the difference is what makes this dependency small.** A day model that produces **breach-comparison-correct** equity paths is exactly the inner loop a Monte Carlo harness needs, and [`rng.ts`](../../packages/rithmic/src/simulator/rng.ts) states that *"DETERMINISM IS THE WHOLE VALUE OF THIS PACKAGE, so the randomness is keyed"*. **Keyed determinism is precisely what a reproducible trial loop requires** -- a harness whose runs cannot be reproduced cannot support `AS-M21-01`'s remedy at all.

> **So the harness is a TRIAL LOOP and an AGGREGATOR over an inner model that already exists and is already deterministic.** That is a far smaller thing than a harness from nothing, and it is a different thing from a port.

**It is named as a dependency with its own delivery**, in the shape [M07](../plans/M07-risk-abuse.md)'s `DEP-M7-nn` rows take:

| | Dependency | On | What is lost without it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DEP-M21-01** | A Monte Carlo harness: a trial loop and aggregator over `packages/rithmic/src/simulator`'s day model | **Its own session, `FOLD-05` P7** | **Requirements (b) and (d) do not exist.** The parameter form, the comparison and the publish workflow still stand, so the module degrades to a versioned editor rather than failing |

**`M21`'s plan must carry that row rather than assuming the harness**, and the degradation column is the useful half: it says what ships if the harness slips, which is the question a delivery decision actually turns on.

### 4. `AS-M21-01` has a structural answer, and it is the argument for a table

**Four adversarial scenarios are named. One of them decides a schema question.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **AS-M21-01** | A published config whose simulation ran against **stale calibration**. The numbers looked right, the calibration was months old, the plan is live |
| **AS-M21-02** | A sensitivity sweep at a sample size too small to separate the arms, read as a signal |
| **AS-M21-03** | A draft published without the diff being read, changing a parameter nobody intended to touch |
| **AS-M21-04** | Simulation compute heavy enough to affect the production database it reads from |

**`AS-M21-01` is the only one with a structural answer rather than a procedural one**, and the distinction is why it is called out. `AS-M21-02` and `AS-M21-03` are answered by requirements (b) and (e) -- showing the sample size, showing the diff -- and both remain defeatable by a reader who does not look. **`AS-M21-01` is not defeatable by attention**, because the staleness is invisible at the moment of the decision: the numbers are as plausible as fresh ones.

> **RULED: the calibration identity and its sample size are bound into the simulation result, and carried onto the publish record.** A published version can always be traced to what its decision was based on.

**That is the argument for `0045_simulation_runs.sql`, and it is made here rather than reserved on instinct.** **A console that stores nothing cannot tell you what a decision was based on**, and the whole reason for moving this decision inside the system was that the spreadsheet left no record. A designer that reproduces the spreadsheet's amnesia in a nicer interface would be admitting a module for no gain.

**The migration is CONTINGENT and stays that way here.** `0045` is reserved in [FOLD-05](../plans/FOLD-05-plan-config-and-designer.md) and **this ADR does not write it**: whether a run is a row, an artifact, or a hash on the publish record is a schema question, it is money-adjacent because it lands beside a published plan version, and it belongs to its own session under [ADR-003](ADR-003.md).

### 5. What this ADR does not do

**It does not write the `M21` plan document.** That is `FOLD-05` P4, and a module admitted by an ADR whose scope is set by that same ADR is exactly the arrangement that keeps a post-FREEZE module from growing by implication.

**It does not decide the console's surface, its authorization, or its place in the admin parity work.** Dual control on publish is required by (e) and cites [ADR-010](ADR-010.md); how it is enforced is the plan's.

**And it does not admit a second module by implication.** The harness in section 3 is a dependency of `M21`, delivered in `packages/`, not a module of its own.

- **Alternatives considered.**

  - **Refuse the module and keep plan design as a code round trip.** Rejected on the intent in section 1: it is not the convenience that is being bought, it is a record of what each priced decision was based on, and today that record is a spreadsheet outside the corpus.
  - **Admit it as a feature of [M06](../plans/M06-admin-ops-console.md)** rather than as a module. Rejected: M06 is frozen, so it is an amendment either way, and the six requirements include a compute dependency and a contingent migration. **A module admitted honestly is easier to bound than a feature that grows inside a frozen plan.**
  - **Assume the "ported Monte Carlo harness" exists**, as the referral's phrasing invites. Rejected in section 3, and it is the alternative most likely to have been taken silently: it would have put requirements (b) and (d) in a plan with no delivery behind them.
  - **Build the harness first and rule afterwards.** Rejected: it inverts the constitution's own order, and `DEP-M21-01`'s degradation column already lets the module ship without it.
  - **Bind calibration to the run and NOT to the publish record.** Rejected in section 4: the run is where the number is produced and the publish record is where the consequence lands. Tracing has to reach the second.

- **Consequences:**

  - **The corpus gains a twenty-first module and the ownership index gains a partition.** `GS-312` to `GS-316` are in the registry against `M21`; none is written.
  - **`FOLD-05` P4 is unblocked and P7 is not scheduled by this ADR.** The plan document may be written on this ruling; the harness has its own session and its own delivery.
  - **`0045` stays contingent.** Section 4 makes the argument for it; the schema decision is a separate session.
  - **`ADR-070` is what makes this module possible and the two are independent rulings**, and it is unlinked for the reason section 1 gives. ADR-070 section 1 keeps `plan_versions.rules` an unconstrained blob so a plan shape can change without a migration. **If that ruling goes the other way, `M21`'s experiment loop needs a migration per shape and the intent in section 1 does not survive.** Neither ADR depends on the other's approval, and this dependency is stated so the two are not read in isolation.
  - **Nothing in P1 or P2 changes.** No migration, no engine behaviour, no schema, no gate that runs today.

- **Founder approval: PENDING.** This admits the first new module after FREEZE and sets the standard by which the next one will be argued. It is not taken on a session's authority ([E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md)). **Recommended for acceptance.**
