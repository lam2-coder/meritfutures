### 3. How the survey was derived, so a later row can reproduce it rather than rebuild it

**THE INSTRUMENT IS SHIPPED AND THE RECIPE IS NOT, AND THAT IS THE ONE METHODOLOGICAL DECISION IN THIS ROW.** Three entries have now derived a figure over this class with a line-oriented search written inside a session and thrown away with it, and all three figures were low. `covers-census.mjs` is the precedent for keeping the scaffolding: its own header records two rows rebuilding one instrument to check each other's number, and *"a third would have rebuilt it again."*

**THE SENTENCE IS THE UNIT AND THE LINE IS NOT, AND THAT IS WHY THE SIBLING COUNTS ARE LOW.** A claim in this tree is written inside a wrapped block comment, so its subject lands on one line and its verb on the next, and `git grep` sees neither half. The instrument joins a contiguous comment run into one text and matches within a sentence. Two further reader decisions are load-bearing and both were forced by real misses:

- **A FULL STOP INSIDE `` `schema.ts` `` DOES NOT END A SENTENCE.** A naive splitter cuts *"nothing in this tree compares a `schema."* from *"ts` column type against the DDL"*, which severs almost every claim in this corpus at its own subject, because this corpus writes a filename inside prose constantly. The terminator has to be followed by whitespace or the end of the text.
- **A QUOTATION OF A CLAIM IS NOT A CLAIM.** A repair on `RI-14`'s idiom keeps the false sentence beside its correction, so a repaired file still matches. The instrument marks those `framed` and reports them as their own bucket rather than deducting them, so a frame it does not know cannot silently shrink the population.

**IT REPORTS TWO GRAMMARS AND NEVER ONE NUMBER**, which is `covers-census.mjs`'s discipline taken verbatim, for the same reason: *"carries a limitation clause"* and *"says nothing checks something"* are both judgements about English, and one figure hides the judgement.

| Grammar | Subject | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **NARROW** | `no check`, `no gate`, `no test`, `no invariant`, `no suite`, `no runner`, `no probe`, `no leg`, `no linter`, `no assertion`, `no reader`, with a verification or observation verb within 12 words | **A FLOOR.** Those nouns name nothing else in this corpus |
| **WIDE** | NARROW plus `nothing`, `nobody`, `no one`, plus `unchecked`, `uncompared`, `unenforced` and their siblings | **A CEILING.** It reaches the claims NARROW cannot see, and also a large body of sentences about the DOMAIN |

**`rule` AND `case` ARE DELIBERATELY OUTSIDE NARROW AND THE SUITE ASSERTS IT.** This corpus calls a business rule a rule and a golden fixture a case, so *"an unbroken holiday: no rule fires"* and *"the `folded` arm, so no case reads through a union"* would both enter the floor. **A floor that is not a floor is worse than a wider ceiling**, and admitting either noun raises the NARROW source figure by more than half with nothing in the class.

**REPRODUCTION IS ONE COMMAND:** `node packages/tooling/checks/absence-grammar-census.mjs`, and `--list` names every site. **The figures below are in this entry and not in that file**, on [ADR-383](ADR-383.md) section 8 and [ADR-386](ADR-386.md) section 7's ruling that a live cardinal in prose is a claim that decays at the rate the tree moves. This is a dated record; that file is not.

### 9. The shape decision, and the argument against it

**NEITHER, FOR THE CLASS.** Section 4's population is 430 to 600 live source sentences and 1,292 live document sentences unclassified; section 7's claims share a grammar and not a probe. **A control over the class would be a shape rather than a check**, on [ADR-429](ADR-429.md) section 10's own words, which this row read rather than inherited: *"a ninth instance of it bought under time pressure is worse than the hole reported plainly."* A grammar-keyed gate is satisfiable by REWORDING, which converts an honest sentence into a liability and teaches the next author to write the claim in a shape the scanner does not carry. **That is worse than the tree it would police.**

**NOT A FAMILY EITHER, AND THIS IS THE ANSWER THE DISPATCH MADE ROOM FOR AND THE SURVEY CLOSED.** "Build the first member and name the rest" requires a first member that can land. Section 8 measured that the best-specified candidate in the corpus, on the claim three entries chose as the most tractable, **lands 16 findings red**. **A family whose first member cannot be built is a family named and not begun**, and naming it is section 15.

**AND NOT ONE ARTIFACT, WHICH NEEDS NO ARGUMENT ONCE THE POPULATION IS DERIVED.**

**THE ARGUMENT AGAINST, STATED SO THE ROW THAT DISAGREES ARGUES WITH SOMETHING.** [ADR-440](ADR-440.md) section 9.2's defence of the register is real and this row did not refute it: *"a stale row degrades to a MISSED site rather than to a false green"*, because the probe answers from the tree. **That property survives everything above.** The honest counter is narrower than a refusal: a register of four or five hand-written probes over four or five claims is a **fine thing to own** and it is not a control on the class, and the only failure available to it is being SOLD as one. **A row that wants it should build it and describe it as what it is: four claims, watched.** What this row refuses is the inference from three instances to a control, and the reason is that the fourth instance (section 6) was found by reading, in the repair of the third, by a row commissioned to survey rather than to look.

**AND THE CHEAPER THING THAT WOULD HAVE CAUGHT MORE THAN ANY OF IT.** Thirteen of the sixteen sites in section 8 are a sentence about ONE fact, copied. [ADR-440](ADR-440.md) section 9.1's counterfactual is that a register would have stopped the copying. **So would a convention that a claim about the suite is written ONCE and cited**, which is this corpus's own `RI-14` and `RI-15` grammar applied to a claim instead of to a pointer, costs no runner, and is section 15 item 4 rather than this row's to take.
