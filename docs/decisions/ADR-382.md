### 3. The expiry constructor, settled rather than declined again

**THE DISPATCH ASKED WHETHER THE NAMED HAZARD CAN BE ANSWERED. IT CANNOT, AND WHAT WOULD ANSWER IT IS NAMED AND ABSENT.**

The hazard [ADR-378](ADR-378.md) section 3 states is that a default sink makes an otherwise-unbuildable job buildable over a value that rejects. **THAT IS TRUE AND THIS ROW MEASURED IT SHARPER IN TWO PLACES.**

**FIRST, THE REFUSAL IS NOT ANNOUNCED BY THE RUN.** Each leg of [`sweeps/expiry.ts`](../../apps/worker/src/sweeps/expiry.ts) wraps its transaction in its own `catch` and converts the error into a per-row `disposition: 'failed'` with a detail (`:683`, `:756`, `:899`). **So a sweep over a rejecting sink does not throw. It completes, returns a report and exits zero.** A supervisor sees a healthy hourly job, the dead-man switch fires on the job's ABSENCE and the job is present, and every held payout stays held. That is `ADR-239`'s defect with a clock in front of it, which [`expiry-adapter.ts:51`](../../apps/worker/src/sweeps/expiry-adapter.ts) already names, plus the reason the report does not carry the failure out.

**SECOND, THE ONE CONTROL THAT WOULD SEE IT READS THE ESTATE RATHER THAN THE JOB, AND IT IS NOT BUILT.** [`CRON_INVENTORY`](../ops/runbooks/CRON_INVENTORY.md) line 38 is the nightly **hold and freeze expiry assertion**, **S1 and unsuppressible**, and it asserts *no row sits past its own expiry, evaluated independently of whether the sweep above reported success*. **It carries no `BUILT` marker**, on a page where ten rows do, and no module under `apps/worker/src` reads `hold_expires_at` or `freeze_expires_at` except the sweep itself and `db.ts`. **A sweep that releases nothing is exactly what that assertion exists to catch, and it does not exist.**

**AND THE DISTINCTION FROM THE TWO DOORS DOWN THAT DID TAKE THIS EDIT IS WHAT THE JOB WITHHOLDS, NOT WHAT THE SINK LOOKS LIKE.** [`breaker/adapter.ts:333`](../../apps/worker/src/breaker/adapter.ts) and [`detectors/adapter.ts:334`](../../apps/worker/src/detectors/adapter.ts) both default their sink to a refusal and say so in their own headers. **A detector run whose sink refuses comes back `unrecorded` and produces nothing; nothing is withheld from a trader.** A breaker evaluation declines earlier still, on `OQ-M6-02`'s unstated `minSample`. **An expiry sweep whose sink refuses converts a bounded hold into an unbounded one**, which is [ADR-040](ADR-040.md)'s denial nobody authorized. **The precedent is real and it does not reach this row.**

**SO THE RULING IS A STANDING BAR AND NOT A FOURTH DEFERRAL.** `expirySweepIo` keeps `events` as a required positional parameter with no default. **THE BAR LIFTS ON EITHER OF TWO CONDITIONS AND ON NOTHING ELSE**, stated so a fifth row does not rediscover the argument:

1. **`P5-n` writes a real `ExpiryEventPort` producer this deployable can reach**, which is the blocker [`index.ts:593`](../../apps/worker/src/index.ts) already names; or
2. **the nightly hold and freeze expiry assertion is built and scheduled**, so a sweep that releases nothing is caught by a control that does not depend on the job being absent.

**NEITHER IS THIS ROW'S AND NEITHER IS INVENTED HERE.** A default alone lifts nothing, and the four preceding records are hereby not a queue of deferrals but one ruling with two exits.

### 9. Where this entry is most likely wrong

**THE BAR'S TWO EXIT CONDITIONS ARE A JUDGMENT AND THE SECOND ONE IS THE ARGUABLE HALF.** Condition 2 says a built nightly estate assertion would make the default safe enough to take. **A reader who holds that a job which runs and releases nothing is unacceptable at ANY detection latency will keep only condition 1**, and that reader is not wrong: the assertion catches the failure the morning after rather than preventing it. **The entry takes the looser reading because the alternative is a job that never runs at all, which the same S1 alarms on today**, and states the choice rather than burying it.

**SECTION 6'S SWEEP IS OVER PROSE AND ITS RECALL IS NOT MEASURED.** It enumerated report-lag phrasings and checked the live ones at primary source. **A stale report written in words the sweep did not enumerate is invisible to it**, which is exactly the weakness [ADR-378](ADR-378.md) records for its own `8.1`. The conclusion *not a pattern* is therefore *not a pattern in the shapes swept*, and it is worth no more than that.

**AND SECTION 4 ESTABLISHES THAT THE ONE-EDIT PATH COMPILES; IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH THAT ANY ROW WOULD TAKE IT.** Three rows have now declined. **The argument for saying it out loud is that the three declined on a reason that is false**, and a bar whose stated ground fails on inspection is a bar the fourth row talks itself past.
