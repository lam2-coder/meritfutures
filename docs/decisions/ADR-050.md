## ADR-050: INV-06 gains a stated R-31 exception, and the funded-reset floor is citable  (2026-08-17, status: accepted)

**This ADR records a founder ruling. It resolves nothing on its own authority.** The contradiction was found in [session 45](../sessions/2026-08-16-session-45.md), one of the two candidate resolutions was tested and refuted in [session 47](../sessions/2026-08-16-session-47.md), and both sessions stopped short of ruling for the reason both of them stated: a session that rules on a contradiction it found in its own path is a session grading its own work.

- **Context:** **[M01](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)'s `INV-06` and its `R-31` cannot both be read literally, and the arithmetic that separates them is the corpus's own.**

  `INV-06` reads "the floor never decreases. **No exception, no phase qualifier**, no settlement carve-out", which is the strengthened form [ADR-014](ADR-014.md) gave it when it removed the post-payout recompute. `R-31` sets `floor = size_cents - funded drawdown_cents` at the eval pass, and `R-12`'s own second clause sanctions it in its own words: "at account open, **and again at the funded reset with the funded drawdown**".

  **On every v1 plan the pass day is also a lock day, so the fall is not an edge case.** Core EOD's eval target is 300,000c and its floor lock triggers at 260,000c of closing profit ([Appendix A.1](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)), so **no Core EOD eval pass can happen below the lock trigger**. `DO-7` has already trailed and locked the floor to at least 5,010,000c when `DO-8` rewrites it to 4,750,000c. `RE-P-01` as literally stated fails on `GS-019`.

  **Three fixtures already state the number.** `GS-019`, `GS-020` and `GS-023` each pin `floor_cents: 4750000` after an eval pass, because `R-12`, `R-31` and the `GS-019` registry row all state it outright. Batches 2 and 3 then wrote nothing further that depends on it, which is the constraint this ruling lifts.

  **One of the two candidate readings is already closed.** Session 47 tested the hypothesis that `INV-06` scoped **per account** rescues `R-31`, on the theory that `D-M2-1`'s "or provisioning a new one" makes the funded account a new account. **Five independent primary sources refuse it** and they are recorded in that session's log and in [`progression.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/progression.ts): [M02](../plans/M02-rithmic-bridge.md)'s `DEP-M2-01` names the platform account as the thing that is re-provisioned, [STATE_MACHINES](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) draws the transition inside one account's `active` state, [`0007`](../../packages/db/migrations/0007_accounts.sql) makes `phase` one column on one row with `purchase_id NOT NULL UNIQUE`, [`0015`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql) carries `phase` per day per `account_id`, and `platform_account_refs` is the mechanism that actually lets one Merit account hold successive platform refs. **Per account is already what the corpus means, which is exactly why it cannot rescue `R-31`.**

  That left two readings and both were the founder's: `INV-06` scoped per `(account, phase)`, which "no phase qualifier" argues against in its own words, or `INV-06` gaining a **stated `R-31` exception**.

### The ruling

> **`INV-06` gains a stated `R-31` exception.** The floor never decreases, **except at the funded reset**, where `R-31` sets it to `size_cents - funded drawdown_cents`. Everywhere else the invariant is unchanged: no phase qualifier, no settlement carve-out, and no second exception is created by this one.

**`4,750,000c` at 50K is therefore correct and citable**, and a fixture may state it without ratifying an unmade ruling. The three fixtures that already state it are confirmed rather than edited.

**The scope alternative is declined rather than merely not chosen.** Scoping the invariant per `(account, phase)` would have made the same arithmetic true, and it was rejected because it weakens the invariant everywhere in order to permit it in one place. An exception names the one step that is permitted; a scope qualifier silently permits every step that crosses a phase boundary, including ones nobody has written yet. **`INV-06` is a property a reader checks against a diff, and a stated exception is the form of it a reader can check.**

### Why the exception is safe, in the one number that decides it

**Loss room is unchanged across the reset, exactly, and that is checkable arithmetic rather than a judgement.** On a Core EOD 50K account passing at a close of 5,300,000c:

| | Balance | Floor | Loss room |
|---|---|---|---|
| After `DO-7` on the pass day | 5,300,000 | 5,050,000 (`hwb - 250,000`, [section 3.4](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)'s `max` against the locked 5,010,000) | **250,000** |
| After `DO-8`'s reset | 5,000,000 | 4,750,000 | **250,000** |

**The balance falls in the same step as the floor and by the same amount**, so the funded account opens with exactly the funded drawdown beneath it, which is what `R-12` says a floor is for. **[ADR-014](ADR-014.md)'s concern was a floor falling under a balance that did not**, which is the post-payout recompute it deleted, and it is not this case. `INV-21`'s worst case is untouched: it is computed against the funded floor, which this ruling leaves where `R-12` puts it.

### What this ruling does NOT reach, and the second half is a live founder item

**`DO-7`'s `INV-06` tripwire is not weakened and must not be.** [`floor.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/floor.ts) throws `EngineInvariantError('INV-06')` if the floor falls within one day's trail-then-lock, and that check never sees this step: it runs at `DO-7`, strictly before the progression block at `DO-8`. **Widening it to span the reset would make it fire on the corpus's own arithmetic**, and narrowing anything else about it would cost the check the future edit to `advanceFloor` it exists to catch. It stays as it is.

**`RE-P-01` may now be written**, with the exception stated in the property rather than in a comment: `floor(d+1) >= floor(d)` for every day except the one carrying `phase.passed`, where the funded reset is the expected value. That is [P2](../plans/P2-rules-engine.md)'s property session and it is unblocked by this ADR.

> **`INV-07` IS IN EXACTLY THE POSITION `INV-06` WAS IN, AND THIS RULING DOES NOT TOUCH IT.** `INV-07` reads "a locked floor never changes again for the life of the account", enforced by `RE-P-02` and `GS-016`. **The same reset clears the lock**: [`progression.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/progression.ts) writes `floorLocked: false` at `DO-8`, derived from [section 3.4](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)'s floor machine starting at `trailing` and from `R-12` naming the funded reset as one of its two entry points, **because a funded account that arrived still locked would have `hwb` frozen at `size_cents` and `R-13` guarded off for the rest of its life, which is the funded floor deleted rather than a reading of `R-31`.** The derivation is sound and it is not the point. **The point is that `R-31` does not name `floorLocked` at all, and `INV-07` says "for the life of the account" in the same words `INV-06` said "no phase qualifier".** `RE-P-02` fails on `GS-019` the way `RE-P-01` did.
>
> **This is reported, not ruled.** The founder ruled `INV-06`; extending the ruling to `INV-07` because the shape matches would be a session ruling on an invariant its own fixtures cross. **No fixture in batch 4 pins `floor_locked` across an eval pass**, which is the same restraint `GS-019` already exercised for a different reason.

- **Consequences:** [M01](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)'s `INV-06` row carries the exception and cites this ADR; that one-line amendment to a frozen document is what this ADR authorizes. **Golden fixtures may cross the eval pass and state the funded-reset floor**, which is what batch 4 does. `RE-P-01` is unblocked. **`INV-07` versus the funded reset becomes a named open founder item** in [STATE.md](../STATE.md) rather than a latent one, and no fixture pins `floor_locked` across the pass until it is ruled. **No rule, no operator, no parameter and no migration moves**: [`progression.ts`](../../packages/rules-engine/src/day/progression.ts) already computes the ruled behavior, so this ADR changes the corpus's description of the engine and not the engine.

- **Alternatives considered:** **`INV-06` scoped per `(account, phase)`** (declined above: it weakens the invariant everywhere to permit it in one place, and "no phase qualifier" argues against it in its own words); **`INV-06` scoped per account** (refuted by five primary sources in session 47, and it is already the corpus's meaning); **changing `R-31` so the funded floor is `max(eval floor, size - funded drawdown)`** (rejected: it contradicts `R-12`, `R-31` and the `GS-019` registry row simultaneously, and it would hand a funded account a floor above its own size on every plan where the lock had engaged, which is a floor the funded drawdown cannot explain); **leaving the contradiction open and writing no fixture that depends on it** (rejected by the founder: three fixtures already depend on it, so the contradiction was not being avoided, only unrecorded).

- **Founder approval (2026-08-17): ACCEPTED.** `INV-06` gains a stated `R-31` exception; 4,750,000c is correct and citable.
