## ADR-050: `INV-06` gains a stated `R-31` exception, and it is exactly one rule  (2026-08-17, status: accepted)

**[M01](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md)'s `INV-06` is amended by this ruling. [Session 45](../sessions/2026-08-16-session-45.md) raised the conflict, [session 47](../sessions/2026-08-16-session-47.md) tested and refuted the reading that would have avoided it, and this ADR closes it. `RE-P-01` is what it unblocks.**

- **Context:** **`INV-06` as frozen reads "the floor never decreases. No exception, no phase qualifier, no settlement carve-out" ([M01 section 1.5](../plans/M01-rules-engine.md), enforced by `RE-P-01`, `GS-010` and `GS-081`). `R-31` lowers the floor.**

  On the eval pass day `R-31` writes `floor = size_cents - funded drawdown_cents`, `R-12` states the same number from the other side ("at account open, **and again at the funded reset with the funded drawdown**"), and `GS-019`'s registry row states it outright: "Balance to 5,000,000, floor to 4,750,000, all funded counters to zero." On CORE-50K the funded floor is 4,750,000c and the eval floor at that moment is **at least the locked 5,010,000c**, because the lock triggers at 260,000c of profit and the eval target is 300,000c, so **every** v1 eval pass is also a lock day. **The fall is therefore at least 260,000c and is usually larger**, because section 3.4's `max` leaves the floor at the *trailed* value on a day that jumps past the trigger: on `GS-019`'s own day the close is 5,400,000c, the floor reaches 5,150,000c, and it falls **400,000c** on the account's best day.

  Three golden fixtures already pin the number: `GS-019`, `GS-020` and `GS-023` each carry `floor_cents: 4750000` after a pass. **`RE-P-01` as literally stated fails on all three**, which is why the property and its generator could not be written while the invariant said what it said.

  **The exposure is one rule, not a class.** `R-19` and `R-48` were the other candidate and [ADR-014](ADR-014.md) already removed them: a settlement reduces `balanceCents` and touches neither `floorCents`, nor `highWaterBalanceCents`, nor `floorLocked`. `GS-081` is that fixture and it is unaffected by this ruling.

### The ruling

> **`INV-06`: the floor never decreases, EXCEPT at the `R-31` funded reset, where it is initialised to `size_cents` minus the funded drawdown (`R-12`, `R-31`, `GS-019`).**

**"No exception, no phase qualifier" exists to prevent UNSTATED exceptions**, which is to say someone quietly introducing a case nobody sees. **A single named, cited, testable exception is the opposite of what that language guards against.** The clause was written to stop the invariant being eroded by additions nobody wrote down; writing one down, with its rule, its second rule and its fixture beside it, is the clause being honoured rather than weakened.

**Scoping to `(account, phase)` makes the invariant technically true by redefining its domain**, and that is why it is rejected. The floor still falls on the pass day, by at least 260,000c and by 400,000c on `GS-019`'s own day; a reader who wants to know what happens at the transition finds an invariant that no longer speaks to it and has to reconstruct why. It **hides the same fact** the exception states, and it costs every future reader the reconstruction. An invariant that is true because its domain was narrowed to exclude the case is not a stronger invariant, it is a quieter one.

**[ADR-014](ADR-014.md)'s permanent floor lock is about SETTLEMENT never resetting the floor.** Its consequence line, "the floor becomes strictly monotone non-decreasing, with no exceptions", is written against the post-payout recompute it had just retired, and its whole argument is about loss room after an extraction: `post_payout_floor_rule` is `none`, so a payout reduces the balance and the floor stays put. **`R-31` is a PHASE TRANSITION, not a settlement**, and at it the balance falls to `size_cents` in the **same step**. Nothing about the trader's loss room narrows; ADR-014's concern was a floor falling under a balance that did not. **The exception is narrow and it is exactly one rule.**

### What the per-account reading could not do, recorded because it was tested

**[Session 47](../sessions/2026-08-16-session-47.md) tested the hypothesis that the funded account is a NEW account**, so that nothing decreases within one account and "no phase qualifier" holds exactly. **Five primary sources refuse it**, and the refutation is not repeated here beyond its conclusion: [M02](../plans/M02-rithmic-bridge.md)'s `DEP-M2-01` names the subject as **the platform account**, [STATE_MACHINES](../architecture/STATE_MACHINES.md) draws `eval_phase --> funded_phase` inside one account's `active` state, [`0007`](../../packages/db/migrations/0007_accounts.sql) makes `phase` one column on one row with `accounts.purchase_id` `NOT NULL UNIQUE`, [`0015`](../../packages/db/migrations/0015_rule_states.sql) is unique on `(account_id, trading_day)` and carries `phase` per day, and `platform_account_refs` is the mechanism by which one Merit account holds successive **platform** refs.

**So per account is already what the corpus means, and that is exactly why it does not help**: read per account, `INV-06` is the reading `R-31` violates. That left two readings and both were the founder's. This ADR takes the second.

It stays exactly as it is |
| **The exception is PINNED, not merely permitted** | `RE-P-01` asserts the reset floor's value at the transition rather than skipping the step. An exception that only excused a decrease would let any decrease through on a pass day, which is the unstated exception this ruling refuses to create |
| **No schema change, no migration** | `rule_states.floor_cents` already stores whatever the fold computes. Nothing about the stored shape moves |

- **Alternatives considered:**

  **`INV-06` scoped per `(account, phase)`** (rejected above: it makes the invariant true by redefining its domain, hides the fall it does not mention, and "no phase qualifier" argues against it in its own words).

  **`INV-06` scoped per account** (rejected because it is not an alternative: [session 47](../sessions/2026-08-16-session-47.md) established that per account is already what the corpus means, and per account is the reading `R-31` violates).

  **Amending `R-31` to carry the eval floor into the funded phase** (rejected: it contradicts `R-12`, `R-31` and `GS-019` at once, and it would hand a funded account a floor above `size_cents - funded drawdown` that no plan parameter produced. `R-31`'s balance reset is "the single largest trader-facing fact in this document" and the floor reset is its other half).

  **Leaving `INV-06` unruled and writing `RE-P-01` around the pass day** (rejected: a property that skips the one transition it cannot explain is `TR-03`'s weakened assertion, and three fixtures would have stayed unratified indefinitely).

  **Retiring `INV-06` and relying on the `DO-7` tripwire** (rejected: the tripwire is a within-day check on `advanceFloor` and cannot see a sequence at all. The invariant and the tripwire cover different intervals and neither substitutes for the other).
