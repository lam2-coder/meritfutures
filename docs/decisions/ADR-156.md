## ADR-156: `OI-28` was closed by a ruling and dispatched as open, and `0047` proves the publish decision sound AT THE WRITE and nothing preserves it after  (2026-08-27, status: proposed)

**Two register rows were dispatched as open. Neither says what the tree says, and the two are wrong in different directions.** `OI-28` was closed by an accepted ruling with founder approval granted, and its row names the session that closed it inside the cell that calls it open: the repair is a register amendment and not an engineering change. `OI-29` was partly closed by `0047`, and what its two surviving checks guarantee is **narrower than every sentence in the corpus that names them**: they are asserted when the publish row is written and are falsifiable by an ordinary `UPDATE` one statement later, measured here under the application role rather than reasoned about.

- **Origin:** session 267's dispatch, which quotes [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md)'s `OI-nn` table verbatim on both rows. The dispatch transcribed the register faithfully; the register is what is wrong. **That is the third time this week**, after `OI-14`, `OI-30` and `OI-18`, and it is why the dispatch's own instruction was to check each row against the tree before planning a repair.
- **This entry installs no control and writes no SQL.** It claims no migration number, adds no `CI-06` letter, changes no engine file and does not touch [ADR-042](ADR-042.md)'s vocabulary. It rules what an installed money-path control guarantees, so the [E2](../../MERIT_BUILD_MASTER_PROMPT.md) read is not owed on a diff. **Section 4 is owed to the founder's attention anyway**, because its subject is the rule contract every funded account is sold under.

### 2. `OI-28`: the token fits, the vocabulary is untouched, and the repair is a row

The dispatch put the question correctly and its answer is already on disk. [ADR-082](ADR-082.md) ruled that **the closed vocabulary widens its DEFINITION and never its token list**: `rail clock` reads *"a third party's own clock, quoted and never computed by Merit: a payment rail's, a calibration vendor's, any counterparty whose day Merit reads and never derives"*, and `rail` names the class after its commonest member. `UNIT_TOKENS` in [`gates.mjs`](../../scripts/corpus/gates.mjs) still holds exactly three members. The measurement that ruling turned on is that **the same `date` columns resolved to the same tokens before and after**, so nothing was re-classified.

**Three things are therefore true and none of them needed this session:** the column's declaration is correct and cites the ruling; a fourth token is refused by an argument that binds the next `date` column arriving from outside; and the refusal is a control rather than a sentence, because `CI-06m/widened-definition-is-not-a-fourth-token` in [`falsify.mjs`](../../scripts/corpus/falsify.mjs) seeds `**Unit: vendor clock**` onto a row that declares `rail clock` and watches `CI-06m` refuse it.

**So no token is widened here and `ADR-042` is not amended.** The dispatch's conditional, *"if no declared token fits, that is the finding"*, does not fire: a token fits and was ruled to fit.

**What did not happen is the register hearing about it.** [ADR-082](ADR-082.md)'s consequences say so in terms, naming the row it could not reach: *"`OI-28` is closed by this ruling and its row is not edited here ... it still reads `open` until a session holding that file amends it."* The design record repeats it at the column, for the reader most likely to follow the link. **Two documents predicted this exact stale row and named the session type that would fix it, and the next dispatch read the row anyway.** A prediction written into the documents a reader arrives at is still prose; the register is what a dispatch reads.

**This session is that session, and the repair is two rows.** No file under `docs/architecture/` is touched: this fence granted the `simulation_runs` design record **only if `OI-28`'s repair were there**, and it is not.

### 4. What the constraint and the trigger actually guarantee, MEASURED

**The claim under test is `0047`'s own, at its `COMMENT ON FUNCTION`:** *"The FK proves a simulation run was named; this proves what it says."* That sentence reads as standing and what it describes is momentary. Both triggers fire on the write of the publish decision and nowhere else: one `BEFORE INSERT` scoped to `NEW.status = 'published'`, one `BEFORE UPDATE` scoped additionally to `OLD.status IS DISTINCT FROM 'published'`. **Nothing re-evaluates either check afterwards, and nothing pins the row they read.**

**The two halves of the link are pinned in opposite directions, which is why only one of them can move.**

| Side | State | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| The **citing** row, `plan_versions.decided_on_simulation_run_id` | **Frozen completely.** A published row admits one transition, to `retired` | [`0028`](../../packages/db/migrations/0028_supersede_plan_version_immutability.sql)'s guard derives its pinned columns from `jsonb_each(to_jsonb(NEW))`, so the column was immutable the moment `0045` created it, with no change to the trigger |
| The **cited** row, `simulation_runs.status` and `.plan_version_id`, the exact two columns both checks read | **Free.** No trigger, no `REVOKE`, no immutability guard, and `merit_app` holds `UPDATE` | [`0045`](../../packages/db/migrations/0045_simulation_runs.sql):69 makes the anchor nullable on purpose and `0045`'s grant note keeps the table out of the append-only pattern deliberately: *"`status` and `completed_at` move by design as a run executes"* |

#### The measurement

PostgreSQL 16.13. Every migration applied forward-only into an empty database under `ON_ERROR_STOP=1`, which is the same construction `0047`'s own defect measurement used. [`probe_publish_decision_is_sound.sql`](../../scripts/db/probe_publish_decision_is_sound.sql) run first against that database and green on all of its assertions, so the control under test is the installed one. Then, inside one rolled-back transaction under **`SET LOCAL ROLE merit_app`**, because a hole only a superuser can reach is a different finding:

```
        step         |                                    verdict
---------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 BASELINE            | a publish decided on a COMPLETE run anchored to THIS row is written,
                     | both checks satisfied
 A: run -> failed    | UPDATE accepted, run status is now 'failed', and the published row still
                     | cites it (1 row)
 A: run -> queued    | UPDATE accepted, run status is now 'queued' with completed_at NULL: the
                     | publish cannot be dated against the run it names
 B: run re-anchored  | UPDATE accepted, the cited run now ran over plan B version <uuid>, and
                     | plan A version 1 is still published citing it
 C: publish side     | REFUSED: plan_version <uuid> is published and immutable. Publish a new
                     | version instead. Columns cha...
 D: re-publish touch | REFUSED: plan_version <uuid> is published. The only permitted transition
                     | is published -> retired wi...
```

**Read `A` and `B` against the refusals `0047` was written to produce.** A publish decided on a `failed` run is refused at the write, naming `OI-29 check A`. The identical end state is reached by publishing on a `complete` run and then failing it, and no control anywhere sees the second path. Check B is the same shape: a publish decided on a run that ran over another version is refused by name, and re-anchoring the run afterwards reaches that end state with one accepted statement.

**`A: run -> queued` is the sharper of the two**, because it removes a property `0047` reasons about explicitly. The header records that check A *"buys a completion time for free"* through `simulation_runs_terminal_has_completion` being a biconditional, so *"a publish can therefore always be dated against the run it names"*. **After the second `UPDATE` it cannot**, and the biconditional is what permits it: moving `status` back off a terminal value requires nulling `completed_at`, and doing both in one statement satisfies every constraint on the table.

**Two things the schema does hold, and they are the reason this is a gap and not a collapse.** The row cannot vanish: `DELETE FROM simulation_runs` on a cited run is refused by `plan_versions_decided_on_simulation_run_id_fkey`, measured under the same role. And the citing side does not move at all: `C` is `0028`'s byte-identity check and `D` is its transition check, so even a column `0028` counts as movable is unwritable on a published row while the row stays published. **So the link is permanent, the citation is permanent, and only the content of the thing cited is free.**

### 5. The ruling

> **`plan_versions_publish_decision_recorded` and `0047`'s two checks are WRITE-TIME guarantees. They establish that a publish decision was sound at the instant it was recorded and they establish nothing about it afterwards. Wherever the corpus names `OI-29`'s two closed states, it names them as write-time states.**
>
> **The asymmetry is the mechanism rather than an oversight, and it is stated so the next reader does not have to find it:** `0028` freezes the citing row completely and `0045` deliberately leaves the cited row free, so the only unpinned half of the link is the half both checks read.
>
> **A third condition is required for the guarantee the control's name suggests, and it is named here and NOT NUMBERED:** *a `simulation_runs` row cited by a `published` `plan_versions` row must not have `status` or `plan_version_id` changed, except for the anchor moving from `NULL` to the row that cites it.* **No migration number is claimed and no `OI` number is claimed.** Migrations are sacred once merged (constitution E2) and the allocator's `OI` table is outside this fence beyond the two rows it holds.

**This does not close `OI-29` and this entry says so rather than reporting a closure.** The dispatch asked for the last two dispatchable items to close. One closes. The other has now been open in three directions, of which `0047` shut two at the write and this entry measures how far "at the write" reaches. **A row moved to closed on the strength of a control that holds for one statement would be `RI-04` again**, a check cited to protect something it asserts nothing about, and the corpus has paid for that shape once already.

### 6. Why nothing is installed here, and what the constraint would actually cost

**The obvious guard is wrong and the corpus's own probe is what proves it.** "Refuse any `UPDATE` to a cited run" refuses [`probe_publish_decision_is_sound.sql`](../../scripts/db/probe_publish_decision_is_sound.sql):160, which is one statement after the publish it has just proved sound:

```sql
UPDATE simulation_runs SET plan_version_id = pv2 WHERE id = r_ok2;
```

That is the legitimate move, and it is legitimate for the reason `0045` makes the column nullable at all: **the run happened over a draft, the draft became a row, and the anchor catches up.** SUCCESS 1 asserts it as ordinary and names it in its own verdict, *"and the run is re-anchored to it afterwards"*. **A guard written from the defect rather than from the table would have turned the probe red and the session would have learned the requirement by breaking it**, which is what happened to `0047` when it read `SUCCESS 4` and case `0028 A` and narrowed check B accordingly. The requirement is inherited here rather than rediscovered.

**So the constraint is narrow, and narrow is why it needs a ruling instead of a cheap `CHECK`.** It must permit `NULL -> the citing row`, refuse every other anchor move, and refuse `status` leaving `complete`, on rows a `published` version cites and on no others. [ADR-087](ADR-087.md) section 6's own foreclosure governs: *"a money-path migration that installs an unruled constraint because it was cheap is how a schema acquires rules nobody decided."*

**And this residue is a different shape from `OI-29b`, which is the part that decides its priority.** `OI-29b` waits on two things that do not exist: a producer for `rules_digest`, and a canonical serialization of the rule contract that `jsonb::text` is measured not to be. **This one waits on nothing.** The falsifying write is on `simulation_runs`, so the application publish path [ADR-087](ADR-087.md) ruled against cannot reach it either way, and the database can close it today with the mechanism `0047` already chose. **It is the cheaper of the two and the one whose cost is entirely in getting the exception right.**

**What is left undone and why, said rather than discovered later:**

| Owed | Why not here |
|---|---|
| The trigger on `simulation_runs`, and its migration number | **NO MIGRATION NUMBER.** The fence, and constitution E2 |
| An `OI` number for the preservation condition | The allocator's `OI` table is held here for the `OI-28` and `OI-29` rows and no others |
| [`simulation_runs`](../architecture/data-model/simulation_runs.md)'s design record, which is where a reader standing at `status` and `plan_version_id` will meet this | This fence granted that file **conditionally on `OI-28`'s repair being there**, and section 2 is why the condition did not fire. **Both columns are already marked load bearing for a control one table over**, and what that record does not say is that the control reads them once |
| The probe case that would watch the preservation refusal | There is nothing installed to watch refuse, and a probe asserting a hole belongs beside the guard that closes it, as `SUCCESS 3` is beside check B |

### 9. Consequences

- **`OI-28` is CLOSED.** Its rows in [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) and in [DELTA_MANIFEST](../../packages/db/DELTA_MANIFEST.md) section 16 are amended in place ([ADR-065](ADR-065.md) T3) to record [ADR-082](ADR-082.md) as the closing ruling. **No engineering change was required and none was made.**
- **`OI-29` stays open, restated.** Both rows record what `0047` closes, that it closes it **at the write**, that `OI-29b` carries the digest state, and that the preservation condition of section 5 is a third thing owed.
- **The guarantee is renamed in the corpus and not in the schema.** `0047`'s `COMMENT ON FUNCTION` is inside a merged migration and can be superseded, never edited (E2). **So the correction can only live where a reader meets it before the comment**, which is the register and this entry, and the design record when a session holds it.
- **No `SD-nn`, no migration, no new `CI-06` letter, no `RI-nn`, no engine change, no dependency and no `VG` row.**

- **Founder approval: PENDING, UNSIGNED.** No diff on the money path: this entry writes no SQL and no code. **Section 4 is nonetheless for the founder's read**, because it narrows a claim about `plan_versions` that three merged documents state without the qualifier.

  **One checkable clause, carrying the direction the ruling could be wrong in rather than the direction it was written for:**

  > **On a database carrying the full migration set, under `SET LOCAL ROLE merit_app`: a publish decided on a `complete` run anchored to the published row is WRITTEN; the subsequent `UPDATE simulation_runs SET status = 'failed'` on that same run is ACCEPTED; and the published row still cites it.** If any of those three changes, a control was installed between this entry and the reading, and this entry's ruling is the thing to re-read rather than the schema.

  **The clause is a NEGATIVE control and that is deliberate.** Every falsification case in this repository seeds a defect and watches a gate go red. **There is nothing here to watch go red**, so the assertion is that the guard does not fire, and the day it starts firing is the day the preservation condition landed. **A clause of that shape rots in exactly one direction and it is the safe one:** it fails when the hole is closed, not when it is widened.

  **Cost if wrong:** a published rule contract cites a simulation run that now reads `failed`, or that now says it ran over a different plan, with a constraint named `plan_versions_publish_decision_recorded` and a function named `assert_publish_decision_is_sound` standing beside it. **A reader asking whether the publish was sound is answered yes by the names and no by the rows**, and `FM-M21-03` calls that amnesia the module was admitted to end.

  **Every load-bearing claim above was executed or read at its source in this session.** Section 4's table is one run of the migration set and one rolled-back transaction, not an inference from the trigger bodies; the probe was run first so the control under test was known to be the installed one; `ADR-156` was verified free against [ALLOCATION](ALLOCATION.md) before it was written; and the stale grep in section 3 is the transcript this session ran rather than the transcript the corpus pinned, which is how it was found.

  **What a founder read adds and this entry cannot.** The approval line already says section 4 is for the founder's read; what a read decides is what to do about it. **The entry NARROWS a claim three merged documents state without the qualifier**: `plan_versions_publish_decision_recorded` and `assert_publish_decision_is_sound` prove the publish decision sound AT THE WRITE, and the run they cite can be updated to `failed`, or re-anchored to a different plan version, one statement later. That is measured under the application role rather than argued, and the entry then declines to install anything: the preservation condition is named as owed and owned by nobody. Whether a money-path control may stand in that state, with two names beside it that a reader will take as standing guarantees, is the judgement. **The second is the approval clause's own shape.** It is a NEGATIVE control asserting that a guard does NOT fire, so it rots in the safe direction and it can never be watched going red, which is the discipline every other clause in this corpus is held to.
