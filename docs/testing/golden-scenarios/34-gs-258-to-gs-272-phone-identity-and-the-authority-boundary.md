## 34. GS-258 to GS-272: phone identity and the authority boundary (FOLD-01)

[ADR-039](../../decisions/ADR-039.md), planned by [FOLD-01](../../plans/FOLD-01-phone-identity.md). **One scenario per ruling point (a) to (e), plus amendments 2 and 3, plus the authority boundary**, which is §6.2's stated minimum of eight. The block runs past it because several of those points are only pinned by a **pair**: (b) needs the refusal and the completion, amendment 3 needs the guard working and the guard with no input, and the boundary needs the server refusal, the disabled render and the end-to-end pass, since a boundary tested only where it refuses is indistinguishable from one that refuses everything.

**The range continues from the registry maximum, derived rather than quoted.** Fifteen hand-maintained numbers in this corpus have been found wrong, and the two most recent were counts of this registry.

**Five of these discharge citations owed by earlier sessions**, named in words in [M19 §7.8](../../plans/M19-kyc-identity.md), [M16 §6](../../plans/M16-notification-center.md) and [M04 §8.2](../../plans/M04-trader-portal.md). Those sessions declined to write identifiers before the rows existed, because `CI-06d` fails on any `GS-nnn` cited anywhere in `docs/` that does not resolve, and pre-claiming in a registry with no claim mechanism is the drift [ADR-034](../../decisions/ADR-034.md) exists to stop. The debt is settled here.

| ID | Scenario | Pins |
|---|---|---|
| GS-258 | **(a)** A registration supplies a `voip` number with a fresh email, a datacenter IP and no digital footprint | The lookup runs, `line_type` is stored as `voip`, **registration completes**, and the four signals are recorded together as the **fleet signature** and scored by D-16. Pins that **VoIP is scored and never rejected**: the assertion is that the refusal does *not* happen, because a legitimate customer on a VoIP line is an ordinary customer and a refused signal is worth less than a scored one. The composite is a named input to a score, never a verdict. AS-M19-09's residual is the case this one does not cover |
| GS-259 | **(a)** The lookup vendor times out at registration | Registration **completes**, the `identity_phones` row is written with `lookup_provider` set and the carrier fields null, and the missing lookup is recorded rather than retried inline. Pins that the registration call site inherits checkout's failure posture **verbatim**, non-blocking and fail-open on timeout, which is the posture [FOLD-01 §3.1](../../plans/FOLD-01-phone-identity.md) made a condition of reusing the vendor rather than buying a second one |
| GS-260 | **(b)** One identity attempts to verify a second phone while its first is live | Refused by `identity_phones_live_per_identity_uq`, a **partial unique index and a genuine database constraint**, not a handler check. Pins the uncontested direction of the hard link: **identity to phone is one live verified phone**, and the replacement path is the (c) ceremony rather than a second row |
| GS-261 | **(b)** A second identity verifies a number already live on another identity | **Verification completes.** The edge is written at the hard-link confidence ceiling, a **severity-5 flag opens against both identities**, and **no state changes automatically**. `identity_phones_live_number_idx` is deliberately **not unique** and that is what buys the time GS-262 needs. The API response is **indistinguishable from an ordinary success**, because naming the prior holder is what AS-M19-05 counter 4 forbids. `INV-M19-13`, and it is the contested half ruled at plan approval |
| GS-262 | **Amendment 3** A banned identity's number is reassigned by the carrier and its new owner registers honestly | The portability check runs on evidence (a) already bought: `last_ported_at` falls **after** the prior holder's restriction date, so the prior row is **released** rather than superseded, `release_evidence` carries the portability record, and `identity_phones_release_is_evidenced` refuses a release that asserts this without showing it. The released row **frees the live index**, so the new customer verifies normally and no operator unpicks anything by hand. **Neither person is ever told about the other.** AS-M19-09, EC-143 |
| GS-263 | **Amendment 3** The same reassignment with **no portability record at all** | The guard has **no input**, nothing is released, the severity-5 flag stays open, and the case ends in a **review queue with nothing enforced**. Pins the residual AS-M19-09 states plainly rather than the happy path twice, and it is the scenario `DEP-M19-09` exists to make rare: a vendor that cannot supply portability history cannot be selected, because this is what the corpus looks like without it. EC-143 |
| GS-264 | **(c)** A phone change runs the full D4 ceremony | `applied` is reached only with **dual-channel verification, the prior number and email both notified, and `withdrawal_hold_until` still in the future**, which is `phone_change_requests_applied_is_complete` executed rather than read. Pins that the 48 hour external-withdrawal hold is **still running when the change lands**, since a hold that expires on application is a hold that protected nothing. EC-146 |
| GS-265 | **(c)** A change request reaches application with the prior number notified and **nothing sent** | The constraint **passes**, because a database can only assert that a timestamp exists. Pins the gap rather than the control: `prior_notified_at` is **storage-enforceable and not send-enforceable**, and this scenario is the executable form of that distinction. It asserts that the **dispatch** exists as an `integration_dispatches` row addressed to the prior channel, which is the assertion the `CHECK` cannot make and the only one that can tell a notified prior holder from a filled column. EC-146, `INV-M16-03` |
| GS-266 | **(d) and C-27** A session established by SMS OTP alone requests a payout destination change | Refused **server-side** with `403`, and the response **names the factor required** so the client can offer it. `sessions.elevated_by_factor` has no `sms_otp` value to write, so the refusal is a **vocabulary** rather than a handler check. The half a rendering test cannot cover, and `INV-M4-15` is the invariant. M04 §8.2's second owed scenario |
| GS-267 | **C-27** The same session loads the portal | **Every read surface renders**, and each sensitive action renders **disabled with its reason and its route to elevation**. Pins that the boundary is **shown rather than hit**: a trader learns what they must do before they act, not after they are refused. The pair with GS-266 is the whole invariant, since a boundary tested only where it refuses is indistinguishable from one that refuses everything. M04 §8.2's first owed scenario |
| GS-268 | **C-27** A SIM-swapped session is used end to end | It **sees everything and changes nothing**: account state, marks, timeline, payout history and the active-sessions list all render, and destination change, contact change and external withdrawal are each refused. Pins the invariant's own sentence as a single executable pass, and pins that the **active-sessions row shows the establishing factor**, which is what makes the swap visible to the person it was taken from. AS-M4-05, SC-M4-11 |
| GS-269 | **(c) and C-27** The phone-change ceremony is visible on the security screen while its hold runs | `SC-M4-11` shows the open request, its state, and `withdrawal_hold_until` as a running time. Pins that the hold is **exposed rather than inferred**: the trader sees the same value the payout path will read, instead of meeting it as an unexplained refusal at the end of it. M04 §8.2's third owed scenario |
| GS-270 | **Amendment 2** A pumping run drives registration OTP volume at attacker-controlled premium-rate numbers | Per-number, per-IP and per-country velocity all bite, and **`notification_kinds.rate_limit_exempt` is false for the pre-identity class by construction**, not by configuration. Pins amendment 2's split: `INV-M16-11` is **CONFIRMED and unamended**, the security and money classes stay exempt, and the fifth class is neither of them. A seed row cannot re-exempt it because the column is generated. AS-M16-07, EC-144, and `CI-06k`'s third assertion is the same fact checked from the tree |
| GS-271 | **Amendment 2, the degraded path** The cost breaker trips mid-registration | **Registration completes.** The identity reaches `pre_funded`, and **the funding gate refuses it until phone verification lands**. The trip, the degraded window and the recovery each **alarm**, and `deferred_registrations` is reported. Pins the founder's ruling that **the breaker degrades and does not stop**, which is the one thing in this fold a careful implementer would have got backwards: fail-closed here protects an SMS bill and destroys the business, and a control whose failure mode is "Merit stops acquiring customers" is one an attacker will trip deliberately. Named by [ADR-039](../../decisions/ADR-039.md) in the ruling itself, because **a degraded path with no fixture is a path that gets removed by whoever finds it confusing**. EC-142 |
| GS-272 | **(e)** SMS delivery and lookup costs land in the Cost Stack | Per-message and per-lookup cost appear as [M03](../../plans/M03-billing-checkout.md) Cost Stack lines attributable to the acquisition path, and a degraded-window spend is attributable to the window rather than smeared across the month. Pins that (e) is a **cost and deliverability** ruling as well as a security-class one, and that the line item exists before anybody has to explain an invoice. EC-149 |
